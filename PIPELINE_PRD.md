# Pipeline PRD — what the three pipelines must do
*2026-08-06 · the WHAT/WHY companion to `PIPELINE_ARCHITECTURE.md` (the HOW). When the two disagree, this doc wins and the architecture gets amended.*

## Product statement
The desk converts public filings and the market tape into three products. Pipelines may automatically **stage** order instructions (see Execution staging), but every execution is a per-order human act by the principal, and court verdicts are never automated:
1. **Verified starter positions in orphaned value** — names nobody researches, screened cheap, trap-verified against primary sources.
2. **Sized entries in dislocated liquid quality** — names/classes repriced by a narrative whose damage claim is testable against current data.
3. **Graded triggers** that convert research inventory (verdicts, watches, classifications) into executed decisions — and a calibration record that grades every claim the desk makes.
Byproducts that are also products: the knowledge graph (mechanisms × channels × latency) and the defect/miss ledgers. A failed pipeline that produces a graded negative is a success at the knowledge level (MAUDE-velocity precedent).

## Users
- **The principal** (Ajay): reviews queues, adjudicates courts, approves/places orders in TWS. Never required to poll — everything decision-ready must surface (morning brief, dashboard, push on CRITICAL).
- **Assistant sessions**: operate pipelines, run courts, write stores. Sessions are views; files are the truth (handoff doctrine). Any session must be able to cold-start a pipeline stage from stores + this doc.
- **Subagent steps**: stateless verifiers/triagers consuming versioned templates; their outputs are data, not chat.

## Pipeline 1 — Orphan verification
**Problem.** Post-MiFID-II/orphan shelves (GB smallcaps, JP 有報 net-nets, KR value-up cohort, Nordics) reprice slowly because nobody does the work; raw screens are ~40-60% traps; manual DD doesn't scale past ~8 names/session. Measured book evidence: small/orphan sleeves +11.8% on cost vs +4.5% rest-of-book (2026-08-06, N=15 — hypothesis, not yet validated).
**Job.** Every fy-current, scale-clean name that surfaces cheap on a covered shelf gets trap-verified against primary sources, and every survivor reaches court-ready state with dated gates wired — without a human remembering any step.

**Functional requirements**
- R1.1 Coverage: EU (ESEF: GB/PL/SE/FI/DK/NO), JP (EDINET), KR (DART) corpora maintained; feed-lag flagged per country, never hidden.
- R1.2 Freshness: filing-index delta detected **same day** (daily poll); new filings extracted within 24h of detection; shortlist re-marked on prices daily.
- R1.3 Candidate queue: new entrants pass the exclusion join (ledger ∪ resting orders ∪ batch history) and two-mark hysteresis; queue is inspectable at any time.
- R1.4 Verification: batch fires at ≥6 candidates or 30d staleness; every name checked against the full trap catalog (cycle, FCF-quality, balance-sheet, no-actor, filer-tie, WHT/stamp, liquidity, price-move); every dated claim carries a primary citation or an explicit UNVERIFIED; verifiers may refute the briefing (corrections are first-class output).
- R1.5 Promotion: Stage-2 → ledger same run; **WATCH cannot exist without a future-dated resolution pack** (hard invariant); AVOID-artifact → defect ledger; AVOID-company → ledger with thesis. Batch doc is generated, never hand-assembled.
- R1.9 **Fair-carry is a product, not a screen-out (2026-08-06, user-directed)**: in orphaned names, "fairly priced by our computation" is a FIRST-CLASS outcome (verdict FAIR-CARRY), not a decline — in unresearched paper the fair-value computation IS the edge (diligence-replication), our orphan FVs carry a documented ~13% conservative bias, and HSBK/III both re-rated through "fair." FAIR-CARRY advances to court like REAL; the court adjudicates the fairly-paid-risk bar (fairness verified, tails bounded/unlevered, sleeve-capped, carry-vs-edge labeled) — never a "no edge → no trade" shortcut. "Priced-in" claims on uncovered names require a coverage check: no coverage, no efficient price.
- R1.8 **Auto-court conveyor (2026-08-06, user-directed)**: names surfaced by the screens flow automatically through trap-verification and — for survivors — the red AND blue court benches, headlessly (`desk/court_queue.py` → `desk/court_runner.py`, hourly cron, ≤4 dispatches/run, output-side validators). Screens enqueue idempotently; ledger names are refused at the door. **Adjudication is deliberately NOT automated**: the runner that dispatched the benches must not also verdict (generator-never-grades-itself) — ADJUDICATE items surface via the queue + consistency_check, and a session adjudicates with the pitch doc + ledger/edge/pack wiring. A queued name stuck >7d in any stage is a CRITICAL breach (the conveyor's unwired-tripwire analog).
- R1.6 Grading: every starter entry cohort-tagged `small_orphan` in the calibration ledger at entry.

**Latency budget** (index poll → filing extracted → candidate → verified → promoted): 1d → 1d → ≤7d → same-run. Alpha horizon is months; the budget exists to bound drift, not to chase ticks.
**Quality bar**: verification precision > coverage — a name wrongly promoted costs a court; a name delayed a week costs ~nothing. Screen-math regressions are permanent golden tests (KSL/VERK/CARD/Tokmanni).
- R1.7 **Fix-verified-e2e (added 2026-08-06, user-directed)**: a screen defect is NOT fixed until the screen has re-run END-TO-END with the fix and the caught names' corrected values are verified in the fresh output. `fix_landed` without an `e2e_verified` stamp (run date + the caught-name assertions) is an open defect for every purpose — the invariant flags it, the monitor emails it. Golden tests are necessary but not sufficient: they prove the guard's math, not that the guard is wired into the run that actually feeds candidates.
**Success metrics** (reviewed monthly): % WATCH with wired packs = 100%; candidate→verified ≤7d p90; advance rate 30-70% band (outside it, the screen or the catalog is wrong); artifact-AVOID rate trending down as defects are fixed; `small_orphan` cohort return vs SPY-same-window at N≥20 = the validation gate for the whole hypothesis.
**Non-goals**: no sizing beyond starters without a court; no auto-orders; no chasing sub-$40M or no-IBKR-line names (KSL rule).

## Pipeline 2 — Class-dislocation refutability
**Problem.** Narrative repricings hit whole classes indiscriminately (saaspocalypse, muni headlines); dispersion inside the class is the alpha. The desk catches single names daily (`broken_print_radar`, `dislocation_sweep`) but has no class lens, and entries happen only when a court happens to convene. Unlike P1 this is capacity-uncapped and correspondingly more crowded: the edge is verification speed and honesty, not access.
**Job.** When a class de-rates on one narrative, detect it T+0, locate each member on the refutability line (damage-absent / damage-arriving / structural) with a named data test, and put the testable ones in front of a court with their gates pre-wired.

**Functional requirements**
- R2.1 Cadence: **daily, after US close** (matches the existing weekday single-name layers). A class event detected T+0; triage complete ≤48h; packs auto-wired in the same triage run.
- R2.2 Detection: fire on cohort median drawdown ≥ threshold AND low cross-sectional dispersion AND high market-residual (beta-bleed guard); a dated narrative anchor ≤ window start required, else flag UNEXPLAINED (never suppress, never backfill an anchor).
- R2.3 Triage: per member, the bear claim + classification + the **named metric and print date** that would refute/confirm it. No metric+date, no classification — hard validator rule.
- R2.4 Routing: rank refutable/damage-absent first; top-K to the court queue; a resolution pack per named data test regardless of whether a court convenes (the HUBS-Q3 pattern).
- R2.5 Grading: every fired event gets a 90d outcome row (dispersion realized? damage-absent > damage-arriving?) cohort-tagged `class_dislocation`. Two consecutive quarters of no discrimination = kill the detector (MAUDE rule).
- R2.6 Decompose, never discard: a breadth-gate rejection (component > 15% of shaped tape) must run a deterministic autopsy — industry-level pockets face the same excess-vs-sector and liquid-anchor gates and are emitted as candidates. Rejecting the blob as ONE cohort is correct; dropping its members is not (measured cost: FLUT/SRAD/DKNG, defect DISC-BLOB-MISS-FLUT, 2026-08-07). Cross-industry narratives inside the blob = v4 (return-series co-movement).

**Success metrics**: detector precision (real dislocations vs beta selloffs) ≥ 70%; fired-event → court-queue ≤48h p90; the 90d damage-absent-minus-damage-arriving spread positive at N≥10 events; zero events discovered retroactively that the detector should have fired on (audited quarterly against the tape).
**Non-goals**: no intraday leg in v1; no automated sizing (courts own caps); no single-name duplication of what `broken_print_radar` already does.

## Pipeline 3 — Trigger conversion & classification hygiene
**Problem.** Verdicts without wired triggers don't trade (STEM.L: tripwire published in prose, +26.6% uncaught). Unclassified positions make attribution blind ($133k at discovery). Ungraded outcomes make every claim unfalsifiable.
**Job.** Every research state that implies a future decision has a dated, machine-checked trigger; every held dollar has an edge classification; every trigger outcome is graded with a miss taxonomy.

**Functional requirements**
- R3.1 Invariants (enforced by `consistency_check`, CRITICAL on breach): WATCH/STAGE2 ⇒ future-dated pack; held STK ⇒ edge classification or explicit waiver; pack past date ⇒ adjudicated ≤72h; batch-verdict name ⇒ in ledger.
- R3.2 Pack lifecycle: armed → due → adjudicated → branch_executed | expired; the branch decision is recorded even when the action is "did nothing" (feeds the missed-entry ledger).
- R3.3 Miss taxonomy on graded outcomes: `unwired_tripwire | late_adjudication | wrong_branch | execution_gap`. A failure class we don't count is a failure class we keep.
- R3.4 Classification sweep: weekly; backlog burned first (16 names / ~$133k as of 2026-08-06).
- R3.5 Surfacing: packs due ≤7d in the morning brief + positions-board next-date column; CRITICAL invariant breaches push-notify.

**Success metrics**: `unwired_tripwire` misses after wiring = 0 (the STEM class extinct); adjudication latency p90 ≤72h; unclassified held cost → $0 and stays; every monthly attribution runs with zero blind-spot caveats.
**Non-goals**: no auto-execution of branches; grading is empirical (calibration ledger), never self-assessed by the generating pipeline (the generator-never-grades-itself invariant).

## Courts — the pitch-doc requirement (all pipelines)
Every court (full court, court-lite, re-court) MUST produce a **pitch doc** before its verdict persists. The pitch doc is the artifact the red/blue teams argue over and the record a future session re-underwrites from. Four mandatory panes:
- RC.1 **What we believe** — the thesis in plain language, with the specific numbers it stands on and their primary sources.
- RC.2 **What the market believes** — the strongest honest statement of the other side, at current price (not a strawman; the batch-2 verifiers' premise-refutation discipline applies to our own briefs).
- RC.3 **Why we might have edge** — the mechanism (information, analysis, horizon, or capacity) and the evidence it exists HERE; name the edge type (EDGE / RP_FAIR / DILIGENCE). "We read the filing carefully" is not an edge claim unless nobody else does (orphan mechanism, cited).
- RC.4 **Why it might be priced in** — the market-implied scenario at the live price (prob-weighted-FV discipline: implied p vs our p); if our number and the implied number are close, say FAIR and let the verdict reflect it.
Plus: scenario FVs (bear/base/bull + weights), kill triggers, and the entry envelope (size cap, limit band, tranche plan).
- RC.5 **Public pitch PDF (2026-08-06, user-directed; amended same day)**: every courted verdict on an owned (or notably declined) name also produces a PLAIN-LANGUAGE pitch document as PDF (`dd_reports/public/{TICKER}_{date}.pdf`) suitable for posting to public value-investing forums. REQUIRED STRUCTURE — the four panes in plain words ("What we believe" / "What the market believes" / "Why we might have an edge" / "Why it might already be priced in") PLUS a **"What we checked ourselves" verification table** (claim | how we checked | what we found | Confirmed/Refuted) distilled from the bench records — the verification work IS the credibility. No house jargon (no verdict codes, detector/court/bench vocabulary); numbers with sources; the bear case at full strength; position + entry disclosed; not-investment-advice note. Storage: `dd_reports/pitch_{TICKER}_{date}.md`, linked from the edge classification (RC.1/RC.2 map to `edge_explainer.we_believe/market_believes`; RC.3/RC.4 become first-class `edge_claim`/`priced_in` fields). Invariant (enforced by consistency_check): no ledger verdict with `court` set and no pitch doc on file after 2026-08-06.

## Execution staging — the AI queue (doctrine amendment 2026-08-06)
Court-approved entry/exit plans are automatically **staged as order instructions in the IBKR AI queue** (`create_order_instruction`) instead of waiting for a session to hand-stage them. This amends the previous fully-manual staging, and sits BELOW Rung 1 of the execution-autonomy ladder (call it Rung 0.5): the pipelines write instructions; **the principal executes each order in IBKR — the desk never transmits an executable order and never will while the Rung-1 gates (Brier < market at n≥20, TCA ≤ +15bps at n≥10, 4 clean integrity weeks, shadow month) are unmet.**
- RE.1 Source of truth: only court-approved plans (entry_plan / edge-classification envelopes) may stage; no pipeline stages from a screen, detector, or triage output directly. Chain: verdict → pitch doc → envelope → instruction.
- RE.2 Envelope-constrained: side, qty, limit, TIF from the court's plan verbatim; LIMIT orders only (never market); local-currency limits per venue convention (the POLI.TA/pence rails).
- RE.3 Reconciliation: staged instructions carry the plan's provenance (court date, pack id); the positions-board Orders section shows queue state (the STAGED labels become queue-aware); an instruction whose underlying verdict is superseded or killed is auto-withdrawn from the queue same-day.
- RE.4 Integrity: every instruction logged to the shadow/TCA ledger at staging and at execution (fills feed the Rung-1 gate evidence); kill switch = delete-all-instructions, one command, tested.
- RE.5 Nothing in this section overrides a pack branch that says "did nothing" — staging an instruction is a proposal to the principal, and unexecuted instructions expire with their gate, recorded as such.
- RE.6 **Same-session sweep (2026-08-06, after the miss)**: an adjudication that produces an entry envelope is NOT COMPLETE until the staging plan is swept into the AI queue — in the SAME session whenever the claude.ai connector is present (the session doing the adjudication IS the "session lane"). The 24h stuck-plan invariant is the backstop, never the plan.

## Cross-cutting requirements
- RX.1 Stores: schema-validated, merge-only, provenance-stamped writes (architecture S1); a corrupted or partial write must be impossible to mistake for data.
- RX.2 Every run leaves a manifest (S2); yesterday's run is diffable against today's.
- RX.3 Agent steps: versioned in-repo templates, structured outputs, output-side validators, model tier per step (volume=Opus, escalation=Fable), dry-run mode, idempotent by input digest.
- RX.4 Fail LOUD: rate limits, partial crawls, auth failures produce alarms, never silent zeros (the grader-crash and dislocation-sweep lessons).
- RX.5 Everything lands on the existing surfaces: registry cron/launchd, `/health` board, morning brief, dashboard. No new operational surfaces to remember.
- RX.6 Cost envelope: agent-verification spend is batch-gated (P1) and event-gated (P2), never cadence-gated — polling more often must not cost more tokens.

## Explicitly out of scope (all pipelines)
Automated order **execution** (staging to the AI queue is in scope per Execution staging above; transmitting executable orders remains out of scope until the execution-autonomy ladder's Rung-1 gates are met — and then only by explicit doctrine change); automated court verdicts; DB/queue infrastructure; intraday P2; growing the RP_FAIR carry book as an "alpha" product.
