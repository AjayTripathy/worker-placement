# Entry point for agents working on 0DTE

Read [AGENT_HANDOFF.md](AGENT_HANDOFF.md), [EXPERIMENT.md](EXPERIMENT.md), and
[README.md](README.md) before changing this module. They describe the research
questions, runnable commands, source boundaries, and unresolved evidence gaps.

Active experiments hash their source code. Inspect the local manifests and run
the documented preflight before editing frozen files. Preserve old registrations
and observations; do not silently accept a new hash or reconstruct a missed
forecast. A new trial uses a new future registration and directory.

Research is paper only. Changing or launching the live execution rail is a
separate action; successful research tests do not authorize it.

Keep credentials, office attribution, feed excerpts, forecasts, broker data and
local paths out of shared fixtures and documentation. Share source, synthetic
tests and this handoff; review data redistribution rights separately.
