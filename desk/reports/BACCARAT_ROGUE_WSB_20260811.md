# Baccarat Rogue

*The scent lingers after the money's left the room.*

## TL;DR

The world's table-gambling whales spent June–July betting the World Cup on their phones instead of flying to Macau. Every regulator's data shows the same signature: **tables cratered, slots flat, online up** — everywhere on Earth, simultaneously, ending on the exact date of the final. Here's the setup as of today: the sell side is actually constructive on WYNN (Moderate Buy, avg PT ~$134, targets *raised* after Q2) — but the tape sits at ~$105, Morgan Stanley cut the Macau sector to "weak growth through Q4," and the options market prices October at the 0th-percentile IV of the past six months because there's no earnings date in it. The tournament ended July 19. Citi's channel check had Macau daily revenue **+9% week-over-week in that exact week**, and JPM's August checks show the daily rate still climbing (~MOP710M→793M, VIP and mass volumes +10–15% y/y). July's full month still printed −8.4% because a month averages over an inflection — which is why the market's monthly instruments are late and the weekly fingerprint was early. Consensus for August: +1.3% y/y (CLSA). My frozen, pre-registered call: **72% that August beats that bar. Graded September 1 by the DICJ print — that's the gambling number (it publishes on the 1st). No re-strikes.** The confirming read lands ~3 weeks later when DSEC arrivals publish: recovery on *flat* arrivals = the wallet came back, not a tourism wave — and honestly, a gaming-revenue beat *with* an arrivals surge would weaken my case even on a passing grade. Both branches are filed pre-print.

## "Didn't the money just move to Singapore forever, like 2021?"

Checked. If the wallet had *relocated* instead of *paused*, Marina Bay Sands' Q2 (April–June — the exact drain window) would show a surge. LVS's own release: MBS net revenue **flat (−0.6%)**, EBITDA **−10.3%**. Nothing arrived in Singapore. And nothing about Singapore explains Pennsylvania. The 2021 exodus was China destroying the junket credit rails — a scheduled soccer tournament has an end date; the enforcement wave that ran alongside it kept going *after* July 19 while Macau's dailies recovered anyway. You date the cause by what has an ending.

## Why the casinos' own earnings look fine (they're not — and it's not luck)

Casino revenue = volume × hold (the house win rate). The cohort that left is the *low-hold* clientele — big main-bet baccarat at ~1.1% edge, no side bets. Their absence mechanically dressed up everyone's win rates as "growth" while volume quietly fell. Wynn Palace printed 29.7% mass hold vs 22.3% a year ago — I built a 607-quarter hold model from primary filings and that's **+11 sigma above trend**. Cards don't do 11 sigma. A new 100,000 sq ft whale room (Chairman's Club, opened February) does. The Street's own framing of the quarter was "beats on Macau *hold*" — i.e., a low-quality beat, normalized away in their models — which is exactly backwards. 

## Why WYNN and why options

Meanwhile October IV was 30.7% against 34.4% realized — 0th percentile of six months — because October has no earnings date and the overwriters sold it as "quiet."
