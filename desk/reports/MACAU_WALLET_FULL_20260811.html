The Gambling-Wallet Recovery

Full record: thesis · research · courts · position selection — SignalOS desk, 2026-08-11 · principal + desk joint work
The trade in one paragraph. For five weeks this summer the world's table-gambling wallet went to the North-America-hosted World Cup. Macau fell 12% then 8%; Vegas baccarat volume fell 22%; Pennsylvania tables fell 21% — while slot floors everywhere were flat and sportsbooks took a record ~$50bn of tournament bets. The wallet was conserved, not destroyed, and it is measurably returning: Macau's daily run-rate inflected upward the exact week the final was played and has climbed every week since. The market, reading luck-distorted headlines, cut sector estimates as if the diversion were structural. The position: WYNN October 105/115 call spreads bought now at the year's cheapest implied volatility (~0.3% of book at risk), with two court-cleared cash follows pre-committed to later gates (LVS on the Sept-1 print, Galaxy on tonight's dividend gates), and a frozen forecast — p=0.72 that August beats the +1.3% consensus bar — grading our skill September 1 no matter what.

2 · The alternative explanations, ranked and killed

Chill-vs-diversion (the principal's challenge): two discriminators rank diversion above enforcement-chill — the recovery began the week the tournament ended while the crackdown continued (enforcement has no final whistle), and Pennsylvania's tables are beyond the Ministry of Public Security's reach. VIP (most payment-rail-dependent) is recovering in line with mass. Conceded: chill may own part of June's ~7pp overshoot beyond the 3–5pp historical tournament drag; the low +1.3% bar absorbs it. Pent-up-demand ruling (principal): accepted — the one permanent chill in this market (2021's junket abolition) targeted payment rails, and even then demand rerouted (Singapore/Manila boomed) rather than died; today's premium mass is the enforcement-resistant survivor. Rerouting geography (principal's correction, adopted): the rerouted wallet goes to Singapore/Manila — not Las Vegas — so LVS, not WYNN, is the rerouting-insured diversified operator.

3 · The empirical program (built this session)

Market layer: a generalizable official-statistics registry (DICJ GGR, DSEC arrivals + occupancy — primary endpoints cracked, keyless) now computes spend-per-visitor monthly and is wired into every court's prompt; it fired in its first live dispatch (LVS's bench). First insight: Q2 arrivals +4.0% while GGR was flat — people came, the wallet didn't.

Hold decomposition (607 quarters, 18 property series, primary filings): there is NO cohort-wide hold elevation (cohort z +0.10, p=0.73; six of twelve Macau series sit below their own trends). The anomaly is Wynn Palace — +11 luck-sigma, unexplained (not cards; whale concentration, undisclosed mix, or a drop-definition change; question posed to the company) — plus Studio City mildly. What is confirmed: structural hold drift at specific operators (MGM +1.36pp/yr, Galaxy +1.18pp/yr, on-trend in H1; MGM quietly deleted its old 16–22% guidance range; Vegas control +0.36pp/yr). Consequences: court luck-normalizations are conservative floors; per-property trends replace flat norms in every pack; multi-point hold moves are labeled UNEXPLAINED until identified, never "luck." The volume-based thesis never depended on hold.

5 · Edge, and its expiration date

For: (1) decomposition discipline — the desk grades volume and per-property trend where the street grades GGR and adjusted EBITDA; (2) cross-venue wallet conservation — nobody in print triangulates Macau × Vegas baccarat drop × sportsbook handle; (3) weekly resolution — the tournament-close inflection was readable a month before the print. Against: the channel checks are public and syndicated; the names are 10–17% off their lows; June's overshoot leaves room for genuine softness (the MS/Jefferies cuts may be partly right); and — the sharpest risk, the principal's framing — the edge decays every Monday as the weekly checks convert our forecast into consensus. Being right on September 1 is worth nothing bought on August 29. The control group: if the sportsbooks retain the diverted cohort (Flutter Q3 pack armed; Entain reads Wednesday), part of Macau's recovery never arrives — the one scenario that breaks the magnitude, instrumented.
