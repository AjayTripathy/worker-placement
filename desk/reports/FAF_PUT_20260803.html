04

Why the premium exists: the buyer cannot get this anywhere else

  • Spread edge — on a dead chain there is no mid to arbitrage against. We rest at our price; the buyer pays it or there is no trade.
  • Capacity edge — nobody structured for size can profitably quote 2-lot LEAP flow. The trade exists because it is too small to compete for. The aggregation-cost moat, in miniature.
  • Constrained demand — the buyer class is borrow-impaired bears and mandate-bound hedgers. Regulation excludes informed insiders: lockup "swap or other arrangement" clauses and anti-hedging policies bar concentrated holders from buying this protection.
Live corroboration: the same day we sold the 70s at 3.40, someone paid 3.50 for the 65s — a worse strike at ~2× its theoretical value (~1.80). The captivity premium is real, collectible, and currently being paid to whoever shows up to sell.

The fill implies 29.8% volatility against 29.2% realized — at actuarial — but ~$120 below the full captivity reservation, because the ask was inherited from the chain's phantom marks instead of derived from the model.

Post-mortem: the ask was taken from the chain's marks rather than derived — on a dead chain the mark is fiction, and it cost ~$120 of captivity premium against the model's reservation of 4.01. The fix is now standing infrastructure: desk/dead_chain_pricer.py is the mandatory staging step for every future leg — realized vol computed from two years of bars, reservation ask = RV-theoretical + 3–5 pt captivity margin, a per-leg implied-vs-realized gate that refuses any ask below actuarial, and after-tax carry printed against the muni hurdle before anything rests.
