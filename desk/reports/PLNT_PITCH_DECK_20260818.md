# PITCH — PLNT — VERDICT

**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION** — no position today at ~$52.54; the honest valuation is ~10.3× (10.9× counting the tax-receivable obligation), not the 9.6× the original brief claimed, and the reopen bid is $45.75.

---

## What we believe

Planet Fitness is a franchisor whose growth engine visibly broke in the first half of 2026 and whose management response is a marketing fix, not a price cut or a model change. Specifically:

- New member joins fell **36% year over year**, and same-store sales growth decelerated from **+8.2% to +1.7%**.
- Total members still rose, 20.8M in December to 21.5M in June — the base is holding, the *funnel* is what shrank.
- The company kept its full-year revenue guide at roughly **+7%** and raised its earnings-per-share guide from +4% to **+6%** — but the EPS raise is entirely share count. The same release *lowered* adjusted net income guidance from −2% to **−3%**, raised the net interest expense guide to $115.0M, and disclosed $75.0M drawn on the variable funding notes. The company bought back $200.0M / 4.0M shares in the quarter. Fewer shares, less profit, more debt.
- The stock is down **54%** from its 52-week high of $114.26.

Our working belief is that this is a *self-inflicted, reversible* demand air pocket (a price increase on the entry-level membership from $10 to $15, plus a marketing miss) rather than a secular decline in low-cost fitness. **We could not prove that.** The alternative reading — that raising the entry price permanently cut off the low-income joiner who is the whole point of the model — fits every disclosed number equally well.

That is why this is not a buy at today's price. It is a name we want to own at a price where we are paid for not knowing.

## What the market believes

The market believes the same thing we do, and is *more* bullish than we are.

Eighteen analysts cover it. The consensus rating is Buy, the mean target is **$67.48**, and the range is **$49.20 to $106.00**. Consensus models fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS at **$3.64, up 11.45%** from $3.27 in 2026 — i.e., the street explicitly underwrites the re-acceleration we are hypothesizing.

But the *price* does not reflect that. At ~$52.49 the stock trades **$3.29 above the single most bearish target on the street** and **22% below the mean**. That is 14.4× the street's own 2027 number for a franchise business with 90%+ recurring revenue. The sell side's opinion and the tape have come apart. Either the street cuts, or the price moves.

## Why we might have edge

1. **The gap between the tape and the street's own floor.** We are not claiming to know something no analyst knows. We are noting that the market is pricing closer to the bear target than to the consensus, which makes this a gradeable disagreement with an external scoreboard, not a private opinion.
2. **A number nobody published.** Reiterating an unchanged full-year guide after a big first half silently sets a low bar for the second half. Backing it out: first half 2026 revenue was **$702.5M vs $617.5M**, up 13.8%. Full year 2025 was $1,296M; ×1.07 = $1,387M. That implies **second-half 2026 revenue of $684.5M, up 0.9%** against $678.5M last year. Management has already guided the back half to flat and said so nowhere in plain words. Anything better than flat is upside to a bar nobody is looking at; the risk is that even flat is at risk (see below).
3. **Franchisees are still spending.** Equipment revenue in the first half was **$147.7M vs $110.0M, up 34.3%**. Franchisees committing capital is a harder signal than anything management says on a call.
4. **A disclosure discrepancy we found ourselves.** A securities class action, *Matsunaga v. Planet Fitness*, No. 1:26-cv-00576 (D.N.H.), was filed **07/14/2026** — twenty-three days before the 10-Q whose legal note describes the company's litigation as "employment-related claims and slip and fall cases." The case is not named. That is a fact about management's candor, and it is ours before it is the street's.

## Why it might be priced in

Stated plainly, this is the stronger side of the argument today.

1. **It is not cheap.** The brief that opened this file claimed an all-time-low 9.6× EV/EBITDA. That number was wrong in four inputs (detailed in the table below). Rebuilt from the actual balance sheet, enterprise value is **$6,000M = 10.26×** the $584.7M 2026 EBITDA estimate, and **$6,362M = 10.88×** once you include the $361.4M tax-receivable obligation owed to the legacy pre-IPO owners — a contractual cash claim that sits ahead of us.
2. **The dislocation is 42% stale.** The stock is **+41.9%** off its $37.03 low. At that low the same rebuild prints **8.26× / 8.88×**. Whoever bought the reversible-cause thesis has already been paid most of it. We would be buying the last third of a re-rating.
3. **Everyone already agrees with us.** Consensus is Buy with a $67 target and a 2027 acceleration in the model. We are not the contrarian here — our own arithmetic (+6% EBITDA) is *below* the street. Being less bullish than a Buy-rated consensus is not an edge.
4. **The unit guide is at serious risk.** Thirty-eight clubs opened in the first half against a reiterated full-year guide of 180–190. That requires **142–152 openings in the second half — 79% of the year.** If that slips, even the flat revenue bar breaks.
5. **The trade-up story is probably not real.** Black Card penetration reportedly rose 210bps to 68%. Killing the $10 joiner mechanically raises the premium tier's share of joins with zero members actually upgrading. Same number, opposite meaning.
6. **The honesty question is live and unresolved.** Until the litigation note question is settled, we cannot treat this as a fairly-priced risk. We cap what we'll pay at 85% of our fair value estimate and sell no options against it.

## What remains unverified

Stated without softening — this list is longer than the confirmed list.

1. **Whether 10.3× is actually an all-time low.** The single quantified reason this file was opened was never tested by anyone. Correcting today's level does not answer a historical question.
2. **Whether the demand break is self-inflicted or the category's.** The one test designed to separate those — comparing this company's app and location sentiment against a peer around the 2026-05-07 breakpoint — never executed. Everything in the "reversible cause" belief rests on an untested premise.
3. **Whether the deceleration is even as bad as the headline.** Our seasonality check was skipped. Part of the +8.2%→+1.7% move may be lapping last year's price increase rather than new weakness.
4. **Same-tenure churn.** Not disclosed at cohort level. Both the bull's "retention is fine" and the bear's "retention is quietly deteriorating" are unfalsifiable on public data.
5. **Whether the Black Card figure means anything.** Not in the filed exhibit, and join mix by tier is not disclosed.
6. **Whether the second-half opening schedule is achievable.** No club-level pipeline is disclosed by geography, so the 142–152 requirement cannot be tested until it either happens or doesn't. Our building-permit trackers do not apply — franchisees build these on leased retail, so the permits are pulled by parties we cannot identify.
7. **Our own 2027 number.** We do not have one. Every valuation argument above rents the street's $3.64. That is a genuine limit on conviction and the main reason the size cap is small.
8. **Average daily volume and therefore executability.** Broker data was permission-denied to both benches. We do not know what size we can build without moving the tape.
9. **The Q3 print date.**
10. **Whether the litigation-note omission is misconduct or a materiality judgment.** We know the case existed 23 days before the filing and is not named. We do not know why. We are treating it as an unresolved honesty question, which caps price and forbids option selling, and we should be clear that this is a precaution, not a conclusion.

**Bottom line for the adjudicator:** the business case survived. Our own valuation did not — it was off by roughly $715M of enterprise value, and correcting it moves this from "unusually cheap" to "ordinary." Two of our skeptic's three strongest kills also failed on re-check, so the file is alive. It is alive at $45.75, not at $52.54.
