# PITCH — HIVE — PROPOSED VERDICT: **FLAT / NO ENTRY into the Aug-18 earnings print; re-open the question afterward as a financing-structure case — PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION**

*HIVE Digital Technologies Ltd. (NASDAQ: HIVE) — Bitcoin miner pivoting to AI/HPC data centers ("BUZZ HPC"). Tape anchor $2.76–2.84 (Aug 7 close $2.84), 52-week range $1.73–$7.84, market cap ~$764M. No position, no orders. Both benches, and the original thesis, independently reached the same bottom line: stay out ahead of the print.*

## What we believe

HIVE's fiscal Q1 report (expected **August 18, 2026** — corroborated by two aggregators but **not confirmed by the company**, which is itself a warning sign) will be ugly, and the stock is not yet priced for the financing problem that follows it.

Specifically:

- **The quarter being reported had nothing good in it.** Mining gross margin was compressing toward roughly 16–22% on weak bitcoin economics (spot hashprice ~$30.88, forwards ~$30 — confirmed against Hashrate Index). None of the new AI contracts (Cohere, the Boden site, the Toronto-area "GTA" project) generated revenue in the quarter. The adjusted-EBITDA headline is a coin flip on currency moves.
- **There is no cushion left to dress the print up.** HIVE sold down its bitcoin treasury to just **150 BTC (~$10.8M)** by fiscal year-end (it sold 331 BTC during the year), so there is no mark-to-market treasury gain available to flatter the numbers.
- **The real story is the funding gap, not the growth story.** The company's own press release attaches "approximately CAD $3.5 billion" (~US$2.55B) of expected capital investment to the 320 MW GTA project, against roughly **$250M of pro-forma liquidity** ($23.1M year-end cash plus ~$233M of convertible-note proceeds raised in April and June — proceeds already earmarked for GPU purchases to hit an 11,000-GPU / $140M AI-cloud revenue target by year-end 2026). That's roughly a 10:1 gap between the build cost and the money on hand, with the stock at $2.84 versus the $4.83 conversion price on the notes — meaning the cheap-financing window that existed in the spring is shut. Every path to funding GTA from here is either a partner's balance sheet or dilution at depressed prices.
- **The company has gone quiet.** Its last press release was June 30; the monthly production updates it used to publish have stopped for all of 2026. Last year it pre-announced its results date in May. Silence from a company that used to over-communicate is signal.

## What the market believes

The story is fully known. Eight analysts cover HIVE at a consensus **Strong Buy with an average price target of $7.06** — 148% above the last close. The bull case (bitcoin miner converts cheap power into AI data-center revenue, $660M annual recurring revenue pathway by end of 2028) is the *consensus* case, not a discovery. Yet the stock trades at $2.84, down 65% from its 52-week high, having round-tripped from ~$4.90 in April to a July low around $2.54–2.57. So the market simultaneously believes the story (per the sell side) and doesn't (per the tape). The gap between the two is most plausibly the financing question we want to underwrite — the market is not paying for growth it doesn't believe the company can fund.

## Why we might have edge

- **We did the balance-sheet work the headline numbers hide.** The published fiscal-year-end cash figure is $23.1M — not the ~$48M the original thesis carried, and thin against the capex commitments. Even adding digital currencies ($10.8M) and investments ($9.7M) only gets to $43.6M.
- **We caught the treasury liquidation.** "Bitcoin miner with a BTC war chest" is stale — the war chest is gone (150 BTC). Anyone still modeling treasury support is wrong.
- **We sized the funding gap from primary documents**, including correcting our own red bench's currency error (details below): ~US$2.55B of expected GTA build cost versus ~$250M of liquidity, with the convert conversion price 70% above the stock.
- **We noticed what stopped being said.** The disappearance of monthly production releases and the missing results-date announcement are behavioral evidence that doesn't show up in any model.
- **The re-scoped question is genuinely open.** Whether Bell or a project-finance partner funds the GTA build — versus HIVE issuing equity-linked paper at depressed prices — is adjudicable within months and determines whether the $660M revenue pathway accrues to current shareholders or to new capital.

## Why it might be priced in

Honestly, much of it may be:

- The stock is *already* down 65% from its high and gave back the entire April rally. A 24% gross-margin quarter from a bitcoin miner in a $30-hashprice environment surprises nobody.
- The sell side's $7.06 target coexisting with a $2.84 price means the market has already discounted the growth story heavily — plausibly *for exactly the financing reasons we identified*. Our "edge" may just be arithmetic the market did months ago.
- Fiscal Q4 (already reported) showed the inflection the bulls need: full-year revenue of $297.8M (+158%), full-year adjusted EBITDA of +$72.9M, HPC revenue reaching $4.6M in the quarter with $35M of annualized recurring revenue. If Cohere revenue lands on schedule, the next few prints improve mechanically.
- **We could not check short interest** (a tooling gap this session). If the short side is crowded, a not-terrible print could squeeze, and our tactical "stay flat" would miss it. This is a known blind spot, not a settled question.

## PROPOSED ENTRY BANDS

**The courts say no entry now.** Unanimous FLAT into the August 18 print. These are therefore **reopen bands**, built from the benches' own numbers:

- **No position and no resting orders through August 18.** Nothing about the reported quarter can be good (margin ~16–22%E, zero new-contract revenue, no treasury gains available), and there is no washout price on the tape — July's $2.54–2.57 lows were not a capitulation we can buy against at $2.84.
- **Price-based tactical reopen: a close below ~$2.54** (taking out the July closing lows) after the print would constitute the washout entry the thesis looked for and didn't find. Tactical only, small, and only if the print didn't reveal a new financing action already dilutive at those levels.
- **Fundamental reopen — any ONE of these re-convenes the court as a financing-structure case:**
  - A disclosed **GTA financing structure that is non-dilutive** (Bell-funded build, partner capital, or project finance);
  - The **Q1 filing shows >$200M cash with quarterly capex <$50M** and HPC revenue >$8M for the quarter;
  - **Bitcoin above ~$80k**, re-widening the mining margin that funds everything else.
- **Structural marker: $4.83** (the convertible-note conversion price). Below it, the $245M of 2031 notes behave like straight debt and any new money is dilutive; a durable move above it re-opens the cheap-financing window and materially changes the dilution math. Treat a reclaim of $4.83 as a regime change, not a chase signal.
- **Upside reference, not a target:** consensus sits at $7.06; the April rejection high was ~$4.90.
- **Sizing:** none authorized. The post-print court decides size; the case's court-worthiness was cut from 7/10 to **5/10** and narrowed to the financing question, so any eventual position starts small.
- **Catalyst dates:** earnings **~Aug 18, 2026** (unconfirmed by company); **year-end 2026** checkpoint for 11,000 GPUs / $140M AI-cloud recurring revenue; Cohere go-live timing (undated — itself a gap); GTA financing disclosure (undated, could come any time).
