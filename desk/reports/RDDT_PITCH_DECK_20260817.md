# PITCH — RDDT — PROPOSED VERDICT

**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION: NO ENTRY. Stay flat through 2026-08-21. Reopen only if price falls into $136–$140 or lower AND Reddit publicly discloses the Google licensing renewal (or the Q3 filing shows 2027 committed licensing above $100M).**

---

## What we believe

Reddit is an honest, cash-generating company whose stock has fallen 42% from its 52-week high of $282.95 to $164.43, and the fall does not appear to be caused by anything dishonest in the accounting. Three things support that:

- **The company is not inflating its numbers with stock grants.** Stock-based compensation for the first six months of 2026 was $169.3M, *down* 3% from $174.5M a year earlier, while revenue grew 61%. Most fast-growing internet companies do the opposite.
- **The balance sheet is real.** $2.786B of cash and marketable securities against $20.9M of debt (which is just office leases). Operating cash flow of $574.1M in six months against $2.2M of capital spending.
- **Management is buying its own stock with actual cash.** $239.6M spent on 1,523,502 shares in six months — an average price of about $157 — with $760.4M of authorization left, roughly 2.4% of the company.

What we think we are looking at is a good business that got expensive, then got cheap on the way down, and is currently sitting in between. It is not a bargain, and it is not broken.

## What the market believes

The market believes this is a high-growth advertising company that also owns a scarce pile of human-written text that AI companies must pay for. Thirty-three analysts cover it with an average price target of $215.63 — 31% above today's price. It was added to the S&P 500, announced 2026-08-14 and effective before the open on 2026-08-18, which forces index funds to buy it whether they like it or not.

The market is *not* wrong about the business. What the market is arguably wrong about is the price of the AI-licensing piece — it prices it as a durable annuity, when the filings show it as a business that gets re-negotiated every year.

## Why we might have edge

1. **The AI-licensing revenue is smaller and less locked-in than the story implies.** The "Other revenue" line is about $43M per quarter, or $172M a year. Press reporting says Google pays roughly $60M/yr and OpenAI roughly $70M/yr — about 76% of that line — and both are up for renewal in 2026, with the Google talks publicly described as stalled. So roughly $130M of revenue, at very high margin, is on an undated renewal clock. That is only about 3% of total revenue, but it is a much larger share of the *narrative*.
2. **The tax rate is about to jump, and almost nobody models it.** Reddit paid $3,987K of tax on $256,835K of pretax income last quarter — an effective rate of 1.55%. The company says in its own filing that it may release its valuation allowance within the next twelve months. When that happens the reported tax rate steps up toward roughly 23%, which is about $180M a year of real cash going out the door. Earnings-based valuations that use today's 1.55% rate are overstating the company by a wide margin.
3. **The forced index buying has already happened.** The stock went from $158.12 to $178.09 (+12.6%) on the 8/14 announcement and has since given back to $164.43 — down 7.7% *before* the 8/18 effective date. Our instinct was "don't buy into someone else's forced buying." That instinct was aimed at buying that has already largely occurred.

## Why it might be priced in

- **Thirty-three analysts and index-mandated ownership.** Nothing here is undiscovered. There is no informational advantage in a name this covered; whatever edge exists is in *discipline about price*, not in knowing something others don't.
- **The stock is already 38% off its low of $119.27.** Whoever was going to buy the panic has bought it. We are not early.
- **The licensing risk is public.** The stalled Google talks were reported on 2026-07-23. This is not a secret we found.
- **The real driver is advertising, and we have no independent read on it.** Advertising is 94.7% of revenue and grew 64% last quarter — against a year-ago quarter that itself grew about 78% (Q2'25 $499.6M vs Q2'24 $281.2M — confirmed from the filing data). The company guided Q3 to +47–49%, a 15-point slowdown. That may be perfectly normal as comparisons get harder, or it may be the start of something. **We did not verify which**, because both tools that would have told us failed (see below). That is the single biggest hole in this pitch.
