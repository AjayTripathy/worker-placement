1 · The four-idea frame

Market believes

Appalachian gas is a commodity price-taker with no torque. The Middle East shock came and went, LNG

tightness is a 2028 story someone else will capture at the liquefaction plant, and EQT is a levered bet on

a Henry Hub strip that keeps disappointing. Own the integrateds instead.

We believe

Two claims, and the court separated them deliberately because only one survived.

The claim that DIED — and this is our own former thesis, killed by our own re-court. The

"Hormuz-gap" idea was that a Strait-of-Hormuz disruption would propagate into US gas. It was tested

against the strongest possible stimulus: transits collapsed from 39 to 11 per day, three IRGC strikes hit

Qatari LNG infrastructure, the ceasefire collapsed. Through all of it **EQT went DOWN and set its

52-week low of $47.94 inside the window** — verified on the tape, and re-verified by an independent

spot-check. Front-month Henry Hub sat near $2.89. The conclusion is mechanical and now empirically

retired: the global LNG shock does not reach Appalachian molecules. The MVP/basis choke means the

arbitrage accrues to Gulf Coast liquefaction, not to the producer. That claim is dead and this doc records

it as dead.

The claim that SURVIVED, and it is the whole position. Management has left 2027 almost entirely

unhedged in order to capture LNG and datacenter demand — and the Q2 hedge table proves it. Against a

2027 volume base that annualises near the raised 2026 guide, EQT carries only **387 MMDth of 2027 NYMEX

hedges ≈ 16% of gas volumes**. Better still, the structure of even that 16% preserves upside: it is a

three-way collar — long $3.00 puts, short $2.50 puts, short $4.51 calls — so the hedged sleeve still

participates all the way to $4.51/Dth, and 84% of 2027 volume is naked long the strip. This is a

strip re-rate that grinds over months, and it lives in the shares we already hold regardless of what

instrument we use to add.

The demand side is now contracted, not hypothetical. In this quarter alone EQT signed a **10-year

agreement to supply 325,000 Dth/d to CPV's Shay Energy Center** (a 2-GW plant in Doddridge County, WV) and

a 5-year, 0.5-mtpa LNG offtake SPA with a large Asian integrated beginning 2028, worth ~$45M of 2028

free cash flow at recent strip. That is the datacenter-and-LNG demand thesis converting into signed

paper.

The operational quarter was genuinely good: 634 Bcfe of sales volume above the high end of guidance,

capex 9% below the low end, unit costs at the low end, $330M of free cash flow attributable to EQT,

production guidance raised ~90 Bcfe while full-year capex was cut $25M. The $0.39 adjusted EPS

"miss" was a realised-price effect ($2.65/Mcfe versus $2.81 a year ago), which sits outside the free-cash-

flow and hedge gates this thesis is actually built on.

Why we might have edge

Two answers, and they point in opposite directions — which is exactly why the position is small.

On the existing 95 shares: there is a real, specific, non-consensus claim — that EQT's 2027 optionality

is materially larger than a hedged-producer framing implies, because management deliberately dismantled

the hedge book to capture it. The 16% figure is verifiable from a table most screens never open, and the

three-way structure that preserves upside inside the hedged sleeve is a second-order detail almost nobody

models. That is a structural/neglect edge, and it is genuine.

On any incremental add at $53: there is no edge, and the court said so. Its words: *"The add at

52.50 is therefore a CARRY instrument → the paid version dominates the free one."* The convexity we want

is already owned; buying more shares at the money does not buy more convexity, it buys more beta. That is

why the court replaced the equity rung with a cash-secured put — the paid version of the same exposure.

Carry-vs-edge label on the increment: carry, and not obviously well-paid carry.

Why it may be priced in / why we may be wrong

1. We have already been wrong once on this name, in public, on a testable claim. The Hormuz thesis was

falsified by the market in real time. That should discount our confidence in the remaining claim by

more than it comfortably does — both claims came from the same view of how gas demand transmits.

2. The 2027 upside is a strip bet dressed as a company story. Unhedged means unhedged in both

directions. If Henry Hub sits at $2.75 through 2027, the "convexity" is simply an absence of

protection, and EQT will underperform hedged peers.

3. The re-rate is slow by construction. The court's own words are "a STRIP RE-RATE that GRINDS over

months." There is no dated catalyst. Nothing forces the market to pay for 2027 optionality in 2026.

4. The short put is a real, if small, obligation. At $52.50 strike with the stock at $53.29, it is

near the money into a September expiry. Assignment is the intended outcome, not a failure — but it is

an obligation to buy, and it consumes the ladder capacity the core table wants to use for lot 1.

Mechanism falsifier

Management re-hedging 2027 materially above ~30% — them selling the convexity we hold — kills the

surviving claim outright. Buy to close the put, re-court the equity. Secondary: an FCF or production guide

cut at any 8-K; sustained Henry Hub below $2.40. Explicitly not a falsifier: full Hormuz resolution.

The 2027 story survives resolution — that is precisely what distinguished EQT from the cancelled tanker

rungs, and it is why this position was not adverse-selected by a peace headline.

Edge type

component that is already owned in the existing 95 shares. Sizing must read the first label, not the

Rotation partner

The core table names XOM. This doc dissents.

XOM is the wrong rotation partner for EQT, and the reason is this name's own decisive finding. The

court's central empirical result is that Appalachian gas is decoupled from global oil and LNG shocks —

that is precisely why EQT fell to its 52-week low during the Hormuz escalation while integrateds rallied.

A rotation partner exists to hold the same beta for 31 days. XOM demonstrably does not hold EQT's beta;

using it would leave the book unhedged on the very exposure the harvest is meant to preserve, and it would

do so in the direction the court proved is adverse.

Recommended instead: a second Appalachian dry-gas producer — EXE (Expand Energy) as first choice,

AR (Antero Resources) or RRC (Range Resources) as alternates. All three share EQT's actual risk

factors: Appalachian basis differentials, MVP/egress constraints, Henry Hub strip, the same

datacenter-and-LNG demand narrative and the same weather beta. Not substantially identical: different

issuers, different acreage, materially different hedge books (which is the whole EQT thesis), different

liquids mix and different balance sheets. No cross-ownership. Comfortably outside §1091 — the securities

of two different corporations are not "substantially identical" merely because they are in the same

industry.

Keep XOM as a distant third choice for a partial-beta placeholder only if the principal wants a

mega-cap parking place, and label it as such.

Harvest rule for this name

  • Harvest window: any lot ≥ $1,200 and ≥ 8% below basis. Against the $52.51 basis that is

~$48.30 — essentially the 52-week low, so this is a live and reachable trigger.

  • Mechanics: specific-ID lot sale; rotate to EXE (not XOM) for the 31-day window; re-enter EQT on

day 32.

  • The option leg is the constraint, and it is a hard one. Selling the 95 shares while short the

$52.50 put converts a covered-ish position into a naked short put. Under §1091 the loss on the stock

is disallowed if a substantially identical security — including an option to acquire it — is bought

within the window; a written put is not a purchase, so it does not itself trigger the wash sale, but

assignment inside the 61-day window does, and it would disallow the harvested loss entirely.

Rule: do not harvest EQT shares while the Sep-18 put is open. Either close the put first or wait

until after expiry. This is the single most likely way to accidentally void a harvest in this book, and

it is worth carrying as a general rule for any name carrying a short put.

  • Volatility makes this a good TLH name once the option is out of the way: a 52-week range of

$47.94–$68.21 is a 42% spread on a mega-liquid stock — frequent loss lots, cheap round trips.

  • Cross-book check: EQT is on the core table's wash-sale coordination list — the options overlay

is named there explicitly. The court also directed that **EQT be added to the Parametric restriction

list**; verify that this was actually done before any harvest, because an unrestricted Parametric buy

inside the window would void the loss without anyone noticing.

One honest counter-argument, recorded against my own conclusion. The court's ≤190-share cap was set

would give ~1,140 shares (~$61k). That is a coherent position, and if the principal takes it, **$61k is

the number, not $90k** — and it still needs the court to bless the instrument question, because the

court's objection was to at-the-money equity as an instrument, not only to the share count.
