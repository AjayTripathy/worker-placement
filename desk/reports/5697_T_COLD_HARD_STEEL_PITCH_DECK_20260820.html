The business behind the net-net — real industrial moat, not paper assets

  • Microcap Illiquidity: At $48.4k ADV, a full position size would require 10+ trading days to exit in a crisis. Capping the sleeve at 0.50% ($5k starter / $10k max) guarantees full liquidity within 1–2 days.
  • PFIC §1297(e) Sensitivity: At 47.18% passive assets, a 9.7% drop in share price pushes the passive ratio to the 50.0% cliff. Sizing modestly ensures minimal taxable exposure.
  • Automotive Cyclicality: Sanyu operates with a thin 6.0% operating margin. While the ¥4.02B cash fortress eliminates solvency risk, cyclical steel downturns can compress earnings.
  • Dead-Money Horizon: Capital allocation reforms in Japan take 12–24 months to materialize. We are paid 3.8% carry to wait.
