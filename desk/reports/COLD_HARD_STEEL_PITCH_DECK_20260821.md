## 04 · The clock — the TSE reform regime, read precisely

**What is real:** the exchange's capital-efficiency campaign (disclose your plan to fix sub-1×
book, or explain), and a genuine cultural break in capital return — the precedents are all
verified events: **Citizen** cancelled 25.6% of its shares (+42%), **Dai Nippon Printing** ran a
record ¥300B buyback, **Taisei Oncho's** management took it private at a premium, and
**Dai-ichi Life's** unsolicited topping bid for Benefit One was accepted — hostile offers now
win in Japan.

**What is not real:** a forcing function on this cohort's timetable. The pressure is strongest
on Prime (7239), advisory on Standard, and the precedents skew large-cap. For the microcaps the
catalyst arrives when a family, parent, or activist decides — which is why the position is
carried at starter size on a dividend, not levered to a date.

## 07 · Execution — chunked, tape-first, one decision a day

Each envelope places **one DAY-order chunk per Tokyo session**: 1–2 board lots, at most ~2% of
the name's 20-day median volume, limit at the lower of tape and prior close, capped by the
ceiling. Orders display one lot. Nothing rests overnight; nothing rests into prints. A failed or
cancelled placement releases the day and reports the broker's own reason. The whole cohort fills
over roughly two to four weeks — deliberately: in books this thin, **patience is the execution
edge**, and the same discipline that builds the position is what will someday exit it.

## 08 · Why only starter size — the honest slide

1. **The catalyst is unowned.** No date forces a re-rating on the Standard-market names. The
   dead-money branch is real and can run years; the dividend and the cash floor are what make
   that branch survivable, and at 0.25–0.75% per name it is survivable many times over.
2. **Carry is modest, not the thesis.** Weighted ~2.7–3.5% on the sized names. This cohort pays
   you to wait; it does not pay you well.
3. **JPY is unhedged.** A 10% currency move swamps a year of carry in either direction.
4. **Liquidity is symmetrical.** Entry discipline is exit discipline; the thin names are sized
   so the exit is measured in days, not months.
5. **One inherited position is not part of this pitch:** 9713 Royal Hotel (800 sh) entered the
   book outside the desk's process and is being resolved on its own gates (share-count
   reconciliation; capex-funding verification). It is excluded from this cohort's math.

## 10 · What this cohort reliably delivers — and what it doesn't

**Reliably:** a bounded-downside book of audited cash and running factories, bought below
liquidation marks, paying a modest verified dividend, executed at a pace the order books never
notice — with every gate in code and every claim tied to a primary document.

**Not reliably:** a date. The re-rating arrives when Tokyo's slowest-moving owners decide, or
when the exchange's polite pressure finally compounds. We sized for the honest version: cheap
enough that being early is fine, small enough that being wrong is cheap, and instrumented enough
that whichever way it breaks, we hear it the same day.
