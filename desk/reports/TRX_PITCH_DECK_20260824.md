# PITCH — TRX — VERDICT

**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION** — recommend **no position, no ladder, no resting orders** in TRX Gold Corp at $1.21: both benches killed the thesis on the same arithmetic (the company reports 100% of a mine it owns 55% of), and the corrected price is ~11.5x attributable EBITDA and ~34x attributable earnings *at a record gold price*, not the 5–6x the original brief claimed.

---

## What we believe

We believed, going in, that TRX was a screen-error trade: stock screeners showed it at about 13x earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization (EBITDA), while the company's own press release showed $66.8M of EBITDA over the last twelve months against a ~$396M market value — roughly 6x. Buy the gap, wait for the screens to catch up.

After the adversarial review, **we believe the opposite of the brief that started this.** Specifically:

1. **The gap is arithmetic, not opportunity.** TRX owns 55% of the Buckreef mine; the Tanzanian state mining company STAMICO owns 45%. Accounting rules make TRX report 100% of Buckreef's revenue and EBITDA, then subtract the partner's share further down. The headline $66.8M EBITDA is the whole mine. TRX's share is roughly $31–33M after also carrying 100% of head-office costs at the parent. Against an enterprise value of ~$371M, that is **~11.5x**, not 6x. The screen's 13.1x is wrong for a different reason (it adds the partner's book equity to enterprise value), but it lands within about 12% of the truth. There is no hidden cheap business.

2. **The share of profit that actually reaches TRX shareholders is worse than 55%.** In the cleanest quarter available — Q3 FY26, after the warrants were gone and no derivative noise — consolidated net income was $8,371k, of which **$5,532k went to the 45% partner and $2,839k to TRX shareholders**. The partner took 66% of the profit on a 45% stake, because all corporate overhead and exploration spending sits at the parent. Annualize the $2,839k and TRX shareholders are earning ~$11.4M a year on a $396M company — **about 34x earnings, in a quarter where the company realized a record $4,703 per ounce of gold.**

3. **Free cash flow is negative and the tax bill has not started.** Nine months: operating cash flow $20,493k, capital spending $23,013k, **cash taxes paid $0**. Free cash flow is roughly −$2.5M. The gap between EBITDA and cash is not tax — it is a growing ore stockpile ($9,088k → $13,348k). Tanzania's 30% corporate tax is ahead of the run-rate, not behind it.

4. **The expansion is real and is not funded.** Management is building a 3,500 tonne-per-day SAG mill circuit *plus* upgrading the existing 2,000 tpd plant — genuinely beyond the 3,000 tpd contemplated in last year's preliminary economic assessment. The warrants that funded the last $19.6M are all exercised and gone. The disclosed bank facility is **$5.0M with $2.9M drawn — about $2.1M undrawn**, against a program the company has described (in a transcript, not in a filing) as $45–50M. The funding for the growth story does not currently exist.

Primary source for the above, and the document we defer to over any transcript or secondary summary: [TRX Gold Q3 FY26 6-K, Exhibit 99.1 and financial statements](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1173643/000117184326004688/exh_991.htm).

## What the market believes

The market is close to right, which is the uncomfortable part.

- **Price and history.** $1.209 today. Down 56.8% from a $2.80 52-week high, but **up 256% from a $0.34 low**. The 26-week high is $1.97. This is not a forgotten stock; it is a stock that already tripled and then gave half of it back.
- **What the price implies.** $396M market value, $371M enterprise value, ~11.5x TRX's share of EBITDA, ~23x TRX's share of operating cash flow, ~34x TRX's share of earnings at record gold prices. That is a full-to-fair price for a growing single-asset producer — not a distressed one.
- **The analysts.** Coverage is thin and the published targets disagree by venue: stockanalysis.com shows a $2.36 average and a "Strong Buy"; MarketBeat shows **$1.70 average across 2 analysts, range $1.10–$2.30**. Both are up from here, and both are essentially a gold-price call.
- **The number the analysts actually underwrite.** FY26 earnings per share of **$0.06 (Zacks) to $0.08 (stockanalysis consensus)** — an *after-partner* figure. Our own bottom-up work lands at $0.044 for nine months, annualizing to about $0.06. **We land on the low end of the consensus range, with no mechanism to explain a gap.** When you and the street agree on the load-bearing number, you do not have a variant view.

## Why we might have edge

Stated honestly — these are the residual candidates, and none of them is enough.

1. **The 66%-to-the-partner allocation is genuinely unpublished.** We could not find a source that applies the 45% partner interest to the multiple. The most-followed screen does the *opposite* — it adds the partner's equity to enterprise value, which inflates the multiple for the wrong reason and happens to land near the right answer. So the finding is real and differentiated. **But it is an edge that says "avoid," not "buy."** The trade it implies is a short, and a short is disqualified here (small-cap gold junior, momentum-prone, borrow unknown, and the thesis would be a bet against the gold price, which we have no view on).
2. **Thin coverage.** Two to four analysts. Genuinely under-followed names sometimes misprice. But under-followed cuts both ways, and here it has not produced a discount — it has produced a full price with a wide target range.
3. **The throughput is bigger than the study everyone values.** This is the one real upside asymmetry. The published preliminary economic assessment contemplates 3,000 tpd. The company's own July release says 3,500 tpd SAG *plus* an upgraded 2,000 tpd plant, "well beyond" the study. Implied recovered grade in Q3 is about 1.26 g/t. At 5,500 tpd for 350 days, that is roughly **77,000 ounces a year, not the 60,000 the study implies.** If the expansion happens as described *and* is funded without dilution, the value at today's gold price is around $1.71/share (+41%). **That is a real number and it is why this deck is a "no" rather than a "never."** It is also entirely unverified against a study document we have not seen, and it assumes free money.

## Why it might be priced in

- The 55/45 joint venture is disclosed in every company presentation. The *fact* is consensus; only its application to the multiple is not.
- Analyst EPS of $0.06–$0.08 already reflects the partner's share. The street is modelling the after-partner business correctly.
- The expansion has been announced, with a letter of award and a 12–18 month timeline in the release. The market has had it since July 15.
- The +256% move off the low says the gold-leverage story was bought, not missed.
- The company's own summary of the last twelve months — $46.4M of "investments at Buckreef" — is public and prominent. That the $46.4M bundles $19.3M of working-capital stockpile build with $27.1M of actual capital assets is a presentation issue, not a secret.
- **The upside case is a gold-price case.** At $3,500/oz gold, the fully-funded, no-dilution version of this company is worth about $1.13/share — *below today's price*. The funded prize only clears +50% above roughly $4,700/oz. Financing was never the binding constraint on the upside; the gold price is. Anyone who wants that exposure can buy it more cleanly.

## What could go wrong

This is a pre-mortem for the **reopen entry** described below, not for a position we hold today (we hold none). Three failure narratives, worst first.

**Failure 1 — We buy the flush and the flush is information, not noise.** Our reopen price band ($0.55–0.70) is 42–55% below today. Realistically, the only thing that takes a levered single-asset gold producer down 50% is a gold drawdown, an operational failure at Buckreef, or a dilutive raise. In two of those three cases the price band is not a discount — it is the market correctly repricing a permanently smaller business. We fill our ladder, gold keeps falling, and the $3,500-gold deck value of $1.13 becomes the *ceiling* rather than the floor. **Tripwire: yes** — gold below $3,500/oz is a hard disqualifier, and any raise filing (F-3, 424B5, F-10, or a 6-K announcing an offering) cancels the ladder.

**Failure 2 — The funding gap closes by issuing stock, repeatedly.** The company needs roughly $45–50M for the expansion (a transcript number, not a filed one), holds $26.8M of cash, generates negative free cash flow, and has ~$2.1M of undrawn bank facility. Warrants — which supplied $19.6M and 15% share growth in nine months — are extinguished. The base case is an equity raise. Every 15% of dilution takes ~15% off the attributable-per-share math, and it stacks. **Tripwire: yes** — any registration or offering filing. But note the tripwire fires *after* the announcement, and small-cap raises are typically announced overnight at a discount, so the tripwire limits the second loss, not the first.

**Failure 3 — Tanzania, and there is no tripwire for it.** TRX's 55% share of the mine is a contract with a state-owned partner (STAMICO) inside a jurisdiction whose mining code, royalty regime, export rules, local-content requirements and permitting sit with a government that is also our counterparty. A change to any of them — or a partner decision to reinterpret the waterfall on parent advances, or a local dispute at the mine gate — reaches us **only when the company chooses to file a 6-K about it, which is after the fact.** There is no docket to watch, no scheduled disclosure, no regulator publication we can subscribe to, no third-party data feed. **Unknown-unknown class: no-disclosure-plane counterparty-sovereign risk** — a risk whose native disclosure surface is a foreign government's internal decision-making, which publishes nothing on a schedule we can monitor. A second facet sits in the same class: **single-asset operational blindness.** One pit, one mill. A wall failure, a mill outage, a grade reconciliation miss — as a foreign private issuer filing 6-Ks quarterly rather than 10-Qs, we may not learn for up to three months, and the tape will know before we do. Both facets are *unmonitorable by construction*, not by our neglect, and the honest mitigation is size, not sensors.

**The reflexive and structural leg.**
- **We do not know how big we are relative to this stock.** The liquidity check failed — the broker contract search was permission-denied, so we have no live quote and **no average daily volume figure at all.** For a $1.21 stock with a sub-$400M market value on NYSE American, a 0.5–1.0% book position is plausibly multiple days of volume to build and more to exit. We are proposing a reopen band without knowing whether we can get out of it. That is a stated gap, not an assumption.
- **Our own reopen band bids into our own predicted flow.** We have written down that the price only reaches $0.55–0.70 in a gold flush. In a gold flush, every levered gold junior is being sold by the same holders for the same reason, liquidity in exactly this kind of name evaporates, and our resting ladder is the bid that gets filled first and worst. Our standing rule is not to place a ladder into flow we ourselves forecast. The reopen band therefore must be *manually re-courted when hit*, never left resting.
- **Doctrine interaction — this would be gold beta wearing a stock-picking costume.** The upside case resolves almost entirely to the gold price, on a company where we receive 55% of the mine and 34x earnings for the privilege. If we want gold, we should own gold or a funded, wholly-owned producer. Adding TRX would mean paying single-asset, minority-earnings, sovereign-jurisdiction risk to obtain exposure we can buy cleanly elsewhere.
- **Doctrine interaction — the "flat needs a named kill" rule is satisfied.** We do not go flat on vibes. The named kill is the consolidation arithmetic, confirmed to the dollar by both benches against the company's own financial statements. This is not a silence being graded as a rejection.

**If this position loses money, the most likely reason will be ___** that we bought a gold-price drawdown thinking it was a valuation discount, and then got diluted at the bottom by the equity raise the expansion always required.

## PROPOSED ENTRY BANDS

**Both benches say no entry. There is no immediate buy, no starter, no ladder, and no resting order.** What follows are the reopen conditions.

**Reopen path A — price.** Re-court on a print of:
- **$0.70 or below** — this is 6x our share of EBITDA ($32M → $192M enterprise value, +$21.4M net cash → ~$213M market value → $0.65/share; band top set at $0.70 for slippage).
- **$0.52 or below** — 15x our share of earnings ($11.4M × 15 = $171M → $0.52/share).

That band is 42–57% below today, which is the honest statement that **price alone is not a realistic reopen path.** Critically: **do not leave a resting order at these levels.** They are only reached in a gold flush, which is the exact condition under which the fill is adverse selection and the exit disappears. If hit, re-court manually within one session.

**Reopen path B — a filed restructuring of the joint venture.** A 6-K that *executes* (not discusses) a move toward an 80/20 or 84/16 non-dilutable-government framework re-rates our share of EBITDA by roughly 45% overnight. **Re-court immediately at any price up to $1.75.** Transcript language does not count — and note that the claim this is "advanced" is currently unsourced.

**Reopen path C — the FY26 annual report (Form 40-F, expected ~2026-12-01).** Re-court if it shows **all** of: operating cash flow ≥$45M; the ore stockpile *converted*, not grown; cash taxes still nil under a confirmed ruling rather than a timing accident; and a customer-concentration note naming the doré offtake counterparty and refinery.

**Reopen path D — the expansion study.** Publication of the 3,500 tpd SAG + upgraded 2,000 tpd study (or an updated economic assessment) confirming **≥90,000 oz steady-state with a funded, non-dilutive capital path.** Even then, the defending bench's own table says a +50% return needs gold above ~$4,700/oz.

**Sizing, if and only if a path reopens and survives a fresh court:** starter of **0.5% of the book, maximum** — half on the trigger, the second half only after a successful live volume pull. **Hard veto on any order at any size until the broker liquidity check succeeds and we can state days-of-average-volume.**

**Hard disqualifiers that cancel all four paths:** (i) any equity or convertible raise filing; (ii) gold below $3,500/oz — at which the fully-funded, undiluted deck is worth ~$1.13/share, below today's price.

## What remains unverified

1. **Tradability.** No live quote, no average daily volume, no bid/ask, no borrow. The broker check was permission-denied. We cannot size this position responsibly today.
2. **The $45–50M capital budget.** Transcript and secondary only; absent from the vetted filing, which gives only a letter of award and a 12–18 month completion estimate.
3. **Post-expansion production.** The 77,000 oz figure is derived from an implied Q3 recovered grade of ~1.26 g/t extrapolated to 5,500 tpd. **No study document has been read.** The 60,000 oz alternative comes from the older 3,000 tpd assessment. The truth could be either, or neither.
4. **Whether joint-venture restructuring talks exist at all.** The prosecuting bench's sole reopen trigger cited "advanced talks"; the defending bench found no primary or secondary source for it in 2026.
5. **The gold doré buyer.** Neither the offtake counterparty nor the refinery is named in anything we have read. Customs shipping data cannot check this — doré exported to a non-US refiner never appears in US bill-of-lading records. The FY26 40-F customer-concentration note is the only instrument that resolves it.
6. **Tanzanian tax status.** Zero cash tax paid on nine months is a fact; *why* is not established. We do not know whether it is a loss-carryforward shield, a negotiated ruling, or a timing artifact — and the three have very different forward implications.
7. **Spot gold today.** The benches used $4,400/oz for the "spot" scenario; the company realized $4,703/oz in Q3. We did not independently verify the current price, and every scenario in this deck is sensitive to it.
8. **Buckreef's actual location coordinates.** Only the Oakville, Ontario head office is geocoded in our data. Any satellite or environmental check would image the wrong asset. Not load-bearing for this thesis, but it means a whole class of physical verification is closed to us.
9. **The next results date.** Vendor-derived. No company statement confirms 2026-11-30.
