What we believe

  2. AI Server Liquid-Cooling Growth Vector: High-TDP AI server racks utilizing direct-to-chip liquid cooling require fail-safe, sub-millimeter thermal monitoring. SEMITEC’s thin-film sensors are being adopted across AI server architectures, providing a high-margin growth vector uncorrelated with consumer electronics.

  1. Auto EV Lumpiness & Slowdown: The market views SEMITEC as a cyclical automotive parts supplier vulnerable to global EV battery management system (BMS) adoption lumpiness and OEM cost-down pressure.
  2. Chinese Domestic Substitution: Concerns that domestic Chinese sensor manufacturers will commoditize lower-tier thermistors in the Chinese auto supply chain.
  3. Capital Allocation Lethargy: The company has historically hoarded excess cash on the balance sheet without a permanent, formulaic capital return policy.

Why we might have edge

  • De-averaging Medical/AI from Legacy Auto: The market prices SEMITEC as a generic auto-parts cyclical at ~5x EV/EBIT. In reality, the high-margin, FDA-regulated medical sensor business plus the emerging AI liquid-cooling vector provide >60% of earnings durability.
