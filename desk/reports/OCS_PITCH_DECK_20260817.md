# PITCH — OCS — VERDICT

**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION** — No position, no entry at $13.14: both benches land on "fairly priced, not mispriced" — buy interest only reopens at $9.50 or below, and the short is refused.

---

## What we believe

Oculis is a Swiss eye-drug developer, no approved products, $13.14 a share, down 62% from its 52-week high and 25% off the low. It is well funded (CHF 228.3M cash, no debt, an undrawn CHF 100M facility, spending into the second half of 2029), so this is not a solvency story. It is a story about one trial.

That trial, PREDICT-1, tests licaminlimab in dry eye disease, but only in patients carrying a particular genetic variant. Here is the thing we believe and think the market has not done the arithmetic on: **the trial is probably too small to succeed on the effect the company has already demonstrated.** The earlier Phase 2 study measured the same endpoint — patient-reported eye discomfort at Day 29 — across 131 patients and got a treatment difference of −4.3 (90% confidence interval −7.7 to −0.8, p=0.041). Back out the standard error from that interval and you get roughly 2.10. PREDICT-1 will analyze about 107 genetically-selected patients, roughly 53 per arm, which pushes the standard error to about 2.32 and means it needs an effect of about **4.6 or better** to hit statistical significance. That is *larger* than the effect the parent trial actually found. So the trial only works if selecting for the genetic variant amplifies the drug's effect — and the only estimate of that amplification, the "five- to seven-fold more pronounced response" the company markets, comes from four treated patients in a paper that has since been retracted.

Second: even a win is not a marketing application. It is a single Phase 2b/3 with a symptom-only primary endpoint, in a disease where the FDA has historically wanted both a symptom and a physical sign, and it requires a companion genetic test to be approved alongside the drug.

Third: if it does win, the company is likely to sell stock into the rally. It sold 2.25M shares for $61.3M at about $27.20 in the weeks before its last trial failure. The shelf registration is live, an S-8 was filed on 2026-08-06, and the board holds authority for 31.0M more ordinary shares plus 12.7M conditional shares against 61.7M outstanding.

And yet — after correcting our own bench's arithmetic — fair value comes out around **$10–13**, against a $13.14 tape. That is not a short. It is a decline to play.

## What the market believes

Ten sell-side analysts rate it Strong Buy with price targets from $20.02 to $44.79, average $35.98. The stock trades 34% below the *lowest* of those targets, which means the sell side has not marked down for the May 29 DIAMOND failure and the tape has. The market's own implied view, read off price: at $13.14 with roughly 63.8M fully-issued shares and $4.42/share of cash, the equity is paying somewhere around $280–450M for the pipeline, depending on how much credit you give the second asset. Read one way, that is a near-certain approval on a $400–450M-peak-sales drug. Read the other way, it is roughly a 45% chance of approval on a $300M-peak drug plus $150–250M for privosegtor. Both readings are defensible, which is itself the answer.

## Why we might have edge

- **The power arithmetic above.** Neither the sell side nor, as far as we can see, the promotional materials, address that enrichment shrank the sample below the parent trial's while keeping the same endpoint. Enrichment quietly converted a replication study into a bet on an unreplicated multiplier.
- **The strong number lives only in the promotional venue.** The 5–7× genotype amplification appears in the press release announcing PREDICT-1; the peer-reviewed paper it traces to has been retracted, and that paper's own text called the analysis "exploratory rather than the primary objective."
- **The follow-on trial could not have replicated it.** RELIEF (n=122) measured physical signs — corneal staining, Schirmer's — not symptoms. Its genotype subgroup was 23 patients on inferior corneal staining, difference −0.59 (95% CI −1.165 to −0.017). So the symptom effect in genotype-positive patients has been observed once, ever.
- **Timing is likely later than guided.** First patient randomized 2026-04-16; over 45% of 160 randomized as of 2026-08-06, about 4.5 patients a week. The remaining ~88 take roughly 19 more weeks, putting last-patient-in near end-December, plus a 29-day treatment period and database lock — realistically topline in Q1–Q2 2027, not "around year-end."
- **A win is a financing event.** Revealed preference says the upside gets harvested by the issuer.

## Why it might be priced in

Symmetry matters here, and our red bench got this backwards, so let us be plain:

- **The endpoint is not fake.** PREDICT-1's exact primary endpoint was the parent Phase 2's own primary endpoint, it was met in the full population, and that publication has not been retracted. Only the genotype multiplier is the shaky number.
- **Nothing was hidden in RELIEF.** It pre-specified no symptom outcome at all. It was designed as a signs trial. There is no suppressed result — the absence of concealment is itself a finding, and it cuts against us.
- **The second asset is real and we were pricing it at zero.** Privosegtor holds FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation and EMA PRIME in acute optic neuritis, an indication with no approved therapy. Give it $150–250M and the implied value of licaminlimab drops to roughly $280–380M — about a 45% chance of approval on a $300M-peak drug. That is fair pay for the risk, not overpay.
- **The stock has already fallen 62%.** The de-rating we would be forecasting has largely happened.
- **The downside is bounded.** $4.42–$4.57/share of cash under the price.

**Reopen to buy — evidence, at any price:**
- The RELIEF genotype subgroup's Day-29 *symptom* result disclosed with sample size and confidence interval — that would be the first independent replication on the endpoint that decides approval.

**Do not buy a positive topline print.** Per the financing pattern above, wait for the equity offering to clear before pricing the win. If we ever own it, the reference trim levels from the corrected table are **$16.14** (the price implied by a $300M-peak approval) and **$19.71** (+50% from here); the hard floor under any position is the **$4.42–$4.57/share** of cash.
