# PITCH — AAUC — VERDICT

**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION**

*Allied Gold Corporation (TSX/NYSE: AAUC) — Canadian-domiciled gold producer, assets in Mali, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia. Tape $23.58 (2026-08-22). No position, no orders.*

---

## What we believe

Allied is three months away from turning its biggest capital project — the Kurmuk mine in Ethiopia — from a hole in the ground into cash flow. The mine is more than 90% committed and paid for, $167.7M of construction spending went out in the first half of 2026 alone, and the company has told the market twice that operations start in August with first gold weeks later. Company guidance puts Kurmuk at 240,000–270,000 oz in 2027 (the high end above what it had previously said) and ~300,000 oz in 2028, inside a company-wide 640,000–680,000 oz in 2027.

That volume step arrives just as two things that have been suppressing the company's realized gold price go away. Allied has a hedge (a "collar") on 60,000 oz that caps its selling price at $3,125/oz while spot gold is $4,590. That collar, plus its streaming and prepayment obligations, cost the company **$411/oz of realized price in Q2 and $532/oz in the first half**. The collar settles by the end of 2026. The gold prepayments are 12-month contracts that run out during 2027. So 2027 gets more ounces *and* a materially higher price per ounce on the ounces it keeps.

Run the numbers with the corrected share count (145.5M fully diluted, including the deep in-the-money convertible) and the corrected enterprise value (~$3.09B), and the stock needs gold to hold roughly **$3,500–3,550/oz** to justify a 50% gain from here. Spot is $4,590. That is a ~23% cushion.

We believe this is a fairly-priced, gold-price-dependent position with a real near-term operational catalyst, and it is worth owning small.

## What the market believes

The market believes this is a broken-deal orphan. Zijin Mining agreed to buy the whole company for C$5.5B (C$44/share), the deal died in July on cross-border regulatory grounds, and the merger-arbitrage holders who owned it for the deal have been leaving. The 52-week high of $32.13 **is the dead deal price** — so the stock's "28% drawdown" is measuring distance from a price that no longer exists.

The market also appears to believe the mine is on schedule and costs are fine, because the company's own press release describes second-quarter all-in sustaining costs as "in line." They are not in line, and that is the crux of the disagreement (below).

We could not retrieve a single published sell-side 2027 production or free-cash-flow estimate. **We are reporting that as a coverage gap, not skipping it.** It means we do not actually have an outside number to disagree with — which is itself a reason to size small, and a reason not to claim the name is "efficiently priced."

## Why we might have edge

1. **The cost problem is hiding in plain sight, and it cuts against us — which is why finding it matters.** The company guided 2026 costs at **$1,750–$1,900/oz** on a consolidated mine-site basis, assuming $4,250 gold. It reported **$2,192/oz in Q2 and $2,229/oz in the first half**, on the same basis, reconciled in the same document, with only $20/oz attributed to the higher gold price. That is 17–27% over the top of guidance. Anyone modelling 2027 margins off the guidance range is modelling a number the company has missed for two straight quarters. We are not.

2. **Cost guidance was quietly not restated.** In the August 5 release, *production* guidance was reaffirmed. *Cost* guidance was simply not mentioned. Reaffirming the good half and going silent on the bad half is a disclosure tell, and it says the company knows the range is at risk.

3. **The schedule slipped and almost nobody has marked it.** Wheaton Precious Metals — which vetted the project as a counterparty before writing a stream cheque — said in [its December 2024 announcement](https://www.wheatonpm.com/news/news-details/2024/Wheaton-Precious-Metals-Announces-the-Acquisition-of-a-Gold-Stream-from-Allied-Golds-Kurmuk-Project/default.aspx) that Kurmuk production was "expected to begin by the second quarter of 2026." It is now August/September 2026. That is one to two quarters late. The offset our own bench found: the *size* of 2027 output was raised even as the *timing* slipped.

4. **The "informed buyer walked away and re-priced the company lower" story is wrong.** More on this below — it was the single strongest bear argument in our court and it did not survive contact with the primary document.

5. **The revenue that is already sold is smaller and shorter-lived than a balance-sheet glance suggests.** $383.9M of deferred revenue looks alarming next to $626.6M of equity. But the Wheaton stream is capped at 255,000 oz cumulatively over the mine's life, Allied still collects 15% of spot on streamed ounces, and the prepayments are 12-month contracts. 2027 leakage is roughly 60,000 oz of a 640,000–680,000 oz year — about 9% — **and it expires.** It is not a permanent tax on the asset.

## Why it might be priced in

- **The dominant variable is the gold price, not anything we discovered.** At $3,000 gold this equity is worth roughly $14/share — down 40%. The mine, the schedule, the cost overrun and the streams are all second-order next to that. We would be buying a leveraged bet on gold after gold has made an all-time high. Nothing in our work gives us an edge on the gold price.
- **Costs above guidance may already be in the price.** Two quarters of misses are public. A 17–27% overrun is not a secret; it is in the MD&A.
- **We have no external estimate to be different from.** Without a sell-side number, "the market is wrong about 2027" is an assertion we cannot test.
- **Jurisdiction is genuinely hard.** Mali and Ethiopia. The prior-year comparatives show a $42.2M "Settlement of Mali matters." Mali fiscal extraction is real, recurring, and not going away.
- **Three overhangs land in the same window.** The collar expiry, the ~December 10 expiry of the four-month Canadian hold on Zijin's shares, and year-end all sit in December 2026.
- **We could not independently verify the tape.** The broker contract-search tool was permission-denied to both benches. Our price is the data pack's, same-day, not re-verified.
