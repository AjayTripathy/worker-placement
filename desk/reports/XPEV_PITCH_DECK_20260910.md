**ADJUDICATED 2026-09-16 — FLAT into Oct 1 (conviction 7/10).** The court found nothing to own at this price; the reopen gates below are the only path back.

## What we believe

XPeng is neither collapsing nor recovering. It is flat, and flat is not what either the original brief or the red bench described.

The unit line: 8 months of 2026 delivered 243,111 vehicles, **−10.5% year-over-year**, with the damage concentrated in Q1 (62,682, −33.3%). August printed 39,107, +4% YoY, the third straight month of YoY growth — but Jun/Jul/Aug 2025 were the 2025 trough (~34.6k/36.6k/37.7k), so the "growth streak" is a comp artifact. Sept-2025 was 41,581 and Oct-2025 was 42,013. The Q3 guide of 115–121k units means September lands somewhere in 37.9k–43.9k, and the **midpoint prints −1.7% YoY**. The streak mechanically ends on October 1 unless September beats a record.

The profit line: Q2-26 revenue ¥19.74b (+8.0%), but vehicle revenue only ¥17.05b (+1.0%) and **vehicle gross margin 12.1% vs 14.3% a year ago**. Net loss ¥1.34b vs ¥0.48b — near-tripled — on a record 103,295 units. The offset the red bench left out: Q1-26 loss was ¥1.78b, so the loss **narrowed 25% sequentially**. Cash ¥40.48b but **down ¥1.61b in a record quarter**.

The floor: the robotics subsidiary raised >$900M at >$6.3b post-money (IDG-led, Tencent/Alibaba). XPeng retains ~86% ≈ **$5.4b of mark**, plus roughly **$2.4b net cash (unverified — debt line never fetched)** = ~$7.8b, or **$8.14 per ADS**, against a ~$9.96b market cap at $10.40. The entire auto business plus Robotaxi is being valued at **~$2.2b, about 0.22x sales**.

So the belief is narrow and cheap-sounding: *at $10.40 you are paying ~$2.29/ADS for a ~$10b-revenue automaker, because the market refuses to credit an illiquid private mark.* That is a real asymmetry. It is not yet an entry, because the two variables that decide whether the auto stub is worth $2b or $0 — September units and Q3 vehicle margin — both print after today, and both benches converged on FLAT.

## What the market believes

Consensus (31 analysts, post-print) sits at **FY26 revenue CN¥86.8b**, cut from ¥92.5–94.1b. Do the bridge: H1 was ¥32.77b, Q3 guide midpoint is ¥22.55b, which forces **Q4 to ¥31.5b ≈ 165k units** against an all-time record of 116,249. That is a +40% sequential quarter no monthly datapoint supports. Our number is ¥80–82b (−6% to −8%).

At the same time, the market is not paying for the robotics mark at all, and it is short ~36–47M ADS (unreconciled between sources) at 2.56 days-to-cover on 14.2M ADV. The tape is at a fresh 52-week low, −63% from $28.23. So the market simultaneously believes (a) revenue estimates that are too high, and (b) an equity value below the sum of the disclosed parts. Those beliefs are inconsistent, which is usually where money is, and usually also where a value trap is.

## Why we might have edge

1. **The Q4 bridge.** The ¥86.8b consensus is arithmetically dependent on a quarter 42% above the year-ago comp. That is checkable from published monthly counts. We think consensus grinds down another 6–8%.
2. **The comp calendar.** Neither the sell-side headline nor the red bench ran the seasonality check: Q3-25 = 116,007 vs a Q3-26 guide of 115–121k (−0.9% to +4.3%); Q4-25 = 116,249 is the hardest comp of the year; FY26 units track ~404–414k vs FY25 429,445, a **−4% to −6% unit decline year**. Knowing the "recovery" headline flips negative on October 1 is a real, dated, tradable piece of information — the red bench called that print "low-information" and was overturned on it.
3. **The stub math.** A third-party-priced subsidiary round creates a public mark that, added to cash, exceeds most of the parent's cap. Few sell-side models carry it explicitly.
4. **We know where our own analysis broke.** The originating brief made a sign error and built a cross-check on a fabricated delivery count. We caught both. A process that publishes its own refutations is worth something.

## Why it might be priced in

Honestly: most of it probably is.

- The "third consecutive month of YoY growth" framing was **published by CnEVPost on 2026-09-01** — the red bench tagged its own finding NOVEL when it was consensus on the tape the same day.
- "Loss nearly triples" was also a wire headline. Nothing in the Q2 decomposition is private information; the company **disclosed 12.1% vehicle margin in its own bullet list alongside the 20.7% blended figure**, so the red bench's "masking" charge fails on the facts.
- The consensus source we lean on is literally titled *analysts are cutting their estimates* — the divergence is **decaying, not static**. We are betting on the last 6% of a cut already in motion.
- The −63% drawdown has already paid for the bear case. And the stock printed a **new low 16 days after** the $6.3b robotics mark was announced — the market saw the mark and sold anyway. Our own mechanism note explains why (illiquid, preferred-stacked, non-distributable), which means **our doctrine predicts the discount persists**. Betting on it closing is betting against our own model.
- 0.67x EV/S on the consolidated name is not a distressed multiple for a Chinese EV maker in a price war.

## What could go wrong

**Failure 1 — The margin is structural, not a launch-cost trough (most likely).** The 12.1% vehicle margin is the price war, not G9L/GX ramp costs. Q3 prints 11–12%, Q4 worse on the hardest comp of the year, cash burn continues past the ¥1.61b QoQ, and the stub goes from cheap to correctly-priced-at-zero. *Tripwire: Q3 vehicle margin < 12.0%; monthly ASP proxy = (quarterly vehicle rev ÷ units) falling two prints in a row; cash down >¥2b QoQ.* Fully monitorable.

**Failure 2 — The $5.4b floor is not a floor (the reflexive/structural leg).** The robotics mark is a private SPA we have not read. Standard venture terms — 1x liquidation preference, no distribution rights to the parent, transfer restrictions — mean the parent's "86%" is a residual claim on an illiquid asset, not $5.4b of value. A haircut to 50–70% of the naive mark takes the SOTP floor from $8.14 to roughly **$5.90–$7.00**, and every band below is wrong.

Layered on that is the flow structure, and here our own behavior is part of the problem. Our size is *not* the issue: at ADV 14.2M ADS (~$148M/day), a 1.0%-of-book position is on the order of 25k–100k shares, i.e. **0.2–0.7% of one day's volume** — we can exit inside a session and we move nothing. The reflexive damage runs the other way. Our entry gate ("buy if September ≥ 41,581") is the *same* trigger the 36–47M-ADS short base uses to cover, on 2.56 days-to-cover. A good October 1 print gaps the stock, the shorts and we buy the same tick, and our confirmation discipline systematically fills us at the top of the squeeze — then the squeeze unwinds into a November margin print that our gate never tested. Three of our doctrines collide here: *buy below sum-of-parts* says buy now at $10.40; *never add into a name making fresh 52-week lows* says do not; *only enter on confirmation* breaks the tie by guaranteeing we are the last and worst-priced buyer. We have no rule that resolves this, and we should say so rather than pretend the gate is free. *Partial tripwire: a robotics follow-on or down-round disclosure; HKEX/EDGAR placement filings.* No tripwire at all on the SPA terms themselves — they are private and unfiled.

**Failure 3 — NO MONITORABLE TRIPWIRE.** A state action or structural discontinuity we cannot sample: PRC or US regulatory change to the dual-primary/ADR structure, a data or autonomy rule that halts the Guangzhou driverless permit path after an incident, an index-provider deletion forcing passive supply, or an insider/related-party fact sitting in the two **unread Form 4s (2026-07-06, 2026-07-16)** that are themselves anomalous on an all-6-K foreign-private-issuer docket. There is no filing cadence, no monthly series, and no satellite pixel that leads any of these. **Unknown-unknown class: sovereign/regulatory discontinuity in a jurisdiction whose disclosure surface we cannot sample** — with a secondary class of **insider-conduct / consolidation-accounting opacity** (the common-control-merger detector was NOT-FIRED on an *absence* in the 6-K inventory, which is not the same as a negative). We cannot arm a sensor for these. We can only size for them, which argues for the ≤0.5-sleeve cap regardless of how good the entry looks.

If this position loses money, the most likely reason will be that we bought a flat-volume, margin-compressing automaker for the sake of an illiquid robotics mark we never read the terms on, and the market went on refusing to credit that mark — exactly as our own mechanism note said it would — while the auto stub we thought was free turned out to be correctly priced at zero.
