# PITCH — OTF — VERDICT

**ADJUDICATED 2026-08-18 — KILL**

---

## What we believe

Blue Owl Technology Finance Corp. (NYSE: OTF, CIK 1747777) is a $14.7B lender to software and technology companies, managed from the outside by Blue Owl for a fee. It listed on the NYSE in June 2025. It trades around $11.13 against a book value of $16.48 per share — roughly 68 cents on the dollar of stated net assets.

We came to this name because it carried the deepest discount of the two Blue Owl business-development companies, and we expected that discount to be telling us something about how honestly the manager marks its loans. It is not. **The discount is telling us what the book earns.**

The one number that decides this: after all fees, the portfolio produces about **7.3% a year on its own net assets** (run-rate earnings of roughly $1.20 per share against $16.48 of book). If we require a 10% return to own a leveraged credit vehicle, then a book earning 7.3% is worth about 0.73 times its stated value. The stock trades at 0.68–0.70 times. **There is no gap to harvest.** As the defence bench put it: mark integrity is real here, and irrelevant at a justified price-to-book.

Three supporting facts, all from the audited Q2 filing:

- **The dividend is not covered by earnings.** The base distribution is $0.35 a quarter ($1.40 a year) against run-rate earnings of $0.30 a quarter ($1.20 a year). There is $0.32 a share of retained past income to plug the gap, which lasts roughly six quarters. Nothing breaks soon; nothing improves either.
- **They borrowed to pay it.** In the first half of 2026 debt rose $869.3M while investments rose only $394.5M. About **$475M of new borrowing at a 6.50% coupon funded distributions and buybacks, not new loans.** That interest cost is permanent and earns nothing.
- **The cushion is thinning fast.** Asset coverage fell from 227.9% at year-end to **205.3%** at 30 June — 23 points in two quarters. The legal minimum is 150%. This is the live tripwire, and the prosecution bench under-weighted it.

Position: **none. Size zero. No orders. No ladder.**

## What the market believes

Essentially the same thing we just concluded — which is precisely why there is no edge here.

The market is pricing a mid-7% return on equity at roughly a 10% required return, and getting 0.70x book. The discount is not unique to OTF: FSK, NMFC, MFIC and TCPC trade at comparable discounts *without* OTF's post-listing fee increase, so the fee step cannot be what explains a discount the whole cohort shares.

The tape is unremarkable: $11.13 against a 52-week range of $9.75–$15.82 — about 14% off the low, 30% off the high. Pre-listing lock-ups released in full on 2026-06-12 and the stock **rose** afterwards. There is no forced seller and no dislocation to exploit.

The only sell-side anchor either bench could locate is a **Truist price target of $16, cut from $17** — a price target, which is the weakest kind of anchor, and no consensus forward earnings estimate was ever found. We did not put our number beside theirs on the operative line, and that is a recorded gap.

## Why we might have edge

The honest list, and it is short. Every item here is a reason this is not a fraud or a blow-up. None is a reason to own it at 0.68x book earning 7.3%.

- **The manager discloses payment-in-kind income on both bases** (interest and dividends separately), which is better disclosure than several peers. Our attempt to build an advantage on it failed in both directions — see the table.
- **Q2 marks turned back up.** Unrealized gains of +$34.7M after a bad Q1; book value essentially flat quarter to quarter ($16.49 → $16.48).
- **Realized gains of +$83.9M in the half.** That is cash-good, not a mark.
- **Distributable earnings covered declared distributions 1.07x** ($393.9M vs $367.6M) — the headline half-year "loss" is unrealized mark movement, which is neither cash nor taxable.
- **Buybacks at 0.70x book are genuinely accretive**, and the prosecution conceded it.
- **The fee increase is finished, not ongoing.** Management fee Q1'26 $53,913k ≈ Q2'26 $53,948k — flat.
- **Management published the honest earnings number and we called it spin.** See row 3 of the table; this is the largest single error we made on this name.

## Why it might be priced in

All of it. The arithmetic that kills the idea is available to anyone who reads the filing:

- The fee step is 14 months old and was disclosed at the June 2025 listing.
- The discount is cohort-wide, so it is a sector valuation, not a company secret.
- The incentive fee's income component has no cumulative total-return lookback — a permanent, disclosed, structural drag on returns to shareholders.
- The asset-coverage erosion is in the filed balance sheet.

And the sensitivity is brutal. At a 10% required return, justified value is $12.03. At **10.5%**, justified value is **$11.46** — under the price the benches ran on. The entire apparent "gap" between the tape and fair value sits inside the noise of our own choice of discount rate. That is what priced-in looks like.

## PROPOSED ENTRY BANDS

**There is no entry band. No position, no ladder, no resting order, size zero.** The stock is not mispriced; it is correctly priced for what it earns.

Reopen gates, stated verbatim from the adjudication:

> - NONE. Re-court only on **TWO CONSECUTIVE QUARTERS of clean NII ≥$0.33 WITH asset coverage stable ≥210%**, or **a base cut to a covered level that re-rates ROE above the fee load**. The 2026-11-04 print is a **WATCH, not a trigger**. Note: at a 7.3% post-fee ROE, 0.70x NAV is fair — **a wider discount is not automatically an entry**.

Reference arithmetic, so nobody has to re-derive it when the gates are tested:

- Book value per share **$16.48** (30 June 2026). Justified value at a 10% required return is **$12.03**; at 10.5%, **$11.46**.
- $0.33 a quarter of clean earnings = $1.32 a year = **8.0% return on equity** ⇒ justified 0.80x book ⇒ **~$13.18**. That is the first level at which this becomes a conversation rather than a valuation.
- Asset coverage must be **stable at ≥210%**, not merely above it. Below 200% is a deterioration signal, not a discount opportunity.
- "Clean" means: earnings normalised for the capital-gains incentive fee accrual, exactly as done in row 2 above. A quarter that looks good only because the fee swung negative does **not** count toward the two.

## What remains unverified

Stated plainly, including the things that embarrass us:

1. **The 10% required return is an assumption, not a measurement.** The whole "fairly priced" conclusion rests on it. At 10.5% the stock is already above fair value; at 9% it would be ~20% cheap. This is the softest leg of the decisive finding, and the defence bench flagged it against itself.
2. **The cause of the fee step** — permanent post-listing rate versus a waiver that rolls off — is unread. Note 3 was behind filing truncation.
3. **The floating-rate income decomposition never ran.** With a 97% floating book, we cannot say whether income growth came from the rate index or from spread, and therefore cannot say what happens if the index falls.
4. **Peer PIK percentages were never put on a like basis.** Our cohort comparison is unusable in both directions.
5. **Non-accrual detail was never read from the schedule of investments** by either bench — only the summary percentage.
6. **The mark-recognition ladder that got this name courted is void** until recomputed excluding each manager's largest single position.
7. **No consensus forward earnings estimate was located.** We killed this on our own arithmetic without ever placing our number beside the street's on the operative line.
8. **OTF II's separate financials were never verified**, so the merger exchange ratio at issue in the litigation notice is unexamined by us.
9. **No trading-volume or ownership-crowding read was run** (broker permission was not granted in session). Immaterial at a $5.3B NYSE listing, but stated rather than skipped.
10. **The 30 June 2025 comparatives were read through a summariser, not line by line.**
11. **The next print date is vendor-derived and unconfirmed.**
12. **The tape used by the benches ($11.5718) differs from the tape in the final evidence pack ($11.13).** Immaterial to the verdict, but it means the discount figures quoted across the benches (29.8%) and the adjudication (32.5%) are the same fact at two different prices.
