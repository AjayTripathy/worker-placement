02

The business

Two engines: one recurring, one sovereign

Travel Health (~DKK 3.0bn guided 2026) is the recurring engine: rabies (RabAvert/Rabipur, 78% US market share), tick-borne encephalitis (Encepur), typhoid and cholera, plus the newly launched chikungunya vaccine Vimkunya — now in the US and 14 European countries. Q2 2026 travel-health revenue grew 45% (56% adjusting for discontinued partnerships), with rabies +55% and Encepur +58%. This is demand that recurs with human movement, not with outbreaks.

Public Preparedness (~DKK 2.5bn guided 2026) is the sovereign engine: the world's preferred mpox/smallpox vaccine (JYNNEOS/IMVANEX/IMVAMUNE), sold to governments building stockpiles. Lumpy by quarter, contracted by nature — ~DKK 2.3bn of 2026 already secured, and ~DKK 800M already secured for 2027. The US government exercised $97M of freeze-dried JYNNEOS options in Q2; another >DKK 700M contract landed from an undisclosed government.

03

The special situation

DKK 250 was bid. 40% of the register said no.

In 2025, Nordic Capital and Permira ran a full tender for Bavarian Nordic at DKK 250 — best and final. It lapsed on November 5, 2025 with 60% tendered: short of the threshold, because an ATP-led bloc of roughly 40% of the register refused a 38% premium in cash.

A lapsed bid is not a dead fact. It is a revealed reservation price: the most informed, largest holders told you — with capital, not words — that the business is worth more than DKK 250 to them. That number, not a DCF, anchors the ceiling of this position.

  • The bid is legally dead. A re-offer bar of ~12 months applies (source-conflicted at court; treated as clearing by ~November 2026).
  • The asset got cheaper while the bidders' thesis aged. Since the tender lapsed the company has raised guidance twice and the stock spent months 25–30% below the refused price.
  • PE appetite was demonstrated, not hypothesized. The dormant M&A call is why the position tolerates zero carry: it is priced as an option, frozen at p = 0.30 by November 2027, where blue's breakeven was ~4–5%.

Why today mattered

The win isn't the 9% — it's the grade

Position P&L today: +DKK 3,300 (~$510); since entry +DKK 5,400 (~$835), +15.7%. Small in dollars — a half-starter is supposed to be. What's large is the information:

  • Blue's central claim is now twice-confirmed. The thesis was that the May raise was real and the market was still trading the stale February story. Companies whose raised guides are fake do not raise them again and attach a 45%-margin quarter.
  • Red's 2027 cliff got smaller in public. ~DKK 800M of 2027 preparedness revenue is already secured, plus a >DKK 700M contract spanning 2026–27 and exercised US options. The cliff argument now fights disclosed backlog, not projections.
  • The dead-money concession is being retired by management. Zero-carry was the reason this was a half-starter. A rolling cancel-the-shares buyback is carry.
  • The refused DKK 250 is 19% above even today's close. The reservation-price anchor survives the rally with room to spare.
What cut against us (report honestly): Vimkunya's FY26 revenue was cut from DKK 250M to 200M because CDC's ACIP recommendation — issued April 2025 — has still not been published in the MMWR, stalling US uptake. This is the named US vaccine-policy tail from our own kill list showing up in miniature: real, contained (~1% of revenue), and worth watching precisely because we predicted its shape. Also: H1 operating cash flow was −DKK 401M on delivery phasing — the cash builds in H2, but verify it does.

09

Risks

What would change our mind

  • Guide reversal: FY26 landing below DKK 5.7bn / preparedness below 2.3bn secured — the raise unwinding would resurrect red's entire case.
  • US policy tail: HHS/vaccine-policy action touching JYNNEOS or the travel franchise. The ACIP/MMWR stall on Vimkunya is the small-scale proof this tail is live.
  • BARDA/US options not exercised on future tranches — the sovereign engine's visibility is contracts, and contracts can stop.
  • ATP bloc selling down: the reservation price is only as good as the register that set it. A large refusing holder distributing stock deletes the anchor.
  • Re-offer bar expiring quietly: if November 2027 passes with no approach, the 0.30 call grades MISS, the option premium in our tree goes to zero, and this must stand as an ~8× operating story or be exited.
  • H2 cash conversion: −DKK 401M H1 operating cash flow must reverse with H2 deliveries; if it doesn't, the buyback capacity story weakens.
