The two staged names

Northpointe Bancshares (NPB)

Starter 0.5% — red-capped$17.30 · 1.0× TBV

The case. A February-2025 IPO orphan compounding quietly: ROTCE 14.7% at 1.02× tangible book, charge-offs of 0.03%, uninsured deposits just 7.7% (the 2023 run-vector absent), EPS +21% while the stock sat flat — multiple compression on a growing bank. The market prices the funding model, not the franchise: 58.6% brokered funding earns a thin 2.33% NIM at ~1.0 deposit beta, so the multiple probably never re-rates. The case is EPS compounding at a held multiple — the court never claimed more.

Red team — sustained at the low end (red 4/10)

  • The hoped-for sticky custodial layer is real but immaterial (5.7% of deposits) — the funding bear stands fully intact, hence 0.5% size, not 0.75%.
  • Castle Creek still holds ~20% with registration rights and no shelf filed — and the company literally paid a $2.50/share special dividend to buy deadline extensions. Series B registration comes due January 2027: a dated supply event, now on the calendar. Tripwire: any S-3 resale shelf → re-court.
  • MPP warehouse book: zero charge-offs since 2010 through two crises — but concentration is undisclosed, so the WAL-style counterparty tail is unquantifiable. Watch, not comfort.

Entry: 290 @ $17.00 GTC limit-only (~1.0× TBV). Q3 pack separate: reaction driver = MPP growth + NIM under falling SOFR + any Castle Creek news, not the EPS line. Kills: wholesale >70% · NIM <2.15% · any MPP counterparty loss · S-3 shelf · haircut loosening · the streak breaking.

Hosiden (6804.T)

Starter 0.5% at the band¥2,826 · band ¥2,550–2,700

The case. Nintendo's Switch-2 assembler at 8.6× peak / ~11× diluted-guide earnings with net cash equal to 49% of the market cap and a ~5% shareholder yield with real, cancelled buybacks. The trough is observed, not modeled — FY24-25 still earned meaningfully with the console segment at bottom — and ex-cash the guide multiple is ~5.7×. The trap is singular and named: Nintendo is 78.0% of total revenue (the customer note, not a segment proxy), on purchase orders at will.

Red team — sustained at 5.5 with diluted math (red 5/10)

  • The convertible is ~5% in the money: all math moved to 52.9M diluted shares — base FV ¥2,950 (was ¥3,100). The entry band gains a second justification: it sits below the ¥2,683 conversion price, where convert-arb supply stops capping the tape.
  • The Q1 question (prints ~Aug-7) is a reconciliation: Nintendo guides year-2 hardware −17%; Hosiden guides amusement −5.9%. Holding that gap requires share/mix gains the filings don't evidence — the print must show them or the guide is optimism.
  • The demonstrated trough was partly FX-rescued (the company's own words); FX-adjusted trough EPS ~¥150–170. The bear FV already embedded it; the narrative was flattered.

Entry: 300 @ ¥2,650 GTC limit-only — resting below spot; a fill through the print window is a washout fill at 4.6–5.2× ex-cash. Kills: amusement guide cut (the reconciliation failing) · mobile recovery failing · yen sustained <145 · actual CB conversions >2%/qtr · a decline steeper than Switch-1's worst year.

The showcase: two verdicts the red bench reversed

Jungheinrich (red 8/10): the court called the FY26 guide "confirmed" — it had been cut by ad-hoc six days before the court sat (EBIT to €340–400M, FCF to ~nil, "competitive pressures" named), which meant the court's own kill bar was already breached at sitting. The red also corrected the multiple (1.04× book, not 0.8×) and surfaced €1.8bn of financial-services liabilities outside the "net credit" line. Lesson codified: every court sweeps ad-hocs/8-Ks to the sitting date — the live-corpus rule, now with a second scar to its name. What survives: the order-intake inflection is real, which keeps a positive (orders-only) read-through for held KALMAR.

Lagardère (red 6/10): the court's "observable buyback catalyst" turned out to be a shelf with zero flow in twelve weeks of AMF filings; its SOTP overstated Travel Retail EBIT by ~€86M, double-counted corporate, and omitted minorities (~17% of profit, in the crown-jewel units); and the Vivendi precedent shows the French-regime "protection" delivering years of litigation, not a tender. Honest base ~€22.5; spot was at the bear edge, not below it. Re-banded €16–17 with the event pack as the only path back.
