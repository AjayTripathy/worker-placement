ADMA BiologicsADMA · Nasdaq
Envelope 123 · BUY 1,485 @ $8.08 GTC · killed
Overturned to WAIT — stand aside

What the market believes

ADMA channel-stuffed its differentiated IG product to fake growth; a short report + a guidance cut + a filed fraud suit mean the franchise is impaired. Repriced −55% YTD to 52-week lows; shorts pressed to a 2-year high, not covering.

What we believe

The business is real (37% operating margin, ~7× forward EBITDA), insiders bought the dip, and management disclosed the working-capital deterioration and cut guidance rather than hiding it — so it clears the honesty bar. But it is not the clean "refuted short" the finder claimed.

Decisive court finding (overturns the buy): a full forensic entity-resolution pass confirmed the short report's central structural claim. "Genesis BioPharma Services" resolves to Genesis Bio-Pharmaceuticals Ltd — a 35-year-old, Grossman-controlled pharmaceutical wholesaler co-located in ADMA's own headquarters building, which appears nowhere in any ADMA filing (confirmed by full-text EDGAR search). ADMA's rebuttal is categorical — no Grossman entity has "distributed, taken title to or otherwise possessed" its products — which makes this a binary: either that statement is false (a genuine undisclosed related-party round-trip), or the related party merely lists the product aspirationally and never moves it. The one causal fact — does ADMA's product actually flow through Genesis — is honestly UNVERIFIABLE at the top evidence bar (the archived product page was robots/CAPTCHA-blocked). Supporting the bear underneath: DSO has climbed to a record 105.6 days and a securities-fraud complaint (Mazzarino, D.N.J.) pleading this exact issue is filed, lead-plaintiff deadline Aug-10.

Verdict: WAIT — envelope killed. You are not paid to underwrite an unresolved undisclosed-related-party binary with a fraud-tail, sitting on top of record-DSO deterioration, a live securities suit, and shorts pressed to a 2-year high — that disqualifies a long here. It is also not a proven fraud, so not a short. Stand aside until it resolves. Binary-closers to watch: (1) a clean archived capture of the Genesis products/services pages + the NJ Dept. of Health wholesale-drug licence for Genesis Bio-Pharmaceuticals Ltd; (2) the Q2 10-Q (~Aug 6–8), an amended related-party note, or Mazzarino discovery quantifying any dollar flow through Genesis.

Nature's SunshineNATR · Nasdaq
Envelope 124 · BUY 353 @ $19.81 GTC · ~$7k
Starter — small

What the market believes

A no-growth legacy multi-level marketer — a melting annuity worth a low multiple, cheap because the distributor base is aging and eroding.

What we believe

It isn't melting: revenue is at an all-time high ($480M FY25), every segment grew in local currency, and the volume-incentive payout ratio is flat-to-falling (30.1% vs 30.9% — a dying MLM pays more, not less, to hold a shrinking base). Net cash, disciplined buyback shrinking the share count.

The honest catch: the headline earnings and "18× P/E" are flattered by a non-operating currency swing — normalize it and the multiple is ~21×, so this is fairly valued quality, not deeply cheap. And China is now 100%-owned (post the Fosun buyout) with 46% of revenue in Asia and most cash offshore — a real, unhedgeable tail. Hence a small starter, not a full position.

Tripwires: the volume-incentive ratio turning up (the cleanest melting-signal); any China direct-selling / pyramid-scheme enforcement action or service-fee tightening.

RCM TechnologiesRCMT · Nasdaq
Envelope 125 · BUY 223 @ $26.81 GTC · suspended
Overturned to WAIT — order suspended

What the market believes

Either a cheap staffing name or a post-COVID travel-nurse-rate peak about to mean-revert. The verify pass confirmed the business is real: net debt not net cash (EV/EBIT ~8.8×), sticky school nursing (+15%, the whale is NYC's Dept. of Education), engineering re-inflection real in the print.

What the court found the verify missed

Every stated number is true — but the thesis rested on FY25 audited figures produced under an adverse internal-control opinion. A verify confirms the numbers; only the court asked whether they can be trusted. They can't, fully.

Decisive court finding (overturns the buy): RCMT's FY25 auditor attested that internal control over financial reporting was NOT effective — four material weaknesses, three of them top-of-house (control environment, risk assessment, monitoring — pervasive, not one cycle), reaffirmed as of April 2026. Management concedes they "could lead to a misstatement of substantially all account balances." Then RCMT fired the auditor 26 days after the 10-K (third auditor swap in three cycles). The thesis explicitly claimed "the marketed takeaway does not diverge from the audited numbers" — now false by omission. Modeling a ~25% control-event tail drops fair value to ~$26.43, below the $26.81 limit — so the order buys at zero-to-negative expected value, and the +5% edge can't absorb it. Not a kill (business real, litigation clean, no hidden related party, no restatement yet) — but a clear WAIT.

Tripwires: BUY — the FY26 10-K reports the four weaknesses remediated + a clean control opinion. KILL — any 8-K Item 4.02 (non-reliance), a restated period, or a further auditor change. Value re-opens below ~$19–20, where a margin of safety could pay for the control risk even unremediated.
