# PITCH — AXTI — PROPOSED VERDICT
**REJECT ENTRY NOW — stay flat; reopen the case only at or below $64.25 (the price management just sold stock at) or on a filed permit-grant 8-K — PROPOSED, PENDING ADJUDICATION.**

## What we believe
AXT makes semiconductor substrates — chiefly indium phosphide (InP) wafers, the raw material for the optical transceivers that connect AI data centers. Essentially all of its production is in China, through a subsidiary (Tongmei) of which outside investors own about 14.5%. The business is genuinely inflecting: record InP demand, Chinese export-permit approvals speeding up, and Lumentum — a major US optics maker — has paid roughly $87 million in cash deposits to reserve InP capacity through 2031. The CFO stated on the Q2 call that about $66 million of Q3 revenue is already covered by granted permits or needs no permit at all, with permit grants on top of that being pure upside.

But we believe **none of this is a reason to buy at today's price**. The stock has risen roughly 25–40x from its 52-week low and trades within about 10–15% of its all-time regular-session high. It is up ~121% just since July 29. At a pro-forma enterprise value of roughly $3.7 billion, the market is paying about 14x annualized guided revenue and about 65x annualized guided GAAP earnings for a company whose entire manufacturing base sits inside China's export-control regime, whose China subsidiary leaks 14.5% of its value to minority holders, and whose management sold 9.84 million new shares (about 15% of the company) at ~$64.25 net just weeks ago. The good news is real; it is also fully — arguably more than fully — in the price.

## What the market believes
The market believes AXT is the pure-play bottleneck supplier for AI optics and is pricing near-perfection: continued permit flow, the Lumentum relationship treated as locked-in demand, and the ~$130M/quarter InP trajectory extending. The name is crowded by every measure the court checked: fresh 13G institutional filings (July 27 and August 12), leveraged single-stock ETFs launched August 13, 19–30 million-share trading days, and an insider Form 144 sale filed on August 7 — the day the stock topped. This is the opposite of a neglected or dislocated situation. Critically, the court found **no fear was ever priced in**: the supposed 43% drawdown in the data pack turned out to be a data artifact (see below), so there is no discount to harvest.

## Why we might have edge
- **We read the actual contracts.** The bull pitch called the Lumentum arrangement "the cohort's only contractual revenue floor" with "shortfall and refund provisions." The filed exhibit ([8-K exhibit 99.3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001051627/000143774926024883/ex_993544.htm)) says the deposits are "shipment credits towards the purchase of products until exhausted" — a prepayment, not a take-or-pay floor, and the shortfall/refund language simply does not exist. The thesis fabricated it.
- **We decomposed the tape.** The pack's "$143.16 high → 43% drawdown" appears in no actual trading session (window high: $90.39 on 08-10). The dislocation the bull case wanted to buy mostly never happened.
- **We caught the venue gap on the key number.** The $66M "permit-covered" figure appears only on the earnings call — not in the 10-Q (including MD&A, confirmed by full read), not in the press release, and a full-text search of AXT's entire SEC corpus for "permit-exempt" returns zero hits.
- **We have management's own price mark**: they sold 15% of the company at ~$64.25, about 21% below the current tape. That is the most honest valuation signal in the record.

## Why it might be priced in
Symmetry requires saying it plainly: our "edge" here is mostly *negative* edge — knowing what not to pay. The bull's core facts survived the court: record InP revenue is real, permits are flowing faster, the $87M of Lumentum deposits are real cash skin in the game, and the blue bench established that the $66M figure, while call-only, is a scripted CFO statement carrying securities-law liability — a real public representation, not a rumor. AXT's press releases contain no guidance at all, so call-only forward disclosure is this company's normal practice, not selective disclosure. And permit-grant 8-Ks are free upside options on top of a guide floor that assumes zero new permits. If the AI-optics buildout keeps compounding, today's 14x sales could look ordinary in hindsight. Our claim is not that the story is false — it is that at ~10% off the all-time high, with the crowd fully aboard, we are being offered no compensation for China-jurisdiction risk, export-control risk, the 14.5% minority leakage, and 65x earnings.
