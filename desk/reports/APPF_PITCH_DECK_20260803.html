That is the signal channel: three dated 8-K guidance points moving up while price went $219 → $142.56 → $191.69. It cleanly separates de-rate from derailment, it is free, and it is primary-sourced. Its weakness is equally plain: the half-life is about one earnings cycle — the tape closed most of the gap within eight sessions of the July print. By the time a percent-off-high screen surfaces this signature, the re-rate is usually underway.

What is actually driving growth

Q2-2026 decomposition ties exactly: customers +6.3% × units-per-customer +1.5% × revenue-per-unit +10.6% = +19.3%. So 55% of growth is monetization, 45% volume — and inside monetization, subscription-per-unit is decelerating (+8.9% → +5.5%) while VAS-per-unit holds (+12.9%). The thesis rests on ARPU, and ARPU currently rests on VAS. That is a narrower engine than the headline suggests, and it is why this is a 5, not a 6.

06 / 10The demerits — kept in the record, not argued away

Four things the bull deck would have omitted

  • The margin expansion is bought from R&D. R&D spend +9.3% against revenue +19.3% (ratio 19.8% → 18.1%) — in the same window the CEO's new PSUs (April 2026, running 2027–2030) began keying off income from operations. Margin beats may be purchased with future growth. Watch the ratio, not the beat.
  • Capital allocation bought high and abstained low. $50.0M repurchased at $204.77 avg, $125.0M at $177.95 — then $0 in the quarter the stock touched $142.56. $125.0M remains authorized. The charitable read (investments shifted to cash, dry powder for M&A) is two-sided risk at a 5.8× EV/S acquirer; a deal >$300M at >6× EV/S is a hard kill below.
  • A $219.3M cloud/AI commitment through 2031 sits in cost of revenue ($36.2M short-term) — the gross-margin decay driver (−90bps y/y), and the bet underneath the entire AI-VAS bull case.
  • Two earnings flatterers are expiring on a schedule: the deferred tax asset drains ~16 more months (a ~$32M/yr FCF headwind from late-2027), and the effective tax rate is normalizing (14.3% → 23.6%) as stock-comp windfalls vanished with the price — reflexive in both directions.

Screen-row corrections carried into the record: the row's "fwd P/E 22.8" was FY2027 — on FY2026 it is 28.3×; "earnings +18%" is basis-dependent (GAAP NI +15.5%, non-GAAP EPS +23.9%).

The entire +10.6% "edge" is our 25% weight on an AI-ARPU re-acceleration we cannot verify from any primary source — a probability disagreement, not a fact disagreement. That is why no capital moves at spot, and why the competitive channel we could not sweep (Yardi / RealPage / Entrata, the sector AI-de-rate narrative) argues for the gate rather than the buy.
