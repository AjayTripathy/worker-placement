# THE CASE AGAINST NUZEFATIDE — a biology bear pitch with freezable predictions
**2026-08-24 · principal-directed ("I am totally ok to make a prediction against the biology of nuzefatide") · informs the deferred BCYC entry (instr #100), rules nothing**

**The pitch in one paragraph.** Bicycle Therapeutics has redirected its burn onto nuzefatide
(BT5528), an EphA2-targeted toxin conjugate. EphA2 is a four-time clinical graveyard — a fact the
sponsor's own 2026 review concedes by name. The drug's only published efficacy number, from its own
Phase 1 in *JCO* (Sept 2024), is a **6.7% response rate at the selected go-forward dose** — chemo-noise
level — while the format's pharmacology inverts its sales pitch: the targeting molecule survives
**under an hour** in circulation but the free toxin it releases circulates for **two days**. The
company then chose, as its pivot indication, the one tumor type (2L+ pancreatic) where single-digit
response benchmarks make a weak drug spinnable — with a 39-patient trial that cannot be falsified
until 2029. We predict the imminent final-data disclosure does not rescue the asset, and we say so
with frozen numbers.

---

## 1 · The EphA2 graveyard (the prior)
Every prior clinical program against this target has been halted — **the sponsor's own review says
so verbatim**: "efficacy and safety issues have halted clinical development of previous
EphA2-targeting agents including MEDI-547, DS-8895a, MM-310, and dasatinib" (Bennett et al. —
BicycleTx authors — *J Transl Med* 2026, PMID 41782030).
- **MEDI-547** (MedImmune, antibody-ADC, auristatin): Phase 1 **stopped at the first dose level** —
  six patients at 0.08 mg/kg, all discontinued; treatment-related bleeding and coagulation events
  (hemorrhage n=3, epistaxis n=2); "lower doses were not explored and an MTD could not be selected"
  (*Invest New Drugs* 2013, PMID 22370972). The target sits on normal vasculature; hitting it with a
  long-circulating toxin was a disaster.
- **DS-8895a** (Daiichi, afucosylated ADCC antibody): two Phase 1s (PMID 31412935, 35404015) —
  safety and imaging established, no efficacy signal worth developing; discontinued.
- **MM-310** (EphA2-targeted docetaxel nanoliposome) and **dasatinib** (EphA2 among off-targets):
  halted (per the sponsor review; MM-310's own literature trail is thin — flagged, not embellished).
**Base rate: 0-for-4.** No EphA2-directed therapeutic has ever cleared Phase 2. Any new EphA2
conjugate starts from a single-digit prior before its own data speaks.

## 2 · Nuzefatide's own number — published, and worse than "undisclosed"
The desk's earlier dive recorded the pivot asset's efficacy as "a characterization, not a number."
**Correction: the number exists.** The first-in-human dose-escalation was published in *JCO*
(2024;42:3443-52, PMID 39231383, n=45):
- **At the selected RP2D (6.5 mg/m² q2w): ORR 6.7%, disease-control rate 20.0%.**
- Tox: manageable (G≥3 neutropenia 22.2%; no MEDI-547-style bleeding) — the format genuinely
  retired the class safety failure. Dosing reached 10 mg/m² vs MEDI-547's death at first dose.
- **PK, from the same paper: BT5528 half-life 0.4–0.7 h; released MMAE half-life 35–47 h.** The
  tumor sees the targeting vector for minutes; the body sees free payload for days. The G≥3
  neutropenia is the signature of exactly that — systemic MMAE without the targeting.
The "no headline ORR after seven years" pattern is now explained: there is a number, and it does
not get headlined. The undisclosed remainder (the ~240-patient expansion cohorts, PCD passed
7/31/26) is what the imminent disclosure covers — see the prediction.

## 3 · The format-vs-indication contradiction
Bicycle's core claim: small size → fast, deep tumor penetration; renal clearance → brief exposure →
safety. The mirror: **brief exposure must still deliver enough payload**, and the chosen pivot
indication is metastatic **pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma — the most stroma-dense, hypoperfused
major solid tumor**. A sub-hour delivery window into the least-perfused tumor class is a mechanistic
bet *against* the indication's defining biology. Honest mirror check, conceded: EphA2 target
expression in PDAC is real (abundant by IHC, Giaginis 2010, PMID 19949912; the company shows 16/16
PDX expression and human Ga-68 imaging engagement) — the target is present; the question is whether
a transient vector at 8 mg/m² q2w can deliver a cytotoxic dose through PDAC stroma when it managed
6.7% ORR in better-perfused tumors.

## 4 · Indication-choice forensics
With ~240 expansion slots dosed across urothelial, ovarian, NSCLC, HNSCC and TNBC since 2019 —
through at least four ASCO/ESMO cycles — no expansion cohort's response rate has ever led a press
release. The sole company characterization is "promising anti-tumor activity" on an n=14 mUC+nivo
cohort. This company press-releases zelenectide data constantly; **selective silence is selection.**
Then the pivot trial (NCT07450859) chose second-line-plus PDAC: n=39, single-arm, ORR primary,
first dosed April 2026, primary completion **March 2029**. In 2L+ PDAC, chemotherapy benchmarks run
single digits to mid-teens — it is the only indication where a 10–15% ORR reads as a win. The
structure is bar-lowering plus falsifiability-deferral: three fundable years before the asset can
fail.

## 5 · The revealed-preference chain
March 2026: the company **killed the asset with randomized data** (zelenectide: 58–62% confirmed 1L
BICR ORR) after adverse regulatory feedback — and pivoted the firm onto the asset with a published
6.7% RP2D ORR and an unfalsifiable-until-2029 lead trial. Zero insider buys on record. A strategic
process five months without closure. The uncharitable-but-arguable read stands: **the least
falsifiable asset is the most financeable one**, and the burn (~$50M/qtr against $510M) now runs
through it.

## 6 · The honest counter-case (what would make the bear wrong)
- The tox reboot is real and unprecedented for this target — "safe at active-class doses" is an
  achievement no prior EphA2 program managed; safety alone has partnering value in radioligand form.
- Escalation-stage ORR **understates** enriched expansion cohorts as a rule; the 6.7% is n≈15 at one
  dose in mixed tumors; the undisclosed n=14 mUC cohort runs WITH nivolumab (combo uplift possible).
- EphA2 expression in PDAC is verified (16/16 PDX, human imaging); an 8 mg/m² dose is above the
  RP2D used for the 6.7%.
- The platform carries $240M of cumulative partner inflows (Bayer/Novartis/Ionis/Genentech) — outside
  money keeps validating the *binders*, whatever the toxin-conjugate results say.
**Falsifiers, stated in advance:** a disclosed confirmed ORR ≥20% (n≥20) in any BT5528-100 cohort;
or ≥3 responses in the first ~15 PDAC patients at any interim. Either kills this pitch and triggers
the entry court instead.
