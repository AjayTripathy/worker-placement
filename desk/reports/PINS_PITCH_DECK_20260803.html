Two honest corrections the bull version omits

  • Elliott is a bondholder with a board seat, not an equity buyer. The $1.0B came as 1.75% converts due 2031, conversion price $22.72, with a board seat (10-Q Note 12, related party). 06 / 12The bear case — stated at full strength

Saturated core, margin going backward, and a fifth of revenue paid in stock

  • The Q2 guide has margins going backward — the strongest bear datum, absent from the bull narrative. Q2'26 guided adj EBITDA $256–276M (mid $266M) vs $250.8M a year ago: +6.1% EBITDA on +14.5% revenue, margin 23.3% vs 25.1% (−180bps). If tvScientific media-resale mix, the AWS commitment, and the April RSU grant are the cause, the operating-leverage leg of the bull case is dead. Counter: Q1 beat its EBITDA guide mid by +19% and held margin flat — the company guides low.
  • The core market is saturated. US&Canada MAU: 99→101→102→103→105→106M — +4% for five straight quarters — while producing 74% of revenue at $7.12 ARPU. All user growth is Rest-of-World at $0.20 ARPU, 36x lower. The bull case requires ARPU to carry everything. Counter: US&Ca ARPU accelerated +4%→+9%; Europe +27%, RoW +59%.
  • Stock comp is permanent and the buyback is partly a treadmill. SBC 21% of revenue; ~$1.86B of future expense already granted (including 48.5M RSUs struck at the lows). Owner earnings ~$1.55/share, 24% below headline.
  • tvScientific is 91% goodwill and intangibles ($375.1M goodwill on $465.1M) — bought growth, not assets.
  • UNVERIFIABLE is not clean: consensus positioning, the exact Q2 date, Elliott's equity stake (if any), and the AI/LLM-disintermediation bear all went unverified (web budget exhausted at run time). Priced into the 5/10, and into the tiny T1.
