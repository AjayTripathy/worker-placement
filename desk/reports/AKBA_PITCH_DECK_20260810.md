# PITCH — AKBA — PROPOSED VERDICT (one line; label clearly "PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION")

**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION: REJECT — do not buy at any price while CMS's published $0.9437-per-treatment payment stands against the ~$61 per treatment Vafseo collects today; revisit only on the event gates below.** (Both court benches independently reached REJECT at conviction 9/10.)

## What we believe

Akebia Therapeutics (~$0.84/share, 2.6% above its 52-week low, down 75% from its high) faces a published, date-certain collapse in how Medicare pays for its lead drug. Vafseo, an oral anemia drug for dialysis patients, currently earns roughly $61 per treatment under a temporary Medicare add-on payment (called TDAPA) that expires December 31, 2026. On June 26, 2026, CMS published its proposed rule for 2027, and the replacement payment is exact and tiny: **$0.9437 per treatment** — paid on *every* dialysis claim whether Vafseo is used or not, meaning a dialysis clinic's marginal reimbursement for actually dispensing Vafseo drops to approximately **zero** (Federal Register, 91 FR 38784, Table 12, p. 38811: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-06-26/pdf/2026-12925.pdf). Management's own guidance of "ESA-range pricing" implies roughly an 80% price cut, so even flat 2027 revenue would require 4–5× the patient count in one year, sold into clinics with no financial incentive to use the drug.

The same rule adds a second cliff neither the original pitch nor the first bench modeled: Akebia's other drug, Auryxia (a phosphate binder), gets folded into the fixed base payment rate at +$15.96 on January 1, 2027, ending its own add-on and putting it in direct in-bundle competition with generics.

The balance sheet compounds this: $155.5M cash against a $29.3M first-half burn looks like two years of runway, but that math is explicitly conditioned on refinancing a $49.6M senior secured term loan — hard to do cheaply with a sub-$1 stock.

## What the market believes

The market appears to already have this roughly right. The stock fell 24% on August 5 second-quarter results and sits at $0.844, near its 52-week low of $0.823 — and, critically, all of that trading happened *after* the June 26 rule publication. The market traded with the $0.9437 number in hand for six weeks before our pitch was written. The price is consistent with a market pricing in: reimbursement cliff on the lead drug, second cliff on the second drug, and financing risk in a sub-$1 equity.

## Why we might have edge

Stated honestly: **the original pitch's claimed edge does not exist.** The pitch argued that management had given direction but not magnitude on the 2027 payment change, and that we could reconstruct the post-TDAPA price from public CMS data before the market. CMS published the exact number six weeks before the pitch was written. There is nothing left to reconstruct.

What survives, per the defending bench, is small and bounded:

- **Quarterly updates could raise the add-on.** CMS proposes updating the post-TDAPA amount quarterly using the most recent utilization data; Vafseo's 2026 spending run-rate (~$85M+) could lift $0.9437 toward roughly $2–3 per treatment. That is still ~5% of today's realized price — it cannot reopen the case, only soften the floor.
- **A payment channel outside the Medicare bundle math.** Capitated, value-based dialysis operators (the US Renal Care type) internalize the hospitalization benefit seen in the VOICE trial and could pay for Vafseo on its merits. Plausible, unquantified, and under 2% penetration today.
- **A precedent cuts both ways.** DefenCath's post-TDAPA add-on actually *rose* across rule years ($2.37 → $5.60), so post-cliff utilization can grow — the prosecuting bench's claim that the Korsuva precedent proves utilization always collapses was overstated.

## Why it might be priced in

It is not merely "priced in" — the information asymmetry the pitch depended on expired before the pitch existed. The June 26 rule predates the August 5 selloff; the number cross-foots with the company's own financials ($0.9437 ÷ 0.65 risk-sharing factor × ~40M annual treatments ≈ $58M ≈ actual 2025 Vafseo revenue), so there is no ambiguity for the market to be confused about. The residual bull arguments above are worth cents against a ~$60 payment gap. Both benches also note the stock has **not** overshot to an obviously irrational level: financing-spiral risk (refinancing a secured loan with a sub-$1 stock, plus a fresh employee stock plan registration adding dilution) means the downside tail is genuinely open, not bounded.

## PROPOSED ENTRY BANDS

**The courts say no entry — there is no price at which this is a buy while the published reimbursement math stands.** The gap between $0.94–$3 (published/plausible payment) and ~$61 (current realized price) cannot be closed by any volume path either bench could construct, and the downside tail (financing spiral) is unbounded. Sizing: **0% of book.**

**Reopen conditions (from the benches' own findings), any one of which reopens a full re-court:**

1. **Final rule restructures the methodology** for this drug class — the CY2027 ESRD final rule is expected late October / early November 2026, and the comment docket shows documented stakeholder pressure on exactly this mechanism (p. 38811). This is the principal reopen gate.
2. **A signed volume contract with DaVita or Fresenius** committing 2027 Vafseo volumes at a disclosed net price of roughly **$25/treatment or better** — that would prove a payment channel outside the bundle math.
3. **TDAPA-extension legislation reaching committee markup** in Congress.
4. **A verified balance sheet showing cash through 2028 without the loan refinancing** (requires finally reading the blocked 10-Q equity note).

**Informational price gates (not entries):**
- **Below $0.50:** treat as confirmation of the financing-spiral scenario; check for a Nasdaq minimum-price deficiency notice and refinancing terms.
- **Above $1.56 (the 26-week high) with none of the four reopen events having occurred:** the market knows something we don't — mandatory review of what we missed.

**Catalyst dates:** CY2027 ESRD final rule ~late Oct/early Nov 2026 (date not fixed); Q3 earnings 2026-11-09 (from a data feed, **not company-confirmed**); January 1, 2027 — Vafseo's per-use Medicare payment ends (date-certain under the existing TDAPA term) and Auryxia's bundling takes effect (proposed, pending the final rule).
