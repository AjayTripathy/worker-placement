### ACCESS (N=230, 36wk) — the properly-powered study
Source: 8-K 2025-12-08 Ex-99.1; Rosenstock et al., Nature Medicine 32:2941–2947 (2026).
5 mg start, titration to 45/90/120 mg. Placebo-adjusted: **−8.2 / −9.8 / −11.3%** (all
p<0.0001); placebo −0.8%. At 120 mg: 86% ≥5%, 70% ≥10%. SBP −6.4 to −7.5; HR +2.0 to +3.8;
no QTc.

**Estimand (load-bearing, PR's own footnote):** on-treatment/hypothetical — "efficacy had all
randomized participants remained on study treatment… without initiating rescue" — NOT ITT
treatment-policy.

**Tolerability (Nature Med Table 2):** GI TEAE 79–89%; nausea 65–71%; vomiting 32–45%;
**AE discontinuation 13.3 / 7.7 / 11.1% (pooled 10.4% = 18/173, verified)** vs placebo 5.4%.

**Hepatic — the danuglipron question:** company line "no DILI, no persistent liver enzyme
elevations, no QTc" is TRUE AS WRITTEN; Extended Data Table 6 shows **≥3× ULN in 8 participants,
≥5× ULN in 1, zero ≥10×**, all resolving on drug. Fully disclosed in the publication, never in a
press release — hierarchical disclosure, not concealment; register as absence-of-DILI at n>625,
not absence-of-transaminase-signal.

**Publication correction logged:** Nat Med 32:3095 (2026-06-24): Fig 3b 120mg LS mean −12.1 →
**−12.7**; Fig 4 (GI tolerability) initially missing bottom-right panel data and vomiting-panel
arrows. Figure-integrity note, not masking.

### ACCESS II (N=85) — where the headline comes from
Registered primaries are **safety/PK, not weight**. 44-week: placebo-adjusted **−16.3% (180mg)**
/ −16.0% (240mg); **placebo GAINED +1.1%** (vs −0.8% in the powered study — ~2 points of the
headline is placebo divergence). **Per-arm N never disclosed anywhere** (~20/arm inferred, not
verified; hasResults: false). The superiority claim runs off the smaller, softer-endpointed
study; the powered study's −11.3% is the honest anchor.

### Tolerability fix (2.5 mg start — the Phase 3 regimen)
Body Comp interim (~20wk median): 3.4% AE discontinuation. OLE interim (~20wk): **2.0%**;
prior-active at 120mg: **16.2% (40.5 lbs) at 56 weeks, no plateau**. ACCESS II ≥120mg wk28–44:
1 (3.7%) discontinuation. **All interim, immature windows** — see §3 trap.

### Phase 3 ACCOMPLISH — one silent change
Arms 45/90/180mg + placebo, 2.5mg start, wk-76 primary, 71 US sites. Dec-25 and Mar-26 releases
both said "up to 240 mg"; actual Phase 3 tops at **180 mg** — disclosed plainly in August, but
the 240mg upside-optionality framing quietly retired. The two lower Ph3 doses (45/90) delivered
−8.2/−9.8% — below orforglipron's approved top dose.

## 2. THE BIOLOGY, MECHANISTICALLY

**Company claim:** aleniglipron "designed to be a biased GPCR agonist, selectively activating
G-protein signaling." **Verification: UNVERIFIABLE — zero PubMed hits for GSBR-1290**; no
published cAMP/β-arrestin bias factor, residence time, or binding data. Company assertion only.

- **Why bias is sought:** GLP-1R couples Gs (cAMP → satiety/insulinotropy) and β-arrestin
  (internalization/desensitization). The design thesis: less arrestin = sustained surface
  receptor + possibly decoupled nausea. **ACCESS does not support the decoupling** — nausea
  65–71%, vomiting 32–45% are at/above class high end.
- **Efficacy ceiling:** observed ~−12% absolute at 36wk (powered), ~−15/−16% at 44–56wk in
  small/open-label settings, no plateau. Orals cluster low-to-mid teens; injectable tirzepatide/
  retatrutide a tier above. Ceiling not yet demonstrably broken.
- **Hepatotoxicity — molecule-specific, not class:** danuglipron (2x/day) died on ALT; TERN-601
  died on adjudicated DILI; **FOUNDAYO's label carries no hepatic warning in §5 at all**;
  aleniglipron clean at n>625. Two structurally distinct molecules cleared the bar danuglipron
  failed. Class hypothesis not supported.
- **Food effect — settled competitive fact, open GPCR question:** FOUNDAYO "with or without
  food"; peptide orals require fasting + water limits. Aleniglipron as a small molecule should
  land in the FOUNDAYO camp — **but no aleniglipron food-effect data has ever been disclosed**,
  and the Ph3 registry text doesn't specify. The ACCG-2671 Ph1 explicitly includes food effect;
  the lead program never reported one. Cheap management question.
- **What amylin adds:** hindbrain/area-postrema satiation on a partly distinct pathway; hoped
  additive loss, milder aversion, lean-mass preservation. **None verified for ACCG-2671 —
  no human data.** Body Comp study (lean mass secondary, Q4-26) is the first real lean datum,
  and it's for aleniglipron alone.

**Phase 3 headline should print below Phase 2 for estimand reasons alone.**

**THE TOLERABILITY-WINDOW TRAP:** 2.0–3.4% at ~20wk median interim vs completed 72-week label
rates. Matched basis (5mg/36wk): aleniglipron 10.4% sits ON TOP of FOUNDAYO's 72-week 8–10%,
nausea ~2×, GI +10 points. The differentiation claim has no primary support yet.

**External anchor:** Kamrul-Hasan et al. NMA (Obes Sci Pract 2026;12(4):e70181, 9 RCTs,
N=5,766): oral semaglutide **−11.6%**, orforglipron **−11.8%** placebo-adjusted. ACCESS's
−11.3% is squarely in the pack. Only the N=85 study separates aleniglipron.
