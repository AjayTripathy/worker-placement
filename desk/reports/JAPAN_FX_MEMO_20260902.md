# Japan Sleeve FX Decision Memo — 2026-09-02

**PROPOSED — NOT DECIDED. Principal decides.**

## The exposure

The Japan sleeve is ~¥13.0M (~$88k at ~147) across 14 names, fully **unhedged**. Every yen of
stock-picking alpha rides USDJPY: a 10% yen depreciation erases ~$8.8k of USD value regardless
of how the stocks do. The four new sweep candidates (8135/1810/6118/8795), if courted into
positions, take the sleeve toward ~$110–130k — the FX question stops being noise around then.

## The case for staying unhedged (recommended)

1. **The steepener thesis and the yen are the same trade.** The sleeve's newest legs (T&D, the
   capital-return inflections) are bets that Japanese rates normalize upward. Rising JGB yields
   compress the US–Japan rate differential — the single biggest structural driver of yen
   weakness since 2022. If the thesis works, the yen strengthens *with* it; hedging would pay
   away the currency leg of our own macro view.
2. **Hedging costs the differential.** A USDJPY forward/futures hedge earns the buyer the rate
   differential — as the *yen borrower* we would pay roughly the Fed-funds-minus-BoJ spread,
   ~3.5–4%/yr at current policy rates, against a sleeve whose dividend yield is ~2–3%. The
   hedge would consume more carry than the sleeve produces.
3. **Sizing**: at ~9% of the book, a ±10% currency move is ±0.9% of NAV — real but inside the
   band of single-name noise we accept daily.

## The case for hedging (the honest other side)

- If US yields keep ripping while the BoJ stays timid, the differential *widens* and the yen
  can fall hard even as JGB yields rise (2022–24 precedent: JGB yields rose AND the yen fell
  to 160+). The steepener-helps-yen argument assumes relative, not absolute, normalization.
- A US-recession scenario that cuts Fed rates would strengthen the yen violently — but that
  scenario also *helps* the unhedged position, so it argues for staying unhedged, not against.

## Recommendation

**Stay unhedged while the sleeve is <15% of the book, and set a tripwire instead of a hedge:**
if USDJPY closes above 160 (yen breaking down despite the steepener), bring the question back
as a court item with the 2022-24 precedent as the frame. Cost of this policy: zero carry paid;
risk accepted: ~±1% of NAV per ±10% currency move. Revisit automatically if the sleeve crosses
15% of NAV or the BoJ formally caps yields (YCC-redux would break the steepener-yen link).
