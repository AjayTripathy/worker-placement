02 / 12The setup — rank 1 of 502, and why that ranking lied

The generator's pitch: 39% net cash, 4.5x EBIT, one analyst

SFPI came out of the Euronext shelf as its top-scored name of 502: a coverage orphan (1 analyst, no published target) at 4.5x EV/EBIT with €74.8M of net cash against a €192M cap — the archetypal too-small-to-cover bargain. Revenue €625M across four industrial divisions (Access/DOM Security, Air/NEU-JKF, Heat, Home), P/B 0.70, FCF yield screened at 16%.

The court's job was to disconfirm first. It found the pitch's two load-bearing numbers were both materially wrong — and then found something real underneath them anyway. Both corrections and the find are below; the deck leads with the corrections because the corrections are the credibility.

Central question as courted: archetypal coverage-orphan bargain, or a melting family-controlled value trap where the cash never reaches minorities? Answer: neither — and the framing itself was the trap. It is a fairly-priced, honestly-run roll-up that destroyed margin while buying revenue, now showing a primary-confirmed cost-out inflection the tape refuses to price.

04 / 12Refutation 2 — margin destruction by acquisition

Revenue +10% since 2021. Operating profit −41%.

The mechanism has a name: WO&WO. €29M paid in August 2022; goodwill (€18.9M) written to zero inside two years; the Munich arbitration to claw back the price lost in January 2025 (plus €1.1M of costs); the subsidiary recapitalized by board minute in 2024 and 2025 — quantum of the recap not found in any filing. The €141M cash pile sits with the same allocator. That is why governance is the risk that has to be priced — the open question was only which instrument prices it (slide 8).

08 / 12The adjudication — one risk, counted three times

The court's gate-only entry was overturned for a taxonomy violation

The court ruled: do not initiate above €1.88. The fable blue team (the standing type-II adversary on every gate verdict) found the structure internally inconsistent and the principal accepted the correction in full:

  • The pension correction is already in the FV (€2.64 uses the adjusted €35.3M net cash). The governance risk is already charged a second time inside the multiple (6.75x vs 6–9x comps). Gating on it a third time through a below-spot price gate is triple-counting one risk through the wrong instrument — governance is business risk, and business risk takes a SIZE CUT, never a price gate (response taxonomy, v1.1).
  • The gate could not fill on thesis-friendly tape. Regime-conditioned P(touch €1.88 before the September catalyst) ≈ 0.10; P(touch €1.80) ≈ 0. With zero scheduled news before Sep-23, a sub-€1.90 August tape is noise or beta — not premise-death. The gate fills mainly in the world where the thesis is dying.
  • Regret math (court's own scenario table): gate-only ≈ $475 expected forgone versus ≈ $175 expected loss avoided — regret-negative ~2.7:1. The court's 30% bear weight and its own entry structure contradicted each other; holding the tape's 65–70% bear weight instead would have required a PASS, not a WATCH. It cannot hold both.

The adjudicated principle, now a standing lesson: when a court verifies fairness and bounds the tails, RP_FAIR is ownable at fair price, sleeve-capped — an entry plan that is exclusively below-spot price-gated contradicts the classification it sits next to.
