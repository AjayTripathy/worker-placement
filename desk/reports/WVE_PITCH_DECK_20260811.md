# PITCH — WVE — PROPOSED VERDICT (one line; label clearly "PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION")

**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION: REJECT — no position. Wave's FDA meeting is a catalyst whose favorable branch mostly enriches a stronger competitor (Beam). Watch with three reopen triggers; do not buy the dip.**

## What we believe

Wave Life Sciences (~$1.00B market cap, ~$510M enterprise value after $490.6M cash) is a genetic-medicines company whose nearest catalyst is an FDA meeting, scheduled for "end of summer 2026," about a possible accelerated-approval path for WVE-006, its RNA-editing treatment for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (AATD).

We believe the trade fails on competitive position, and that the failure is close to symmetric across the catalyst's outcomes:

- **Wave is not the best drug on the measure that matters.** Wave's own May 2026 data show its chronic dosing produces about 7.2–7.7 µM of functional (M-type) AAT protein. Beam Therapeutics' BEAM-302 — a one-time gene-editing treatment — produces roughly 15.1 µM functional protein (94% of 16.1 µM total), rising toward ~30 µM during infection, and Beam dosed its first pivotal-trial patient in August 2026. Beam also corrects the disease-causing protein, so the "only Wave treats both lung and liver" differentiator is false — Beam does both, at roughly twice the level, from a single dose. Primary source for Wave's own numbers: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/18/3297034/0/en/Wave-Life-Sciences-Announces-Positive-Update-on-RestorAATion-2-Trial-WVE-006-GalNAc-RNA-Editing-Achieves-MZ-Like-Phenotype-Across-Both-Biweekly-and-Monthly-Dosing.html
- **A win at the FDA is a public good.** If the agency blesses blood AAT levels as an approvable surrogate endpoint, that blessing applies to everyone — and it helps Beam and Sanofi (whose INBRX-101 already met all endpoints in Phase 2 on functional AAT, targeting approval by mid-2027) more than it helps Wave, because they clear the commonly cited ~11 µM protective threshold on functional protein and Wave does not. If the agency balks, Wave faces a long conventional Phase 3 it would need to finance.
- **The informed party already passed.** GSK, Wave's respiratory partner with full data access, returned WVE-006's rights in February 2026 — months before this "asymmetric" catalyst. That reads as an informed pass, not as Wave "regaining upside" (the broader GSK collaboration continues, so this was asset-specific).
- **Any good news likely meets a raise.** The company burns ~$56M per quarter, losses are widening, and a live shelf registration (carried over in the August 10 redomiciliation filings) means a favorable FDA readout is likely met with an equity offering that caps the pop.

Red bench conviction on the kill: 7/10. Blue bench sustained it at 8/10. Neither bench found the bull's starter position earned.

## What the market believes

The stock is at $5.175, down 76% from its 52-week high of $21.73 and sitting **3% above its 52-week low** — this is a knife, not a bounce. At this price the market is paying about $510M above cash for the entire pipeline: it has largely written off near-term AATD leadership, is skeptical of the DMD program (the company itself softened from "intends to file an NDA in 2026" to "exploring partnerships in advance of filing"), and is aware Beam's data landed harder. Some option value for the FDA meeting is presumably still embedded, since the meeting has been public since roughly February.

## Why we might have edge

Honestly: not much, and the court concluded we don't have enough to act. The candidate edges the bull case offered were:

1. **A dated, publicly decidable binary** — refuted. The "catalyst" is a granted meeting request; the date is non-public, minutes arrive ~30 days later to the company only, and disclosure timing is company-controlled (likely narrated at a fall investor day). Verbatim language checked in the Q2 press release: https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/wave-life-sciences-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-and-provides-business-update
2. **Cheapness** — partially real. Both original briefs used a stale $680M enterprise value; at the pack's tape it's ~$510M, about 25% cheaper than either bench argued from. That doesn't flip the competitive findings, but it means the option costs less than the court's rhetoric implied. This is the honest residue of the case and the reason for a watch list entry rather than a tombstone.
3. **One pro-bull nuance the red bench omitted** (credit to blue): Beam dosing a pivotal cohort implies the FDA has already accepted an AAT-level registrational endpoint *for someone*. So the disaster branch ("surrogate rejected, decade of Phase 3") is less likely than red framed. The problem is that the benign branch crowns Beam, not Wave.

## Why it might be priced in

The 76% drawdown suggests the market has already done much of our work: Beam's superior data (March 2026), the GSK handback (February 2026), and the DMD softening are all public and old. What's arguably *not* fully priced is the reflexive financing (good news → raise → capped upside) — but that cuts against buying, not for it. Conversely, the FDA meeting is public and some hope premium likely remains in the $510M; a bad or ambiguous readout still has room to hurt.

## PROPOSED ENTRY BANDS

**The court says no entry at any price today** — the objection is competitive, not valuational, so there is no price that fixes it. Per doctrine, business-risk findings cut size (here, to zero), and a new 52-week low triggers re-review, never an automatic buy. Reopen bands and conditions, from the benches' own math:

- **Reopen trigger A (data):** Wave multidose data (400/600mg) showing functional M-AAT **≥ 11 µM**. This is the single number that un-kills the thesis. Re-court immediately on the PR, before modeling.
- **Reopen trigger B (regulatory):** FDA meeting minutes or company disclosure showing the agency will accept **total** serum AAT (Z-fraction included) as registrational for chronic dosing. This would grade Wave on a curve it can pass.
- **Reopen trigger C (validation):** A real-economics AATD or DMD partnership post-GSK-handback — an informed party re-underwriting the asset.
- **Price band 1 — $5.02 (below):** new 52-week low. Cause-check only (what broke, or is this the pre-meeting bleed?). Not a buy signal.
- **Price band 2 — ~$2.55 (below):** price ≈ net cash per share ($490.6M ÷ 193.3M shares = $2.54). The pipeline would be priced at zero; mandatory re-court on option-cheapness grounds even with no trigger A–C.
- **Sizing if a reopen trigger fires:** the court set none — a reopened case goes back through the full red/blue process before any size is proposed. Blue explicitly flagged that the corrected ~$510M EV "tightens the sizing question a future re-court should ask."
- **Catalyst dates:** FDA meeting — "end of summer 2026," exact date non-public, minutes ~30 days after; Q3 earnings ~2026-11-09 (yfinance-derived, **unconfirmed** — no company PR names it); Beam pivotal cohort enrolling now (readout timing unannounced).
