## What we believe

Sigma Lithium runs one asset — Grota do Cirilo in Minas Gerais, Brazil. On September 5-ish a first-instance court in Teófilo Otoni, a town more than 300km from the mine, issued a preliminary order suspending the company's environmental licenses, barring new ones, and setting a daily fine. Three days later the company filed a 6-K saying, in substance, that it had received no legal communication, that mining and industrial activity continue, and that it still expects 240,000t of concentrate within 12 months and 330,000t in FY27 ([SEC 6-K, 2026-09-08, EX-99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001848309/000129281426004491/ex99-1.htm)).

What we actually believe, stated plainly:

1. **The daily fine is not a near-term cash event.** The company's own primary text says it "would only be due only in the event the charges resulted in a final negative ruling," with appeals through the Supreme Tribunals, and that similar cases take multiple years. A fine can *accrue* and still not be *enforceable*. Both benches agreed on this. It is the single best-supported fact in the file.
2. **The company is thin on cash relative to a halt.** $16.7M cash at 6/30/26 against roughly $8–14M of EBITDA lost per halted month. That is under two months of cover. Even after Red corrected the original brief's overstated halt cost, this conclusion did not change sign.
3. **The stock is not pricing a dead mine, but it is not pricing a perfect one either.** At the benches' own inputs — 330kt nameplate, $668/t all-in cash cost, 111.4M shares, ~$100M net debt, 6x EBITDA — today's $11.05 embeds about **$1,340/t** realized concentrate price at full nameplate. Last disclosed realized was $2,089/t. That is roughly a one-third haircut to last-reported unit economics.
4. **But our own expected value at $11.05 is below the price.** Our probability-weighted fair value is **$8.26** (table below). The gap between "the tape has haircut something real" and "we get paid to own it" is where this pitch dies. We are not long.

## What the market believes

The market believes two things at once, and they don't fit together.

The tape has taken the stock down 54.7% from its 52-week high — but it is still up **140% off the 52-week low**, with a 26-week high of $23.65. That is a giveback inside a large advance, not a capitulation.

The sell-side, meanwhile, carries a **consensus Sell rating with a $16.75 price target and a $13.00–$20.50 range**. The *low* end of the street's range sits 18% above the tape. A Sell rating attached to a target 52% above the price is a stale target — the external anchor has not been marked to the injunction at all. (Source is an aggregator, UNVERIFIED at both benches.)

The market also believes the news is old news. Every hard fact in the kill case — the order, the license suspension, the new-license bar, the fine, the 2.7km-vs-8km consultation-radius finding, the court-appointed georeferencing expert — was on the wires before our evidence pack was built. Red tagged this CONSENSUS and honorably refused to let it carry a verdict by itself.

## Why we might have edge

- **The street's anchor is unmarked and gradeable.** We can name the mechanism precisely: the street prices 330kt with zero consent-defect discount. That is a specific, checkable disagreement, not a vibe. Blue restored court-worthiness to ~6/10 on this basis.
- **The inventory-buffer insight (Blue's novel finding).** 2Q26 was $55M revenue on 35,400t produced. At the disclosed $2,089/t realized, tonnes *sold* were only ~26.3kt — a **~9.1kt inventory build, 25.6% of quarterly output, roughly three weeks of shipments.** That means revenue, shipments, and the guided "$60M of cash inflows during the third quarter" can all print completely normally *while the pit is dark*. Both original briefs proposed the early-October production PR as the resolving datum without realizing it is shipment-lagged. If we are right about this and the market is not, we get an extra ~3 weeks of warning before consensus updates.
- **The jurisdictional axis nobody has priced (Blue's finding B/C).** If the August TAC agreement with Minas Gerais state authorities is judicially homologated, a preliminary order from an arguably non-competent venue 300km away has a weak claim on it. A venue-competence challenge is plausibly the fastest, highest-probability vacatur path. Neither bench weighted it. Neither, apparently, has the street.
- **One independent physical check exists that literally nobody has run.** Sentinel-2 10m imagery over the pit, waste dumps, and tailings filter-cake stack, differenced between 2026-09-05 and early October, answers "is the pit moving?" without asking either the company or the court. It is cheap, weekly, and pre-print.

- **Our EV is negative at the tape.** $8.26 vs $11.05. If the distribution below is roughly right, the market is already paying more than the odds justify. That is the honest read, and it is the reason this deck proposes nothing.

A process defect of that kind is not curable by paying a fine and is not visible in tonnage, cash, imagery, or the tape.
