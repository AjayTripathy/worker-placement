# PITCH — BAVA.CO — VERDICT

ADJUDICATED  — HOLD the existing starter-half at current size; no buying at DKK 209.40; rungs derived but nothing placed; the position is fair-to-modestly-rich on our own best numbers, not cheap.

---

## What we believe

Bavarian Nordic is a Danish vaccine maker that earns money two ways. One is travel vaccines sold through clinics (rabies, tick-borne encephalitis, typhoid, and a newly launched chikungunya shot). The other is selling smallpox/mpox vaccine into government emergency stockpiles — mostly the US and Europe. The first is steady and growing. The second is lumpy: a government either signs a big order in a given year or it doesn't.

Our view, after both sides argued it out:

- The company earns roughly DKK 1.6–1.7 billion a year in operating profit before one-offs, and has done so three years running (2024: 1.60bn actual; 2025: 1.73bn actual; 2026: 1.71bn now guided). That is a real, delivered earnings level, not a projection.
- Revenue is falling about 9% this year, but profit is flat. The decline is entirely in the lumpy government-stockpile business; the travel business is growing. A buyer of the earnings stream should not care much about that particular revenue decline.
- Put those earnings on a modest 8.5x multiple, add the cash left after buybacks, and you get roughly DKK 210 a share in the normal case. Weighted against a downside case (DKK ~138) and a smaller chance of a renewed takeover at DKK 250, fair value lands near **DKK 199**.
- The stock is DKK 209.40. So we think it is worth roughly what it costs, maybe slightly less. That justifies holding what we own and justifies buying nothing.

We do **not** believe there is a cheap-stock argument here today. What we own, we own because the earnings base is more durable than the falling revenue line suggests — not because of a discount.

## What the market believes

The market believes broadly what we believe, and that is itself an important finding.

The company published upgraded guidance on 21 August 2026 (revenue DKK 5,700m, ~30% margin). The consensus figure circulating afterwards was revenue DKK 5,707m and a 30% margin — effectively identical. The stock rose 9.1% on the day, which tells us the *print* was a surprise even if the *post-print* consensus is not.

The market also carries a specific piece of history: in autumn 2025 a private-equity consortium (Nordic Capital and Permira, through a vehicle called Innosera) bid for the whole company, raised its price from DKK 233 to DKK 250, declared that "best and final," failed to get enough shareholders to accept, and walked away on 5 November 2025 ([offer-withdrawal wire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/11/06/3182703/0/en/consortium-consisting-of-nordic-capital-and-permira-announces-preliminary-result-of-the-takeover-offer-to-shareholders-of-bavarian-nordic-and-that-the-offer-is-withdrawn-and-will-n.html)). A meaningful slice of today's shareholder register is people who bought expecting that deal and are still holding. Published analyst targets sit around DKK 267.5 with a "Strong Buy" tilt — i.e. the street is more bullish than we are.

The stock is at a 26-week high, 22% above its 52-week low and 14.5% below its 52-week high. Nothing about the tape says "unloved."

## Why we might have edge

Three honest candidates, in descending order of strength:

1. **We checked the seasonality; most write-ups did not.** The obvious bear reading of today's guidance is: the company banked DKK 1,090m of profit in the first half at a 35% margin, and guiding to DKK 1,710m for the year means the second half only earns ~DKK 620m at a ~24% margin — a collapse. We pulled last year's audited numbers and computed the same figure: the second half of 2025 implied 23.8%. This year implies 23.8%. It is the same number. This company always loads research, launch and depreciation costs into the back half. Anyone treating that as new bad news is misreading a recurring seasonal pattern.

2. **We think the "only DKK 800m secured for 2027" scare is a calendar artifact.** The August report always discloses next year's contracted revenue thinly, because government stockpile orders book on roughly a two-quarter lead. Last August's report did not even quantify 2026 — and 2026 landed at ~DKK 2,500m of stockpile revenue with DKK 2,300m secured. Comparing "secured for next year" against "secured for this year" at mid-year measures the calendar, not demand.

3. **The board's own behaviour disagrees with the bear case.** Months after rejecting DKK 250 a share, the board authorised DKK 1.25bn of buybacks with the shares cancelled. A board that just ran a takeover defence buying back stock in size is not a board that thinks fair value is DKK 184.

Note what edge we are *not* claiming: we have no differentiated 2026 forecast. We agree with the street on this year down to the decimal.

## Why it might be priced in

Stated plainly, because much of it probably is:

- **We match consensus on 2026.** The entire disagreement is about 2027, and 2027 is genuinely unknowable until the next stockpile orders land. Neither bench could verify it.
- **The travel-vaccine strength is partly a competitor's problem, not our skill.** Rabies sales +55% and tick-borne encephalitis +58% coincide with a Sanofi supply shortage; our own rabies product only came back on line in May 2026, so part of the growth is restocking. The street already models this reversing — consensus second-half travel revenue is *below* the first half.
- **The takeover story cuts against us more than for us.** DKK 250 is the price at which the only known buyer said "this is our final number" and then left. Fundamentals since are worse on the revenue line. Treating DKK 250 as a floor under the stock is backwards; it is a demonstrated ceiling for that buyer.
- **The tape offers no dislocation to exploit.** Above the 26-week high, above our own fair value, held by a post-deal shareholder base, with sell-side targets above us.
- **Cash is going out the door.** Net cash fell from DKK 3.3bn to DKK 2.3bn in six months; operating cash flow was **negative DKK 401m** in the first half against **positive DKK 883m** a year earlier — a DKK 1,284m swing. Buying back DKK 750m of stock at 209 while the downside case is 140 destroys about DKK 3 a share if the downside case is the one that happens.

## PROPOSED ENTRY BANDS

**The courts say no entry.** Blue and red agree on this independently: the price is above our fair value, above the top of the add band, and at a 26-week high. There is no buy here.

One procedural gap, stated plainly: the adjudication record instructs that the reopen gates be reproduced verbatim, **but the reopen-gates block in that record is empty**, and the adjudication carries no recommendation or conviction value. The bands below are therefore reconstructed from the two benches' own arithmetic, not copied from the adjudication. They should be treated as provisional until the adjudication block is filled in.

**Reopen bands (reconstructed, all prices in DKK):**

- **No add above 203.** That is the top of the previously derived add band. Today at 209.40 we are above it.
- **Add band 172–203, and entry inside it is necessary but not sufficient.** Both benches were explicit: the third add-gate firing does not on its own authorise a purchase. A price inside the band plus at least one of the confirmations below is the bar.
- **Meaningful add only below 184** — the red bench's weighted fair value. Between 184 and 203 the stock is merely not-expensive; below 184 it is cheap on the more sceptical of our two trees.
- **Downside reference 138–139.** Both benches' bear case, built on 2027 stockpile profit near DKK 1.2bn at a 7x multiple. This is the level to size against, not a target.
- **First trim 233.** The pre-raise offer price, about 1.17x our fair value. At that level the takeover option is fully paid for.
- **Second trim 260.** Deliberately set above the failed 250 best-and-final so that a resting sell order cannot signal anything to an approaching buyer. Red's finding that 250 is a ceiling makes this rung stronger, not weaker.
- **Sizing.** Hold the existing starter-half; do not increase. Any future add is a half-rung at most, and only from inside the band with a confirmation in hand.
- **Staging.** Per the principal's 21 August 2026 amendment: **derive only, stage nothing** until the long-term holding clock matures around July 2027. Hard-event sells and kill-trigger sells are exempt from that and execute regardless of tax position.

**Kill triggers (these sell, tax-blind):**
1. Q3 operating cash flow not at least +DKK 600m (blue's threshold; red wanted +800m). The first half was −401m; if that does not reverse, the delivery-phasing explanation is dead and it is a demand problem.
2. Secured 2027 stockpile revenue still below DKK 1,500m at the Q3 report.
3. A named-price re-offer rumour — which cuts both ways and requires a live decision, not an automatic hold.

**Catalyst dates:**
- **Q3 2026 interim report** — the binding event. Date **not confirmed by any company release we have**; historically early November. Must be confirmed from the company's financial calendar before any sensor is armed against it.
- **~February 2027** — the earliest date US federal award data would surface an early-2027 stockpile obligation, given the roughly 98-day reporting lag.
- **5 November 2026** — one year from the withdrawn offer. Blue asserted the Danish re-bid restriction "has long since run"; that is unverified and should be checked against the Danish takeover rules rather than assumed.
- **~July 2027** — long-term holding clock on the existing shares.
