02 / 12The frame — and a framing the court killed

The winner signature is here. The "quiet quality" label is not.

The quality-drawdown screen was built from an autopsy of the book's own winners — quality businesses bought 40–77% below their own three-year highs while their estimates held or rose (HubSpot −77%, monday.com −77%, Cognizant −53%, SAP −48% at entry; median −46%). ISRG at −39% from its high, with estimates rising, fits the signature almost exactly.

What it does not fit is the handoff's "quiet quality — least dramatic" tier. The court's first finding was that this is the most violent large-cap tape in the cohort: a double top with two distinct crashes, a −15.1% single earnings week on 18.2M shares, and a +22.5% week a year earlier. The reaction function is ±15–22% per print. Entry structure has to respect that — which is why this deck ends in a ladder and a print-date rule, not a market order.

One number to hold through the deck: price −39.1% · forward EPS +35.6% · multiple −55.1%. The entire drawdown is multiple, and the multiple moved against rising earnings.

03 / 12Cause check — a double top, dated

Two crashes, four causes, all disclosed

Today's +6.25% is unexplained. XLV was flat and MDT +1.1%, so it is not sector beta; no 13D exists; HSBC downgraded the same morning. The court refuses to chase a move it cannot name — the ladder below starts under it, and resolving the cause is a precondition for the first tranche.

The margin and opex revisions alone are worth roughly +$209M pre-tax ≈ +$0.46/share of FY26 earnings power — delivered during the exact window the stock halved its multiple. Actuals corroborate: GAAP gross margin 66.3% → 67.8%, GAAP operating margin 30.5% → 33.6% (+310bp), H1 operating cash flow +52%, installed base +12%, and da Vinci 5 placements 246 vs 180 (+37%) — the flagship rollout is accelerating, not stalling.

The honest counterweight, kept at full strength: the procedure deceleration (18% → guided ~14.5%) is real and partly structural — penetration maturity plus a permanent GLP-1 drag on bariatric volumes, confirmed from two independent filings (ISRG's own 10-Q and Medtronic's FY26 10-K). ISRG disclosed it cleanly and the market repriced it. Re-detecting honestly disclosed bad news is not alpha — which is why this is classed RP_FAIR with a skew overlay, not an edge.

05 / 12The skew — a statute and a stay, both reads

Guidance assumes a tariff its own 10-Q says was scheduled to sunset in July

The July-16 earnings release states the gross-margin guide "assumes such tariffs remain in place through the end of the year" — embedding ~1.0% of revenue ≈ $122M of cost. The 10-Q filed five days later states the replacement Section 122 tariff "took effect on February 24, 2026, and will remain in effect for 150 days, the maximum period that Section 122 permits without congressional action." February 24 + 150 days = 2026-07-24. If the tariff lapsed on schedule, roughly $53M pre-tax (~$0.12/share, ~+100bp of H2 gross-margin run rate) comes back, plus an unbooked IEEPA refund contingency.

Both reads, honestly

  • The bull read: a conservative-direction divergence between the marketed guide and the company's own filed statute — clean on honesty, positive on estimates. The guide-raise would be near-mechanical in October.
  • The red-team read (which cut the call from 0.60 to 0.55): management guided "through year-end" eight days before the sunset date printed in its own 10-Q. ISRG's counsel did not forget the statute. The parsimonious explanation is informed caution — the Federal Circuit extended its stay on 2026-06-11, keeping the 10% tariffs in place pending proceedings, and Section 232/301 substitution is the administration's stated intent. Whether Congress acted by July 24 is UNVERIFIED.

A statutory date printed in a 10-Q covered by fifteen analysts is not an informational moat. The court's original "small verified EDGE" label was struck; what remains is positive skew on a public fact with a confounded resolution — worth owning at fair price, not worth paying an edge premium for.
