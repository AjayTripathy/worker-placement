## 1. WHAT THE RESIDUAL ASSET IS
**To Versant:** MS NOW (ex-MSNBC), CNBC, USA Network, Golf Channel, E!, SYFY, Oxygen True Crime; digital
= Fandango, Rotten Tomatoes, GolfNow, GolfPass, SportsEngine (most of which was sold in Q2-26). Sports
carried across: WWE SmackDown, NASCAR, WNBA, Premier League, PAC-12, USGA to 2032.

**Comcast kept:** NBC broadcast, **Bravo**, and **Peacock** — and Versant's own risk factors concede the
cost: its "negotiating position with MVPDs may be weakened without the ability to sell our programming
along with NBCU's broadcast television networks, the Bravo cable network and Peacock" (10-K Item 1A).
**CNBC is licensed, not owned** — held under a 5-year trademark licence from Comcast.

So Versant is the declining linear-cable half, sold separately, with weaker carriage leverage than it had
inside NBCU. Comcast harvested **$2.25B of cash** on the way out.

## 4. THE STRUCTURAL OBJECTIONS
1. **Dual-class control.** 377,775 Class B shares carry 15 votes each = **33⅓% of voting power, held
   entirely by Brian L. Roberts** (10-K Item 1A). A control block held by the former parent's chairman,
   in a company Comcast just extracted $2.25B from, with no economic stake to match.
2. **Comcast concentration is now invisible.** Sales to Comcast as a related party were **$1.34B / $1.26B
   / $928M in 2023/24/25 — 18.0% / 17.8% / 13.9% of revenue.** Post-separation "transactions with Comcast
   are not considered related party transactions" (10-Q Note 10), so **Versant no longer discloses
   Comcast-customer revenue at all.** A ~14–18% distributor concentration vanished from the filings on a
   technicality. That is a genuine disclosure regression, and worth a REVIEW flag.
3. **Zero carriage disclosure.** The strings Charter, DirecTV, YouTube TV and Dish appear **nowhere** in
   the 10-K or 10-Q. No renewal dates, no counterparties, no 10%-customer table. For a business whose
   entire value is affiliate fees, the single most important contract set is undisclosed.
4. **The Comcast commercial agreements cost real money**: the standalone-EBITDA bridge quantifies
   **$186M (2025) / $215M (2024)** of "incremental costs of commercial agreements with Comcast."
5. **Capital allocation is drifting from the melting-ice-cube playbook** — $530M for Full Swing Golf
   (signed 07-06-26). Cash going into acquisitions rather than out to holders is the wrong direction here.

  and −17% ratings in 2025**, across networks reaching 49–60M households. That is the industry base, and
  it applies directly to the Bravo/NBC carriage economics **Comcast retained**.
- **Q1-26 linear distribution revenue declined −7.3%, accelerating, despite contractual rate escalators.**
  Rate increases are no longer offsetting volume.
- **CMCSA's reported Media-segment growth post-2026-01-02 is not comparable to prior periods** — roughly
  $6.7B of revenue and $2.4B of EBITDA left the segment. Any CMCSA "Media improvement" narrative must be
  restated for this.
- Comcast retains ~2 years of commercial entanglement: NBCU sells Versant's domestic linear ad inventory,
  two-way sports time-purchase agreements, content and trademark licences, and TSA services.
- Comcast harvested $2.25B cash and shed a $2.4B-EBITDA declining asset — read as a **positive** for
  CMCSA's mix and a confirmation that management sees the linear base as terminal.

## 8. VERDICT — **PASS 3/10**

At 3.0x EBITDA with 0.8x leverage, a 4.1% dividend, a $1B buyback and a ~17–21% normalised FCF yield,
VSNT is not expensive and is not a fraud — it is honestly disclosed decline. But every element of the
court's frame fails: the business is secularly shrinking with the core line *accelerating* downward, the
+34% bounce off a 117-session-old low is late, capital is leaking into acquisitions rather than returns,
the controlling vote sits with the former parent's chairman, and the affiliate contracts that determine
the entire terminal value are undisclosed.

Our fair value is **+2.1% from spot — statistically indistinguishable from fairly priced.** Per doctrine
(*fairly-paid risk is ownable, no edge required*) this could in principle be a RISK_PREMIUM carry
position. It fails that bar too: the carry is a 4.1% dividend against an asset whose cash flows decline
5–8% a year, and the tail (terminal-value repricing) is neither bounded nor cheap to hedge.

**No entry at spot. Re-open below $30–32** (≈2.4x EBITDA, ≈5% dividend yield, near the Q1 buyback VWAP
of $37.12 less a margin of safety) where the FCF yield does the work regardless of the decline rate.
**If entered, RISK_PREMIUM classification, 0.4% cap, carry explicitly labelled — never an edge claim.**
