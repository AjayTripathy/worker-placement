## 3. THE RULING: DE-RATE, AND THE DE-RATE IS CORRECT

Estimate trajectory (the HUBS/CTSH/SAP test): revenue guide mid **+$300M (+9.1%)**, adj EBITDA guide mid
**+$2.5M (+0.8%)**, adj net income guide mid **+$2.5M**. Estimates are flat-to-up. Multiple:

- pre-print $27.86 → cap $2,813M + PF net debt $753M = EV $3,566M / $297.5M mid = **11.99x**
- post-print $21.95 → cap $2,216M + $753M = EV $2,969M / $297.5M = **9.98x**

So: **two turns of multiple came out on a guidance raise.** Formally a de-rate, not a derailment.

But the court's job is to ask *whether the de-rate was wrong*, and here it was not. What the market
repriced is the **quality of the revenue**, and the primary sources support it:

- +$411M of H1 revenue produced **exactly zero** incremental operating income (B1).
- The company raised revenue guidance $300M for $10M of gross profit — a **3.3% incremental margin** (B3).
- The implied full-year base margin guide was **cut 40bps** inside a release headlined "RAISES GUIDANCE" (B2).
- 77% of the book is unit- or fixed-price with **annually-reset, capped** escalators; the 10-Q concedes
  actual costs can exceed the caps (B7). The $6M fuel item is not contractually recoverable inside FY26,
  and the guide assumes elevated fuel persists only "through the third quarter."
- Revenue mix is shifting into the **lowest-margin segment** (US Gas 46.5% → 50.9% of revenue at 5.95%
  base GPM) and into **bid** work (17.6% → 25.0% of revenue since FY23).

This is a takeaway-vs-data divergence — the marketed takeaway ("record revenue, +44% adjusted net income,
guidance RAISED") inverts the emphasis on data that show flat operating profit, a cut margin guide, and
EPS growth financed by a 14% share issuance. It is a **divergence, not a concealment**: every datum is in
the release and the 10-Q, and the market found all of it the same session. **Disclosed and instantly
priced ⇒ no honesty alpha here.** Cataloguing it as an anti-masking observation: the company chose the
flattering frame but did not hide the numbers, and a -21.5% same-day move is the market grading the frame.

**The bull leg the seed leaned on does not survive contact with the history.** "H2 9.0% vs H1 6.3%" is not
the right comparison. Q1 is the trough; Q2-Q4 normally blend to 9.3-10.3%. Q2-26 base GPM printed 7.90%
against a 8.90% prior year — a 100bps miss **in the first quarter of the normal band**. The guide now needs
H2 to snap straight back to ~9.0% (and ≥8.55% just to hold the low end), i.e. the y/y decline must stop and
reverse in one quarter, with an acquisition integrating, fuel assumed to normalise in Q4, and a residual
-55bps of US Gas deterioration that management has not explained (B8).
