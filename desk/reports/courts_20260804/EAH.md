## Decisive tests
**(1) Single-product concentration — CONFIRMED at 92%, no erosion visible.** AR2025 note 3: **Aivlosin
£72,914k of £79,596k = 91.6%** (FY24 92.2%). Protection is *not* composition-of-matter — the product is >20
years old; the moat is per-territory marketing authorisations, formulation patents, trademarks, technical
service. The company's **own risk register rates "Potential threat from generic producers" HIGH likelihood /
HIGH impact**, and the FY25 CFO review names **"generic price pressure"** behind a poor year in Mexico; the
FY26 deck frames the edge as "advantages over **generic macrolides**". *But* gross margin went 42.1 → 45.1 →
**48.6%** on +10% revenue. A product in active erosion does not do that. Real long-run risk, **REFUTED as a
present-tense derailment**.

**(2) China is the swing geography and is the one thing going wrong.** ECO Biok revenue FY22→FY25: **26.8 →
24.1 → 21.6 → 19.5** (four consecutive declines); FY26 +5% reported / +9% cc — first stabilisation. Then the
15-Jul-26 outlook, verbatim: **"Post period end trading in-line except China."** Collection: group debtor days
131→109 (improving), but the **ECL allowance rose 4.3% → 6.9% of gross trade receivables** (21.7% of overdue;
30% on the £3.6m >6-month bucket), and the FY26 admin-cost bridge attributes the **+£4.1m** increase to bonus
accrual **and receivables provisions**.

**(3) The 6.5% EBIT margin — SOLVED, and it is not price erosion.** Core trading runs adj EBITDA margins of
**18/13/14/16%** (FY22–25), China 14–27%; the **R&D unit is a −£5.9m/yr line with no revenue**. FY26 R&D cash
spend **£8.8m (£5.2m expensed + £3.6m capitalised) = 10% of revenue**. Gross margin 48.6% is a proper branded-
pharma margin. **Not a mis-classified distributor, not a broken business** — a ~16%-margin niche pharma
running a pipeline through the P&L.

**(4) Staleness — SEVERE, CONFIRMED.** `bs_date` 2025-09-30 is the **H1 FY26 interim** (published 1-Dec-25),
**10 months old**, paired with FY26 full-year flows. The **audited FY26 results were published 15-Jul-2026,
