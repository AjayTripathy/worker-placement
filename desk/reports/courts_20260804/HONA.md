### Quality-of-earnings: three items to hold against the headline
1. **$328M FY25 / $84M Q1-26 of non-service PENSION INCOME** runs through Other expense (income), net

   adjusted EPS while its former parent strips it out, that is a quality-of-earnings tell in itself.
2. **Environmental: read the cash, not the accrual.** FY25 accrual **$389M** (vs $235M FY24, $204M

**Residual-asset test — would we hold it if the bounce fails?** Yes on quality. The reservations are

- `concurrent_reverse_split_guard` — HON reverse-split 1-for-2 **one minute after** the distribution,
  specifically to preserve its price-weighted Dow weight. A spin price series that adjusts for the spin
  but not the split silently corrupts both the parent comparison and the sum-of-parts (it cost me a
  factor of ~1.5 on HONA's share of pre-spin value until caught).
- `nongaap_addback_recurrence_audit` — classify every add-back before striking a multiple. HONA's
  bridge adds back a **permanent $225M/yr parent royalty** and a **$389M/yr environmental accrual**,

  earnings containing **$328M of non-cash pension income**. Parent royalty, legacy environmental,
  excluded management-adjustment dis-synergies and non-service pension income are the four structural
  spinco add-backs and should all fire automatically.
- `investor_day_before_distribution` — **the pre-spin investor day is frequently NOT an 8-K exhibit.**

  EDGAR and behind an unreachable IR host. The generator should record `investor_day_date` and
  `guidance_retrieved: false` as an explicit coverage flag rather than letting the absence read as
  "no guidance."
- `first_print_date_from_ir_release` — HONA's 08-05 date was announced by press release on 07-17 and is
  trivially reachable, yet the store carried no print date. For a cohort where every name is 30–75 days
