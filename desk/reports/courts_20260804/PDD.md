## 0. The one-paragraph ruling

PDD's operating business is **de-rating, not derailing** — operating profit troughed in Q1-2025 and has
printed +3%, +8%, +22% year-over-year in the three quarters since, on revenue growth that has stabilised
at +9/+12/+11%. On the arithmetic it is genuinely cheap: 9.1x trailing earnings, 5.0–5.9x EV/EBIT against
$44.6–57.8bn of net cash and zero debt. **That is not why we are declining it.** We are declining it
because of what the cash *is*: in its FY2025 annual report, filed 2026-04-29, PDD tells U.S. holders in
its own words that **"it is possible that we were a PFIC for the taxable year ended December 31, 2025"** —
a direct reversal of the FY2024 annual report, which said **"we do not believe that we were a PFIC for
the taxable year ended December 31, 2024."** The company simultaneously states it **will not** provide
the information needed for the favourable QEF election. For a taxable U.S. account, that converts a 9x
compounder into an instrument whose gains are taxed annually as ordinary income (mark-to-market election)
or under the punitive excess-distribution regime with an interest charge (no election). The discount and
the defect are the *same variable* — the cash. This is a defect in the instrument, not a risk to be
sized down.

## 2. Mode B — first principles, no promoter framing

### B1. What is a U.S. taxable holder actually buying? — **the decisive question**

A Cayman holding company with no operations of its own, whose balance sheet at 2026-03-31 is
**RMB 607.5bn (US$88.1bn) of cash and securities against RMB 638bn of total assets** — i.e. ~95% of the
asset base is financial, wrapped around an e-commerce business with **RMB 1.15bn of property, plant and
equipment**. Under the U.S. PFIC asset test, a public foreign issuer's assets are valued at
**market capitalisation + liabilities**; cash and securities are *per se* passive (working capital
included). Passive assets are fixed in the numerator; the denominator falls with the share price.

**A de-rate at a cash-rich foreign issuer mechanically manufactures PFIC status.** Run it:

| input (primary source) | value |
|---|---|
| Passive assets, 2026-03-31 (cash 123,041 + restricted cash 76,213 + short-term investments 313,030 + other non-current 95,179) | RMB 607,463m = **$88.1bn** |
| Total liabilities | RMB 214,277m = $31.1bn |
| Market cap @ $90.15 (1,423.4m ADS basic) | $128.3bn |
| Total asset fair value (cap + liabilities) | $159.4bn |
| **Passive ratio** | **55.3% — fails the 50% test** |
| Market cap at which the test passes | **$145.1bn = $101.91/ADS basic, $98.02 diluted** |

The stock must rise ~13% for the static test to clear. It cleared comfortably at the 52w high of $139.41.
The gross-income test (passive income RMB ~28.3bn of RMB 460bn total) passes easily — **the asset test is
the binding constraint, and the share price is the swing variable.**

**Claim → method → authority → finding**

| claim | how verified | authority | finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| PDD's own PFIC assessment reversed year-over-year | Read both annual reports' Item 3.D risk factor and Item 10.E taxation section verbatim | FY2024 20-F (filed 2025-04-28) vs FY2025 20-F (filed 2026-04-29) | **CONFIRMED.** FY2024: *"we do not believe that we were a PFIC for the taxable year ended December 31, 2024."* FY2025: *"it is possible that we were a PFIC for the taxable year ended December 31, 2025, and it is possible that we may be a PFIC for the current taxable year and for future taxable years."* |
| No QEF election is available | Same filing | FY2025 20-F, Item 10.E | **CONFIRMED.** *"We do not intend to provide information necessary for U.S. holders to make qualified electing fund elections."* |
| Status is self-reinforcing on a de-rate | Same filing | FY2025 20-F, Item 10.E | **CONFIRMED.** *"If our market capitalization is less than anticipated or subsequently declines, we may be or become a PFIC… If we determine not to deploy significant amounts of cash for active purposes… our risk of being a PFIC may substantially increase."* |
| Once a PFIC, always a PFIC for that holder | Same filing | FY2025 20-F, Item 10.E | **CONFIRMED**, absent a purging election. |

**Consequence for this book.** The household's IBKR account is taxable, and the 2026 plan is
equities-for-maximum-tax-loss-harvesting with long-term-gain deferral. A PFIC is structurally
incompatible with both:
- *Mark-to-market election* (available — the ADSs are Nasdaq-listed): every year's appreciation is
  **ordinary income**, ~50% all-in, no deferral. Losses are deductible only to the extent of prior
  mark-to-market inclusions — so the tax treatment is **asymmetric against us**.
- *No election*: excess-distribution regime — the entire gain on sale is spread across the holding
  period, taxed at each year's **highest** rate, plus an interest charge.
- Annual Form 8621 filing.

A base-case +7% twelve-month gross return becomes ~+3.5% after tax with an unchanged downside. That is
not a position; it is a fee paid for the privilege of being right.

**Why this is honesty-alpha and not just a fact.** Roughly every China ADR carries a PFIC paragraph.
The signal is not the paragraph's presence — it is **the word change inside it**, which no press
release, no 6-K, and no earnings call mentioned. Masking channel: identical-looking boilerplate that
readers skip. Signal channel: annual 20-F risk-factor diff. Latency: ~16 months from the taxable
year-end to the filing that discloses it — U.S. holders learn only after they already own it.
**Recommend adding a "risk-factor PFIC-language diff" detector to the ADR recall floor.**

All figures RMB millions, from the quarterly 6-K exhibits and the audited FY 20-F.

**The operating series bottomed in Q1-2025 and has risen year-over-year for three consecutive prints.**
Revenue growth stabilised in a +9% to +12% band four quarters ago. On the classic test — multiple
compressed while estimates hold — this reads **de-rate**, the HUBS/CTSH/SAP pattern.

**But the mix is eroding, and that is what the aggregate hides** (the segment-flooring lesson):

| revenue line | Q1-25 | Q1-26 | YoY | FY24→FY25 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Online marketing services & others (domestic ads — high margin) | 48,722 | 49,936 | **+2.5%** | +10.0% |

Gross margin: **FY2024 60.9% → FY2025 56.3% (-460bp)**; Q1-25 57.2% → Q1-26 55.9% (-130bp). The
company's own cost commentary attributes it to *"increased fulfilment fees, bandwidth and server costs,
and payment processing fees"* — the cost signature of a lower-margin, physically-fulfilled mix.

**And the Q1-2026 operating beat was manufactured with one lever, once.** Revenue +11%; total operating
expenses **+2.9%**; sales & marketing **+1.1%** (33,403 → 33,773); G&A **-4.8%**. Only R&D grew (+23%).
Freezing marketing while revenue grows 11% is not a repeatable margin engine.

**Ruling: de-rate on the printed operating line; derailment risk sits in the composition and is
entirely ahead of us, not behind.**

### B4. Honesty read on the multi-quarter "profitability will decline" messaging

Verified verbatim across five consecutive releases (VP of Finance Jun Liu unless noted):

- **Q1-25 (2025-05-27):** *"a slowdown in growth rate is expected… Our financial results may continue to reflect the impact of sustained investments."*
- **Q2-25 (2025-08-25):** *"Revenues growth further moderated this quarter amid intense competition… the sustained investments may continue to weigh on short-term profitability."*
- **Q3-25 (2025-11-18):** *"revenues growth continued to moderate… financial results may continue to fluctuate from quarter to quarter."*
- **Q4/FY25 (2026-03-25):** *"These investments are firm and long-term, and will inevitably affect our financial performance."*
- **Q1-26 (2026-05-27):** Co-CEO Zhao — *"supply chain investment is where we will place our greatest conviction… an all-in mindset."* Liu — *"We are investing resolutely… we are prepared to invest over the long term."*

**Finding, two-sided.**

*For FY2025 this was honest.* They said profit would fall; operating profit fell 14%. Under the
honesty-alpha framework this is **CLEAN** — a disclosed impairment is not a lie, and re-detecting it is
not alpha.

*For Q1-2026 the takeaway diverges from the data — in the sandbagging direction.* Management escalated
the investment language while the P&L showed total opex **+2.9%** on revenue **+11%**, S&M **+1.1%**,
G&A **-4.8%**, and operating margin **+160bp**. That is expectations management downward, which is a
materially different animal from a promoter talking results up.

**But the counter-hypothesis is unresolvable and I will not claim the sandbag.** PDD's merchant-support
programme is plausibly delivered as **fee reductions** — which suppresses *revenue* rather than showing
as *expense*. That would fully explain online-marketing revenue at +2.5% without any opex line moving,
and would mean the "investment" is real and simply netted at the top line. **PDD publishes no GMV, no
MAU/DAU, no order volume, no take rate, no Temu revenue, no geographic split, and operates as a single
reportable segment.** There is no way to distinguish "the domestic ad business is losing share" from
"the domestic ad business is deliberately rebating." **This is the top UNVERIFIABLE and it caps
conviction on both the bull and bear side.**

### B7. The Temu / de-minimis bear case — REFUTED as a growth story, live as a margin and fine story

De-minimis is dead: the Section 321 exemption ceased for China/HK imports **2025-05-02** and was
eliminated for **all countries 2025-08-29** (20-F, Item 4.B and Item 3.D). Yet:

- Transaction services revenue — the line that carries Temu — grew **+19.2% in Q4-2025** and **+19.9% in
  Q1-2026**, after a mid-2025 trough that dragged FY2025 to +9.3%. **The tariff shock is in the base and
  growth resumed.**
- What is *not* refuted is the **cost** of that resumption: cost of revenue +15% on revenue +11% in
  Q1-26, driven by fulfilment — the signature of shifting from air-freighted parcels to local
  fulfilment.
- **Live, dated, disclosed regulatory pipeline** (all from the FY2025 20-F): EU fixed customs duty of
  ~€3 per item category on sub-€150 parcels **from July 2026**; an additional EU processing fee **from
  November 2026**; an Italian handling fee **from 2026-07-01**; a French ~€2 parcel tax **already
  effective March 2026**. And the open EU DSA proceeding, where a confirmed non-compliance decision
  *"could entail fines of up to 6% of the total worldwide annual turnover"* — **~$3.8bn** against
  trailing revenue of $64.1bn. Disclosed, unquantified, genuinely tail-shaped.

### B8. AI-complex conflict — a genuine non-conflict, stated positively

The queue flags `ai_complex: false` and I agree, and it is worth stating affirmatively rather than
skipping. **The entry does not require the AI-capex cycle holding.** PDD's R&D is RMB 16.5bn/yr (3.8% of
revenue); property, plant and equipment is **RMB 1,153m on a RMB 637,704m balance sheet**; there is no
disclosed AI capex programme, no data-centre build, and no GPU commitment in the contractual-obligations
note. Its cost base is people and marketing, not compute. Against the frozen house call
**AI-BREAK | 2027-12-31 @ 0.45**, PDD is **neutral-to-mildly-positive** in the break scenario: a capex
bust does not touch its P&L, and a consumer-trade-down environment is its core competency. With ~$5.2M /
26% of the household already AI-complex, a large-cap growth name that is orthogonal to that call is
scarce and has real portfolio value. **This is the strongest argument for the name and it is not enough
to overcome §B1.**

## 4. Four-idea frame

1. **The trade the queue implies:** buy a 49%-3yr-CAGR platform at 5x EV/EBIT with 35-45% of the cap in
   net cash, -44% off its high. → **Declined.** The cheapness metric and the tax defect are the same
   variable; the screen cannot see that.
2. **The operating trade:** operating profit inflected three quarters ago and the tariff bear case is in
   the base. → **Real, and worth ~5/10 in a tax-exempt vehicle.** Not ownable here.
3. **The capital-return option:** at 9x with $44-58bn of net cash, a buyback is by a wide margin the
   highest-return use of capital available to this management, and it would simultaneously shrink the
   PFIC numerator, break the trapped-cash objection, and re-rate the multiple. → **This is the only
   thing that makes PDD a buy for a taxable U.S. holder.** Management has stated the opposite policy.
   Watch the 6-K feed; this is the single highest-value tripwire in the name.
4. **The generalisable finding:** the risk-factor PFIC-language diff. Every cash-rich foreign issuer
   that de-rates hard is a candidate. → **Ship it as a detector.**

## 10. Mechanism for the knowledge graph

**Name:** *Cash-rich foreign-issuer de-rate manufactures PFIC status.*

- **Setup:** a non-U.S. issuer whose balance sheet is majority cash/securities, whose share price falls
  hard, and which does not distribute capital.
- **Mechanism:** the PFIC asset test values a public issuer's assets at market cap + liabilities.
  Passive assets sit fixed in the numerator; the denominator collapses with the multiple. The de-rate
  *causes* the tax status.
- **Masking channel:** a PFIC risk-factor paragraph that looks identical to the boilerplate in ~every
  ADR filing. Readers skip it because it is always there.
- **Signal channel:** the **year-over-year diff of the sentence inside it** — "we do not believe we were
  a PFIC" → "it is possible that we were a PFIC." Free, in the 20-F, never in a press release, never on
  a call.
- **Signal-to-price latency:** ~16 months from the taxable year-end to the filing that discloses it; and
  since PFIC taint attaches to the *holder's* whole holding period, the damage is done before disclosure.
- **Population:** every cash-rich foreign issuer trading well below its own history. Screen: passive
  assets ÷ (market cap + liabilities) > 0.45.
- **Deliverable:** add a **risk-factor PFIC-language diff** check to the ADR recall floor, firing on any
  foreign private issuer where the passive ratio exceeds 0.45. PDD is the first specimen.

*Corroborating prior art in this repo: the EDINET shelf lesson that the PFIC gate anti-selected on
drawdown. That was recorded as a gate defect. This court shows it is not a defect — it is the mechanism
working correctly. **Drawdown is a cause of PFIC status, not a coincidence with it.***
