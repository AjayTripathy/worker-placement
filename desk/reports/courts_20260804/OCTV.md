## 4. THE BUSINESS — and the honesty finding

**Not just Asset Lifecycle Intelligence.** Octave is four Hexagon units combined: ALI, Safety
Infrastructure & Geospatial, ETQ (SaaS quality management), and Bricsys (CAD). Organized as Design
(~40% of FY25 revenue) / Build (~10%) / Operate (~30%) / Protect (~20%). ~7,200 employees, 45
countries, **4,464 customers** in 140+ countries, **421 large customers (≥$500k ARR)**, >60% of the
Global Fortune 500. Single reportable segment. **No customer >2% of revenue.** GRR 97% (99% large),
NRR 105%. Competes with Autodesk, Bentley, AVEVA, Esri, Procore, IBM Maximo, IFS, Tyler, Motorola,
CentralSquare. Holds a US federal subsidiary under a **DCSA Proxy Agreement** — management has no
visibility into parts of that business.

This is a real, durable, low-concentration installed base. The residual-asset question ("would we hold
it if the bounce fails?") answers **yes on quality**. The problem is growth and the print.
