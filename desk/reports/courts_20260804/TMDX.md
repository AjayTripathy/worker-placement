The queue calls this "single-platform (OCS)." The filing shows it is worse than that — it is

accelerating. Data: a single US-liver franchise carrying two declining franchises.** This is a
takeaway-vs-data divergence, not merely a disclosed weak spot.

### B6. The honesty finding — the masking channel, and why it is now exhausted
This is the encodable microstructure item.

FY2025 net income was **$190.3M / $4.87 diluted**, of which **$82.8M was a one-time income-tax

highlights. Not a lie.

But trace the takeaway against the data:

- **Q4-25 print (2026-02-24):** lead highlight = *"net income of $105.4 million, or $2.62 per diluted
  share."* Underneath it, operating income was **$21.3M, down 42% sequentially**, and operating

  (137.78 → 145.26).
- **Q1-26 print (2026-05-05):** no tax cushion left. The operating line is now the only thing
  visible. **Stock −23.2%.**

**Masking channel:** a one-time deferred-tax valuation-allowance release placed at the top of the
highlights reel, which converted a decelerating operating quarter into a record-EPS headline.
**Signal channel:** the operating-margin series, which had already turned down two quarters earlier.
**Signal-to-price latency: ≈10 weeks (2026-02-24 → 2026-05-06).**

Two consequences:
1. **The screen is reading the artifact.** Trailing GAAP EPS $4.87 → 16.7x trailing P/E looks like
   deep quality-at-a-discount. Ex the one-time benefit, FY25 EPS ≈ $2.75 → ≈29.6x, and FY26 is
   tracking $1.20-1.60 → **51-68x**. This is the GARP vertical's documented base-effect/peak-earnings
   PEG distortion, and it is very likely load-bearing in the 87.3 queue score.
2. **The channel is spent.** There is no second valuation allowance. It also means part of the EPS
   "collapse" is mechanical, not operational — see the honest calibration below.

**Honest calibration (blue-teaming my own finding):** of the $18.4M y/y net-income decline in Q1-26,
roughly **$7.3M is the tax-rate normalization** the allowance release itself caused (Q1-25 carried a
~6% effective rate; Q1-26 ~33%). So ~40% of the headline EPS collapse is non-operating and permanent
but explained. **The operating deterioration — op income −52%, margin −1,147bps — is the real
number, and it is severe on its own.** I do not need the EPS number to make the case and I am not
resting on it.
