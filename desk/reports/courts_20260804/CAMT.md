### B1. The headline is a sequential half-over-half frame. **This is the honesty finding.**

> "Q1 revenues of $121.7 million; **Expects over 25% revenue growth in 2H26 versus 1H26** based on strong
> order momentum"

The number a reader carries away is "**over 25% revenue growth**." The number in the body is:

> "Revenues for the first quarter of 2026 were $121.7 million. This compares to first quarter 2025 revenues of
> $118.6 million, representing a **year-over-year growth of 2.5%**."

**Grade: REVIEW, not ELEVATE.** The comparison basis is explicitly labelled ("2H26 versus 1H26") and the y/y
number appears in the second bullet of the same release. Per the secondary-emphasis doctrine this is
disclosed emphasis, not masking — a *flattering frame*, not a divergent takeaway. But it is the single most
load-bearing sentence in the bull case and it is a **sequential** claim wearing a growth headline.

- GAAP operating income **$27.3M, −17% y/y** (margin **27.6% → 22.4%**)

- GAAP net income $31.6M / $0.63, −8% y/y

operating leverage — the opposite of what a company on the cusp of a 25% ramp should be printing.
