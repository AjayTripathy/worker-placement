## 7. FOUR-IDEA FRAME
1. **Consensus idea:** a -75% drawdown on a 73%-gross-margin consumer subscription with a fortress
   balance sheet, now at ~13x trailing free cash flow.
2. **Bear idea:** LLMs make language instruction free and infinitely personalised; a gamified
   flashcard app has no defensible moat against that, and the halving of growth is the first read.
3. **Our variant:** the growth halved on *saturation*, disclosed by management before the AI bear
   became consensus — so the AI story is being used to explain a maturity problem. That makes the
   terminal value higher than the bear thinks and the growth rate lower than the bull thinks, which
   is exactly where the price now sits. Fair, not cheap.
4. **The idea that kills ours:** Duolingo's moat is *habit* (streaks), not content, and habit-based
   consumer subscriptions historically re-accelerate on price and tier mix. If Max attach is driving
   ARPU faster than DAU is decaying, 3.8x forward sales is too low and this is a 2x.
