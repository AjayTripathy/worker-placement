## 2. THE DE-RATE vs DERAILMENT TEST — the whole job

### 2a. The estimate cut was tiny. The price move was not.

| | FY26 revenue | FY26 organic | FY26 adj EPS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial guide (2026-02-12 Q4'25 release) | $9.825–10.025B | +3–5% | **$7.00–7.10** |
| Revised guide (2026-05-07 Q1'26 release) | $9.680–9.960B | +2–5% | **$6.85–7.00** |
| **Delta at midpoint** | **−1.1%** | −1pp at low end | **−1.8%** |
| Consensus now (stockanalysis, 19 analysts, Jul-26) | $9.75B | — | **$6.86** — *below the guide midpoint* |

A **−1.8% EPS guidance trim produced a −21.5% single-day and a −50.8% peak-to-trough move.** That is
a ~28x amplification. The market did not reprice the 2026 number. It repriced the **terminal growth
algorithm** — from ~34x (2023) to 15.8x (pre-print) to **11.1x**.

So the surface reading is "classic de-rate: multiple crushed, estimates intact." That reading is
**wrong**, and the next two tests are why.

### 2b. The Q1 number is worse than it prints. (Masking channel #1)

Reported: *"Revenue of $2.3 Billion, Growing 3%... Adjusted Diluted EPS of $1.53 [+9%]... Flat
Organic Operational Growth."* Underneath (all from the 8-K EX-99.1, verbatim):

- **The $100M calendar pull-in.** "Revenue in the International segment was positively impacted by
  operational changes made in connection with the company's Fiscal Year Alignment, which contributed
  an estimated **$100 million** in revenue to the quarter." On $2,262M of revenue that is **+4.4pp**.
  Zoetis's "organic operational" definition excludes FX and M&A — it does **not** exclude this. So
  **clean organic revenue was approximately −4.4%, not flat.** International's +10% organic is ~0%
  clean.
- **The EPS growth is buyback, not earnings.** Adjusted net income $646M vs $633M = **+2%** (+1%
  organic). Adjusted diluted EPS +9%. Diluted shares 422.4M vs 448.0M = **−5.7%**. Seven of the nine
  points of EPS growth are share count.
- Gross margin is the one genuinely clean line: 71.7% vs 71.9%. No price collapse in the P&L *yet*.

**Finding: the marketed takeaway ("flat organic, EPS +9%") diverges from the data (≈−4% organic,
adjusted profit +1-2%).** This is takeaway-vs-data divergence, not merely disclosed bad news — the
$100M is disclosed in paragraph 4 while the headline bullet says "Growing 3%."

### 2c. The FY26 guide has an arithmetic problem. (The Aug-6 test)

The Q4'25 release states the Q4'25 International pull-forward "**is not expected to recur at the end
of fiscal year 2026**," and the Q1'26 $100M came from (i) price increases *moved into* Q1 and (ii)
December-2025 orders *processed in* Q1. Both are one-time **pull-ins**. They do not repeat in Q2–Q4.

So: Q1 organic was flat *including* +4.4pp of pull-in. To reach the FY26 guide of **+2% to +5%
organic**, Q2–Q4 must run **+2.7% to +6.7% clean** — off a US companion-animal base that just
printed **−11%**. The guide is back-half-loaded against an H1 run-rate that does not support it.

**This is the single most important forward-looking finding on the name, and it resolves on 2026-08-06.**

### 2d. THE DECISIVE MODE-B TEST: is this the end market, or is it share loss?

Zoetis's own explanation leads with the end market: *"Pet owners demonstrated increased price
sensitivity, resulting in a decline in veterinary visits and softer demand for premium innovative
products."* Competition is the second clause.

I tested that framing against the two independent peers who share the identical end market. All
prices IBKR close 2026-08-03; ELAN path re-verified on 24 monthly bars with corporate actions
requested (**none returned — no split, the move is real price**):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ZTS** | $77.10 | $148.04 | **−48%** | ~+2% rep / **flat organic (−4% clean)** | **11.2x** | $32.6B |
| **ELAN** (Elanco — the direct competitor) | $25.72 | $13.87 | **+85%**, 7% off its 52w HIGH | **+10.5%** | **23.7x** | $12.85B |
| **IDXX** (vet diagnostics = vet-visit volume proxy) | $567.84 | $662.24 | −14% | — | — | — |

ELAN monthly path: $13.68 (Jul-25) → $18.35 (Aug-25) → $22.15 (Oct-25) → $24.08 (Jan-26) → $26.40
(Feb-26) → $25.72. **Elanco was UP in May-2026, the month Zoetis fell 33%.**

Decomposition: IDXX at −14% sizes the honest end-market drag. Zoetis is −48%. The residual ~−34pp is
**Zoetis-specific**. And Elanco — selling into the same soft market, to the same price-sensitive pet
owners, through the same clinics — grew revenue **+10.5%** while Zoetis grew **0% organic**. A 10pp
growth gap between the #1 and #2 in the same categories in the same quarter cannot be a vet-visit
cycle.

Zoetis's own filings supply the mechanism, so this is corroborated on both sides:
- Q1'26 8-K: *"The company's key dermatology franchise and **Simparica Trio** faced heightened
  competitive pressure"* — Elanco's Zenrelia (derm) and Credelio Quattro (parasiticide) are the
  named categories.
- Q1'26 8-K: *"the impact of **generic competition on the Convenia® and Cerenia®** brands"*
- FY25 10-K patent section (CONFIRMED verbatim): active-ingredient patents have **expired** on
  Draxxin (tulathromycin), Excede/Naxcel (ceftiofur) and **Cerenia**; "one generic version of
  Cerenia injectable has been approved in the U.S."
- Librela (bedinvetmab) sales **declining** in both US and International — a launch mAb going
  backwards, which is why Lenivia/Portela (the 3-month successors) matter so much.

**FINDING — REFUTED: "this is an end-market/vet-visit cycle" is not the dominant term.** The
dominant term is a competitive share transfer to Elanco in dermatology and parasiticides, compounded
by generic entry on the legacy tail. The end market explains roughly a quarter of the move.

**RULING: DERAILMENT (share loss), not the HUBS/CTSH/SAP de-rate pattern.** The multiple compressed
*because* the franchise is being taken. Estimates are falling and the multiple is following — the
multiple has simply run ahead of the estimates, which is the ambiguous middle, not a bargain.

## 4. FOUR-IDEA FRAME

1. **What the promoter says:** a 72%-GM animal-health duopolist, 75-year franchise, 12 potential
   blockbusters, at 11x — the greatest quality-compounder discount in a decade.
2. **What the data says:** the crown-jewel US companion-animal business shrank **11%** while the
   direct competitor grew **10.5%**; clean organic revenue was **≈−4%**; EPS "growth" was buyback;
   and $3.8B was borrowed to buy the stock 50% higher.
3. **What has to be true to win:** US companion animal must stop declining within 2–3 quarters, and
   Lenivia + Portela must actually replace Librela. Then $6.90 is a trough and 11x is absurd.
4. **What kills it:** the Aug-6 print shows another double-digit US companion decline and a second
   FY26 guide cut. At that point the terminal question is "what is the durable earnings base," and
   11x on a shrinking base is not cheap — it is correct.
