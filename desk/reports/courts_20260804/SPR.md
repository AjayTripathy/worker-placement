## 3. THE OTHER GUARD FAILED IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION — 27% FCF YIELD IS LAND LIQUIDATION

The FCF yield of **26.7%** that put Springfield 13th of 249 is a working-capital release, and the
guard that exists to catch that **cannot fire on a profitable company** (`wc_fcf` requires EBIT<0 —
see gate grade §3a).

**The mechanism is a single named transaction: on 17 Feb 2025 Springfield sold 2,500 plots to
Barratt Redrow for £64m.** That one deal simultaneously produced (i) the inventory draw of £20.4m
(FY24 £244.3m → FY25 £223.9m) that *is* most of the "free cash flow", (ii) the FY25 revenue and
gross-margin uplift (gross margin 16.3% → 18.6%), and (iii) the cash that repaid the debt.

**The deleveraging, the profit peak and the FCF yield are all the same event, counted three times.**
And it was paid for by shrinking the land bank by 2,500 plots.

The 04-Jun-2026 Trading Update confirms the reversal: **FY26 revenue ~£245m versus FY25's £280.6m —
down 12.7%.** yfinance shows `forwardPE` **12.44x** against `trailingPE` **9.05x**: consensus
expects EPS to fall roughly a third.
