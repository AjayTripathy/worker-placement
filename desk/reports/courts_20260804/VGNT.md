## 1. WHAT THE RESIDUAL ASSET ACTUALLY IS — the decisive fact
Aptiv split into two halves. **Versigent is the low-quality half.**

> "In connection with and prior to the Spin-Off, Aptiv transferred its **Electrical Distribution Systems
> segment** to the Company." — 8-K 2026-04-01, Item 2.01

Aptiv retained Advanced Safety & User Experience and Engineered Components. Versigent is the
**wiring-harness business**: labour-intensive, Mexico- and Europe-heavy, copper-pass-through, commodity,
and structurally the lowest-margin part of the automotive supply chain. SIC 3714. This is not a software
or electronics asset and should not be valued as one.

Jersey-incorporated, HQ Schaffhausen, Switzerland, **files 10-K/10-Q as a US domestic filer** (not 20-F,
so no PFIC or foreign-reporting complication). Ordinary shares $0.01 par.

## 4. THE MARGIN BRIDGE — where I corrected my own first read

The headline looks alarming: Q1-26 operating income **$74M vs $123M**, −40% on +9.3% revenue. Reading the
actual statement rather than the summary reverses most of it:

| Q1, $M | 2026 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Net sales | 2,212 | 2,024 (+9.3%) |
| Cost of sales | 1,968 | 1,775 |
| *Gross profit* | *244 (11.0%)* | *249 (12.3%)* |
| SG&A | 97 | 105 |
| **Restructuring** | **46** | **16** |
| **Separation costs** | **26** | **5** |
| **Operating income** | **74** | **123** |

**Ex-restructuring and separation: $146M (6.6%) vs $144M (7.1%)** — adjusted EBIT dollars were roughly
**flat (+1.4%)** on +9.3% revenue, a ~50bp margin decline, not 280bp. My initial "severe compression"
read was wrong and is corrected here.

What survives the correction, and matters:
- **Gross margin fell 130bp (12.3% → 11.0%)** on rising volume — price/cost pressure in the core.
  SG&A was cut to offset it. That is a one-time lever.
- **Capex ($66M) exceeds D&A ($61M)** — this is a capital-consuming business, and capex nearly doubled YoY.
- **Q1 free cash flow was NEGATIVE ~$30M** (operating cash flow $36M − capex $66M).
- **Restructuring is chronic here**, not exceptional — $16M in the year-ago quarter too, plus a $4M
  pension curtailment on a planned European site closure. Adding all $46M back flatters the run-rate.

**Normalised** (restructuring at ~$22M/qtr rather than $46M): EBIT ~$124M/qtr → ~$496M/yr; less ~$133M
of annual interest → ~$363M pre-tax → ~$290M net → **EPS ~$4.10**.

## 9. VERDICT — **PASS 3/10**

The queue asked the right question — "*pattern RIPPING +48%, quality-at-price question now*" — and the
answer is that **the rip is real and the quality is not**. This is the commodity half of the Aptiv split,
carrying $2.1B of debt raised to pay the former parent, with negative book equity, 11% gross margins,
capex above depreciation, negative Q1 free cash flow, and chronic restructuring. The market has re-rated
it +51% in four months. On our numbers fair value sits ~11% *below* spot.

It is not a disaster and not a short — at ~10x normalised earnings with a dividend and buyback it is
approximately correctly priced. It is simply **not a quality-at-discount asset**, and the frame this
court applies has no room for it at $42.

**No entry at spot. Re-open only below $30–33** (≈7x normalised EPS, ≈5.5x EBITDA) — a level that would
require a genuine auto down-cycle, at which point the earnings assumption also needs re-cutting. **If
entered there, 0.4% cap.** Practically: this is a watch-only name.

**Dated kills / re-open blocks:**
- Q2 (08-04) shows adjusted EBIT margin below 5.5% ex-restructuring → the gross-margin erosion is
  structural → close the file for the year.
- Net leverage above 3.0x at any print → exit/stay out.
- Dividend cut or buyback suspension → the 05-13 capital-return signal was hollow → stay out.
