## 7. FOUR-IDEA FRAME

- **What the market believes:** agentic AI cannibalizes seat-based SaaS; Salesforce is the largest,
  most seat-dependent target; cut the multiple by a third and wait for the bookings to confirm.
- **What we believe:** the confirmation isn't arriving. Organic cRPO has held ~9% cc for two quarters,
  the FY27 revenue and EPS guides have both been *raised* while the stock fell 30% YTD, and the
  business now shrinks its own float ~4-5%/yr from free cash flow at 13x. Separately, an AI-capex
  break — the house's own 45% call — is a *tailwind* to the channel that caused this de-rate.
- **Why it might already be priced:** the organic step-down is real (cRPO cc 11% → 9%; Q2 guided to
  5.9% organic) and Informatica is masking it in the headline. A 13x multiple on a ~6-8% organic grower
  with $39.5B of new debt is not obviously wrong. Starboard, the discipline check, has left.
- **What resolves it:** the **Q4 FY27 print (~Feb-2027)** — Informatica fully anniversaries and reported
  growth *equals* organic for the first time in five quarters. The mask expires on a date the company
  cannot move.

**Mechanism (for the knowledge graph):**
*Masking channel* = acquisition-inclusive headline growth (Informatica ~4pts) covering a ~200bp organic
deceleration, with the deflator disclosed for revenue but **withdrawn for cRPO**.
*Signal channel* = organic cRPO cc, recoverable by subtracting the Informatica cRPO contribution the
company stopped printing.
*Signal-to-price latency* = **~6 months, and dated**: the mask mechanically expires at the Q4 FY27
print (~Feb-2027). This is a rare case where the masking channel has a known expiry rather than an
open-ended one.
