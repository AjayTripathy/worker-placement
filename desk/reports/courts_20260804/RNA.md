## 4. WHAT YOU GET FOR THE NEGATIVE $50M

- **ATR-1072** — siRNA-AOC targeting PRKAG2, for PRKAG2 syndrome. **IND CLEARED 2026-07-14**, enabling the
  "Corventis" Phase 1/2. The Q1 10-Q had guided the IND filing to "the second half of 2026" — **so this
  was delivered on or ahead of schedule.** Preclinical: marked PRKAG2 mRNA and protein reduction in
  mouse heart, improved diastolic function and restored conduction in a disease model, NHP tolerability
  evaluated.
- **ATR-1086** — siRNA-AOC targeting phospholamban (PLN), for PLN cardiomyopathy. In the humanized
  hPLN-R14/R14 model (untreated animals die within 8 weeks), treatment gave **100% survival through ≥20
  weeks** with substantially improved ejection fraction.
- **BMS collaboration** (assumed from Avidity, originally Nov-2023, ex-MyoKardia) — **paid a $15.0M
  development-candidate milestone in Q1-2026**. This is a live, paying, validating relationship.
- **Eli Lilly agreement** — assumed; remaining milestones fully constrained in the transaction price.
- **$45.7M of deferred revenue** still to be recognised.

The platform is not speculative in the abstract: the AOC delivery approach is what Novartis paid up for
when it bought Avidity. What is unproven is that the cardiac-targeted version works in humans.

## 5. THE HONEST BEAR CASE (why it is cheap, and may stay cheap)
1. **It is a below-cash biotech, and below-cash biotechs convert cash into sunk R&D.** Q1 operating cash
   burn was $31.1M (inflated by spin/retention costs — G&A was $20.3M vs $2.1M a year earlier).
   Normalised ~$25M/qtr means the $250M becomes $150M by end-2027. The discount can persist while the
   asset backing it shrinks.
2. **Ultra-rare indications.** PRKAG2 syndrome and PLN cardiomyopathy are very small populations. Even
   clean data supports a modest commercial outcome absent a platform read-through.
3. **No catalyst until Phase 1 data (2027+).** IND clearance was the catalyst, and the stock *fell*
   through it (12.15 on 07-14 → 11.33 today). That is the most informative single fact here: **this
   market is not pricing news at all.**
4. **No re-rating mechanism.** Zero coverage, $2.25M/day of liquidity, Russell 2000 only. Cheapness with
   no transmission channel is not an edge — it is a wait.

## 9. VERDICT — **WATCH 4/10**, tiny starter only

This is the only name in the tier-4 batch with a verified positive edge, and the cheapness is real,
primary-sourced, and correctly adjusted for the deferred-revenue trap. The cap structure is clean, the
company delivered its IND on schedule, and two blue-chip partners are paying it milestones.

But it is a pre-Phase-1 biotech that burns ~$25M a quarter with no coverage, no catalyst inside twelve
months, and no mechanism to close the discount. **Buying below cash is not the same as getting the cash.**

**Entry band $10.00–11.25.** **Size 0.20% standalone, 0.30% hard cap** — an eighth of the ruled cap.
Rationale for the small size: $2.25M/day of dollar volume makes anything larger unexitable, and the
outcome distribution is genuinely binary. Limits only, ≤15% of prints. This is a lottery ticket bought at
a discount to its own cash, sized as such.

**Dated kills:**
- Any equity raise below net cash per share → the discount is being institutionalised → exit.
- Quarterly operating burn >$32M for two consecutive quarters → runway math breaks → exit.
- ATR-1072 Phase 1/2 clinical hold or safety signal → exit.
- Market cap exceeds 1.15x net cash without new clinical data → the edge is spent → trim to zero.
