## FINDING 2 — this is how the screen got fooled

Consensus moved UP in the days before the queue was built: FY26 EPS $1.903 → **$1.971** and the
near-quarter $0.563 → $0.583, with 4 up-revisions in 7 days. A de-rate-vs-derailment test that reads
"estimates rising while the multiple is compressed" scores that as the **HUBS/CTSH winner pattern.**

It is a false positive by construction: **the consensus is being marked up on a non-recurring
customs refund and an extra fiscal week.** Any screen that reads estimate direction without checking
estimate *composition* will keep buying tariff refunds. **Recommended gate: when GAAP and adjusted
guidance diverge by >10%, use the adjusted line for the estimate-trajectory test and flag the
delta's cause.**
