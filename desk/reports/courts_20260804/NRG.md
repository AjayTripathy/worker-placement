**Answer to Q2: roughly $810–830M/yr of PJM capacity revenue is contracted through 31-May-2029, with

- Every LDA cleared **flat to RTO with $0.00 adders** in all three collared years. Any East model

- **The collar severs AI transmission in BOTH directions.** A booming AI load cannot lift NRG's PJM
  capacity revenue above the cap; a breaking AI cycle cannot cut it below the floor. Supply is
  genuinely short (2028/29 cleared **6,831 MW BELOW** the reliability requirement; 14.4% IRM vs a 20%
