## 2. THE RESIDUAL ASSET — better than the price action deserves, and that matters

I initially mis-framed this as an empty shell. It is not. From the 10-Q (2026-05-14) MD&A:

- **ANB033** — CD122 antagonist (blocks IL-15/IL-2 signalling). **This is now the value driver.**
  80-subject Phase 1a healthy-volunteer topline (Oct-2025): no SAE discontinuations, no DLTs, 2–3 week
  half-life, full receptor occupancy, 70–75% reduction in CD122+ CD8 T cells. Two Phase 1b trials
  running: **celiac disease, 60 patients, topline guided Q4-2026**; **eosinophilic esophagitis, 50
  patients, topline guided mid-2027**.
- **rosnilimab** — pathogenic T-cell depleter (PD-1 agonist). Completed a **424-patient randomised
  placebo-controlled Phase 2b in RA that HIT its primary endpoint**; End-of-Phase-2 FDA meeting
  completed Q1-2026. **But: the Phase 2 ulcerative colitis study FAILED and was abandoned Nov-2025**,
  and management is **seeking a partner/out-licence rather than funding Phase 3 itself**. So the RA
  asset is a partnering option, not a self-funded programme — which cuts both the capital need and the
  upside participation.
- **ANB101** — BDCA2 modulator, Phase 1a healthy volunteers nearing completion.
- **Vanda milestone rights** — up to **$35.0M** remaining on imsidolimab (IL-36R, two completed Phase 3s
  in GPP, Vanda-run): $5M US approval, $5M EU approval, $25M on >$100M sales. Retained by TRAX under the
  Separation and Distribution Agreement. A real, near-dated option.

So the pipeline is real. The problem is entirely the price struck on it.

### 2a. Where the Phase 2b is softer than the headline (honestly disclosed — see §5)

The 10-Q prints the full endpoint table. Reading it rather than the summary sentence:

| Endpoint, Week 12 | Placebo (N=106) | 100mg Q4W | 400mg Q4W | 600mg Q2W |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DAS28-CRP (mean Δ) — **primary** | −1.69 | −2.06** | −2.12** | −2.06** |
| ACR20 | **52.8%** | 68.9%* | 70.1%** | 75.2%*** |
| ACR50 | 33.0% | 44.3% (ns) | 36.4% (ns) | 46.7%* |
| **ACR70** | **17.9%** | **21.7% (ns)** | **21.5% (ns)** | **21.9% (ns)** |
| CDAI ≤10 (LDA) | 31.1% | 46.2%* | 49.5%** | 38.1% (ns) |

Four observations the summary sentence does not carry:
1. **ACR70 is null** — no separation and no significance at any dose. ACR70 is the depth-of-response
   endpoint that drives commercial differentiation in RA.
2. **The placebo arm ran hot** — 52.8% placebo ACR20 against a 25–35% norm in RA trials. That compresses
   every placebo-adjusted delta (ACR20 delta is 16–22pp; approved RA biologics typically show 30–40pp
   over a 25–30% placebo).
3. **Dose response is non-monotonic** — 400mg is *below* 100mg on ACR50; the top dose (600mg Q2W) is the
   *weakest and non-significant* on CDAI-LDA while being the strongest on ACR20.
4. The "**deepening of responses through six months**" claim rests on a **blinded all-active extension
   with no placebo control**, entered only by the 69% (220/318) of treated patients who had already
   achieved CDAI-LDA at Week 14 — a responder-enriched, uncontrolled cohort. The filing itself
   pre-explains the selection ("strict criteria at Week 14 ... prevented additional patients with
   meaningful improvement from continuing").

Primary endpoint achieved: yes, CONFIRMED. De-risked to the degree the market is now paying for: no.

## 8. VERDICT — **AVOID 2/10**

Not because the asset is bad, and not because the move was irrational. The CD122 read-across is real:
a competitor validated the mechanism clinically and another paid $2.2B for it, and TRAX genuinely is the
remaining pure-play. The problem is **sequencing**. The stock has taken ~122% of re-rating *in advance
of its own first efficacy readout*, is 3.8% off an all-time high, and sits at **3.34x the $13.81 price
sophisticated crossover investors paid fifteen weeks ago** — a placement into which the single most
informed holder on the register (EcoR1) sold 4.7M shares.

You are being asked to underwrite ANB033's celiac data at a price set by someone else's vitiligo data.
That is a worse bet than owning the same binary at the pre-read-across price, and the asymmetry now runs
against you: a clean Q4 readout is substantially priced, an ambiguous one is not.

**No entry band at any price today.** Re-open on the **event, not the level**: the ANB033 celiac Phase 1b
topline guided to **Q4-2026**. If that reads out clean and the stock has not already discounted it, this
becomes a legitimate court. Until then there is nothing to own.
