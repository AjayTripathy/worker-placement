## 3. THE WINNER-PATTERN TEST — **FAILS. A mix illusion, not a divergence**

Superficially textbook: guidance raised, price -6%, multiple at a decade low, -70% from the 2024 high.
It does not survive inspection.

1. **The raise is a raise of a decline.** Adjusted FY26 growth midpoint moves from -8% to -3%.
   "Estimates up" means "less bad," not "growing." The HUBS/CTSH/SAP pattern requires stable-to-rising
   estimates on an *unchanged terminal*.
2. **Revisions split by period.** FY26 *reported* estimates go UP on a **DKK 26.8bn non-recurring**
   reversal (H1 reported EPS/ADR is already DKK 15.66, +25%). H2'26 and FY27 *underlying* estimates go
   DOWN. The raise backfills H1 already banked while guiding the back half negative.
3. **The terminal is repricing, and it is now DATED AND SIZED.** Effective **2027-01-01**, WAC falls to
   **USD 675** on Wegovy 2.4/7.2mg and tablets to 25mg, and on Ozempic 0.5/1/2mg and Ozempic pill
   7/14mg — **~50% cut on Wegovy, ~35% on Ozempic** — which the company states "is expected to impact
   Novo Nordisk's **cash flow** in 2027." Separately, **SCOTUS denied cert on 18 May 2026** on Novo's
   IRA challenge; that avenue is closed.
4. **The optionality is being written off in real time — three hits, not one.**
   - **ZEUS (NCT05021835**, ziltivekimab Ph3, status COMPLETED, primary completion 2026-06-09) missed
     its MACE endpoint 07-31; the report warns it **"may result in a non-cash impairment charge in Q3
     2026"** — a *second* impairment already flagged.
   - **DKK 6,328m impaired in Q2**, monlunabant DKK 4.0bn named.
   - **REIMAGINE 4**, CagriSema head-to-head vs tirzepatide 15mg in T2D: **did not meet HbA1c
     non-inferiority**, and was numerically behind on weight (**15.2% vs 15.8%** weight loss; **1.9pp
     vs 2.2pp** HbA1c at 68 weeks). The CagriSema differentiation case is badly damaged five months
     before its US decision.

**Answer to the sweep-level question: NO. This is not the first true estimates-up/price-down
divergence. It is a derailment with one clean quarter inside it.** The multiple sits at a decade low
because the terminal is lower, and the market priced that in fifteen minutes.

Honestly stated against us: Q2's +7%/+11% CER is a large beat against a -4%/-12% guide; the oral launch
is the strongest GLP-1 volume launch ever recorded in the US and is admittedly under-captured in
third-party script data; International compounds at +8–10%; and a new CEO (**Maziar Mike Doustdar**,
who signed the announcement) has every incentive to sandbag a reset year in which he has already
impaired the pipeline and re-based the metric. That is why the bull weight is 0.22, not 0.10.
