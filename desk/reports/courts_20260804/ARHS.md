## 2. MODE B FIRST — first-principles, no promoter framing

### B-1. Is this brand impaired, or is it a cycle? **CYCLE — and I can date the stop.**

The decisive test is not revenue (which unit growth flatters) but **margin, and whether the margin
erosion is still running.**

| | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | **FY2026 GUIDE** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Net revenue | $1,228.9M | $1,287.7M | $1,271.1M | $1,379.2M | $1,430–1,470M |
| GAAP operating margin | **15.0%** | **12.8%** | **6.8%** | **6.4%** | ~7.0% |
| Gross margin | 42.7% | 42.0% | 39.4% | 38.9% | — |
| **Adjusted EBITDA** | — | — | **$133.3M** | **$145.1M** | **$150–161M** |
| **Adj EBITDA margin** | — | — | **10.5%** | **10.5%** | **10.5%–11.0%** |

**Adjusted EBITDA margin has been 10.5% in FY24, 10.5% in FY25, and is guided to 10.5–11.0% in FY26.**
The reset ran 2022→2024 and then *stopped dead*. That is the signature of a normalisation, not an
impairment. An impaired brand does not hold a flat margin for three years while opening showrooms.

### B-2. Decompose showroom growth vs comps — is unit growth masking comp declines? **NO, not in FY25.**

Company disclosure (new supplemental schedules, FY25 8-K):

| FY2025 | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | **FY** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Comparable WRITTEN Sales** (demand, 13-mo) | +4.1% | −3.6% | +7.4% | −2.8% | **+1.3%** |
| **Comparable DELIVERED Sales** (revenue, 15-mo) | −1.5% | +10.5% | +4.1% | +1.4% | **+3.6%** |

FY25 revenue +8.5% = **delivered comp +3.6% + non-comp (new/relocated boxes) ~+4.9%.** Both legs are
positive. There is **no masking** — the units are additive on top of a positive comp, not a substitute
for a negative one.

**Q1'26 is where it turns:** revenue **+0.9%** = **delivered comp −1.7%** + non-comp ~+2.6%; and the
leading series, **written comp, printed −5.7%.**

Units grew 3.9% but **square footage grew 9.5%** — they are relocating into materially bigger boxes

drifting. This is the real cost of the growth algorithm and it is *not* in the headline metrics.
It is, however, computable from disclosure the company voluntarily added this year (§B-6).

- Since then, **estimates have been stable, not falling**: FY25 revenue landed at the *high end* of guide,

  May with the written comp at its worst reading in five quarters. Adjusted EBITDA margin: 10.5%, 10.5%,

- **But the FY26 guide is back-end loaded and untested.** FY comp delivered 0–3% with Q1 at −1.7% and Q2
  guided **−5% to 0%** requires H2 delivered comps of roughly **+2% to +6%** — which requires written
  comps to turn sharply positive in Q2/Q3. Q2'26 laps an easy **−3.6%**; Q3'26 laps a hard **+7.4%**.
  **The Aug-6 print is the first honest test.**

**Where it trades:**
