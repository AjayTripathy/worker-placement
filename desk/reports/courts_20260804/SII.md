Total episode −37.9%. Unlike ARIS and MUX, **SII genuinely fell about twice its price-beta.** On a
naive reading that is idiosyncratic, and the name deserves a real look. It does not survive one:

**The residual IS the factor, arriving through a different channel with a lag.** Sprott's revenue is
a management fee struck on AUM, and its AUM is dominated by **physical precious-metals trusts**
(PHYS/PSLV/CEF) plus PM/critical-materials ETFs. Ex-flows, **fee revenue moves ~1:1 with the metal
price**, while the *share price* beta to GDX is only 0.57 because miners are operationally levered
and a fee-gatherer is not. So the correct comparison is not price-beta at all — it is:

| | move |
|---|---:|
| GLD off its Jan-2026 high | **−25.1%** |
| **SII FY+1 consensus EPS, 90d change** | **4.44 vs 5.675 = −21.8%** |
| SII FY0 consensus EPS, 90d change | 4.425 vs 4.496 = −1.6% |

**A −25% metal price producing a −22% cut to next-year fee earnings is a 0.9 pass-through.** The
"idiosyncratic" 23pp residual is the market re-rating a fee stream that mechanically tracks the same
commodity — an operating-leverage lag, not company-specific news.
**Ruling: FRAME-REJECT (soft).** Not a costume in the regression sense; a costume in the economic
sense. This is a new sub-class worth naming: **factor pass-through with a lag** — the price beta
understates the earnings beta, so the residual looks like alpha and is not.

## (d) WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS OWNABLE
Sprott is a legitimately good business — capital-light, high-margin, structurally advantaged in
physical PM vehicles, and it pays a dividend (ex-date 2026-05-18). The problem is not quality; it is
that **owning SII is owning gold with a fee-margin multiplier and an asset-manager multiple on top.**
That is a decision for the PM sleeve, sized against the household's existing metal exposure — not a
"quality-at-own-history-discount" pick. If the desk affirmatively wants levered gold-AUM exposure,
SII is a defensible expression; it should be adjudicated in that queue, at that sizing, not this one.
