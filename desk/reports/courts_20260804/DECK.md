## Mode B — LED. The divergence
Management **raised full-year EPS guidance** and the sell-side forward EPS is **$8.36 against
trailing $7.03 = +19%**. The quarter just reported had operating income **−6.1%** and margin
**−190bps**. Those two things cannot both be trend.

**So the estimate line and the operating line are pointed in opposite directions**, and the gap is
being bridged by buyback (EPS +1.1% on net income −6.5% implies ~7% share-count reduction) and by
assumed tariff mitigation. The de-rate is the market discounting the *estimate* line toward the
*operating* line. That is a legitimate de-rate — the business is intact, the multiple is
compressing on a nameable, non-terminal cause — but it is **not** the HUBS/CTSH/SAP pattern of
"multiple compressed on *stable or rising* estimates." Estimates are rising while the underlying
operating trend falls. Half-winner, half-warning; hence 5/10 and not higher.

Peer check: ONON −23.9% and NKE −44.3% over 12 months vs DECK **−12.4%** — DECK is the *best*
performer in a bad footwear tape, so this is not a sector-beta drawdown, and equally there is no
sector recovery to ride.
