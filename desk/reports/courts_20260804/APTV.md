### B3. Growth-over-market has collapsed — CONFIRMED from the filing, not inferred

The seed asks whether the historic +6-8pp growth-over-market still holds. The 10-Q states it directly:

> "Our volumes increased 3% for the three months ended June 30, 2026 … compared to **flat** global automotive
> production (**flat on an Aptiv weighted market basis**)… Our volumes increased **1%** for the six months …

- **Q2-26 GoM = +3pp. H1-26 GoM = +2pp.** Cross-check on the full year: FY26 guide $12,700M mid against a derived
  FY25 New-Aptiv base of ~$12,354M = **+2.8% reported / +2% adjusted** (management's own midpoint
  characterisation), against a market of −1% to −2% ⇒ **GoM ≈ +3 to +4pp**. The two methods agree.
- **The seed's "+6-8pp historic" benchmark is NOT verifiable from primary sources and is not relied on here.**
  What the record actually shows: FY2020/21/22 10-Ks printed "above-market sales growth of 10% / 15% / 11%" —
  wording that is ambiguous as to spread-versus-total — and **Aptiv has printed no such number in the three 10-Ks
  since, nor on the Q2-26 call.** So the honest statement is not "GoM fell from 6-8pp to 2-3pp"; it is

- Either way this is content-per-vehicle deceleration, not production beta.

Also structural, from the same MD&A: 2025 global production was **+4% but Aptiv's weighted market only +1%** —
because Aptiv is underweight China (which grew 10%) and overweight NA/EMEA (which fell). **Aptiv's own addressable
market structurally underperforms global production by ~3pp/yr on current mix.** That is a permanent headwind
independent of any cycle.

**Answer to the court question:** the FY cut is *both* production beta and content deceleration, but the GoM
compression from ~+6-8pp to +2-3pp is the larger and the more permanent of the two.

Peer-filed 2026 production assumptions (pulled from their own 8-K/6-K rather than a paywalled forecast, which is
better evidence): **Autoliv 8-K 2026-07-17 — global LVP "will decline by around 2.5%"**; **BorgWarner 8-K

with NA nudged UP (14.9M → 15.0M) and Europe UP (16.6M → 16.8M) but China CUT 0.8M (32.0M → 31.2M, −2.5%)**.
Magna's revision independently corroborates that the 2026 production weakness is **China-specific, not global** —
