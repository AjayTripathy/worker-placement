## 3. MODE B — FIRST PRINCIPLES, DISCONFIRMING

Decisive question with no promoter framing: **what am I actually buying at 8.9x, and is its quality improving or deteriorating?**

**Finding B1 — the earnings mix is deteriorating in quality even as EPS grows.** All enterprise growth came from the capital-intensive, risk-bearing, cyclically-*peaking* insurance leg (+17%), while the asset-light toll-booth that justifies the multiple shrank (−2%). And 2026 is a *good* year for the medical-cost cycle (UNH's Q2 MCR came 180bps light; ELV/CNC rallying). You are paying for the toll-booth and being handed insurance earnings at the top of their cycle. **Convexity check: ~100bps of MCR on the FY26 CH premium base ≈ $470M pre-tax ≈ $1.44/share. A 200bp cycle turn is ~−$2.9/share.**

**Finding B2 — the buyback engine has stopped, and it is a balance-sheet constraint, not a choice.** Verified from audited XBRL and the 10-Q:
- FY25: repurchased **11.9M shares / $3.6B**. H1-25 alone: 8.2M shares / $2.6B.
- H1-26: **0.9M shares / $250M** — down ~90% y/y. **No authorization top-up** ($6,730M remaining in Jan–Apr, $6,480M in May–Jun; the $250M decline is pure repurchase).
- Shares outstanding: 273.7M (Jan-25) → 263.5M (Jan-26) → **264.2M (Jul-24-26). The count went UP in 2026** — option issuance now exceeds buyback.
- Why: short-term debt **tripled to $2.79B** (from $592M); total debt $31.9B; debt-to-cap 42.8%; and of $6.6B cash & short-term investments, **only ~$0.8B sits at the parent or non-regulated subsidiaries.**
- **The FY26 guide explicitly "includes the impact of expected future share repurchases" and assumed WASO of 261–265M. Actual shares sit at 264.2M — the top of the range. The $30.45 has essentially no share-count support.**

This is a genuine new negative the 2026-07-22 court did not price.

**Finding B3 — an $88M unattributed litigation swing.** Special items (Note 15) carry a Q2-26 litigation charge of **$77M pre-tax / $0.28 per share** against **$0 in Q2-25**, reversing an $11M *benefit* in Q1-26. Note 14 (Contingencies) names **not one matter** — it is word-for-word identical to the Q1-26 boilerplate. A charge with no named matter is not proof of anything, but it is the escalation tell and it is now on the watch list (K5).

**Finding B4 — "adjusted" excludes a multi-year restructuring.** The strategic optimization program (Note 12) has cost **$748M severance + $109M asset impairments** program-to-date and "will continue through 2028" — ~$3.25/share of cumulative cost permanently excluded from adjusted EPS. Standard practice, but it is a real cash cost and the adjusted number flatters it.

**Finding B5 — the IFP exit is announced but unquantified.** One sentence in MD&A, first appearing in this 10-Q: *"In April 2026, the Company announced its planned exit from the Individual and Family Plans medical business as of January 1, 2027."* No cost, no charge, no exit-liability accrual, no ASC 420 disclosure, and no mention in the notes. IFP premiums run ~$860M/quarter. A Q3 or Q4 exit charge is a live, unquantified possibility.

### Honesty adjudication — the point of this desk
Marketed takeaway: *"Reports Strong Second Quarter 2026 Results, Raises 2026 Outlook."* The underlying data: the strategic asset is in profit decline, the cyclical asset is at peak, and the "raise" is $0.10 (0.33%) — a rounding reaffirm dressed as a raise.

**But the divergence is EMPHASIS, not concealment.** The release discloses PBS −27% in plain numbers, names the driver, discloses the −4% pharmacy customer decline, discloses the MCR and its base effect. Per the honesty-boundary doctrine, headline framing over honest body disclosure is a **REVIEW_FLAG, not an ELEVATE.** And the market plainly read it — the stock fell 3% on the print and 9% since.

**There is no honesty alpha in CI. Nothing is hidden and the divergence is already priced. This is a valuation question, not an integrity question.** Recording that as a negative finding is itself the useful output: it is the anti-masking result, and it keeps CI classified as carry rather than edge.

## 5. RULING

### Classification: **RP_FAIR (unchanged). 5/10. HOLD + RE-SIZE DOWN.**

Why not higher despite an +18pp modelled edge: the fairness is verified and the tails are bounded, but the "edge" is cheapness plus de-rate-recovery — **not a calibratable alpha overlay.** The market's stated concern (PBS −27%, pharmacy attrition, GLP-1 deceleration) is *correct and disclosed*; there is no information asymmetry to harvest. Per the fairly-paid-risk doctrine this is ownable as carry at a capped size, and the edge overlay is not claimed for calibration.

Why not lower: the position's **two pre-committed kill tripwires were tested against the actual print and both refuted.** Estimates rose three times this year while the multiple fell ~20%. Selling into that on price action alone is precisely the sell-the-cut behaviour §6 forbids.

### 5a. HARVEST RULING: **DO NOT HARVEST.** (Decisive — this was the live question.)

Checked `desk/data/edge_classifications/TLH_CORE_20260802.json`: **CI IS a designated TLH_CORE build leg** — target $80,000, axis `healthcare_managed_care`, currently ~12% built ($9.2k of $80k), rotation partner MCK.

The tax mechanics resolve this cleanly and against harvesting:
- Harvest banks a **~$660 short-term loss ≈ ~$330 benefit** at ~50% combined.
- It simultaneously opens a **31-day wash-sale window to ~2026-09-05**, during which lots 2 and 3 cannot be repurchased without disallowance.
- **That freezes the intended remaining deployment to bank $330** — in a name sitting at its 13-week low with both of its own kill tripwires refuted. The ratio is absurd.
- A $660 loss is **0.02% of the $3.3M base.** It is not a TLH asset worth managing. The position's job is to become large enough to *become* one later.

**Ruling: HOLD. Do not sell.** The TLH-core build survives the print — at a reduced size.

### 5b. SIZE: cut the TLH_CORE target **$80,000 → $40,000** (1.21% of $3.3M = the contract's ruled per-name cap)

Three verified reasons, each a business-risk finding → per the response taxonomy, business risk cuts SIZE (it does not kill and it is not a price gate):
1. **The Evernorth guide has been frozen at $6,900 all year** while only the insurance leg was raised. The toll-booth thesis is not being confirmed by the company's own guidance.

3. The healthcare axis already carries HCA and ISRG per the contract; $80,000 (2.4%) breached the ruled 1.2% cap regardless.

## 8. FROZEN CALL — **PROPOSE ONLY**

**Key: `CI|2026-10-29`** · **our_p = 0.68**

**Bar:** *At its Q3-2026 earnings release, The Cigna Group reports Evernorth Specialty & Care Services adjusted pre-tax income of at least **$1,021M** (≥ +10% vs Q3-2025's $928M) **AND** does not cut the FY26 Evernorth pre-tax guide below $6,900M.*

Against: generic/biosimilar adoption is explicitly cited as a growth driver and is volume-positive but price-negative; Q3 laps a +11% comp; and the Evernorth guide, while never cut, has also never been raised.

### Four-idea frame
1. **THE CALL** — S&C ≥ +10% y/y **and** the Evernorth guide holds. p = 0.68.
2. **WHAT MAKES US WRONG** — S&C decelerates below +10%. That would mean the specialty engine carrying 68% of Evernorth pre-tax is itself compressing, not just the PBS spread leg. The "melting ice cube is only a third of the business" defence collapses and the bear's 7.7x is the right multiple.
3. **WHAT WE LEARN EITHER WAY** — whether PBS −27% is a *contained, self-inflicted rebate-free transition* (base/bull) or the leading edge of enterprise-wide PBM economics compression (bear). That is the precise question the −10% slide is arguing about, and it resolves in **one line of one table.**
4. **POSITION CONSEQUENCE** — HIT: the ladder stays, the $40k target is defensible, consider restoring toward $60k. MISS: K2 fires — exit the starter, cancel both rungs, do not average into a compressing specialty book.
