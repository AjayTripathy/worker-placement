## 4. CAPITAL STRUCTURE AND THE TWO REAL RISKS

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Shares outstanding (2026-07-23) | 158,847,369 |
| **Market cap @ $62.23** | **$9.885B** |
| Debt (6/30/26) | ~$2.0B ($1.0B 5.625% notes due 2033 + $1.0B TLB at S+175) |
| Cash | ~$750M · **net debt ~$1.25B → ~1.3x** |
| **EV** | **~$11.14B** |
| **EV / FY26E adj EBITDA (mid $1,045M)** | **10.7x** |
| **P/E on FY26E adj EPS (mid $2.85)** | **21.8x** |
| Dividend | $0.075/qtr = 0.48% yield · **no buyback authorization exists** (10-K Item 5: "None") |

**Risk 1 — the consideration is eroding.** At $62.23, ESI holders receive $10.00 + 0.500 × $62.23 =
**$41.12**, versus the **$50.10 implied at announcement — down 17.9%**. The SOLS decline mechanically
shrinks what ESI holders get, which raises the probability the ESI vote fails or the deal must be
sweetened. This cuts both ways and is the central special-situation variable.

**Risk 2 — an uncapped environmental indemnity next to a conspicuous disclosure hole.**
Under the Separation Agreement Solstice was "generally contractually allocated **all environmental
liabilities associated with the Solstice Advanced Materials business**" and must indemnify Honeywell
"**including toxic tort or other hazardous material exposure claims, for uncapped amounts**"
(10-K Item 1A). Recorded reserve: **$54M** at 6/30/26, and the company states it **cannot estimate a
range of reasonably possible loss in excess of recorded liabilities**.

**The honesty flag:** the strings *PFAS*, *polyfluoroalkyl*, *PFOA*, and *forever chemical* appear
**ZERO times** across the FY2025 10-K, the Q2-26 10-Q, and the Form 10 information statement — only four
hits of generic "future regulations related to fluorinated chemistries." For a company whose largest
segment is fluorine-value-chain refrigerants, that is an **absence of expected disclosure**, and it sits
directly against an uncapped toxic-tort indemnity backed by a $54M reserve. Per doctrine, absence of
expected masking is a finding — and so is absence of expected *disclosure*. I am not asserting a
liability exists; I am recording that a $9.9B fluorochemicals company has not addressed the single most
predictable tail in its industry (HFO degradation to TFA is an active EU regulatory question).
**Classification: REVIEW flag, not ELEVATE** — it is an omission of a risk topic, not a divergence
between a marketed takeaway and disclosed data.

## 7. VERDICT — **WATCH 5/10**, no capital at spot

The best residual asset in this batch: beat-and-raised four days ago, 25% EBITDA margins, a sole-source
US uranium-conversion franchise growing 27%, and a genuine de-rate rather than a derailment. But you
cannot buy the standalone business — you can only buy it wrapped in a $14.5B, 44%-dilutive,
sub-investment-grade acquisition that will not close until 1H-2027, plus an uncapped and unquantified
environmental indemnity. A +10.9% edge does not pay for that stack.

**Entry band $54–58** (below spot; brackets the 07-29 low of $53.87 and the pre-announcement deal-adjusted
zone). **Size 0.4% initial, 0.8% cap** — half the ruled cap, because the deal, not the business, sets the
variance. Limits only. **Tranche, do not chase.**

**Dated kills:**
- ESI vote fails AND SOLS does not re-rate above $70 within 20 sessions → thesis was deal-arb, not asset → exit.
- FY26 adj EBITDA guide cut below $1,000M at any print → the de-rate was a derailment after all → exit.
- Any PFAS/TFA-related litigation or reserve increase >$150M → exit (the uncapped tail is live).
- Pro-forma net leverage guided above 4.0x at close → exit.
