## THE COURT: is the mark inside EBIT? — intake hypothesis REFUTED, answer worse elsewhere

The company's preferred "operational NPAT $12.4M" also strips the $5.85M associate loss and a $2.57M prior-year tax under-provision; taken at face value that is **24.4x** (5.5c/share on the 225.3M year-end count — FY25 EPS was struck on a weighted 198.9M).

## Ruling — 3/10, NOT A DRAWDOWN
The frame is quality-at-own-history-discount and CAPD fails it three ways. It is **not a de-rate on stable estimates** — revenue estimates are *rising* and twice reiterated, while earnings estimates **do not exist** and management has pre-warned that demobilisation costs consume the H1 beat. It is **not a derailment** either — the business is real, growing 37.6%, refinanced to 2029/2030, and has just exited its worst loss centre. What it is: a **junior-gold-cycle round-trip**, +60% then −36%, in a company where ~38% of market cap is a three-stock junior-gold equity book at fair value through P&L, and where the operating business delivers **$3.4M statutory / $12.4M adjusted** of attributable earnings against $303M of cap. Owning CAPD means owning an undiscounted, unhedged, concentrated junior-gold fund stapled to an African-weighted services roll-up. Secondary label **SCREEN ERROR** — fields `ebit`/`am`, authority AR25 Notes 10/11/32; corrected figure **8.2x audited operating profit and 24–90x owner earnings**, not 7.6x.
