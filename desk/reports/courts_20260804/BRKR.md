## 3. COURT ITEM 2 — BOOKINGS vs REVENUE: WHICH IS THE LEADING TRUTH?

**Finding: bookings are the leading truth for *revenue* — and revenue is not the problem. The bull's best pillar does not address the actual risk.**

- BSI organic bookings **+10%** yoy; book-to-bill **>1.0x for four consecutive quarters**; revenue organic +2.8% in Q2 (-0.8% H1). Bookings are outrunning revenue by ~7pts, so backlog has been building for a year.
- Bruker's instrument lead times run ~6–12 months (NMR magnets longer, mass spec shorter, semi metrology 6–9 months). Mechanically, revenue should converge toward bookings growth with a 2–4 quarter lag. That supports **FY27 revenue** and is the most credible part of the bull case. Management's *"significant organic margin expansion and non-GAAP EPS growth not only this year, but in 2027 as well"* has a real leading indicator behind its revenue leg.
- **But the FY26 guide's binding constraint is margin, not revenue.** H2-26 needs only +2.2–3.8% revenue — already covered by backlog. Bookings tell you nothing about whether operating margin steps 410–480bps sequentially. **The bull pillar and the bear risk are on different axes.** That mismatch is the central finding of this court.

**Verification gap (top UNVERIFIABLE, item 1 of 2):** Bruker discloses **no backlog dollar figure and no reconciliation** for bookings or book-to-bill anywhere in the press release or the FY25 10-K. Both are unaudited management metrics. "+10% organic bookings" is BSI-only (~92% of revenue) and instrument orders are lumpy — a small number of large semi or NMR orders could carry it, and no order-size distribution is given. **The single most load-bearing bull datum in this name cannot be verified from any primary source.**

## 4. COURT ITEM 3 — THE IMPAIRMENT READ

**This is the strongest adversarial finding in the court, and it comes entirely from audited primary documents.**

The Q2-26 press release discloses the $134.9M in one line with **no reporting-unit attribution**. The units were given only on the call: **automation and spatial biology** (Quiver Quantitative, 2026-08-04). The FY25 10-K (filed 2026-02-27, Note 6 / critical accounting estimates) names them precisely — and reveals that this is the **second** impairment of the same assets:

> *"The results of the valuation indicated that the carrying amount of the **Bruker Spatial Biology ("BSB") reporting unit** within the Company's BSI NANO Segment and **Automation ("AUT") reporting unit** within the Company's BSI BioSpin Segment exceeded their fair value. As a result, during the year ended December 31, 2025, the Company recorded a **goodwill impairment charge of $96.5 million**…"*
> *"The Company recorded **no goodwill impairment for the years ended December 31, 2024, and 2023.**"*

| Event | Amount | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 2025 | **$96.5M** | BSB + AUT |
| Q2 2026 | **$134.9M** | BSB + AUT ("spatial biology" + "automation") |
| **Cumulative in 12 months** | **$231.4M** | **the same two reporting units, twice** |

**Deal attribution — now fully verified from the 10-K (this replaces my earlier estimate):**

| Deal | Closed | → Reporting unit | Purchase (net of cash) | Goodwill booked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Chemspeed Technologies AG** | Mar-2024 | BioSpin → **Automation** | **$174.8M** | $127.8M |
| **NanoString** (Ch.11 asset purchase) | May-2024 | NANO → **Spatial Biology** | **$392.1M** | $253.8M |
| **Combined** | | | **$566.9M** | **$381.6M** |

The Q3-2025 charge split is disclosed: **BSB $54.0M / AUT $42.5M**. Cumulative $231.4M therefore equals:

> **60.6% of all goodwill originally booked on Chemspeed + NanoString**, and **40.8% of their combined $566.9M purchase price** — written off in 21 months.

*(Bounding the Q2 split, which awaits the 10-Q: AUT's original goodwill was $127.8M and $42.5M is already gone, so **at least $49.6M of the Q2-26 charge must be Spatial Biology**.)*

*Two premise corrections for the record:* **PhenomeX/Berkeley Lights is a 2023 deal, not 2024**, and Spectral Instruments and Nion closed in 2024.

**PhenomeX carried ZERO goodwill** — it was a *bargain purchase* ($109.4M price, $0 goodwill, $32.0M intangibles, a **+$144.1M gain**). This is analytically important: PhenomeX is half of the merged BSB+Cellular Analysis unit that triggered the Q2 test, but **it cannot itself source a goodwill write-down.** The goodwill at risk in the merged unit is essentially all NanoString's. Bounding the Q2 split accordingly: if the entire $134.9M landed on BSB, cumulative BSB would be $188.9M = **74% of NanoString's goodwill and 48% of its purchase price** — plausible, but **UNVERIFIED until the 10-Q**. The floor established above ($49.6M) is the defensible number today.

The Q3-25 test used discount rates of **10.0%–19.5%** — the top of that range is a venture-grade cost of capital being applied to businesses acquired 12 months earlier.

**And the units are being restructured, not just impaired:** Q1-2026 launched a **BSI NANO restructuring plan** ($12.3M charge) covering division mergers, headcount reduction, facility consolidation, and — the telling item — **discontinuation of certain product offerings.** Products acquired in 2024 are being killed in 2026.

### The buried liability — both impaired units carry PERPETUAL royalties

This is new and it is the reason these units cannot simply grow out of the problem:

- **10x Genomics settlement (2025-05-12): $68.0M in four quarterly installments *plus ongoing royalties on GeoMx and CosMx*** — i.e. on NanoString's entire product line.
- **AbCellera / UBC settlement (2025-12-17): $36.0M *plus ongoing royalties on the Beacon Optofluidic platform*** (Bruker Cellular Analysis, merged into BSB in Q2-26).

**$104.0M of cash on top of a $566.9M purchase price, and a permanent royalty drag on the revenue of both impaired businesses.** The legal accrual fell $86.0M → $5.4M. A business carrying a perpetual royalty on its whole product line has a structurally lower terminal margin than the acquisition model assumed — which is precisely what a twice-failed DCF looks like from the inside.

### Segment evidence — the impaired segment loses money

FY2025 segment operating margin is disclosed on **two bases that both foot to $68.2M**, and the divergence is the story:

| Segment | FY25 Rev | CODM basis (Note 20) | **Fully-allocated (MD&A)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| BSI BioSpin | $878.8M | 17.8% | 11.9% |
| BSI CALID | $1,210.2M | 21.7% | 12.7% |
| **BSI NANO** | **$1,084.3M** | 9.3% | **−6.6%** |
| BEST | $270.9M | 9.9% | 7.2% |

**BSI NANO — the segment holding Spatial Biology — lost money in FY2025 on a fully-allocated basis.** And in Q1-26 (CODM basis) *every* segment compressed: BioSpin 14.6% (from 17.9%), CALID 19.8% (from 22.0%), NANO **5.9%** (from 9.0%), BEST 11.4%.

### Disclosure credit where due — the charge was pre-announced

The Q1-2026 10-Q (filed 2026-05-07) told the market this was coming:

> *"Subsequent to March 31, 2026, the Company completed the **merger of the Bruker Spatial Biology division and the Bruker Cellular Analysis Business Unit**… This merger **triggered an interim goodwill impairment test**, and if there is any resulting impairment charge, **it will be recorded in the second quarter of 2026.**"*

**The impairment was not news on 2026-08-04 — it was disclosed as pending three months earlier.** This is a real integrity credit, and it independently confirms the §7 reading that the -22% was about the second-half guide, not the charge.

### Not exited — subsumed

Effective ~2026-07-01 Bruker reorganised into four groups (BioSpin, Nano, **BMID** carved out of CALID, BEST), targeting *"an additional $20 million of cost reductions in fiscal year 2027."* **No divestiture of Spatial Biology or Automation was announced, and no revenue, margin, or breakeven timeline was given for either unit.** The CFO's only characterisation was that they *"continue to experience operating losses."*

Two consequences: the loss-making units stay in the P&L funding the FY27 margin promise, and the segment re-cut will **break yoy segment comparability from Q3-26** — a transparency degradation arriving exactly when the ramp needs grading. Add it to the monitoring list.

**Is it a clean write-off of overpayment? No — and this is the point.**

A goodwill impairment is triggered when fair value falls below carrying value, and Bruker states it computed fair value using *"a weighted combination of the market approach and the income approach,"* where the income approach is *"a discounted cash flow model with inputs developed using both internal and market-based data."* **The DCF inputs are management's own forward operating forecasts for those units.**

So: in Q3-2025 management wrote these units down to a fair value derived from its own forecast. **Nine months later that forecast was wrong again, requiring a second and larger write-down.** Management's forward-forecasting of these businesses has now failed twice inside twelve months — and the second failure was disclosed **in the same release as a guide that requires 100% of the year's EPS growth to come from management's forward operating forecasts.** That is the cleanest available evidence on forecast integrity, and it is unfavourable.

**Margin-story impact:** the charge is non-cash and excluded from non-GAAP, so it has **zero** effect on the $2.10–2.15 guide. But BSB sits in BSI NANO and AUT in BSI BioSpin — and those are precisely the two segments that are not growing (H1-26: **BioSpin $393.2M, -2.5%**; **Nano $507.3M, -0.3%**; all H1 growth came from CALID at +10.7%, itself acquisition-driven via ELITechGroup). The impaired units are a drag on the very organic-margin-expansion program the H2 guide depends on.

**Live further-impairment risk.** The FY25 10-K states management judged it *"more likely than not that the fair value of **four** of the Company's reporting units was less than their carrying amount"* and ran quantitative tests on all four; two failed. **Two more units were at the line at year-end 2025.** Goodwill + intangibles + other long-term assets are **$3,113.8M against total shareholders' equity of $2,400.2M** — tangible equity is materially negative, and the FY26 annual test lands in Q4.
