## 2. De-rate vs derailment

**Neither — and the contract's frame does not apply.** The HUBS/CTSH/SAP winner pattern is a multiple
compressing on *stable or rising* estimates. Here the estimates are falling **and the multiple is
falling with them**: volume guided down 13%, unit cost guided up 8–29%, and the realised-price
tailwind has already reversed (Q1-26 realised $4,820 → Q2-26 $4,535 → spot $4,156; the last 30
sessions average $4,066). That is the derailment shape. What makes it *not* a derailment in the
usual sense is that the derailment is **commodity-cyclical and the balance sheet absorbs it**: net
cash is 46% of the market cap, there is no debt, and there are no dilutive instruments.

**Call it what it is: a commodity beta unwind sitting on top of an undisclosed depletion clock.**

## 5. PFIC — the shelf's "clean" tag will break (US taxable book)

The shelf reports `pfic: false`, `pfic_severity: "clean"`, `pfic_fmv_share: 0.308` — computed on
**stale Dec-25 cash of $137.8M**. Recompute on §1297's FMV basis for a publicly traded issuer
(total assets ≈ market cap + liabilities = $489.3M + $27.1M = **$516.4M**):

| Date | Cash | Passive share of FMV assets |
|---|---|---|
| 31-Dec-2025 (shelf's input) | $137.8M | 26.7% |
| **30-Jun-2026 (actual)** | **$207.5M** | **40.2%** |
| 31-Dec-2026 (est., gold $4,100) | ~$280M | **54.2%** |
| 31-Dec-2026 (gold holds $4,500) | ~$335M | **64.9%** |

**§1297(a) is disjunctive** — *either* 75% passive income *or* ≥50% passive assets makes it a PFIC.
On income THX is unambiguously active, so the **house screen's conjunctive gate will not fire** — but
the statutory asset test can trip on its own, and on this trajectory it trips **within two quarters**,
before any production stops. It gets far worse in the 2027–28 production gap: a company with
$350–450M of cash and a mine that has ceased producing, pre-Douta, fails the asset test outright.
Consequence in a taxable US account: §1291 excess-distribution treatment unless a QEF election is
available (an AIM/TSXV microcap is unlikely to furnish a PFIC Annual Information Statement) or an
MTM election under §1296, which taxes unrealised gains as ordinary income annually.

**This is a reason to decline that is independent of the fundamentals, and it is the addendum's
"de-rate manufactures status" mechanism running through depletion instead of price.**
→ **Routed to the principal: the conjunctive PFIC gate under-fires on a depleting producer that is
accumulating cash. Recommend an `asset_test_trajectory` overlay.**

   taken into a $2,800 gold tape and $254M of peak-cycle cash is converted into a marginal mine.
