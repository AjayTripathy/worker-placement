## 3. DE-RATE vs DERAILMENT — the cliff is being navigated, and it is paid for
The classic patent-cliff decomposition, resolved by the Q2 print:
- **Cliff leg (Eylea):** legacy EYLEA is eroding to biosimilars, but EYLEA HD has now **crossed over**
  and is the larger product. That is the transition the bears priced as impossible in 2024-25.
- **Engine leg (Dupixent):** >$6B and still compounding; Regeneron collects a collaboration share
  rather than gross sales, which is why the reported gross margin (44.5%) understates the economics.
- **The honest residual:** consensus FY27 EPS (**50.21**) is still **BELOW** FY26 (**52.60**). The
  Street models an *earnings decline* next year. The cliff is navigated, not finished.
- **Margin:** operating margin has gone 38.9% (FY22) -> 25.8% (FY25) on R&D intensity. Simply Wall St
  (08-01) headlined "margin squeeze despite 17% revenue growth". Revenue growth is not translating.
Ruling: **DE-RATE that has already re-rated.** Business intact, estimates rising, multiple recovered.
