## 3. DE-RATE vs DERAILMENT — neither; it is a levered no-growth cash cow
Revenue derailed two years ago and has stabilised flat. EPS grinds up because the share count falls.
The relevant question is not de-rate-vs-derailment, it is **value trap vs carry** — and the answer
turns entirely on how the yield is measured.

## 4. CAP STRUCTURE FIRST — and the correction to the queue's "14% FCF yield"
Q1'26 (2026-03-31) balance sheet: shares **242.23M** (down from 249.09M at 12-31 = **-2.8% in one
quarter**); total debt **$6,414M**; cash **$1,254M** -> **net debt $5,160M**.
**EV = 242.23M x 26.11 + 5,160M = $11.48B.**
TTM free cash flow = 124.0 + 101.2 + 279.4 + 304.9 = **$809.5M**.
- **FCF / equity market cap = 809.5 / 6,325 = 12.8%** <- this is the "14% FCF yield" in the queue note.
- **FCF / EV = 809.5 / 11,480 = 7.1%** <- this is the unlevered economics.
The gap between those two numbers **is** the debt. At ~2.9x net-debt/EBITDA (adj. EBITDA ~$1.8B), a
12.8% equity yield is a 7.1% asset yield plus financial leverage. **That is not a bargain; it is a
carry trade whose distributions (a ~4.2% dividend, ~$265M/yr, plus $611M TTM of buybacks) are being
funded ahead of a $6.4B debt stack.** Calling it a 14% FCF yield without naming the leverage is the
kind of takeaway-vs-data divergence this desk exists to catch — here it is in our own screen note.
Also: goodwill $7,325M against total equity $3,964M -> **tangible book is about -$4.9B**. Under the
tier-1/2 frame book value is irrelevant, so this is disclosed for the debt-capacity question only.
SBC is genuinely low: ~$20M/quarter = **1.5% of revenue** (real software honesty credit).

## 5. AI-COMPLEX POSITION (frozen house call AI-BREAK|2027-12-31 @ 0.45)
OpenText is an AI **adopter**, not AI-capex-levered: agentic-AI and sovereign-cloud investment
(EUR 105M, 400 jobs in Cork/Galway, 06-12; S3NS/Google Cloud partnership 04-13). Does the entry
require the AI cycle holding? **No.** Does the de-rate price the break? **Not applicable** — the
multiple is 14x EV/FCF, a no-growth multiple, not an AI multiple. The genuine AI exposure is the
opposite tail: if agentic AI commoditises content-services middleware faster than OpenText can sell
it, the flat revenue line becomes a declining one. That risk is not in the 45% house call at all.
