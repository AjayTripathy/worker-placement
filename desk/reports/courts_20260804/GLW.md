## THE AI-BREAK CONFLICT — stated, not averaged away

**Does the entry require the cycle holding? YES, unambiguously.**
FY27 consensus is $22.86bn revenue (+18.9%) with Optical the engine (+32% and accelerating,
Enterprise +65%). That is a straight-line extrapolation of hyperscaler capex. Strip it and the
residual is display glass, specialty materials, Hemlock polysilicon and a loss-making solar segment
— a ~$10–11bn, mid-teens-margin, GDP-growth business.

**Does the de-rate already price the break? NO.**
- Cycle holds → 34.8× FY27 core EPS on 28% growth. PEG ~1.25. Defensible, not cheap.
- Cycle breaks by end-2027 (our p=0.45) → Optical growth stops and the *estimates fall with the
  multiple*. Plausible FY27 core EPS ~$2.90 at a 15–18× "industrial glass" multiple = **$44–52.**

This is the structural trap: the only scenario in which GLW gets cheap is the scenario in which its
earnings power is also impaired. There is no state of the world where you get the franchise at a
discount with the growth intact. That asymmetry is why the answer is no at $149.65 and would still
be no at $120.

**Concentration overlay:** the household is already ~26% AI-complex. Adding an 80.6%-IV,
34.8×-FY27 AI-infrastructure name is the wrong marginal position even if the standalone were a 5/10.
