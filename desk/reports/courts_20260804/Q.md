## 3. THE AI-BREAK CONFLICT — stated explicitly, not averaged away

House call frozen at **AI-BREAK | 2027-12-31 @ p=0.45**. Household AI-complex exposure already ~$5.2M / 26%.

**Management's own quantification is the only hard number that exists:** "the data center/high
performance computing/artificial intelligence end market accounts for **approximately 15% of our 2024 net
sales** and is our fastest-growing sector" (Form 10 information statement). No updated AI-revenue figure
has been published — the FY25/FY26 share is **UNVERIFIABLE**.

**Does the entry require the cycle holding?** Partially, and more than the 15% suggests. AI is ~15% of
the base but is doing a disproportionate share of the *growth*, and the qualitative attribution in the
10-K ties both segments' volume gains to advanced nodes, advanced packaging and thermal management —
i.e. the AI complex. Consumables economics soften the blow (Qnity sells per-wafer materials, not
equipment, so revenue falls far less than capex in a downturn), but at **~33.6x forward earnings** the
multiple carries cycle optimism regardless of the revenue mix.

**Does the de-rate already price the break?** **No.** The peak multiple was ~44x forward; today's ~33.6x
is a normalisation off an extreme, not the ~22–24x a genuine capex break would impose. The −24% has
priced *some* enthusiasm out, not the house scenario. **Do not treat this de-rate as AI-break protection.**
