## 5. THE MONEE CREDIT BOOK — the top UNVERIFIABLE and the real risk
Monee is a **consumer lender operating inside its own marketplace**, with 20M borrowers. In that
structure, provisioning policy is the honesty channel: a marketplace that both originates the demand
and books the loan can defer credit recognition by extending duration, and the P&L looks like
operating leverage until it doesn't. I could not pull NPL ratios, provision-to-loan trends, or
vintage curves from any primary source in this pass. **This is a gap, not a clean.** It is also the
single input that would move the bear scenario more than any other.

## 8. FOUR-IDEA FRAME
1. **Consensus idea:** three compounding engines (Shopee/Garena/Monee), 30% revenue growth, first
   real operating leverage, 2.1x forward sales with $7B net cash.
2. **Bear idea:** the operating leverage is *credit* — Monee's contribution grows faster than the
   e-commerce economics and its provisions are pro-cyclical; the SEA consumer plus a Brazil build
   plus TikTok Shop means the take rate is the battleground, not the GMV.
3. **Our variant:** the leg that actually slowed is *sequential* revenue (+3.6% q/q in Q1'26 after
   three quarters of 9-15%) — earlier than the annual figures reveal, and the market has been
   discounting it since September 2025. The de-rate is legitimate; the entry timing is not, 8 days
   out from the print that resolves it.
4. **The idea that kills ours:** if Monee's book is prime-skewed and short-duration, the "credit
   cycle" bear is a phantom, the operating leverage is durable, and 21x FY27 for a 21%-grower with
   net cash is simply cheap. We cannot currently distinguish these two worlds.
