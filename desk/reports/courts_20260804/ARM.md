## 3. Which leg of the AI cycle funds the earnings

Two legs, and the marketed one is not the big one.

- **Royalty $715M (55% of revenue).** The majority of this remains **smartphone and consumer** unit royalties — a mature, unit-driven, Armv9/CSS-rate-uplift story. Data-centre royalties "more than doubled" but off a base small enough that total royalty growth was still only +22%. Arithmetic: if the total grew 22% and one component doubled, the rest grew appreciably less than 22%. The letter does not disclose the split, and that omission is the single largest disclosure gap in this name.
- **Licence $574M (45%).** Lumpy by construction. Management's own normalized metric — **ACV — grew 13%.** The gap between +23% reported and +13% ACV is roughly 10pp of timing. The shareholder letter reports both, so this is disclosed, not concealed; but the marketed takeaway ("record first-quarter licensing and royalty revenue") leans on the lumpy line while the run-rate line grew at half the pace.

**The AI leg is genuinely real** — NVIDIA Vera in full production on Arm; Google Axion as TPU host CPU; AWS Graviton5 with a multi-year Meta agreement for "tens of millions of cores"; Azure Cobalt 200 on Neoverse CSS; Qualcomm Dragonfly C1000. Those are corroborated third-party commitments, and they are why the royalty mix is improving. The objection is not that the AI leg is fake. It is the price and the margin structure.

## 4. The finding the screen's quality gate cannot see

The screen admitted ARM on `gross_margin_pct = 98%`. In March-2026 ARM launched the **Arm AGI CPU — production silicon**, and this quarter told shareholders it has "**secured the manufacturing capacity required**" for what is now a "**greater than $2 billion**" demand book across FY27–FY28, up from $1B a quarter earlier.

Selling silicon is a 30–55%-gross-margin business. IP licensing is a 98%-gross-margin business.

**The growth leg ARM is marketing is the leg that mechanically destroys the quality metric on which it was selected.** It also converts ARM from a zero-capital royalty model into one carrying **capacity commitments** — a take-or-pay-shaped obligation — entered into precisely into a cycle the house prices at a 45% chance of breaking by end-2027. This is the same structural exposure the AI-BREAK call is written about, arriving at a company the market still values as pure IP.

**Encode:** a gross-margin quality gate is invalid when the company's own disclosed growth driver is a business-model move down the stack. The gate must read the *marginal* mix, not the trailing blended margin.
