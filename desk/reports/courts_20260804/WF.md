## 1. THE TWO FACTS THAT DECIDE THE CASE

### 1.1 — ₩581bn of FY2025 profit is a one-time bargain-purchase gain

> "The eight-fold increase in other non-operating income was primarily attributable to **a bargain
> purchase gain of ₩581 billion that was recognized in 2025**, which in turn was attributable to our
> acquisitions of Tongyang Life Insurance and ABL Life Insurance in **July 2025**."
> — **Woori 20-F FY2025, filed 2026-04-22, MD&A "Other Non-Operating Income (Expenses)"** ✅ read directly

A non-cash IFRS 3 gain from buying two insurers below net asset value. Real (it added book), but it is
not earnings power, it does not repeat, and — because the deals closed in **July 2025** — **it sits in
H2-2025, which is exactly the half inside the trailing-twelve-month window.** Corroborated by the
effective tax rate falling to 21.1% in 2025 from 24.9% (bargain-purchase gains are largely non-taxable).

| | as reported | ex-bargain-gain |
|---|---|---|
| FY2025 attributable NI | ₩3,124,346 mn | **₩2,543,346 mn** |
| FY2025 ROE | 9.0% | **≈7.6%** |
| **TTM attributable NI (H2-25 + H1-26)** | ₩3,181,280 mn | **₩2,600,280 mn** |
| **TTM EPS** | ₩4,370 | **₩3,572** |
| **TTM P/E at KRW 33,650** | **7.70×** | **9.42×** |
| TTM ROE (on ex-hybrid equity ₩35.37trn) | 8.99% | **7.35%** |

**Any screen showing Woori at 7.7× trailing earnings is capitalising an acquisition-accounting entry.**
The clean number is 9.4×.

### 1.2 — Most of the CET1 improvement is a land revaluation, and the surplus is not distributable

CET1 went 12.89% (4Q25) → 13.60% (1Q26) → 13.71% (2Q26). Woori's own prospectus discloses the cause:

> "as of **March 31, 2026**, the Wholly-Owning Company conducted an **asset revaluation of its property
> and equipment**. However, such revaluation is **an accounting effect that does not involve any cash
> inflow**, and capital ratios may be affected by future changes in asset values and market conditions."
> — **Form CB / Rule 802 prospectus, 2026-07-16**, Basel-III capital section ✅ read directly

Approximately **+60bp of the +82bp CET1 improvement is that revaluation** (attribution from the H1-2026
Fact Book — magnitude agent-sourced, existence and timing confirmed primary), and the revaluation
surplus is **not distributable** under the Korean Commercial Code. Organic CET1 is therefore ≈**13.1%**,
barely over the 13.0% line and *below* the 13.2% maintenance target.

**This guts the strongest bull leg.** Woori's December-2024 value-up plan maps year-end CET1 ≥13.0% to
~50% total shareholder return (vs 36.8% delivered in FY25). The ladder may still trigger *mechanically*
— but the capital that pushed CET1 over the line **cannot fund the buyback the ladder promises.**

## 2. THE SELF-REFUTATION, ADJUDICATED

### 2.1 The ledger's metric is degenerate and its inputs are irreproducible

"P/B per unit of delivered ROE" is **P/B ÷ ROE**. Since P/B ≡ ROE × P/E, it is **identically P/E ÷ 100**
— the earnings multiple in a costume, not a quality-adjusted value measure. So "KB is 24% better paid"
means only "KB trades at a 24% lower P/E."

The stated inputs (0.060 vs 0.0485) are not reproducible from any combination of the two companies'
disclosed P/B and ROE at any date in 2026 — 0.0485 would require KB to trade at 4.85× earnings, which
it never has. **The number is an arithmetic artifact.** Verdict on the *metric*: **REFUTED.**

### 2.2 Four supporting facts are false or stale

| ledger claim | primary finding | status |
|---|---|---|
| "no securities arm" | Woori operates banking, insurance, credit card, capital finance, **financial investment (securities)**, asset management and real-estate trust (Form CB business description). Woori Investment & Securities profit +2.3× (Seoul Economic Daily 2026-08-02). | **REFUTED** |
| "payout 31.8%" | FY25 **total shareholder return 36.8%** (32.0% dividend + 4.8% buyback), 40.0% incl. the tax-exempt effect. | **understated** |
| "CET1 ~100bp thinner (caps buybacks)" | CET1 **13.71%** vs KB 13.74% / Shinhan 13.43% / Hana 13.21% — **parity, not a gap** (though see §1.2 on quality). | **REFUTED** |
| "cancellation +33%/yr" | FY26 buyback ₩350bn vs FY25 ₩150bn = **+133.3%** — apparently a transcription error. | **understated** |

> **RULING.** The ledger's **metric is refuted**, its **stated numbers are irreproducible**, and **four

### 2.4 What genuinely survives in WF's favour

1. **Buyback accretion asymmetry — real and durable.** Woori's July tranche (0.667% of shares at 0.69×
   book) is **+0.21% accretive to BVPS**; KB's (1.139% at 1.06× book) is **−0.15% dilutive**. Woori's own
   prospectus: *"the benefits of share cancellations are maximized when the Company's PBR is below
   1.0×."* Same policy, opposite sign. But Woori is buying back **₩350bn against KB's ₩3.7trn plan** —
   the better *mechanism* deployed at one-tenth the *scale* is not a thesis.
2. **Dividend yield 4.14% vs KB's 2.71%**, with genuine tax-exempt dividend capacity (₩6.3trn, first
   Korean financial holdco; the Q2-26 dividend was paid from capital reserve and is non-taxable **in
   Korea**). The US-holder treaty benefit is **not verified** — do not underwrite it.
3. **Still the 2026 laggard** (+23% YTD vs +37–40%). Optionality if the Korea re-rate broadens.
4. **The regulatory overhang is smaller than the ledger implied** — see §3. That is *not* good news for
   the thesis: it removes governance as the explanation for the discount, leaving only the ROE gap.

### 2.5 A bull leg that does NOT survive: the mandatory-cancellation law

Verified against the statute: **Commercial Act Art. 341-4, inserted by Act No. 21448, promulgated and
effective 2026-03-06** — treasury shares must be cancelled within 1 year of acquisition; legacy stock by
~2027-09-06; **no financial-holding-company carve-out.** Effect is already visible: ~₩43.1trn cancelled
Jan–May 2026 vs ₩21.4trn in all of 2025.

**It is close to irrelevant for Woori**, which held **53,945 treasury shares (0.01%)** at 2026-07-24.
There is no hoard to force out. The law re-rates *hoarders*. Do not credit it in the WF bull case.
*(A related new date worth tracking sector-wide: FSC/KRX low-PBR "naming and shaming" — bottom quartile
of KOSPI by sector PBR for 3 consecutive years, first publication **2026-11-02**, still DRAFT as of
2026-07-28. At 0.69× Woori is the cheapest big-four bank but almost certainly not bottom-quartile
within financials.)*

## 11. FOUR-IDEA FRAME

1. **The refutation's reasoning is dead; its conclusion is alive.** P/B ÷ ROE is P/E in disguise, its
   inputs are irreproducible, and four supporting facts are false or stale — yet on a clean four-name
   peer set Woori sits at P/B÷ROE 0.0767 against a sector median of 0.0759, i.e. **~1.3% above
   peer-consistent value.** "Correctly priced," reached the wrong way.
2. **The apparent cheapness is acquisition accounting.** ₩581bn of bargain-purchase gain (18.6% of FY25
   profit, booked H2-25) sits inside the trailing window. Remove it and the 7.7× trailing P/E becomes
