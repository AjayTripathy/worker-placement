# PITCH — LI — VERDICT
**PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION** — Open a **half-size STARTER (0.75% of book) in Li Auto ADS (LI) at ~$11.74, but only after the i9 price is public on 2026-09-16**, with a hard add-gate on the 2026-10-01 September delivery print (≥34,000 units with the revenue guide intact) and a hard kill-gate on the already-filed Interim Report's cash decomposition.

---

## What we believe

Li Auto is trading at $11.735, which is 0.7% above its 52-week low and 56.7% below its 52-week high (pack tape, ibkr_gw). At that price, after subtracting the company's cash, the market is paying roughly **$1.1bn for a business that books about RMB 25.7bn of revenue every quarter** — call it 0.07x annualized sales. That is the whole idea in one sentence: you are being handed an operating car company for close to nothing, provided the cash is real and the business stops going backwards.

Two things make us think it might stop going backwards:

1. **Revenue per car is already rising, and it already showed up in margin.** 1Q26: RMB 23.0bn on 95,142 cars = RMB 241.7k per car. 2Q26: RMB 25.67bn on 98,330 cars = RMB 261.1k per car. That is +8.0% per car in one quarter, on only +3.4% more cars — and vehicle gross margin went from 6.1% to 9.4% in the same quarter. This is a printed fact, not a forecast.
2. **The company is buying its own stock in dollars.** More than $630m of a $1bn authorization has been executed. That matters twice: it is a real bid under the stock, and it is proof that at least $630m of the cash pile is genuinely outside China and spendable, which puts a floor under the "the cash is trapped onshore" fear.

We do **not** believe the burn has been fixed. Operating cash flow in 2Q26 was **+RMB 15.0m on RMB 25.7bn of revenue** — that is 0.06%, i.e. zero — against a RMB 1.7bn net loss. This is a cheap balance sheet attached to a business that does not yet make cash.

## What the market believes

The market believes the guide is a disappointment and the price war is not over. 2Q26 revenue of RMB 25.67bn **beat** the ~RMB 25.07bn consensus and the stock fell anyway, and the street called the 3Q guide (95–100k cars, RMB 26.6–28.0bn) weak — meaning the street had been carrying a 3Q revenue number above RMB 27.3bn. So the tape is not ignoring an inflection; it is pricing a guide that came in under where the street was, in a sector where XPeng and NIO are also near lows. The stock made new lows *after* the 2Q print and *after* the i9 was announced. Nobody here is asleep.

## Why we might have edge

- **The per-car step is observable, not assumed.** The most common bear objection — "the +7% ASP step in the guide is just the revenue range divided by the unit range, a tautology" — is answered by the prior quarter, where the same-sized step actually printed alongside a 330bp vehicle-margin improvement. Bears are treating a realized mechanism as a residual.
- **The trapped-cash discount is measurably too big.** The buyback dollars are hard evidence of offshore fungibility. Most people apply the China-holdco haircut as an unbounded fear rather than a bounded number.
- **The share count and ADS ratio were widely mis-struck.** Each ADS represents two Class A ordinary shares ([20-F FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1791706/000110465926041705/li-20251231x20f.htm)). Popular data sites carry a market cap near $10.83bn; struck off the company's own RMB 1.69 net loss per ADS on a RMB 1.7bn loss, weighted ADS are ~1.006bn and market cap is ~$11.81bn. Small edge, but it means anyone quoting "EV ≈ zero" is ~10% too generous, and we are not.
- **A binding document is sitting unread.** The 2026 Interim Report (Exhibit 99.1 to the [6-K filed 2026-09-08](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001791706&type=6-K)) contains the cash composition and margin detail both of our benches called decisive. It is public and free. Reading it before position sizing is edge available for the cost of a fetch.

## Why it might be priced in

- 0.7% off the low, −56.7% from the high, in a sector where the peers are also at lows. The cheapness is not a secret; it is the consensus view of a structurally impaired industry.
- Every value buyer is looking at the same EV-≈-$1bn screen and the same Oct 1 delivery date. We are not early to a private fact; we are one of many people keyed to a public one.
- The buyback that supports the stock is also the reason the float shrinks slowly and the stock stays cheap: management is signalling it has nothing better to do with the cash, which is not a growth signal.
- "Cheap on EV/sales" for a company with zero operating cash flow is not cheap; it is correctly priced for a business that has not proven it converts revenue to cash.

## What could go wrong

**Failure narrative 1 — the ASP step and the volume beat cancel each other out (monitorable).** Revenue = units × price. The 3Q guide caps revenue at RMB 28.0bn. If September merely holds August's 37,679 cars, the quarter is 105,826 units and revenue per car is RMB 264.6k — only +1.2% over 2Q, and the margin-expansion mechanism dies. If September comes in at the guide's implied 26.9–31.9k, revenue per car is RMB 278–295k but the franchise is visibly shrinking. Our bull case needs the narrow middle: ~34,000 September cars (102,147 for the quarter, +3.9% sequential) with revenue per car still ~RMB 274k, +5.0%. That is a real needle to thread. **Tripwire: the Oct 1 delivery 6-K plus any guide revision.**

**Failure narrative 2 — the balance sheet is not what the screen says (monitorable).** The RMB 87.5bn headline is a company-defined "cash position" — cash plus restricted cash plus time deposits plus short- and long-term investments. It is not spendable net cash, and it has not been netted against roughly RMB 54bn of liabilities dominated by supplier payables. Actual cash and equivalents look closer to RMB 40.1bn on a secondary source we have not confirmed. If unrestricted cash plus sub-12-month instruments, net of debt and trade payables, comes in under ~RMB 40bn, the EV-≈-$1bn thesis is not wrong at the margin — it is wrong at the core. **Tripwire: the Interim Report decomposition (readable today) and the 3Q cash-flow statement.**

**Failure narrative 3 — NO MONITORABLE TRIPWIRE.** A rule change we cannot see coming and cannot hedge: Beijing intervening in the EV price war (mandated price floors, subsidy resets, purchase-tax changes), a change in the US listing/audit regime for China ADRs, capital-control tightening that severs the offshore/onshore link the buyback currently proves, or an ADR-ratio/venue action driven by the Hong Kong listing. There is no filing, no print, and no data feed that warns us; the first news *is* the repricing, and it arrives on a Sunday. **Unknown-unknown class: sovereign/venue discontinuity — the rules of the container change between prints, and no instrument we own reports on the container.** We treat this as sizing-only: it is the single largest reason the starter is half-size rather than full.

**The reflexive / structural leg.** Our own size is not the problem: LI ADS trades an estimated ~15m shares (~$175m) a day, and a 0.75%-of-book starter is under 2% of one day's volume at any book under $500m — we do not move this tape, and exit is not the constraint (ADV is our estimate, not a pack figure). The structural risks are other people's flows and our own doctrines: (a) the remaining ~$370m of buyback is *predicted flow that supports us today and disappears when the authorization is exhausted* — we would be buying partly into a bid we cannot renew; (b) sector beta — XPEV/NIO at lows means index and China-EV basket selling can override anything Li Auto does, and we never checked the beta confound; (c) doctrine interaction — our "buyback proves offshore cash" rule and our "China-holdco tail is unbounded" rule point in opposite directions on the same fact, and we resolved it in the bull's favor with a $630m floor that only covers 6% of the claimed cash pile; (d) our two entry gates (i9 price, then Sept deliveries) are four sessions and two weeks out, so a good-news gap through the bands leaves us chasing at a worse price with the same unresolved cash question.

If this position loses money, the most likely reason will be **that the China EV price war forces Li Auto to fund the i9 and the BEV transition out of price — the +7% revenue-per-unit step never converts into vehicle margin, September volumes come in soft, and the "free balance sheet" turns out to be supplier payables rather than net cash.**

**Table dispersion:** σ_table = **$1.77 = 15.1% of spot** over a ~21-day resolution window. Annualized, the table is asserting **~63% volatility**.

**Expression math (candidate strikes priced off the table itself):** C_ours = Σ pᵢ × max(Sᵢ − K, 0)
- K = $11: **$1.275**
- K = $12.50: **$0.375**
- K = $13: **$0.250**

**Decision rule, stated in advance:** pull the real chain and use **chain-level midpoint IV on the specific strikes** — never the aggregate underlying IV feed; those two diverge badly and the TSSI episode (71 vs 101) is the standing lesson. Then: if the nearest post-Oct-1 monthly **12.50 call mids below $0.375** and chain midpoint IV sits below ~63%, the ADD-1 gate should be expressed in calls, because the option is cheaper than our own distribution and the gate is binary anyway. If the 12.50 mid is above $0.375 or chain IV is above ~63%, IV is rich to our table and the add goes on in **stock**, with covered calls at the 14 strike permitted against the starter (the table pays nothing above 14, so we are selling something we do not believe in). Cash-secured puts at 10.50 are the alternative rich-IV route, but only after the Interim Report clears — selling puts under an unread cash decomposition is selling insurance on a fire you have not inspected.

**VRP:** cannot be computed. IV is unknown (no chain in the pack) and RV is unknown (the pack carries no price history, only the 52-week range). **VRP = UNKNOWN, and that is a gap, not a zero.** Our rough prior for a large China EV ADR is RV in the 55–70% area, which would put VRP near zero and argue for stock — but that is a prior, not a measurement, and it is item 10 in the drain queue.

**Bimodality:** the table is fork-dated across **two** separate events — the i9 price on Sept 16 and the deliveries on Oct 1. A single option cannot straddle only one of them, so any option expression must expire **after Oct 2**, which forces the nearest monthly (mid-October). Expect the chain to price a single fat event, whereas we face two sequential ones with the second dominant; that mismatch usually leaves the post-Oct expiry slightly cheap relative to a two-fork distribution.

**TAX overlay:** the loss branch has to land in tax year 2026. A mid-October or November 2026 expiry does that. **Any expiry past Dec 31, 2026 is excluded** — it pushes the loss into 2027 and this is a harvest year; the edge here (EV +1.2%) does not remotely dominate numerically, so the exception does not apply. Stock, if used, is harvestable at will and passes the overlay trivially.
