# PITCH — 1128.HK — PROPOSED VERDICT

**REJECT — no entry, no orders. (PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION.)** Both benches independently reached REJECT at conviction 8/10. The growth story died on volume, the income story died on capital spending, and the name is too well-covered for either to be a secret.

## What we believe

Wynn Macau (1128.HK) is not the cheap compounder the original thesis claimed. The pitch had two legs, and our own adversarial review killed both:

**The growth leg is an accounting shadow, not demand.** Wynn Palace's mass-market casino earnings grew ~27% year over year in Q2 2026, but the amount of money customers actually put on the tables (the "drop") grew only 3.0% — from $1,844M to $1,900M. The entire earnings jump came from the house win percentage spiking to 29.7% from 22.3% a year earlier ([Wynn Resorts Q2 2026 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wynn-resorts-limited-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-302842811.html)). Our review's key insight: this is not a lucky streak. VIP betting turnover collapsed at the same time (−32% at Palace, −56% at the peninsula property), and when high-volume/low-margin VIP play exits, the blended win percentage on what remains mechanically rises. The elevated win rate is the *shadow of missing premium volume*, not new demand. Consistent with that: the win percentage was a normal 21.8% as recently as Q4 2025 — the elevation is first-half-2026 only, exactly when VIP volume halved. Recomputed at a normal win rate, Macau earnings growth in the first half was roughly zero.

**The income leg doesn't survive the capital-spending bill.** The thesis said ~$600M of annual cash generation comfortably covers the ~$272M dividend. It counted only maintenance-level spending and omitted the board-approved growth program: the [US$950M Enclave tower at Wynn Palace](https://focusgn.com/asia-pacific/wynn-unveils-us950m-enclave-expansion-at-wynn-palace-macau) (land approval [cleared July 2026](https://asgam.com/2026/07/22/macau-government-gives-green-light-for-wynn-to-proceed-with-new-hotel-development-at-wynn-palace/)) plus an event center and theater through 2028, with 2026 Macau expansion spending guided at $350–400M. Part of this program is required under the 2022 government concession, so it cannot be deferred to protect the dividend. True free cash flow after growth spending (~$250–350M) roughly equals the dividend — meaning zero debt paydown at ~3.7× leverage, with a US$600M convertible bond due 2029 unaddressed.

**A watch-item, not a kill:** the 72% parent (Wynn Resorts) needs cash for its UAE project (budget up $600M, opening slipped to 2027), which makes the near-term dividend declaration *more* likely — the parent wants the money upstream — but pushes the real payout-cut risk to the final dividend around March 2027. Minority holders' income is being funded by balance-sheet degradation.

## What the market believes

The market appears to have this roughly right, which is uncomfortable but true. The stock trades at 5.71, down 23% from its 52-week high, at about 7× earnings — a multiple that *looks* cheap against reported growth. But the operating detail has been public since the parent's August 4 results, the name carries 20+ sell-side analysts, and the stock did not re-rate on the "+27%" quarter (it fell 4.9% in July and was down 0.7% on August 10). Professional analysts routinely adjust casino results for win-percentage swings. The market is pricing a covered, leveraged Macau operator whose headline growth is win-rate-driven and whose dividend competes with a mandatory building program.

## Why we might have edge

Honestly: not much, and not enough to own the stock. What survives the court:

1. **A sharper decomposition than the consensus shorthand.** The distinction between "lucky win rate" (mean-reverts, strip it out) and "composition effect from VIP exit" (persistent while VIP stays away, but not creditable as demand growth) is more precise than typical hold-adjustment, and it produces testable predictions — see the reopen conditions below.
2. **A dated, checkable trigger list.** We know exactly what would make this name interesting again, and when the data arrives (August 19 interim results; September 1 Macau government monthly revenue data).
3. **A cross-name insight.** Wynn's own tape — premium volume shrinking while win rate masks it — weakly *supports* the broader thesis that the Asian premium gambling wallet is being diverted, which feeds our related Wynn Resorts (parent) work. The Macau interest, if any, lives in that pairing, not in owning 1128 outright.

## Why it might be priced in

Because it demonstrably is. Twenty-plus analysts, the operating data public for a week before our review, and no upward re-rating on the headline growth number. The "cheap on rising numbers" observation is consensus-visible, and the consensus correctly declined to pay for win-rate-driven numbers. Separately, the dividend-repeat scenario at the August 19 interim (HK$0.185, matching precedent) is the *expected* outcome — precedent-priced — so there is no asymmetric upside in holding through the announcement; the asymmetric surprise is a capex-driven dividend rebase, which is a downside event.

- **Reopen #1 — volume-confirmed win rate (Q3 2026 data, via the parent's ~early-November release):** Palace mass win rate ≥28% **together with** mass drop growth ≥5% year over year. Rate *and* volume rising together would mean genuine premium demand, not composition. Either alone does not reopen.
