# PITCH — KLAR — PROPOSED VERDICT (PROPOSED — PENDING ADJUDICATION): Do not buy before the August 18 earnings report; a small initial position is allowed only after the report, and only if three specific disclosures all confirm.

## What we believe

Klarna's consumer-credit story is *damaged but not disproven*. The original bull thesis said: credit losses are improving even as the loan mix shifts toward riskier longer-term lending, so the reported flat loss-provision rate (0.55% of merchandise volume in Q1) must mean each product is individually getting better. Our own adversarial review broke that argument. Klarna's own Q1 2026 earnings release ([primary source, p.6](https://s205.q4cdn.com/644747736/files/doc_earnings/2026/q1/earnings-result/Q1-26-Klarna-Group-plc-Earnings-Release.pdf)) says the low provision rate is partly caused by something else entirely: the company sold $1.2 billion of receivables off its balance sheet in Q1, and loans it no longer holds don't need loss provisions. Management can raise or lower this ratio by choosing how many loans to sell.

What survives: the underlying growth is real (revenue excluding one-off gains still grew about 36% year over year, with interest income up 56%), and the question "is credit actually improving?" is answerable on August 18, when the Q2 report and a new supplemental disclosure pack arrive. Our position is flat until then. If the report shows (a) provisions at or below 0.60% of volume, (b) receivable sales at or below Q1's $1.2 billion pace (or numbers we can normalize for it), and (c) 2026 loan-vintage delinquency curves at or below 2025's, we take a small initial position at whatever price that confirmation costs — treating it as fairly paid risk, not a mispricing.

## What the market believes

The stock closed at $19.465 — 94% of its 26-week high of $20.74, up 61% from the $12.06 low, but still 59% below the $47.48 post-IPO high. The market has already paid for the recovery story: the roughly 9% pop on the Apple partnership news is in the price, and the consensus read of Q1 (echoed in [coverage like Seeking Alpha's](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4592844-klarna-stock-gains-after-q1-earnings-shine-as-credit-loss-provision-drops)) was "credit loss provisions dropped — credit is improving." The market is taking the headline provision ratio at face value. It is also apparently comfortable with Q2 profit guidance of $30–50 million, *below* Q1's $68 million — meaning a "profit fell" headline on August 18 is the base case, not a surprise.

## Why we might have edge

- **We read the exhibits, not the prose.** Management's text says delinquencies "continue to trend down," but the vintage charts in their own release (p.7) show 2025 US Fair-Financing loans running *above* 2024 loans at the same age, and charge-off cohorts plateauing around 3.7–3.8%, higher than earlier cohorts. Few readers reconcile the charts against the narrative.
- **We decomposed the profit.** $57 million of the $68 million Q1 adjusted operating profit — about 84% — came from one-time gains on selling receivables, which pull forward lifetime loan economics into a single quarter. The "profitable now" story rests heavily on a lumpy, management-controlled line.
- **We know what the August 18 report must show.** Most observers will react to the headline provision ratio. We know that ratio can be steered by selling more loans, so we'll be reading the receivable-sale volume and the new supplemental pack's vintage curves — a like-for-like test the headline number can't fake.

## Why it might be priced in

- The stock already rerated 61% off the low and sits at its 26-week high; the recovery narrative and the Apple partnership are paid for.
- Bull-side symmetry our sceptic bench conceded: ex-gain revenue growth of ~36% is genuine, and the market may simply be pricing that correctly. Even if all three of our confirmation legs pass, this is likely *fair compensation for consumer-credit risk*, not a market error.
- The "credit improving" headline has already run once (the stock gained on the Q1 release); a clean Q2 confirmation may add little at this price.
