03 / 12Cause check — two events, only one about Descartes

The 37% fall decomposes into an organic stall and a sector storm

So the bear that matters was never tariffs or a guidance cut — Descartes has never cut guidance and never printed a revenue decline in this window. The bear is that the growth engine stalled in mid-2025. Which makes the next slide the whole case.

04 / 12The finding — the organic series nobody prints

Headline growth was flat at 15–25%. Underneath it, organic went 0.9% → ~11%

Descartes' press releases only ever say "revenues up 15%." The MD&A discloses the acquisition contribution every quarter; two of the five organic figures below exist nowhere and were derived as residuals, then cross-checked against the annual filing — the four quarterly figures sum to the year's stated $25.1M organic exactly.

The market de-rated the stock 37% across exactly the window in which the thing that broke it repaired itself. That sentence is the pitch. Everything else in this deck is verification, disconfirmation, and structure.

The disconfirming experiment the bear fails

The coherent bear says the reacceleration was tariff-chaos rent, killed when the Supreme Court struck the IEEPA tariffs on 2026-02-20. Test it: the Q2 FY27 baseline was struck seventy days after the ruling — and printed an all-time high, +3.0% sequential, the strongest build in three quarters. The contracted book did not roll over post-ruling. The quarter that spans the ruling printed +11.1% organic. It is one data point, and we do not overclaim it — but it is the bear's own experiment, and the bear failed it.

Red-team caveat, kept: all four calibration points sit inside the tariff-chaos regime; a bear-world ratio of ~1.15–1.17 maps the same baseline to $194–198M. That is why the frozen call on slide 12 is conjunctive — revenue and the organic line — rather than revenue alone.

08 / 12The bear case — stated at full strength

What we are paying up for, and what the red team changed

  • It is not cheap. 38x trailing GAAP earnings, ~4.3% FCF yield, 8.7x sales. Anyone underwriting this as a value stock is wrong; the entire case is growth-durability at a compressed-for-it multiple.
  • The organic repair is partly bucket migration. The red team quantified 2–3pp of the +11.1% as lapped acquisitions (Sellercloud, 3GTMS, Finale cohort) rolling into the organic base. True same-store ≈ 8–9%. The record carries the corrected number.
  • The calibration was fit in a chaos regime. All four baseline ratios come from tariff-turmoil quarters. If the transactional overage was rent, the same baseline maps to $194–198M — which is why a $197–199M September print resolves on the organic line, not the headline.
  • Tariff-complexity demand can normalize. SCOTUS struck the IEEPA tariffs; if Section 232/301 substitution is slower or narrower, filing complexity fades. Instrumented quarterly: a Q3 FY27 baseline below $172M is a hard exit, no debate.
  • The entry tape is uncomfortable. +16.6% off an 8-session low on no filed cause. We do not pay $76.70; the ladder starts 4% lower and the fill probability before the print is ~0.79 — a real gate that fills on noise, not on thesis-death (the wrong-side-gate test was run and passed).
  • Minor: the ~$30M Drivin acquisition is single-sourced and not yet visible in any SEC filing we read — carried in the model at ~$4M/yr and flagged UNCONFIRMED.

Red-team disposition: 6/10 upheld; size trimmed 1.0% → 0.85%; freezable call made conjunctive; record text corrected to same-store ~8–9%. Two attacks landed, one glanced, three missed — and one of the misses (estimate-revision direction) came back as new evidence for the thesis.
