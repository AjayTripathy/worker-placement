The edge condition all four required — the FLL null defined them

1 · Thin coverage (1–4 analysts) — the analyst is stale & beatable
2 · Low idiosyncratic vol (<1.5%/day, <3x levered, no live corp-action) — the binding gate
3 · A nowcastable metric with free public lead data
4 · A crystallization clock (print / revision date)
The tax constraint. Medium-frequency = short holding periods = short-term gains at ~54% CA all-in (or ~37% while they displace the earmarked September loss pool, which is finite). An MFT edge must clear a much higher bar than the fair-carry book — which is exactly why this sleeve stays on paper until Layer 2 beats coin net of tax across adequate n.
