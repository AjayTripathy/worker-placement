## 1 · The four-idea frame

### Market believes
A former hard-market darling now cracking. Gross written premium is falling, growth is over, the whole
E&S party is ending — de-rate it from 6x book and keep de-rating. The stock is ~26% below its 52-week
high and the tape treats each GWP print as confirmation.

### We believe
The crack is **one division**, and Kinsale prints the decomposition itself rather than burying it:
Commercial Property GWP fell **−32.7% in Q2** (−30.9% 1H) on rate declines and standard-carrier
re-entry, while **everything else grew +3.7% in Q2** (+4.8% 1H). Casualty — roughly 71% of the book —
is where the franchise lives, and it is still growing. Underneath, the numbers are elite by any
underwriting standard: **75.5% combined ratio**, current accident-year loss ratio **57.0% ex-cat**
(improved 110bps YoY), book value per share **$89.34** (from $84.66 at 12/31/25), operating ROE **24.4%
annualised 1H / 25.2% Q2**. Cat losses were trivial ($5.4M pre-tax). This is a contained, honestly-
disclosed cyclical soft-turn in one line, not a franchise breaking.

**What Q2 changed, stated against ourselves.** Two things got worse, and neither is hidden:
1. **The ex-property growth line halved.** Our record carried +6.0% (Q1-26); Q2 printed **+3.7%**. Two
   more quarters of decay at that rate puts it through zero — which is our own kill trigger.
2. **The headline combined ratio improved only because development widened.** Reported combined went
   75.8% → **75.5%**, but favorable prior-year development rose from 3.9 to **4.5 points ($19.4M)**.
   **Ex-development, the combined ratio *deteriorated* 79.7% → 80.0%.** The current accident year did
   genuinely improve (58.1 → 57.0 loss ratio); the expense ratio rose ~100bps on lower ceding commissions
   as retention went up. Kinsale states the mechanism in its own text ("largely due to... higher favorable
   development") and prints the PYD line in a table.

### Why we might have edge
**Modest, and it is a valuation-reset claim rather than an information claim.** The market re-based a
6x-book stock to ~4.0x on a soft-turn confined to a single division while ROE only slipped from 29% to
~25%. Buying an elite E&S underwriter at a Gordon-fair multiple is quality-at-a-reset-price — but the
EC record is explicit that expected total return is **+8%** and edge is **+3pp inside a ±10pp band**,
which is not a mispricing claim. Carry-vs-edge label: **mostly carry with a small VALUE overlay.** The
one genuine honesty-alpha finding is a *negative* one for the bear case: on takeaway-vs-data grading,
**Kinsale is CLEAN** — the marketed story ("we're still growing") is printed in the same paragraph as the
number that contradicts it for property, and the development benefit is disclosed rather than passed off
as underwriting improvement. There is no liar here to exclude. That is worth saying plainly, because the
product is the exclusion of liars, and this name does not qualify for the catch.

### Why it may be priced in / why we may be wrong
The strongest bear: **it is a premium multiple in a confirmed softening market, and we are now paying it.**
At $356.42 on $89.34 of book, the stock trades at **3.99x** — above the 3.82x the court underwrote and
above the ~4.0x Gordon-fair line at a haircut 20% ROE. It is only +24% off its 52-week low. The
soft market is not a forecast: excess/umbrella casualty renewal rates ran **6.3–10.7% in 1H-26 versus
12–16% a year earlier**, E&S property is −10–15%, and US E&S grew 7.8% in 2025, the first single-digit
year since 2017. If standard carriers re-enter **casualty** the way they re-entered property, the +3.7%
becomes negative and a 4x-book stock compresses toward book. And the reserve story is structurally
young: Kinsale IPO'd in 2016, only accident years through ~2021 are fully seasoned, and the long-tail
casualty book has never been through an adverse cycle. Every mature year has re-estimated down — but
"every year so far" on a nine-year book is a smaller claim than it sounds.

### Mechanism falsifier
**Ex-Commercial-Property GWP growth turning negative in either of the next two quarters** falsifies "the
deceleration is contained to property-cat" — it would mean the casualty divisions are shrinking too, and
the franchise growth engine has stalled. Cut. Secondary falsifiers: casualty prior-year development turning
net adverse beyond the small disclosed construction-defect pocket (~$8M, AYs 2016–19); current-AY loss
ratio breaking above ~62%.

### Edge type
**RISK_PREMIUM with a VALUE overlay** (de-rated quality). Not EDGE. Sizing reads this label.
