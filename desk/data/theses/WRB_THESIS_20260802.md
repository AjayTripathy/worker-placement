## 1 · The four-idea frame

### Market believes
The safe, disciplined insurer to hide in. Bid it near its high in a defensive rotation for the ~20% ROE,
the multi-decade reserve reputation, and a float engine that gets paid more the longer rates stay high.

### We believe
Mostly right on the quality — and we say so plainly. The NII engine is real, still growing, and still a
tailwind: **$418.7M in Q2 (+10.4%)**, ~80% of net income, on a portfolio where management states
"current reinvestment rates continue to exceed our annual book yield." The multiple is fundamentally
supported (2.73x book / 2.79x tangible = only ~+10.5% over WRB's own 5-year median, versus the +23%
premium that made us fade TRV). Gordon cross-check at a ~19.7% sustained ROE justifies 2.7–4.6x across a
CoE 9.5–11% / g 6–8% grid; today's 2.73x sits at the **conservative end** and prices in only a ~14–15%
ROE. And it diversifies a book that is otherwise long reinsurance (EG) and small-cap E&S (KNSL).

Two things we believe that the consensus framing does not say:
1. **The "well-reserved" reputation is stale for current years.** The Insurance segment developed
   **$7M adverse in 1H-2026** and **$44M adverse in FY2025**, concentrated in umbrella/excess and auto
   liability, accident years **2019–2023**, attributed by the company to social inflation. Small against
   $19.7B of net reserves — but directionally wrong, in the longest-tail lines, and it is a slow fuse.
2. **Pricing has stepped down, not drifted down.** Renewal rate change ex-comp went
   **+7.9% (FY24) → +7.6% (FY25) → +7.2% (Q1-26) → +3.8% (Q2-26)**. That is roughly a halving in one
   quarter, against loss-cost trend that management itself says is rising.

Own it for the carry at fair value; size against the reserve tail; do not tell yourself it is cheap.

### Why we might have edge
**We may not, and the doc says so.** The EC record puts expected total return at **+3%** and edge at
**+1pp inside a ±10pp band** — i.e. inside noise. This is **RP_FAIR carry**: the deploying-book default
class, where the bar is *fairness verified + tails bounded and unlevered + sleeve-capped*, not an edge
claim. The one honest overlay is modest and named: the market may be extrapolating TRV-style
peak-crowding onto a multiple that is only about half as extended versus its own history, which leaves
some re-rate room if the reserve creep stays contained. That is a **carry position with a small optional
overlay**, not a mispricing.

**One thing found in this pass that *is* a small, repeatable edge — and it is a process edge, not a
valuation one.** WRB's rate-change disclosure does not live in the earnings release. The 7/20 press
release describes "attractive opportunities across select liability lines... appropriate risk-adjusted
returns and **favorable pricing**" with **no rate number anywhere**. The actual +3.8% appeared in the
10-Q MD&A **eleven days later, on 7/31**. The stock went $75.65 (7/20) → $72.54 (7/31), −4.1%, with the
two largest down-days on 7/30–7/31. Masking channel: a qualitative adjective substituted for a metric in
the 8-K. Signal channel: one sentence in 10-Q Item 2. Signal-to-price latency: **~11 days**. Worth a
standing watch: read WRB's 10-Q, not its release.

### Why it may be priced in / why we may be wrong
The strongest bear, stated at full strength: **this is the TRV scissors setting up.** Adverse social-
inflation development in long-tail casualty accelerating *while* renewal pricing decelerates below
loss-cost trend is precisely the combination that turned Travelers into a fade. Q2 supplied a real data
point on the pricing blade (+3.8%) and the reserve blade is already modestly adverse. If ROE compresses
from ~19.7% toward ~14%, the 2.73x multiple has no support and de-rates toward ~2.2x — that is the $60
bear, −17% from here, and you are entering 6.5% below a 52-week high with essentially no valuation
cushion. Additionally: NPW growth has decelerated to +2.4% consolidated and the Reinsurance & Monoline
leg is *shrinking* (gross premium −8%, casualty −13%) — the company is choosing to shrink, which protects
margin but shrinks the earnings base the multiple is struck on.

### Mechanism falsifier
**Insurance-segment adverse prior-year development above $75M in any single quarter, or above $150M
cumulative in 2026** falsifies "contained, well-disclosed drag." Secondary: current-accident-year ex-cat
loss ratio rising above ~61% (from the verified 59.6%), or ex-comp renewal rate change turning **negative**
ex-property. Any of these means the reserve book is deteriorating into a soft market — trim, do not argue.

### Edge type
**RISK_PREMIUM (RP_FAIR carry)** with a modest VALUE overlay. Carry-vs-edge label: **carry is the return
driver; the edge overlay is ≈+1pp and is not sized for.**

WRB + KNSL together at core size = $240k = 4.0% of the forward base, on top of EG. The P&C/insurance

no-hard-cap doctrine that is not a ceiling, but per the sizing-amendment convention it is the kind of
aggregate that forces a sizing court before a further add. **Flagged, not blocked.**

One dissent worth recording against myself: the Q2 rate print (+3.8%) is new information that post-dates
the court and cuts *against* the name. It does not trip a kill trigger and it does not change the RP_FAIR
label, but a court that had seen it might have staged Lot 1 lower than $71.50. The ladder above therefore
leans the weight into Lots 2 and 3 rather than the top rung.
