## 1 · The four-idea frame

### Market believes
Everest is a serial casualty under-reserver — three consecutive years of charges — whose mid-teens ROE
cannot be trusted. Penalty-box it near book and assume social inflation keeps eating capital, while the
reinsurance cycle softens the earnings power that is supposed to pay you for the wait.

### We believe
New management kitchen-sinked and ring-fenced the legacy book (adverse-development cover with ~$1.0B of
net forward protection, 99.7% unused at 12/31/25, facing *rated* fronts — State National and MS Transverse,
not an unrated counterparty), exited the bad business (casualty premium now shrinking 23–25% a quarter),
and a record float-income base plus a doubled buyback pays you while AY2025 seasons. At 0.94x book on a
14.9% operating ROE, you are paid roughly 11% of shareholder yield to hold a franchise whose worst years
are already ceded.

**What the Jul-29 print did to that claim.** It did not break it — but it did not advance it either.
Group PYD printed exactly **$0.0M**, which is the shape you get when adverse casualty is netted against
favorable short-tail property. Everest's own Q1 release said the YTD favorable was "driven by well-seasoned
**property** reserves." Across 1H-2026, property has carried the entire favorable line and casualty has
contributed nothing. That is not a lie — the company discloses each segment's PYD line — but it means the
one thing we were buying evidence of (a second consecutive *clean casualty* quarter) is **not evidenced**.

### Why we might have edge
**Thin, and honestly labelled RP_FAIR carry with a re-rating option.** The classification in the EC record
is explicit: RP_FAIR (~11% total shareholder yield) plus a "real-but-unproven penalty-box re-rating option."
The red team already killed the re-rating as a *valuation input* and reclassified the name CARRY-ONLY.
After Jul-29 the honest statement is: **we probably do not have edge here.** The carry is real, the
discount to peers is real, and the disagreement (is casualty fixed?) is exactly the question the market is
also asking and neither of us can answer until the 10-Q. Carry-vs-edge label: **carry**, with the edge
overlay currently **unearned**.

### Why it may be priced in / why we may be wrong
Three strong versions:
1. **The reinsurance cycle is turning down harder than the carry can absorb.** Casualty XOL premium fell
   **−25.0%** and pro-rata **−22.8%** in Q2 — at or below the bottom of the range our own record assumed
   (−13% to −24%). Property-cat renewal pricing is down 15–25% at the mid-year dates. The E in the P/E is
   falling while we underwrite the multiple.
2. **0.94x book may be exactly right.** Three straight years of misses is a legitimate base rate. A
   14.9% operating ROE that is 2/3 investment income in a softening market does not obviously deserve
   1.1–1.2x.
3. **The $0 PYD is the tell, not the reassurance.** Net-zero group development is what a book looks like
   when it is spending its property redundancy to pay for casualty deterioration. We cannot rule this in
   or out from the release, and *unverifiable is not clean*.

### Mechanism falsifier
**Any AY2025-or-later casualty adverse development above $300M through YE2027** falsifies "the new picks
are conservative" — the ring-fence only capped years that were already blown. That is a kill, not a trim.
The nearer-term, weaker falsifier: the Q2-2026 10-Q showing segment casualty PYD materially adverse with
property offsetting it → the carry thesis survives but the re-rating option is dead and the position
stays capped at the starter permanently.

### Edge type
**RISK_PREMIUM (RP_FAIR carry).** Not EDGE. Sizing must read this label.
