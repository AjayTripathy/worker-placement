**Why not STRUCTURAL outright.** The margin break has three separable drivers, and only one is structural:

## COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked)

**None qualify as damage-absent.** LMB's predicted damage is in the numbers twice over, so it cannot be ranked here honestly. It is nonetheless court-worthy on a **dispersion-misread** basis — three specific facts the tape appears to be pricing wrong:

- **The Capital Group overhang is behind the stock, not ahead of it.** CII went from 823,702 sh / 7.1% at 3/31/26 ([13G/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1606163/000156223026000069/primary_doc.xml)) to **0 sh / 0.0%** at 6/30/26 ([13G/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1606163/000156223026000112/primary_doc.xml)). - **The plaintiff-bar notices have a very thin predicate.** The cited hook is a March 11, 2025 CEO remark about sharing material price increases with customers, invoked seventeen months later. These notices are near-automatic after any >20% gap and are not independent evidence.
- **Adjusted EPS was $0.64 in both Q1 and Q2.** The company printed the same adjusted number twice; consensus moved from ~$0.21 to ~$0.93 in between. The Q2 "31% miss" is substantially an expectations whipsaw. *(Consensus figures are secondary-sourced — a court should confirm them.)*
