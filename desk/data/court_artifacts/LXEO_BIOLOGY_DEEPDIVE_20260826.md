## THE DISEASE AND THE DRUG, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Friedreich ataxia is caused by inheriting two damaged copies of the *FXN* gene, which leaves the
body short of a mitochondrial protein called frataxin. Nerves degenerate — that is the visible
disease — but **the heart is what kills people**, through a thickened, energy-starved
cardiomyopathy. LX2006 is a single intravenous infusion of a heart-tropic AAV (serotype rh.10)
carrying a normal copy of *FXN*. It does not touch the neurologic disease. The entire investment
case is: can one infusion put enough frataxin back into heart muscle, durably enough, to shrink
the thickened heart — and will the FDA accept that shrinkage as an approvable surrogate?

---

## 1. DOES THE DEMONSTRATED EXPRESSION CLEAR THE THRESHOLD FOR CARDIAC BENEFIT?

**Claim under test:** the dose used in the pivotal trial produces enough cardiac frataxin to matter.
**Method:** cardiac biopsy frataxin at 3 months, all dosed patients with biopsies, pooled across two
open-label trials. **Authority:** *JAMA Cardiology* 2026, PMID **42307907** (NCT05302271 +
NCT05445323), abstract pulled from PubMed this session.

**Finding — this is the strongest fact in the file.** Frataxin rose **monotonically with dose**:
**+20% (1.8×10¹¹), +81% (5.6×10¹¹), +123% (1.2×10¹² vg/kg)** in 8/8 biopsied patients. The pivotal
uses the top dose. Untreated FA patients typically run ~5–35% of normal frataxin; **heterozygous
carriers sit near 50% of normal and are clinically healthy** — that carrier level is the natural
benchmark for "enough." A ~123% increase on a ~30% baseline lands near ~65–70% of normal, i.e.
**above the carrier threshold, in the tissue that matters, at the dose being registered.**

A clean dose-response in the target organ is the single hardest thing for a gene therapy to fake,
and it is present. **The FDA agreed and acted on it:** per the 6/15/26 8-K (EX-99.1, verbatim),
the agency **removed the cardiac frataxin co-primary endpoint** because it is "no longer necessary
to demonstrate proof of mechanism for LX2006." A regulator deleting your mechanism endpoint is a
regulator stating on the record that the mechanism is established. **Proof of mechanism: settled.**

**The overexpression trap is not a live risk at this dose.** Frataxin is toxic when massively
overexpressed — cardiac toxicity above ~20-fold normal, safe to ~9-fold (PMID 33209958, *Mol Ther
Methods Clin Dev*). At roughly 0.7× normal we are **more than an order of magnitude below the
toxic ceiling**. The therapeutic window here is unusually wide.

## 2. DURABILITY — WHERE THE RECORD IS THINNEST

**Claim under test:** expression persists long enough for a one-time therapy to be a therapy.
**Method:** biology first principles + the human biomarker record. **Authority:** same JAMA paper.

**The favorable biology:** cardiomyocytes are **post-mitotic** — they do not divide, so the AAV
genome, which sits outside the chromosome as an episome, is not diluted away by cell division.
This is why cardiac AAV should behave more like retina (durable) than like the liver of a growing
child (diluted). The failure mode is not dilution but **immune clearance of transduced muscle** and
promoter silencing.

**The evidence we have is indirect but real.** Over a mean **20 months** of follow-up,
**high-sensitivity troponin I — a direct readout of ongoing heart-muscle injury — fell ≥10% in 15
of 17 patients** and rose in only 2. If transgene expression were being lost, that injury marker
should drift back up. It has not. That is the best available durability evidence.

**The gap, stated plainly (Mode B, absence-of-evidence):** *there is no human cardiac frataxin
measurement beyond 3 months anywhere in the record.* Nobody has re-biopsied. For a program whose
entire value proposition is one-and-done durability, the absence of a 12- or 24-month biopsy
substudy is a conspicuous hole — and it is the reason a durability claim here is an inference from
cell biology plus a biomarker, not a measurement.

**One warning shot:** among 4 serious adverse events (3 tied to the prednisone immunosuppression),
there was **one possibly vector-related myocarditis at 12 months**, resolved. Myocarditis is the
exact clinical signature of immune attack on transduced cardiomyocytes. n=1, resolved, in 17
patients — not disqualifying, but it is the mechanism by which durability would fail, observed once.

## 3. THE REGISTRATIONAL PATH — AND THE ARITHMETIC PROBLEM THAT DRIVES MY POSITION

**Claim under test:** the pivotal as designed can produce the result it needs.
**Method:** protocol + SAP from the 6/15/26 8-K and the trial record NCT07721025 (pulled this
session). **Authority:** both primary.

The design is better than the court credited: **randomized allocation**, a **concurrent untreated
control** (13 treated vs 13 untreated, crossover at 6 months), **blinded central imaging readers**,
and an enrichment criterion — only patients whose LV mass index is **≥2 SD above normal** enroll.
Randomization plus central blinded reads means regression-to-the-mean affects both arms and cancels.
Primary endpoint: **percent change in LV mass index at Week 26**. The SAP is **powered to detect an
effect size of ≥15%**. Topline **2H 2027**, BLA under accelerated approval **1H 2028**.

**Here is my substantive disagreement with the bull case, and it is arithmetic, not vibes.**
A 15% *effect size* is the gap between arms. It must come from treated-arm shrinkage plus
control-arm growth. What the Phase I/II data actually shows: **LVMI fell ≥10% in 9 of 17 patients
and merely "stabilized" in 8**. Reading that generously, the pooled mean reduction is somewhere
around 6–9%. For the pivotal to clear 15%, **either the untreated arm must add roughly 6–9% of LV
mass in six months — an annualized pace far faster than published adult FA natural history, where
LV mass is broadly stable and in later stages actually *declines* as hypertrophy burns out into
fibrosis and wall thinning — or the high-dose subgroup must shrink hearts substantially harder than
the pooled average.**

The second is genuinely plausible, and it is the crux of the whole file: the pooled figure includes
**cohort 1, which achieved only +20% frataxin and is almost certainly sub-therapeutic**. If the
LVMI responders concentrate in cohorts 2–3, the high-dose-only effect could be double the pooled
number and the design is well-calibrated. **The JAMA abstract does not break LVMI down by dose
cohort. That single unpublished number is the most decision-relevant fact in this name**, and it
is knowable — full text, or the ASGCT April 2026 deck.

That Lexeo is running **CLARITY-FA**, a natural-history study with *identical* inclusion criteria,
tells you the company knows the control-progression rate is the load-bearing assumption. It is a
hedge against exactly the risk I am naming.

Two secondary fragilities: **n=13 per arm** with CMR test–retest variability of ~5–8% leaves little
margin; and **six months is short** for cardiac mass regression, which in better-understood settings
(hypertrophy relief after valve replacement) takes 6–12 months to reach substantial magnitude.

## 4. COMPETITIVE AND PLATFORM READ-THROUGH

**In FA cardiomyopathy, LX2006 has no gene-therapy competitor.** Omaveloxolone (approved) is a
neurologic antioxidant, not a cardiac therapy, and mouse work shows it improves cardiac function
without preventing death (PMID 40501183). First-mover status in the cardiac indication is real.

The cardiac-AAV *platform* read is favorable and comes from the PKP2 field: **Rocket's RP-A601
(NCT05885412) raised target protein in 3 of 3 Phase 1 biopsies**, and **Tenaya's TN-401 (RIDGE-1,
NCT06228924)** is dosing. Independent groups delivering measurable protein to human heart muscle
with AAV is external validation of the delivery premise LX2006 depends on. Note the direction of
the read: those programs **validate LX2006's platform while competing directly with LX2020**.

**A binding correction for the desk (catalyst-identity):** the Q4 2026 catalyst — 12-month
high-dose data — belongs to **LX2020 in PKP2 arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy** (the HEROIC-PKP2 trial,
NCT06109181, verified as Lexeo's own study). **It says nothing about the LX2006 registrational
path**, which does not read out until **2H 2027**. The existing `LXEO-Q4DUR` call is correctly
specified against LX2020, but it must not be treated as a read on the FA program that carries the
valuation. Per the 8/12/26 PR, LX2020 has RMAT and is "generally well tolerated across ten
participants dosed with no clinically significant complement activation."

## 5. DISCONFIRMERS — WHAT WOULD KILL MY VIEW

1. **High-dose-only LVMI breakdown from Phase I/II shows cohort-3 mean reduction ≥15%.** My
   effect-size skepticism is then simply wrong, and p should move to ~0.50+. *(Knowable now — JAMA
   full text or the ASGCT deck. This is the highest-value unverified item in the name.)*
2. **CLARITY-FA interim shows untreated LVMI rising ≥7% per six months** in the enriched population.
   Then the 15% gap is reachable without heroic treated-arm effects, and the design is right.
3. **A second vector-related myocarditis, or troponin rebound in the long-follow-up cohort.** Either
   would break the durability inference that carries the one-time-therapy premise — and durability
   is inferred here, never measured past 3 months.

**Mode B — what we should already see if the biology worked, and do we?** Dose-dependent frataxin in
the target organ: **yes**. Sustained fall in a cardiac-injury biomarker over ~20 months: **yes**.
LVMI moving in the right direction with nobody clearly worsening: **yes**. A regulator deleting the
mechanism endpoint: **yes**. What we should see and do *not*: **any expression measurement past 3
months**, and **the by-dose LVMI breakdown**. The failing-biology signature — expression decay,
troponin rebound, LVMI drift upward — **is absent**.
