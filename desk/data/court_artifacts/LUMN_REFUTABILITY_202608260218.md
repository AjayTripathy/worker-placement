## The decisive reconstruction (fully primary, and it is *not* in the headline)

The number that draws value buyers is FY26 FCF guidance of **$1.9–2.1B against a ~$5.9B market cap — a ~33% FCF yield.** The cash flow statement shows what is behind it ([8-K Ex-99.1 p.10](https://s21.q4cdn.com/756714007/files/doc_earnings/2026/q2/earnings-result/LUMN-Q2-2026-8-K-Exhibit-99-1_02-03-26.pdf); [10-Q, filed 2026-08-04](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000018926/000001892626000060/lumn-20260630.htm)):

The reported FCF improvement is entirely the prepayment ramp; **underlying cash generation got worse by ~$590M YoY.** The guidance arithmetic agrees: EBITDA $3.2B − capex $3.3B − net cash interest $0.70B + $0.40B tax refund ≈ **−$0.4B**, so essentially all of the $2.0B guide is prepayments plus the **$729M of Mass Markets divestiture proceeds routed through cash flow from operations** — *company-disclosed but sourced here to the Q1 release and secondary coverage, not verified in the Q2 primary I read* ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/lumen-q2-2026-slides-strategic-revenue-crosses-50-threshold-93CH-4836227)).

**The sharpest single fact:** on the balance sheet ([p.9](https://s21.q4cdn.com/756714007/files/doc_earnings/2026/q2/earnings-result/LUMN-Q2-2026-8-H-Exhibit-99-1_02-03-26.pdf)), the *current* portion of deferred revenue is **flat** ($1,005M → $999M) while *long-term* deferred revenue went **$6,406M → $8,178M (+28% in six months)**. The cash collected is for **long-dated** delivery, but the capex to serve it is being spent now. That inverts the P&L in 2027-29: revenue and EBITDA recognize against **zero incremental cash**. Capex ($3.2–3.4B) also exceeds D&A ($2.6–2.8B) while EBITDA falls — incremental capital is not yet earning.
