The refutation test collapses to asset value, and asset value is *below* the sale price by ~7×, not above it. Three primary facts make the discount look under-done, not over-done:

1. **The issuer prices its own equity at 20¢ on the dollar.** $0.53 vs. a $2.65 last sale is an 80% discount on 38.5% of the share count — and the 424B5 dilution table concedes new investors got an *increase* in net tangible book of $0.04/share, i.e. they bought below book while the tape trades at 4.5–6.7× book. That is the Nasdaq 5615(a)(3) home-country exemption — the mechanism that lets a 20%+ below-market issuance proceed without the 5635(d) shareholder vote. Sequencing is the finding.
