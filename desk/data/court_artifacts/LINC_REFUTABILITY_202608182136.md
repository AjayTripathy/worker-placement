## Guidance-arithmetic reconstruction (the part the tape got wrong in *both* directions)

Management reiterated FY26: revenue $590–600M, adj. EBITDA $76–80M, diluted EPS $0.74–0.83, start growth +10–14%, capex $70–75M. Testing each leg against FY25 actuals (revenue $518.2M, diluted EPS $0.65):

- **Revenue guide is nearly locked, and the reiteration is low-information.** H1'26 = $144.0M + $142.6M = $286.6M. H2 therefore needs only **$303.4–313.4M vs H2'25 $284.2M = +6.8% to +10.3%** — against a student body already **+10.4%** and enrolled, plus tuition. The company was *already* guiding a sharp H2 deceleration. Bulls treating "reiterated guidance" as vindication are over-reading a low bar.
- **EPS guide is credible, not stretched.** H1'26 EPS $0.20 ($0.14 + $0.06) looks alarming vs a $0.74–0.83 full year, but LINC is severely back-half weighted: H2'26 needs **$0.54–0.63 vs H2'25 ≈$0.55**, i.e. roughly flat to +15%. I checked this specifically because the H1 ratio looked like a red flag; it isn't one.
- **The START guide is the only stretched leg — and it is very stretched.** Using reported growth rates with an approximate seasonal start-mix (Q3 is the largest start quarter), hitting even the **low end** of +10–14% requires H2 starts to snap back from **+1% to roughly +9.5%**, and the midpoint requires **~+13%**. Management explicitly committed to "double-digit start growth in the third quarter." *(Start-mix weights are my derivation, not disclosed; the +1%/+19.5% growth rates and the 5,500 Q1 count are primary.)*

**Valuation — this is what kills the trade.** At $27.75 on ~31.3M diluted shares: mcap ≈ $869M; cash $44.2M less $26.0M revolver debt = ~$18M net cash; EV ≈ **$851M**. That is **~10.6–11.2x** FY26 guided EBITDA and **~33–38x** guided EPS — with capex $70–75M against EBITDA $76–80M, so **FCF ≈ zero**. The 56.34→27.75 move took the multiple from ~22x to ~11x EBITDA. That is a growth-multiple normalization on a broken leading indicator, which is the correct response, not an overshoot. A −50% drawdown here has *not* produced a cheap asset.
