## PRINT PROXIMITY: 2026-10-29 (Q3) — yfinance-derived, UNCONFIRMED; NOT within 5 trading days. **But a decisive binary corporate event IS: the 2026-08-14 special meeting (5 trading days out; proxy deadline 10:00 ET 2026-08-12), followed by the 2026-08-18 RMAX election deadline.** Verified from the meeting-materials release and the RMAX DEFM14A. I run the reconstruction against that event.

**Print-decisive reconstruction — what pre-event public data resolves the loudest claim.** The loudest bear claim is "the vote is a formality and REAX is about to lever itself onto a melting ice cube." Three pieces resolve from filings already public:

- **Approval risk is lower than the drawdown implies, not higher.** It is a Canadian plan-of-arrangement *special resolution* (10-for-1 consolidation + exchange into New Wildlife/Real REMAX Group + replacement awards, voted as one item; record date 2026-06-29), so the standard bar is 66⅔% of votes cast. Only ~16% is locked by director/officer support agreements — but the board is unanimous, **HSR early termination was already granted**, and acquirer-side vote failures are historically rare. Treat approval as ~90%, not a coin flip.
- **The consideration has already de-rated, and that is the deal's own damage report.** The 5.150 exchange ratio was struck off a ~$2.68 REAX reference to equal the $13.80 cash election. At $1.99 the stock leg is worth **$10.25**, a 26% shortfall; with the cash pool capped at $60–80M (~25% of RMAX's ~19.5M shares), blended consideration is ~$11.1. RMAX holders default to 5.15 shares if they don't elect by Aug 18. This is knowable now and does not require the print.
- **The genuinely dark variable lands *after* the vote, not at it.** The $550M Morgan Stanley/Apollo facility is a **364-day senior secured bridge**, to be termed out into debt or capital markets *before* closing. The coupon on that term-out — unpublished — is what decides whether ~$490M of net debt against ~$157M pre-synergy ($187M synergized) PF Adjusted EBITDA is a 5.9x-EBITDA bargain or an interest-expense trap. Management's own target is sub-2.0x net debt/Adj EBITDA only by the end of the *second full fiscal year* after close ([deal PR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1581091/000110465926049039/tm2612789d2_ex99-1.htm)).

**PRE-PRINT POSITION: FLAT.** Book is already NO POSITION / NO ORDERS, so this is a hold-the-line call, not a change. The Aug-14 vote is a ~90%-known outcome that has been public since April and buys no information; the decision-relevant unknown — the cost of the permanent financing that replaces the bridge — resolves *later*, and taking the position now pays full price for a coin you can't read. First tranche belongs after the vote/election clear, priced against the post-10-for-1 ~$20 quote, with the term-out coupon as the gating fact. Explicit branch to watch: a *failed* vote likely sends standalone REAX **up** (30% grower, no debt, $86.6M cash, ~5.5x) — that is ~10% of the distribution and an acceptable miss.

**Two things a court must not let pass unchallenged:** REAX's Adjusted EBITDA excludes heavy stock-based agent compensation — equity *is* how this company buys its 26% agent growth, and $27.6M on $700.6M of gross-commission revenue is a 3.9% margin with GAAP still negative. And the $47.2M CFO lands in the seasonal peak quarter on commission-payable float; it should not be annualized.

**COURT-WORTHINESS REAX: 7/10** — the sector narrative is refuted on the tape of the Aug-6 print, but whether issuing 40% of the company at a 63%-off-high price to buy a −6%-revenue franchisor is accretive or destructive is unresolved and would swing sizing from zero to a full position.
