The court-worthiness below is therefore earned on *unresolved magnitude*, not on absent damage — a distinction that matters, because the [earnings-dislocation template has graded 0-for-7](#) in this book and "management says it's temporary" is precisely that template's tell. CEO Adelson: headwinds are "a temporary disruption and not a fundamental resetting of our outlook," market is "K-shaped" with the middle market not recovered; backlog and new mandates "at record levels."

Two things a court would have to adjudicate, both live:
1. **Cyclical vs structural on mid-market software M&A.** Flat CF deal count with −25% fee/deal is the signature of a *mix* shift to smaller mandates, which mean-reverts; a −54% tech/software collapse is the signature of buyers refusing to underwrite SaaS terminal value under AI, which does not. Same quarter supports both readings. This question generalizes well beyond HLI.
2. **A likely artifact worth pricing separately.** The 2026‑08‑10 S‑3ASR / 2026‑08‑13 424B7 that the press framed as a "new shelf registration" altering the case registers **365,078 Class A shares** (255,422 Waller Helms + 109,656 7MA) issuable 1:1 on conversion of *already-issued* Class B — **0.53% of 68.9M diluted shares, zero new dilution** (8-K excerpt in the evidence pack, Item 8.01).
