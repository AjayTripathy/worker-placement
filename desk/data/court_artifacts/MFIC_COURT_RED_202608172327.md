### STRONGEST SINGLE KILL
The desk vacated the last court because blue's kill was a tooling artifact — so here is one that is arithmetic, not fetch-dependent, and drawn entirely from the vetted 8-K exhibit. MFIC has **$228.8M of net assets between today and the 1940 Act 150% asset-coverage floor**, and it lost **$50.3M in the most recent quarter alone** — 4.5 quarters of runway on the *conservative* (decelerating) cut, ~3.7 on the 1H26 average. Every instrument it had for defending that cushion is now spent: cash is **$43.3M** (down from $98.2M), the repurchase program is **"fully utilized"** with no new authorization, and the buyback was doing **42% of the NAV defense** (−$1.15/sh gross vs −$0.81/sh reported). Meanwhile the asset side is not being rebuilt — **$6M of new commitments and zero new portfolio companies in Q2** on a $2.77B book shrinking 16.8% YoY at a yield that fell 110bp. That is a levered pool in runoff, absorbing a 5.3×-YoY loss rate, with a manager paid on gross assets and publicly reassured that "our fee structure helps mitigate the impact of losses." The 27% discount does not compensate: at the realized loss rate the discount is consumed in under three quarters, and *before* the equity is wiped, breaching 150% forces asset sales into the manager's own marks and legally blocks the distribution that is the entire retail bid. The thesis's own reopen gate — non-accruals ≤5% at cost — is met at 4.6%, and it does not matter: that gate tests *disclosure*, and MFIC's disclosure is fine. What kills it is the **balance sheet**, and MFIC's non-accrual recognition at ~55c is the softest in the cohort anyway, so the 4.6% is the least-written-down 4.6% on the board.

### WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND

3. **New commitments ≥ $150M/qtr with ≥5 new portfolio companies** — proof it is an originating fund, not a runoff pool (finding 1 is the only one of my FATALs that a single quarter can reverse).
4. The 10-Q **Note 5 + related-party note read in full from a non-truncating fetch**, showing (a) the base fee is on *net* not gross assets, and (b) affiliated-bucket cost growth reversing.
5. A **dated catalyst** — an Apollo-level fee waiver, a merger, or a MidCap Financial asset purchase at a stated price — that crystallizes NAV. There is none today, and per the TCPC ruling this desk does not advance a levered BDC without one.
