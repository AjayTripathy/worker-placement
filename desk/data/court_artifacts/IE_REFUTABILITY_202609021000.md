### Print-decisive reconstruction (pre-print public data only)

The company published its own price-sensitivity table (June 23, 2025 PFS, Table 1, after-tax NPV₈%): $3.75→$0.91B · $4.25 (base)→$1.38B/20.0% IRR · $4.83 (then-spot)→$1.91B/24.0% · $5.00→$2.07B. That is a near-linear **~$0.93B of after-tax NPV per $1.00/lb** of copper. Initial capex $1.24B, C1 cash cost $1.32/lb (first quartile), 72kt/yr cathode, 23-yr life, reserves 136Mt @ 1.08% Cu.

- **Copper deck:** the old base was $4.25/lb. Spot is $6.60 (ATH $6.7775 touched Aug 26) — **55% above the base case**. Linear extrapolation past the table's $5.00 ceiling gives ~$3.5B after-tax NPV at spot; treat that as an upper bound, not a forecast.
- **The trap in that math:** S-K 1300 studies are not run at spot. A realistic updated base deck of $4.75–5.25 implies ~$1.84–2.2B before capex.
- **Capex direction is up:** the $64.7M TBM plus added engineering and two years of mining inflation plausibly takes $1.24B → $1.5–1.7B. Capex is ~dollar-for-dollar NPV at t=0, so subtract ~$300–450M.
- **Net:** headline updated PFS base-case NPV most likely prints **~$1.5–2.2B**, with a spot-sensitivity row near $3.0–3.3B. Against an EV of **~$1.36B** ($1.58B cap − $220.2M net cash).
- **Equity gap (the real sizing variable):** capex ~$1.6B less EXIM $1.1B and the credit-approved $200M bank facility leaves ~$300M, plus ~$100M/yr corporate burn to 2028 against $256.9M cash → **~$300M residual equity ≈ 30M shares ≈ 19% dilution** at $9.90. Bounded and known, but the last raise was 11.5M shares at **$15.00** (Oct 2025) — 34% above spot.

**PRE-PRINT POSITION: STARTER.** EV is ~1.0x an NPV struck at a copper price 55% below spot and ~0.38x NPV at actual spot, with damage absent on the narrative being priced — but the PFS is two-sided (capex inflation is near-certain, and a study is the conventional precursor to the financing raise), so I will not size in front of flow I am myself predicting.
