**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):**

1. **LGN** — At $50.71 the fully-exchanged cap is ~$5.48B (76.9M A + 31.2M B); EV including the $342.7M tax-receivable-agreement liability is ~$6.56B = **11.4x** the FY26 EBITDA midpoint, versus EMCOR at ~16.3x LTM / ~18x NTM and Comfort Systems at ~26.7x LTM / ~29x NTM — both of which also posted record backlogs and raised guidance, and neither of which fell more than ~12% over three months against LGN's −53%. LGN is priced at or below the *cheapest* comp while growing revenue 111% (60% organic) and is trading 6% below the price at which Blackstone cleared 15.4M shares in April, and at less than half the lowest of 17 analyst targets ($80 low, $106.41 mean). The dispersion the market ignored is that its data-center segment's margins are holding while its non-data-center segment is what broke.

The court must be run against three named size-variables, not the demand thesis:
- **Run-rate SBC is the whole valuation question.** Q2 SBC was $65.3M (5.2% of revenue) vs $7.7M PY, of which $52.0M is "Time Interests" — 1H total $101.7M plus $15.6M performance interests (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2052568/000205256826000026/R70.htm). If that is IPO-vesting run-off, 11.4x stands; if it is permanent, EBITDA-less-SBC is ~$400–425M and the stock is ~15–16x — i.e. *at EMCOR's multiple*, and the discount is roughly correct. The 10-Q does not disclose unrecognized cost or the remaining vesting period in the XBRL detail; that is a directly answerable court task.
- **Sponsor flow.** Blackstone still beneficially owns **50,647,086 shares = 46.9%** as of 6/30/26 after three sales (IPO 9/15/25, secondary 12/15/25, secondary 4/8/26 at $54.00) — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2052568/000119312526340346/0001193125-26-340346.txt. Distributions have come on a ~3–4 month cadence; the next window is plausibly post-Q3 print. Per the flow gate, do not bid into a predicted sponsor print.
- **Cash conversion behind the headline.** 1H OCF $194.5M is flattered by the $101.7M SBC add-back; ex-SBC and after $41.9M capex, 1H cash generation is ~$55M against a $6.6B EV, and $281.3M went out the door on acquisitions funded by a $200M term-loan draw. Q2 GAAP *operating income was negative* ($6.6M loss). The roll-up's amortization ($66.0M in 1H, rising) is a recurring cost of the growth, not an add-back to wave through.

Open item the court should also pull, not asserted here: end-customer concentration behind the 60% data-center backlog (unverified — a single-hyperscaler dependency would change the tail, and the "backlog *and awarded contracts*" construction includes non-firm awards).

**COURT-WORTHINESS LGN: 8/10 — demand damage is provably absent and the name is at/below the cheapest comp's multiple, but run-rate SBC and a 46.9% sponsor overhang swing the sizing from "starter" to "pass," and only a court resolves them.**

**PRINT PROXIMITY: 2026-11-13 — UNCONFIRMED (yfinance-derived; no Legence press release has yet named a Q3 2026 date, and the company's pattern is to announce ~3 weeks ahead; the year-ago Q3 was reported 2025-11-14). ~49 trading days out — NOT within 5 trading days, so the print-decisive reconstruction is not gating here.**

Position stance (not required at this print distance, offered because the court is being escalated): **STARTER, small.** No named kill exists — the demand thesis survives its own refuting metric and leverage is low — so the clean-court default applies rather than FLAT. But size stays minimal until the SBC run-rate is resolved, and any ladder should sit at/below the $54.00 April clearing price rather than adding into the likely post-Q3 sponsor distribution window.
