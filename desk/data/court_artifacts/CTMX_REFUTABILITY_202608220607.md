**Where I disagree with my own bull case, and it matters for sizing:** DAMAGE-ABSENT is a verdict on the *screened* narrative only. It does not say the discount is wrong. The market is not discounting CTMX for revenue decline; it is discounting a **post-raise overhang** (basic shares 129.1M → 218.9M on a $250M follow-on sold straight into the March +44% spike) and a **genuine clinical binary**. The named risks a court must price: 43% ORR at n=7 is not a real number and should regress at n=113; a **Grade 5 treatment-related acute kidney injury** occurred (Aug 2025, SRC supported continuation, [release](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/cytomx-therapeutics-provides-update-on-cx-2051-phase-1-study)); and Grade 3 diarrhea is only down to **~10% even with loperamide + budesonide prophylaxis**, which caps how far upstream the drug can move. That tolerability ceiling is the fulcrum of the 3L-vs-4L decision management said is still open.

**Catalyst identity, bound:** the price-moving asset is **CTMX-2051-101 / varsetatug masetecan (Varseta-M, CX-2051)** — not CX-801, whose first combination data is not due until 1H 2027 and is therefore not the near-term variable. Enrollment is **complete at 113 patients**, with the 40-patient dose-optimization goal met across 8.6 and 10 mg/kg Q3W as of end-April 2026. The decisive metric is the **end-2026 Phase 1 update**, which management said will carry a preliminary median PFS estimate **and the first overall-survival look** from escalation and expansion — OS being the expected primary endpoint of the registrational study.
