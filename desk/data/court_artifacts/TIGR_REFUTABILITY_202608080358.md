**Dispersion check against the only comparable cohort member (FUTU):** on the narrative's own metric the two are *similarly* damaged — FUTU: 13% of funded accounts / 17% of client assets / ~20% of revenue mainland ([yicaiglobal](https://www.yicaiglobal.com/news/chinese-mainland-client-assets-account-for-17-of-futus-total-ceo-says)); TIGR: 10% of assets / 20–25% of net revenue. Fine-to-revenue is near-identical (TIGR RMB411M on ~RMB4.0bn TTM revenue ≈10.2%; FUTU RMB1.85bn on ~RMB20.2bn ≈9.1%), which independently corroborates *proportional* mainland dependence and refutes any "TIGR is cleaner" claim. The dispersion is therefore in **price, not damage**: TIGR −64.5% from its 2025-08-25 high of $13.52 at ~5x ex-penalty earnings and **~1.00x book** ($842.8M equity / 178.7M ADS = $4.72 vs $4.815 spot), versus FUTU −46.2% at 12.1x.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):**
- *The strict damage-absent set is **EMPTY**.* TIGR does not qualify — the damage is disclosed, quantified by management, and legally mandated. It is nominated to court on the adjacent axis the triage is meant to surface: **equal damage, unequal price**. TIGR carries FUTU-equivalent mainland dependence (~20–25% vs ~20% of revenue; 9–10% fine/revenue both) while absorbing 1.4x the drawdown and pricing at 0.4x FUTU's multiple, and the whole quantified liability (~$110–137M of annual net revenue) runs off over 8 quarters against a base growing +27% YoY with $596.0M of own cash (excl. customer funds) inside an $857M market cap.

**Honest counterweights the court must carry (they are why this isn't a 9):** (1) ~1.0x book is TIGR's *historical* trough-to-mid zone, not an outlier — the bull case rests entirely on ROE being structurally higher now (FY2025 non-GAAP NI $186.5M on ~$0.8bn equity) than the 5–10% of 2022-23; (2) the runoff block is high-velocity — 10% of assets throwing 20–25% of revenue means ~2.2x revenue per asset dollar, so **incremental** margin loss is far worse than the revenue line implies (plausibly 45–60% of the $47.6M quarterly operating income at full runoff); (3) the genuine unhedgeable tail is CSRC's shift to *territory-based* oversight reaching offshore-resident PRC nationals in the HK/Singapore book — unquantified and unquantifiable from public data; (4) plaintiff-firm investigations (Rosen, Block & Leviton) are open but no certified class yet; (5) own cash fell $195M QoQ ($791.0M → $596.0M) — needs an explanation before any net-cash argument is used.

**PRE-PRINT POSITION: FLAT (no starter before 2026-08-26/27).** Book shows no position and no orders; the Q2 quarter contains only ~6 weeks post-crackdown, so it is a partial read on runoff velocity — but it carries the one metric that decides the case (mainland % of net revenue, restated, plus ex-mainland net inflow), and TIGR is a −25%-in-a-day tape. Enter after the disclosure, not into it.

**COURT-WORTHINESS TIGR: 7/10 — damage is real and disclosed, but FUTU-equal exposure priced at ~1.0x book / ~5x ex-penalty earnings makes the sizing decision genuinely two-sided, and the decisive inputs (incremental margin of the runoff block, migratability of "consolidated account" revenue, the offshore-PRC-national tail, the $195M own-cash drawdown) are all resolvable from public filings before the Aug-26/27 print.**
