The record argued entirely about the financing PATH; the PRIZE was never priced. **The finding: at spot lithium, magic funding is worth approximately today's price.** The market cap ($574M) already equals the fully-funded, fully-de-risked NPV at the current price deck. The stock is therefore an option on LITHIUM PRICE wearing a financing-catalyst costume — FID/debt-close headlines only pay if lithium is ≥ ~$22–24k/t, and at the DFS's own implied deck the fully-funded upside is +30–50%, not a multi-bagger. This makes the courts' FLAT stance correct for a sharper reason than either bench wrote: the financing risk everyone argued about is not even the binding constraint on upside.

**What it changes:** (1) re-court triggers amended — a debt-commitment 6-K alone is NOT an entry signal at spot lithium; entry requires price×funding jointly (lithium ≥ ~$22k AND financing signed) or a tape well below the spot-deck NPV (~<$1.75 = paying nothing for the option); (2) the ATM-stacking detector finding gets its economic frame: management selling equity at ~spot-deck NAV is not obviously dilutive-destructive — it is selling the option's carrying cost.

**Caveats (stated, not hidden):** flat-price NPV, simple 25% tax haircut, 3-yr build, no East Texas JV value (SLI 55% — real but pre-DFS, unpriced upside), no demo-plant value; the full SEDAR DFS (with its own NPV/sensitivity table) remains unread — a re-court should pull it and replace this approximation.
