**Why not STRUCTURAL, and why not DAMAGE-ABSENT** — the two-year stack is the discriminator. Q3'26 guide midpoint $1,615M vs Q3'24 ≈ $1,131M (= 1,922 ÷ 1.70) is **+19.5% CAGR**, still roughly on management's "20%+ compounded" line; that is not terminal decline. But the seasonal read refutes the pure-comp defense: Q2→Q3 bookings normally step up **+33.7% (2025)** and **+18.8% (2024)**; the Q3'26 guide is **+1.2% to +6.2%** sequentially — 13–30pp of *seasonally adjusted* damage that no comp artifact explains. Management's own attribution is a mix of an RFY discovery-algorithm change deliberately trading near-term monetization for 28-day retention, rolloff of 2025-vintage viral titles, Kids/Select age-gating, and disabling cross-experience game passes (letter pp. 2, 5–6).

**Cash line is where the damage is sharpest and least discussed:** Q3 FCF guided to **$(60)M–$5M** against $443M in Q3'25, OCF $110–175M vs $546M (letter p. 12) — a ~$450M swing, only ~$210M of which is capex timing + the disclosed $40M creator-payout working-capital headwind. And the headline "revenue +36%" is a GAAP artifact: it is 2025's bookings amortizing over a 27-month payer life, which Roblox states in footnote 5. Ballast on the other side: $6.1B cash + investments vs $1.0B debt (net cash ~$5.1B), $3B buyback authorized May 19 with 8.2M shares repurchased in Q2, MUPs +15%, top-10 titles now ~20% of hours vs 30% three years ago, US O18 DAUs +32% / hours +27% at 50%+ higher monetization. Two unsized liabilities: SBC ran $282M in the quarter (~$1.1B/yr against 1H26 FCF of $890M — economic FCF ≈ 0), and Q3 net-loss guidance **explicitly excludes loss-contingency accruals** (letter p. 12, fn B) with securities class actions filed over pre-crash age-verification disclosure.
