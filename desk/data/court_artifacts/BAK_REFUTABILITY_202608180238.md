### Print-decisive reconstruction (pre-deadline public data only)

Loudest bull claim: *a $1B EBITDA quarter lets Braskem negotiate from strength and avoid restructuring.* Three items in the already-filed 2Q26 disclosure resolve it without waiting for Aug 24:

1. **Peak spreads produced negative cash.** $1,043M EBITDA converted to ~$210M recurring cash generation and **−$15M net cash consumption** after $136M interest, $112M capex, $547M working-capital build and Alagoas outflows (issuer release, above). The best spread environment available to this asset base does not self-fund it.
2. **Braskem Idesa idled 57% of its PE capacity into those spreads.** Mexico utilization fell to **43% from 55%**, explicitly to preserve liquidity, while PE USA-ethane spreads ran 55% above historical average ([earnings call, 2026-08-14](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-braskem-misses-q2-2026-estimates-as-shares-fall-93CH-4861416)). A producer voluntarily shutting in volume at peak margin is a working-capital constraint, not an operating choice. This is the single most decisive datum and it is already public.
3. **The negotiated terms are equity-negative on both branches.** Creditors rejected the gentle amend-and-extend tender; live proposals contemplate "a possible **capitalization** and the granting of **security interests over assets**" (6-K, 2026-07-28) — i.e. dilution of the $887M stub and encumbrance of the assets behind it. Elliott is pressing for a Petrobras injection *before* creditors accept losses; Petrobras (36.1% economic / ~47% voting) has not committed ([ICIS](https://www.icis.com/explore/resources/news/2026/04/20/11199425/brazil-s-ig4-seals-debt-for-equity-deal-for-novonor-s-braskem-stake-pledges-financial-restructuring/)). Deal → dilution/pledge. No deal → creditors resume enforcement, judicial-recovery risk.

Forward arithmetic confirms it: consultants forecast Q3 PE-naphtha at **$316/t, −59% q/q** — roughly 43% *below* the historical average implied by the pack's own "$773 = 39% above average." Against ~$544M/yr interest plus $485M/yr capex plus ~R$3.2B residual Alagoas provision, normalized EBITDA in the $192M/quarter zone is structurally cash-negative. Management's own words: 2Q26 was "tactical value capture in a volatile environment, rather than a structural change."

**PRE-PRINT POSITION: FLAT — and explicitly no price gate.** We hold nothing; the correct action is to hold nothing through Aug 24. A resting bid below market into a restructuring binary is an accidental short-vol position that fills precisely on the branch where the equity is being capitalized away — the forced-seller adverse-selection inversion in textbook form. The only equity-positive branch (a Petrobras injection on non-dilutive terms) is a lottery ticket the counterparties are actively negotiating *against*, and it is a RISK response, which under the taxonomy sets SIZE, not price. Size is zero.

### COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):

**None.** No member of this triage classifies DAMAGE-ABSENT. BAK's 2Q26 print refutes the cohort's spread damage and the equity fell anyway — which disqualifies the name from the cohort rather than promoting it. Recording the empty set as the finding: the dispersion the cohort sweep flagged here is capital-structure dispersion, not narrative dispersion, and routing it to a spread-focused court would produce a well-argued answer to the wrong question.

**COURT-WORTHINESS BAK: 2/10** — a court cannot move size off zero into a dated solvency binary whose live terms already contemplate capitalizing the equity away; the discount is correct and the decision is overdetermined by filed data.
