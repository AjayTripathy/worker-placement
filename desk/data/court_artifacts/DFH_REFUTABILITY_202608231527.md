## COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):

**None.** DFH does not qualify as DAMAGE-ABSENT. The demand leg alone is refuted; the margin and leverage legs are confirmed in the last audited print. Promoting it on the strength of the order line would be exactly the single-metric error R2.3 exists to prevent.

It is nonetheless **court-worthy on a different axis** — the screen's own premise is wrong in a way that changes sizing:

1. **The "cheap on book" trigger is arithmetically false.** Screens show P/B **0.95x** off $15.75 BVPS — but that BVPS divides *total* DFH stockholders' equity ($1,432M) including the **$150M Series A convertible preferred that is being redeemed for cash on 2026-09-14** at $1,028.50/sh (~$154.3M, 8-K 2026-08-14). Common book is ~$1,278M → **BVPS ≈ $14.08 → P/B ≈ 1.04x**, not 0.95x. Strip **$377.4M of goodwill** (26% of assets, from a serial roll-up) and TBVPS ≈ **$9.92 → P/TBV ≈ 1.48x**. A deep-value homebuilder trigger firing at 1.5x tangible book is a mis-fire.
2. **The junior capital is private-credit priced and its terms are undisclosed.** Pro-forma structure (8-K 2026-08-18): $1.5B revolver, ~$1.3B senior unsecured notes, ~$80M junior subordinated notes, and **~$675M of *redeemable* preferred** (~$225M refinancing the Series A, **$450M new at merger**) — committed by Goldman Sachs, Bank of America and **affiliates of Kennedy Lewis Asset Management**. Coupon, PIK toggle, conversion and change-of-control terms are **not public**. Per response taxonomy, that is a **data→kill**, not a price gate: pro-forma equity value is not underwritable without the preferred's coupon and seniority.
3. **A non-obvious GAAP trap post-close.** DFH already carries a ~10-point wedge between GAAP HB GM (14.2%) and adjusted (24.2%) from capitalized interest and purchase-accounting amortization. Buying Beazer at **0.8x book** forces a fair-value step-up of Beazer inventory, which will **mechanically compress reported GAAP gross margin through 2027** — the exact metric the cohort narrative keys on. Bears will read purchase accounting as demand damage; bulls citing "$100M run-rate synergies, accretive to 2027" will be fighting the optics. That divergence is worth a bench.
4. **Governance datum (minor, non-decisive).** The 2026-08-21 8-K amends the 2021 Equity Incentive Plan to carve a **$400,000 director-comp exception for a non-employee Co-Chairman** — i.e. Rick Beckwitt, ex-Lennar, appointed 2026-07-14 — *and* modifies the **definition of "Fair Market Value" to allow greater flexibility** in pricing awards, with the stock 53% off its high. Approved by written consent, no meeting (DEF 14C). Beckwitt is a genuine quality signal; the FMV-flexibility amendment at a 52-week low is an RP flag, not a kill.

Counterweights a court must blue-team: 15.8% short of a ~27M-share float is squeeze fuel into a Q4 close; TTM FCF is **−$161.75M** with negative OCF in every Q1 of the pack's XBRL series; Altman Z **2.97**; and the controlling shareholder is concurrently running an MLB franchise acquired for ~$1.7B in Oct 2025 (he has stated DFH shares are *not* the primary Rays collateral — an assertion, not a filing, and worth verifying against Form 4/13D pledge disclosure before sizing).
