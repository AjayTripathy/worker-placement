On 2026-03-17 the company disclosed that "the existing Duravelo-2 trial design is no longer considered acceptable as an approval path for zelenectide in mUC," deprioritized zelenectide, moved to discontinue Duravelo-3 and Duravelo-4, dropped BT7480 internal development, and cut ~30% of headcount ([8-K ex99-1, 2026-03-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001761612/000110465926028954/tm268887d1_ex99-1.htm)). Asset-side damage is **confirmed and complete**, not feared.

Q2 actual was $49.4M. **Q3 must print cash ≥ ~$470M at 9/30/26 (burn ≤$40M).** If Q3 cash prints below ~$460M, the 50%-opex-reduction claim is not landing, runway compresses toward 2029, and this row flips to DAMAGE-ARRIVING. That is the single number to read on the print.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):**

1. **BCYC** — The market is applying a distress multiple (0.58× gross cash, 0.72× cash net of every liability, negative EV of ~$214M) to a balance sheet with **no debt, no ATM, no maturity wall, and a 3.5-year runway**, while the deprioritized asset's own data are not bad — 62% confirmed ORR in 1L mUC with ~4× lower skin toxicity and ~half the peripheral neuropathy vs published SOC ([ASCO 2026 release, 2026-05-22](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/bicycle-therapeutics-to-present-initial-duravelo-2-data-at-2026-asco-annual-meeting)) — the kill was a *regulatory-path* kill, not an efficacy kill, which is precisely the kind of asset that gets partnered rather than zeroed. Dispersion the cohort ignored: BCYC screens on the same dd52 as names that are pre-financing and levered; it is neither.

**What a court actually has to resolve (why the score is 7 and not 9):** the red team's material is strong and not refuted by any metric above — (a) there is no mechanism to convert $510M of cash into shareholder cash: BCYC is an England & Wales plc with a **classified board** ("Charles Swanton resigned as a Class II director," [8-K 2026-08-20](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1761612/000110465926099221/)), so the standard US negative-EV activist path is materially harder and should not be assumed; (b) management's stated plan is to spend that cash on nuzefatide/BT5528 in **recurrent pancreatic cancer** (first patient dosed April 2026) — a historically low-probability indication — plus BT1702, whose Phase 1 does not even start until 2027, i.e. no value-creating readout for ~18 months; (c) the residual near-term catalyst is undated: "additional Duravelo-2 data from the randomized Phase 2 trial in 2H 2026," which is an *asset-you-already-deprioritized* readout with ambiguous read-through; (d) the loss of a marquee clinical-advisory/board scientist three days before the sweep is unexplained beyond "personal reasons." The court's job is to price the *discount to cash*, not to argue about solvency — that part is settled.
