### Framework note (worth wiring, sibling to "beta-bleed is not a dislocation")

`dd52`-driven dislocation screens will **systematically over-fire on post-binary-event biotech for exactly 12 months**, because the 52-week high is a pre-event price from a dead information regime. Suggested guard: censor or flag any candidate whose 52-week drawdown is dominated by a **single-session gap** (e.g. max 1-day return < −35% within the window), and re-rank on a post-event anchor (26-week high, or high-since-event) instead. MLTX would have scored −25% rather than −70.9% under that rule — still interesting, but not the sector-outlier the sweep reported.
