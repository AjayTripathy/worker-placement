PRINT-DECISIVE RECONSTRUCTION (pre-print, public data only). Loudest bear claim: "BCRED is a slow-motion run that forces asset sales, marks down the book, and craters BX's fee-earning AUM." Public pre-print data already resolves three of its four legs. (a) Run velocity: gross requests went 7% → 10% → 10% of shares, and the dollar ask *fell* $4.5B → $4.3B quarter-on-quarter — a plateau at a level the 5% cap absorbs. (b) Forced-selling: the fund states repayments plus new subscriptions cover ~160% of this quarter's repurchases against >$17B of cash and undrawn capacity at 0.8x leverage versus a 2.0x regulatory limit — the gate is a pacing device, not a liquidity failure. (c) Marks: non-accruals fell at both vehicles (BCRED 1.4%→1.1% FV; BXSL 3.1%→1.8% FV), the opposite of the narrative's direction of travel. (d) Unresolved, and the only leg that matters: the *repricing of new money* — Q3 subscriptions of $750M against ~$2.2B of repurchase means BCRED shrinks even with the gate holding, and the Oct/Nov prints carry the September-30 NAV and the final Q3 repurchase dollar value. That is what the 10-Q cannot pre-empt.
