**The dispersion the cohort ignores — and the fact that kills the easy long:**

- *For the name:* Gamer & Creator Peripherals is **$115.9M, +13% YoY, at 44.9% GM** (up from 40%) — ~37% of revenue but roughly **half of gross profit**, and memory pricing doesn't touch it. Fanatec sim-racing + Elgato Marketplace (transactions >2x YoY) + a named GTA 6 launch tailwind. Balance sheet is **net cash ~$75M** ($193.9M cash vs $118.7M debt) with **$74.8M of Q2 operating cash flow**.
- *Against the name:* the beat that caused the pop was **~entirely the tariff refund**. Ex-refund, Q2 adj EBITDA was **$16.6M vs a $15.5M guide high end** and non-GAAP EPS **$0.09 vs $0.05–0.07** — a real but small beat, not the $0.23-vs-$0.09 headline. The company states it is **"materially complete with the tariff refund process."** The FY adj-EBITDA raise (prior $100–115M → $121–131M, midpoint **+$18.5M**) is **$14.3M refund**, i.e. **~$4M organic**.
- *Guide is not heroic, though:* H1-26 actual = $354.5M + $314.3M = **$668.8M**; FY guide $1.40–1.47B implies **H2 of $731–801M vs H2-25 of $782.6M** (FY25 $1,472.5M less H1-25 $689.9M) — i.e. **−6.6% to +2.4% YoY, midpoint −2%**. The raise does not require a recovery in DIY; it requires peripherals to keep compounding while components bleed.
- *Valuation (my arithmetic, flag it):* EV ≈ $1.15B on the pack's $1.23B mcap less net cash → **~9.9–10.8x FY26 adj EBITDA ex-refund ($107–117M)** and **~13.5–15.2x ex-refund non-GAAP EPS ($0.72–0.81)**. Not distressed. And at $10.91 the stock is still **+143% off its 52w low** and only ~$1.3 above its pre-print level — this is a winner giving back, not a washed-out name.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):** *none.* CRSR does not qualify — the narrative's damage is present in the named metrics and pre-guided by management. Listing it here would require ignoring the tariff-refund adjustment to the "record margin."

**The three questions a court would actually have to settle (why it's still worth running):**
1. **Refund allocation.** Was the $15.6M refund booked inside C&S? If so, underlying C&S GM ≈ 18.4% and underlying memory GM is *already* high-teens today — meaning the Q4 guide-down is a floor, not a slope. If it sat elsewhere, 23.4% is clean and Q4 is a genuine −500bp step. The release does not disclose the segment split; this single fact swings the H2 gross-profit bridge by ~$15M.
2. **Is peripherals' 13% durable or GTA-6-dated?** Half the gross profit rests on it, and management's own FY bridge names a single game launch.
3. **"Deferred, not lost."** Management's DIY thesis is that upgrade demand accumulates. That is the entire terminal-value argument and it is currently untested — GS's −15% PC-unit call is the disconfirming prior.

A court that answers #1 from the 10-Q segment footnote alone would move this from "fair" to a live sizing decision.
