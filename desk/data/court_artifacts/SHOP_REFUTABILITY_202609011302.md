**Thesis-premise audit (the brief's own asks):**
- *Evidence SHOP is building on the channel:* **YES** — AI Toolkit connectors to ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity/Manus/Replit/Vercel, Catalog (converts 2x scraped data), partnerships named with OpenAI/Google/Meta/Microsoft.
- *Is OpenAI commerce GMV disclosed/material:* **NO.** Not in the PR, not in the 10-Q. Call-only multiples on an unnamed base.
- *In-chat checkout premise:* **DEAD.** OpenAI ended Instant Checkout and moved commerce to brand-owned ChatGPT apps; only **~12 Shopify merchants ever went live** against the 1M+ announced, per Finkelstein at an investor conference ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/shopify-says-purchases-are-coming-inside-chatgpt-through-agentic-storefronts-as-openai-retreats-on-instant-checkout/), [PYMNTS 2026-03-11](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/openai-moves-commerce-focus-to-brand-owned-chatgpt-apps/)).
- *Symmetric risk that OpenAI builds its own merchant rail:* **REALIZED, partially.** Walmart shipped an in-app ChatGPT service with its own linking, loyalty and payments — large merchants route around any platform rail.

**Blue-team counter I owe the ratchet-check:** Instant Checkout's death may be *bullish* for SHOP's take rate. Agentic storefronts (launched March 2026, now on for all Shopify stores) push the transaction back to the merchant's own Shopify-Payments storefront rather than consummating it inside OpenAI's rail — Shopify taxes the GMV either way, and in-chat checkout was the version that threatened to disintermediate its checkout. That inversion is genuinely non-consensus. It does not, however, change the sizing decision, because it is unmeasurable at current disclosure.
