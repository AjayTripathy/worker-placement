**Why this isn't a price dislocation at all — the dilution channel.** Post a 1-for-30 reverse split, Class A+B ADS went **92.5M (2026-02-04) → 128.7M (2026-03-19)** on the PIPE, and the pack's own $2.338B mcap ÷ $13.70 implies **~170.6M today** — roughly **+84% share count in six months** (last figure derived from the pack, not a filed count). Geely/Volvo completed **~$640M of debt-to-equity conversion on 2026-07-01** (6-K 0001104659-26-079627; 13D/A 0001104659-26-080363), and Volvo termed out a $726M shareholder loan to Dec-2031. Continuous related-party conversion *is* the funding model. A dd52-based screen reads that transfer of per-share value as a discount; it isn't one. Note also that the July conversion is balance-sheet, not liquidity — it adds no cash against a −$235M/qtr adjusted-EBITDA run rate on $676M.

**And the covenant tell:** the Q1 "in compliance with all covenants as of March 31, 2026" statement is true only after the $950M Club loan's revenue and debt-to-asset tests were *amended for Q1 and every remaining 2026 test period*. Compliance was achieved by moving the goalposts, not by performance.

**Steelman, stated fairly:** US was only ~6% of Q1 retail, so the ban's direct revenue hit is small; Polestar 4 production has started in Korea and Polestar 5 deliveries begin Q4, which localizes tariff-exposed build. If H1 gross margin prints back positive on 8/27, the "structurally negative unit economics" leg genuinely weakens. That is the one live refutation path — but it doesn't reach the equity, because the recovery accrues first to the converting lender, and the US exclusion caps TAM permanently regardless.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):** none. PSNY has no damage-absent leg — the refuting metric (gross margin) has already crossed to negative, the volume guide is broken at the half, and the loudest bear claim (US regulatory exclusion) is a *completed government decision*, not a forecast.
