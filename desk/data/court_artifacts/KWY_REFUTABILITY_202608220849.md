**Why this is not a dispersion trade:** the cohort-median comparison is a broken anchor. Per mispricing-artifact doctrine, an edge is real only against a same-event *external* anchor; here the "event" never touched KWY's P&L, so the excess drawdown carries no information about mispricing. What actually de-rated the stock:

- **Valuation was never distressed.** At $10.03 × 28.6M sh `[10-Q-derived]` = ~$287M equity + $59.9M net debt → EV ≈ **$347M** vs pro-forma portfolio LTM EBITDA **$22–23M** = **~15.4× EV/EBITDA**. At the $16.46 high that was ~23×. This is a multiple de-rate on a levered microcap rollup, not a discount to value.
- **Negative tangible equity.** Shareholders' equity **$15.3M** against goodwill **$65.4M** + intangibles **$48.5M** on **$227.8M** total assets `[10-Q-derived]` → tangible book ≈ **−$98.6M**; P/B ~20×. GAAP losses persist (H1-26 net loss to common **−$3.7M**; H1 interest **$2.8M**, amortization **$4.8M**).
- **A named, dated near-term kill variable.** Three portfolio companies were out of covenant compliance in Q2. CFO Kent Hansen on the call: the facilities are *"non-recourse to Kingsway Corporation"* with no cross-default, and the three represent *"under 10% of portfolio EBITDA."* `[10-Q-derived]` **$22.8M of debt was reclassified to current**; SNS revolver extended to **2026-12-02**, DDI facility matures **2026-11-01**, KPH's $3.3M matured **2026-08-14 with no waiver disclosed**. Unrestricted cash is only ~$9.7M (net-debt bridge: $69.6M total debt − $59.9M net debt) against that $22.8M current stack.
- **Float mechanics, not mispricing.** ~60% insider ownership, no sell-side coverage, and Capricorn Fund Managers cut from 1,908,242 sh (6.6%) at 2026-01-30 to 1,410,526 sh (4.9%) by 2026-06-30 ([SC 13G/A filed 2026-07-13](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001072627/000190136126000012/primary_doc.xml)) — a ~500k-share exit into a ~11M-share free float. That is supply, and it is a permanent-undiscovery structure, not a closing discount.
- **The Form 4 cluster is noise.** The 15 Form 4s since June look like conviction buying; they are semi-monthly **ESPP** payroll purchases — Hansen 158 sh @ $9.87 and CEO Fitzgerald 254 sh @ $9.87 on 2026-08-17 ([Form 4, acc. 0001072627-26-000050](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001072627/000107262726000050/form4.xml)). Do not score these as insider signal.
