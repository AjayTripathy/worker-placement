## Context

LEN is not a cohort member sold on a shared narrative — it is a single-issuer **instrument court** (which of two claims on the same cash flows is mispriced). The screen's implied narrative is "the LEN/LEN.B spread at 1.6% is 0 pctile of a 15.8%-mean, 6y weekly distribution, therefore the discount reverts." I ran that claim down to primary share counts and same-day closes. It fails on three independent tests, and the classification is **STRUCTURAL**: the compressed spread is correct, and the historical 15.8% mean is not a recoverable anchor.

**CLASSIFICATION: STRUCTURAL.** Both legs are economically identical — the 10-Q reports a *single* combined "Basic and diluted earnings per share $1.24" line, no two-class method, same $2.00 dividend. The only differences are votes (B = 10x), liquidity, and index eligibility. Three findings kill the reversion premise:

1. **No mechanism.** The 10% auto-conversion is ~55 years out at the observed drift and moving *sideways*, not toward the trigger. H1 FY26 buybacks were near-pro-rata (A −5,268,902 / −2.44%; B −827,874 / −2.65%), which holds the ratio flat by design. The Nov-2025 Millrose exchange offer retired **8,049,594 Class A shares only** ([final results](https://newsroom.lennar.com/2025-11-26-Lennar-Announces-Final-Results-of-Exchange-Offer)) — that pushed B's share *up*, away from the trigger. GAMCO's ask to include Class B was refused.
2. **The screen has a nonstationarity bug.** A percentile rank against a 6-year window on a *secularly trending* series manufactures 0-pctile readings mechanically. LEN's spread is at **0 pctile vs 6y and ~50th pctile vs 3 months**. GEF (1st pctile, discount *widening*) is a genuine outlier; LEN is trend-tail. **Fix: require the reading to be an outlier vs a trailing 6–12m window before flagging.** BELFA self-validation does not cover this failure mode — it was a widening too.
3. **The trade is on the wrong side of the flow.** LEN ADV ≈ 2.4M sh (~$207M/day); LEN.B ADV ≈ 54–165k sh (~$4.6–14M/day) — a ~30–40x liquidity gap. Lennar's own H1 repurchases ran ~54.6k A/day = **2.3% of A's ADV** but ~6.7k B/day = **8–12% of B's ADV**. Short-B/long-A means shorting a 54k-ADV name into the issuer's own persistent bid, with an unresolved convertibility campaign as an open upside tail on the short leg. Violates the value-ladder flow gate.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):** *None.* No member survives triage as damage-absent. The dispersion the screen found is not mispricing — it is the correct price of a structure whose convergence catalysts were tested and defeated (April 2026 vote) and whose mechanical trigger is ~55 years away. LEN is ruled **STRUCTURAL / no spread trade**.

**One actionable by-product (instrument selection, not a position):** if the desk ever wants LEN exposure on its own merits (−39% from highs, ~13x, $86.33 vs $79.83 52w low), buy **Class A**. The standard homebuilder heuristic "always buy the B line" is dead here: 1.6% of discount does not pay for 40x worse liquidity above ~$1–2M of size. That heuristic was right at −16%; it is wrong at −1.6%.
