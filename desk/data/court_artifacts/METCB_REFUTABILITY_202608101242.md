Ramaco Class B — cause-check first, then triage.

**Cause of the −70.2% dd52 is not the coal narrative.** METCB is a *tracking stub* on Ramaco's "CORE" assets (royalty/tolling/IP + the Brook Mine rare-earth prospect), distributed 1-for-5 to Class A holders in June 2023. Its paired Class A (METC) is down harder over the same window (−82% off a $57.80 high to $10.24 vs B's $21.24→$6.44), so both classes deflated one shared premium: the Brook Mine REE story that peaked into Wolfpack Research's 23-Oct-2025 "Potemkin Mine" report. The met-coal business underneath is at trough but intact — $99/t cash cost, fourth straight sub-$100 quarter, first-quartile on the US cost curve, +$5.7M adj. EBITDA, $400.1M liquidity. Energy-sector damage is largely **absent**; that just isn't what got sold.

Two things settle the triage, and both are charter-level rather than fundamental:

1. **§4.1(E) of the Second A&R Certificate of Incorporation** lets the Board, "in its sole discretion," exchange *all* Class B into Class A at an **Optional Conversion Ratio = 20-day average market value of B ÷ 20-day average market value of A**. The conversion is struck *at* the prevailing discount, so no conversion event can ever close the B-vs-A gap. §4.1(D) does make the shares otherwise "identical," but that pari-passu residual claim has no reachable catalyst.
2. **§4.1(G)** gives the Board authority to set different dividend rights per class — rate, and whether payable in cash or stock. The CORE/REE "mineral royalty" Class B holders are told to expect is a 2023 FAQ *anticipation*, not a charter right, and can be taken to zero by board resolution.
