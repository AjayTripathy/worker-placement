### The three things a court has to adjudicate (all resolvable from pre-print public data)

**1. The $20M IEEPA refund is either the whole Q3 beat or the whole Q3 guide.** The 10-Q discloses, as a subsequent event, ~$20M of IEEPA tariff refunds received after 6/30, to be recognized in **cost of sales in Q3 2026**. The evidence pack's own XBRL tag list independently corroborates the disclosure's existence and period (`crox:IEEPATariffRefundMember us-gaap:SubsequentEventMember 2026-07-01 2026-07-30`); the $20M *magnitude* is single-sourced and should be re-pulled with the full-fingerprint fetcher before sizing ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001334036/000133403626000052/crox-20260630.htm) — direct fetch 403'd here).

- $20M × (1 − 18% adj. tax) ÷ ~52M diluted ≈ **$0.29–0.33/sh**.
- The guide-to-consensus gap that flushed the stock is $3.25 mid vs $3.54 = **$0.29**. Near-exact match.
- $20M ÷ ~$996M Q3 revenue = **~200bps of gross margin**, against a guided Q3 adj. GM of **+170bps YoY**.

Two branches, opposite signs: if the guide **excludes** it (gain-contingency treatment, "not able to predict the timing"), the entire consensus shortfall is arithmetically reversible and the guide is conservative. If it **includes** it — and the company knew about it on 7/30, the day it set the guide — then *all* of the guided Q3 margin expansion is a non-recurring customs refund and underlying GM is **−30bps**, which is a cleaner explanation of the month-long grind than either the tax story or the sector day. I could not resolve this from public secondary sources; that irreducibility is most of the court's value.

**2. RP_TAINTED flips the sizing decision binary.** NYT (Drucker/Freedman, **2026-08-05**) reports a two-person Malta entity holding >$3B of IP, cutting 2023 tax by **$218.6M** per Maltese filings, with economic-substance audit risk flagged ([TipRanks/The Fly, 8/5](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/crocs-using-malta-office-to-dodge-taxes-nyt-says-thefly-news); [TaxProf](https://taxprofblog.aals.org/2026/08/12/n-y-times-crocs-has-a-trick-for-dodging-taxes-a-tiny-office-in-malta/)). Adjusted ETR guided ~18% vs a ~24–25% normalized blend → FY26 adj. EPS $13.85 → **~$12.75 (−8%)**; fwd P/E 8.6x → 9.3x. That alone is survivable. The doctrine cap is not: RP_TAINTED forbids RP_FAIR and caps at 0.85 × E[fv] — **0.85 × $138.25 street PT = $117.51, below the $119.08 spot.** Under the tainted branch there is no entry here at any size; under the untainted branch there is a starter. Nothing else in this file has that property.

**3. The import-nowcast's first live test has a primary-source contradiction.** Census 6402 cal-Q2 +9.2% YoY against a flat revenue guide is the desk's edge, but: (a) the spring-25 base is the IEEPA tariff-shock trough — a real, large base-effect contaminant, not a caveat to wave through; (b) 6402 is aggregate market, not Crocs share; and (c) most directly, **Crocs's own footwear units are down high-single-digits with inventory −4%** — you do not inflect shipments +9% while your own unit inventory falls HSD and you guide flat. Either the inflection is competitors', or it is Q4 holiday build rather than Q3 revenue. Grade the vertical on this, not on the price outcome.
