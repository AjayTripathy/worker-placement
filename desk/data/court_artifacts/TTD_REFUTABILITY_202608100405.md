### The three facts that decide the classification

**1. They broke their own floor.** In May 2026 TTD guided Q2 to *"at least $750M"* revenue and *"approximately $260M"* adj EBITDA. They printed **$715.1M / $241.3M** — a $35M miss below an "at-least" floor. This is the load-bearing fact, and it cuts directly against the strongest bull argument (that ≥$650M is a sandbag set by a one-month-old CFO). You cannot underwrite a floor from a team that just demonstrated the floor isn't binding.

**2. The FY26 ≥40% adj-EBITDA-margin framework is arithmetically dead, not merely at risk.** 9M-2026 implied adj EBITDA is $606.3M on $2,054M revenue = **29.5%**. Reaching 40% for the full year would require a Q4 margin of **61–67%** across any plausible Q4 revenue ($800M–$1.0B), against a company best-ever near ~47%. That target was reiterated one quarter ago.

**3. Decremental margin >100%.** Q2→Q3 guide: revenue −$65M, adj EBITDA −$81M. They are spending *into* the decline (four new C-suite hires — CFO Nate Olmstead, CMO Sarah Gavin, CCO Kristi Argyilan, CBDO Ron Lamprecht; Olmstead is the **fourth CFO in 14 months**). Green said the guide "embeds time for new executives to get up to speed" — an explicit sandbag admission that fact #1 makes untrustworthy.

### Why this is NOT damage-absent

The metric management offers as reassurance — **customer retention "over 95%"** — is non-diagnostic. It is a customer-*count* statistic TTD has cited for a decade, and it is fully consistent with the bear case: advertisers stay on the platform while reallocating dollars to Amazon DSP. Guideline third-party data has Amazon going from **<10% to ~20% of tracked programmatic spend in 15 months**. Retention cannot refute budget reallocation; only dollar-based spend retention could, and TTD doesn't disclose it.

Genuine counter-evidence exists but is second-order: international mix 14%→17% with EMEA/APAC CTV +50%, China +100%, audio fastest-growing four quarters running, JBPs at 217 clients (+38% YoY) growing ~6× the corporate rate. All real; none of it is large enough to offset a −12% consolidated guide.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):** **NONE.** No member clears the damage-absent bar. TTD's predicted damage is present in the P&L, in the guide, and in the abandoned margin framework. Reporting an empty list is the correct output here rather than promoting the least-damaged member.
