## PRINT-DECISIVE RECONSTRUCTION (pre-print, public data only)

The loudest claim is *"the 2025 profitability turnaround is dead — growth is rented from Douyin and more dilution is coming."* Three things already resolve it without the print:

**1. Marginal marketing ROI is already below 1.0x — this is arithmetic from the Q1 release, not a forecast.**

| Q1'26 vs Q1'25 | Δ |
|---|---|
| Revenue RMB1,021.0M vs 833.5M | **+187.5M** |
| Gross profit RMB819.2M vs ~659.0M (+24.3%) | **+160.2M** |
| S&M RMB737.2M vs ~554.3M (+33%) | **+182.9M** |

The incremental marketing dollar bought RMB160.2M of gross profit for RMB182.9M of spend — **RMB22.7M destroyed at the margin, before a yuan of R&D or G&A.** S&M went 66.5% → **72.2%** of revenue (+570bp). Yatsen names the cause itself: "increased brand-building investments and **higher traffic acquisition costs on the Douyin platform**."

**2. The guide decelerates into the *promotional* quarter.** Q2'25 was RMB1,088.1M (backed out: 4,300 − 833.5 − 998.4 − 1,380). The RMB1.20–1.30bn guide = +10.3% to +19.5%, down from +22.5% in Q1 and +26.7% for FY25. Q2 contains 618 — the heaviest discount festival of the year — so S&M% is structurally unlikely to *fall* in Q2. Guiding growth down while spending up into the promo quarter is the wrong pairing.

**3. The cash line will look great and it will be financing, not operations.** Cash+restricted fell RMB1.05bn → RMB934.2M in Q1 with OCF at **−RMB90.0M** (reversing from positive). Q2 will show cash *rising* on the ~$60M first convert tranche (closed 5/21). Do not read that as a business inflection.

**Dilution overhang:** $120M total at a **$4.63** conversion price (20% premium to the pre-agreement 5-day VWAP), converting only after 364 days. Against 1.876bn ordinary shares (20:1 ADS), full conversion ≈ **~28% dilution, ~31% with the warrants**. The genuine bull datum: founder Jinfeng Huang, Trustar and Hillhouse funded at $4.63 with the stock at ~$2.39 on the closing date — insiders committing at a large premium to spot. But that is a *2027* strike, and the second tranche is still only "expected later this year."

**PRE-PRINT POSITION: FLAT.** The marginal-ROI deficit is public and unresolved, the guide decelerates into 618, and a 39%-above-spot convert caps the upside — there is no pre-print asymmetry worth owning, and the print itself is the cheap resolver.
