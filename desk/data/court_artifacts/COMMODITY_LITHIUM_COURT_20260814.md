## 1. SUPPLY CURVE — **UNDER-VERIFIED, treat as directional only**

I could not obtain a segment-by-segment cost curve with switch-on prices. **No cost-curve number
below should be traded on.** What I *can* verify is the more important thing — the causal history:

**The 2025-26 move was an ADMINISTRATIVE supply shock, not a structural shortage.** Verified chain:

1. Aug-2025 — CATL's Jianxiawo lepidolite mine (Jiangxi, one of the world's largest) suspended after
   its **mining licence expired**. Price ~¥82,000/t at that point.
2. Sep-2025 → May-2026 — price rallies to ¥180–200,500 (+~145%).
3. 2026 — **Jianxiawo "cleared security permits to restore activity" following its prolonged
   suspension, and Australian miners reactivated previously suspended projects.**
   (Trading Economics market commentary, 2026-08-14.)
4. Price −24% from the high.

This is the decisive supply fact and it cuts bearish. Supply that was switched off by a **permit** has
near-zero restart cost and reverses the moment the administrative condition clears — unlike
price-driven curtailments, which need capex and time. The market re-rated as if a structural
shortage had arrived; what had actually arrived was a paperwork outage.

**Corroborating supply behaviour from the two majors' own filings (VERIFIED, primary):**

- **SQM (Q1-26, 6-K filed 2026-05-27):** running **"at full capacity"**; **raised** FY volume growth
  guidance from **10% to 15%**; Q1 volumes 69.0kt LCE (+25% YoY); International division spodumene
  SC6 volumes **+108% YoY**; Mt Holland at full capacity; Kwinana refinery ramping; Salar Futuro
  entering environmental permitting.
- **Albemarle (Q2-26, 8-K filed 2026-08-05):** FY26 Energy Storage volumes **225–235kt LCE**,
  "production volumes expected to increase year over year"; **Wodgina "better-than-planned output."**

Both majors are maximising volume into the rally. That is the classic response that caps price.

**The one genuine bullish supply event — and what it tells us:** a **fire at Talison CGP3 on
June 9, 2026** (Greenbushes; the world's best spodumene asset). ALB expects "minimal impact" to
volumes, partly offset by Wodgina. **Note what happened next: the price kept falling.** A real
physical outage at the lowest-cost mine on earth could not lift the market. When bullish supply news
cannot move a price, the marginal buyer is absent. That is my own finding, from ALB's filing against
the price tape, and it is a bearish microstructure tell.

**UNVERIFIED and material:** marginal cash cost of Chinese lepidolite and African spodumene; the
Jianxiawo ramp *rate*; whether Kemerton/Bald Hill/Finniss/Ngungaju specifically restarted; 2026-28
greenfield schedule. **A cost-curve floor is the single input that would most change this verdict**
— if the marginal tonne costs $17-19/kg, then spot at $21/kg is near cost and the asymmetry is
upward. I could not establish it. Flagged as the top open item.

### 4a. THE ONE REAL FINDING — realized price lags spot by one quarter

**A self-correction first.** My initial read was that ALB's own FY frame ($2.4–2.6B) minus H1 actual
($1,521.9M) implied an H2 collapse to $0.88–1.08B — i.e. that ALB was guiding H2 down ~40% at flat
prices. **That reading was wrong and I withdraw it.** Pulling the Q1 release showed the scenario
table is **byte-identical** across Q1 and Q2. ALB published it in early May knowing only Q1
($663.8M), then beat with Q2 ($858.1M) and **left the table unrevised**. It is a stale
price-sensitivity frame carried forward, not a bottoms-up H2 forecast. Consensus anchored on it
would understate — but ALB is not guiding H2 to halve.

**Now the finding.** Two candidate lag models, tested against the two disclosed quarters:

- Q2-26 realized **$19.53/kg** (disclosed); Q1-26 realized **~$17.4/kg** (derived: Q1 ES sales
  $891.2M, price +51% YoY — *this one is my estimate, not disclosed*).
- Spot averages: Q4-25 ≈ $17/kg, Q1-26 ≈ $20/kg (ALB's own label), Q2-26 ≈ $24.5/kg.

| Model | Q1 fit | Q2 fit | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Realized ≈ contemporaneous spot | 87% | 80% | drifting — poor fit |
| **Realized ≈ PRIOR quarter's spot** | **102%** | **98%** | **tight — accepted** |

**ALB's realized price tracks the previous quarter's average spot at near-full pass-through.**

The consequence is the actionable part: **Q3-2026 realized ≈ Q2-2026 average spot ≈ $24/kg**, versus
Q2's $19.53 — roughly **+23% sequentially**, on ~57kt of volume, at Energy Storage's ~57% margin with
much higher incremental margin on price. **ALB's Q3 should print UP sequentially — reporting the May
peak — even though the commodity is 24% below that peak today.** The market is marking the equity to
current spot while the company is about to report peak-quarter economics.

Signal channel: Q2 spot, already public and already observed. Price channel: equity marked to
today's spot. Latency: ~1 quarter, forced to resolution by the Q3 print (~2026-11-04).

**Skeptic's pass, and it lands hard.** (1) ALB is an $18.5B large-cap with heavy coverage; sell-side
models the contract lag routinely. **I did NOT verify consensus, so I cannot claim this is
unpriced** — the mechanism is verified, its *pricing* is UNVERIFIED. (2) The fit is n=2, and one of
the two points is my own estimate rather than a disclosure. (3) Mix (spodumene vs salts) moves
realized price independently of spot. (4) The CGP3 fire may hit Q3 costs. (5) ALB has already
rallied +18% off its July low and +4.25% today — which may mean the market is *already* pricing
this and I am late rather than early. Genuinely ambiguous.

That is why this is a 5/10 finding funded small, not a thesis.

**Note the shape:** Q4-26 realized ≈ Q3-26 spot ≈ $21/kg — *down* from Q3's ~$24. The sequential
peak is Q3. This is a single-print trade with a decaying tail, which disciplines the expiry choice
in Section 5.

## 7. VERDICT

**NO EDGE on lithium price direction. Conviction 3/10 on the commodity.**

Mode B, leading: this desk has no business forecasting the Chinese lithium carbonate price. It is a
liquid, heavily-researched commodity priced on GFEX by physical participants with far better
information on Jiangxi lepidolite economics and provincial permitting than I can reach from public
English-language sources. **Today I could not obtain even the open interest or inventory of the
price-setting venue.** A direction called from that position would be manufactured conviction, which
the brief explicitly and correctly licenses me to decline.

What survives the disconfirming pass, in descending confidence:

1. **The 2025-26 rally was an administrative supply shock, not a structural shortage** (VERIFIED
   chain: licence expiry → +145% → permit cleared → −24%). Extrapolating it is a mistake. This is
   the highest-confidence claim in the document and it is a *negative* one.
2. **A real fire at the world's best spodumene mine could not lift the price.** Bearish tell,
   derived from ALB's filing against the tape.
3. **Both majors are maximising volume into the rally** (SQM at full capacity raising volume guidance
   10%→15%, SC6 +108%; ALB Wodgina above plan). Classic price-capping behaviour.
4. **ALB at $136 implies $17–19/kg vs $21.2/kg spot** — the equity needs lithium only to hold, not
   to rise. Contingent on a 9× multiple; at 11× the claim dies.
5. **Realized price lags spot by one quarter, so ALB's Q3 print is largely pre-determined by tape
   already observed** (Section 4a). Real mechanism, thin evidence (n=2, one point estimated),
   and **whether it is already priced is UNVERIFIED**. 5/10.

**Position: NONE opened by this court.** If the desk wants the exposure, the only defensible
expression is a **small ALB Jan-27 150/200 call spread sized as a starter/lottery** — explicitly
**not** a lithium bull bet but a bet on (4) + (5): a fairly-priced-to-cheap equity with a
pre-determined near-term print. Size it to the 5/10 finding, not to the commodity story. Defined
risk, one leg, no adds before the Q3 print.

**What would change this verdict:** a marginal-cost curve showing the incremental tonne at
$17–19/kg (would convert (4) into a genuine floor-based asymmetry), or GFEX inventory/OI showing
long liquidation rather than new shorts into the June break. **Both are obtainable; neither was
obtainable today.** Build the GFEX connector before this question is asked again.
