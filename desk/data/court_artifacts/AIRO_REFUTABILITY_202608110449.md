## Print-decisive reconstruction (mandatory — print ≤5 trading days)

The loudest bear claim after Q1 was *"backlog won't convert; the FY guide is fiction."* Two pieces of pre-print public data resolve different halves of it, and they point opposite ways.

**Half 1 — revenue timing: partially REFUTED, pre-print.** On 2026-07-16 AIRO announced completion of "a major order of its RQ-35 ISTAR" for a global defense customer, explicitly **completed during the second quarter of 2026**, with the CEO stating vertical integration let them convert the order "on schedule" ([company PR](https://theairogroup.com/airo-completes-major-delivery-of-its-rq-35-istar-uas-highlighting-production-scale/), corroborated by [defence-industry.eu](https://defence-industry.eu/airo-completes-major-rq-35-heidrun-uas-delivery-to-global-defence-customer-strengthening-unmanned-isr-production-capacity/) and [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/airo-completes-unmanned-aircraft-delivery-to-defense-customer-93CH-4795197)). AIRO's fiscal Q2 is calendar Apr–Jun. Units and dollar value were **not disclosed**, so this is directional, not quantitative — but management choosing to press-release an in-quarter delivery four weeks before the print is positive self-selection.

**Half 2 — the guide: arithmetically near-dead, from filings alone.** This is the part the tape has not done.

- *Revenue:* FY2025 revenue $90.91M ([stockanalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/airo/financials/)). FY2026 guide of +15–25% ⇒ $104.5–113.6M. Q1 delivered $8.9M. **Q2–Q4 must therefore average ~$31.9–34.9M/qtr — a ~3.6× step-up off Q1**, versus the ~$26.4M/qtr Q2–Q4 run-rate 2025 actually achieved. Not impossible given 2025 was also back-loaded, but it requires the best three quarters in company history, consecutively.
- *Adjusted EBITDA — the binding constraint:* FY guide is "negative mid- to high-teens," call it −$15M to −$19M. **Q1 alone was −$12.8M. That leaves −$2.2M to −$6.2M for Q2–Q4 combined.** Backing out Q1's ~$4.4M of non-cash addbacks (adj EBITDA −$12.8M vs operating loss −$17.2M) gives ~$15.2M/qtr cash opex, ~$45.6M over three quarters. To land inside the guide, Q2–Q4 gross profit must be ~$39.6–43.6M on ~$96–105M of revenue — i.e. **gross margin must recover from 26.6% back to ~41–42% while revenue triples.** Both, simultaneously, starting this quarter.

So the decisive Aug-13 line is not revenue. Revenue will likely step up — the RQ-35 delivery is already public. The decisive line is **gross margin**, and second, whether the FY adj EBITDA guide is reaffirmed or quietly cut. A revenue beat paired with a sub-35% margin and a reaffirmed guide is management buying one more quarter.

**Balance sheet / dilution clock:** $54.2M cash, $1.2M debt at 3/31. At the Q1 burn rate that is roughly four quarters. Net cash ~$53M against a $268M cap ⇒ EV ~$215M, ~2.0× the *guided* revenue year (~3.4× if you haircut the guide to a flat $90M). The "buyback" in some headlines is not an ongoing program — it was a Sept-2025 secondary-linked repurchase of 815k shares from selling holders ([StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/AIRO/airo-announces-launch-of-public-offering-of-common-scwogxluwzu3.html)); do not read it as a live support bid.
