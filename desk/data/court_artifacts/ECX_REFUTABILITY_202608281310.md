## ECX — refutability triage

ECX's −60.4% dd52 is not a dislocation event: it is a 10-month de-rate off a $2.65–2.70 high set in **early November 2025**, kicked off by a Q3'25 revenue miss ($219.9M vs $271.7M consensus) and cemented by the **going-concern note in the FY2025 20-F** (filed 2026-03-30). The tape confirms no event — ECX closed $1.04 on its 2026-08-11 print day (−0.95%) and has been range-bound $1.00–$1.14 for four weeks, 22% *above* its 52-week low. Two narratives are separable here and they classify differently, so I name both.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):**
None. ECX does not qualify. The only damage-absent leg (gross profit dollars vs. the memory narrative) is real and cleanly evidenced, but it is not the leg setting the price — the price is set by an auditor-flagged going-concern condition that no adversarial court can resolve from public filings, because the decisive variable is whether Chinese bank facilities and Geely-affiliated related-party support get rolled. A court cannot observe that; it can only restate the risk.

**Guide-to-guide math (the one thing a court *could* have argued, pre-computed so it needn't):** H1'26 revenue $356.7M vs H1'25 $323.3M = **+10.3% YoY** at the half, against a reaffirmed **$1.0–1.1B** full-year guide (FY25 ≈ $847.9M, derived: H1'25 $323.3M + Q3'25 $219.9M + Q4'25 $304.7M from the $881.3M TTM). The low end needs H2'26 = **$643M (+80% H/H, +22.6% YoY)**; the high end needs $743M (+41.7% YoY). Against the easy Q3'25 comp the low end is reachable — Q2'26 already ran +45% YoY — but the top of the range is not credible on H1 run-rate. **Q3'26 tripwire: revenue below ~$270M (+23% YoY) breaks the $1.0B floor**; adjusted EBITDA turning back negative kills the four-quarter positive-EBITDA bull leg outright.

**PRINT PROXIMITY: 2026-11-03 — UNCONFIRMED (yfinance-derived; no company PR names it, and the 2026-07-28 scheduling PR named only the Aug 11 Q2 date). ~46 trading days out; the decisive print already landed 2026-08-11.** No pre-print reconstruction is owed under the 5-day rule. Pre-print position, stated anyway since the tripwire is now armed: **FLAT** — the named kill is the going-concern note plus $444.8M of short-term borrowings against $156.2M of cash that is $117.8M spoken-for, while the company simultaneously funds a $266M acquisition from a negative-equity balance sheet; converts struck at $2.62 are 144% out of the money, so they are debt maturing 2028, not equity relief.

**Two honesty flags.** (a) SEC.gov 403'd my fetcher on every direct attempt; the Q1/Q2 figures above were read from the company's own PR Newswire and PR Asia distributions of the identical exhibit 99.1, which I treat as equivalent — but the **going-concern language and the $339.8M net-current-liability figure were read from a secondary summary of the 20-F, not the 20-F itself.** That is the load-bearing fact in this triage and it is VERIFICATION-OWED against the primary before any sizing action. (b) **H1'26 operating cash flow is the single most decisive missing number** and it is not in the earnings release; the 20-F states three consecutive years of negative OCF, and whether that broke in H1'26 is what separates "inflecting" from "structurally impaired." Route both to the unverified ledger.

**COURT-WORTHINESS ECX: 3/10** — the discount has a named, auditor-attested cause a court cannot adjudicate (bank rollover), and the dd52 trigger is a stale-window artifact off a Nov-2025 high with no dispersion event to arbitrage.
