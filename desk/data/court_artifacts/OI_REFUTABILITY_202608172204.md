2. **The credit market's read, which currently contradicts the equity's.** In May 2026 O-I refinanced $612M of 6.625% 2027 notes with **$500M of 9.500% senior notes due 2033, priced at par** ([filing coverage](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/OI/8-k-o-i-glass-inc-de-reports-material-event-120b508f9d4f.html)).
