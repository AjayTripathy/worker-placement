## KILL #9 — the SEC comment letter. OVERTURNED as an honesty catch.

**Red's authority is real and correctly quoted.** monday's 2025-10-09 response, signed Glazer, does
say it does "not disclose or internally use GAAP revenue by product," that "no other discrete
financial information… is available on a disaggregated basis," that its systems "were not designed
to… report operating margin at the product level," and that CRM + dev + service "combined
constituted approximately **7.7%** of the Company's total ARR" **as of December 31, 2024**.
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891325003480/filename1.htm

**Attack 1 — the comparison is not time-consistent, and the gap closes.** The SEC's own comment #4
dates the other number: "a recent announcement in your **Q2 2025 shareholder letter** that the CRM
product reached $100 million in ARR." The Q2-25 release is dated **2025-08-11**
(https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891325002734/zk2533548.htm): "monday CRM
recently reached $100 million in ARR, just three years since launch." So red set a **12/31/24 stock**
against an **8/11/25 stock** — 7.4 months apart — and called the difference a divergence.

**Attack 2 — the company published the reconciling checkpoint itself.** Q3-25 release, 2025-11-10,
headline: "**New products now account for more than 10% of total ARR**," body: "New products (monday
CRM, monday dev, monday service, monday campaigns) now account for more than 10% of total ARR."
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891325003708/zk2533950.htm

That is the *same metric family* red says was "never given to investors," disclosed to investors one
quarter after the regulator letter, at a **higher** value. It also anchors the denominator, which
red says is undisclosed: Q4-24 release, Glazer quote, "surpassing **$1 billion** in annual recurring
revenue (ARR)"
(https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000117891325000369/zk2532674.htm). Total ARR tracks
4× quarterly revenue closely (4 × $268.0M = $1.072B vs disclosed ">$1B").

**The reconciliation, all three points, one basis:**

| Date | Basket | Total ARR (≈4× qtr rev) | Basket $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12/31/24 | CRM+dev+service = **7.7%** | ~$1.07B | ~$82M |
| 8/11/25 | CRM alone = **$100M** | ~$1.20B | (interp. basket ~$119M → CRM = 84%) |
| 9/30/25 | CRM+dev+service+campaigns = **>10%** | ~$1.27B | **>$127M** |

Basket $82M → >$127M in nine months = **+55%, ~78% annualized**. Back-solving CRM at 84% of the
basket puts CRM at ~$69M on 12/31/24 → $100M by 8/11/25 = ~80% annualized. **Internally consistent
at every point.** Red's headline gap — "$83M for all three vs $100M for CRM alone" — is an artifact
of ignoring 7.4 months of compounding, and the company's own intermediate disclosure closes it.

**Attack 3 — the accounting position was regulator-tested and accepted.** The single-segment
conclusion under ASC 280-10-50-1 and the no-disaggregation conclusion under ASC 280-10-50-40 are the
standard positions for a single-platform company where the CODMs receive only consolidated non-GAAP
operating income. The SEC **completed its review with no further comment, no restatement, and no
required change to the segment note**, 2025-12-18: "We have completed our review of your filing."
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1845338/000000000025011685/filename1.pdf

**Attack 4 — "unfalsifiable by construction" is contradicted by the record.** The claim is
falsifiable, and the company published the falsifying number itself. It also *retired* the framing:

**What survives.** Two things, and I will not paper over them. (i) "New products % of total ARR"

2024. It is a restatement of the ~2pp/quarter glide the EC already prices, not independent evidence.
