### Print-decisive reconstruction — pre-print public data resolving the loudest bear claim

**Loudest bear claim:** Fair Financing's longer duration forces upfront IFRS-9 lifetime provisioning, so credit costs outrun the revenue they fund; the Q4-25 print (provisions +102% YoY, −27% stock day, $14.36 low) is the template.

**The whole print reduces to one number, and the threshold is computable from filings already public:**

1. **Derive the Q2-25 comp.** Klarna Bank AB reports H1-25 credit losses at **0.57% of GMV**; Q1-25 was **0.54%**; management states Q1 ≈ 20% and Q2 ≈ 23% of full-year volume. Backing Q1 out of H1 at those weights puts **Q2-2025 ≈ 0.60% of GMV**. Q2 is structurally the *worse* credit quarter — Q1 carries post-holiday collections and tax-refund paydown, which management explicitly called "seasonally stronger collection performance."
2. **The threshold.** On the Q2-26 GMV guide of $35.5–36.5bn: **≤60bps (≤$216m) = narrative refuted on the tape** — the mix drag is absorbed at a flat-to-better YoY rate with Fair Financing now 12%+ of GMV. **>65bps (>$234m) = DAMAGE-ARRIVING**, reclassify.
3. **Why 5bps decides the quarter.** 5bps of $36bn GMV = **$18m**, against an adj-operating-income guide of **$30–50m**. A single 5bp credit slip consumes roughly half the guided profit range. This is a credit-rate print, not a revenue print.
4. **What already de-risks it, pre-print.** The 2026-07-16 SRT is a *repeat* buyer — a sophisticated credit investor with data-room access re-underwrote the book in mid-July, on Q2 performance data, at size ($12bn of lending capacity). CFO Neglén's framing ("vote of confidence in the quality of our underwriting") is promotional, but the transaction clearing at all is not. Paired with the €900m Germany capital-efficiency platform (Jul 9), the balance sheet is moving *capital-lighter* into the print.
5. **The revenue-guide scare is mechanical, not deterioration.** Q2 guide of $960m–$1,000m on $35.5–36.5bn GMV implies a **~2.72% take rate vs 3.00% in Q1** — a 28bp sequential drop that looks alarming. FX explains only ~3bps (USD/EUR averaged 0.8607 in Q2, ~1.4% stronger QoQ, on a ~2/3 non-US revenue base). **The rest is a denominator artifact** *(my derivation, not company-stated)*: Fair Financing interest income accrues on the *book*, which peaks post-holiday in Q1, while GMV is seasonally lowest in Q1 (20% of FY) and steps up in Q2 (23%). Interest-income-over-GMV is therefore mechanically highest in Q1. H1 blended lands at **2.86%**, still above the reiterated FY floor of >2.8% of GMV — the FY guide was set knowing this shape.
6. **Un-consensused forward optionality lands in the Q3 guide, not Q2.** Apple Upgrade (announced 7/28, Klarna as leasing provider for iPhone/Mac/iPad/Watch on 12–36 month terms; stock +9% on the news) and J.P.
