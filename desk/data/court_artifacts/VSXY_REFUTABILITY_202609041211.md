**The decisive reconstruction (post-print, from primary filings only)**

1. **The raise is smaller than the beat.** FY26 adj OI guide went $550–580M → $560–590M (**+$10M** at midpoint). Q2 beat its own guide by **+$29M** ($124M vs $95M mid). Residual: management **cut implied H2 by ~$19M**. That, not the quarter, is what traded.
2. **Q3 guide is the whole move.** Q3 adj OI guided **$10–20M on $1.570–1.600B** = ~**0.95% operating margin**, versus Q2's realized **7.7%**. YoY incremental margin collapses from ~52% (Q2) to ~13% (Q3 guided, vs Q3-25 adj OI of $0M on $1.472B, [Q3-25 EX-99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1856437/000185643725000044/ex991vscoq32025earningsrel.htm)).
3. **The mechanism is disclosed and dated.** The 10-Q states **new Section 301 tariffs of 10–12.5% took effect 2026-07-24** — Q3 (began 2026-08-02) is the **first full quarter carrying them**. Separately, the $148M IEEPA cash refund ($140M pre-tax: $135M in COGS, $4M interest, $1M G&A) is explicitly non-recurring: *"we did not record a receivable related to any potential future IEEPA tariff refunds."*
4. **The damage the company conceded is 3.3% of FY OI; the tape took 13–18%.** $19M implied H2 cut against a $575M FY adj-OI midpoint cannot arithmetically support the de-rate. Balance sheet corroborates: cash **$522M vs $188M LY**, LTD $971M, $100M repurchased YTD at avg $45.27 (stock $74.43).

**What the print did NOT resolve — and the reason a court is worth running:** management guided only through FY26, i.e. ~5.5 months of Section 301. **FY2027 carries a full year.** If the 10–12.5% duty touches the same narrow sourcing base implied by the $19M H2 number, FY27 run-rate is a ~$40M nuisance and the de-rate is wrong. If H2 is being masked by pre-buy inventory/vendor concessions and the true base is broad, FY27 adj OI takes a $150–250M hole and the de-rate is *correct*. That variable is resolvable from primary sources (10-K country-of-origin sourcing disclosure + import customs data via the existing customs connector) — it is a work-queue item, not a kill.
