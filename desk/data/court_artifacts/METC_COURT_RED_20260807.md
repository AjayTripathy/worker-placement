### B2a. It is a scandium project, and the plan is 122% of world demand

Per the verification pass, **60–68% of the project's contained value is scandium**, and the planned output is **~122% of total global scandium demand**.

This reframes everything. Scandium is not a rare-earth magnet metal — it is a tiny specialty market (global consumption is on the order of tens of tonnes per year). A project whose value is two-thirds scandium and whose output exceeds the entire world's consumption **destroys its own price deck on commissioning**. You cannot sell 122% of world demand at the price used to value the first 1%. Any NPV struck at prevailing scandium prices is therefore not a valuation of this project; it is a valuation of a project one-tenth its size.

It also explains the narrative migration in §B4: management now describes ~75% of anticipated revenue as *"tied to key critical mineral commodities whose main demand driver is the semiconductor industry."* That is the scandium/gallium-type basket — **not** the neodymium-praseodymium-dysprosium-terbium magnet basket the equity was sold on during the 2025 rare-earth mania. The word "rare earths" is doing work the underlying metallurgy does not support.
