**Why STRUCTURAL and not DAMAGE-ARRIVING.** The refutable operating claim (China-EV/tariff demand damage) is *already partly refuted* — revenue compounded sequentially every quarter of 2025 and gross margin went positive (9% FY25). If the discount were an operating-narrative discount, this would be a court-worthy dispersion name. It isn't, because three items sit under the price that the print cannot refute and one of which the print can only worsen:

1. **Floating-strike parent convertible, no disclosed floor.** Per the 6-K in the evidence pack (2026-06-15, primary): Geely International (HK) buys a **US$128,324,684.58** senior note, 364-day, SOFR+3.35%, convertible at the Investor's option from day 30, at a conversion price equal to *"the volume-weighted average of the last reported sale price of the Company's ADSs over the 10 consecutive trading days immediately preceding the applicable conversion date."* Struck at the tape, not at a fixed price. At $1.05 that is ~122M ADS ≈ **23% of the $552M cap**; at $0.70 it is ~183M ADS ≈ 35%. Dilution is *unbounded as the price falls* and the note **ranks senior to all other unsecured debt** (ex the $10M ATW note). This is the single most important fact for sizing and it is reflexive: it converts weakness into more weakness and caps strength by manufacturing shares into any rally at VWAP.
2. **Auditor going-concern doubt on a −$1.33B-equity balance sheet.** $73M cash against $1.84B debt and a 0.38 current ratio. The funding mechanism is visibly *incremental related-party borrowing* — debt grew ~$347M across H2-2025, which is approximately the burn.
3. **Forced absorption of Lotus UK via Geely's put.** LOT expects to acquire 51% of Lotus UK **non-cash, on a pre-agreed pricing method, pursuant to Geely's exercise of a put option** — LOT is the writer, not the buyer. Accounted for as a **common-control** acquisition ([company PR, 2026-06-12](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/12/3311333/0/en/Lotus-Tech-Announces-Operational-and-Earnings-Reporting-Updates.html); [6-K Ex-99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1962746/000110465926073564/tm2617843d1_ex99-1.htm)).

### Print-decisive reconstruction (2026-08-27, H1 2026)

Base: H1 2025 = 2,813 deliveries, revenue **$218.3M**, net loss **$313.0M** ([H1-25 release](https://ir.group-lotus.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lotus-technology-reports-unaudited-second-quarter-and-first-0)).

What public pre-print data actually resolves the loudest claim — and the loudest claim is *solvency*, not revenue:

The only *announced* H1-2026 external funding is the Geely note at **$128.3M**, closing June 2026 — i.e. **below** the half's estimated need. The gap has to appear as more short-term related-party borrowing.
- **Therefore the printable numbers are near-determined:** expect cash at 6/30/26 in the **~$50–100M** band (it has not left that band for three quarters despite $347M of new debt) and total borrowings **~$2.0–2.2B**. The revenue line is the *least* informative thing in the release.

Whether that combination is already reflected in H1-26 is unknown pre-print. A revenue "beat" driven by combination is not a beat.
