## Three findings that change the sizing decision

**1. The valuation anchor is wrong by 2.2x — leverage is not "unchecked," it's decisive.**
Cash $307.3M, LT debt $657.7M, **Series A preferred liquidation preference $506.4M**, stockholders' equity **negative $(202.1)M**. True EV = $722M mcap + $350M net debt + $506M pref ≈ **$1.58B** → **7.2x** FY26 adj-EBITDA mid ($220M). Naive mcap/EBITDA reads 3.3x. The equity is a levered stub behind $856M of senior claims.

**2. The dispersion that fired this event is largely artifact.** The blob tagged ARRY *Technology / Semiconductors* and measured excess_dd −0.282 against the **tech sector median**. ARRY is utility-scale solar tracker/BOS hardware. Against its true peer NXT (dd52 ≈ −48%; spot ~$84.72, secondary source — IBKR refresh denied, but the conclusion is robust to ±10%), ARRY's equity gap is ~−14pp, not −28pp. And on an **enterprise** basis ARRY has de-rated **−43%** vs its 52w high — i.e. *less* than net-cash NXT's equity. **Essentially all of the peer-relative dispersion is capital structure, not un-priced fundamental divergence.**

**3. The FY guide does not reconcile to its own components.** H1 actual $565.5M; Q3 guide $310–330M; mgmt says H2 splits ~60% Q4 / 40% Q3. At the Q3 guide *high*, that implies H2 $825M → FY **$1,390M — below the $1,400M floor**. Implied Q4 at FY-mid is ~$565M, **+150% y/y** vs Q4'25's $226M. It is precedented — CFO cited shipping 4.5GW / >$500M in Q2'23 (pack XBRL: $507.7M) — but conditioned on "if the externalities hold," said one day before the externality changed. AWM does **not** bridge it: ~$60M TTM revenue (~$15M/quarter), **funded with cash on hand** (base $153M → cash falls ~$307M to ~$154M) ([AWM PR](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/16/3328824/0/en/array-technologies-to-acquire-affordable-wire-management-awm-creating-new-growth-platform-in-balance-of-system-solutions.html)).
