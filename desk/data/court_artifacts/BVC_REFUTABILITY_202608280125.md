## What I'd actually route from this

The court routing (score 1 → no escalation) is the small half. The large half is a screen-integrity defect that will keep firing:

1. **`mcap` is computed from a stale share count that no split table will ever correct.** Any issuer that restructures via share consolidation + ADR termination + substitution listing — a common path for Cayman/HK Nasdaq microcaps — will carry a market cap inflated by the consolidation ratio indefinitely. That inflation is what promoted BVC past whatever size floor the class-dislocation screen uses and seeded it as the marquee member of a "Finance" cohort. Suggested guard: cross-check `mcap` against `px × shares_outstanding` from a second source at seed time and reject on >20% divergence; and treat a `dei` share-count discontinuity in the absence of a split record as a hard flag.
2. **Add a float/ADV admissibility gate before the court queue.** A 17% move on 2.2% of a 723K-share float is a spread crossing. Rejecting on notional traded (say, <$1M on the trigger bar) would have killed this seed before it consumed two benches.
3. **`sector: "Finance"` is stale** — inherited from the Sentage/Santech consumer-finance era. The company is now an e-commerce reseller and crypto miner. Cohort assignment on a stale SIC will keep mixing incomparable businesses.
