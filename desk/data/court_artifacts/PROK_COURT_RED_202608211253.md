### STRONGEST SINGLE KILL
The thesis rests on one number — 4.6 mL/min/1.73m²/yr, "3× the FDA bar" — and that number cannot bear the weight, by the company's own arithmetic. Group 1 and Group 2 were randomized 1:1 from the same protocol, yet their *pre-injection* slopes differ by 2.4 while their *post-injection* slopes differ by 0.4. Randomization guarantees the first gap should be zero; a genuine dose response would put the separation in the second. The observed pattern — wildly divergent pre-periods converging to a common post-period around −1.3/−1.7 — is what regression-to-the-mean plus trial-entry standard-of-care intensification produces in *any* CKD cohort selected on progressive decline, drug or no drug. PROACT 1 measures the one thing REGEN-007 never estimated: a **sham** arm that gets the same regression and the same SoC intensification. The FDA's own operative wording pre-loads that risk ("versus sham controls… receiving appropriate standard of care therapies"). So the brief's "clinical damage vector is ABSENT" is not a finding — it is an absence of measurement, and it is the half the brief called courtable.
