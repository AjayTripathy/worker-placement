**Three findings that make this STRUCTURAL, not a dislocation:**

1. **The market is paying for an uncapped call on a capped instrument.** Warrant terms ([Warrant Assignment Agreement, 4/10/25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1866364/000121390025031269/ea023813201ex2-1_webull.htm); [ex2-6](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1866364/000121390025035656/ea023569801ex2-6_webull.htm)): redeemable at **$0.01** if BULL closes ≥**$18.00** for 20 of any 30 trading days. That truncates the payoff to a $11.50/$18.00 call spread. At S=$7.32, T=3.68y, r=4%, that spread is worth **$0.79 (σ=40%) / $0.84 (σ=58%) / $0.72 (σ=83%) / $0.46 (σ=120%)** — it **never exceeds ~$0.85 at any volatility**. Yet BULLW at $2.50 is almost exactly the *uncapped* Black-Scholes value at ~58% vol. The entire ~$1.66 premium is an implicit assumption that Webull will never call. **Webull already called its other warrant class — the $10-strike Incentive Warrants — at the first opportunity, redemption dated 2025-06-30** ([company PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/webull-announces-redemption-of-incentive-warrants-302468341.html)). The behavioral precedent refutes the assumption the price depends on.
2. **The screen's two inputs are both artifacts.** `mcap 1,292,319,356` is warrant price × the *common's* share count; actual BULLW market value is **9,675,384 warrants × $2.50 ≈ $24M** ([20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001866364/000121390026041653/ea0283691-20f_webull.htm), no private warrants remain). And `dd52 −0.65` is measured off a spike that has rolled off: BULLW's 26-week high is **$2.66** and it trades **$2.50 — 94% of its 6-month high and +110% off its 52-week low** ([stockanalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/bullw/)). It is not currently dislocating; it has doubled.
3. **Uninvestable regardless.** Volume 10,778 warrants/day ≈ **$27k/day notional**. No sizing decision of ours can be expressed here in either direction.

The genuinely damage-absent facts belong to the **common, not the warrant**: the Yorkville $1.0B standby equity facility — the "dilution overhang" — was **terminated effective 2026-04-06** with only 11.5M shares / $173.2M ever drawn ([company PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/webull-announces-termination-of-standby-equity-purchase-agreement-302734834.html)), and engagement is accelerating, not rolling over.
