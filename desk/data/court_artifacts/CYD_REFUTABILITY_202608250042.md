BUT a cohort-decisive read-across print lands in 3 sessions: Weichai Power (2338.HK/000338.SZ) board meets Thu 2026-08-27 to approve 1H26 interim results.** Weichai is the direct genset comp and discloses data-center engine units (prior period: ~1,400 units, +259%). Treating CYD's 6-month vacuum as "no catalyst" would be the exact error the mandatory section exists to prevent.

**Print-decisive reconstruction (what pre-print public data already resolves the loudest bear claim):**

The loudest bear claim is "data-center engine demand is peaking." It is **already untestable via CYD's FY26 units**, and the filings say why: management disclosed capacity allocation of **~3,500 units to AI data centers and ~1,500 to other power gen out of 5,000 total** — i.e. the 3,500 guide *is the capacity number*. CYD is selling everything it can build. That cuts both ways and is the crux a court must adjudicate: FY26 data-center revenue is effectively de-risked/locked, but FY26 units carry zero information about demand. The genuinely refutable variable is therefore **price/margin, not volume** — which is precisely where management already conceded pressure ("all other engine manufacturers have the capacity expansion program," "suppliers offering discounts to win orders"). Weichai's Aug 27 print is the near-term read on that margin leg, and CYD has *already* disclosed the bad news, so a Weichai margin wobble is largely in the price while a Weichai unit/guide raise is an un-priced upside surprise. Asymmetry favors the long, modestly.

**PRE-PRINT POSITION: STARTER.** No named kill survives — both candidate kills are disconfirmed and the guide went up, so doctrine's clean-court minimum-starter default applies rather than FLAT; but size stays small because the bear leg that *does* survive is structural, not cyclical (see below), the company goes dark for six months, and this is a thin high-beta ADR in an actively de-risking complex. Second tranche conditioned on Weichai's Aug 27 data-center disclosure and on the court resolving profit mix.

**The live bear leg a court must kill or confirm (not resolved here):** CYD trades at **~1.03x book, a 20-year valuation high**, versus the ~0.3x book it held for two decades under the Hong Leong Asia golden share (6 of 11 board appointments), the holdco structure, and the Yulin government's ~24.3% stake. The −37% from the 52wk high may be that structural discount reasserting after an AI-narrative excursion, not a dislocation — and if so there is far more downside than the valuation optics suggest. The counter is that the 0.3x-book era was RMB 300–500m annual profit; 1H26 alone did RMB 841.8m. Book multiple alone does not settle it.
