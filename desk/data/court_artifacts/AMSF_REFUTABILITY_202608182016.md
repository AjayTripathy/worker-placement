### The decisive check the tape is getting wrong in both directions

Reported combined = loss + underwriting expense + policyholder dividend. Adding development back:

| | Q2'25 | Q2'26 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reported combined | 91.7% | 95.4% | **+3.7 pts** |
| Favorable dev (pts of NPE) | 12.4 | 9.45 | −2.9 |
| **Ex-development combined** | **104.1%** | **104.9%** | **+0.8 pts** |
| Current accident-year loss pick | ~71.0% | **72.0%** (mgmt-confirmed on the call) | +1.0 pt |

**AMSF's current accident year has always run above 100 ex-development** — booking at ~71–72% and releasing later *is* the business model, and that model did **not** break this quarter: underlying combined moved 0.8 pts. So "underwriting is collapsing" is refuted. What is confirmed is the thing that actually matters to this holder base: **the release engine is throttling down**, and the special-dividend ladder proves it — **$3.50 (2023) → $3.00 (2024) → $1.00 (2025)** ([Oct-2025 special-dividend 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018979/000119312525256323/d940077dex992.htm)). A −44% drawdown is the market repricing a ~$4.90 total-dividend name to a ~$1.64 total-dividend name. That is arithmetic, not dislocation.

**Why this stays out of DAMAGE-ABSENT:** frequency is up ("returning closer to 2023 levels"), average severity up at six months, management calls 2027 "a generally soft rate environment," and CRO Gagliano: *"Competition definitely remains intense."* ([Q2'26 call transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-amerisafe-q2-2026-eps-miss-weighs-on-shares-93CH-4806519))
