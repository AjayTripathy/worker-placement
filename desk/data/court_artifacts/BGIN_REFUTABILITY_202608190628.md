The narrative isn't merely true of this business model, it's **geared**: BGIN is levered to hashprice twice — once as a self-miner (24,924 rigs deployed, up from 13,241) and again as a vendor whose customers only buy when rigs pay back. The FY25 inventory provision of US$59.3M and US$42.6M PP&E impairment are that gearing crystallizing.

**COURT-WORTHY (damage-absent, ranked):** *none.* No member qualifies. BGIN fails the damage-absent test on its own audited numbers, and it also fails the cheapness test that would make a court worth running: US$217M market cap (113.1M A+B shares × $1.92) against US$54.5M book and US$48.8M of gross cash+crypto — i.e. ~4× book and ~4.5× liquid assets, with negative gross margin. This is not a discount to NAV; it is a premium to a shrinking one. Runway is the live question management asserts is fine ($26.3M cash "sufficient"), but 2025 consumed ~US$88.5M of net cash *including* the US$30M IPO — and as an FPI inside 12 months of listing BGIN is not F-3 eligible until ~Oct 2026, so any raise is an F-1 follow-on or PIPE, both 6-K events. No such filing exists; nothing at all has been filed since 2026-06-09.

Two disconfirming facts I did chase, both real but non-decisive: 4nm BT1 achieved first-pass silicon (2026-03-17) and the first ASIC-customization project shipped 3,000 machines ([6-K, 2026-05-26](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/BGIN/6-k-bgin-blockchain-ltd-current-report-foreign-issuer-8d4c47e5e054.html)). A next-gen chip into a market where incumbents' rigs are below breakeven is an option, not a floor — and it consumes the cash that's in question.

**COURT-WORTHINESS BGIN: 2/10** — the discount is the cohort's, not an outlier's, and the sizing decision (zero) doesn't move whichever way a red/blue bench argues.
