3. **What single disclosure would settle the thesis, and does it exist?** GitLab Duo Agent Platform / consumption **revenue**. It is **disclosed nowhere** — not in the 10-K, the 10-Q, either earnings release, or the investor slides. The only quantification that exists anywhere is an internal, admittedly-evolving metric (CRR) that the company states in an SEC filing is **"not material to the Company's financial performance at this time."** That is the kill: the thing that must be true to justify 4.33x is the one thing the issuer will not put a number on.

4. **Is GitLab self-referentially short its own billing base?** Partly, and in its own words. A company acting on the belief that agents replace ~14% of its own knowledge workforce is asserting a mechanism that applies directly to its customers' developer headcount. The letter's own counter-argument (demand for software expands; $/user going "to thousands") is a *conviction*, not a disclosure — and it is precisely what the un-disclosed Duo revenue would test.

## WHAT REFUTES THE BEAR — stated in full, because the court needs it

A red bench that suppresses these is not adversarial, it is advocacy. Four facts materially damage the "structurally displaced, correctly derating" thesis:

1. **The FY27 revenue guide was RAISED, not cut.** $1,099–1,118M (2026-03-03) → **$1,112–1,118M** (2026-06-02); the low end came up $13M. There is no guide-down.
2. **The FY27 non-GAAP operating income guide was also RAISED.** $129–137M → **$135–141M**. The ~460bps of margin compression was fully disclosed in the *original* March guide and has since **improved**. It is deliberate, pre-announced reinvestment — not deterioration discovered mid-year.
3. **Q1 FY27 beat its own guide by 3.6–4.4%** ($264.2M actual vs $253–255M guided). Applying the same beat to Q2 puts the print near **$284M ≈ +20.4% y/y**, not the +15.7% the guide implies. The guide is demonstrably conservative, which materially undercuts reading the guided deceleration as the true trajectory.
4. **cRPO +24% y/y, stable across two consecutive quarters, running ABOVE reported revenue growth (+23%) and far above the FY27 guide (+17%).** This is the single most damaging fact for a structural-collapse thesis: contracted near-term backlog is not decaying. (Counter: it is flattered by the duration compression in B8 — but the level is not in dispute.)

Also favouring the long: SBC is falling in **absolute dollars** ($55.8M → $50.1M, −10.3% y/y) and from 26.0% → **19.0%** of revenue; net cash is $1.36B with zero debt; the buyback executed at $21.04 against $36.62 today; >$1M customers +26%.

**And the market is not asleep.** Underlying implied vol **79.0% annualised, 81st percentile of the last 52 weeks**; the Sep-18 $37.50 straddle marks **23.8%** (42-day implied move), with **$0.72 of genuine put skew** after adjusting for intrinsic. There is no complacency premium to harvest here.
