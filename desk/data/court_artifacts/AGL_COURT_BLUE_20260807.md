### 2.4 The migration path is demonstrated, not hypothetical

1. **MSSP is permanent statute** (Social Security Act §1899), not a demonstration model. It does not

2. **agilon's MSSP ACOs elected the Enhanced Track** — 10-K: *"Our MSSP ACOs selected the Enhanced
   Track, in which the ACO assumes partial accountability for the total cost of care… (risk sharing
   is set at 75%)."* That is real two-sided risk, not token shared savings.
3. They executed the migration **three times, a full year before the deadline**, in direct response to
