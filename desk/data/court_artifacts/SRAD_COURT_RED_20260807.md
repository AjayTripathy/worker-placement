# PART I — MODE B: FIRST PRINCIPLES (leading with B, per doctrine)

I derived the decisive questions before reading either the triage's framing or the promoters'. Two of the three killed the thesis on their own.

### B-1. "Beat + raise" — is the raise real?
It is not a raise. **It is a cut, and a large one.** Both guides are in the same two filings, four weeks apart:

**This answers priority (4) completely.** The stock did not fall 15% on a beat. Note also the new FCF carve-out: litigation costs are now material enough to require exclusion from a guidance metric. That is a disclosure, not an accident.

### B-3. Is SRAD actually "net cash"? — **NO. This is the strongest kill.**
Our hard rule: pull cap structure before asserting net cash, EV, or a multiple. Doing so destroys the "~8x forward EBITDA" that made this look like a deep-value setup.

From the audited FY2025 20-F, **Note 22, Trade and other payables** (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1836470/000110465926035485/srad-20251231x20f.htm):

| in €'000 | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|---|---|---|
| License fee payables for capitalized sport rights licenses — **current** | **319,362** | 178,296 |
| License fee payables for capitalized sport rights licenses — **non-current** | **1,209,876** | 895,679 |
| **Total sport-rights license fee payables** | **€1,529.2M** | **€1,074.0M** (+42% YoY) |

Against which: **Loans and borrowings €11.0M current + €51.8M non-current = €62.9M** (substantially IFRS-16 lease liabilities — the 20-F states ROU lease liabilities are presented within loans and borrowings).

These payables are **interest-bearing**. Note 10, Finance costs:

| in €'000 | 2025 | 2024 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accrued interest on license fee payables** | **80,593** | **71,892** | **27,369** |
| Interest on loans and borrowings | 5,693 | 5,280 | 3,745 |
| **Total finance costs** | **86,531** | 78,870 | 33,731 |

**Accrued interest on sport-rights licenses is 93.1% of FY2025 total finance costs** (80,593 / 86,531) and grew **+194% in two years**. The 20-F states plainly: *"Finance costs consist primarily of interest expense on sport rights license payables fees."*

Yet the Q2-2026 release states the company had **"total liquidity of €501 million as of June 30, 2026 … and no debt outstanding."**

At H1-2026 the obligation is still ~**€1.45BN** (non-current trade payables €1,111.5M — 100% license fee payables on the FY25 presentation — plus ~€335M current, applying the FY25 74.8% license share of current trade payables). Cash €251.1M, loans/borrowings €61.0M.

**"No debt outstanding" is technically true and substantively false.** A €1.45BN discounted, interest-bearing, contractually committed obligation sits inside *trade payables*. Every screen that computes net debt as (loans and borrowings − cash) reports SRAD as **net cash ~€190M**. That is exactly how our triage got there.

**Valuation recomputed** (shares: 221,390,294 Class A + 903,670,701 Class B at 1/10 economic = 90.37M Class-A-equivalent → **~311.8M** as of 2026-03-12, less ~16M repurchased through 2026-07-31 → **~296M** Class-A-equivalent; buyback progress per Q1/Q2 releases: 12.5M/$228M at Apr 24 → 26M/$422M at Jul 31):

| Frame | EV | ÷ FY26 adj. EBITDA (€364M mid) | Multiple |
|---|---|---|---|
| **As triaged** (conventional net cash) | €3.07BN | €364M | **8.4x** ← reproduces our "~8x" |
| **Rights payables treated as debt** | €4.52BN | €364M | **12.4x** |
| **Rights payables as debt AND rights interest expensed** | €4.52BN | ~€276M | **16.4x** |
| **Cross-check: equity / FCF** (FY26 FCF ≈ 56% × €364M ≈ €204M) | €3.26BN equity | €204M | **16.0x P/FCF** |

Market cap at $12.70 × ~296M = **$3.76BN = €3.26BN** at 1.1519.

**Two methodologically independent routes converge on ~16x.** The honest number is **~12–16x, not 8x.** For a 19–21% cc grower ~16x FCF is *fair* — it is not expensive. But **the margin of safety that justified an 8/10 DAMAGE-ABSENT score does not exist.** The cheapness was an artifact of the balance-sheet classification.

FY2025 free cash flow was **€167.2M** against adj. EBITDA of €296.8M — **56% conversion**. The cash statement does not lie about the discount unwind; adjusted EBITDA does.

# PART IV — PRIORITY (3): CHANNEL-INDIFFERENCE, TESTED HONESTLY

The triage's channel-indifference claim is **real but narrower than argued**.

**What survives:** SRAD is paid on rights and product fees, not on book P&L. US revenue grew +9.9% in H1 while US OSB handle contracted −3.9% — a ~14pt wedge, verified on the revenue leg from the primary release. Managed Betting Services, the turnover-sensitive line, was **flat (−1% H1, 0% Q2)** — precisely the line that *should* track handle, and it did. Kalshi and Polymarket are signed. If books consolidate, SRAD's rights are the scarce asset; if exchanges expand, SRAD sells them the same feed. **SRAD does get paid on either branch.**

**What does not survive:**
1. **The +27% is substantially inorganic.** SRAD's own words: *"a 27% increase in Betting & Gaming Content reflecting contributions related to the acquisition of IMG ARENA."* Using it as evidence of organic channel-indifference is a category error.
2. **The company itself flags the channel that is damaging it**: *"Revenue growth was partially offset by moderating U.S. market growth."* US Q1 was **+3.9%**; the H1 +9.9% is carried by Q2's +16%, which itself carries IMG ARENA.
3. **IMG ARENA integration is a live cost, not a clean bolt-on.** Restructuring costs **€10.7M in Q2 alone / €11.8M H1** (vs €0 in Q2-25), explicitly *"associated with the integration of the acquired IMG business, including employee separations."*
4. **Rights-cost step-ups are the structural squeeze, and they are visible.** Sport-rights license payables grew **+42% YoY** (€1,074M → €1,529M) — that is the renewal calendar capitalising onto the balance sheet in real time, with the **MLB renewal** named in the FY25 cash-flow discussion and the **NBA** eight-year agreement (signed 2021-11-16, running through the 2030-31 season) carrying warrants for **3.00% of fully diluted Class A shares** at $0.01. Accrued interest on those obligations rose from €27.4M (2023) to €80.6M (2025). **SRAD's largest cost is fixed, rising and contractual; its customers' revenue is falling.** MW's ex-employees claim top-tier league deals are loss-leaders — unverifiable, but the direction of the payable is not.

**Net:** channel-indifference is a genuine structural insulation from the *handle* channel. It is not insulation from the *rights-cost* channel, and the rights-cost channel is where the margin guide just broke.

# STRONGEST KILL

**The triage bought a mispricing that does not exist, on two facts that are both wrong.**

The 8/10 DAMAGE-ABSENT score rested on: *"net cash, ~8x forward EBITDA, fell 15% on a beat with a raised guide."* In fact —

- The guide was **cut**: adjusted EBITDA €390–400M → €360–368M (−8%), margin expansion 200–225bps → 70–100bps, fourteen weeks after being reiterated. **The 15% drop was a rational repricing of a guide-down, not a substitution-panic overreaction.** There is no dislocation to buy.
- The "net cash / 8x" is a **balance-sheet classification artifact**. SRAD carries ~€1.45BN of discounted, interest-bearing sport-rights license payables inside *trade payables* — throwing off €80.6M of annual interest, 93.1% of all finance costs, up 194% in two years — while telling investors it has **"no debt outstanding."** Load them properly and 8.4x becomes **12.4x–16.4x**, corroborated independently at **16.0x P/FCF**. Fair for the growth. Not a deep-value setup.

And the honesty overlay is what makes it a REJECT rather than a re-rate: **SRAD deleted, from the FY2025 20-F filed 2026-03-27, the standalone risk factor it had carried in FY2022, FY2023 and FY2024 — "A significant amount of our revenue is indirectly derived from jurisdictions where we or our clients are not required to hold a license…" — its only quantitative admission of grey-market dependence.** Four weeks later, under short attack, its rebuttal told investors its "risk disclosures with the SEC **have emphasized**" exactly that fact and invited them to read the annual report. The underlying activity was not shown to have stopped; only the admission stopped. The IR justification — that markets moved "toward regulation **or prohibition**" — is internally incoherent, because prohibition increases legal exposure to that revenue.

That is a **takeaway-versus-data divergence**, which is precisely the pattern this desk exists to exclude — distinct from, and independent of, whether Muddy Waters or Callisto proved any specific customer relationship. A genuinely impaired business that discloses its impairment is CLEAN. SRAD carried the disclosure for three years, removed it in the year it came under scrutiny, and then cited it as if it were still there.

# CONVICTION: 8/10 — **RECOMMEND REJECT**

Held back from 9–10 by three honest concessions to the long:
1. **Muddy Waters covered on publication by its own disclosure.** The most prominent bear does not hold the position.
2. **No regulator has acted.** The entire deplatforming scenario — the only path from "grey-market revenue exists" to "20–40% of revenue disappears" — requires a regulator to compel it. None has, in 3½ months, and litigation costs *fell*. Grey-market revenue is discounted-value, not zero-value.
3. **The wedge is real and it survived this court.** US revenue +9.9% against −3.9% handle, with the turnover-sensitive line flat, is a genuine and useful microstructure finding. SRAD is measurably decoupled from handle, and it is paid whether books or exchanges win. **That finding should be preserved in the knowledge graph even though the trade is rejected** — it is the correct discriminator between a feed vendor and a book, and it will be reusable.

But none of that is the question. The question is whether a −60.6% drawdown is a mispricing. It is not: **~19% of the drawdown was two documented short reports whose central factual concessions the company declined to contest; the largest single remaining leg was an 8% guide-down that our record had inverted into a raise; and the "8x net-cash" cheapness that made the whole thing look asymmetric is an artifact of €1.45BN of interest-bearing obligations booked as trade payables.** Re-rate the multiple honestly and the asymmetry is gone before the governance question is even reached.

**REJECT.** Do not advance to blue.

**Recommended disposition:** move SRAD to a **dated WATCH** keyed to the Q3-2026 print (2026-11-05) and the FY2026 20-F disclosure test (2027-03). It becomes interesting again only if H2 margin delivers *and* the deleted disclosure returns quantified — i.e. if the business proves the guide honest and the filings prove the company honest. Either alone is insufficient.
