Pre-merger shares **244.72M** → pro forma **~371.6M**, Serra Verde holders **34.1%**, pro-forma market cap ~$6.8B at spot.

**The loudest bear claim, and whether public data resolves it:** management projects Serra Verde at **$550–650M annualized EBITDA by end-2027**, explicitly *"on the basis of 100% separated oxide sales"* at Phase 1 nameplate of **~6,400 t TREO/yr**. Serra Verde has been in commercial production since Jan-2024 but sells **MREC (mixed concentrate)**, which realizes a fraction of separated-oxide value. So the number embeds three unbuilt steps: ramp to nameplate, separation capacity, and 100% separated-oxide realization — a management projection inside a merger proxy, the least reliable class of number there is.

**The arithmetic that the bulls have not reconciled, and it is decisive:** 6,400 t = 6.4M kg. A $550–650M EBITDA on that tonnage requires realized blended pricing far above $100/kg — i.e. it is a **heavy-rare-earth (Dy/Tb) price bet at ex-China spot** (~$800/kg Dy, >$4,000/kg Tb). But the offtake that supposedly de-risks it is written to **Pentagon floors of $110/kg NdPr, $575/kg Dy, $2,050/kg Tb** — roughly **half of current ex-China spot** on both heavies — and the SPV takes **100% of Phase I production for 15 years** on take-or-pay. If those floors are *fixed prices* rather than *floors with upside participation*, the offtake caps USAR out of exactly the scarcity regime that makes the EBITDA projection work, and the $300M-over-5-years minimum ($60M/yr) is trivial against the projection. **This single term — participation vs. cap — is the whole thesis, it lives in the Offtake Amendment described in the 8/24 8-K Item 8.01, and I could not retrieve it: SEC.gov Archives returned HTTP 403 to every fetch this session.** Everything above is sourced from the evidence pack's 8-K excerpt plus issuer IR/GlobeNewswire, not from the amendment text.
