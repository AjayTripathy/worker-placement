**Two flags worth writing to the graph beyond this name:**

- **Cohort-membership artifact.** POET is a false positive for the class-dislocation screen: the −27.4% excess drawdown is fully explained by single-name events, so there is no same-event external anchor to price against. This is the "beta-bleed ≠ dislocation" failure mode running in reverse — idiosyncratic collapse masquerading as cohort dispersion. The screen should cause-check excess drawdown against a single-name event log before emitting a triage.
- **New detector candidate: consideration-paid-to-customer capitalized as an asset.** A vendor booking warrants granted *to* a customer as a balance-sheet asset that amortizes against that customer's future revenue is a narrow, high-precision, machine-detectable pattern (IFRS 15 consideration payable to a customer). It converts a headline PO into a materially smaller net number, and it is invisible to any screen reading revenue or backlog alone. Here it nets a $50M PO down to ~$19.9M.

Bottom line: **FLAT stands, and it stands on a named kill** — revenue at 2.7% of opex with the flagship PO obtained by paying the customer 13% of the company — not on a rubber stamp. No position, no order, nothing to size.
