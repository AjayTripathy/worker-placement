**What a court would actually adjudicate** (the sizing-relevant dispute, not the attractiveness):
- **The $13.30 anchor vs. the $10.59 tape.** A chemicals strategic agreed to take ~20% at a 25.6% premium to today's price five weeks ago; the tape sits 6.4% off its 52-week low. Either the market is pricing deal-break/regulatory risk on the Tessenderlo close, or it disagrees with a strategic's terminal value. That is a binary a court can resolve — and the PR discloses only "customary conditions, including regulatory approvals," with **no board-seat, standstill, lock-up or shareholder-approval terms found in the release** (the stock-purchase agreement exhibit is the missing document; sec.gov returned 403 to this run, so the 10-Q and the 6/30 8-K exhibit are **UNREAD** here).

There is no obvious valuation gap to unlock; the entire case rests on 2027 EBITDA direction.
