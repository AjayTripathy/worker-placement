### The arithmetic that makes this interesting

At $20.51 × 57.67M shares = **$1.183B** market cap; net debt ~$1.356B ($1,369M debt vs $12.56M cash, Q2-26) → **EV ~$2.54B**. Against FY26 guidance of $425–435M adj. EBITDA that is **~5.9x EV/EBITDA** (~6.4x pro-forma for the deal), on 5% organic growth with expanding margins. The reaffirmed ≥$100M FY27 FCF is an **~8.5% FCF yield on today's cap**.

The reaction destroyed **$234M** of market cap against **~$205M** of up-front cash — the market marked eCential at *below zero* and charged extra for the re-levering. But the naive "overreaction > deal size" read does not survive contact: capitalizing the company's own ~150bp of 2027 dilution (~$37M on ~$2.45B revenue) at the stock's own 5.9x multiple is ~$218M of EV, so a zero-terminal-value view justifies more than $234M, not less. The market's move is arithmetically defensible. What it is *also* doing is implicitly disbelieving a guidance reaffirmation issued in the same document — and that disbelief is the tradable question.
