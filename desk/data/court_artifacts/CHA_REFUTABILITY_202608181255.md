**PRINT-DECISIVE RECONSTRUCTION (pre-print filings only).** The Q2 print carries **three optical traps, two of which point the wrong way:**

1. **SSSG will "improve" for purely mechanical reasons.** Q1'26's two-year stack is 0.840 × 0.811 = **0.681 (−31.9% vs Q1'24)**. Lapping Q2'25's 0.770 at an unchanged stack yields **−11.5%**. So **−11.5% is the zero-information line**: better than ~−8% = genuine recovery; −9% to −13% = flat on a 2-yr basis, i.e. the Q1 inflection was comp + Qwen voucher; worse than −14% = deterioration *despite* the comp doing the work. (Neutral lines for later quarters: Q3'26 −5.6%, Q4'26 −8.6%.)
2. **GAAP net income will look spectacular and mean nothing.** Q2'25 GAAP NI was RMB77.2M only because RMB552.5M of IPO-linked SBC ran through it. Q1'26 SBC is RMB59.0M. A headline of several-hundred-percent GAAP NI growth is close to arithmetically guaranteed and is pure base artifact.
3. **The honest comp is the hard one.** Q2'25 **non-GAAP** NI was **RMB629.8M** — the highest quarterly figure in the series. Q1'26 non-GAAP was RMB506.7M and falling YoY. CHA must clear RMB629.8M to show real earnings growth, which the −16% same-store base makes unlikely.

The decisive pre-print datum I could **not** resolve from public filings, and which a court must: **how much of the US$1,036.0M sits onshore in RMB versus offshore at the Cayman holdco.** The entire "cash is 53% of market cap" leg depends on it. Second unresolved item: the franchised→company-owned conversion (company-owned revenue **+230.4%**, +599 stores, operating costs +216.6%) — strategic margin capture, or CHA absorbing distressed franchisees and silently converting an asset-light royalty model into a lease-and-capex operator? Franchised revenue fell 12.9% while store count rose 12.7%; those do not sit together comfortably. Third: the **F-3ASR filed 2026-06-05** is a universal shelf expressly permitting *selling shareholders* ([company IR copy](https://investor.chagee.com/static-files/980b3a41-a486-4c47-9d75-b536b1ed42bb)) — a supply signal that directly contradicts the buyback, filed four days after the buyback commenced.

**PRE-PRINT POSITION: FLAT.** Not because the name is unattractive — at ~3.5x EV/trough-earnings it plainly is — but because the single metric that resolves the thesis prints in 7 sessions, the reconstruction converts it into a falsifiable rule (**beat −8% = genuine; −9/−13% = noise; worse than −14% = short the recovery story**), and buying ahead of it means paying for optionality available free in a week.
