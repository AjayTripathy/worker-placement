## What a court has to adjudicate (the honest bear, unlaundered)

- **The 2H cash-flow bridge is the single most falsifiable claim.** 1H26 operating cash flow was **−$5.5M**; the company guides **$70–80M of OCF in 2H26** and year-end leverage falling from 3.2x (6/30) to ~2.5x. That is the whole deleveraging story in one un-earned hockey stick, and it is reconstructible from public filings before the November print.
- **The reduced guide still embeds a Q4 acceleration.** 1H26 actual $355.2M + Q3 guide $190–210M leaves implied Q4 of ~$195–225M vs $193.3M in Q4'25 — i.e. **+8.5% YoY at the midpoint** after Q1 −5.2% and Q2 −20.4%. The low end of the range is clean; the midpoint is not.
- **Forecast credibility is spent.** Guidance of $840–900M was *reiterated* on 2026-05-06 and cut by $115M on 2026-08-05. The Q3/Q4 numbers come from the same apparatus.
- **"Temporary" may not be.** The Clean Air Act §112 presidential exemption mechanism runs **two years and is renewable**; EPA's MATS extension for 47 facilities runs to July 8, 2029, and the authority was invoked seven times in 2025 covering 180+ facilities. Management's word for the $15–25M is "temporary"; the statute permits it to be sticky. This is the strongest form of the bear and a court must price it, not wave at it.
- **Capital allocation already misfired.** ~1.6M shares repurchased for ~$30M in 1H26 (~$18.75 avg) — at 3.2x leverage with negative operating cash flow, now marked at $15.36.
- **Segment margin is where the testing damage is visible:** M&A adjusted EBITDA margin 26.2% vs 29.1%, −290bp on operating deleverage. Revenue held; profitability did not.
- **Event overlay cuts both ways.** Board-led strategic review "including strategic alternatives," announced alongside a limited-duration rights plan (15% trigger, $105 exercise, record date 2026-08-17, expiry 2027-08-04) adopted in response to "significant and undisclosed accumulation of shares and derivative securities." Nomura filed a passive 13G on 2026-08-14 for 2,097,249 shares (6.0%) — that is *not* the accumulator. Short interest is 13.5% of float. A pill adopted by the board that just cut guidance is as easily read as entrenchment as it is as a prelude to a sale.
- **Discovery state is DISCOVERING/CROWDED, not UNDISCOVERED** — NYSE-listed, sell-side covered, activist present, 13.5% short. Per the mispricing-artifact rule the edge is only real against a same-event **external** anchor: the court owes a peer read-across (Clean Harbors / Tetra Tech / NV5 / Veralto) on whether air-permitting waivers and pass-through compression show up in their prints too. I have not established that, and the DAMAGE-ABSENT call is provisional until it is.
