### The load-bearing arithmetic (this is what a court would fight over)

Management's own revision sets a hard, falsifiable bar:

- 1H revised 営業利益 ¥260M − Q1 actual (−¥525.6M) ⟹ **implied Q2 = +¥785.6M**, versus prior-year Q2 of **+¥598.5M** (1H ¥798M − Q1 ¥199.5M). That is **+31% YoY acceleration immediately after a loss quarter.**
- FY guide ¥1,770M − 1H ¥260M ⟹ **2H must deliver ¥1,510M = 85% of full-year profit**, vs prior-year 2H ¥1,647M (−8.3%). Q1 progress against the FY 経常 guide is **−34.4%**.

**The single highest-value unknown, and it is not resolvable by a court:** whether inventory writedowns reverse under 洗替法. If they do, the ¥291M Q1 loss reverses into Q2 and the underlying ask drops to ~¥494.6M — **−17% YoY, i.e. conservative**. If 切放法, the ask is a genuine +31% acceleration. The 短信 does not state the method. This is one accounting-policy note in the FY2026/3 有価証券報告書, not a red/blue deliberation.

### Balance sheet — the finding, and its honest counterweight

In the quarter metals fell and they wrote inventory down, they **grew inventory 57.2%** (¥6,179M → ¥9,713M), funded by burning **46.8% of cash** (¥6,300M → ¥3,354M) and adding ¥608M short-term debt. Inventory is now **116.7% of net assets**; equity ratio fell 53.3% → 44.4%, BPS ¥651.86 → ¥579.13 (−11.2%).

Counterweight the bear case must clear: **前受金 rose ¥1,948M** (¥1,723M → ¥3,671M), matching **55%** of the inventory build. So roughly ¥1,586M is the unmatched directional exposure — materially less alarming than the gross figure. A further −10% in metals on the unmatched slice ≈ −¥159M pre-tax (~1.9% of book), not the −11.7% the headline number implies.

**discovery_state = DISCOVERING/PRICED, not UNDISCOVERED.** No 決算説明会, no 決算補足説明資料, no visible analyst coverage — but the information is a two-page Japanese disclosure that every holder read on Aug 14, and the tape moved −17% over the following five sessions. Absence of coverage is not the edge here; the market processed this.

**Two traps in the vendor data:** the FY2027/3 dividend forecast is **未定 (all dashes in the 短信)** — the 4.2–5.0% yields on Nikkei/stockanalysis are trailing ¥34, not forward. And they paid ~¥489M of dividends in Q1 *against* a ¥555M loss (利益剰余金 −¥1,044M = loss + payout). PBR 1.23x and PER 10.2x both rest on numbers that just moved against them. PFIC: not a concern — active recycling/refining income, fails both PFIC tests comfortably; Japanese withholding 15.315%, treaty-reducible to 10%, moot while the dividend is 未定.
