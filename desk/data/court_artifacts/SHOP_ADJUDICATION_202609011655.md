## Why no band

Taxonomy says a valuation objection gates on price — but a price band exists to pre-authorize
an entry the desk *wants* at that price. SHOP sits on the frozen software axis (worst axis in
every rotation stress); no bench or court has made the intentional-add case; and any −30%
class de-rate re-surfaces it mechanically through the dislocation screens. A standing band
here would be a wish, not an instrument. Re-entry gates on **evidence**: a disclosed dollar or
percentage denominator for AI/agentic GMV — the number that converts the tripled-YoY claim
into something underwritable.
