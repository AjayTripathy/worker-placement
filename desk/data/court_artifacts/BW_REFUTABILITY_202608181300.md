### Why this is RP_TAINTED, not a dislocation

The counterparty structure is the whole case, and it is a related-party ring rather than an arm's-length contract:

- The $2.4B design-build award (full NTP, March 2026) is with **Base Electron**, an IPP **formed in December 2025** — *after* B&W announced the limited notice to proceed in November 2025.
- **B. Riley Financial owns ~19% of BW** and holds a board-nomination right under an April 2019 investor rights agreement. **B. Riley Securities was lead book-running manager on the May 18 $230M raise at $18.50.** BW's CEO Kenneth Young is a B. Riley executive. Wolfpack Research (3/12/26) reports Base Electron shares B. Riley's Santa Monica address with **Bryant Riley as a director**.
- Only **$434M of the $2.4B is fixed-fee**, and Applied Digital's guarantee is reportedly terminable for as little as **$50M**.
- A securities class action is live over alleged omissions on the deal ([InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/regulation-legal-compliance/investors-sue-babcock-wilcox-over-allegedly-inflated-24b-ai-deal/266163)).

That is the same shape the OBDC bench rejected last session: the party whose affiliates sit on both sides of the transaction also supplies the measurement. Under the **RP_TAINTED gate**, BW can never be RP_FAIR while this is live — and unlike OBDC there is no discount to argue about, because there is no valuation support: ~$1.39B EV against a raised FY26 guide of $80–105M **that itself includes Base Electron** is 13–17x on the disputed number and worse ex-it. You are not being paid to hold the litigation.

Two doctrinal cross-checks both fail as well. **Beta-bleed ≠ dislocation:** `discovery_state` here is CROWDED/actively-adjudicating, not UNDISCOVERED — a published short report and multiple class actions mean the market is not ignoring the dispersion, it is pricing it. **Mispricing artifact taxonomy:** an edge is real only against a same-event *external* anchor, and no external anchor exists for Base Electron's economics. The Siemens Energy agreement for 20 STG sets is an anchor on the *supply* side only — it confirms B&W is committing capital to the project, which if anything raises the exposure rather than validating the payer.
