and WorkFusion must fit inside that alongside four full quarters of organic new logos, which cannot
be negative. Red's $61M scenario exceeds the entire pool. Its 4.0× scenario ($47.5M) leaves $8.3M for
a year of organic landing. The DBNR datum red itself tabled bounds its own worst case out of existence.

**(b) The purchase-price allocation looks like an acqui-tech, not a revenue book.** From the 10-Q and

Fair inference, but it is an inference, and purchase accounting is still provisional.

**(c) Red's masking mechanism is REFUTED — this is the part that matters for the knowledge graph.**

[prepared-remarks PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_c1eb38d194b79d3069d92fed730007a8/uipath/db/1195/21722/webcast_transcript/UiPath+-+1Q+2027+-+Earnings+Transcript.pdf),

**Why this overturns rather than merely weakens.** Red's thesis is that agentic substitution shows up
as failure to win new logos and attrition of the tail, and that DBNR is structurally blind to it. Fine
— but then test it on the instrument that *is* sensitive to substitution in the only units that matter:
**dollars**. Gross dollar retention of **97%** means 3% of ARR churns in a year. The tail UiPath lost
across three years is worth roughly **$20.7M of ARR — about 1.7% of FY23 ARR**. In FY26, new-logo ARR
of ~$69.9M replaced ~$50.0M of gross-churned ARR, meaning **each departing logo is replaced by one
landing at ~1.40× its ARR**.

Red graded PATH on a **unit count in a business that does not sell units**. That is the mirror image
of the error red correctly charged the triage with: choosing an instrument blind to the failure mode.
A logo count weights a $5k Community-tier account and a $1M enterprise deployment identically. The
$1M cohort — the accounts that would actually be lost to a Microsoft or an open-source agent framework
— **grew 18% and accelerated.**

**What I concede.** This is *passive* mix, not an announced cull. Nine earnings calls were searched and
there is **no** management statement about deliberately shifting SMB to partners, raising minimum deal
sizes, or cutting the self-serve/Community tier. Small customers are leaving on their own and being
replaced by larger ones; UiPath is not pushing them out. Red's best remaining evidence is real: the
FY26 10-K quietly **deleted** the SMB coverage bullet rather than announcing a change of motion. And no
analyst has ever pressed management on the flat logo line in Q&A, so the divergence has never been
adversarially tested on a call. That is a genuine open flank — but it is a disclosure-quality point,
not the "land motion is dead" structural finding red filed.

**Honest caveats.** Management has never explained the mix — across four calls they never used

Net: red observed something real, mislabelled its cause, its vintage and its direction, and double-counted
