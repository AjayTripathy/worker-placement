**Two defects in the evidence pack, flagged rather than used:**

1. **`XBRL QUARTERLY ocf: 2026-03-31 = -227,953` is the SPAC shell's cash flow, not the operating company's.** CIK 2049662 was Cartesian Growth Corp III until 2026-06-05; a −$228K quarterly OCF is a blank-check entity, irreconcilable with a $19.9M H1 net loss and $14.5M H1 non-GAAP opex. Do not use it for burn math. `sbc` and `dil_sh` are empty for the same reason — there is no comparable prior-period XBRL under this CIK.
2. **`dd52`/`excess_dd` are computed over a 10-week trading history.** Any screen keying off a 52-week window should suppress or re-scale issuers with <6 months of listed history; otherwise every de-SPAC whose debut popped will present as a maximal-dislocation candidate. That is the generalizable fix this event argues for.
