### The narrative splits into three limbs, and they do not agree

**Limb 1 — "wavelength was a fiction": DAMAGE-ABSENT.** Wavelength revenue **$14.8M, +9.2% QoQ, +63.8% YoY**; 1,137 wave-enabled locations; IPv4 leasing **$18.1M, +25% YoY** off 37.8M owned addresses of which only ~15M are leased at a stable $0.40/address/mo. The growth engines the class action says were vapor are compounding. Constraint is disclosed as equipment/power/data-center space, not demand. ([prnewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cogent-communications-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-302844087.html))

**Limb 2 — "the business is melting": DAMAGE-ARRIVING, largely deliberate.** Service revenue $235.6M (−1.5% QoQ, −4.3% YoY) but the decline is concentrated in off-net (**$84.5M, −17.3% YoY**) — legacy Sprint grooming, margin-accretive by construction. On-net **+2.3% YoY**, net-centric **+1.6% QoQ**. EBITDA-as-adjusted $71.1M, **+1.3% QoQ, margin 30.2%**; headcount 1,682 from 1,795 (**−6.3% QoQ**); most of $240M annualized Sprint cost savings achieved. Operationally this is stabilizing, not melting.

**Limb 3 — solvency: this is where the −76% lives, and it is closest to STRUCTURAL.** Gross debt $2.34B, cash $369.7M, net debt $1.97B, net leverage **6.23x** (from 6.79x — improved *by asset sales, not cash flow*). Three hard, dated facts:
- **$750M of 7.00% unsecured notes mature June 2027.** Management says refi "completed in the third quarter" — i.e. *now* — possibly for less than full size using data-center proceeds, at an explicitly higher cost of capital. ([Q1-26 call](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-cogent-q1-2026-beats-eps-forecast-stock-plummets-93CH-4656474))
- **The T-Mobile IP Transit Services Agreement expires ~2026-11-01** (54 months from the 2023-05-01 close). It contributed **$33.3M of cash in Q2** and **$25.0M of the gap between $46.1M reported and $71.1M adjusted EBITDA**. That is a contractual ~$100M/yr cash cliff, only partly offset by ~$45M of integration costs disappearing in 2027.
- **The covenant was already amended**, not comfortably met: 2032 secured-notes ratio-liens basket raised **4.00x → 4.75x**, with a commitment to apply ≥$175M of data-center proceeds to debt repurchase. Amendments are a symptom, not a solution.

Post-cliff run-rate EBITDA is therefore ~$184M (reported basis) to ~$229M (crediting the $45M cost roll-off). Against **true** obligations — the company's $2.34B gross debt *excludes* roughly $480–570M of IRU/finance-lease obligations, which is why third-party EV prints at **$2.93B** vs the $2.44B you get from mcap + company-defined net debt — that is **~10.6–13.3x**, not 6.23x. Headline leverage understates the stack.

### Why a court still has information value

At $475M market cap against a ~$2.9B EV, the equity is a ~5x-levered stub on an asset-monetization race. The assets are large and *demonstrably* carried below market: 10 data centers sold for $224.2M net at a **$130.7M book gain**; 14 more (≈55MW) still marketed, with four LOIs **rejected on price**; 37.8M IPv4 addresses with 22.8M unleased. Those are appraisable from primary sources, and small changes in the appraisal swing the equity between zero and a multiple. That is precisely the case where adversarial work changes sizing.

Two findings a court must stress-test, both of which cut against the bulls:
1. **Management's revealed preference is the debt, not the equity.** They bought $20.4M par of their own 2032 secured notes at **91.955** in Q2 and $118.4M more at **90.071** in July. Insiders are buying a first-lien claim at a ~10% discount rather than the stub at $9.92. First-lien paper below par is the credit market pricing recovery risk — and it caps how much option value the equity can carry.
2. **A live credibility gap.** On the Aug 6 call management reiterated multi-year targets of **6–8% revenue growth and ~200bps annual EBITDA-margin expansion** while printing **−4.3% YoY**. That is structurally the same forward-promise-vs-print divergence that produced the wavelength blowup and the securities class action (class period 2024-02-29 → 2026-05-01, lead-plaintiff deadline **2026-09-21**).
