## THE DECISIVE DOCUMENT BLUE FOUND AND RED DID NOT READ

Asana's FY26 10-K, filed 2026-03-13, defines the metric red's entire Kill #1 rests on:

> "Our **reported dollar-based net retention rate equals the simple arithmetic average of our quarterly dollar-based net retention rate for the four quarters ending with the most recent fiscal quarter.**"
> — [10-K FY26, Item 7 MD&A, Key Business Metrics](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477720/000147772026000021/asan-20260131.htm)

The reported NRR is a **lagging four-quarter simple average of the in-quarter NRRs.** By construction it cannot show an in-quarter inflection until four quarters after the inflection begins, and — with values rounded to whole percent — a rising in-quarter series is arithmetically compatible with a reported number pinned at 96%. Worked example consistent with every filed figure: in-quarter 94.5 / 95.5 / 96.5 / 97.0 → average 95.9 → **reports as 96%.**

Red graded the CFO's claim **REFUTED**. The company's own stated definition means the reported series **cannot refute it** — it can only confirm it four quarters late. The correct grade is UNVERIFIABLE, which is red's own alternate grade. The FATAL severity is not supported.

Second correction red missed: the reported series is **not "flat for six quarters."** Pulled directly from nine consecutive 8-K EX-99.1 exhibits (overall / Core / $100k+):

| Qtr | Q1FY25 | Q2FY25 | Q3FY25 | Q4FY25 | Q1FY26 | Q2FY26 | Q3FY26 | Q4FY26 | Q1FY27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overall | 100 | 98 | 96 | 96 | **95** | 96 | 96 | 96 | 96 |
| Core | 102 | 99 | 98 | 97 | **96** | 96 | 97 | 97 | 97 |
| $100k+ | 108 | 103 | 99 | 96 | **95** | **95** | 96 | 96 | 96 |

That is **trough-and-recover**, not flat-decay. The trough is Q1 FY26. Overall and $100k+ are each **+1pt versus the same quarter a year ago**. Red presented "flat" as if flat were the indictment; flat at the bottom of a 12-point collapse is the stabilization the thesis claimed.
