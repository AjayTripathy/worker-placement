## THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION
**1. Africa is not a China-growth proxy — the linkage mostly runs the wrong way.** African BRI
economies (Kenya, Zambia, Ethiopia, Angola) are China's *debtors*: the exposure is to Chinese
credit withdrawal and commodity demand, not to Chinese consumer growth. Kenya owes ~$6-8B to
China EXIM against the SGR; Zambia defaulted into a Chinese-creditor restructuring; when China
slows, BRI flows stop and these sovereigns' USD curves widen. Holding Kenyan assets is closer to
being **short China credit** than long China growth. The one instrument on the list that is
genuinely "China exposure under better (Dutch) governance" is **Prosus** — and its persistent
NAV discount is the market pricing exactly the governance/holding-structure friction the
question hopes to escape. There is no free lunch on that axis, but PRX at a discount is the
honest way to buy the actual premise if China exposure is the goal.

**2. The governance advantage is real but it swaps into convertibility risk — the same trapped-cash
question the desk just adjudicated on USNA's China cash, relocated to Lagos and Cairo.** Common-law
minority protections and real share ownership (no VIEs) are genuine advantages of the LSE/JSE/GDR
channel. But the binding constraint moves one layer down: Nigeria's 2016-17 and 2022-23 FX regimes
trapped MTN's, Airtel's and IHS's dollars exactly the way a China repatriation scare would; Egypt
devalued twice against CBKD holders. The names that cleared BOTH tests over a decade are the
Georgian banks and CIB Egypt — which is why they compounded +265% to +738% in USD.

**3. The sovereign floor is passable only by very-high-ROE businesses.** With Kenya local 10y at
12.5%, Egypt at 22.9% and Nigeria at 17.1% (USD eurobonds high-single-digit), bond-dominance kills
ordinary equities: only 20-40% ROE franchises at low-single-digit multiples (frontier banks,
USD-indexed towers post-repair) clear the hurdle. This is precisely the desk's existing EM-bank
two-leg pattern — the screen's conclusion is an *extension* of HSBK/BGEO/CBKD, not a new strategy.

**4. Recommended for refutability-stage entry (principal review first, nothing enqueued):**
- **CBKD (CIB Egypt)** — the strongest: devaluation-tested 30%+ ROE compounder, USD GDR, already
  on the watchlist with an alert at 2.20 vs tape 2.73; the two-leg EM-bank method applies directly
  (deposit-franchise moat + the 2022-23 deval as the natural experiment). ADV ~$1.5M supports
  starter-size ladders.
- **IHS (IHS Towers)** — the dislocation candidate: NYSE-listed African towers that ate the naira
  and renegotiated its contracts to USD-linkage; if the repair is real it is the rare frontier
  asset whose cash flows are now hard-currency while its price still carries the soft-currency scar.
- **PRX (Prosus)** — only if the principal's actual goal is China exposure with better governance;
  it is that, literally, at a discount.

**Not actionable:** anything Kenya-listed (no market access), HTWS (FX basket), MTN/VOD/NPN (fair
