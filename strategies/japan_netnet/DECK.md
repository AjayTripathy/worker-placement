## Thesis
The pitch: eight Japanese small-caps sold off, the sell-offs are benign round-trips of momentum
spikes (the pitch highlights one at −32%), and two FY26 upward revisions prove the businesses are
fine — so you are buying cash-rich deep value at a governance-reform tailwind (TSE's "cost of
capital / P/B > 1" campaign forcing buybacks and dividends).

What we actually believe after checking the tape: the *basket* is a legitimate low-multiple
Japanese cyclical-value sleeve, but the headline single-name narrative does **not** describe most
of what is in it, and the "no impairment" claim is false for at least one name. This is a
diversified mean-reversion + capital-return-pressure carry play. Buy it as a basket for that,
sized honestly for liquidity — not for the valve story.

## Mechanism / what resolves it
- **Value convergence via governance:** TSE's disclosure-or-explain regime keeps pressure on
  sub-1.0x P/B companies to return cash. Buybacks/dividend hikes and occasional MBO/tender offers
  are the realistic catalysts that close the gap. Resolution horizon: multi-quarter to years.
- **Cyclical earnings recovery** on the industrial names (steel, auto parts) re-rates the multiple.
- **Falsifier (what would prove us wrong):** if, on EDINET/XBRL pull, net cash + investment
  securities do **not** exceed a large fraction of market cap (i.e. these are not actually
  net-nets), the floor thesis is dead and this is just cheap-because-deserved cyclicals. Second
  falsifier: if the drawdown names keep cutting guidance rather than revising up, "benign
  round-trip" is refuted and it was impairment.

## Risks & kill triggers
- **Identity mismatch (the biggest one):** the pitch describes a "specialty butterfly-valve /
  fluid-control maker (LNG/ships/plants/semis)." **None of the eight tickers is that company.**
  6229 is Okumura Engineering (construction), 5408 steel, 3388 electrical, 5697 scrap, 7222/7239
  auto parts, 4625 paints, 8104 textile trading. The compelling single-name story is unattached
  to the sleeve — do not let it carry the pitch.
- **"No impairment" is false for 7222 Nissan Shatai:** down ~27% and sitting near its 52-week low
  is consistent with genuine deterioration tied to Nissan-parent distress, not a benign momentum
  round-trip. Kill/watch: cut if it and Nissan keep guiding down.
- **Half the basket is the opposite of dislocated:** 3388, 5697, 7239, 8104 are trading at or
  near 52-week highs. Buying them is momentum, not deep-value mean-reversion. Trim/exclude on
  entry unless the net-net floor is verified.
- **Net-net floor unverified:** balance sheets not pulled this pass. If NCAV/net-cash does not
  clear the market cap, the downside floor evaporates. Verify on EDINET/XBRL before adding.
- **Liquidity:** several names too thin to exit in a stress window.
- **FX:** unhedged yen; weak yen erodes USD returns even when local prices hold.
- **Value-trap governance:** TSE pressure is a tailwind, not a schedule; cash can sit idle for
  years with entrenched boards and cross-holdings.

## How it fits goals & scenarios
- **Goal fit:** long-horizon growth / retirement capital — patient, tax-agnostic (no near-term
  income need); wrong for spending or liquidity-floor goals given the illiquidity.
- **Scenario behavior:** *helps* in a global-industrial upcycle and in a yen-neutral-to-strong,
  reform-accelerating Japan; *hurts* in a global recession (high cyclical beta), in a sharp yen
  depreciation (USD drag), and in a risk-off flight where small-cap illiquidity gaps prices down.
  Low direct rate sensitivity; correlated to global industrials (~1.0) and broad equity (~0.7).

## What could make this wrong (the honest box)
1. **The story is on the wrong basket.** The valve/LNG maker with two FY26 upward revisions and a
   −32% benign round-trip is not among these eight tickers. If the intent was to own *that*
   company, this pack does not do it — resolve the ticker (likely a different TSE code, e.g. the
   valve names around 62xx that are absent here) before committing.
2. **Net-net claim unproven.** No EDINET/XBRL pull yet. If these are not actually below NCAV /
   net cash, there is no deep-value floor and the sleeve is just cheap cyclicals.
3. **Only ~2 of 8 fit the "benign round-trip" tape.** 6229 (and partly 4625) match; the rest are
   either at highs (momentum, not value) or in a real drawdown (7222).
4. **Liquidity may make the good ideas un-actionable.** The two cleanest round-trip names (6229,
   4625) are also among the thinnest.
