# Muni dislocation — thesis deck

**Bucket:** defensive / ballast · **Edge:** RP_FAIR · **Author:** signalos

## Thesis
Individual investors sell municipal bonds in **odd lots** (< $100k) and under
duress (estates, tax dates, fund outflows) into a thin, dealer-intermediated
market. Those prints clear **below evaluated (matrix) value**. Buying the
dislocation earns a **fair, tax-advantaged yield** — not an informational edge, a
paid liquidity/complexity premium — with **duration you choose** to match a spend.

## Mechanism / what resolves it
The bond either **matures/gets called at par** or re-rates to evaluated value as a
larger buyer steps in. The falsifier: a **credit** reason for the discount (not
liquidity) — screen issuer fundamentals before treating a discount as free.

## How it fits goals & scenarios
- **Goal fit:** ballast for a near-term spend — low equity beta, you set the
  maturity to the goal date, and it stays liquid enough to fund the purchase.
- **Scenarios:** holds in an equity crash and an AI wipeout; the real risk is a
  **rate shock** (duration) — keep it short-dated for a near-term goal.

## Risks & kill triggers
- Discount is credit, not liquidity → skip.
- Duration mismatched to the goal date → rate shock impairs the spend.
