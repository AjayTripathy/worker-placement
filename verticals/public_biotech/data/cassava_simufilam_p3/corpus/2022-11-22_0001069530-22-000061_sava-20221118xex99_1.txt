(Nasdaq: SAVA ) (the Company or Cassava Sciences ) , a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on Alzheimer s disease , today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement with several healthcare-focused and other institutiona l investors for the purchase of 1,666,667 shares of its common stock, at a purchase price of $30 per share, for gross proceeds of approximately $ 50 million, in a registered direct offering. The closing of the offering is expected to occur on or about November 22, 20 2 2, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. H.C. Wainwright & Co. is acting as the exclusive placement agent for the offering . Cassava Sciences intends to use the net proceeds from this offering for working capital and general corporate purposes , including development of simufilam , the Company s lead drug candidate for the treatment of Alzheimer s disease . The shares of common stock are being offered by Cassava Sciences pursuant to a n automatic "shelf" registration statement on Form S-3 , which was filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the SEC ) on February 10, 2021 and became effective immediately upon filing , and the prospectus contained therein . About Cassava Sciences , Inc. Cassava Sciences, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company based in Austin, Texas.
