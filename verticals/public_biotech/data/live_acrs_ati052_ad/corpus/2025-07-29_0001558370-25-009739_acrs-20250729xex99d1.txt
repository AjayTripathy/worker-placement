ATI-2138: SAD/MAD Study Summary ATI-2138 was generally well tolerated at all doses tested in the trial. No serious adverse events were reported. ATI-2138 was rapidly absorbed. Multiple doses ranging from 10 to 80 mg daily over two weeks in healthy volunteers showed linear PK and dose dependent increases in exposure. At 10-30 mg daily, ATI-2138 plasma concentration reached the targeted level established using preclinical data.
