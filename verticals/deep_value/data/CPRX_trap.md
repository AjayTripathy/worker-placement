Total revenue +5.7% YoY masks a violent mix shift: Firdapse + Agamree compounding while Fycompa

grew +15.5% YoY (Q1'26 73.2 vs Q1'25 63.4) — still strong, driven by the high-margin Firdapse/Agamree mix.

**FIRDAPSE (66% of revenue — the load-bearing question, now RESOLVED in the bull's favor):**
- Jan-2023: Paragraph IV ANDAs from Teva, Hetero, Lupin (+Inventia Oct-2023). CPRX sued, triggered
  30-month stays.
- **All four generic challengers have now SETTLED with dated, deferred market-entry:**
  - Inventia (settled Jul-2024): no entry before all patents expire (Feb-2037 latest).
  - Lupin (Jun-2024 / Aug-2025): converted 5 of 6 to Paragraph III; deferred entry.
  - Teva (Jan-2025): no market entry before **Feb-25-2035**.
  - **Hetero (last holdout) — settled 2026-05-07 (8-K): no entry before January 2035.**
- Orange Book patents run to **Feb-2037** (US Patent 10,626,088).
- Net: the Firdapse generic cliff that the bear case feared is **contractually deferred to 2035** with
  the composition patent to 2037. Patent fears were over-discounted — the bull was right on the
  decisive variable. (This is also exactly why Angelini is paying up.)

**AGAMREE (vamorolone, DMD — the diversifier/growth driver):**
- Licensed from Santhera (Jul-2023, $75M upfront + $36M approval milestone + 5%+ royalty, North
  America). CPRX also holds ~9.4% of Santhera equity.
- US New Chemical Entity exclusivity to **Oct-2028**; Orphan Drug Exclusivity to **Oct-2030**;
  seven Orange Book patents from May-2029 to **Jul-2040** (PTE requested). Long, clean runway.
- Ramping +66% YoY; manufacturing being transitioned to a US supplier by end-2026.

**FYCOMPA (perampanel, epilepsy — acquired from Eisai):**
- **Loss of exclusivity already realized.** Three generic tablets + one generic oral suspension on
  market since ~Dec-2025. Revenue −61% YoY and company explicitly guides further decline. This is the
  honestly-disclosed cliff — and it is small (9% of rev) and shrinking, so its drag is contained.

## (3) VERDICT

- **As a value-rotation long: NOT INVESTABLE / effectively a TRAP for the screen.** The 11.6x /
  8.6% earnings yield is an artifact of a stock already re-priced to a $31.50 cash takeout. There is
  no fundamental discount left to capture — buying here buys the deal terms, not the franchise.
- **As an underlying business: REAL high-ROIC, net-cash, growing orphan platform** whose decisive
  risk (Firdapse generic cliff) was over-discounted and is now contractually settled to 2035/2037.
  The bull thesis was substantively correct — which is precisely why a strategic acquirer is paying
  $3.85B equity / ~$3.1B EV (11.6x EBIT) to take it private. The honesty grade is CLEAN: Fycompa's
  cliff is plainly disclosed; the marketed takeaway does not diverge from the data.

## (4) ENTRY / FAIR VALUE

- **FV = $31.50/share** (the announced, board-approved cash merger price). Not a DCF question anymore.
- **Trade, if any = merger arbitrage, not value.** Spread at $31.50 ≈ **0%** — no positive carry;
  any fill above ~$31.45 is a negative-expected-value arb after the residual (shareholder-vote /
  outside-date) risk. Downside on a (low-probability) deal break is to a standalone fundamental

  the current price.
- **Action: PASS for the cheap-and-quality basket. Remove from the value screen** (corporate-event
  override). If already held below $31.50, hold to close / tender into the deal; do not add here.
