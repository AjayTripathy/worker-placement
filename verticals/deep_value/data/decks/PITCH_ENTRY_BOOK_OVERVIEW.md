## Slide 3 — How the book was built (the funnel)

- **~16 names deep-diligenced across three sweeps** (frontier banks → diversified slate → catalyst sleeve); every one got claim-verification against primary filings *plus* an independent attempt to kill the thesis.
- **The exclusions are the proof the filter works:** a spent catalyst caught before money rode it (SSRM); a fairly-priced name the market already pays up for (CPRI); an untradeable state-seized trap (Ferrexpo); an edge that a +46% rally had already taken (Raiffeisen); a "cheap" bank whose low P/E was peak-rate earnings (PKO).
- **Depth of work is not proportional to initial stake** — it's what *earns the right to size up*. Diligence is cheap; mis-deployed capital is not.

## Slide 5 — Portfolio construction: caps on correlated sleeves, not just names

- **AI-software sleeve (HUBS + MNDY + S): ≤7% initial, 12% ceiling.** All three are bets that AI augments rather than destroys seat-based software. Individually a 7, a 7, and a 4 — together, one factor. The cap means HubSpot scaling toward 7% mechanically limits the others.
- **Frontier-bank sleeve (TBC + BGEO + Halyk): ~5–6% cap** — shared political/sanctions/FX tails; binary-tail names stay **unlevered**.
- **Leverage policy:** margin funds *more diligence-survivors* and *deferring winners* (borrow rather than realize gains; interest deductible) — never sizing up an unproven forecast.
- All caps defined on **net liquidation value**, not gross, so leverage can't silently inflate them.

## Slide 6 — The tax overlay (why deploying now is right in a high-tax year)

- **Losses harvested this year offset gains taxed at ~50%+ (CA top bracket); winners held into next year defer at likely lower rates.** A zero-edge bet carries ~+2–4% after-tax expected value; break-even win rate drops to ~44%.
- **Mechanical year-end rule per lot:** catalyst resolves against us → exit (harvest if red) · any lot red near **Dec-15** → harvest · green at year-end → hold into January+, prefer long-term.
- Every position is wash-sale-checked against the ~471-name Parametric SMA before entry (all current names clean).

## Slide 7 — Honesty page: what would make us wrong

- **The AI-software sleeve is the big bet:** if AI genuinely collapses seat-based software over the next 2 years, HUBS/MNDY/S lose together — that's why the sleeve is capped, not why it's avoided. The current *data* (retention rising, per-customer revenue accelerating) contradicts the fear; the fear is about the future data.
- **Our own forecasting record is unproven** — a pre-registered prediction ledger now scores every dated call against what the market implied, starting with the Jul-28/Jul-30 prints. Until that record validates, conviction comes from *verified facts and priced-in pessimism*, not from our probabilities.
- Known soft spots per name are on each deck's kill-trigger slide; two print dates (MNDY, S) are estimates pending company confirmation.
