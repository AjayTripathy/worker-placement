## Convergence findings (both reviewers, independently — must-fix)

**1. "The two biggest risks are shrinking / Halyk self-cut its sanctions channel" — FRAMING WRONG.**
The Mir (Feb-2024) and Zolotaya Korona (Aug-2024) exits were **OFAC-forced compliance**, not voluntary de-risking — Halyk dropped Mir four days after the US designated its operator (NSPK), and halted Zolotaya Korona after its developer was sanctioned. 
- *SignalOS:* dates solid, framing is spin; the honest version ("Halyk complied with US designations, so the channel is closed either way") is *still supportive*.
- *Skeptic:* pushes further — it's evidence OFAC is actively working Kazakh rails (escalation), not detente.
- **Adjudication:** Reword required. The *effect* (the Russia retail channel is closed) genuinely supports the thesis, and complying-not-evading is a plus for a US holder. But the skeptic's "escalation on Halyk" also **overstates** — these were Russia-side network designations Halyk was connected to, not Halyk-specific targeting. Truth in the middle: **the channel is closed (good), and the secondary-sanctions environment remains a live binary (the real risk).** The deck's "risks are shrinking" is too clean; the skeptic's "risks are intensifying" is too dark.

**2. "The looting fear is disproven, in the audit" — OVER-CLAIM on a solid datum.**
- *SignalOS:* the 0.101% figure is exact and the family is a net depositor — genuinely clean. **But** the audited related-party note discloses its *own carve-out*: shareholder-participated entities management deems controlled by "unrelated parties" via **trust-management arrangements** are excluded from the related-party line. So the defensible claim is "**disclosed** related-party lending is negligible," not "looting disproven."
- *Skeptic:* same point, sharper — IAS-24 captures only formally-designated parties (the most gameable note); "refuted" is really "not evidenced in the note that would be gamed first."
- **Adjudication:** Reword required, thesis intact. This is an **over-confident narrator, not a hidden liability** — the carve-out is itself disclosed, and there's real alignment (the family also *funds* the bank, holding ~KZT 340bn of Halyk's own debt). Change "disproven / empirically absent" → "**disclosed** related-party lending is negligible (0.1%; family is a net depositor and a funder); the residual is the disclosed trust-management carve-out — a judgment, not a hidden exposure."

> Two independent reviewers landing on the same two slides = these get fixed regardless of anything else.

- The whole slate is **one correlated EM/frontier-bank factor** — show TBC+BGEO+Halyk (+KB) against the ~5–6% cap as a single bet.
