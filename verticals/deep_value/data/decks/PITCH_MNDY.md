Verdict: **BUY** — deep-value re-rating; accumulate, **entered-after-biased** into the Q2 print.
Conviction: **7 / 10.** Spot **~$76.26**. Probability-weighted fair value **~$117** (**+53%**).

One line: *A profitable, net-cash software grower is priced at a distressed sales multiple for a debate about AI's terminal effect on the category — we accumulate patiently, and let the stock's own habit of falling after good prints do the buying for us.*

---

## Slide 2 — The setup: a multiple cut to a third, on a real debate

- monday.com trades at **1.66x** forward (FY26) enterprise-value-to-sales. Its own historical band ran near a **4.6x** median — so the sales multiple has compressed to roughly **a third** of where it lived, even as revenue keeps growing.
- **What the market is pricing.** Back the scenarios out of the price and the market is paying for roughly a **58% chance of the bear / terminal-impairment case** — that AI eventually hollows out the horizontal work-management category — and assigns **essentially zero value to the bull.**
- Our read of the data supports a materially lower bear probability. That ~28-point gap between the market's implied odds and ours is the trade. **This is not an honesty catch** — disclosure is clean throughout (see Slide 4); it is a probability disagreement on a crowded, emotional debate.

> What could make this wrong: one cohort *is* actually cracking (Slide 5). The bear isn't imaginary — it has one real foothold, and that is our number-one watch item.

---

## Slide 3 — The thesis in plain words

- monday.com is **profitable, growing revenue ~24%, generating free cash, buying back stock, and sitting on net cash worth a third of its market value.** It is priced as if the business is impaired.
- The deceleration the bears point to is **real but gradual** — a twelve-quarter *glide* of about 2 points per quarter, **not a cliff** — and management has been honest about it, even *guiding retention down* rather than hiding it.
- So the trade is: **buy a cash-rich, still-growing business at a liquidation-adjacent multiple**, accept that the re-rate will be slow because the debate is crowded, and **size in patiently** — starting small now and adding into the post-print weakness this stock reliably hands you.

## Slide 5 — The bear case we tried to make, and what survived

We ran an independent pass built to **break the thesis.** Unlike HubSpot, here part of the bear *stuck* — and we lead with it.

- **The bear:** AI compresses seats and pricing across horizontal work-management; the biggest customers defect first; net-new expansion stalls; the multiple was right to collapse toward the cash.
- **What survived — the real crack:** the **>$100k-ARR cohort cracked in Q1.** Net-adds in that cohort fell to **+88 — the low of the series** — and its retention *inverted* below the smaller >$50k cohort. Large-customer expansion is where an AI-seat bear would show up first, and it is exactly where the softness appeared. **This is a genuine, disconfirming finding — the single most important thing to watch.**
- **What did NOT break:** the *self-serve and small-customer* base is still healthy, free cash flow is real, the balance sheet is a floor, and the overall glide has no cliff. The bear has **one** foothold, not the whole house.

> This is why we **enter small and patient**, not big and now. The thesis is intact but it has a live fault line; the >$100k cohort is on a two-quarter clock (Slide 9).

## Slide 7 — Entry structure and the print

**Decision: accumulate, entered-AFTER-biased.**

- **Catalyst:** Q2 FY26 prints **~Aug-10 to Aug-17 (our estimate — not company-confirmed).** Frozen read: a **headline beat (~$362–368M revenue)**, AI-consumption detail **inconclusive**, retention ~109–110%.
- **Why bias entry to *after* the print.** This stock has a documented habit of **selling off on good news**: recent post-print reactions to beats were **−30% / −12% / −21%.** Buying *before* means paying up for a catalyst that historically fades. So we let the recurring gap-down do the accumulating.
- **Structure:**
  - **Starter now** — justified purely by the net-cash floor and a Street low above spot; you are not paying for the catalyst.
  - **Build into post-print weakness (~Aug-10+),** targeting the **$58–84** accumulation band, **sweet spot below $72** — into the recurring gap-down.

> Contrast with HubSpot deliberately: HUBS we enter *before* its print because the bad news is already priced; MNDY we enter *after* because good news here tends to be sold. Same discipline, opposite tactic — driven by each stock's own reaction pattern.

## Slide 8 — Sizing and the scale path

**A patient build behind a live fault line.**

- **Starter 1.5%** now (the net-cash floor and above-spot Street low justify a toe-hold, nothing more).
- **Build to 3–5%** after the Q2 print — **preferably into post-print weakness**, not into a relief rally.
- **Max (5–10%) only** if **two** things turn: AI as a share of net-new ARR **inflects off ~3%**, *and* the **>$100k net-adds re-accelerate.** Until then the >$100k crack keeps the position capped.

## Slide 9 — Kill triggers

We exit or stand down — no debate — if any of these print. Stated plainly:

- **Overall net-dollar retention falls below 108%,** *or* the **>$100k cohort's retention falls below 112%.**
- **>$100k net-adds decline for a second straight quarter** (Q1's +88 was already the series low).
- **Revenue prints below guidance** (breaks the five-for-five beat pattern).
- **AI as a share of net-new ARR stalls off ~3% over two-to-three quarters.**
- **Net-adds in the >10-user base turn negative** (self-serve / search-driven acquisition erosion reaching the core).
- **Free-cash-flow-margin guide cut below 18%,** *or* cash-torching M&A — the net-cash floor is load-bearing to this thesis.
