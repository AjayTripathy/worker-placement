### 2. Cycle / demand durability — how much supercycle is priced
- **Transmission backlog ≈ €17B** (incl. EGL4, the >€2.3B UK interconnector signed Feb-2026) + ~€2B not yet booked. Against FY25 Transmission revenue of €3,262M, ~5x annual revenue → genuine 4–5yr visibility. The real secular core: HVDC ~€4B, submarine power ~€11.6B (offshore-wind + interconnectors).
- **But Transmission is only €3,262M of €19,650M = 17% of revenue (≈24% of adj. EBITDA).** The other **~83% of revenue / ~76% of EBITDA** (Power Grid, Electrification I&C+Specialties, Digital) is **book-and-ship, short-cycle, copper- and construction/industrial/data-center-exposed** — no multi-year backlog.
- **Implied growth at the multiple:** ~31x forward / ~3% FCF yield prices sustained low-double-digit EPS growth for ~5+ years with no margin reversion. FY26 guide +12.6% adj. EBITDA midpoint is solid, but **the margin-expansion lever is largely spent: Transmission already hit its 2028 margin target (18.3% FY25, 20.9% Q4) three years early.** Future upside must come from volume/capacity conversion (Monna Lisa vessel, Finland), execution-dependent, not a free re-rate.
- After **+67.6% YTD**, the supercycle is substantially in the price.

### 3. Mode B — disconfirming pass
- **Copper pass-through optics — REAL but DISCLOSED (anti-masking, positive).** Headline revenue is metal-inflated; Prysmian proactively reports margins "at standard metal prices" and organic growth net of metal. Use EV/EBITDA and EBIT, not EV/Sales.
- **HVDC fixed-price execution — the genuine forward idiosyncratic risk.** €17B+ of large multi-year fixed-price submarine/HVDC contracts; the cable industry (incl. Prysmian's Western Link, Nexans/NKT) has a history of fixed-price project losses. FY25 shows zero write-downs ("smooth project execution") — a clean print — but the marquee 20.9% Q4 transmission margin is being extrapolated; a single large project slip would dent it.
- **Margin reversion already visible in Power Grid:** Q4 margin fell to 12.1% from 15.4% on an overhead-line metal-premium surge — confirms the cyclical exposure of the non-transmission ~76%.
- **US tariffs — net POSITIVE.** Prysmian/Encore is vertically integrated in the US (Freeport AZ copper, TX rod mill); copper-import tariffs raise domestic prices and disadvantage import-dependent competitors. FY26 guidance explicitly excludes US-tariff impact (disclosed both ways).
- **Hybrid leverage — mild structural flag.** Reported net debt €3,097M *excludes* the €1.0B May-2025 hybrid (BB, 5.25%, 50% equity content) + a prior hybrid. Economically debt-like; a credit/EV-conscious view adds ~€1.0–1.75B back → true leverage ~2.0x, EV ~€46–47B. Standard for the sector, fully disclosed.
- **Honesty/disclosure: CLEAN, takeaway MATCHES data.** "Record net income" openly attributes €346M to the YOFC disposal; adjusted EBITDA excludes that gain (Annex B removes €341M non-operating income); guidance caveats stated plainly; the "best year ever / 2028 target hit early" framing is backed by the printed 20.9% Q4 margin. **No liar to exclude — the only legitimate gripe is valuation.**

### 4. Ukraine exposure — reality check
**Negligible — thematic sentiment, not a sized mechanism.** Only a 2011 Crimea 80MW solar-PV cable job and generic subsea-sabotage commentary surface; no Ukraine grid-rebuild contract in the FY25 backlog, no Ukraine line item. Prysmian's secular demand is European interconnectors/offshore wind (EGL4 UK, Tunisia-Italy Elmed, RTE France, Finland) + US grid/data-center — not Ukraine. **Own it (or not) on the European/US grid-CapEx supercycle, full stop.**

### 5. Load-bearing risks & unverifiable
- **Risks (ranked):** (1) multiple de-rate on order-intake/book-to-bill deceleration (~76% of EBITDA is cyclical; 31x prices it as if all backlog-covered transmission); (2) margin reversion (transmission re-rate lever largely spent); (3) HVDC fixed-price project execution on the €17B+ book; (4) copper/metal rollover; (5) hybrid-adjusted leverage ~2.0x vs 1.3x reported; (6) legacy antitrust cash tail.
- **Unverifiable (≠ clean):** FY2025 financials board-approved but **"still being audited"** as of the 26-Feb release; the Street forward EPS €4.66 / ~31x is vendor-sourced with a PPA-amortization add-back not cleanly reconstructable (primary-derived numbers are trailing 33.5x / clean-trailing ~46x); backlog €17B+€2B and HVDC/submarine splits are management-stated, not audited.

**Framing for the sleeve:** clean, well-run, genuinely high-quality compounder — but a +68%-YTD momentum name at ~31x forward / ~3% FCF yield. Right *business*, wrong *price* for a value-conscious sleeve; WATCH for a copper- or order-intake-driven drawdown rather than chase it here.
