### 2. Cycle position — TROUGH-to-early-recovery, NOT a trap
- EPS recovered from a depressed €0.72 (2024 carried €51m impairment) to €1.52; **2022 peak EPS was ~€3+**. Earnings well below peak.
- Op EBITDA margin 16.5% vs ~18-19% peak = mid-cycle, modestly off peak.
- **North America is the cyclical drag: EBITDA −31%, revenue −12%** (US new-build/pipe weakness). Europe East +1%, Europe West +6% (Terreal full-year).
- **Verdict: the low EV/EBITDA sits on roughly trough-to-mid EBITDA → cyclical value, not a peak-earnings value trap.**

**Terreal effect (confirmed but smaller than expected):** Terreal (French roof tiles, ~€1bn) added EV (debt) and EBITDA, so EV/EBITDA roughly preserved; its PPA intangible amortization depresses net income, which is why P/E (14.8x) looks rich vs EV/EBITDA (5.4x). But D&A rose only €15m YoY — Terreal was already partly consolidated in 2024. The dominant cause of the EBITDA-to-earnings gap is **structural capital intensity** (D&A ~50% of EBITDA for a brick/kiln business) plus interest on €1.6bn debt — not a one-off Terreal distortion.

### 3. Mode B — disconfirming pass
- **The real bear case is ROCE, not leverage: 8.4% and falling.** The market awards a low EV/EBITDA *because* this is a low-return, capital-heavy cyclical — a re-rating may not arrive even if earnings recover. "Cheap" is partly deserved.
- **North America deteriorating fast** — the diversification leg is the worst performer.
- **FCF €474m is partly a working-capital release** (downturn destocking) + cut growth capex (€133m vs €177m). Do not capitalize the 19% FCF yield as steady-state.
- Morgan Stanley cut to **Underweight on new-build risk** — a credible external bear.
- **Honesty: CLEAN.** Marketed takeaway ("maintained revenues and margins despite weak new-build") matches the data exactly (revenue +1%, margin −30bps). Goodwill unchanged with no impairment dodge. No takeaway-vs-data divergence.

### 4. Ukraine reality-check
**Zero Ukraine mentions in the FY2025 report.** Europe East = €1,180m = **25.8% of revenue → "CEE ~26%" confirmed.** Direct Ukraine is **not disclosed** — strategy is to supply from neighboring-country plants, so direct Ukraine revenue is immaterial/unverifiable. **The "~1-2% direct Ukraine" figure cannot be verified — treat as optionality, not revenue.** The thesis depends on the **European cycle**, not Ukraine.

### 5. Verdict — WIENERBERGER
- **(a) Value grade: GENUINE CYCLICAL VALUE, low-quality tier.** Cheap on EV/EBITDA (5.4x verified) and cash flow, on trough-to-mid earnings; not a trap — but cheap-for-a-reason low-ROCE heavy industrial.
- **(b) DD verdict: STARTER (small).**
- **(c) Load-bearing risks:** (1) North America EBITDA −31% accelerating; (2) European new-build stays depressed if rate cuts stall (MS Underweight); (3) structural ROCE 8.4% caps any re-rating.
- **(d) Unverifiable:** direct Ukraine revenue (zero disclosure); true mid-cycle normalized EBITDA; steady-state FCF (2025 flattered by WC release).

- **Honesty: CLEAN, audited large-cap.** Only the "record/robust" framing-over-flat-organic tension noted above.

The **~€280m Lviv "IQ Campus"** is a **capex commitment, not revenue** — immaterial to a €9.2bn-revenue company (and partially war-damaged). **Kingspan's standalone thesis has zero dependence on Ukraine.**
