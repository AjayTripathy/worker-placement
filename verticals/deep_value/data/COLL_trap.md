## 3. The decisive trap question — PATENT / LOE timeline (per 10-K "Patents")

**Nucynta franchise = $196M = 25% of revenue is in ACTIVE GENERIC EROSION right now.** Q1'26 already shows it: Nucynta IR −4.9%, Nucynta ER −7.4% YoY, and Collegium launched its own **authorized generics** ("Nucynta IR AG" $1.3M, "Nucynta ER AG" $1.4M new lines) — the standard end-of-life harvesting move that confirms the brand cliff has arrived, not is approaching.

**Opioid concentration:** 81% of revenue is opioid/pain (ex-Jornay). The ADHD pivot (Jornay, + pending **AZSTARYS** acquisition closing Q2'26 via delayed-draw term loan) is real diversification but only 19% today.

## 4. Trap hypotheses — adjudicated

- **(a) Patent-cliff melting annuity — PARTIALLY TRUE / the core of the discount.** Nucynta (25%) is melting NOW. But Xtampza (26%, protected to 2032) and Belbuca (28%, to 2027–2032) are durable, and Jornay (19%) is GROWING +36%. So ~73% of revenue has multi-year runway; ~25% is in decline. This is a *partially* melting annuity with a growing offset, not a 100% cliff. The market is pricing the Nucynta loss roughly correctly — that is WHY it's "cheap."
- **(b) Opioid overhang — REAL but disclosed.** 81% opioid concentration, DEA-quota and payer exposure, ESG screen-out. This is a permanent multiple cap, honestly disclosed. Not a hidden lie.
- **(c) +EBIT durable or acquisition-driven — MIXED.** The 2024–25 step-up is Ironshore/Jornay-driven (acquisition), but Jornay is *organically* growing +36% post-deal, so it's not purely inorganic optics. Underlying pain franchise is flat-to-eroding.
- **(d) Debt post-Ironshore — CONFIRMED, and the screen missed it entirely.** $803M debt, ~$503M net debt incl. royalty. Leverage ~1.2x net-debt/adj-EBITDA — manageable, well-covered by $330M FCF, but it is NOT net cash. Buyback also smaller than the bull frame: only **$25.1M repurchased in FY2025** (vs $60M in FY2024) — they DOWNSHIFTED buybacks to fund debt/M&A (AZSTARYS).

## 5. Honesty-alpha read (takeaway-vs-data)

Collegium **discloses everything**: the Nucynta LOE, the Hikma generic launch dates, the debt, the amortization, the authorized-generic harvesting. There is **no marketed-takeaway-vs-data divergence** in the filings — this is a *clean discloser of a partially-impaired book*. Under the honesty framework that makes the COMPANY clean. The lie, such as it is, lives in the **SCREEN**, not the 10-K: a stale/mis-tagged data feed manufactured "net cash + 4.9x + 21% EY." Same failure mode as DCGO — the finder fabricated the bargain; the verifier (recompute from latest 10-Q) dissolves it down to an honest ~8–9x EV/EBIT / 5x EV/FCF specialty pharma with a melting quarter of its book.

## 6. Is it REAL cheap, then?

On **FCF** — yes, modestly: ~5x EV/FCF / 28% equity FCF yield for a business that, net of Nucynta runoff, still throws off real cash from protected (Xtampza to 2032) and growing (Jornay) franchises. On **GAAP EV/EBIT** — no, it's ~8–9x, a normal de-rated specialty-pharma multiple that correctly reflects (i) the Nucynta cliff, (ii) opioid stigma cap, (iii) the debt. The bull case ("market over-discounts opioid + patent fears") is only ~half right: opioid stigma is over-discounted, but the Nucynta patent fear is CORRECTLY discounted because it is happening.

## 7. Entry / FV (if treating as a watch-buy)

Frame it as a sum-of-parts harvest+grow, NOT a clean compounder:
- Durable book (Belbuca + Xtampza + Jornay ≈ $570M rev, growing low-single to +36%) supports the bulk of EV.

- **Entry discipline:** only attractive in the **low-$30s** (near the 52w low $29–31, where it traded in past quarters) to compensate for the Nucynta runoff + opioid cap + leverage + AZSTARYS integration risk. At $36.59 it's roughly fair, not a fat pitch.
