# YELP (NYSE: YELP) — Deep-Value Trap-Filter

**Verdict: TRAP (leaning) → at best FAIR-but-decelerating.** Not a clean "cheap durable cash
compounder." The screen's quality optics are real on a trailing basis, but the forward operating
engine is decelerating toward stall and the headline statistics are inflated by stale inputs and a
just-closed acquisition. The 6.6x is on a business whose CORE advertising revenue is now in YoY
DECLINE, masked by (a) an aggressive buyback shrinking the denominator and (b) a tiny, fast-growing
"Other" (AI) line doing all the top-line work.

## 2. Revenue trajectory + mix — the decisive evidence

Total net revenue is barely growing: $358.5M (1Q25) → **$361.5M (1Q26) = +0.8% YoY.** Underneath
that flat line, the engine is breaking down. Advertising revenue by category (from the shareholder
letter, $000s):

| | 1Q25 | 1Q26 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Services advertising | 231,576 | 233,786 | **+1.0%** |
| Restaurants/Retail & Other (RR&O) | 110,425 | 98,702 | **−10.6%** |
| **Total advertising** | **342,001** | **332,488** | **−2.8%** |
| Other (AI: Yelp Assistant / RAC / leads) | 16,533 | 28,969 | **+75%** |
| **Total net revenue** | 358,534 | 361,457 | +0.8% |

**The bull thesis — "Services growth offsets the RR&O melt" — is breaking in real time.** Services
YoY growth has collapsed: **+14% (1Q25) → +8% → +7% → +3% → +1% (1Q26).** RR&O is accelerating its
decline (−3% a year ago → −11% now). **Net of the new AI line, total advertising revenue is already
in YoY DECLINE (−2.8%).** The only thing holding the top line positive is "Other" (+75%), a $29M/qtr
line the company is trying to grow to a $250M run-rate by end-2028 — i.e. the entire growth case now
rests on an unproven AI-lead-gen product, not the legacy review-advertising franchise.

**Engagement / unit economics confirm the melting-ice-cube mechanism:**
- **Paying Advertising Locations: 517k → 485k = −6% YoY.** The advertiser base is shrinking.
- **Ad Clicks: −10% YoY.** Traffic monetization is contracting.
- **Average CPC: +8% YoY** — revenue is being held up by raising price-per-click into a shrinking
  click/advertiser base. That is the classic late-stage ad-platform tell (squeeze price as volume
  fades), not healthy demand.
- Adjusted EBITDA $85M → $79M (−7%); Net income $24M → $18M (−27%, also hit by Hatch deal/integration
  costs and a higher tax rate).

**Mechanism (honesty-alpha framing):** masking channel = *buyback-driven per-share optics + a small
high-growth segment papering over core decline*; signal channel = *category-level YoY decomposition +
paying-locations/ad-clicks operating metrics*; latency = the market is already pricing it (−21% YTD,
near 52-wk low), so this is NOT a hidden divergence the tape hasn't seen — it is a value-trap setup
the screen surfaced precisely because the price fell faster than trailing EBIT.

## 3. Is the marketed takeaway diverging from the data? (the honesty test)

Yelp's own disclosure is **honest** — they print the −6% paying locations, the −10% ad clicks, the
−11% RR&O, the Services deceleration, and the Hatch cash/revolver mechanics in plain sight. This is
NOT a liar. So the catch here is not concealment; it's that **the SCREEN's takeaway ("cheap,
high-quality, net-cash cash cow, buying back stock") diverges from the company's own forward data:**
the net-cash is gone, the buyback is shrinking shares of a business whose core revenue line has
turned negative, and trailing 20% ROIC / 24% FCF is a backward-looking snapshot of a decelerating
engine. The trap is the *value-screen artifact*, not a corporate lie.

---

## 4. Bull case that would have to be true to make this REAL

For YELP to be a re-rater rather than a trap, you need to believe: (a) the AI "Other" line scales to
$250M run-rate (~2.5x) and re-accelerates total revenue past the legacy decline; (b) Services
re-inflects off +1%; (c) the ~$280–320M FCF holds while they integrate Hatch and pay the $30M
retention. Possible, but it is a turnaround/AI-execution bet, not a "buy a cheap durable cash cow"
bet. The trailing multiple is cheap **because** the forward case is contested.

---

## 5. Verdict & where it could be FAIR

- **As a "quality cheap compounder" for a small-cap-value basket: REJECT.** It fails the durability
  leg. Core advertising revenue is in YoY decline; the growth offset (Services) has decelerated to
  +1%; paying advertisers and ad clicks are both shrinking; net-cash is gone. The 7.4x EV/EBIT and
  24% FCF yield are the signature of a value trap, not a bargain — the market has marked it down 21%
  YTD for exactly these reasons.
- **As a deep-value / buyback-arbitrage / optionality name: FAIR, not a screaming buy.** ~21% TTM FCF
  yield + $414M buyback authorization on a 55.9M share float is a genuine self-liquidating return IF
  FCF merely holds flat. The free option is the AI "Other" line. Reasonable only sized small and
  bought lower.

**Entry / FV if you take the FAIR (not REAL) view:** Put a no-growth-to-slight-decline DCF on it.
~$280M sustainable FCF, 10–11% discount, modest terminal decline → roughly $1.3–1.6B equity value,
i.e. **fair value ~$23–28/share** — meaning at $24 it is approximately fairly priced, NOT cheap, for
a melting core. The asymmetry only becomes interesting nearer the 52-wk low (~$19–20), where the
buyback yield + AI option give downside support. **Do not classify as REAL cheap-and-quality.**
