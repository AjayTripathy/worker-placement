# TBC Bank Group (LSE: TBCG) — Full Deep DD: Funding Moat + Geopolitical Risk
**Two-mode diligence, both legs closed to audited primary sources. 2026-06-29. Verdict: verified STARTER.**

TBCG (base ticker TBCG) is the Georgia-banks EM-carry name. The thesis: a structural ~21% through-cycle ROE driven by a low-cost CASA deposit base, NOT a transient rate-cycle windfall. Two legs verified below.

## LEG 1 — FUNDING MOAT: CONFIRMED (the "1.24-on-1" question)

**Verdict:** the ~21% ROE is structural, not a rate-cycle artifact. The funding moat is real and audited.

### Deposit mix (audited, 31-Dec-2025; Annual Report 2025 Note 20, pp.324–325)
| Segment | Current/settlement (CASA) | Term | Total | CASA % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Individuals (retail) | 6,328,304 | 7,641,683 | 13,969,987 | 45.3% |
| Other legal entities (corporate) | 6,844,712 | 3,344,612 | 10,189,324 | 67.2% |
| State / public | 869,520 | 631,227 | 1,500,747 | 57.9% |
| **Total** | **14,042,536** | **11,617,522** | **25,660,058** | **54.7%** |

- **CASA = 54.7%** of the book (GEL '000). The advertised ~10% is the *marginal GEL term* rate, NOT the blended cost.
- **Counter-intuitive tell:** CASA is corporate-skewed (corp 67% CASA); retail is term-heavy (55% term ~10% GEL). The cheap demand funding comes from corporates, not retail.
- Currency (Georgia book): ~48% GEL / 41% USD / 9% EUR (≈52% FX), improving from 46% GEL (de-dollarization ongoing).
- UNVERIFIABLE: CASA-share *within* each currency (GEL-CASA vs FX-CASA) not disclosed.

### True blended cost of funds (computed from Note 30)
- Interest expense on customer accounts FY2025 = GEL 1,377,118k; avg deposits 24,261,946k → **cost of customer deposits = 5.68%** (ties to disclosed 5.7%; **Georgia standalone 4.6%**, Uzbekistan 24.6% drags the group up).
- Total cost of funds = 2,336,887 / 33,645,166 avg interest-bearing liabilities = **6.95%** (ties to disclosed 6.8%).

### NIM reconciliation
- Asset yield = interest income 4,689,341 / avg earning assets 34,133,130 = **13.74%**.
- Funding cost on earning-asset base = 6.85%. **13.74% − 6.85% = 6.89% ≈ reported 7.0% NIM.** Reconciles within ~10bps → **funding moat CONFIRMED**.

### Durability / rate-windfall test
- **Pre-spike proof:** ROE was 22.0% (2018) and 23.0% (2019) — before the 2022-23 rate spike and before Uzbekistan. The decade is 20–27% ex-COVID; FY2025 24.2%. The ~21% assumption is conservative.
- **NBG policy rate ~8.0%, held through 2025** — already below the 2023 peak (~11%) and ≈2018-19 levels; margins held.
- **Near-closed rate gap (Note 37, pp.366–367, audited):** +200bp → profit GEL 3M lower; −200bp → GEL 6M higher, vs GEL 1,420M net profit. **A rate cut is neutral-to-marginally-POSITIVE for NII.** The "spread compresses when NBG cuts" bear is REFUTED on the bank's own disclosure.

**Caveats:** (1) the "CASA moat" is half-true — the low blended cost owes as much to cheap FX deposits (~52% of the Georgia book) as to GEL CASA, so the de-dollarization tailwind is partial; (2) Group 7.0% NIM is Uzbekistan-flattered (21.6% NIM there) but Uzb is only ~9% of profit; (3) the genuine risk is the ASSET side, not the hedged funding side.

## LEG 2 — GEOPOLITICAL RISK: QUANTIFIED (the natural experiment passed)

**Verdict:** the political discount fairly-to-generously compensates; the one genuine tail (bank-level/correspondent-banking sanctions) is binary and unhedgeable → SMALL-MODERATE STARTER, not max size.

### The decisive datum — what actually happened under the worst stress
Through the 2024-25 crisis (contested Oct-2024 election, foreign-agents law, frozen EU accession, six months of 100k-person protests):
- **TBC deposits GREW** +12.2% YoY (Q4-24), +13% (Q1-25) — straight through the protest peak.
- **NBG FX reserves hit a RECORD >$6bn** — accumulated, did NOT burn defending the GEL; the **GEL strengthened**.
- **Dollarization FELL** 5.6pp to 46.6% by Mar-2026 (the Oct-24 +2pp blip fully reversed).
- **Sovereign held BB** — no downgrade; Fitch UPGRADED its outlook back to Stable (Nov-25); 2 of 3 agencies Stable (Moody's the lone Ba2/negative holdout).

The feared deposit run did not materialize → the funding-collapse thesis is empirically refuted.

### Bank-sovereign linkage
TBC: Fitch BB/Stable; Moody's Ba2 (operating) / Ba3 holdco issuer, negative — tracks the sovereign BB floor. No bank downgrade during the crisis; the transmission channel never opened.

### Sanctions tail — the distinction that matters
- **No Georgian bank is OFAC-sanctioned.** TBC is proactively sanctions-compliant.
- **Sanctioned = individuals** (Ivanishvili Dec-2024 Global Magnitsky; officials). The **MEGOBARI Act targets officials, NOT banks**, and is NOT law (passed House 6-May-2025, stalled in Senate, stripped from the NDAA Aug-2025).
- **The genuine tail** = a *sectoral* escalation (a systemic Georgian bank designated for Russia-circumvention → USD correspondent curtailment). Base rate strongly against it (no major Georgian bank named despite years of scrutiny). This is the binary, unhedgeable left tail.
