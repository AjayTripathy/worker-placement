## 2. The one genuine catch — "asset-light marketplace" is inverted
Decomposing FY25 gross profit: the **3P/"service" (marketplace) line runs at only 10.2% gross margin** (it's mostly last-mile logistics), while the **1P/product (own-inventory reselling) line earns 29.8% GM and 85% of total gross profit.** True marketplace commission is just ~$19.6M = 1.5% of revenue. **So GCT is a working-capital-intensive 1P distributor + cross-border 3PL — not the asset-light, network-effects marketplace the narrative implies.** This is fully disclosed (a valuation input, not a lie), and it justifies *a* multiple discount (distributor, not software) — but **not the China-fraud discount the price embeds.** The moat that *is* real: a 35-facility / ~11M-sq-ft SFR fulfillment network financed via leases (so it scales without capex bloat), which holds the goods (anti-disintermediation) and drives genuine buyer stickiness — the 2024 buyer cohort *grew* spend ~42% YoY, $133K spend/active buyer. The moat that's *overstated*: the "two-sided network-effects marketplace." Call it a narrow logistics-anchored moat on a cyclical 1P book riding a China→West arbitrage that's slowly eroding.

## 3. Tariff/China — survivable, and the regime de-escalated
- **The trap-filter (and the market) missed that the IEEPA "reciprocal" tariffs were struck down by SCOTUS on Feb-20-2026.** The durable stack on China furniture is now ~17-42% (Sec-301 7.5% + Sec-232 25% on *wood/upholstered* + a temporary 10% §122 bridge), Vietnam ~10-17% — *far below* the headline 145% the price still fears.
- **Pass-through is the verified mechanism, not absorption:** gross margin held 23-24% flat-to-*up* through two full years of escalation including de-minimis elimination and live Section 232; MD&A confirms "unit prices increased." On 3P GMV (54% of GMV) the duty falls on the *seller*, and GCT's commission is nearly tariff-insulated.
- **The real cost is migrating to S&M** (+68% YoY in Q1'26 — buying volume to defend pass-through), so **watch EBIT margin, not just gross margin.**
- **Scenarios:** base = a manageable multi-hundred-bps drag (≈ where it is). Bear (Sec-232 furniture rises to the delayed 50% on Jan-1-2027 + a new reciprocal regime, Vietnam included) = a severe ~30-45% EBIT dent → EV/EBIT to ~10x, but **solvent, net-cash, FCF-positive — it bends, doesn't break.** Existential risk would require demand collapse + pass-through failing + ~100% China-origin (none of which the data supports).
- **The irreducible gap:** GCT does NOT disclose China-origin GMV % anywhere → the bear can't be precisely sized from filings. **This is why you basket-size, not concentrate.** Dated risks: §122 (10%) expires Jul-24-2026; the Sec-232 furniture step-up to 30-50% is delayed to Jan-1-2027.
