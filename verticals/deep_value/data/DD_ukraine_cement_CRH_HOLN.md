- **(b) DD verdict: WATCH.** Paying ~11x EV/EBITDA and ~18–20x earnings on very likely peak US-construction profitability, with an IIJA funding cliff dated Sept 30, 2026.
- **Prior CONFIRMED, multiple RICHER than "16x."** Trailing P/E ~19.8x; ~18x on 2026 guidance. "16x" is a sell-side forward-adjusted number.

### 2. Cycle position — the trap test (this is the case)
**Americas = ~71% of group EBITDA** (Americas Materials $4,002m + Americas Building $1,474m = $5,476m of $7,681m segment total). The "~75% of net income" framing is right.

**IIJA is cresting, not ramping.** FY2026 is the **final year** of the IIJA (authorization expires **Sept 30, 2026**); after that, formula highway programs revert to pre-IIJA levels absent reauthorization. 2026 is "peak execution" — public highway/street construction decelerating to ~+1% growth off a record base, and a 5-yr reauthorization faces a projected $166bn revenue-outlay gap (Highway Trust Fund insolvency). **At ~11x trailing EV/EBITDA on peak Americas earnings, the stock is fair-to-full, not cheap.**

### 3. Mode B — disconfirming pass (the strongest finding)
**Organic revenue was NEGATIVE in all three segments in FY2025** (CRH's own organic bridge, 8-K Appendix 1):
- Americas Materials: +$961m acquisitions, **−$73m organic**
- Americas Building: +$203m acquisitions, **−$102m organic**
- International: +$1,057m acquisitions, **−$90m organic**

**~100% of CRH's top-line growth was M&A; underlying organic volume flat-to-down (~−$265m).** All organic profit gains from price + cost, and pricing now *decelerating* (cement +1%, aggregates +3–4%). Textbook late-cycle signature. Net debt rose $3.6bn YoY to fund deals (largest: Eco Material) + buyback. Capex $2.7bn.

**Honesty: CLEAN.** Full currency/acquisition/divestiture/organic bridge disclosed by segment; deals named; net debt and 2026 guidance reconciled line-by-line. The headline ("+5% revenue, +11% EBITDA") doesn't diverge from the data once you read the bridge — it's transparently M&A-and-price-driven. The catch isn't dishonesty; it's that headline growth quality is lower than a casual read suggests, and the cycle is late.

### 4. Ukraine — rounding error: confirmed
CRH operates **5 of Ukraine's ~8 cement plants, ~6.75 Mt capacity, soon #1 producer** (Volyn-Cement + YUGCement via Dyckerhoff Ukraine, ~€100m, Oct 2024; ~$80m total wartime investment). Against a $37.4bn / ~$88bn-EV group, Ukraine is **well under 1% of revenue and EBITDA** — immaterial. The reconstruction story is an option, not a thesis.

### 5. Risks / unverifiable
- **Risks:** IIJA reauthorization cliff (2027); flat-to-negative organic volumes; serial-acquirer integration & debt; residential/weather drag; pricing deceleration.
- **Unverifiable:** the reconciliation of CRH's "adjusted FCF ~$5.0bn" vs $2.9bn OCF-less-capex (pin before underwriting FCF yield); 2027+ US infra funding.

### 2. Cycle position (post-spin)
Europe CHF 1,467m / +7.4% (54% of sales, recovering); Latin America CHF 953m / −0.2% (30.6% margin, FX-exposed); Asia/ME/Africa CHF 935m / +14.1%. **More diversified and earlier-cycle than CRH** — guides positive organic 2026 (net sales +3–5%, recurring EBIT +8–10%). On current organic momentum Holcim screens better than CRH's *negative* 2025 organic.

### 3. Mode B — disconfirming pass
- **Post-spin coherence:** Holcim spun off its *highest-growth* asset (Amrize/NA, June 23 2025). Remaining Holcim is lower-growth, more EM/FX-levered, with minority leakage. Stranded-cost / dis-synergy risk from the split is the real Mode-B question.
- **Growth quality:** organic +2.9% net sales is genuine (better than CRH), but still leans on a bolt-on M&A engine (18 acquisitions in 2025).
- **Honesty flag — disclosed, CLEAN but flag:** headline "+5.0% EPS / +3.9% net income" is on a before-impairment-and-divestments basis. **Reported EPS collapsed to CHF 0.70** from non-cash recycling of accumulated currency-translation losses on the Nigeria divestment. Real GAAP charge, non-cash, non-recurring; both figures disclosed plainly → not masking. Use pre-impairment 3.22 for operating valuation; know the reported number is messy.

### 4. Ukraine / CEE — immaterial: confirmed
Holcim has **no material Ukraine play** — exited Russia 2022, no Ukrainian cement footprint of note. **Not a way to play Ukraine reconstruction.**

### 5. Risks / unverifiable
- **Risks:** post-spin overhead/dis-synergy; EM FX translation; minority-interest drag; lower structural growth than pre-spin; high reported-vs-adjusted earnings gap.
- **Unverifiable:** exact recurring EBITDA is *derived* (CHF ~3,989m via the 54% cash-conversion identity); precise post-spin stranded-cost run-rate.

## Bottom line (both names)
Neither is deep value. **Both ~11x EV/EBITDA — fair-to-full for aggregates-heavy building materials at/near a cyclical peak** (historic band ~8–10x trough / 11–13x peak). **CRH = higher-quality business at the fuller price on the more clearly peaking (US/IIJA) cycle** (P/E closer to 18–20x than 16x). **Holcim = lower geographic quality but cheaper on cash (5.4% FCF yield, 0.9x net debt/EBITDA) and earlier-cycle, with post-spin uncertainty and messier reported earnings.** WATCH both; the Ukraine angle is a rounding error for CRH, absent for Holcim.
