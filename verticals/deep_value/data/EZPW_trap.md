## 2. Is the growth structural or a cyclical gold+stress spike? — DECOMPOSED

**(a) Structural / durable — VERIFIED real:**
- **PLO (pawn loans outstanding): $349.4M vs $261.8M = +33.4% YoY.** This is the lending book and the right quality metric.
- **Pawn service charges (recurring lending revenue): +30.4% Q2, +21.5% H1.**
- Same-store strength is disclosed: Latam loan demand +16% (+13% same-store) on larger average loan size + strong demand; US merchandise sales +9% (+7% same-store), sales GM +170bps to 38%. So a real organic core, ~20–30%.
- Store count expanded to ~1,500 across 16 countries.

**(b) Gold-scrap spike — CYCLICAL, low-quality, NON-recurring:**
- **Jewelry scrap sales: $81.2M vs $20.9M in Q2 = +288%** ( +222% H1). This is melting forfeited jewelry for gold content — a direct gold-price windfall, not lending.
- **~33% of the entire YoY gross-profit increase came from jewelry scrap.** The 10-Q attributes it explicitly to "the elevated gold price environment." Gold has run hard into 2026; this line mean-reverts (or compresses) when gold stalls. Treat ~$25–30M of the GP delta as cyclical/non-durable.

**(c) Acquisition — INORGANIC, inflates "growth" optics:**
- Founders One / Simple Management Group (SMG) closed 2026-01-02 (87.7%→100% Founders, 93% SMG by May 2026): +105 stores across 12 countries, new "SMG" reporting segment. Q2 SMG added ~$17.8M merchandise + $19.1M scrap revenue that simply wasn't in the prior-year base. The +98% EBIT/+62% headline conflates organic + acquired + gold. SMG is also early-integration risk (the SMG CEO departed in April 2026; EZCORP "assumed direct operational management").

**Net:** the +98% quarter ≈ (organic ~20-30%) + (gold-scrap windfall) + (3 months of an acquisition vs zero base). Durable run-rate growth is the ~20–30% organic PLO/PSC line, not ~100%.

## 3. Counter-cyclical-hedge value for a rotation basket

**Largely VERIFIED — this IS a genuine diversifier, with one caveat.**
- Mechanism is real: every loan is collateralized by physical goods held in-store, so credit loss is structurally low (downside = re-sell the collateral). Consumer financial stress drives pawn demand; gold strength lifts both collateral values and scrap margins. PLO +33% + larger average loan sizes is consistent with a stressed-consumer tailwind.
- Caveat 1 — **it is currently long gold, not just long stress.** ~1/3 of GP growth is a gold-price windfall that correlates with the macro "fear" trade; in a deflationary/risk-off shock *without* high gold it would do less well. It's a stress+hard-asset hedge, not a pure recession hedge.
- Caveat 2 — **the 2020 COVID record:** FY2020 (the one true modern recession) was an operating LOSS (EBIT −$48M) because lockdowns shut stores and consumers used stimulus to redeem pawns. So "does well in any downturn" is too strong — it does well in *inflationary stressed-consumer* regimes (2022-2026), poorly in a liquidity-flush shutdown.
