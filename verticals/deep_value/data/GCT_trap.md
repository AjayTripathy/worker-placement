The decisive China-small-cap QoE red flags **do not apply** here: Big-Four (KPMG China) audited,
**no VIE**, no financial debt, real net cash, no customer concentration, FY OCF > net income.
The cheapness is real and the cheapest interpretation (China-fraud discount) is wrong. What the bull
"asset-light marketplace" framing **hides** is that 68% of revenue is lower-margin 1P product
reselling (balance-sheet inventory), and gross margin has compressed ~300 bps on tariff/competitive
cost — so it deserves *a* discount, just not this one. Net: mispriced to the downside.

## 3. Tariff / China exposure — quantified, and the margin tells the story

- **Mechanism:** GCT sources from >500 suppliers across Asia (187 sourcing staff in mainland
  China/Vietnam/Malaysia); a large share of GMV and of its own 1P inventory is China-origin furniture
  shipped to US/EU. Section 301 + reciprocal tariffs on Chinese/Vietnamese furniture + de-minimis
  removal raise its sellers' and its own landed cost. This is a **real, direct** exposure and is
  disclosed as a material risk factor (not quantified by GMV-origin %).
- **Where it shows up — gross margin:** 26–27% (2023) → 24–25% (2024) → **23.2–23.9% (last 4
  quarters)**. ~300 bps of compression, now **stabilized** for a full year *through* the 2025 tariff
  escalation. Operating income kept rising (Q1'26 $42.5M = record) because opex leverage offset the
  GM erosion. Tariff is being absorbed, not crushing the model — so far.
- **Active de-risking:** Jan-2026 acquisition of **New Classic Home Furnishings**, a *US-domestic*
  home-furnishings wholesaler ($18.7M) — moves revenue toward tariff-insulated domestic supply. Germany
  product revenue +80% YoY ($51.6M→$92.7M) diversifies the end-market off the US.
- **Signal-to-price latency:** tariff hits land in gross margin with ~1–2 quarter lag; the market has
  already marked GCT down −18% YTD and to the low end of its range, pricing a tariff/China discount
  that the margin data says is, so far, overstated.

## 4. Growth durability — decelerated, partly inorganic, but not faded

- Revenue: 2023 $704M → 2024 **$1,161M (+65%, the Covid-furniture-boom comp)** → 2025 $1,290M
  **(+11%)** → Q1'26 +32% YoY. The "+22% EBIT YoY" in the screen reflects the tail of the 2024 boom;
  the true organic run-rate is low-double-digit, with the Q1 reacceleration **partly the New Classic
  acquisition** (inorganic). Honest read: a ~10–15% grower, not a 30% grower.
- Business mix the bull framing hides: **Q1'26 = $243M (68%) 1P/off-platform PRODUCT reselling vs
  $116M (32%) service** (and only **$5.5M/qtr** is true marketplace commission). It is materially MORE
  capital-intensive / lower-margin than "asset-light B2B marketplace" implies. Takeaway-vs-data
  divergence noted — but disclosed in full in the geography note, so it is a *valuation* input, not a
  lie.

## 5. Mechanism (the honesty-alpha edge here)

- **Masking channel:** category-discount mispricing — the market applies a blanket "China small-cap +
  tariff" haircut (the discount that fits frauds and VIE shells) to a name that is, on primary
  evidence, Big-Four-audited, VIE-free, debt-free, net-cash, cash-converting, and tariff-resilient at
  the margin line. The fear-narrative is doing the work the financials don't support.
- **Signal channel:** auditor identity + VIE note + OCF>NI conversion + the stabilized gross-margin
  series — all in primary filings, none requiring estimation.
- **Latency:** tariff headlines move the price quarters before the margin print; GM has already
  printed flat for a year, so the gap between perceived and realized risk is the edge.

## 6. Entry / fair value (if REAL)

- At 5.1x EV/EBIT and 8.6x P/E with $9.85/sh net cash and 15% FCF yield, the bar is a multiple
  re-rate, not heroic growth. A modest, defensible 8x EV/EBIT on a conservative ~$150M normalized EBIT
  = $1.2B EV + $362M net cash = ~$1.56B equity = **~$42/sh (+31%)**; even holding 5.1x flat, the
  ~15% FCF yield + buybacks (already repurchasing) carries the return.
- **Entry:** current $32 (low end of range) is a reasonable starter. Basket-size it (small-cap China
  single-name + the real tariff/PRC-cash-trapped tails). Add on tariff-headline weakness toward the
  high-$20s.
- **Kill / re-underwrite triggers:** (1) gross margin breaks below ~22% for 2 quarters (tariff
  finally biting); (2) any restatement/auditor change away from KPMG; (3) PRC cash-repatriation
  restriction disclosed; (4) the >10% supplier relationship disrupted; (5) organic (ex-acquisition)
  revenue turns negative.
