# IDT Corporation (NYSE: IDT) — Deep-Value Trap-Filter / SOTP Verdict

**Date:** 2026-06-26 · **Live px (IBKR last):** $57.34 · **Mcap:** ~$1,426M · **EV:** ~$1,169M
**Primary source:** Q3 FY2026 10-Q (period ended 2026-04-30, filed 2026-06-09, accession 0001437749-26-019975) + FY2025 10-K (accession 0001493152-25-016071). July fiscal year.

## VERDICT: FAIR — not a trap, not a cheap-and-quality SOTP either. **PASS for the basket.**

The bull SOTP thesis ("growers worth more than the whole, telecom thrown in free") does **NOT** survive the math on the latest quarter. At a defensible BASE case, sum-of-parts equity is ~$1,263M — roughly **11% BELOW** the $1,426M market cap. You only get a positive SOTP gap with aggressive multiples (BULL = +8%). The market is already paying for the growth segments AND a fair-to-cheap price for the cash-cow telecom. This is a well-run, honestly-disclosed, fully-valued holdco — not a mispriced one.

- Claim → how verified → authority → finding: screen flagged +60% EBIT growth; recomputed from the 10-Q income statement → SEC 10-Q condensed consolidated statements of income → **consolidated income from operations grew +12.0% YoY** (Q3: $29.8M vs $26.6M; 9-mo: $88.0M vs $78.5M). The "+60%" is a stale single-quarter-base artifact, not the run-rate. Durability: the +12% is real and broad-based, not one-time, but it is 12% — not 60%.

Growth is **real but more modest and more decelerating** than the bull frame ("each ~20-40%"):
- **NRS** is the gem: +21% rev, 22% op margin, 39.3k active POS terminals (vs 35.6k), rising recurring revenue/terminal. High-quality recurring-payments compounder.
- **BOSS Money** (the bulk of Fintech): $114.2M of $128.9M 9-mo Fintech rev, +12.4% YoY and **decelerating**; Fintech SG&A rose to 44.8% of rev (Q3) from 41.5% — card-processing/chargeback margin pressure. Not the 30-40% grower the thesis implies.
- **net2phone** is the laggard: only **+10%** revenue, single-digit op margin, heavy D&A. The AI repositioning is early; not yet a premium SaaS comp.
- **TradComm** is NOT just melting — it's **flat (+0.2% rev)** and still throws off ~$62M EBIT. It's a cash cow stabilizing, not collapsing. This actually cuts AGAINST a "tiny growth tail" frame: the legacy is 57% of EBIT.

**The SOTP does not materially exceed mcap.** Even generous BASE multiples land 11% below the current price. The bull case needs NRS at 16x EBITDA and the telecom at 4x just to clear +8%.

### Reverse cross-check (the killer):
At BASE multiples for the three growth segments (net of minority = ~$763M EV), the market is implying **Traditional Comms at ~$405M EV = 5.8x EBITDA / 6.5x EBIT.** That is a **fair-to-slightly-cheap** price for a flat cash cow — it is NOT being thrown in free. The bull thesis ("telecom ~free") is arithmetically false at today's price.

## 4. Governance / spin-off optionality
- **Founder-controlled:** Howard Jonas controls via super-voting Class A (1.57M Class A vs 23.3M Class B). Long, genuine track record of value-unlocking spin-offs: Zedge (2016), Rafael Holdings (2018), IDW Media, Genie Energy. This is the real call option — a future NRS or net2phone separation could re-rate those segments to standalone multiples.
- **BUT:** a prior net2phone SPAC/de-SPAC was explored and not consummated; NRS has a redeemable minority (Alta Fox 2.5%, redemption right around the 5th anniversary of Sept-2021) signaling a possible future liquidity event. Optionality is real but unscheduled — you are not paid to wait at today's price (no SOTP discount to harvest).
- Controlled-company governance = the usual minority-shareholder caveat; capital allocation has historically been shareholder-friendly (buybacks: treasury grew to $163.4M, +$19.5M in 9mo).

## 5. Trap hypotheses — adjudicated
- (a) "Consolidated 11.9x isn't cheap, telecom drags the blend" → **CONFIRMED as the operative reality.** 10.6x EV/EBIT is fair, not cheap; the blend is dominated by a flat (not collapsing) telecom.
- (b) "+60% EBIT one-time or durable" → the +60% is **REFUTED** (real number is +12%); the +12% is durable/broad but modest.
- Bull SOTP → **REFUTED at current price.** Growth real, but priced.

## What would flip it to REAL (entry/FV if it cheapens)
- **Below ~$48** (BASE SOTP $51 with margin of safety) the SOTP discount opens and you'd own NRS + Fintech + net2phone with the cash cow at a genuine discount → BUY candidate.
- A confirmed **NRS spin/IPO** at >12x EBITDA, or net2phone reaccelerating to >20% with margin expansion, re-rates the whole.
- At **$57**, FAIR. No SOTP edge. Honest, high-quality, fully-priced. **Decline for a cheap-and-quality rotation basket** on price; keep on the watchlist for a pullback or a spin catalyst.
