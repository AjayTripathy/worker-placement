## The structural finding
There is **no name that is both genuinely HNW *and* a diversifier** — it's a hard trade-off:
- **Orthogonal underwriters** (Chubb, Hiscox; factor-orthogonality ~85) → but HNW is only **8–12%** of them.
- **Real HNW franchises** (Northern Trust ~45%) → but they're **equity-market beta**, so they amplify an equity book rather than hedge it.
- **Alt-managers** (BX, APO) → low on both, plus a private-credit tail.
