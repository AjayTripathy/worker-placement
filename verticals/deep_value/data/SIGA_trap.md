The cash the screen credits is already ~$43M smaller than the 3/31 number.

- **EV/EBIT — the trap is in the denominator:**

  - FY2025 EBIT **$23.7M** → EV/EBIT **5.7x**.

**Finding: REFUTED as a "cheap-and-real" thesis.** The 6.6x is arithmetically correct but is struck on collapsing, lumpy government-procurement EBIT. The "10% FCF yield" is a backward-looking artifact of harvested BARDA deliveries, not a forward run-rate.

## 2. EBIT is a peak-procurement spike, not a run-rate (Mode B — lead finding)

Revenue ($M): 2021 133.7 / 2022 110.8 / 2023 139.9 / 2024 138.7 / **2025 94.6** / Q1-26 **6.24** (Q1-25 was 7.04).
EBIT ($M): 2022 42.7 / 2023 83.6 / 2024 70.0 / **2025 23.7** / **Q1-26 −5.3**.

This is not an annuity — it is episodic delivery against discrete option exercises. 2018 alone booked $477M revenue / $355M EBIT (single FDA-approval-triggered stockpile delivery). The 2022–24 plateau was mpox-outbreak-driven international + completing the 19C oral deliveries. Both drivers are now spent (below).

## 3. The order book — the 19C BARDA contract is essentially fully harvested (decisive)

From the 10-Q 19C note (authority: SEC filing, contract as amended thru 6/3/25):
- Contract contemplates **~$630M total**. Of that: $79.2M base period, **$545.2M exercised options**, and **only $5.6M unexercised options remain** — and management says it "currently does not expect [them] to be required."
- **Oral TPOXX: $450.2M cumulative delivered and accepted against the $450.2M exercised-option cap → fully delivered.** There is no remaining contracted oral procurement under 19C.
- IV TPOXX: all three IV BDS options and all three IV FDP options already exercised; $61.4M of $76.8M cumulatively delivered/set-aside. Tail is small.
- Deferred revenue on the balance sheet is just **$8.9M** — that is the visible contracted backlog left to recognize. There is no large funded backlog.

**The "BARDA always re-orders" annuity is a hope, not a contract.** A *future* replenishment buy would require a NEW BARDA procurement contract that does not currently exist in the filings. UNVERIFIABLE whether/when one comes — and **UNVERIFIABLE ≠ clean**; you cannot underwrite EBIT off an option BARDA holds in its sole discretion and that isn't on paper.

## 4. International (the mpox kicker) has gone to zero

## 5. Single-product, single-buyer binary — confirmed, and the catalysts are optional

- One product (tecovirimat, oral + IV), one dominant buyer (US SNS via BARDA). Storefront/whale connectors N/A (buyer is disclosed = BARDA; no undisclosed-customer masking — this is honestly disclosed concentration, not a hidden tell).
- Forward optionality is real but unfunded: smallpox-PEP label expansion — targeting a Supplemental NDA "within the next twelve months," gated on ongoing immunogenicity sample analyses (not yet filed); pediatric formulation (BARDA-funded dev, +$13.2M added 6/3/25). These could seed a future SNS buy but are call options, not run-rate.

## 6. The dividend is a cash-liquidation, not earnings-funded

- SIGA pays ONE ~$43M special dividend/yr: 2023 $32.1M, 2024 $43.1M, 2025 $43.5M, 2026 $0.60/sh = $43.0M (paid 4/23/26).
- FY2025 net income was **$23.3M** vs the $43.5M dividend → **payout ≈ 1.9x earnings**, funded by drawing the cash balance (155.0M 12/25 → 145.6M 3/26 → ~102M post-dividend). The "special dividend" is partly **returning the harvested-procurement cash pile**, not distributing recurring free cash flow. At negative run-rate EBIT, continued $43M/yr dividends are a melting ice cube absent a new BARDA order.
- New tell: the 2026-06-10 8-K authorized **+6.5M shares** under the stock plan (~9% of shares outstanding) — potential dilution overhang.

## VERDICT — TRAP

Honesty-alpha read: SIGA **discloses all of this cleanly** (the contract note literally states the oral book is fully delivered and only $5.6M of unexercised options remain "not expected to be required"). There is no marketing-vs-data divergence — management isn't lying. **The trap is in the SCREEN, not in SIGA's disclosure:** a TTM/stale-annual EV/EBIT denominator manufactured a "6.6x quality cheap" tag on a business whose contracted backlog is essentially exhausted and whose current quarterly run-rate is an operating loss. This is the same failure mode DCGO flagged for the deep-value finder (stale-annual data manufactures the bargain). For a "quality small-cap-value, real-bargains" basket: **EXCLUDE.** It is not a quality compounder; it is a gov-procurement liquidation with embedded, unfunded re-order optionality.
