## 4. Weichai control / related-party — discount is JUSTIFIED, not overdone

- **Controlled company (Nasdaq Rule 5615(c)(1)).** Weichai (China; Weichai America = wholly-owned sub of Weichai Power Co. Ltd.) directly owns ~46% and, via irrevocable proxies/voting agreements over the Founders' shares (Shareholders Agreement 3/20/2017), **controls >50% of the vote** (DEF 14A 2026-06-09). PSIX claims the controlled-company exemption from independent-board and independent-comp-committee requirements — i.e., minority holders have weak governance protection by design.
- **Recurring cash leak to the parent:** a Feb-2025 "MOR" management agreement requires PSIX to pay Weichai **1.75% of gross revenues** on certain Weichai-built engines, quarterly, through Dec-2029.
- **Take-or-pay to a Weichai affiliate (SWIEC):** annual minimum purchase requirements of ~$49.9M (2026/27) rising to ~$95.1M (2028/29), **$290.1M total** — a fixed commitment to buy parent-affiliated supply regardless of demand, a real margin/working-capital risk if PSIX volumes fall (which they now are).
- **Control-contingent debt:** the $135M revolver (extended to 7/30/2027) has a covenant triggered if the majority shareholder holds <50% of equity — financing is entangled with the parent's stake.

This is a discount-for-a-reason: related-party supply dependence, a gross-revenue royalty to the controller, a $290M take-or-pay, and a board the controller can populate. The market is not over-applying the discount; it is pricing genuine minority-shareholder subordination.

- **Latency:** screens run on TTM/annual lag; the inflection already printed in Q1'26 and the price already round-tripped −33% YTD. The data is visible; the screen just hasn't caught up to the quarter.
