## (3) Deal-cyclicality exposure — the real residual risk (contained, disclosed)

- The cyclical leg is **CM-CCM transactional + Venue**, tied to IPO/M&A/SPAC/debt volumes. The 10-Q explicitly flags that **Q4 2025 US gov't shutdowns disrupted IPO/M&A.** This is genuine cyclicality and the honest disclosure of it.
- BUT: the company has structurally de-risked it — recurring software (ActiveDisclosure/Arc subscriptions) + compliance filing (quarterly/annual reporting cycle, non-discretionary) + counter-cyclical bankruptcy/restructuring document tools. Management states the strategy is "driving annual recurring revenue to mitigate market volatility."
- Q1'26 detail confirms the cycle is currently *soft, not collapsing*: print/distribution rose on "higher capital markets transactional volumes" while tech-enabled services fell on "lower capital markets transactional volumes" — i.e. deal mix is shifting but transactional revenue isn't in freefall.
