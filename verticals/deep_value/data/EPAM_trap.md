## (2) The two trap questions

### (a) AI disruption — VERIFIED as net-positive SO FAR, but it is the genuine open risk
**Bull evidence (real, from filings/PR):**
- **>$125M "pure AI revenue" in Q1-2026** (~9% of revenue), with five consecutive quarters of double-digit *sequential* AI revenue growth.
- Multi-year **Anthropic partnership**; 20,000 employees trained, 1,300 certified, targeting 10,000 certifications by end-2026 → EPAM is positioning as the AI-*implementation* arm enterprises hire, the opposite of the UPWK gig-disruption fear.
- Non-GAAP operating margin 14.3% and non-GAAP EPS +18.7% YoY — AI tooling is helping EPAM's *own* delivery efficiency (utilization program), not (yet) cannibalizing its revenue.

**Bear evidence (the disconfirming hunt):**
- Organic growth is still only 2.5–5.0% — if AI were a clear demand tailwind for a premium engineering firm, you'd expect organic acceleration, not a guide-down. The AI revenue is growing but is NOT yet large enough to offset softness in legacy staff-augmentation work.
- **Delivery headcount FELL** — ~56,500 delivery pros at 3/31/26, −0.2% q/q, under an active **2025 Cost Optimization Program** (workforce reductions, ~$13M more charges into Q2-26). Falling headcount alongside rising AI revenue is consistent with *both* the bull ("more revenue per head via AI") and the bear ("AI compresses billable hours per project"). **This is the unresolved crux — UNVERIFIABLE which dominates over a multi-year horizon.**
- Pricing/utilization disclosures are not granular enough in the public filings to confirm bill-rate erosion either way → **UNVERIFIABLE.**

**Net:** AI is a **measured net-positive today** (>$125M and accelerating, margin-accretive to EPAM's own delivery). The *secular* question — does GenAI eventually shrink the labor-arbitrage body of work faster than AI-implementation demand grows? — **cannot be answered from the data and is the single biggest reason this is not a clean BUY.**

### (b) Delivery geography — VERIFIED de-risked
- **India is now the LARGEST delivery location** (~12,200 delivery professionals at 12/31/25, +2,150 added in 2025 and still growing). The Belarus/Ukraine/Russia concentration that sank the 2022 thesis is materially diversified.
- Ukraine operations continue "at productivity consistent with pre-attack levels"; $100M humanitarian commitment ~drawn down ($7.1M remaining). $60.4M PP&E still in Russia (impaired/de-emphasized).
- Geography risk is **largely resolved** — this leg of the bear is mostly closed. CLEAN disclosure.

### Client concentration — CLEAN
No client >10% of segment revenue (FY25/24/23). Some softness flagged in Business Information & Media from two former top-10 clients — disclosed, not hidden.
