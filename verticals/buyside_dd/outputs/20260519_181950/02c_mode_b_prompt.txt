VERIFICATION TOOLS — f_library predicates the framework knows how to check:
- `acquired_at_arms_length` — If sponsor or affiliate sold to themselves at uplift, it's a related-party fee extraction
- `acquired_at_price` — Sponsor's claimed acquisition price should match recorded deed consideration; If MLS shows a recent sale, sponsor's claimed acquisition price should match
- `building_condition_is_disclosed` — Open code violations should be disclosed; surprises here add capex liability
- `charges_rent` — Sponsor's claimed in-place rent should fall within market comparable range for the area+type; If building is rent-stabilized (J-51, 421a, or pre-1974 6+ units), claimed rent must be ≤ legal max
- `filed_form_d_for_direct_safes` — Reg D 506(b)/(c) requires Form D within 15 days of first sale. If the issuer took direct SAFE checks (not via SPV), a parent-entity Form D should exist on EDGAR.
- `led_investment_round` — Named lead investor should appear in: issuer's Form D relatedPersons, publicly-listed portfolio of the lead, or own SAFE document. Total absence is SEVERE — the round may not have closed at the structure represented.
- `legal_status_is_clean` — Sponsor (and affiliates) should have no undisclosed litigation in PACER or state courts
- `lien_status_is_clean` — No mechanic's liens, tax liens, or UCC filings against the property beyond disclosed
- `located_at_address` — If issuer claims a specific factory/office address, the city's permit DB should have at least one permit at that address. Recent permits with industrial scope (electrical service upgrade, mechanical, fire-suppression) corroborate active build-out. Zero permits = SEVERE absence of record.
- `occupancy_is_stable` — High eviction filings inconsistent with claimed stable occupancy
- `operates_industrial_facility` — If sponsor claims a building/project in city X, the city's permit DB should have at least one matching permit. Zero hits across multiple name variants is a SEVERE absence-of-record signal.; Claimed manufacturing facility should appear in OSHA establishment search if it's been operating long enough to be inspected. New/small factories may legitimately have no record (mitigates severity).
- `previously_acquired` — Sponsor's claimed prior acquisitions should be findable in deed records under the LLC's name or known affiliates
- `prior_fund_returned_irr` — Sponsor's claimed prior-fund returns should match Form ADV / SEC filings if registered
- `projects_cash_distribution` — Projected LP distributions shouldn't depend on exit price >2x acquisition or rent growth >market+3%
- `projects_exit_cap_rate` — Projected exit cap rate should be within +/- 1% of market spot for asset+market
- `projects_noi` — Projected NOI per unit should fall within reasonable range for asset class+market
- `property_market_value` — Sponsor's claimed market value shouldn't be wildly above the assessor's estimate
- `property_tax_baseline_is_stable` — If recent transfer occurred, Taxable Value will reset on next assessment (MCL 211.27a). Sponsor's projected taxes must account for this.
- `property_tax_status_is_current` — Property taxes should be paid current; delinquency is a deal flag
- `raise_amount` — Sponsor's claimed raise amount should match the SEC Form D primary doc within 5%.
- `registered_as_entity` — Claimed sponsor entity should appear in state corporate registry. Zero hits = SEVERE.
- `rehab_spend_planned` — Stated rehab budget should be roughly proportional to building permit cost estimates
- `total_paid_to_AHC` — Investor's stated total paid should match the sum of their executed SAFE purchase amounts.
- `valuation_cap` — Claimed valuation cap must match the cap actually written on the executed SAFE. CAUTION: SAFE caps are not market-validated priced valuations — they are forward-looking ceilings.
