## PART 1 — Channel map (how medspas pull peptide/GLP-1 + aesthetics volume)

Medspa = cash-pay consumer front-end. Three revenue rails sharing the same foot traffic:
1. **GLP-1 weight-loss program** — compounded sema/tirzepatide (cheap, shortage-era) OR branded (Wegovy/Zepbound).
   This is the rail under regulatory attack (Part 2).
2. **Peptide therapeutics** — BPC-157, etc. Mostly 503A-compounded / research-chemical gray market; same crackdown vector.
3. **Aesthetics injectables/devices** riding the same traffic — neurotoxins (Botox/Jeuveau/Daxxify),
   dermal fillers (Juvederm/Restylane), biostimulators (Sculptra/Radiesse), body contouring + energy devices (InMode RF).

**Industry structure = the reason public plays are picks-and-shovels.** Med-spa operators are ~81% single-location
owner-operated; only ~3% PE-owned. The roll-ups (Milan Laser/LG+Sixth Street, LaserAway/Ares — exploring sale Jun-2026,
Ideal Image/L Catterton+TPG) are PRIVATE. The one public franchisor, **European Wax Center (EWCZ), is being TAKEN PRIVATE
by General Atlantic (FY25)** — so no durable public medspa-operator long exists. Public exposure = (a) injectable/device
SUPPLIERS and (b) telehealth GLP-1 DISTRIBUTORS.

**The non-obvious durable link — "GLP-1 face."** Rapid GLP-1 weight loss causes midface volume loss (61% of patients;
~7% midface volume loss per 10kg) + skin laxity → drives demand for **biostimulatory fillers (Sculptra/PLLA, Radiesse/CaHA)**,
which the 2025 international consensus now recommends FIRST for weight-loss patients ($850-1,200/vial). 1/3 of physicians
say GLP-1 has INCREASED filler volume in their practice; 60% of GLP-1 aesthetics patients get the drug from a provider who
also does aesthetics (up from 49%). **This demand is independent of GLP-1 legality** — it keys off the weight loss, not the
prescription channel. That is the clean, crackdown-proof beneficiary vein.

## PART 2 — The compounding crackdown (the critical risk)

- Shortage ENDED: tirzepatide off-list late-2024 (503B enforcement discretion to 2025-03-19); semaglutide off-list Feb-2025.
- Off-shortage, 503A pharmacies are barred from "essentially a copy" compounding; the legal basis for cheap compounded GLP-1 is GONE.
- **2026-04-30: FDA proposed to permanently EXCLUDE semaglutide/tirzepatide/liraglutide from the 503B Bulks List** (no clinical
  need) — comment period to 2026-06-29. Closes large-scale bulk compounding even if nominated.
- **Novo v. Hims (filed 2026-02-09)** → DISMISSED after Hims pivoted to a Novo branded collaboration (offers Wegovy/Ozempic).
  ~1.5M Americans on compounded GLP-1 = the recapture pool flowing back to branded (Novo/Lilly + their branded telehealth partners).

**Read:** the crackdown is a Novo/Lilly TAILWIND (leakage recapture) and a medspa/telehealth-compounded HEADWIND.
A clean medspa play must survive the compounded shutdown via (a) aesthetics-injectable demand (GLP-1-legality-independent),
or (b) BRANDED-GLP-1 distribution — NOT compounded-sema arbitrage.
