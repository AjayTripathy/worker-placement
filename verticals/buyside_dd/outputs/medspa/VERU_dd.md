## MODE B — FIRST PRINCIPLES (LEAD) — is this real early-edge, or serial-pivot dilution trap?

**Verdict: NOT a fraud, NOT a serial-pivot dressing — the science here is materially better than the
SARM/serial-pivot prior would predict — BUT the equity is structurally a dilution-gated binary, and
the catalyst the screen called "near-term" is ~Q1-2027, not now. Net: the EDGE is in the warrant
structure and entry price, not in an "undiscovered" data divergence.**

The four disconfirmation tests the brief asked for:

**(a) Does enobosarm's actual prior muscle data support efficacy, or is it hope?**
Partly supported, and better than the bear frame assumes. Enobosarm (GTx-024 / ostarine) has a
*positive* elderly LBM/physical-function Phase 2 (PMC3177038) and the cancer-cachexia POWER trials
(PMC4853438) that hit LBM but **missed the co-primary physical-function (stair-climb) bar** — that
miss is exactly why FDA never approved it for cachexia. The new QUALITY (NCT06282458) read out
genuinely positive on BOTH axes for the 3mg dose: 99.1% relative reduction in lean-mass loss
(p<0.001) AND stair-climb power preservation (16% vs placebo decline, p=0.0066). The 6mg dose missed
physical function (p=0.0505) — honestly disclosed. **This is the strongest enobosarm muscle dataset
in 15 years, and it's the first time the LBM AND function endpoints both cleared in the same trial.**
So "just hope" is REFUTED — but note enobosarm has hit LBM before and still failed to get a label;
LBM is the easy endpoint, function is the hard one, and the historical class pattern is LBM-yes /
function-marginal.

**(b) Is the SARM class safety/regulatory path real?**
Mixed, leans favorable here. FDA has been hostile to SARMs broadly (DEA scheduling pressure,
hepatotoxicity, recreational-abuse stigma). But the Sept-2025 FDA meeting (verified below) gave Veru
an explicit, on-record acceptable PRIMARY ENDPOINT (incremental weight loss on top of GLP-1) and
confirmed 3mg as the dose — that is concrete regulatory clarity, not spin. QUALITY safety was clean
(one transient mild ALT bump, no treatment-related SAEs, FEWER GI AEs than semaglutide alone).
**Caveat that survives:** the FDA agreed weight-loss-delta is an acceptable endpoint — that REFRAMES
the program away from the muscle/function story the equity is marketed on. The approvable claim FDA
endorsed is "more weight loss," not "better quality weight loss." That is a subtle
**takeaway-vs-data divergence**: the marketed identity (muscle preservation) ≠ the regulatory pathway
(incremental fat/weight loss). Encode it.

**(c) Is the "undiscovered / early" read real, or neglected-because-burned?**
Largely **neglected-because-burned, and not actually early.** VERU traded ~$20+ on the
sabizabulin/COVID-19 EUA hype and collapsed when FDA's advisory committee declined it (2022–23); it
also wrote off ENTADFI/BPH and FC2/sexual-health (revenue went to $0 by FY2025 — verified). The
market has been burned twice and discounts management's narrative reflexively. That is a real
source of mispricing — but it is NOT "undiscovered": QUALITY topline, the FDA-clarity PR, the $25M
financing, and PLATEAU enrollment are all public and covered (HCPLive, BioSpace, multiple trade
press). The screen's "undiscovered divergence" label is **UNVERIFIABLE / likely overstated** — this
is a known, covered, twice-burned name, not a dark corner.

**(d) Does the cap table destroy equity upside before the catalyst — even if the science works?**
**This is the decisive finding.** The Oct-2025 raise (424B5, verified) layered on a heavy warrant
structure on top of a 16.05M base. As-converted the share count roughly **2.6×'s to ~41.8M**, and
the Series A/B warrants strike at **$3.00 — i.e., the entire move above $3 hands ~16.8M shares of
supply to warrant holders.** So even a successful interim that re-rates the stock pulls warrant
exercise (good: ~$50M of non-dilutive-to-cash funding for Phase 3) but caps per-share upside hard.
The structure is engineered so the company gets funded on good news and the equity holder eats the
dilution — classic micro-cap binary. **The dilution math materially blunts the bull case even if the
science wins.**

**Mode-B bottom line:** The honest grade is **CLEAN on disclosure, CAUTION on structure.** Veru is
NOT lying — QUALITY is real, the FDA clarity is real, the burn and dilution are fully disclosed. But
the screen's framing ("clean undiscovered near-term divergence, $0.25B mcap") diverges from the
primary record on three of four load-bearing inputs (mcap wrong, catalyst is ~9 months out not
"near-term," and it's covered not undiscovered). The exploitable edge that DOES survive is narrow:
**twice-burned-name discount × genuinely improved Phase 2b data × entry below the $3.00 warrant wall.**
