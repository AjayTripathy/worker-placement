## MODE B — First-principles disconfirmation (LEAD)

**The cycle, from the tape (IBKR 2-yr monthly):** Jan-2025 ~$341 → **Feb-2025 crash to $232 (intra-month low $197)** on the 2025 destocking/SmartDose guide-down → Apr-2025 low **~$187** → recovered to **$346.56 now**, nearly a double off the bottom, ~1.6% under the 52-wk high. **You are buying the top of the post-crash recovery, not a dislocation.**

**(a) Is ~39x pricing in a tailwind that's already over-earned?** Partly. The "39x" is **forward on RAISED adj-EPS** ($8.40–8.75; 346.56/8.575 = **40.4x fwd**), not trailing. Trailing GAAP P/E is **51x** (TTM thru Q1-26 ~46x). Earnings are NOT depressed — they are re-accelerating: Q1-26 adj-EPS +46.9%, FY26 guide raised one quarter after being set. So the multiple is not optically inflated by a trough; it's a genuine ~40x premium for ~7–9% organic + mix-accretive margin. That premium is defensible on moat but leaves **no margin of safety at spot** — any organic-growth wobble re-rates a 40x name hard (Feb-2025 was −40% in a month).

**(b) Is destocking truly over or could it recur?** **Over, and confirmed by hard data, not assertion.** Q1-26: +21.0% reported / **+15.3% organic**, HVP Components $409.3M +29.6% (+22.6% organic), and management RAISED FY26 organic to 7–9% (from 5–7% set in Feb). The masking-channel concern (a destocking-recovery story told before the volumes arrive) is **refuted** — the volumes are in the print. *Recurrence risk is structural, not cyclical:* a future demand air-pocket (e.g., a GLP-1 customer over-builds again) could repeat the 2024-25 inventory whipsaw. WST's own forward-looking language still flags "customers' changing inventory requirements" as a risk. So destocking-over is VERIFIED for now; destocking-can-recur is a permanent feature of a component supplier to a small number of fast-ramping customers.

**(c) Oral-GLP-1 / injection-frequency — the real structural tail. This is the catch, and WST discloses it honestly.** The 10-K risk factor states verbatim: *"…depends to a substantial extent on customers' continued sales and development of products that are delivered by injection, such as GLP-1s. If our customers… opt for products delivered via alternative means, such as oral GLP-1s…"* — WST **names oral GLP-1 as the threat itself**. Mechanism of exposure: WST's economics are per-injection-event (one elastomer closure + plunger per pen/vial fill). The threat is two-pronged: (i) **oral GLP-1** (Lilly orforglipron, Pfizer/Novo orals) removes the injectable container entirely; (ii) **lower-frequency/higher-concentration dosing** (monthly vs weekly) cuts unit count per patient-year. Tell that WST is repositioning ahead of this: it is **selling the SmartDose 3.5mL On-Body wearable-injector platform to AbbVie (~$112.5M, mid-2026 close)** — divesting a device format rather than doubling down. This is honest disclosure (CLEAN on the honesty axis), but it is a genuine multi-year demand-elasticity risk that a 40x multiple is not discounting. **Honesty grade: CLEAN — marketed takeaway (every-pen tailwind) does not diverge from the data; the company itself prints the oral-GLP-1 caveat in its risk factors.**

**(d) Customer concentration — the undisclosed whale, and it is rising fast.** Top customer = **15.8% of consolidated sales ($485.9M) in 2025**, up from 12-ish% in 2024 and **10.9% ($322.1M) in 2023** — a ~$164M / 5pp jump in two years, "contributing to net sales in both Proprietary and Contract-Manufactured segments." Top-10 = 47.6%. The customer is **NOT named** — and the trajectory (a single account scaling with the GLP-1 ramp, spanning components + contract device assembly) is the textbook undisclosed-concentration whale. Almost certainly **Novo Nordisk or Eli Lilly**. The risk is symmetric to the upside: this account is the operating-leverage engine AND the single point of failure if that customer in-sources, dual-sources, or shifts to oral.

**Synthesis:** Quality compounder with a verified, mix-accretive GLP-1 tailwind, a real qualification moat, net cash, and clean honesty — but trading at a no-margin-of-safety ~40x fwd / ~46x trailing, ~1.6% off its high, with a disclosed oral-GLP-1 demand-elasticity tail and a fast-rising unnamed ~16% customer. **Own it on a pullback, not at the print.**
