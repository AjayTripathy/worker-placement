The screen flagged INMD as "cyclical deteriorating, GLP-1 thesis not in numbers." That framing is
now stale. **On June 15, 2026, the CEO-led group (Moshe Mizrahy, via M.N. Business Strategy Ltd.)
delivered a non-binding offer to take InMode private at $16.20/share in cash**, no financing

So the question is no longer purely "trough value vs trap." It is now: **merger-arb spread on a
controlled-company MBO**, with the standalone value case as the downside if the deal breaks.

- Stock traded $13.21–$13.57 through June 15–23 (offer already delivered/received) — **no pre-leak
  run-up**. It gapped $13.35 → $14.79 on **June 24** (848K vol vs ~120K normal) only when the
  company publicly confirmed. Clean repricing, not a crowded/anticipated event.
- **Current spread to offer: ($16.20 / $14.81) − 1 = +9.4%.** That is a wide, not a tight, arb
  spread — the market is pricing meaningful deal/price-cut risk (CEO is the buyer; he sets the
  standalone price by controlling the business and has every incentive to lowball).

## MODE A — verify the bull

1. **Cash-rich + buyback floor — VERIFIED.** $537.2M cash, zero debt, $8.41/share. Buyback is real
   and aggressive: $127.4M returned in 2025, plus a new 10% authorization (Mar 13, 2026) under which
   2.55M shares were bought in Q1'26 at avg $13.65 ($34.8M), and $52.7M / 3.86M shares YTD. Diluted
   share count fell 69.4M → 63.9M YoY. **Downside cushion is genuine, not narrative.**
2. **Margin/FCF resilience — PARTIALLY VERIFIED, deteriorating.** FY25 op cash $85.3M (down from
   $132.7M FY24). Gross margin still 75–79%. BUT GAAP operating margin collapsed Q1'26 to 12% (from
   20% Q1'25); net income −36% YoY despite +5% revenue — cost of goods up + the new NA sales
   structure + new foreign subsidiaries are eating the model. Profitability "lower than expected"
   per the CEO. Resilience is fading, not holding.
3. **Q1'26 revenue +5% YoY ($82.0M vs $77.9M)** — the FY25 trough (−6% to $370.5M) is moderating,
   with the US "contributing meaningfully." Early stabilization is real at the top line.
4. **GLP-1 body-contouring / skin-laxity tailwind — UNVERIFIABLE / purely narrative.** I found NO
   primary-source evidence in bookings, guidance, or segment commentary tying demand to GLP-1
   skin-laxity. Management's own framing is generic ("demand may be deferred, will not be
   diminished") — no GLP-1 attribution at all. FY26 guide is FLAT-to-DOWN ($365–375M vs $370.5M),
   which is the opposite of a tailwind showing up in numbers. **The GLP-1-body angle is a thesis
   the company is not even marketing. Flag: UNVERIFIABLE — not clean.**

## MODE B — disconfirm (lead with this; the framing hid it)

1. **FY24 earnings were inflated by a one-time tax benefit.** FY24 GAAP net income ($181.3M) >
   non-GAAP ($142.7M) — backwards, the tell of a large non-recurring tax credit. So the "−6% revenue
   / EPS $2.25 → $1.43" decline overstates operating deterioration on the revenue line but the
   **clean earnings base is lower than the GAAP headline implies** — the trap-grade question
   (peak-earnings optics) partially applies.
2. **Devices are capex-cyclical, one-time big-ticket sales.** Recurring consumables+service is only
   **~22% of revenue** ($81.4M of $370.5M FY25). 78% is discretionary capital equipment that
   med-spas defer in a soft-consumer / high-rate environment. This is structurally NOT a razor/blade
   recurring model — the bear's core point stands.
3. **Governance / disclosure red flags cluster — this is the honesty-alpha catch:**
   - CFO Yair Malca resigned effective immediately (May 5, 2026); Chairman Dr. Anghel resigned same
     window; a third CFO signs the June filings (Moshik Itzkovich). Churn at the top during a
     take-under.
   - **Pending securities class action** (C.D. Cal., Cement Masons v. InMode) alleging false
     statements on "sales practices and financial outlook" — court already let 5 of 24 statements
     survive dismissal (Sept 12, 2025) and plaintiffs repled. Live litigation risk on disclosure
     integrity.
   - **The buyer is the CEO.** A controlled-company MBO at a 57%-cash discount is the textbook
     conflicted-insider take-under: management controls the standalone narrative (cut guidance,
     blame macro, churn the C-suite) and then offers to buy the whole thing cheap. The $16.20 offer
     is only ~1.0x EV/Sales and barely above the cash + a low-single-digit multiple on the stub.
4. **New IP threat to the flagship.** Serendia LLC sued (June 22, 2026, C.D. Cal.) + filed an **ITC
   complaint seeking a limited exclusion order** on importation of **Morpheus8** microneedling
   devices — InMode's marquee product. An ITC import ban is a tail risk to the core franchise.
5. **Melting-ice-cube-with-cash vs trough:** Q1 +5% argues "trough." Flat FY26 guide + margin
   collapse + 78% non-recurring + flagship IP/ITC risk argue the operating business is structurally
   soft. Net read: a **real trough with a deteriorating operating model**, where the cash — not the
   business — is what's being fought over.
