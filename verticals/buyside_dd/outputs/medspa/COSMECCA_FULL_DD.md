## 1. Forensic financials & accounting quality — CLEAN, two tells to monitor

**Claim:** ~13% operating margin, fast growth, sound books.
**How verified:** Recomputed from DART primary filings (FY25 annual rcpNo 20260330001688; audit report 20260319901999; Q1-26 20260515001672) + FnGuide.
**Finding:**
- **Audit: unqualified (적정) FY23-25**, auditor 다산 (Dasan) — a **regulator-*designated* auditor** (mandatory rotation), *more* independent than a company-appointed one. ICFR effective, no restatement, no going-concern.
- **The margin is operating leverage on idle capacity, not a one-off.** Domestic OPM went 3.4%→8.5%→10.4%→~11% purely on rising utilization off a **<50% base** (post-IPO capex created stranded fixed cost; filling it is the whole margin story). **But 13% is a *blended* figure** — the 14.9% Q3'25 print was a seasonal high; Q4'25 and Q1'26 printed ~11.8%. Honest read: **12-13%, mid-cycle, with utilization upside — not a confirmed 14%+ floor.**
- **Two forensic tells (both disclosed, neither fatal):**
  1. **Receivables building ~1.8× faster than revenue** — DSO ~82→93 days; AR +39% vs revenue +22% in FY25. This is exactly where the auditor placed its *sole* Key Audit Matter (revenue recognition). **Mitigant:** bad-debt provisioning is *not* spiking (no realized credit deterioration yet). A trend-to-watch on the Q2 print, not a current alarm.
  2. **A ~21% minority-interest leak** (Englewood's other owners take ~₩12B/yr of consolidated net income). **But** the Mar-2026 tender (Englewood 50%→66.7%, ~₩43B cash) recaptures ~⅓ of it — *this is why Q1-26 controlling net income (+112.8%) outran operating profit (+78%)*. Genuine, underappreciated per-share accretion the headline OP growth hides.
- **Related-party: CLEAN.** All inter-company trade is with consolidated subsidiaries; "transactions with related parties outside the major-shareholder group: NONE." The founder-entity-siphon risk specific to Korean family ODMs is *absent*.
- **The cautionary number: FCF turned negative in FY25 (−₩6.3B; ~−0.9% yield)** on a capex binge (3.7× D&A) + the receivables build. Debt rose ~40% (short-term +68%) funding capex + the Englewood tender → swing from near-net-cash to ~₩120B net debt. **This is the multiple that should temper how much you pay.**

## 2. Industry & competitive position — a structurally-advantaged *niche* rider of a *cyclical* boom (both true)

**Finding:** Cosmecca is the **smallest of the big-three K-beauty ODMs** (Cosmax & Kolmar are each ~7-14× larger, both crossed ₩2tn in 2025). Its durable edge is narrow but real; its growth *rate* is not durable.
- **The real edge: the only *profitable* dual-region (Korea + US) ODM of the three.** Its US arm (Englewood) runs ~14% OP margin while **Cosmax's US sub is only reaching breakeven and Kolmar's US arm is losing money.** In a tariff/onshoring world, that's the structurally-right asset — peers are *racing to build* what Cosmecca already has.
- **Margin proof:** Q1-26 was the *only* one of the three where operating profit outgrew revenue (+78% vs +56%); Cosmax's operating leverage went the *wrong* way.
- **But no wide moat:** same formula-library/smart-factory toolkit as the two leaders; indie-heavy customer book = *lowest* switching-cost mix (the speed advantage and churn risk are the same coin). The "TSMC of beauty" framing overstates it.
- **#1 forward risk — capacity glut 2027-28:** all three are building simultaneously into the same US thesis (Cosmax >4× capacity by '27, Kolmar 2nd US plant, Cosmecca +21%). If demand decelerates toward the ~1.1× import run-rate while capacity doubles, utilization/price-war risk is real. *Near-term*, Cosmecca's <50% utilization is runway it can fill *before* the glut bites — a timing edge.

## 3. The US / Englewood onshoring option — REAL but SMALL, SLOW, low-margin; the bull over-weights it

**Claim (screen):** Englewood Lab is a unique US-onshoring differentiator capturing the tariff trade.
**Finding:** Real, but materially smaller and slower than the marketing implies — and structurally mis-described.
- **Structural correction:** "Englewood Lab" is *itself a separately KOSDAQ-listed company* (950140) Cosmecca controls (now 66.7%), with three layers. **The actual US plant (Totowa, NJ) is the *slowest* (+12.3%) and *lowest-margin* (6.0% OP) leg.** The growth/margin engine inside "Englewood" is its *Korean* sub (Incheon, +19.8%, 20.6% OP) — i.e. part of the "US onshoring story" is a *Korea* factory making cheap product for US brands (the *opposite* of a tariff dodge).
- **Onshoring magnitude UNVERIFIABLE (≠ clean):** no disclosed onshoring contract win, no capex figure, no capacity-expansion announcement, no pricing premium (US plant OM is only 6%). The one *hard* margin gain (Totowa gross margin 21%→25%) is **automation, not onshoring.**
- **Hiring-velocity check (connector run):** the NJ site's open reqs are overhead/R&D-heavy (production share 0.11) — **steady-state staffing of an already-built automated plant, NOT a capacity ramp.** Actively fails to confirm the bull's "watch NJ hiring as the ramp tell."
- **A real product exists** (the "Ready-To-Go OTC" 5-SKU sunscreen line — "launch a US OTC in 6 months at Totowa") = genuine embedded optionality in the exact post-tariff demand pocket. **Size it ~3-5% of group fair value and as call-option upside on a 2026-27 *named* onshoring contract — not a thesis pillar.** The buy case rests on the Korea export engine + group margin, with Englewood a free option.
