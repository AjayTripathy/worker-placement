## MODE B — first-principles (LEAD)

**Synthesis:** The bull thesis is *directionally right but mis-attributed and mis-timed.* It is a
genuine, diversified, margin-inflecting ODM — but (1) the marketed US-onshoring engine is the SLOW
part, (2) the +56% is a Korea-export print that won't sustain at that rate, and (3) the easy money
(2x run) is already made and already given back. Net: a good business at a fair-to-cheap price, but
**buy the steady compounder, not the parabola.**

| # | Bear point | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | ODM = commoditized low-margin vs Cosmax/Kolmar scale | **Partly REFUTED.** OP margin 13.0% (2025A) / 13.4% (2026E) is *higher* than larger peers in Q1-26 (KBR: "margins diverge"); driver is **domestic utilization <50%** = structural operating-leverage runway, not a cyclical fluke. | Bull-supportive |
| b | +56% is a small-base / one-customer spike | **Half-true → managed.** It IS a low-base spike (Korea unit +91.3%, skincare +106.5%, suncare +173.6% — unsustainable rates). BUT **top-5 customers <35%** (Samsung Securities) = no single-customer dependency. Growth rate de-rates; revenue base is durable. | The real catch (rate, not durability) |
| c | Stock ran hard on a public print — already priced | **CONFIRMED, then unwound.** Peaked 106,400 (Feb-26), now 66,500 (-38%), flat YTD. The momentum is NOT priced *now* — it already de-rated. Entry timing is *favorable*, not late. | Reframes (a)negative→positive |
| d | Indie brands faddish (viral today, nobody tomorrow) | **Mitigated.** <35% top-5 concentration + OGM ("global-spec mfg") model spreads SNS-trend risk across a broad indie book. Residual MoCRA/SNS-volatility risk is real but diversified. | Residual, not fatal |
| e | Census says boom steady-not-surging — is +56% sustainable? | **CONFIRMED — and the key reconciliation.** Your HS3304-99 cross-check (1.13x, low-double-digit, no surge) means the +56% is **share-gain + margin-mix + a low base, riding a ~12% market** — NOT a parabolic end-market. So the *market* supports a steady ~15–20% grower, NOT a repeat +56%. This is the load-bearing Mode-B finding: **underwrite the base case, fade the momentum extrapolation.** | Decisive: caps the bull |

**Mode B verdict:** The marketed takeaway ("fastest-growing ODM + US-onshoring optionality, re-rating
to 10%") **diverges from its own data** on two axes: (1) the US sub grew only +16.9% — the engine is
Korean export, not onshoring; (2) margins already re-rated *past* 10% to 13%. Neither divergence is a
*lie* (all disclosed in the segment cut) — so on the honesty axis this is **CLEAN, not a catch.** The
divergence is a *framing optimism* the screen inherited. Correctly re-attributed, it's still a buy —
just a steady-compounder buy at ~10x forward post-drawdown, not a momentum buy.
