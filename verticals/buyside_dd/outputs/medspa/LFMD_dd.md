## MODE B — first-principles disconfirmation (lead with this)

### B1. Is this a HIMS-style compounded→branded margin trap? NO — the data refutes it.
The bear thesis: when the FDA compounded-semaglutide carve-out closed, telehealth players lost the fat margin on cheap compounded drug and became thin pass-throughs on branded Wegovy ($499 NovoCare) / Zepbound (Lilly Direct). HIMS is the proof case of the trap:

| | **HIMS** (the trap) | **LFMD** (the question) |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin trajectory | **73% → 65%** (Q1-25→Q1-26, **−800 bps**) | **84% → 88%** (Q1-25→Q1-26, **+420 bps**) |
| Q1-26 result | $92M net loss on the GLP-1 shift | $9.6M net loss (continuing ops) |
| Driver | branded-drug pass-through crushed product margin | "favorable mix + lower fulfillment costs" per 10-Q |

LFMD's gross margin went the *wrong way for the bear* — it EXPANDED through the branded transition. **Why the mechanism differs:** HIMS internalized a fat-margin compounded-sema supply chain and is now a thin reseller of branded drug. LFMD's model is a **recurring subscription/consult fee**, with drug fulfillment largely third-party (CenterWell for Novo Wegovy/Ozempic; Lilly Direct for Zepbound). LFMD never booked the drug as high-margin product revenue at HIMS's scale, so losing it didn't compress GM. Nearly all of LFMD's $50M/qtr is recurring subscription revenue (per CFO). **Profit was never "recaptured" because it was never drug-margin profit to begin with — the unit is the subscription, and that GM held at ~86-88%.**

**Verdict on recapture economics: the branded transition is GROSS-MARGIN-SAFE for LFMD.** This is a genuine divergence from HIMS and the load-bearing reason LFMD is not the same screen-out.

### B2. So where IS the problem? CAC and ARPU — not COGS.
The honest bear case relocates from gross margin to two real soft spots:

- **The loss is a marketing-spend loss.** Q1-2026 telehealth S&M = **$29.9M, up 34% YoY on flat revenue** — ~60% of revenue spent on customer acquisition. That flipped adj EBITDA from **+$3.7M (Q1-25) to −$4.5M (Q1-26)**. Management calls it "planned, front-loaded patient acquisition." Believable (they added a record 42K net subs), but it is the entire loss.
- **ARPU per subscriber fell ~21%.** Active subs grew **+26% YoY (290.7K → 365.0K)** while revenue was **flat (−1%)**. Implied revenue/subscriber/quarter dropped from ~$175 to ~$137 (~−21%). This is the *real* GLP-1 fingerprint: the new cohorts (Women's Health, insurance-covered, branded-GLP-1) monetize at lower ARPU than the legacy RexMD men's-health/compounded-weight book. **Subscriber volume is being recaptured; revenue-per-subscriber is not.** That is the divergence the GM-up headline masks.

**Net Mode-B read:** This is NOT a COGS margin trap (that was the screen-out reason for HIMS and it does not apply). It IS a "buy growth now, monetize later" bet with two unproven legs — (a) that the front-loaded CAC converts to LTV and adj EBITDA swings back positive per guidance, and (b) that ARPU stabilizes as insurance-covered and cross-sell layer onto the subscriber base. Both are management promises with one quarter of contrary print.

### B3. Bear's hard facts to respect
- FY2026 guide ($220-230M rev / **+$12-17M adj EBITDA**) requires a ~$16-21M adj-EBITDA swing off a −$4.5M Q1 — back-half-loaded, execution-dependent.
- Accumulated deficit $238M; never GAAP-profitable on continuing ops (FY25 net income was the WorkSimpli gain).
- $35.8M Series A preferred bleeds **$3.1M/yr** in dividends ahead of common; it sits in EV-to-common.
- ATM shelf live ($44.6M available) — dilution optionality if growth spend overruns.

1. **Not the HIMS trap** — the compounded→branded transition EXPANDED gross margin (84→88%) instead of compressing it, because LFMD monetizes a subscription, not drug margin. The screen's margin-trap fear is REFUTED on primary data.

**The one thing that keeps it WATCH not BUY:** Q1-2026 turned adj-EBITDA-negative on a 34% CAC surge AND ARPU/sub fell ~21%. The bull case is entirely "the front-loaded spend converts and H2 adj EBITDA inflects to the +$12-17M guide." That is a one-quarter-old, management-promised inflection with contrary print. **Trigger to upgrade to BUY: Q2-2026 print at/above the −$2M-to-+$1M adj-EBITDA guide AND ARPU stabilizing.** Size accordingly — small contrarian satellite behind EOLS, not a core position.
