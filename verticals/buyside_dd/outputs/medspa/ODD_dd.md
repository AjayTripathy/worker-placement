## MODE B — first-principles disconfirmation (the bear, steelmanned, and where it landed)

- **(a) Paid-social treadmill — CONFIRMED, this is the whole story.** The bear case wasn't hypothetical; it printed. ODD's growth was a function of a single ad platform's auction efficiency, and that platform unilaterally doubled CAC with no notice and no correlation to the market. - **(b) Two-brand concentration — CONFIRMED.** Il Makiage carries the funnel; its CPA is what broke. SpoiledChild/METHODIQ don't yet diversify the acquisition-cost risk because they ride the same paid-social machine.
- **(c) "AI molecule discovery as hype to justify a tech multiple" — REFUTED on substance, PARTIALLY CONFIRMED on timing.** The lab is real (see Verification 2). But it contributed $0 of differentiated revenue through FY25 and the tech multiple it was *priced at* (the stock was a ~$70 / ~$4B+ name) has already been taken away — the market de-rated it to ~0.86x sales. So the "costume" critique is moot: the costume's already been removed by the tape.
- **(d) Dual-class + insider selling — REAL governance flag.** Holtzman holds all Class B, **73.3% of the vote** — public holders cannot influence outcomes. Watch for secondary/lockup selling into any bounce (the IPO was July 2023; standard locks long expired — selling is at-will). Mitigant: insiders are *buying back* (convert + $200M authorization), not dumping, so far.
- **(e) Pricing perfection — REFUTED.** It's pricing *impairment*, near the base case. The risk now is the opposite: pricing-in-permanence of a problem management argues is transient.

**Honesty-alpha read (takeaway-vs-data divergence):** management's takeaway ("transient algorithm dislocation, H2 recovery, 11 straight beats") is reasonably *consistent* with its own disclosed data — they disclosed the CPA index, the loss, the first-order poisoning, and gave conservative guidance they then beat. This is **disclosed bad news, honestly framed → CLEAN on the honesty axis.** The catch here is not a liar; it's a real platform-risk that the bull framing of the *original screen* (owned-IP moat → sticky) hid. The divergence is screen-vs-data, not management-vs-data.
