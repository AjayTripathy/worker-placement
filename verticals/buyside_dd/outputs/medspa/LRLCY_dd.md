## VERDICT: OWN-NOW (build on weakness) — durable compounder, full but NOT stretched multiple

The single most important finding inverts the prompt's framing premise. The bear case as written assumes a
**decelerating** business carrying a **premium-to-history** multiple ("priced for perfection"). The primary data says
the opposite on **both** legs: the business is **RE-ACCELERATING** (Q1 +2.6% → Q2 +3.7% → Q4 +6.0% LFL, three straight
quarters of acceleration) **while the multiple has DE-RATED** (EV/EBITDA ~19.5–20.2x vs its own 10-yr median ~20.5x and a
historical 16x–34x range; the de-rate already happened in 2023–24). You are not paying up into a slowdown — you are paying
a fair-to-slightly-below-median multiple into an inflecting reacceleration. That is the entry a serial compounder rarely gives you.

This is a **WAIT only if** you require a literal margin-of-safety discount to median, which a franchise of this quality
does not offer outside a market dislocation. For a quality-anchor slot, **fair value IS the entry** here — own it, and use
any 52-wk-range pullback toward the entry band to add.

## MODE B FIRST (lead) — steelman the bear, then test each leg against primary data

**Bear leg (a): "~30x multiple priced for a growth rate the company no longer delivers; decel ~11%→mid-single while multiple stays full."**
→ **REFUTED on the multiple, REFRAMED on growth.**
- Multiple: fwd P/E ~27.8x, EV/EBITDA ~19.5–20.2x. EV/EBITDA is **~5% BELOW the 10-yr median (20.5x)**, range 15.8x–34x.
  The de-rate from the ~34x covid-peak to ~20x already occurred. The premium-to-perfection compression is **in the past, not ahead.**
- Growth: yes it decelerated from post-covid low-double-digit to FY25 **+4.0% LFL** — but the **trough is behind it.**
  Quarterly LFL: **Q1 +2.6% → Q2 +3.7% → Q3 (accel) → Q4 +6.0%.** The multiple is not pricing perfection against a falling
  line; it's pricing ~4-5% against a line that is **turning up.** Bear leg as written is stale.

**Bear leg (b): "China is STRUCTURALLY weaker, not cyclical."** → **PARTLY CONCEDED, but de-risked by the company's own actions.**
- North Asia FY25 **+0.5% LFL / -2.2% reported**; travel-retail (Hainan) still impaired. China is genuinely a structural
  step-down from the boom years — CONCEDED, this is real and the company itself is reducing dependence.
- BUT mainland China **returned to growth** in H2-25 and the selective/prestige market re-inflected; Luxe outperformed there.
  And L'Oréal has deliberately re-weighted toward the **US, emerging markets, and Europe** (e-commerce >30% of sales). China
  is now a **drag that is shrinking as a share of the story**, not the load-bearing engine. UNVERIFIABLE whether China
  re-accelerates durably — treat as a permanent slower contributor, not a returning growth driver. (≠ clean, but ≠ thesis-breaking.)

**Bear leg (c): "the derm-beauty air-pocket hits CeraVe/La Roche-Posay — the supposed engine."** → **REFUTED. This is the decisive catch.**
- This is exactly where the prompt expected the GALD-style air-pocket to surface. It did NOT.
- Dermatological Beauty FY25: **+5.5% LFL**, **26.1% operating margin (highest of the 4 divisions)**, €7.20B sales.
- **Q4 Dermatological Beauty +11.5% LFL — DOUBLE-DIGIT, the strongest-momentum division exiting the year.**
- **SkinCeuticals crossed €1B → now 3 brands >€1B (La Roche-Posay, CeraVe, SkinCeuticals).** CeraVe turned around
  (Intensive Moisturising Lotion; strong in China/EM). La Roche-Posay = #1 group growth contributor (Cicaplast, Melasyl).
- **GALD cross-read (we own the comparison — L'Oréal holds 20% of Galderma):** the air-pocket our GALD deep-dive found is in
  **injectable aesthetics / neuromodulators** (procedure-driven, discretionary, weight-loss-adjacent). L'Oréal's
  Dermatological Beauty is **dermo-COSMETICS (daily medical-grade skincare)** — a structurally more resilient, repeat-purchase,
  lower-ticket category. Galderma itself still grew skincare +8% and aesthetics +10.5% in 9M-25. **The category the bear feared
  is adjacent, not the same;** the marketed "engine" claim holds against the data. This is the honesty-alpha result:
  **takeaway (engine intact & accelerating) does NOT diverge from the data — CONSISTENT.**

**Bear leg (d): "€388 near 52w highs, growth slowing = no margin of safety."** → **HALF-TRUE, correctly priced.**
- True you're at ~73% up the range with no discount-to-median. But "near highs" coincides with **re-acceleration + a de-rated
  multiple**, so the absence of a screaming discount is the *correct* price for the quality, not a warning. The honest
  margin-of-safety here is **business durability + a non-stretched multiple**, not a cheap optical entry. For a compounder that
  "never gets cheap," demanding a deep discount = never owning it. That's the TINA trade-off, taken with eyes open.

**Bull synthesis:** best-in-class diversified beauty compounder; the dermo-cosmetics secular winner (3 brands >€1B, 26% margin,
re-accelerating to double-digits); record 20.2% group operating margin; ~18% ROE / ~17% ROIC; 74% gross margin; €7.2B FCF;
net debt only ~€2B (essentially unlevered); a 20% Galderma stake as a cheap call on injectable aesthetics; pricing power intact.

## THE DECISIVE TINA CALL

**OWN-NOW**, not WAIT. The prompt's WAIT thesis rests on "premium multiple + decelerating growth = no margin of safety."
The primary data dissolves both load-bearing legs: the multiple is **de-rated to ~5% below its own 10-yr median**, and growth
is **re-accelerating** (Q4 LFL +6.0%, derm-beauty +11.5%) — the inflection is up, not down. The supposed-engine air-pocket
(GALD's catch) is in injectable aesthetics, an **adjacent** category L'Oréal touches only via a 20% financial stake, not in
its accelerating dermo-cosmetics core. With record 20.2% margins, ~18% ROE, €7.2B FCF, a near-unlevered balance sheet, and a
multiple that already corrected, **fair value is the entry** for this slot.

The honest caveats (≠clean): (1) China is a **permanently slower** contributor — size the thesis on the US/EM/Europe + derm
engine, not on a China-boom return (UNVERIFIABLE that it re-accelerates durably). (2) No optical discount — the margin of
safety is **durability + a non-stretched multiple**, not a cheap price; the real downside trigger is a **rate-regime
back-up** compressing long-duration multiples, not the franchise. (3) Loyalty-bonus FY25 specifics and Kering Beauté
integration economics remain to confirm. None breaks the OWN-NOW call; they set the discipline: **build at spot, add on the
€350-375 pullback, and let the Q1-26 print confirm the reaccel.**
