## MODE B — FIRST-PRINCIPLES DISCONFIRMATION (lead)

**The decisive number (Q2 FY26, qtr ended 3/31/26), from the 5/5/26 earnings release:**
- Net sales +1.8% YoY — but decomposed: **volume +10.8% / price-mix −9.0%.**
- Premier Protein RTD: **volume +11.7% / price-mix −9.4%.**
- → They are **buying volume with promotions.** Management: *"Heightened consumer price sensitivity together with a sustained promotional environment adversely impacted our sales mix."*

**The margin verdict:**
- GAAP gross margin **32.3% → 27.0%** YoY; **Adjusted** gross margin **34.5% → 22.7%** (−1,180 bps). Only ~190 bps is a one-time inventory write-off (an $11.3M third-party ingredient that failed QC). The other ~1,000 bps = **input-cost inflation (incl. tariffs) + freight + promotional/unfavorable mix.**
- H1 FY26 Adj EBITDA **$144.1M vs $243.9M (−41%)**; Adj EBITDA margin ~22% → **~14%**.

**The guidance verdict (the de-rating engine):**
- FY26 Adj EBITDA guide cut **$425–455M (Nov'25) → $315–335M (May'26)** = **~26–29% cut in a single fiscal year.** Net-sales growth target cut from ~6% to **0–2%.** The "long-term financial algorithm" (7–9% growth / 18–20% margin) is broken in year one.
- FY25 was excellent for contrast: +16.1% sales, $481.6M Adj EBITDA. The fall is recent and steep.

**Steelman the SHORT — graded:**
- **(a) Appetite-suppression net-shrinks the category?** REFUTED on volume. Premier RTD volume +11.7%, consumption +2.9% (Circana), penetration rising. GLP-1 users *are* told to up protein/preserve lean mass and the demand shows it. The short's literal thesis is wrong.
- **(b) Faddish/competitive — share erosion + margin pressure?** **VERIFIED and decisive.** −9.4% price/mix and a 1,000 bps margin loss is the signature of a commoditizing category where every CPG is launching protein SKUs and private label is undercutting. This — not appetite suppression — is the real bear.
- **(c) Concentration risk?** VERIFIED & severe. **Walmart (incl. Sam's) + Costco + Amazon = ~74% of FY25 net sales** (10-K). One contract manufacturer makes **~half of all RTD shakes** (single point of failure on the flagship). A reset/promo demand from any one of three buyers moves the whole P&L.
- **(d) Multiple pricing decel?** The decel already happened and the multiple already compressed (−81% in 12m). EV/EBITDA ~7.5x on the *cut* number — optically cheap, but on a number that has missed 2 quarters running.
- **(e) Leverage?** VERIFIED. Total debt **$1,185M**, cash **$33M** → net debt **~$1.15B**; ~**3.5x** net leverage on guided EBITDA (was ~2.3x a year ago). Revolver drawn (5.82% rate). Interest expense rising ($16.5M→$20.1M/qtr). Yet they **repurchased $123M of stock in H1 at avg $29.18** (now $11) — value-destructive capital allocation into the decline.

**Steelman the BULL — graded:**
- Protein is a structural macro tailwind GLP-1 *accelerates* — **PARTIALLY TRUE** (volume + penetration confirm demand). But "structural tailwind" ≠ "pricing power." Demand growth that requires −9% price to capture is low-quality growth.
- Penetration still low / category healthy — TRUE per management and Circana, but doesn't fix the margin structure.
- Leadership: Premier is #1 RTD protein — TRUE, but #1 in a commoditizing aisle is being defended with promo dollars.

**Mode-B synthesis:** The honesty-alpha catch here is **takeaway-vs-data divergence**. Management's marketed takeaway is "brand metrics remain strong — volume growth, brand equity, household penetration" and they list GLP-1 as a category tailwind in the 10-K. All technically true. But the *load-bearing* fact — that the franchise can no longer convert that demand into margin — is disclosed only in the price/mix decomposition and the EBITDA guide cut. The bull thesis ("GLP-1-helped protein node") survives on the volume line and **dies on the margin line.** Net: GLP-1 is mildly demand-*helpful*, but the asset is structurally a **leveraged, concentrated, commoditizing CPG** whose earnings power just reset ~30% lower.
