## EXECUTION + POSITIONING DISCIPLINE (read first — this is the load-bearing caveat)

**Every name in this sleeve is a FOREIGN (KRX/KOSDAQ) primary listing.** For a US self-managed SMA that means:
1. **Execution friction.** No US-exchange line. KRX-direct requires a broker with Korea market access
   (IBKR offers KRX). KRW FX, T+2, Korea's transaction tax (~0.15–0.18%), and a *foreign-investor
   registration / omnibus* wrinkle. ADR availability is sparse (see ADR section — effectively KRX-direct only).
2. **POSITIONING IS US-BLIND.** SEC-FTD and FINRA short-interest **do not exist** for KRX issues. The
   `discovery_state()` conditioning layer is built on US microstructure (StockTwits, SEC-FTD, FINRA-SI,
   13F). Run naively on a KRX ticker it returns **positioning_score = 0 / attention ≈ 0** — a pure
   **artifact**, NOT evidence the name is undiscovered. **HARD GUARD:** no Korean name is ever tagged
   `UNDISCOVERED`. They are logged `POSITIONING_UNVERIFIABLE_KRX`, and we substitute the
   **FOREIGN-CROWDING WORKAROUND** below for a real read.

### FOREIGN-CROWDING WORKAROUND — Korea-native microstructure (the substitute discovery read)
Where checkable, the crowding read uses KRX-native channels instead of the absent US ones:
- **Foreign-ownership ratio (외국인보유비율)** + its trend — KRX/KIND publish per-issue daily; rising
  foreign-own into a name = global funds already crowding it; a high *level* (~30–40%+) = well-discovered.
- **Short-balance / securities-lending (공매도 / 대차잔고)** — KRX short-sale & loan-balance tape is the
  Korea analogue to FINRA-SI; a spiking loan balance = the squeeze/crowding tell.
- **Investor-class flows (투자자별 매매동향)** — foreign vs institution vs retail net buy/sell; persistent
  foreign net-buy = discovery in progress.
- **Sell-side coverage density + consensus TP gap** — number of covering brokers and price-to-consensus.
  Heavy coverage + price already near/through consensus TP = discovered/priced.

A name is treated as **"least-discovered (workaround)"** only when it is *small, thinly-covered, NOT
foreign-crowded, and not already through consensus* — and even then we flag it UNVERIFIABLE, never clean.

## THE VERIFIABLE DEMAND DRIVER (Mode-B corroboration)

The US K-beauty import boom is the one tailwind we can **measure**, not take on a press release:
- Korean cosmetics exports hit a record **$11.4B in 2025 (+12.3% YoY)**; Korea overtook the US to become
  the **world's #2 cosmetics exporter** (behind France).
- **The US is now Korea's #1 destination at $2.19B (+15.1% YoY)** — first time the US tops the list.
- Basic skincare = $8.54B of the total (the cosmeceutical-relevant category), makeup $1.51B.
- **VERIFIABLE TELL:** Korea→US cosmetics flow (HS **3304**, makeup/skincare prep) is measurable on
  **US Census trade-flow** + Korea Customs (KCS) monthly export tape. NH/Korea-Customs publish 10-day
  flash export prints (e.g., a +24% YoY, record first-10-days print drove Silicon2 +10% on 2026-04-13).
  This is the ODM/export sleeve's hard-to-fake throughput cross-check — the same role Census/USASpending
  play elsewhere in SignalOS. **Use it to corroborate the ODM/export names specifically.**
- Adjacent: **K-health supplements US exports +65% YoY** (the "next K-beauty") — relevant to Kolmar BNH.

## RANKED SHORTLIST — 5 strongest deep-dive candidates
(best cosmeceutical tailwind × least-priced × cleanest verifiability × least-bad foreign execution)

### 1. Cosmecca Korea — 241710 (KOSDAQ) — sub-seg 1, ODM picks-and-shovels — TOP PICK
~$621M mcap. The cleanest cosmeceutical-native expression of the US import boom: the **smallest of the
three majors but the fastest-growing** — Q1-26 revenue **+56.4%** to ₩185.1B, OP **+78%** to ₩21.9B,
with domestic OP margin re-rating 3.4%(2022)→8.5%(2023)→~10.4%. Critically it owns **Englewood Lab**, a
**US (New Jersey) manufacturing subsidiary** (+16.9% to ₩49.7B) — so it captures BOTH the export boom AND
the *onshoring/tariff-insulation* trade as US indie brands move production stateside. It manufactures the
finished clinical formulations for the indie/US brands — pure picks-and-shovels, no single-brand fad risk.
**Why it makes the cut:** best growth × smallest size × the only ODM with a US production footprint
(verifiable via Englewood Lab capex/hiring + HS-3304 throughput), at the lowest absolute valuation of the
three. **Foreign-crowding read (workaround):** smallest and *least sell-side-covered* of the three ODMs,
so genuinely the least-crowded of the picks-and-shovels — BUT it ran hard (the +56% print is public) and
foreign-own/coverage have been rising; treat as **least-discovered-of-the-ODMs, NOT undiscovered** —
`POSITIONING_UNVERIFIABLE_KRX`, confirm via KRX foreign-own trend + loan-balance before sizing.

### 2. Cosmax — 192820 (KOSPI) — sub-seg 1, ODM picks-and-shovels — the scale leader
~$1.38B mcap, ~0.8x sales, P/E ~12.5 (EPS $9.67 on $121.61). The largest ODM by output (₩1.61T 2025
production) and the cleanest **diversified** US-indie play: **US subsidiary +46% YoY**, world-leading
formula library serving 600+ brands incl. global micro-indies (lip/blush/body). Net profit **+312%** in
Q1-26. The bear point is the **margin gap** — revenue +16% but OP only +3% (China + mix), so the indie
volume isn't fully dropping to margin yet.
**Why it makes the cut:** the lowest-multiple way to own the structural ODM tailwind at scale; if the
margin normalizes the re-rate is large, and you're not paying for it. **Foreign-crowding read:** **foreign
ownership ~36.5%, free float 72.2%** — this is a *well-discovered, foreign-crowded* large-cap ODM (high
foreign-own LEVEL is the tell). Not a hidden name; the edge here is the cheap multiple + margin-inflection
optionality, not obscurity. `POSITIONING_UNVERIFIABLE_KRX` but workaround read = **discovered/crowded**.

### 3. Silicon2 — 257720 (KOSDAQ) — sub-seg 2, export/distribution aggregator — purest boom-beta
~$2B mcap, ~2.5x sales. The cleanest *aggregator* play: it distributes/exports indie K-beauty brands
globally (stylekorean.com B2B platform, logistics + influencer marketing) — a **direct levered proxy on
the HS-3304 export tape** with no single-brand risk. Revenue **+101%**, EPS **+217%** (2024). It literally
trades on the export flash prints (jumped +10% on the record early-April export number).
**Why it makes the cut:** highest *correlation to the one verifiable driver* in the whole universe — if you
believe the Census/KCS import data, this is the most direct expression. **Foreign-crowding read:** the
tightest *coupling to public export prints is double-edged* — the market already trades it ON those prints,
so attention is demonstrably high and the multiple (~2.5x sales, well above the ODMs) shows it's
**discovering/discovered**. Momentum-rich, not cheap; size as a beta sleeve, not a value name.
`POSITIONING_UNVERIFIABLE_KRX`; workaround = **discovering, attention elevated.**

### 4. Amorepacific — 090430 (KOSPI) — sub-seg 4, de-rated major turnaround — the value/quality leg
~$5.7B mcap, fwd P/E ~21x (trailing 37x vs peer ~70x). The de-rated major furthest along its **China→US/Japan
pivot**: 2025 sales back over ₩4T (+9.5%) first time in 3 yrs, OP **+50%+**, overseas revenue +15% with
**US the engine**. Owns the cosmeceutical-relevant crown jewels — **Cosrx** (snail-mucin / actives, US
hero), **Laneige** (US Sephora #1 lip), **Sulwhasoo** (fermented-actives prestige), plus **Aestura**
(derma line, fast-traction). The remaining risk is Cosrx normalization (post-boom) and China still soft.
**Why it makes the cut:** the only large-cap *quality* leg — you own a portfolio of cosmeceutical-native
brands (Cosrx/Aestura are the actives-native ones) at a turnaround multiple, with the US specifically the
proven growth driver. **Foreign-crowding read:** **~30% foreign institutional ownership, heavily covered**
— a *consensus turnaround*, well-discovered. Edge is the multiple/quality, not obscurity. Suh-family
holdco structure controls the vote. `POSITIONING_UNVERIFIABLE_KRX`; workaround = **discovered/consensus.**

### 5. VT Cosmetics (VT Co.) — 018290 (KOSDAQ) — sub-seg 3, branded momentum — the actives-native brand
~$0.8B mcap (₩1.08T). The branded-momentum name that is genuinely **cosmeceutical-native**: the **Reedle
Shot** franchise is "OG liquid microneedling" + **PDRN / exosome / Cica / Dragon's-blood** actives —
viral on US TikTok Shop and Amazon, a real active-ingredient story (vs a color-cosmetics fad). Smaller and

**Why it makes the cut:** the branded-momentum slot that actually fits the actives arms-race thesis, at

read:** the **Cube Entertainment cross-ownership overhang** (VT's parent bought Cube) is a governance/
capital-allocation distraction; KOSDAQ small-cap = thin, volatile, hardest execution. Less foreign-crowded
than the majors (workaround = least-discovered of the branded names) but UNVERIFIABLE and event-risky;
size small. `POSITIONING_UNVERIFIABLE_KRX`.

## ADR AVAILABILITY — effectively KRX-DIRECT ONLY

There is **no liquid, sponsored US ADR** for any of these names. None of Cosmax, Kolmar Korea, Cosmecca,
Silicon2, APR, VT, Amorepacific, or LG H&H maintains an active NYSE/Nasdaq-listed ADR. (Amorepacific has
historically had only thin/unsponsored OTC pink representation at best; nothing investable for an SMA.)

**Practical access for a US self-managed SMA:**
- **KRX-direct via a Korea-enabled broker (IBKR supports KRX/KOSDAQ).** This is the route for all 5
  shortlist names. Expect KRW FX, T+2, ~0.15–0.18% Korea securities transaction tax, and one-time
  foreign-investor registration handling (broker omnibus).
- **No US-listed clean substitute.** The only US-listed *adjacent* exposure is the **Amplify/etc. K-beauty
  thematic ETF** route or owning the **Western cosmeceutical book already in the sleeve** (ELF/ODD etc.) —
  not a substitute for direct Korean exposure.
- **Bottom line:** every name is foreign-execution. Cosmax/Amorepacific (KOSPI large-caps) are the most
  liquid/easiest to fill; Silicon2/VT/Cosmecca (KOSDAQ) are thinner — size accordingly.
