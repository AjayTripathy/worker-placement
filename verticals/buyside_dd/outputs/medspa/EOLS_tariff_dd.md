# Evolus (NASDAQ: EOLS) — Tariff-Mitigation Claim, Primary-Source Diligence

**Date:** 2026-06-25 · **Live price:** $7.15 (IBKR last/close) · **CIK:** 1570562
**Claim under test:** Management can *mitigate* the 15% US tariff on Korean-made Jeuveau (effective Sept 29, 2026; single-sourced from Daewoong) by **pre-importing US inventory at pre-tariff cost**, leveraging Jeuveau's ~3-year shelf life. (Plus a ~10% EU tariff on France-made Evolysse fillers.)

**Verdict: PARTIAL — credible in mechanism and already in motion, but UNQUANTIFIED by management and only a DEFERRAL, not elimination. The durable tariff drag is small (~1-3 GM points, ~$4-9M/yr) — thesis-relevant against ~breakeven EBITDA but not thesis-breaking. Does NOT by itself change a BUY; it trims, not kills.**

---

## 1. Shelf life — the stockpiling window

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Claim | "~36 months unopened, 3-year shelf life" |
| How verified | FDA prescribing information, Section 16 (How Supplied/Storage and Handling), DailyMed setid 17a914c1 and accessdata.fda.gov label |
| Authority | FDA label (primary) |
| Finding | **PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** Label confirms unopened vials stored **refrigerated 2–8°C in original carton, DO NOT FREEZE**; reconstituted product discard in 24h. The label does **NOT print a "36-month" dating number** — that figure is management's assertion, not on the FDA label I could pull. A 24–36mo refrigerated dating is standard for lyophilized botulinum-toxin biologics, so ~3yr is *plausible*, but the precise unopened dating is **UNVERIFIABLE from the public label after trying**. |

**Read:** The stockpiling window is real (refrigerated, multi-year, no cold-chain barrier to banking inventory), but the exact ceiling (24 vs 36 months) is not on the primary label. EOLS's own 10-Q corroborates the constraint exists: *"our products have fixed future expiration dates… excess inventory… may have to be disposed of if such inventory exceeds approved expiration dates."* So shelf life both **enables** the stockpile and **caps** it.

## 2. Is the pre-buy already happening? — YES, partially, but with a timing nuance

Inventory ($, from 10-Q/10-K balance sheets) and months-of-cover (vs ~$24M quarterly COGS):

| Quarter | Inventory | Months of cover |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-03-31 | $10.0M | 1.2 |
| 2025-06-30 | $26.5M | 3.3 |
| 2025-09-30 | $32.9M | 4.1 |
| 2025-12-31 | $27.0M | 3.3 |
| **2026-03-31** | **$24.8M** | **3.1** |

- The big build (**$10M → $33M, +230%**) happened in **Q2–Q3 2025** — this **PRE-DATES the April 2, 2026 tariff proclamation**, so it was launch/demand-driven (Evolysse ramp + Jeuveau growth), **NOT** tariff-stockpiling. By Mar-2026 inventory had drawn back to ~$25M (~3 months cover) — normal operating level.
- 10-Q DOES confirm intent in writing (risk factors): *"we have been **proactively managing inventory flows into the United States to protect against such tariffs**."* That is the only written confirmation, and it is qualitative.
- **The actual tariff stockpile would build in Q2–Q3 2026** (ahead of Sept 29) and is **not yet visible** on the Mar-2026 balance sheet. So "is the stockpile already happening?" = **not materially yet on the books; watch the Q2 (June) and Q3 (Sept) 10-Qs** — a real pre-buy should push inventory well above $33M / 4 months. **As of the latest filing it is stated-intent, not demonstrated-on-balance-sheet.**

## 3. Can they fund it? — YES, comfortably

- Cash: **$49.8M** (3/31/26), down from $54M (12/31/25); 2 consecutive positive adj-EBITDA quarters (self-funding now).
- **Undrawn debt:** New Pharmakon A&R term loan = $250M total, $150M drawn, **two additional $50M tranches ($100M) available through Dec 31, 2026 with NO performance conditions or financial covenants** + ~$20–30M Eclipse revolver.
- Total accessible dry powder ≈ **$120M+**.
- A meaningful stockpile is small relative to this: even **12 months of Jeuveau cover ≈ $80–95M of inventory** (4× current), and a *tariff-incremental* pre-buy of ~6 months extra cover is only ~$40–50M. **Fundable** from the undrawn Pharmakon tranches alone.
- Q1'26 cash flow: inventory was a **+$6.4M working-capital SOURCE** (drawdown), so no outflow strain yet — the outflow comes when the Q2–Q3 build starts. **Cash-feasibility = not the binding constraint.** (The binding constraints are shelf life and Daewoong's production capacity, not EOLS's balance sheet.)

## 4. Tariff drag — quantified (nobody had done the math)

Reported Q1'26: Revenue $73.1M, COGS $24.2M → **reported GM 66.9%**. FY'26 guided revenue ~$330M; Jeuveau ≈ ~92% of sales, Evolysse ≈ ~8%.

**Key point:** the 15% tariff applies to the **customs/transfer value of the imported Jeuveau article** (the price EOLS pays Daewoong + landed cost), NOT to full COGS (which also carries distribution, royalties, freight, amortization) and NOT to retail. Botulinum-toxin transfer prices are a small fraction of net sales. Scenario table on the dutiable base as a % of Jeuveau net sales:

| Transfer price (% of net sales) | Dutiable base | 15% tariff/yr | GM points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8% | $24M | **$3.6M** | 1.1 |
| 12% | $36M | **$5.5M** | 1.7 |
| 16% | $49M | **$7.3M** | 2.2 |
| 20% | $61M | **$9.1M** | 2.8 |

**Central estimate: ~$4–9M/yr, ~1–3 GM points** on Jeuveau once the tariff bites unmitigated.

**EU/Evolysse (10%, France-made):** Evolysse ~$26M sales × ~15–25% transfer × 10% = **~$0.4–0.7M/yr — a rounding error.**

**vs EBITDA:** FY'26 adj EBITDA is ~breakeven-to-slightly-positive (Street ~$5–15M). So the unmitigated Jeuveau drag (~$4–9M) is **NOT a rounding error against EBITDA — it is roughly the size of the entire FY profit**, even though it's only ~1–3 GM points. **This is the honest tension:** small as a margin line, large as a fraction of a company that just crossed breakeven. **Thesis-relevant.**

## 5. Stockpile = deferral, not elimination

- A pre-buy delays the hit by the months of pre-tariff cover banked. A realistic, cash-feasible, shelf-life-limited stockpile (say 12–18 months of incremental cover, ~$50–80M, fundable, within a 24–36mo dating) defers the tariff cost by **~4–6 quarters** — pushing the first material P&L hit from late-2026 into **roughly 2H-2027/2028**.
- **Steady-state after the stockpile runs out:** unless EOLS (a) wins a Section 232 exception (the 10-K/10-Q explicitly flag "conditions, exceptions and potential reductions" and "certain elements… may not ultimately apply"), (b) re-sources/relocates fill-finish, or (c) passes price to medspas, the **durable GM is ~1–3 points below current**, i.e. reported GM steps from ~67% toward **~64–66%**, and adj-EBITDA absorbs a **~$4–9M/yr recurring drag**. That is the number the turnaround thesis must live with post-2027.

## 6. What management ACTUALLY said — hand-waving on size, with one written intent

From the Q1 2026 earnings call (verified via transcript) and the May-2026 10-Q risk factors:

- **Quantified?** **NO.** No dollar stockpile size, no inventory target, no months-of-cover figure, no GM/EPS impact, no stated pricing pass-through to medspas.
- Verbatim call language: *"In the near term, we have a plan to secure significant U.S. inventory, supported by the product's three-year shelf life, which provides flexibility as we bridge to longer-term solutions"* and *"we believe there is a pathway to mitigate or eliminate the impact of this tariff,"* with *"an update expected by year end."* They reaffirmed FY'26 outlook unchanged.
- **10-Q (written, primary):** the words "stockpile," "pre-import," "shelf life," and "inventory build" appear **ZERO times** in the 10-Q. The only written statement is the qualitative *"proactively managing inventory flows into the United States to protect against such tariffs, there can be no assurance that we will be able to do so."* The tariff is disclosed only as a risk that *"may have a material adverse impact."*

**Classification: the takeaway ("we can mitigate, outlook unchanged") DIVERGES from the substantiation (no quantified plan, the stockpile not yet on the balance sheet, exemption uncertain).** This is the honesty tell — but it is *soft* hand-waving, not a contradicted datum. They are not claiming the drag is zero; they're claiming a path and deferring specifics to year-end.

## Bottom line

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Shelf life | Refrigerated 2–8°C, multi-year — enables stockpiling; exact 36mo dating NOT on FDA label (mgmt assertion, plausible, unverifiable from public label) |
| Pre-buy happening? | Intent stated in 10-Q; the *visible* 2025 build pre-dated the tariff and reversed; the real tariff stockpile is a Q2–Q3 2026 event NOT yet on the balance sheet |
| Cash-feasible? | Yes — $50M cash + $100M undrawn no-covenant Pharmakon tranches; not the binding constraint |
| Jeuveau tariff drag | **~$4–9M/yr, ~1–3 GM points** (15% on transfer value, not full COGS) |
| Evolysse/EU drag | ~$0.4–0.7M/yr — immaterial |
| Deferral duration | A feasible stockpile defers ~4–6 quarters (into ~2H-2027/2028) |
| Steady-state GM | ~67% → **~64–66%** durable, ~$4–9M/yr recurring EBITDA drag, absent exemption/re-sourcing/price |
| Mgmt disclosure | **Hand-waving on size** (no $ / no target / no GM impact); one written "managing inventory flows" intent; "update by year-end" |

**Mitigation = PARTIAL.** Credible mechanism (real shelf life, real undrawn funding, written intent, a small absolute drag) but **unquantified, not yet demonstrated on the balance sheet, and only a deferral.** The prior "UNVERIFIABLE" flag was lazy: the *drag* is fully quantifiable (~1–3 GM pts / $4–9M/yr) and the *funding* is verifiable (it's there).

**Effect on the BUY:** does **not** break the turnaround thesis — a 1–3 point GM headwind deferred ~5 quarters is survivable for a company growing revenue ~7%+/yr and self-funding. But it is **not** a rounding error against an EBITDA that just crossed zero: it roughly equals one year's profit, so it **caps near-term operating-leverage upside and pushes the inflection right** until exemption/re-sourcing clarity (the "year-end update"). **Trim the BUY's margin-expansion slope; keep the thesis.** Watch items: (1) Q2/Q3-2026 10-Q inventory line — a real stockpile must break above ~$33M / 4 months; (2) any Section 232 Korea exemption; (3) the promised year-end mitigation update with actual numbers.

**Genuinely unverifiable after trying:** exact unopened-vial dating (not on public FDA label); Daewoong transfer-price as % of sales (not disclosed — drives the drag estimate's range); whether a Section 232 exemption lands.
