# Cosmeceutical Sleeve — Candidate Universe

**Book:** Aesthetics / wellness. **New sleeve:** "Cosmeceuticals" — clinical/active-ingredient skincare.
**As-of:** 2026-06-26. **Investor:** US HNW, self-managed SMA (US listings easy; foreign = friction).
**Conditioning layer:** `discovery_state(ticker)` run on all US-listed names; foreign-primary names carry
NO US FINRA-SI / SEC-FTD feed, so positioning is BLIND — they are tagged
`POSITIONING_UNVERIFIABLE` and never called clean-UNDISCOVERED on positioning (GXI hard guard).

## FRAMING DISCIPLINE (read first)
This sleeve must stand on cosmeceutical-NATIVE demand drivers, NOT GLP-1 / "Ozempic face" (covered
elsewhere in the book). The five native drivers underwriting every thesis below:
1. **Skinification / preventative adoption** — younger cohorts onto clinical actives earlier; skincare
   taking share of beauty wallet (ELF: skincare 9%→23% of consumption in 3 yrs is the cleanest print).
2. **Dermatologist-recommendation moat** — CeraVe / La Roche-Posay / SkinCeuticals / Cetaphil / Eucerin
   clinical-credibility brands; derm-recommended = pricing power + repeat.
3. **Active-ingredient science arms race** — peptides, exosomes, growth factors, retinoid innovation,
   microbiome, biotech-derived "bio-identical" actives. This is where the picks-and-shovels live.
4. **Professional / medical-grade channel** — skincare sold through dermatologists & medspas.
5. **DTC + AI personalization with OWNED formulation** — verify the R&D is real (ODD) vs marketing.

## RANKED SHORTLIST — 7 deep-dive candidates
(best cosmeceutical-specific tailwind × least-priced × cleanest verifiability × buyable by a US SMA)

### 1. BEISY — Beiersdorf ADR (Eucerin / Aquaphor Derma) — sub-seg 1, the cleanest derm-credibility compounder
~$24B mcap (BEI.DE primary), ~3.5x sales. The Derma division (Eucerin + Aquaphor) is the single
purest large-cap expression of driver #2: +11.7% organic in 2025 (5th straight double-digit year),
+8.2% in Q1-26 vs a low-single-digit derma market, on proprietary actives Thiamidol® (anti-pigment)
and Epicelline® (longevity). It is dermatologist-recommended, gaining share in the US/Brazil/China.
**Why it makes the cut:** the derm-recommendation moat is *measured*, not marketed; Derma is the growth
engine inside a stock the market prices on sleepy mass NIVEA, so you get the structural tailwind at a
non-glamour multiple. ADR is US-buyable (friction-light). Catalyst: continued Derma-mix re-rating +
the €100M skin-science CVC fund seeding the next active.

### 2. ODD — Oddity Tech — sub-seg 2, DTC with VERIFIABLE owned-formulation R&D
~$0.5B... (see note) — **correction below**, mcap is materially larger; ~$0.8B TTM revenue, high-margin
DTC. Oddity Labs (Boston wet-lab + Tel Aviv AI molecule discovery) is the rare DTC name where the
"biotech actives" claim is structurally real, not a marketing skin: a funded lab hiring bench chemists,
targeting hyperpigmentation/aging actives, feeding owned brands (Il Makiage, SpoiledChild) plus the new
METHODIQ (~$25M yr-1). This hits drivers #3 and #5 simultaneously.
**Why it makes the cut:** the only public pure-play that *owns* its active-ingredient pipeline AND the
DTC distribution — vertical integration of the arms race. R&D is verifiable (lab headcount, hiring).
Catalyst: first Oddity-Labs molecule commercialization / peptide update management flagged for 2026.
*(VERIFY before deep dive: reconcile mcap — sub-$1B float figure looks like a partial-class artifact;
ODD has dual-class structure, confirm fully-diluted economic mcap.)*

### 3. ELF — e.l.f. Beauty — sub-seg 2, skinification mix-shift, cleanest demand print
~$3.6B mcap. The single best *quantified* skinification signal in the universe: skincare went 9%→23%
of global consumption in 3 years, with Naturium (~$250M retail, doubled post-acquisition) and the
Rhode acquisition (~$500M annualized retail, Sephora #1) driving an actives-forward mass mix. +25%
net sales in FY26.
**Why it makes the cut:** demand driver #1 made visible — share-of-wallet shift to clinical actives,
in a US-listed, liquid, founder-led operator. The stock de-rated hard off prior highs (tariff/China
fears), so the cosmeceutical tailwind is available at a reset multiple. Catalyst: Rhode Sephora
door-expansion (<20% global penetration today) + Naturium retail rollout.

### 4. CRDA.L — Croda International — sub-seg 3, the purest beauty-actives picks-and-shovels
~$5B mcap, ~2.3x sales. Consumer Care segment's **Beauty Actives** is leading group growth (Q1-26 the
standout). Supplies the actives INTO every brand above — the most verifiable tailwind because it does
not depend on any single brand winning. Croda is the cleanest cosmetic-actives exposure of the European
ingredient majors (Givaudan/Symrise/DSM-Firmenich are diluted by flavors & fragrance).
**Why it makes the cut:** sells the shovels to the whole skinification gold rush; Beauty Actives is the
explicit growth driver management calls out, and the stock has de-rated from its 2021 specialty-chem
peak. Friction: UK-listed (LSE) — buyable via SMA but not frictionless; positioning is BLIND.
Catalyst: Consumer Care recovery re-rating as Life Sciences (the drag) stabilizes.

### 5. EL — Estée Lauder — sub-seg 1, the prestige-skincare-science TURNAROUND
~$30B mcap, ~2x sales, ~35x forward earnings (depressed, recovery-loaded). Three years of decline
ending; FY26 consensus ~+4.7% sales. The cosmeceutical angle is "active longevity" science (La Mer,
Re-Nutriv) repositioning prestige against medicalized skincare — driver #2/#3 at the luxury tier.
**Why it makes the cut:** highest-quality skincare brand equity in the book, available mid-turnaround
with sentiment washed out; the new management's science-pivot is the catalyst rather than a
finished story. NOT cheap on earnings (turnaround optics), so this is a quality-recovery name, not a
value name. Catalyst: margin-recovery proof-points + China travel-retail stabilization.

### 6. SKIN — Beauty Health (SkinHealth Systems) — sub-seg 4, pro-channel HydraFacial, broken micro-cap
~$0.2-0.3B mcap, <1x sales. The one liquid-enough US public expression of driver #4 (professional /
medspa channel): HydraFacial razor-and-blade (delivery system + consumable serums) sold through
derms/medspas. 2025 adj-EBITDA $45M (from $12M) on a 65% gross margin — operational turnaround under way
even as revenue troughed (-10%), with management guiding growth back in 2027.
**Why it makes the cut:** asymmetric, deeply-discounted pro-channel call option; the consumable razor-blade
model is the right exposure to the medical-grade channel that is otherwise almost entirely PRIVATE.
HIGH RISK (micro-cap, prior accounting/turnover history). Catalyst: 2H26 sequential inflection +
new-handpiece/consumable cycle; analyst targets imply ~2x.

### 7. LRLCY — L'Oréal ADR — sub-seg 1, the compounder that OWNS the derm-credibility franchise
~€44B group sales; Dermatological Beauty division €7.2B, +growth-accelerating-to-double-digits exiting
2025, 26.1% margin (highest in group). Owns CeraVe + La Roche-Posay + SkinCeuticals (each now >€1B) +
Vichy — i.e. it *is* the dermatologist-recommendation moat and also owns the PRIVATE pro-skincare
(SkinCeuticals/skinbetter). The compounder, not a turnaround.
**Why it makes the cut:** the highest-quality, most-diversified way to own the entire cosmeceutical
thesis in one name; Dermatological Beauty is the fastest-growing high-margin division. It is the
benchmark/anchor of the sleeve. Caveat: large-cap, well-owned, full multiple — least "least-priced,"
so it ranks below the more dislocated names despite best business quality. Catalyst: continued
Active-Cosmetics double-digit re-acceleration.

## STRUCTURALLY PRIVATE — the public-market gap
The highest-conviction cosmeceutical category — **professional / medical-grade derm-dispensed skincare**
— is overwhelmingly PRIVATE or buried inside conglomerates. There is almost no clean public pure-play:

- **SkinCeuticals & skinbetter science** → inside **L'Oréal** (LRLCY) — only public access is the
  whole group.
- **Alastin** → inside **Galderma** (GALD, already in book).
- **SkinMedica** → was Allergan/AbbVie pro-skincare (medspa channel).
- **ZO Skin Health** (Dr. Zein Obagi) → **private**.
- **Obagi Medical** → **private** (was public, taken private; Asia-PE ownership).
- **Crown Laboratories** (PanOxyl, StriVectin, Blue Lagoon) → **private**.
- **Paula's Choice** & **Dermalogica** → inside **Unilever** (UL) — diluted in a giant.
- **Drunk Elephant** → inside **Shiseido** (the one public pure-ish derm-cred brand, and it's impairing).
- **Medik8, Augustinus Bader, Biologique Recherche, iS Clinical, Environ, PCA Skin** → private / PE-held.

**Implication:** the cleanest demand-driver #4 (pro channel) is barely investable directly — the public
proxies are (a) razor-blade device platforms (SKIN/HydraFacial), (b) own-the-conglomerate (LRLCY, GALD),
or (c) the ingredient supplier that sells into all of them (CRDA). This is itself the microstructure
finding: **the pro/medical-grade channel is a private-market category; public investors must express it
via picks-and-shovels (actives suppliers) or via the brand-owning majors.**
