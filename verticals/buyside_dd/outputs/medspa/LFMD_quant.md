### A2. The guide and the required H2 swing

- **FY26 guide:** revenue $220-230M (+13-19%) / **adj EBITDA +$12-17M**. Run-rate target >$250M rev / >$25M adj EBITDA exiting.
- **Q2-26 guide (management's own):** revenue **$47-50M (DOWN sequentially from $50.2M)** / adj EBITDA **−$2M to +$1M**.

**Bridge math:**
- H1 (Q1 actual + Q2 guide-mid): rev ~$98.7M, adj EBITDA **~−$5.0M**.
- **Required H2 (Q3+Q4):** revenue **$121-131M**, adj EBITDA **+$17-22M** → **+$8.5-11.0M per quarter**.
- **The swing:** adj EBITDA must go from ~−$0.5M (Q2-guide-mid) to ~+$9.8M/qtr in H2 — **a ~$10M/qtr inflection in a single quarter (Q2→Q3)**, off a business that printed −$4.5M last quarter.

### A3. How aggressive is the leverage assumption? (the load-bearing flag)

To hit the **FY midpoint (+$14.5M)** with rev $225M, total opex (S&M+G&A+other) must be ~$184M. With G&A ~$60M and other ~$15M, that leaves **FY S&M ≈ $109M = ~$27M/qtr average**. Since Q1 was already $29.9M and Q2 ~$30M, the back half requires:

> **S&M to FALL from $29.9M/qtr to ~$24-25M/qtr WHILE revenue grows ~25% sequentially** — S&M as a % of revenue compressing from **60% (Q1) to ~39% (H2)** in two quarters.

This is the single most aggressive assumption in the model. It is *possible* (front-loaded acquisition spend that genuinely tapers as cohorts mature and CAC converts to recurring LTV), but it is the textbook "buy growth now, harvest later" promise with exactly one quarter of contrary print and no H1 cushion.

### A4. ARPU sensitivity — THE CRUX (S&M held flat at $30M/qtr in all three)

| ARPU scenario (per sub/qtr) | Q2 adj EBITDA | Q4 adj EBITDA | **FY26 adj EBITDA** | vs guide (+12-17) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Keep eroding → $123** | −$3.7M | +$0.3M | **−$9.2M** | **MASSIVE MISS** |
| **Stabilize ≈ $137 (flat to Q1)** | −$2.3M | +$5.9M | **+$1.1M** | misses low end |
| **Modest recovery → $148** | −$1.3M | +$10.3M | **+$8.7M** | *still below* +$12M |

**Read:** Even the optimistic ARPU-recovery path with S&M held flat lands FY adj EBITDA at **+$8.7M — below the guide low-end.** Hitting the guide requires ARPU recovery **AND** the absolute S&M reduction from A3 **simultaneously**. The model says the guide is reachable only in the top-right corner of a 2-D (ARPU × S&M-leverage) grid. ARPU is the higher-beta variable: a swing from $123 to $148 (±9% around Q1) moves FY adj EBITDA by ~$18M — i.e., the entire guide range lives inside normal ARPU noise. **This is why ARPU stabilization, not subscriber count, is the real binary.**

**Model-implied Q2 adj EBITDA under base assumptions: ~−$2M to −$1M** (in-line with the −$2M-to-+$1M guide). So Q2 itself is likely a non-event on the EBITDA line — the print's information content is almost entirely in (a) the ARPU/sub disclosure and (b) whether management reaffirms or cuts the H2-loaded FY guide.
