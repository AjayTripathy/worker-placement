## Verdict — conditional; not investable until §7 resolves, and not the deal the deck implies

**Not a fraud.** PCM Encore is a real $1.9B RIA with a clean record, Michael Paulus genuinely founded Assurance IQ ($2.35B Prudential exit), and Barry Dubin really co‑built KBP Brands (the #4 US restaurant franchisee). The catch — the promoter pattern — is **facts mostly true, takeaways tilted**: your own wealth manager is soliciting you into its principals' illiquid, affiliated franchise deals (a conflict its ADV doesn't properly disclose); the headline 4–6x rests on dressed‑up operational assumptions that an honest base case pulls toward **~2x net over a long, illiquid hold**; and the actual asset ("Sublime Huts," 23 stores) cannot be verified from any public source.

**What the "key terms" email improves:** a follow‑up from PCM states **no management fee and no carried interest on this deal, $100k minimum, long‑term hold.** If confirmed in the binding documents, this **removes the single largest economics critique** (net ≈ gross), and — *if* it reflects a true pari‑passu ride alongside PCM's own capital — is the most‑aligned structure an LP can get. It does **not** fix the unverifiable asset, the dressed‑up return math, the long illiquid single‑operator hold, or the ADV disclosure gap. "Long‑term hold" also quietly walks back the deck's "4x by year 5."

## 2 · First‑principles findings (ranked)

**HIGH — Your RIA is selling you its own principals' deal, under‑disclosed in its ADV.** PCM Encore offers *its own advisory clients* co‑investments sponsored by affiliated PCM Growth. The Form ADV Part 2A — which the deck cites "for conflicts of interest" — discloses PCM Hospitality only as a "Paulus may allocate time" footnote, with zero mentions of "co‑invest," "Qdoba," "Cheba," "carried interest," or "private fund"; **Section 7.B private‑fund reporting = "No Information Filed."** (No‑fee softens "monetizing you twice," but the disclosure gap remains.)

**HIGH — Headline 4–6x rests on dressed‑up assumptions; honest base case ≈ 2x net.** AUV overstated ~20% (deck $2.8M vs. Cheba 2024 $2.27M mature / $1.97M all‑store). "Conservative 5x exit" ≈ flat to the 4.7x entry, and top‑of‑range for a niche sub‑80‑unit concept. "Double EBITDA" = ~15%/yr CAGR via capex‑heavy new builds, not organic. If ramp disappoints, at a flat 4.7x exit with ~2.5x leverage the equity compresses toward ~2x (~15% IRR). (IRR: 4x/5yr = 32%; 6x/10yr = 19.6%.)

**HIGH — The actual asset can't be verified.** "Sublime Huts" appears in no public source; the publicly‑identified largest Cheba Hut franchisee is Elevated Inc. (~22 units). You cannot currently verify the asset, its unit count, or standing.

**MODERATE — Logo‑wall inflation + credential framing.** "OpenAI" as a PCM Growth seed/control investment is false (personal/passive at most). Bradford Lin's KKR/JPM logos = a KKR associate (2022–25) + one JPM summer internship. Mike Kulp (unnamed) is KBP's CEO; the "$500M / 100 acquisitions" is KBP‑era, unrealized, never‑exited, imported into a deal where Dubin's independent record is <2 years old with zero realized exits.

**MODERATE — Qdoba thesis false; "pipeline" is a capital obligation.** "Limited competition beyond Chipotle" is wrong (Taco Bell ~7,200 units, Moe's, Del Taco, Rubio's…). The "55‑location pipeline" is a ~$45–50M buildout obligation (50 units × ~$900K–1M), not a gift. Qdoba: AUV ~$1.6–1.7M, ~16% store EBITDA post‑fee, bottom‑quartile ~$926K.

2. **Fees/carry — binding docs, not email:** confirm no management fee and no carry at the PCM co‑invest level. Any operator‑level promote (B Wild / 7 Star Eats) or PCM Growth deal‑level fee (acquisition, monitoring, financing, disposition) ahead of or alongside LPs?
3. **"90%+ PCM's own capital":** confirm the actual $ and % commitment from PCM/principals vs. the client slug for these deals.
4. **Disclosure:** will the co‑invest vehicles be reported as private funds on an amended Form ADV, and where is the affiliated‑co‑investment conflict disclosed? (Part 2A covers PCM Tax and PCM Kinect but not PCM Hospitality.)

**Liquidity, hold & capital calls**
5. **"Long‑term hold":** target hold period; any liquidity (redemptions, transfer/tag‑along, secondary)? Timeline to first distribution?
6. **Capital calls:** $100k fully funded at close, or follow‑on calls (e.g., the Qdoba buildout)? Committed‑vs‑called?
7. **Governance:** information and consent rights (LPAC, major decisions, affiliate transactions) for co‑investors?

**The Cheba Hut asset**
8. **"Sublime Huts" identity:** confirm the legal entity, the 23‑store list/locations, and the Cheba Hut FDD Item 20 franchisee roster confirming Sublime Huts and its unit count. (Clarify the relationship to Elevated Inc., ~22 units, the publicly‑identified largest.)

**Return assumptions**
