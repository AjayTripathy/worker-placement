We have completed a full review of the *Project Evergreen "B Wild"* CIM (63 pp, Summer 2025) and cross-checked it against the Cheba Hut 2025 FDD (Item 7 and Item 19) and our own operator screen. The operating diligence largely clears: Elevated Inc. What the CIM does **not** contain is the **investment**: no purchase price, no sources & uses, no leverage, no fee/promote/carry, no MOIC/IRR/hold, and no bridge from the marketed store-level EBITDA to buyer cash flow. The items below are what we need to underwrite the transaction rather than the restaurant. They are grouped by priority; the Tier-1 items are gating.

---

## Tier 1 — Transaction terms (the gating gap)

1. **Purchase price and the EBITDA base the multiple is struck on.** The teaser referenced ~4.7x; the CIM does not state a price or a base. Please confirm the headline purchase EV and **which EBITDA line the 4.7x is applied to** — the consolidated *store-level* EBITDA (PF TTM-P5 2025 ≈ $14.5M, p64) or a *true/adjusted* EBITDA net of corporate overhead and owner comp.
   - *Why this is first:* the same 4.7x is ~8.0x on true EBITDA if struck on the store-level number, and the choice of base swings implied EV by roughly $28M (≈$68M on store-level vs ≈$40M on true). Nothing else can be sized until this is fixed.
2. **Sources & uses at close** — equity, senior debt, seller rollover/financing, transaction fees and expenses, and any earnout or holdback.
3. **Debt terms** — facility size, rate (fixed/floating + spread), amortization, covenants, and whether the buildout program is separately financed.
4. **Hold period and base-case exit assumption** — target hold and the exit multiple and EBITDA base underwritten (and the support for an exit multiple at or above entry on a sub-80-unit, single-region, cannabis-branded concept).

## Tier 2 — Quality of earnings (store-level → buyer cash flow)

5. **Reviewed or audited trailing P&L** bridging the deck's store-level EBITDA (p49 / p64) to **true EBITDA** — i.e., loading above-store corporate G&A, normalizing owner/executive compensation (Timmons + Archer), and any management fee — and then to **levered free cash flow to equity** (maintenance capex + debt service). The marketed 22.9% margin (p49) is a four-wall + royalty figure; we need what the equity actually earns.
6. **Royalty rate.** The p49 P&L carries royalty at ~4.7% of sales vs Cheba's ~6% standard. Confirm the rate the buyer SPV inherits at close; a reset to 6% compresses store-level margin ~1.3 points (~$0.9M on current sales).
7. **Accounting basis and pro-forma adjustments.** FDD Note 2 indicates figures are cash-basis from POS/internal accounting. Please confirm and provide the **un-pro-forma trailing-actual** EBITDA alongside the "PF TTM-P5" figures — and itemize the pro-forma adjustments (the p64/p65 footnotes annualize 2024 partial-year openings upward).

## Tier 3 — Structure, fees, and alignment

8. **Fee and promote structure** at the B Wild / co-invest level — management fee, carried interest/promote, and any deal-level acquisition, monitoring, or disposition fee taken ahead of LPs. (The "no management fee / no carry / pari-passu" representation has been verbal; we need it in the LPA / subscription documents.)
9. **Capital structure of the commitment** — is the LP commitment fully funded at close, or are there follow-on capital calls to fund the multi-year build program? Please provide committed vs. called and the expected call schedule. (The growth thesis implies a sizeable build program; "fully funded at close" appears unlikely on its face.)
10. **Alignment / PCM principal capital** — confirm the share of B Wild capital that is PCM-principal money and that it invests on the same security, price, and priority as co-investor capital.
