**Method:** Two‑mode review — first‑principles disconfirmation (lead) + claim verification against primary sources (SEC IAPD/Form ADV, SEC EDGAR Form D, public‑company proxies, court records, corporate press).

---

## Verdict

**Not investable as pitched — conditional proceed only after the gaps below are closed.**

Pearlmark is a **real, Generali‑backed manager with clean, verified principals and no disciplinary history.** But the overview is a **promotional document whose three load‑bearing credibility claims — the track record, the fund sizes, and the "skin‑in‑the‑game" identity — each diverge from the firm's own filings and footnotes.** Nothing here is fraud: the firm is **honest in the endnotes and promotional in the headlines.** The diligence signal is that divergence, not an undisclosed datum. An institutional allocator should treat the headline numbers as unconfirmed until the seven asks (§6) are answered in writing.

## 2. First‑principles findings (ranked by severity)

### SEVERE — Marketed fund sizes are not substantiated by the only filings on record
- **PRSM I "first closing… December 2025 at over $450M."** The Form D filed **2025‑12‑17** reports **Total Amount Sold = $0** and **"Date of First Sale: Yet to Occur = true."** The **$1.5B target is real**; the **$450M close is not in the filing.** (Timing nuance: a Form D is due within 15 days of first sale, so a genuine close could post‑date a $0 filing — but "closed at over $450M" is unsupported by the only document and must be reconciled directly.)
- **PEP II "$267M."** Latest Form D (D/A **2025‑03‑10**) main vehicle shows **$133.0M sold / 18 investors.** $267M appears only as a cross‑fund/SMA roll‑up no single filing substantiates, and there is **no post‑"final close" (July 2025) amendment** reflecting $267M. The offerings page also implies a **$300M target that was missed.**
- **Mezz VI "$440M raised."** Main‑fund Form D (D/A **2025‑08‑11**) shows **$378.7M sold / 18 investors.** $440M reconciles only by adding feeder/SMA tranches that **partly double‑count** the same capital (e.g., a feeder reporting $286.8M overlapping the main vehicle). First close **Aug 2024 — verified.**

### HIGH — The track record is orphaned and self‑marked
- **"PEP I — ranked 3rd of 70 2012‑vintage US value‑add/opportunistic funds, tracking to a 51.5% net return."** The page‑13 footnote defines the *ranking* as net **IRR**; the headline "**51.5% net return**" is **not labeled IRR** and is internally coherent only as a cumulative/multiple figure — two different metrics fused into one boast. "*Tracking to generate*" (future tense) on a 2012‑vintage fund that should be fully realized = **unrealized value carried at Pearlmark's own business‑plan marks** (Endnote 2), and the slide's own footnote calls the example **"hypothetical… not representative of a fund's performance."** The "3rd of 70" rank is **unverifiable** (Preqin is paywalled) and is a ranking *of marks*, not realized DPI.
- **"Since 1996 — $14.7B, 600+ investments, 30 years, market‑cycle tested."** Endnote 3 states the predecessor (Transwestern Investment Company, 1996–2012) strategies are **"not comparable"** to today's, and that **"all of the personnel… responsible for managing the predecessor funds are not employed by Pearlmark with the exception of Messrs. Quazzo and Lyons."** The 30‑year record is carried by **two people**, and $14.7B is defined in the deck as a **gross‑deployment tally — not a return record.**
- **"Market‑cycle tested" is selective.** Court records confirm **Transwestern Mezzanine Realty Partners II foreclosed on the 500 W. Monroe office tower (Chicago) in 2011** (*Broadway 500 W. Monroe Mezz II LLC v. Transwestern Mezzanine Realty Partners II*, 80 A.D.3d 483). The deck itself discloses the predecessor **for‑sale‑condo mezzanine book was discontinued** and warehouse‑line leverage dropped from Mezz III on. The honest reading is "tested, partly failed, exited the failing strategies" — which "tested" inverts into a positive. (To the firm's credit, the *current* funds repudiate that structure: no warehouse/repo/CLO leverage.)

### MODERATE‑HIGH — "Skin in the game" pillar contradicted by structure
The pitch's Pillar #1 ("Principal investors vs. advisors — skin in the game, true ownership mindset") is in tension with the firm's own pp.19–20 disclosure: the founders **sold 55.5% control to Conning (closed Mar 2023), ultimately Generali (Apr 2024)**, and Pearlmark is now an **insurance‑captive affiliate** governed by a Conning/Generali‑controlled Board of Managers. GP co‑invest is thin: **~1.2% on flagship Mezz VI ($5.9M / $500M+), ~3.5% on PEP II ($9.3M / $267M).** Disclosed — but the headline pillar diverges from the reality.

### MODERATE‑HIGH — Cross‑capital‑stack conflict, likely anchored by parent capital
Pearlmark simultaneously runs **senior (PRSM I) + mezzanine (Mezz VI) + value‑add equity (PEP II)** across the same middle‑market multifamily/industrial assets. In a workout, the debt funds' interests (foreclose, enforce) are **adverse** to the equity fund's (extend, protect). PRSM I's **"$700M lead investor" is, structurally, most likely Generali insurance capital** (probable, not independently confirmed) — meaning outside LPs are **minority co‑investors alongside the parent**, with the manager incentivized toward the parent's asset‑liability needs over third‑party LP economics.

### MODERATE — Key‑person risk and no standalone live RIA
Two near‑retirement founders (**Quazzo is 66**) are the **only retained track‑record carriers**, atop a thin post‑acquisition bench with **five "[Pending Hire]" roles** on the org chart. Separately, the standalone **Pearlmark RIA (CRD 165471) deregistered 4/9/2018**; post‑Conning the advisory activity likely runs under Conning's registration — but that means **no Pearlmark‑specific Form ADV** exists to verify "$2.6B AUM," custody, or current conflicts.

## 3. What is genuinely good (balance)

- **Deep, permanent‑capital parent (Generali)** — a real positive for a *debt* fund's stability and staying power.
- **Conservative PRSM I structure** — no warehouse/repo/CLO leverage; only a fund‑level facility backed by unfunded commitments.
- **Principals verified clean** — Quazzo (ex‑Goldman RE, ex‑Zell/Equity Institutional Investors, 22‑yr Starwood board, current Marriott Vacations [VAC] and Phillips Edison [PECO] director) and Lyons; **no resume embellishment, no disciplinary history.**
- **The market thesis is real and well‑timed** — the CRE debt‑maturity wall and bank retrenchment genuinely favor alternative lenders.

## 6. Diligence asks — questions to send Pearlmark

**On the track record**
1. Please provide **PEP I's realized DPI and net IRR**, clearly labeled and with the **realized‑vs‑unrealized split**, and confirm whether the "51.5%" figure is a net IRR or a cumulative/multiple return. What share of value remains unrealized, and at what hold assumptions?
2. Please break out the **$14.7B / 600+‑investment record between the Transwestern era (1996–2012) and the Pearlmark era (2012–present)**, and provide **gross‑to‑net** performance for the Pearlmark‑era funds (Mezz III–VI, PEP I–II).
3. What were the **realized loss rates on the Mezz I and Mezz II for‑sale‑condo book** that was discontinued, and on the broader GFC‑era mezzanine portfolio?

**On the fund sizes**
4. Please **reconcile each fund's marketed size to its Form D**: PRSM I's actual closed/funded amount vs "over $450M" (the 12/17/2025 Form D shows $0); PEP II's "$267M" vs the $133.0M on the latest filing; and Mezz VI's "$440M" vs $378.7M — identifying each feeder/SMA tranche and confirming there is no double‑counting.

**On conflicts and alignment**
5. Please provide the **affiliate‑transaction and allocation policy and the LPAC consent rights** governing cross‑fund deals, and confirm whether any PRSM I or Mezz VI loan currently sits in the capital stack of a PEP II (or PEP I) asset.
6. Please **identify the PRSM I "$700M lead investor"** and disclose any **Generali/affiliate side‑letter, MFN, or preferential‑allocation terms**. Are outside LPs co‑investing pari‑passu with the anchor?

**On the firm**
7. Please confirm a **key‑person clause covering both Quazzo and Lyons** and provide the **written succession plan**; and identify the **registered investment adviser of record** for these funds today (Conning's RIA?) and provide its current Form ADV.
