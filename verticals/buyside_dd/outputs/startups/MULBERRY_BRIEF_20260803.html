MULBERRY · 03

The honest read

What is genuinely attractive: the demand shock is real and dated (DFARS 252.225-7052 expands to full supply-chain provenance on 1 Jan 2027, and SmCo is restricted from mining onward — a harder standard than NdFeB's melt-onward rule). Federal capital is unusually available (DPA Title III precedents: e-VAC $94.1M, Noveon $35M). And unlike most reshoring pitches, this team has actually operated the process at commercial scale — that is the rarest input in this sector.
What the memo does to itself: it is written as advocacy, not diligence. It contains no financial statements, no round size, no valuation, no use of proceeds, no unit economics, and no capex plan — while asserting a $550M revenue capacity target. It disparages nine competitors by name without sourcing, including a criminal case (Quadrant) used rhetorically. The Quadrant story cuts both ways: prosecutors charged executives with passing off Chinese-made magnets as domestic — which is precisely the accusation a China-heritage competitor is most exposed to, and the memo raises it without addressing its own vulnerability.

Three questions that decide the investment

  • Who legally owns the IP and the equity, today? Not the plan — the current structure. DFARS/ITAR qualification and any defense prime relationship depends entirely on this answer, and the memo says the compliant structure is still being built.
  • What did the $7M of revenue actually consist of? A "large sample run produced in partnership with our technical partner" is not the same as production output from the Georgia facility. Establish which magnets were made where.
  • What is the capital plan to 1,500 MT/yr? Sintered magnet capacity at that scale is a nine-figure build. Without the capex number, the $550M revenue claim is arithmetic, not a plan.
MULBERRY · 04

Verdict

Interesting, verify-first — 5/10. The sector thesis is one of the few genuinely dated, policy-created supply gaps available to a private investor, and this team is more credible than the average entrant. But every fact that would justify a check is stated rather than shown, and the one structural risk that could void the entire defense thesis — foreign ownership, control, or influence — is explicitly unresolved in the memo's own words.

The correct posture is a diligence sprint, not a pass: request audited financials, the customer POs, the patent schedule, the current cap table with any China-linked holders, and the FOCI mitigation plan. If the $7M is real Georgia production and the ownership structure is already clean, this becomes a serious opportunity with a hard 2027 catalyst. If the revenue is pass-through from a Chinese partner or the structure is unresolved, it is uninvestable for a defense-oriented thesis at any price.
