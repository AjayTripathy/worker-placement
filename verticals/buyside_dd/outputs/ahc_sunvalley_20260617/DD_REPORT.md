## Verdict: NOT INVESTABLE AS PRESENTED
The offering presents AHC's **$58M, 33-unit, for-sale "$2.9M Sun Valley rowhouse"** development. The public record shows a **master-developer-owned, 30-unit *apartment-building* Site Plan** on a Bozeman parcel **AHC does not own or appear on**, marketed at exit prices **~2.2–2.5× the actual local market**, on an **18-month timeline that is physically impossible**, by a **2024 startup with zero delivered homes.** Multiple core representations are contradicted by the entitlement record.

## The decisive finding (primary-sourced)
**American Housing Corp is not the applicant, owner, or representative on the actual City entitlement** — all three roles are the master developer (Bridger Land Group / Blackwood Land Fund LLC), the land is still titled to that entity, and the entitled product is **30 apartment units in 3 buildings**, not 33 for-sale luxury rowhouses. The offering's "Sun Valley" branding, $2.9M for-sale pricing, and "we are developing this" framing are, per the public record, a re-presentation of *someone else's* entitlement on *someone else's* land.

**Fair-line note:** the benign explanation is that AHC is a contracted modular **builder / development partner** on Block 9, and the offering packages an investment in that role. That is plausible and not disqualifying by itself — but it must be reconciled with the record, because what is *marketed* (control, product type, unit count, price, market) diverges from what is *entitled* on every structural axis.

## Market & timing (Mode-B, comp-sourced)
- **Exit price:** $1,006/SF is verified in Ketchum/Sun Valley ID (luxury avg ~$1,018/SF, town median $2.4–2.8M) but **~2.2–2.5× reality in Bozeman** (~$400–450/SF new townhome). Blackwood Groves is an explicit *attainability / mixed-income* master plan (REMU zoning; "Affordable Housing: No" on the app but priced for the median-to-$1M buyer) — the opposite of a $2.9M submarket.
- **Absorption / hold:** 30–33 units at $2.5–2.9M cannot be both built and sold in 18 months in either market. Ketchum absorbs ~100 homes/yr (all price points); Bozeman sold ~74 homes >$2M in all of 2025. Realistic total timeline **~3.5–5 years**, which erases the 44.6% margin through carry and price risk.

## Sponsor (neutral facts)
American Housing Corp — **2024 Austin prefab-rowhouse startup** (steel + fiberglass-cement panels). **One prototype, zero delivered homes, ~5 engineers, ~$200K pre-seed funding.** A factory exists but is nascent (components in certification testing). No demonstrated capacity to deliver a $58M / 30–33-unit development; sector cautionary precedent is **Katerra** ($2B raised, bankrupt 2021). *(The factory's existence is confirmed — consistent with the prior corporate finding that resolved on entity resolution.)*

## Capital structure / LP economics
The waterfall caps the **LP at 1.57× / 35% IRR**, routing **100% of the upside above to the GP** (~$17.3M promote) **plus ~$7.9M of fees** to AHC-affiliated entities. The marketed "35% IRR" is the LP's *ceiling*, not its expectation. Plus the loan-sizing inconsistency (#12).
