The Thesis In One Statement

Credit ≠ price. Underwrite before you reach.

Own the pledges that pay par when tech-driven state revenue collapses — local-property-tax GO and essential-service revenue. Reach for yield only where underwriting proves the spread is compensation, never where the market is pricing a loss. Hold to maturity; harvest the tax shield; never sell into the downturn.

The discipline that separates this from a yield screen

Compensation = (+bps TEY) × P(collect to par). The highest stated yields are the traps; rank by what you actually collect.

Where The Edge Traces

Two insulation mechanisms — plus a parallel.

§53515 levy

School/CCD unlimited-tax GO: a statutory lien on a dedicated, unlimited ad-valorem levy in a segregated I&S fund. Auto-adjusts to cover debt service; insulated from issuer operating distress. Default ≈ 0.

Monopoly revenue

Water / sewer systems: rate-setting monopolies on a captive base. Very low default, no ratepayer substitute. Risk is supply & DSCR, verified from first principles (DWR/Bulletin 118).

RPTTF parallel

Tax-increment (post-2012 successor agencies): a segregated property-tax increment. City general-fund turmoil does not reach the pledge — Oakland's distress ≠ its RPTTF lien. Default ≈ 0.

The AI-crash channel = the State General Fund cap-gains tax

A tech bust craters CA capital-gains revenue → State GF → anything reliant on state aid. All three mechanisms are funded by local property tax, not income tax → structurally AI-insulated (AI-1/2). That is the entire reason the core survives the crash.

Execution Discipline

Where returns are won or lost.

  • Par-call trap: every name is callable at par — never bid above 100. A premium on a near-par callable collapses yield-to-worst to the call and forfeits the premium. All bids capped at par.
  • Taxable gate: odd 3-decimal coupons near par are the taxable-muni signature — verify EMMA TAX STATUS before any ×2.01 gross-up (the gauntlet caught 4 mislabels).
  • De-minimis: deep-discount accretion is taxed as ordinary income (50.25%) — the after-tax TEY already reflects it; never rank on gross YTW.
  • Liquidity tiers: the EMMA two-sided trade tape, not a screen offer, is the execution gate. ~13 names self-directed; the thinner dozen via desk.
  • Migration: fund purchases by selling the fund (VCLAX) in slices — stay duration-matched (rate moves wash out); no rush (~4–5 yr payback on entry friction).

Honest Caveats

Read these before the headline yield.

The Treasury pickup is the tax shield, not credit alpha — pre-tax you give up yield. It's a bet on staying in a high CA+federal bracket; it shrinks if rates fall below ~11% or you leave California.
The SMA edge over a cheap fund is modest (~+0.27 pp after-tax) and partly eroded by one-time odd-lot acquisition cost — the durable value is hold-to-par certainty + the screen, not a yield windfall.
Every reach name reintroduces some exposure the core excludes — charters carry the State-GF/AI channel; even insured CCRC has a thin state-backstop tail. Size against the AI budget, don't free-ride the insulation.
UNVERIFIABLE ≠ clean. Two open items before funding the reach: per-CUSIP tax status on the RPTTF/hospital names, and DSCR/days-cash from the OS on the charters.
The screen is a distress floor, not a ranker — a name passing means "no tripwire fired," not "money-good." Within-tier selection still requires judgment.
