# Hospital Muni — Covenant Tripwire Screen (BBB-tier Universe)

**Run date**: 2026-05-27  
**Obligors evaluated**: 35 (BBB-tier and lower IG)  
**Methodology**: compare current days-cash-on-hand AND DSCR to obligor-specific Master Trust Indenture covenant minimums. Headroom = (current − minimum) / minimum × 100%. Tier bands: GREEN (>30%), YELLOW (15-30%), ORANGE (5-15%), RED (0-5%), TRIPWIRED (current < minimum).

**Why BBB-tier matters**: the AA-tier pilot showed all GREEN because IG-tier names carry 100-500 days cash against 60-75 day covenants. BBB-tier obligors run much closer to covenant minimums — this is where tripwires actually fire.

## Data quality notes

- Covenant terms confidence: see per-row source citations. HIGH = direct from MTI/OS or rating agency quote; MEDIUM = trade press; LOW = industry-typical default.
- Operating metrics confidence: same tagging. BBB-tier names disclose less than IG-tier, so MEDIUM/LOW more common.
- Many BBB-tier names that landed as NO_DATA have missing operating metrics (revenue/margin not surfaced in search) — these are gaps not absences of signal.
- DSCR is the binding covenant for IG-tier names; days-cash is the binding covenant for lower-tier names where liquidity erosion shows up first.
