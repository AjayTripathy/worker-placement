# Municipal Mandate — Proposed Terms (MFO Engagement)

*Negotiating term sheet, not a final agreement. For discussion with the MFO and confirmation by counsel. "Adviser" = the multi-family office; "Client" = the account owner; "Research process" = the Client's own securities-selection and underwriting process (SignalOS).*

---

## 1. Two sleeves, two mandate types

The municipal allocation is split into two sleeves that are **managed and priced differently**, because value is created in different places:

| | **Sleeve A — Core Muni** | **Sleeve B — CA Specialty (Directed)** |
|---|---|---|
| Target size | ~$2,000,000 | ~$1,000,000 |
| Universe | Conventional, liquid, rated CA/national munis | CA insulated school‑GO / essential‑service revenue; intentionally deep‑discount, agency‑unrated, small/illiquid |
| Mandate | **Discretionary** — Adviser selects | **Directed / non‑discretionary** — Client supplies the model |
| Who underwrites credit | Adviser | **Client** (delivered as per‑CUSIP underwriting memos) |
| Adviser's role | Full management | **Execution + custody + reporting only** |
| Proposed fee | 40 bps (or negotiate to 30–35) | **15 bps**, or unbundled (see §5) |

The rest of these terms govern **Sleeve B**, where the bespoke structure lives. Sleeve A is the Adviser's standard discretionary muni service at their standard rate.

---

## 2. Directed mandate — the core mechanic

1. **Client supplies the model.** Client delivers a buy list — CUSIP, side, par quantity, limit price, time‑in‑force — generated and verified by the Research process, refreshed on a stated cadence (daily limit re‑marks available).
2. **Adviser executes as directed.** Adviser acts as executing broker and custodian, **not** as the discretionary manager or credit decision‑maker, for Sleeve B. Adviser does not substitute its own security selection.
3. **No substitution without written approval.** Adviser shall not replace, omit, or re‑weight any directed position, or add a position, without the Client's prior written authorization.
4. **Accumulation window.** Adviser works the list patiently over an agreed window (typically 2–6 weeks), using its dealer relationships, RFQ, and bid‑wanted process to source the odd‑lot and illiquid names.

---

## 3. Deep‑discount carve‑in (the decisive clause)

This sleeve **intentionally and by design** acquires:
- bonds priced **below the de‑minimis threshold** (market‑discount accretion taxed as ordinary income — understood and accepted);
- **agency‑unrated** issues (no third‑party rating on the public feed);
- **small, illiquid** deals that trade infrequently and often offer‑only.

Accordingly:
1. **The Adviser's standard house exclusions for deep‑discount / unrated / small‑deal paper are waived for Sleeve B**, this being a Client‑directed mandate.
2. Each Sleeve‑B purchase is documented as a **Client‑directed (unsolicited) trade**; the Client acknowledges the associated tax (ordinary‑income accretion), liquidity, and concentration characteristics in a one‑time risk acknowledgment.
3. **The Client supplies the credit underwriting** (per‑CUSIP memos: pledge verification, tax status, AB‑1200 / fiscal‑stress checks, hazard overlays, liquidity tape). The Adviser is **not** representing a credit recommendation on Sleeve B and is not the suitability decision‑maker for security selection.

> *Litmus test for the Client: if the Adviser will not execute deep‑discount directed names — i.e., repeats the "we don't buy deep‑discount paper" line as a hard limit rather than a default — then Sleeve B is not a fit for this Adviser and should be self‑directed elsewhere. The entire economic edge lives in exactly the paper their house policy excludes.*

---

## 4. Execution standards (Sleeve B)

1. **Limit discipline.** All orders are **limit only**; no market orders. No lifting an offer more than a stated tolerance (e.g., 0.25 pt) above the last reported MSRB trade without Client authorization.
2. **RFQ to source the second side.** For offer‑only names, Adviser submits RFQs to multiple dealers to source a competing offer toward the Client's limit, rather than lifting a lone rich ask.
3. **Fill‑vs‑limit reporting.** Each fill is reported with execution price, the directed limit, and the prevailing MSRB tape at execution — i.e., realized markup/slippage per trade. (This feeds the Client's decision ledger.)
4. **No principal markups beyond disclosure.** Any dealer markup/markdown is disclosed per MSRB G‑15; Adviser confirms G‑18 fair‑pricing handling.
5. **Settlement, corporate actions, calls/redemptions** processed and reported; call and material‑event notices on held positions surfaced to the Client promptly.

---

## 5. Fee — Sleeve B (the negotiation)

**Headline ask: 15 bps/yr on Sleeve B**, reflecting that the Adviser provides execution, custody, tax‑lot accounting, and consolidated reporting — **not** research, selection, or ongoing credit monitoring (the Client provides those).

Rationale to present: a full 40 bps discretionary fee prices *manager judgment*. On Sleeve B the Adviser exercises none — the Client delivers a verified, limit‑priced model and runs the ongoing surveillance (continuing‑disclosure monitoring, daily re‑marks). The fee should price the *execution‑and‑custody* role it actually is.

**Acceptable alternatives if they resist a 15 bps AUM rate:**
- **Unbundled:** a flat annual custody/admin fee (e.g., $3–5k) **+** disclosed per‑trade execution, **no AUM fee** on Sleeve B. (Often cheaper for a buy‑and‑hold ladder that doesn't turn over.)
- **Tiered:** 20 bps for the first 12 months (build‑out, heavier execution work), stepping to 10–12 bps once the ladder is fully accumulated and in maintenance.

**Walk‑away line:** paying the full 40 bps *discretionary* rate on a sleeve where the Client supplies the alpha and the monitoring is overpaying for an execution role; if the Adviser will not differentiate Sleeve B's fee from Sleeve A's, self‑direct Sleeve B.

## 7. Operating cadence

- **Hold‑to‑maturity default.** Sleeve B is a maturity ladder; minimal turnover. At each call/maturity, reinvestment is **Client‑directed** (new list supplied), not Adviser discretion.
- **Quarterly review:** fills vs. directed limits (realized markup), portfolio vs. a stated muni benchmark net of fees, any flagged disclosure/credit events.
- **Reporting:** monthly statement + the per‑trade execution detail in §4.3.
