## Official-statement read
- Pledge: INSTALLMENT-SALE / Marks-Roos JPA. West County Facilities Financing Authority (a JPA of West County Wastewater District + CSCDA, formed 8/1/2021) issues; bonds payable from Installment Payments made by WEST COUNTY WASTEWATER DISTRICT under an Installment Purchase Agreement (9/1/2021). The Installment Payments are a special obligation of the District, secured by a pledge of and FIRST LIEN on ALL NET REVENUES of the District's wastewater Enterprise (Gross Revenues - O&M). Gross Revenues are broad: all sewer charges PLUS the District's share of County property-tax/tax-increment (redevelopment) allocation PLUS investment earnings; EXCLUDES connection fees and ad-valorem taxes levied to pay GO bonds. Underlying agency = West County Wastewater District (sanitary district founded 1921; western Contra Costa County - San Pablo, parts of Pinole and Richmond, Tara Hills, North Richmond, El Sobrante, etc.; 16.9 sq mi, ~34,000 residences + ~900 commercial, pop ~104,000). DIVERSIFIED ratepayer base: top-10 customers only 11.2% of charges, largest (EBMUD) 4.4%. ~91% of sewer revenue is FLAT charges (consumption-independent, drought-resilient). Billed on the County property-tax roll under a TEETER PLAN -> District receives 100% of billed amounts, no delinquency exposure.
- Lien: FIRST LIEN on Net Revenues, SHARED on a PARITY with four 2015 SWRCB State Revolving Fund (SRF) Loans (~$35.1M total outstanding at 1.0-1.9%). The District covenants it will NOT issue any obligation SENIOR to the Installment Payments (no senior debt permitted). Subordinate debt is permitted under a separate 100% MADS test.
- Rate Covenant: Net Revenues (Gross + Rate-Stabilization-Fund transfers - O&M) >= 120% of Installment Payments + parity DS each Fiscal Year, layered on a sum-sufficient cascade (pay O&M first, then IPs+parity, then other indenture amounts, then additional payments). PRO-FORMA coverage (Table 12) is strong: historical 7.65x/6.90x/4.09x on the small SRF-only DS, settling to ~2.25x/2.49x/2.29x then ~1.64x/1.73x/1.76x (FY24-26) once the 2021 Revenue Bonds (~$4.78M/yr DS) layer on top - comfortably above the 120% covenant. CAVEAT: FY2020-21 AUDITED net revenues dipped to $4.85M (elevated O&M + a depressed year); against post-bond total DS ~$7.49M that single year would have been ~0.65x. The projected 1.6-1.8x recovery DEPENDS on the adopted 5.5%/yr (then 3%) rate increases being realized - rate execution is the key variable.
- Dsrf: NONE. This deal has NO debt-service reserve fund - the TOC and Security section carry only a Rate Stabilization Fund (held by the District, NOT pledged to the bonds), no Reserve Fund/surety. Unusual vs the older insured deals; consistent with a strong-underlying-rating uninsured structure.
- Abt: 120% MADS test. New parity obligations require: no default; AND Net Revenues for the most recent 12 months (ending <=60 days before adoption) >= 120% of Maximum Annual Debt Service on the Installment Payments + all parity to be outstanding (Additional Revenues may be added). Refunding parity that lowers aggregate DS is exempt from (a)/(b). Subordinate obligations allowed on a 100% MADS test.
