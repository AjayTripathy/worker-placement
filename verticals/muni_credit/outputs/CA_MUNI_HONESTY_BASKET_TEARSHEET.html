1. Strategy — Honesty-Alpha Exclusion Screen

This basket is constructed by an honesty-alpha framework: Signal OS is a disclosure-quality (“lying”) detector, not a fundamental-value model. The portfolio is the eligible CA-muni universe MINUS any obligor caught by any narrow, high-precision diligence detector (a union-of-detectors exclusion screen). Each detector is designed to fire on a small slice of the universe (<15%, except the initial news-velocity screen) with high precision; the framework’s contribution is the exclusion of names whose disclosures are misleading, not the re-detection of honestly-disclosed bad news. Insurance wraps (here, California’s Cal-Mortgage / HCAI program) are exploited where they compress spread conditional on underlying credit.

6. Cal-Mortgage (HCAI) Insurance Wrap

The CCRC sleeve (slots 9-10) is insured under California’s Cal-Mortgage / Office of Statewide Health Planning (HCAI) program via a Contract of Insurance (eff. 2023-01-01) insuring principal & interest. On a payment default the State Treasurer issues debentures that are fully and unconditionally guaranteed by the State of California; the investor is effectively buying the State’s AA- credit, not the single-site operator. Cascade monitor as of Jun-2025: the Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance Fund (HFCLIF) held $127,792,891 cash vs the $75M statutory minimum, with one claim in the trailing 18 months (St. Rose, ~$14M). Wrap not impaired. Per the rating-conditional wrap-compression principle, the wrap’s spread benefit is largest where the underlying is weakest.
