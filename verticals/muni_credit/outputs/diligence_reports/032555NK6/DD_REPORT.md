## (b) Security & pledge — what legally compels payment

**Finding: UNLIMITED AD-VALOREM GENERAL OBLIGATION. VERIFIED against the Official Statement.**

- **True GO, not a COP/lease/CFD.** OS cover and body: "The Bonds are **general obligations of the
  District** payable solely from the proceeds of **ad valorem property taxes**" (`raw/official_statement.pdf`,
  cover + "Security and Sources of Payment").
- **Unlimited as to rate or amount (the strongest muni pledge):** "The Board of Supervisors of
  Orange County is empowered and **obligated to annually levy** such ad valorem taxes ... upon all
  property within the District subject to taxation thereby, **without limitation of rate or amount**."
  If the AV base shrinks, the County raises the *rate* — the obligation is uncapped.
- **SB-222 / Gov. Code §53515 statutory lien:** "Pursuant to Section 53515 of the California
  Government Code, each series of the Bonds will be secured by a **statutory lien on all revenues
  received pursuant to the levy and collection of ad valorem** [taxes] ... valid and binding from the
  time the Bonds are executed and delivered." A perfected first lien on the tax stream, ahead of
  general creditors — irrespective of notice.
- **Orange County Teeter Plan (credit positive):** OS confirms Orange County operates the Teeter
  Plan (Cal. Rev. & Tax. Code §4701 et seq.): the County "apportions secured property taxes on an
  **accrual basis when due (irrespective of actual collections)**" to the District. **Taxpayer
  delinquency does not reduce debt-service cash flow** — the County advances the full levy. (Teeter
  excludes Mello-Roos/special-assessment districts; not relevant here — this is a straight GO levy.)
- **Tax status — Tax-exempt.** Bond Counsel (Stradling Yocca Carlson & Rauth): interest and OID are
  excluded from federal gross income, **not** an AMT preference item, and **exempt from California
  personal income tax**.
- **Ratings at issuance:** Moody's **Aa3**, S&P **AA-**. No insurance wrap — the credit rides on the
  District's own unlimited-tax pledge plus the statutory lien (no rating-conditional wrap-compression
  to model).

## (c) Issuer, tax base & enrollment

**Finding: Issuer CONFIRMED; large tax base; ONE material taxpayer concentration (Disney 19.6%);
enrollment declining but it does NOT reach debt service.**

- **CUSIP binding — VERIFIED.** EMMA issue **ES363714** = "Anaheim Elementary School District (Orange
  County, California), General Obligation Bonds, Election of 2010, Series 2016," $61,475,000. The
  08/01/2046 maturity = **3.000% coupon, $23,930,000 par, issued at par (100.00 / 3.00%)**. CUSIP-6
  base **032555** = Anaheim Elementary School District. Cross-checked: EMMA scale JSON, OS maturity
  schedule, and the live trade tape (first prints 2016, consistent with a 2016 issuance).
- **Assessed valuation — large.** OS: total AV (FY2015-16, issuance year) = **$22,261,790,863** on a
  ~350,000-resident base. The 10-year AV history in the OS shows steady growth (local secured
  $15.99B in 2006-07 → $21.22B in 2015-16) through and past the 2008-09 trough. *Vintage caveat: a
  current-year (FY2025-26) AV is UNVERIFIABLE from the documents retrieved at cutoff — EMMA's
  continuing-disclosure partial-view returned empty on repeated AJAX pulls (rate-limit); under CA
  Article XIIIA's ~2%/yr cap-plus-reassessment, AV has directionally grown, not fallen.*
- **Top-taxpayer concentration — MATERIAL, named, mitigated.** OS "Largest Local Secured Taxpayers":
  **Walt Disney Parks and Resorts = ~19.6% of total secured AV** ($4.165B of $22.26B) — the
  Disneyland Resort (two theme parks, three hotels, retail/dining). This is a genuine single-taxpayer
  concentration and is flagged. **Why it does not break the credit:** (i) the pledge is **unlimited
  as to rate** — a Disney AV decline raises the levy *rate*, it does not cut debt service; (ii) the
  **Teeter Plan** means even a Disney *delinquency* is advanced by the County; (iii) the Disneyland
  Resort is a multi-decade, essentially **un-relocatable** fixed asset (the OS notes $16.2B
  parks-segment revenue / $3B operating income at the resort owner); (iv) direct GO debt is only
  0.65% of AV, so coverage is enormous even on a stressed base. Concentration is a credit *texture*
  here, not a credit *risk* to this lien.
- **Debt burden — light.** OS Statement of Direct & Overlapping Debt (ratios to FY2015-16 AV):
  **Direct GO debt = 0.65% of AV**; Total Direct & Overlapping Tax/Assessment Debt = 1.33%; Gross
  Combined Total Debt = 3.45%; Net Combined = 1.80%. Low direct leverage.
- **Enrollment — declining, OPERATING-FUND ONLY.** OS reported FY2016-17 projected **ADA ~17,985**.
  Current cross-checks (district/Wikipedia/ed-data) put PreK-6 enrollment at **~15,000 (2023-24)** —
  consistent with the screen's **-3.2%/yr** decline (≈ -2,900 students over ~7 years off a
  ~17,985 base). **This is a real operating-base erosion** (LCFF per-pupil revenue is enrollment-
  driven; the District faces the same demographic squeeze as most CA urban elementaries). **But GO
  debt service is paid from a SEPARATE, dedicated ad-valorem property levy on the $22B tax base — NOT
  from per-pupil state aid.** Declining enrollment pressures the *general fund*, not the *bond levy*.
  The screen's **AB-1200 POSITIVE** certification confirms no near-term operating distress.
  *Marketability flag (honest):* a multi-year enrollment slide is the kind of narrative that can
  *widen secondary spreads* on a long-dated school GO even when the lien is untouched — i.e., it is a
  **price/liquidity** watch-item at resale, not a credit-impairment item to maturity.

## (f) Tax & yield

**Finding: De-minimis breached → discount accretes as ORDINARY income. The ~8.47% after-tax TEY
already nets this; a naive exempt-only gross-up would overstate it.**

- **Coupon is fully tax-exempt** (federal + CA, non-AMT), per Bond Counsel opinion (§b).
- **The discount is NOT exempt.** Purchase price ~**78.84** vs par 100. With **~20.2 years** to the
  2046 maturity, the IRS de-minimis cushion = 0.25 × 20.2 = **~5.04 pts**, i.e. a threshold price of
  **~94.96**. Purchase at ~78.84 is **far below** that threshold, so the **~21.2-point** market
  discount accretes toward par as **ORDINARY income** — taxed annually under a constant-yield
  election, or as ordinary income at sale/maturity otherwise — **not** as long-term capital gain.
- **TEY math (as screened):** gross yield-to-worst ≈ **4.63%** (EMMA tape; YX = MSRB yield-to-WORST,
  priced to the worse of the 2026 par call vs 2046 maturity — labeled correctly as YTW, not YTM). The
  **after-tax taxable-equivalent yield ≈ 8.47%** already reflects (i) the exempt coupon grossed up at
  the CA top combined bracket and (ii) the ordinary-income drag on the discount accretion netted out.
  The de-minimis breach is a **known, priced** feature, not a hidden catch — it is precisely why the
  headline is stated after-tax.

*Self-discipline note (house rule on TEY conflation): the ~8.47% is an **after-tax
taxable-equivalent** figure — NOT gross YTM and NOT net-of-fees. Gross YTW is ~4.63%.*
