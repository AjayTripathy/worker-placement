# Diligence Report — Vista Unified School District (San Diego County) GO

**CUSIP:** 928346PQ2
**Issue:** General Obligation Bonds, 2018 Election, 2019 Series A (Term Bond)
**Coupon / Maturity:** 4.000% / 2044-08-01
**Cutoff:** 2026-06-20
**Verdict:** **BUY** (insulated GO; enrollment decline is an operating-fund risk that does not reach this pledge)

---

## Three-sentence summary

Vista USD's 928346PQ2 is an uninsured, voter-approved (Prop 39 / 55%) unlimited ad-valorem general
obligation bond secured by a statutory first lien (SB-222, Gov. Code §53515) on the county property-tax
levy — a deep, granular $17.9B tax base (top taxpayer 0.77%, top-20 only 6.42%) with very low leverage
(direct debt 0.47% of AV) and Moody's Aa3 at issuance. The one real negative — a secular, district-projected
enrollment decline (22,400 in FY11-12 falling toward ~19,800 by FY20-21) — is honestly disclosed and is an
**operating general-fund / LCFF risk, not a bond-pledge risk**: debt service is paid from a mandatory
property-tax levy the county must raise as needed regardless of enrollment or assessed value. At ~98.11 the
bond yields 4.15% to worst (≈8.3% taxable-equivalent at the CA top bracket), the discount sits *above* the
de-minimis floor so the coupon stream stays fully tax-exempt, and the only execution caveat is thin secondary
liquidity (close-only IBKR feed, no recent two-sided prints) — a HTM-ladder buy, not a trading position.

**Note on the pledge mechanics (the load-bearing point):** the Bonds are payable *solely* from the
ad-valorem tax the County levies on District property ("Pledged Moneys"); the District's general fund is
**not** a source of repayment (OS pp.12–13). The County Board of Supervisors is *obligated* to levy a tax
rate high enough to cover debt service. If assessed value falls or enrollment shrinks, the county raises the
*rate* — the bondholder's coverage is structural, not operational. This is the distinction that drives the
verdict below.

## b. Tax base — assessed value, concentration, debt-to-AV — VERIFIED

- **AV base (at issuance, FY2018-19):** $17,863,844,311 total ($17.49B local secured). Source: OS pp.13–14,
  California Municipal Statistics. Deep, primarily residential base.
- **AV growth history:** 15-year series shows steady ~5–6%/yr growth (FY17-18 +6.23%, FY18-19 +5.79%),
  with a GFC drawdown (FY09-10 −5.67%, FY10-11 −1.89%) that fully recovered. A drawdown does **not** impair
  the bond — it raises the levy rate.
- **Top-taxpayer concentration (VERIFIED, OS p.18):** #1 MG Preserve at Melrose Apartments 0.77%; #2 Waterleaf
  Apartments 0.67%; **top-20 combined 6.42%** of secured AV. Predominantly apartment complexes and light
  industrial — granular, no single-name dependency. This is an *anti-masking* positive: a school district
  whose largest taxpayer is <1% of AV has essentially no concentration tail.
- **Leverage (VERIFIED, OS p.21):** Direct debt 0.47% of AV; combined direct 0.49%; combined direct +
  overlapping total 1.98% of AV. Very low. (Excludes these Bonds at table date but immaterial to the ratio.)

**Finding:** Tax base is large, granular, and lightly levered. CONSISTENT with the "AI insulation 99" screen —
this is a residential property-tax credit with negligible exposure to the state cap-gains income-tax channel
(school-district GO is paid from local property tax, not the State General Fund).

- **AB-1200:** "The District has filed **positive** certifications for each reporting period in the last five
  years" (OS p.27). VERIFIED — matches the "AB-1200 POSITIVE" screen. General-fund balances positive across the
  shown years.
- **Enrollment — the screen's flagged risk, CONFIRMED:** Historical ADA/enrollment (OS p.23):

  | FY | Enrollment |
  |---|---|
  | 2011-12 | 22,400 |

  | 2018-19 (budgeted) | 21,118 |
  | 2019-20 (projected) | 20,142 |
  | 2020-21 (projected) | 19,808 |

  This is a genuine secular decline (~6% over the historical window, with the District *itself* projecting a
  further ~6% drop into FY20-21). Coastal North County San Diego enrollment pressure (housing cost, declining
  birth cohorts, charter leakage — OS lists 5 District-approved + additional state-approved charters within
  boundaries, p.65) is real and honestly disclosed.

- **Why it does NOT change the GO verdict (the honesty-alpha point):** Enrollment drives **LCFF / ADA-based
  state operating revenue** (the general fund), which the OS expressly states is *not* a source for these
  Bonds. The GO debt service is paid from the unlimited property-tax levy. A district can lose students,
  even close schools, and the bond is still serviced by the county levy on a $17.9B property base. Enrollment
  decline is a credit risk for the District's *operations / COPs / pension capacity*, not for an unlimited-tax
  GO. **Grading on takeaway-vs-data:** the marketed screen ("AB-1200 POSITIVE, insulation 99") does not
  diverge from the data — the data confirms a soft operating picture that is correctly ring-fenced from the
  pledge. No masking; this is a CLEAN disclosed negative, not a buried one.

## d. Call schedule — VERIFIED

- **Optional redemption:** Bonds maturing on/before 2029-08-01 are non-callable; bonds maturing on/after
  2030-08-01 are callable in whole/part on any date **on or after 2029-08-01 at par (100), no premium**
  (OS p.4). This 2044 term bond is therefore par-callable from 2029-08-01.
- **Mandatory sinking fund (this CUSIP, 2044 term):** $1,960k (2040) / $2,150k (2041) / $2,350k (2042) /
  $2,565k (2043) / $2,795k (2044 final) — at par, no premium (OS p.4).
- **Yield-to-worst implication:** at 98.11 (a discount) the worst yield is to **maturity** (4.15%), not to the
  2029 call — a par call on a discount bond can only *help* the holder. Call risk is asymmetric in the
  holder's favor here.

## h. Verdict and mechanism

**BUY — hold-to-maturity ladder allocation.**

**Mechanism (why this is honesty-alpha-clean, not just "good credit"):** the only material negative in the
file — secular enrollment decline — is the kind of disclosed bad news that an over-eager screen might
RED-flag. The edge here is recognizing the **signal-channel mismatch**: enrollment is an operating/LCFF
(general-fund) signal, while the price is set by an **unlimited-tax GO pledge** insulated by SB-222 statutory
lien and a mandatory county levy on a deep, granular, low-leverage property base. The masking channel that
*would* concern us (an operating problem dressed up as a pledge that actually depends on the general fund) is
**absent** — the OS is explicit that the general fund is not a repayment source. Marketed takeaway does not
diverge from the data. The residual risks (flood/EQ to AV; enrollment to operations) are all *base*-side, and
the GO mechanism converts base risk into *rate* risk that bondholders are senior to.

**Conditions on the BUY:**
1. **Liquidity-gated sizing** — HTM only; thin secondary (no IBKR two-sided, no recent prints). Do not size
   above a comfortable hold-to-maturity weight.
2. **Fill the trade-tape coverage gap** — re-pull the EMMA RTRS per-CUSIP tape on a fresh session/IP (or via
   Playwright real-Chrome) before adding, to confirm last-trade level vs the 98.11 mark and current YTW.
3. **Refresh current-state AV & enrollment** — the OS is FY2018-19. Pull the latest annual continuing
   disclosure (EMMA CD endpoint was 403-rate-limited this session) to confirm AV has continued to grow and
   enrollment hasn't deteriorated beyond the projected ~19,800 floor. Neither is expected to change the GO
   verdict, but both update the operating-credit picture.
