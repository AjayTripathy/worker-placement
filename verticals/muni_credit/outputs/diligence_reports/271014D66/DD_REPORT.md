## (a) Verdict, thesis, and resolved issuer

**Verdict: BUY (benchmark-grade, deeply diversified East Bay water-revenue credit; the strongest water name in
the sleeve; honestly disclosed, no red flag).**

**Resolved issuer (CONFIRMED from on-file OS).** Obligor is the **East Bay Municipal Utility District (EBMUD)**,
the large retail water (and separate wastewater) utility serving ~1.4 million people across Alameda and Contra
Costa Counties (Oakland, Berkeley and the East Bay). The security is the **Series 2017 Water System Revenue
Bonds** — a District obligation payable from **Subordinated Water Revenues**. Not a COP/lease/special tax.

**Thesis.** This is the highest-quality water credit in the sleeve: ~1.4M-person service base, **top customer just
4.3% of water-sales revenue** (extreme diversification), uninsured top-tier ratings, and the most liquid name in
the sleeve (178 trades/yr). Two honest structural facts to underwrite: (1) these are **SUBORDINATED** Water
Revenue Bonds (junior lien beneath EBMUD's senior Water System Revenue Bonds), and (2) coverage is managed to a
**1.6x policy target** that included modest Rate-Stabilization-Fund draws in FY2018–19 before improving to 1.77x
with no draws — disclosed and minor against >$350M of reserves. Net of those, it clears every operating gate.

**Supply correction (important):** a prior screen mislabeled EBMUD's supply as "Colorado River / SGMA Kings
basin HIGH." **That is WRONG and is corrected here.** EBMUD runs on **Mokelumne River Sierra snowmelt** (627-sq-mi
watershed, Pardee/Camanche reservoirs) with a Sacramento River (Freeport) drought supplement. EBMUD has **no
Colorado River dependence and is not in the Kings/Tulare SGMA basin**. Its supply risk is Mokelumne hydrology +
Bay-Delta, which is materially different (and more self-sufficient) than the import-fed SoCal names.

## (d) DSCR / rate covenant / WATER SUPPLY / Prop 218

### Debt-service coverage (DSCR)
- **Rate covenant:** the District covenants to set rates so that **Subordinated Water Revenues ≥ 1.1x Debt Service**
  on all Water System Revenue Bonds and Parity Debt (OS "Rate Covenant").
- **Coverage (OS projection + cached continuing-disclosure):** EBMUD manages to a **1.6x policy coverage target**.
  - FY2018 projected 1.60x **including a $26M Rate-Stabilization-Fund draw**; FY2019 1.60x **including a $2M RSF
    draw**; then **1.64x / 1.70x / 1.77x in FY2020–22 with NO further RSF draws** (and RSF deposits resuming).
  - **Engineering assessment:** modest RSF draws were used in FY18–19, but against **>$350M of total unrestricted
    reserves**, and coverage improves organically thereafter to 1.77x with the RSF being *replenished*. This is
    prudent reserve management, **not coverage manufactured to clear a covenant** — the policy target (1.6x) is well
    above the 1.1x covenant, so even an RSF-draw year clears the covenant with a wide margin.
  - **Cached continuing-disclosure coverage series: 2.14 → 2.33 → 2.17 → 2.23 → 2.51x** (all-in basis) — the
    current trajectory is comfortably above both the 1.6x policy target and the 1.1x covenant.

### WATER SUPPLY (load-bearing — lead here; CACHE LABEL CORRECTED)
- **Source: Mokelumne River — Sierra Nevada snowmelt (NOT Colorado River, NOT Kings/SGMA).** EBMUD's primary
  supply is runoff from the **627-square-mile Mokelumne River watershed in the Sierra Nevada**, stored in **Pardee
  and Camanche Reservoirs** and conveyed via the Mokelumne Aqueducts. In drought, EBMUD has access to a
  **Sacramento River supplement via the Freeport Regional Water Authority** (Bay-Delta water).
- **The risk (genuine but self-sufficient relative to peers):**
  - **Mokelumne hydrology / multi-year drought:** snowmelt-dependent; a multi-year drought draws down storage and
    can trigger drought surcharges (the District has a Water Supply Management Plan, drought-response actions, and
    drought surcharges — all disclosed).
  - **Bay-Delta** exposure on the Freeport supplement (regulatory/hydrologic) — but this is a *supplement*, not the
    base supply.
  - **No Colorado River exposure; not in the Kings/Tulare SGMA basin** — the cached "HIGH / Colorado / SGMA Kings"
    tag was a keyword mismatch and is **corrected to: supply risk is Mokelumne-hydrology + Bay-Delta-supplement,
    which is more self-sufficient than the import-fed SoCal names.**
- **Mitigant:** owned Sierra watershed + reservoir storage, the Freeport drought backstop, an active conservation/
  drought-surcharge mechanism, and cost-recovery rate-setting.

### Prop 218
- Water rates are **Prop 218 fees/charges** (majority-protest, cost-of-service). The District adopted a multi-year
  rate schedule. Standard CA friction.
