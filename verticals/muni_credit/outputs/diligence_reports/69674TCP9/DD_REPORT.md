## (a) Verdict, thesis, and resolved issuer

**Verdict: HOLD (clean installment-sale net-revenue structure with organic coverage above covenant, but a
SUBORDINATE lien + State Water Project / adjudicated-Antelope-Valley-groundwater supply + Rate-Stabilization-Fund
reliance keep it firmly at HOLD, not BUY).**

**Resolved issuer (CONFIRMED from on-file OS).** Obligor is the **Palmdale Water District** (a retail water
district in the Antelope Valley, northern LA County); the bonds are issued by its conduit, the **Palmdale Water
District Public Financing Authority**, but are payable from **Net Water Revenues of the District** via an
**Installment Purchase Agreement dated April 1, 2024**. The credit you underwrite is the District's water system,
not the shell Authority. The specific security is the **Subordinate Water Revenue Bonds, Series 2024A**.

**Thesis.** A true installment-sale net-revenue pledge (same family as the EMWD/Eastern template), 110% rate
covenant, organic coverage ~2.14x. Three reasons it is HOLD: (1) these are explicitly **SUBORDINATE** to the
District's outstanding **Senior Obligations** — a junior lien; (2) supply is **State Water Project (Bay-Delta) +
the adjudicated Antelope Valley Groundwater Basin** — both stressed (the cache's "Colorado River" tag is partly
wrong; Palmdale is **SWP + AV groundwater, not Colorado River**); and (3) the coverage stack uses the **Rate
Stabilization Fund** (organic 2.14x, after-RSF-transfer 2.35x) — the organic figure already clears the covenant,
so this is cushion not engineering, but it is a feature to monitor.

## (d) DSCR / rate covenant / WATER SUPPLY / Prop 218

### Debt-service coverage (DSCR)
- **Organic coverage (2023, without RSF transfer): 2.14x;** after-RSF-transfer (if all RSF amounts used): 2.35x
  (cache, OS coverage table). **The organic figure already clears the covenant** — RSF is incremental cushion, not
  the thing that lifts coverage over the line. (Contrast Corcoran, where current coverage sits right on the
  covenant.)
- Subordinate-lien coverage; senior obligations rank ahead of the 2024A.

### Rate covenant
- **110% rate covenant:** the District covenants to fix rates so that **Net Water Revenues equal 110% of Debt
  Service and 100% of Policy Costs** each Fiscal Year (OS "Rate Covenant"). Additional-bonds / reserve test
  references 125% of average Annual Debt Service.

### WATER SUPPLY (load-bearing — lead here; CACHE LABEL PARTLY CORRECTED)
- **Source: State Water Project + adjudicated Antelope Valley Groundwater Basin (NOT Colorado River).**
  - The District holds a **State Water Project (Table A) entitlement**, sourced from the **Bay-Delta** — exposed to
    SWP allocation cuts (Bay-Delta regulatory/hydrologic constraints repeatedly cut SWP allocations).
  - **Local groundwater from the Antelope Valley Groundwater Basin, which is ADJUDICATED** — under the AV
    Judgment the District is entitled to a defined pumping allocation (~39.1% of a defined quantity, plus return-
    flow credit). Pumping is rights-constrained, not freely expandable, and the basin was adjudicated precisely
    because it was in overdraft.
  - The District is developing the **Pure Water Antelope Valley** recycled-water/aquifer-injection project to
    augment supply (a forward mitigant, not yet a delivered supply).
  - **Cache correction:** the screen tagged supply "Colorado River / SGMA HIGH." Palmdale's actual exposure is
    **SWP (Bay-Delta) + adjudicated AV groundwater** — still genuinely HIGH, but the channel is SWP-allocation +
    adjudicated-basin, **not Colorado River.** Corrected.
- **Mitigant:** dual SWP + adjudicated-groundwater portfolio, the Pure Water AV augmentation project (in
  development), and cost-recovery rate-setting.

### Prop 218
- Water rates are **Prop 218 fees/charges** (majority-protest, cost-of-service); multi-year rate schedule adopted.
