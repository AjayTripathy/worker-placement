## (a) Verdict, thesis, and resolved issuer

**Verdict: REJECT.** Four independent REJECT triggers fire on this name: (1) **DSCR sitting on the covenant** —
current continuing-disclosure coverage 1.15x (unaudited) vs a 1.10x covenant, with a **prior-year breach to 1.09x**
in the series; (2) **extreme single-customer dependency** — the system is anchored by the **California State
Prisons**, with top-ten users = 37.1% of revenue; (3) **sole-source groundwater in the worst-subsidence,
critically-overdrafted SGMA basin in California** (Tulare Lake subbasin) with documented land subsidence the City
admits it "does not track"; and (4) **near-non-existent liquidity** (13 trades/yr, 2 two-sided days). Any one of
these would warrant caution; together they make this an unsuitable position for a quality-screened sleeve.

**Resolved issuer (CONFIRMED from on-file OS).** Obligor is the **City of Corcoran Water System**, a ~22-sq-mi
Central Valley farm-and-prison town in **Kings County**. Security is the **Water Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series
2016A**, issued under a March 1, 2008 **Installment Sale Agreement** with the Corcoran Joint Powers Finance
Authority — a true first-lien net-revenue pledge (good structure), but on a fundamentally weak underlying credit
that is **BAM-insured** (the "AA" rating is the insurer's, not Corcoran's).

**Honesty note:** the underlying weakness is *honestly and fully disclosed* in the OS (single-source groundwater,
prison concentration, subsidence, nitrate contamination). This is a genuinely impaired/fragile credit that
discloses its impairment — not a liar. We do not REJECT for dishonesty; we REJECT because the **fundamentals fail
the sleeve's quality rubric** (coverage on the covenant + single-customer + sole-source-overdraft + illiquidity).

## (c) System and customers (concentration test — the catch)

- **System type:** Small retail municipal **water** utility, ~7.5 sq mi, Kings County. Serves residential +
  commercial + the **California State Prisons (Corcoran State Prison + SATF)** located within City limits.
- **Customer concentration — EXTREME / single-customer dependency (REJECT trigger):**
  - **Top-ten water users = 37.1% of total Revenues** (OS / cache).
  - The base is dominated by the **state prison complex and its associated dairy + a local farmer** (the OS
    describes prison-dairy agricultural use and nitrate issues tied to the dairy). Loss, downsizing, or a budget-
    driven curtailment at the State prison would directly impair the revenue base.
  - This is precisely the single-large-offtaker failure mode the sleeve is designed to exclude.

## (d) DSCR / rate covenant / WATER SUPPLY / Prop 218

### Debt-service coverage (DSCR) — sitting on the covenant (REJECT trigger)
- **OS historical coverage:** ~1.40x–2.04x (FY2015 = 1.44x parity-obligation coverage).
- **Current continuing-disclosure series (cache, unaudited): 1.34 → 1.42 → 1.09 → 1.22 → 1.15x.**
  - **A prior year breached to 1.09x — BELOW the 1.10x covenant.** The most-recent reading (1.15x, unaudited) is
    only ~5 bps above the covenant.
  - Coverage has **deteriorated from the OS-vintage ~1.4–2.0x to ~1.1–1.2x** — the opposite of the improving
    trends at the BUY names. There is essentially **no cushion**; a single bad water/rate year breaches.

### Rate covenant
- **1.10x rate covenant** (OS p.186 "1.100"); additional-bonds test 125% of average annual Debt Service. With
  current coverage at 1.15x, the District is operating right at the floor.

### WATER SUPPLY (load-bearing — lead here)
- **Source: GROUNDWATER is the SOLE source of supply** (OS p.4058). Nine wells in two well fields; ~14 MGD capacity
  "depending on the level of groundwater." There is **no surface-water or imported-water alternative** in the
  system itself.
- **The risk (the worst supply profile in the sleeve):**
  - The City sits in the **Tulare Lake subbasin (Kings County)** — among the **most critically-overdrafted SGMA
    basins in California**, with the **most severe documented land subsidence in the state**. The OS states the
    City "is aware of land subsidence above the aquifer underlying its wells … [but] does not track data on the
    rate of subsidence." Subsidence damages well infrastructure and reduces aquifer storage.
  - **Water quality:** documented **nitrate contamination** tied to the adjacent prison dairy, requiring ongoing
    management; the basin's surface-water backstop (Tulare Lake Basin Water Storage District / Kings River) is
    "erratic," providing little water in dry years.
  - SGMA glide-path pumping reductions in the Tulare Lake subbasin directly threaten a sole-source-groundwater
    system with no alternative supply.
- **No meaningful mitigant:** unlike the diversified names, Corcoran has no second source to fall back on. This is
  the single most exposed supply profile of the ten.

### Prop 218
- Water rates are Prop 218 fees/charges; the OS notes rate increases may be needed to comply with the rate
  covenant — i.e., the thin coverage is partly defended by the *ability to raise rates*, which is itself
  218-protestable on a small, concentrated base.

   contamination. No mitigant.
2. **CUSTOMER concentration — extreme single-customer (REJECT-grade):** State prison complex anchors a 37.1%
   top-ten share.
3. **DSCR on the covenant (REJECT-grade):** current 1.15x vs 1.10x covenant, with a 1.09x prior-year breach in the
   series; deteriorating trend, no cushion.
4. **LIQUIDITY (REJECT-grade execution):** 13 trades/yr, 2 two-sided days — near-illiquid.
5. **Structure:** BAM-insured (the wrap is carrying a weak underlying — a tell, not a comfort). First-lien pledge
   is the one structural positive.
6. **Honesty assessment: CLEAN disclosure, REJECT on fundamentals.** The OS honestly discloses every weakness;
   there is no liar to exclude. We REJECT because the underlying fundamentals fail the sleeve's quality rubric
   (coverage / concentration / sole-source supply / liquidity), not because of any takeaway-vs-data divergence.
