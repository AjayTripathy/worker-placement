# Diligence Report — CUSIP 142665GV4

**Carlsbad Unified School District (San Diego County, CA) — Election of 2018 General Obligation Bonds, Series B**
3.000% coupon, term bond due 2046-08-01 · Cutoff: 2026-06-20

## (a) Verdict & buy thesis

**VERDICT: CLEAR TO BUY** (high-quality, S&P "AA" unlimited-tax GO on an affluent coastal tax base;
deep discount is rate-driven, not credit; the only real "catch" — ordinary-income tax on the
discount — is already netted in the after-tax yield; the coastal-flood tail is a monitoring item,
not a default driver).

This is a voter-approved **unlimited ad-valorem general obligation** bond of one of San Diego
County's wealthiest coastal districts. San Diego County is legally **obligated to levy property
taxes without limitation as to rate or amount** to pay it; the levy is backed by a **statutory
first lien** (SB-222 / Gov. Code §53515); and the County runs a **Teeter Plan**, so the District
receives 100% of the secured levy regardless of taxpayer delinquency. Credit quality is **S&P "AA"**
at issuance, leverage is light (**direct GO debt = 0.84% of a $26.7 billion assessed-value base**),
taxpayer concentration is low (**largest taxpayer 1.7% / top-20 ~12%**), and the District carries a
**POSITIVE** state fiscal certification (FY2024-25). The ~80.96 dollar price is a function of the
low 3% coupon in a higher-rate market, not distress — the trade tape shows a regular, two-sided
market. The discount accretes as **ordinary income** (de-minimis breached), and the quoted
**after-tax taxable-equivalent yield (~8.18%)** already nets that drag.

The thesis: buy a near-AA, statutorily-lien-secured, AI-/wildfire-/quake-insulated cash flow at a
deep discount, on a tax base so large and diversified that the one genuine physical question
(coastal flood / sea-level rise) touches a small, NFIP-backstopped sliver of it.

## (b) Security & pledge — what legally compels payment

**Finding: UNLIMITED AD-VALOREM GENERAL OBLIGATION. VERIFIED against the Official Statement.**

- **Claim:** It is a true GO, not a COP / lease / certificate / special-tax (CFD) obligation.
  **VERIFIED** by reading the Official Statement (`raw/official_statement.pdf`, 258 pp, the
  $93,000,000 Carlsbad USD Election of 2018 GO Bonds Series B).
- **The legal mechanism (quoted from the OS cover):** the Board of Supervisors is obligated to levy
  ad valorem taxes "**without limitation as to rate or amount, upon all property within the District
  subject to taxation** … for the payment of principal of and interest on the Bonds when due."
- **Statutory lien (SB-222 / Gov. Code §53515) — quoted from the OS:** "the Bonds will be secured by
  a **statutory lien on all revenues received pursuant to the levy and collection of ad valorem
  property taxes** … The lien automatically attaches, without further action … and is valid and
  binding from the time the Bonds are executed and delivered … enforceable against the District, its
  successor, transferees and creditors … **irrespective of whether such parties have notice of the
  lien** and without the need for physical delivery, recordation, filing or further act." This is the
  strongest form of muni security: a perfected first lien on the tax stream, ahead of general
  creditors.
- **Teeter Plan (credit positive):** San Diego County has adopted the Teeter Plan (Rev. & Tax. Code
  §4701 et seq.); the method "guarantees distribution of 100% of the assessments levied to the
  taxing entity, with the County retaining all penalties and interest." Result: **taxpayer
  delinquency does not reduce debt-service cash flow** — the County advances the full levy.
- **Tax status:** **Tax-exempt.** Bond Counsel (Stradling Yocca Carlson & Rauth) opinion on the OS
  cover: interest (and original issue discount) is excluded from federal gross income, is **not** an
  AMT preference item, and is **exempt from California personal income tax**. This is NOT a taxable
  muni (the taxable-gate signature — odd 3-decimal coupons near par — is absent; this is a clean 3.000%).

There is **no insurance wrap**; the credit rides on the District's own unlimited-tax pledge + the
statutory lien (no rating-conditional wrap-spread compression to model here — the "AA" is the
underlying).

**Enrollment (coastal SD enrollment pressure — a watch item, NOT a debt-service item).**
- OS trend: enrollment **grew** 10,993 (2013-14) → **11,481 (2019-20)**, then dipped to 10,862 in

- This is the real, honestly-acknowledged coastal-SD pressure: neighboring districts are shedding
  students faster — San Diego Unified ~-12% ADA, Oceanside ~-15%, Vista ~-13% over the past decade.
  Carlsbad's ~-7% is **milder than its peers** but the same direction.
- **Why it does NOT impair this bond:** GO debt service is paid from a **separate, dedicated
  ad-valorem property-tax levy** on a $26.7B base, **not** from per-pupil LCFF operating revenue.
  Enrollment decline pressures the *operating fund* (which the POSITIVE AB-1200 cert says is fine for
  3 years); it does not touch the property-tax stream backing the Bonds. Encode it as an
  operating-fund monitoring item, not a credit driver for debt service.

## (f) Tax & yield — deep-discount de-minimis math

**Finding: De-minimis BREACHED → the discount accretes as ORDINARY income. The ~8.18% after-tax TEY
already nets this; a naive exempt-only gross-up would overstate it.**

- **Coupon is fully tax-exempt** (federal + CA, non-AMT), per Bond Counsel (§b).
- **The discount is NOT.** Purchase price ~**80.964** vs par 100. The de-minimis cushion from settle
  (~2026-06-22) to the 2046-08-01 maturity is **0.25% × ~20.11 yrs ≈ 5.03 pts → threshold ≈ 94.97.**
  The purchase price (80.96) is **far below** that threshold, so the **~19.0-point accretion** of the
  discount toward par is taxed as **ordinary income** (annually if a constant-yield election is made,
  else as ordinary income at sale/maturity) — **not** as a long-term capital gain. (Screen flag
  `demin: true` — correct.)
- **TEY math (as screened):** gross **yield-to-worst ≈ 4.44%** (priced to the worse of the 2031 par
  call vs the 2046 maturity). The **after-tax taxable-equivalent yield ≈ 8.18%** already reflects
  (i) the exempt coupon grossed up at the CA top combined bracket and (ii) the ordinary-income tax
  drag on the discount accretion netted out. The de-minimis breach is a **known, priced** feature, not
  a hidden catch — it is precisely why the headline yield is stated after-tax (~8.18%), not as a raw
  exempt gross-up.

*Self-discipline note (house rule on TEY conflation): ~8.18% is an **after-tax taxable-equivalent**
figure, not gross YTM and not net-of-fees. Gross YTW is ~4.44%.*

## (g) Coastal-flood / sea-level note + risks (what could go wrong)

**1. Coastal flood / sea-level rise (the REVIEW screen — a monitoring item, NOT a default driver).**
- The 60% flood-tail screen is below the 90% within-CA hard-tail cut and is **REVIEW, not reject.**
  It fires because Carlsbad is a **coastal** city (the screen includes coastal/storm-surge/sea-level
  exposure, not just riverine).
- **What the exposure actually is (City of Carlsbad Sea Level Rise Vulnerability Assessment):**
  temporary flooding during severe storms + king tides, coastal-bluff erosion, and lagoon flooding —
  concentrated in low-lying spots such as **Carlsbad Boulevard near Encinas Creek** (damaged in the
  Dec-2015 / Jan-2016 storms). State 2024 guidance projects ~1 ft of sea-level rise by 2050. ~4
  census tracts have >25% of buildings with meaningful high-tide/surface/riverine risk.
- **Why it does NOT threaten debt service:** (i) the exposed footprint is a **small slice of a $26.7B
  base** — bluff edges and a few low-lying corridors, not the residential bulk sitting on higher
  coastal terraces; (ii) **NFIP backstops** the flood-insurance channel; (iii) the pledge is
  **unlimited as to rate**, so even a localized AV impairment is met by a higher levy rate, not a
  payment shortfall; (iv) sea-level rise is a **multi-decade** process, slow relative to the
  Article-XIIIA-stable, growing base. *One specific tell worth noting:* the **largest taxpayer,
  Poseidon's seawater desalination plant (1.6–1.7% of AV), is itself a coastal facility** — but it is
  a hardened industrial asset engineered for the shoreline, and even its total loss is <2% of the
  base. Net: REVIEW = "look and confirm it's a slow, diversified, rate-coverable, NFIP-backstopped
  tail," which it is. **Not a default-risk driver.**

**2. Interest-rate / price risk (the dominant risk).** Long-duration 3% coupon; the ~81 price is
*rate-driven*. If rates rise the mark falls — a price/regime risk, **not** credit impairment. HTM
neutralizes it (statutory lien + unlimited levy → par at 2046, subject to the sinking-fund schedule).

**3. Call (mild, in our favor).** Optional redemption at **par on/after 2031-08-01** (bonds maturing
on/before 2031 non-callable). The 2046 term bond also has **mandatory sinking-fund redemption at par
starting 2042-08-01**. At an ~81 price, any optional par call is a windfall to the buyer; the District
has no incentive to call a below-market 3% coupon, so YTW≈YTM(2046) is the realistic case. Asymmetric
upside, not a risk. (Note: the desk structure cache had call_status = UNVERIFIABLE; this report
**resolves it to VERIFIED** from the OS redemption section.)

**4. Earthquake (moderate, Ss 1.10).** San Diego (Rose Canyon / Elsinore) is moderate, below Bay/LA
tiers. Cap correlated quake exposure at the portfolio level; single-name impact is buffered by the
unlimited-rate pledge.

**5. Enrollment decline (operating-fund item, does NOT hit this bond).** ~-7% from 2019 peak, milder
than coastal-SD peers; debt service is on a separate dedicated property levy. POSITIVE AB-1200 cert.

**6. AV-base data vintage (monitoring item).** Verified AV / concentration / debt ratios are the
**FY2020-21** OS vintage. Direction since then is up (Prop-13 cap + coastal market), not down, but a
*current-year* AV and current GO debt-service coverage were not independently re-pulled at cutoff
(EMMA 403). Treat as a monitoring item; the FY2024-25 POSITIVE cert is the freshest independent
fiscal data point.

**7. No distress events found — but absence ≠ exhaustive search.** No default, missed-payment, or
distress material-event notice surfaced. EMMA's material-events list could not be machine-pulled at
cutoff (403). This is "no distress found," not "distress affirmatively ruled out across every filing."
