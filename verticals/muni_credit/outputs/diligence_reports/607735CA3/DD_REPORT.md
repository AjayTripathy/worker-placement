## (a) Verdict & buy thesis

**VERDICT: BUY** (clean unlimited-tax GO; the two real, honestly-surfaced watch-items — a single 10%
taxpayer and a slow SGMA ag-AV tail — are decade-horizon AV-erosion risks, not near-term credit
impairments, and do not break an unlimited-rate pledge held to maturity).

This is a voter-approved **unlimited ad-valorem general obligation** bond of a fiscally-healthy
Central Valley elementary district. Stanislaus County is legally **obligated to levy property taxes
without limitation as to rate or amount** to pay it; the levy is backed by a **statutory first lien**
(SB-222 / Gov. Code §53515); and the County runs a **Teeter Plan**, so the District receives 100% of
the secured levy regardless of taxpayer delinquency. Credit was **Moody's Aa2** at issuance, debt
burden is very light (**direct GO debt = 0.08% of assessed value**; gross combined 1.21%), and the
District carries a **POSITIVE** state fiscal certification (verified against the CDE FY2024-25 second
interim list). The ~81.4 dollar price is a function of the low 3% coupon in a higher-rate market, not
credit distress — the trade tape shows an orderly, genuinely two-sided customer market right at our
level. The discount is taxed as ordinary income (de-minimis decisively breached), and the quoted
**after-tax taxable-equivalent yield (~8.3%)** already nets that drag.

**What keeps this honest (Mode-B catches the framing hid):** two findings the bullet-point screen
summary under-weighted. (1) **Single-taxpayer concentration: E&J Gallo Winery = 10.32% of total AV**
— a real, material concentration, far above the diversified-base norm for our school-GO names. (2)
**SGMA hard flag** — Stanislaus overlies the *critically-overdrafted* San Joaquin Valley
Delta-Mendota subbasin and the tax base is ag-dependent; pumping cuts through 2040 are a slow
levy-base erosion tail. Both are **AV-base risks, not pledge risks**: the pledge is unlimited *as to
rate*, so a shrinking base raises the *rate* rather than impairing debt service, and both risks play
out over a decade against a base that grew ~5-8%/yr into issuance. They are correctly **REVIEW**
items, surfaced and quantified — not disqualifiers — but they make this a *qualified* BUY, not the
"fully diversified, nothing to see" profile of a Chico-type name.

The thesis: buy a near-AA, statutorily-lien-secured, AI-/wildfire-/earthquake-insulated property-tax
cash flow at a deep discount, **knowing and pricing** the Gallo concentration and the SGMA ag-AV
tail, where the only certain "risk" is the ordinary-income tax on the discount that is already in the
after-tax math.

## (b) Security & pledge — what legally compels payment

**Finding: UNLIMITED AD-VALOREM GENERAL OBLIGATION. VERIFIED against the Official Statement.**

- **Claim:** It is a true GO, not a COP / lease / certificate / special-tax (CFD) obligation.
  **Verified** by reading the Official Statement (`raw/official_statement.pdf`, 259 pp; text in
  `raw/os.txt`). The OS cover and security section describe voter-approved GO bonds payable from an
  unlimited ad-valorem tax.
- **The legal mechanism (quoted from the OS):** "The Board of Supervisors of the County has the power
  and is **obligated to levy and collect ad valorem property taxes** for each fiscal year upon the
  taxable property of the District, **without limitation as to rate or amount** (except for certain
  property which is taxable at limited rates)." Repeated in the security section: the District is
  obligated to levy "property taxes upon all property subject to taxation by the District, without
  limitation as to rate or amount." Taxes are levied/collected by **Stanislaus County**.
- **Statutory lien (SB-222 / Gov. Code §53515):** OS, "Statutory Lien" — "Pursuant to State
  Government Code Section 53515, the Bonds will be secured by a **statutory lien on all revenues
  received pursuant to the levy and collection of ad valorem property taxes**" for the bonds. This is
  the strongest form of muni security: a perfected lien on the tax stream, attaching automatically
  and binding on the issuer, successors, and creditors. (OS notes the lien also secures other parity
  GO bonds and does not specify inter-series priority — standard, immaterial for a same-pledge
  buyer.)
- **Teeter Plan (credit positive):** Stanislaus County has adopted the Teeter Plan (Cal. Rev. & Tax.
  §4701 et seq.). Under it "**the District will receive 100% of its ad valorem property tax levied**
  with respect to the Bonds **irrespective of actual delinquencies**." So taxpayer delinquency does
  not reduce debt-service cash flow — the County advances the full levy. (Standard kill-switches: the
  County can discontinue the plan, or terminate it as to the District if the District-wide
  delinquency rate exceeds 3% — neither is in evidence.)
- **Tax status:** **Tax-exempt.** Bond Counsel opinion: interest is "excluded from gross income for
  federal income tax purposes," is **not** an item of tax preference for the federal **alternative
  minimum tax**, and is **exempt from California personal income taxes**.

There is **no insurance wrap** — the credit rides on the District's own unlimited-tax pledge plus the
statutory lien. (No wrap ⇒ no rating-conditional spread compression to model.)

## (f) Tax & yield (deep-discount de-minimis math)

**Finding: De-minimis DECISIVELY breached → the discount accretes as ORDINARY income. The ~8.3%
after-tax TEY already nets this; a naive exempt-only gross-up would overstate it.**

- **Coupon is fully tax-exempt** (federal, non-AMT, + CA), per Bond Counsel (§b).
- **The discount is NOT.** Purchase ~**81.40** vs par 100. With **17.11 years** to the 2043 maturity,
  the IRC de-minimis cushion is 0.25 × 17.11 = **4.28 pts**, i.e. a threshold price of **95.72**. The
  purchase price is **14.3 pts BELOW** that threshold, so the entire **~18.6-pt** accretion toward par
  is taxed as **ordinary income** (annually under a constant-yield election, or at sale/maturity
  otherwise) — **not** long-term capital gain. (Computation in code; `raw/screens_and_sources.json`.)
- **TEY math (as screened):** gross **yield-to-worst ≈ 4.574%** (EMMA tape, field YX = MSRB
  **yield-to-worst** — priced to the worse of the 2027 par call vs 2043 maturity; labeled correctly as
  YTW, not YTM). The headline **after-tax taxable-equivalent yield ≈ 8.3%** already reflects (i) the
  exempt coupon grossed up at the CA top combined bracket and (ii) the **ordinary-income drag on the
  discount accretion** netted out. The de-minimis breach is a *known, priced* feature, not a hidden
  catch — it is *why* the headline yield is stated after-tax rather than as a raw exempt gross-up.

*Self-discipline note (house rule on TEY conflation): ~8.3% is an **after-tax taxable-equivalent**
figure — not gross YTM and not net-of-fees. Gross YTW is ~4.57%.*

## (g) Risks / what could go wrong

1. **Interest-rate / price risk (the dominant risk).** A long-duration 3% coupon; the ~81.4 price is
   **rate-driven**, not credit. If rates rise the mark falls — a price/regime risk, **not** credit
   impairment. HTM neutralizes it (statutory lien + unlimited levy → par at 2043, subject to the
   sinking fund / 2027 par call below).
2. **Single-taxpayer concentration — E&J Gallo at 10.32% of AV (the principal credit watch-item).**
   A Gallo reassessment, relocation, or appeal would shrink the base. **Mitigated** by: unlimited
   *rate* pledge (forces a higher rate, not a shortfall); 0.08% direct-debt-to-AV cushion; and Gallo's
   deep, headquartered, multi-decade fixed plant in Modesto. **Monitor** at each continuing-disclosure
   AV update.
3. **SGMA ag-AV erosion tail (slow, decade-horizon, correlated with #2).** Critically-overdrafted
   Delta-Mendota subbasin + ag-dependent base ⇒ fallowing/down-reassessment of ag land through 2040.
   A drag on AV *growth*, not a default trigger; the unlimited-rate pledge absorbs it. The correlation
   with the Gallo (food-processing) concentration is the reason to treat these two together as one
   "ag-base" risk, not two independent ones.
4. **Flood (REVIEW, below hard cap).** Valley-floor riverine tail at 63%, NFIP/levee-backstopped;
   second-order AV drag via premium repricing, slow. Not a cap; monitor.
5. **Call & sinking fund (in our favor / neutral).** Optionally callable **at par on/after
   2027-08-01**; with the bond at ~81, a call would be a ~23% windfall — the District has no incentive
   to call a below-market 3% coupon, so YTW≈YTM(2043). A **mandatory sinking fund** retires the 2043
   term in steps (2039 $355k, 2040 $365k, 2041 $380k, 2042 $390k, 2043 $400k) — par redemptions that
   *help* a discount buyer (accelerated pull-to-par on a portion).
6. **Operating-fund / enrollment stress (does NOT hit this bond).** CA enrollment decline pressures
   LCFF operating revenue, not the dedicated GO levy; POSITIVE AB-1200 cert indicates no near-term
   operating distress.
7. **Stale continuing disclosure (current AV/coverage UNVERIFIABLE — flagged honestly).** The OS data
   (AV, taxpayers, debt ratios, enrollment) is the **FY2018-19 issuance vintage.** A current-year
   (FY2024-25) AV / coverage / audited-financials package was **not** retrieved from posted EMMA
   continuing disclosures at cutoff. The FY2024-25 POSITIVE certification is the freshest independent
   fiscal data-point and is reassuring, but **current AV, current Gallo %, and current coverage are
   UNVERIFIABLE from primary disclosure** as of this report — a monitoring item, not a clean confirm.
8. **No distress events found — but absence ≠ exhaustive search.** No default, missed-payment, or
   rating-withdrawal surfaced. The EMMA material-events list was not machine-parsed (Terms-of-Use
   wall); this is "**no distress found**," not "distress affirmatively ruled out across every event
   filing."
