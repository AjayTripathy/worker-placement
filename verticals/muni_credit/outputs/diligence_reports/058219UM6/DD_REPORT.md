## (a) Verdict & buy thesis

**VERDICT: BUY** (high-quality unlimited-tax general obligation, deeply discounted, ordinary-income-on-discount already netted in the after-tax yield) — **with two honest monitoring flags: a ~23% enrollment decline since issuance, and the absence of a Teeter Plan (the District bears taxpayer delinquency).**

This is a voter-approved (55% authorization, Nov-2006) **unlimited ad-valorem general obligation** bond of an established Los Angeles Basin unified district. Los Angeles County is legally **obligated to levy property taxes without limitation as to rate or amount** to pay it, and the levy is backed by a **statutory first lien** (California Government Code §53515) that attaches automatically and binds the District's successors and creditors. Credit quality was **investment-grade (Moody's Aa3) at issuance**, the debt burden is light (**direct GO debt = 1.86% of assessed value**), the tax base is large ($5.17B AV) and diversified (top-20 taxpayers = 10.0% of secured AV, no single name above 1.3%), and the District carries a current **POSITIVE** state fiscal certification.

The ~76.65 dollar price is a function of the low 3% coupon in a higher-rate market, **not** credit distress. The discount is taxed as ordinary income (de-minimis breached by a wide margin), and the quoted **after-tax taxable-equivalent yield (~8.6%)** already nets that tax drag.

The thesis: buy a near-AA, statutorily-lien-secured, AI-/wildfire-insulated property-tax cash flow at a deep discount where the dominant "risk" is interest-rate/price (neutralized by hold-to-maturity) and the credit-side watch-items (enrollment, no-Teeter, LA-Basin seismic) are all **downgrade risks, not default risks** for an unlimited-tax GO.

## (b) Security & pledge — what legally compels payment

**Finding: UNLIMITED AD-VALOREM GENERAL OBLIGATION. VERIFIED against the Official Statement (`raw/official_statement.pdf`).**

- **Claim:** It is a true GO, not a COP / lease / certificate / special-tax obligation. **Verified.** The OS cover states: "The Bonds are general obligations of the District payable solely from ad valorem property taxes. The Board of Supervisors of Los Angeles County is empowered and obligated to annually levy ad valorem property taxes upon all property subject to taxation by the District, **without limitation as to rate or amount** … for the payment of principal of and interest on the Bonds when due." (Note: this District also has a *separate* lease-revenue financing through the Baldwin Park/Monrovia Authority — those are a different security and are **not** this bond; UM6 is the direct GO.)
- **Statutory lien (Gov. Code §53515) — VERIFIED (quoted from OS §"Security and Sources of Payment"):** "the Bonds will be secured by a **statutory lien** on all revenues received pursuant to the levy and collection of ad valorem property taxes for the payment thereof. The lien automatically attaches, without further action or authorization … and is valid and binding from the time the Bonds are executed and delivered … **enforceable against the District, its successor, transferees and creditors** … irrespective of whether such parties have notice of the lien and without the need for physical delivery, recordation, filing or further act." This is the strongest standard muni security: a perfected first lien on the tax stream ahead of general creditors. *Honest nuance the OS itself discloses:* the lien secures **all** post-2016 GO bonds of the District pari passu and "does not specify the relative priority … or a method of allocation" if tax revenues were ever insufficient — i.e., the lien is shared across the District's GO stack, not exclusive to this series. Immaterial at a 1.86% debt/AV burden, but disclosed.
- **No Teeter Plan (DIFFERS from a typical Central-Valley district) — VERIFIED:** The OS states "Secured ad valorem property tax collections within the District are **not subject to the Teeter Plan** … and therefore subject to delinquencies." So unlike a Teeter county, the District does **not** automatically receive 100% of the levy — it bears actual taxpayer delinquency. **Mitigant (also in the OS):** the secured delinquency rate has been low and improving — **1.19%–1.44%** in the most recent years shown (FY2014-15 through FY2017-18), down from 2.4% in 2010-11. Low absolute delinquency on a diversified base, but this is a genuine structural difference from a Teeter-backed GO and is flagged.
- **Tax status — VERIFIED tax-exempt.** Bond Counsel (Stradling Yocca Carlson & Rauth) opinion on the OS cover: interest **and original issue discount** are excluded from federal gross income, are **not** an AMT preference item, and are **exempt from California personal income tax**.

There is no bond-insurance wrap; the credit rides on the District's own unlimited-tax pledge and the statutory lien (no rating-conditional wrap-compression to model).

## (f) Tax & yield (de-minimis math)

**Finding: De-minimis breached by a wide margin → the discount accretes as ORDINARY income. The ~8.6% after-tax TEY already nets this; a naive exempt-only gross-up would overstate it.**

- **Coupon is fully tax-exempt** (federal, non-AMT; CA personal income tax), per Bond Counsel (§b). The OS also confirms the bonds carry **original issue discount** that is itself exempt — but on a **secondary purchase at ~76.65**, the relevant tax is the **market discount**, which is *not* exempt.
- **The de-minimis test (computed at cutoff):**
  - Years from purchase (2026-06-20) to maturity (2046-08-01) ≈ **20.1 years**.
  - De-minimis amount = 0.25 pts/yr × 20.1 ≈ **5.03 pts** below par → de-minimis **threshold price ≈ 94.97**.
  - Purchase price **76.65** is **~18 points below** that threshold; the market discount (~**23.3 pts**) **vastly exceeds** de-minimis.
  - **Result:** the accretion of the discount toward par is taxed as **ORDINARY income** (annually under a constant-yield election, or as ordinary income at sale/maturity otherwise) — **not** as long-term capital gain.
- **Yield math (stated with discipline):**
  - **Gross yield-to-maturity (to 2046) ≈ 4.83%** — confirmed by independent recompute from price/coupon/term (matches the book's 4.83%).
  - **Gross yield-to-worst ≈ 4.65%** (EMMA-reported YX = MSRB yield-to-**worst**, priced to the worse of the 2029 par call vs the 2046 maturity — labeled correctly here as YTW, not YTM). Because the bond is deep-discount, a call at par would be a **windfall**, so the District has zero economic incentive to call a below-market 3% coupon → **YTW ≈ YTM(2046)** is the realistic case.
  - **After-tax taxable-equivalent yield ≈ 8.6%** — this is an **after-tax TEY**, not gross YTM and not net-of-fees. It already reflects (i) the exempt coupon grossed up at the CA top combined bracket, **and** (ii) the **ordinary-income tax drag on the discount accretion** netted out. The de-minimis breach is a *known, priced* feature, not a hidden catch — it is precisely why the headline is stated after-tax rather than as a raw exempt gross-up.

*Self-discipline note (house rule on metric conflation): the ~8.6% is an **after-tax taxable-equivalent** figure. Gross YTM ≈ 4.83%; gross YTW ≈ 4.65%. Do not conflate.*

## (g) Risks / what could go wrong

1. **Interest-rate / price risk (the dominant risk).** Long-duration 3% coupon; the ~76.65 price is **rate-driven**, not credit-driven. If rates rise further the mark falls — a price/regime risk, **not** credit impairment. Hold-to-maturity neutralizes it (statutory lien + unlimited levy → principal at par at 2046; 2046 term carries a mandatory sinking fund 2040-2046, so principal returns in tranches over those years rather than as a single 2046 bullet).
2. **No Teeter Plan → District bears delinquency (structural, mitigated).** Unlike a Teeter county, Baldwin Park does **not** receive 100% of the levy regardless of delinquency. Mitigant: recent secured delinquency 1.19-1.44% and falling. A severe local recession could lift delinquency, but the unlimited-rate pledge lets the County re-strike the rate to cover debt service. Flagged, not disqualifying.
3. **Enrollment decline (~23% since issuance) — operating-fund headwind, not a debt-service hit.** Falling rolls pressure LCFF operating revenue and the District's overall fiscal health. It does **not** touch the dedicated GO debt-service levy, and the FY2024-25 **POSITIVE** AB-1200 cert says the District still certifies it can meet its obligations. But it is the honest counter-weight to the bullish framing and the #1 credit monitoring item. *(Enrollment figure is single-source; corroborate at next review.)*
4. **LA-Basin seismic (Ss ≈ 1.90) — downgrade risk, not default risk.** A damaging quake could temporarily depress AV. For an **unlimited-tax** GO this is a rate-of-levy question (raise the rate) plus CA disaster-reassessment relief and state/federal aid — historically a rating event, not a payment default, for CA school GOs. Real, elevated, but the right way to size it is downgrade probability, not default probability.
5. **Flood REVIEW (33% tail).** In the review band but below the 90% hard cutoff; levee/NFIP-backstopped, and (like seismic) an AV/levy question for an unlimited-tax pledge. Monitor, not disqualify.
6. **Shared (pari passu) statutory lien.** The §53515 lien is shared across all post-2016 District GO bonds with no stated allocation priority if revenues were ever insufficient. Immaterial at 1.86% direct debt/AV, but disclosed.
7. **Stale current-year fundamentals (UNVERIFIABLE — flagged honestly).** Current-year (FY2024-25) AV and GO debt-service coverage were **not** retrievable from primary disclosure this session (the recent continuing-disclosure PDFs returned EMMA 403 rate-limit stubs). The FY2024-25 POSITIVE cert is the freshest independent fiscal point. Treat **current AV and current coverage as UNVERIFIABLE** as of this report — a monitoring item, not a clean confirmation.
8. **No distress events affirmatively ruled out.** No default/missed-payment/rating-withdrawal notice surfaced, but the EMMA material-events list could not be machine-scanned this session — this is **"no distress found," not "distress exhaustively ruled out."**

**None of items 2-8 are payment-default channels for an unlimited-tax, statutory-lien GO** — they are downgrade/monitoring channels. The verdict is BUY-with-monitoring, not a clean unqualified BUY.
