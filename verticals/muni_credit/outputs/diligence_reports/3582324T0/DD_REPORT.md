## Three-sentence summary

Every load-bearing claim in the screen verified against primary sources: this is an **unlimited ad-valorem general obligation** of one of California's three largest school districts, secured by a **statutory lien (SB-222 / Govt. Code §53515)** on the property-tax levy, sitting on a large, diffuse, overwhelmingly urban/residential tax base (top-20 taxpayers only 5.3% of assessed value, direct debt ~2.3% of AV), with a **current AB-1200 *positive* certification** the District has held continuously since 2006. The screen's flagged risks are real but low-transmission: the SGMA agricultural-AV erosion channel barely touches this district because **agricultural land is 0.02% of its tax base** (the levy rides on the City of Fresno, 83.5% of AV), and the flood-tail / earthquake flags are tail exposures on the levy base, not repayment risks under an unlimited tax pledge. The one item to correct is **price**: the bond last traded at **99.53 (2026-06-10)**, not 98.45, so it is a modest discount whose call-at-par (8/1/2026, not yet exercised on this maturity) is a holder benefit rather than a risk — yield-to-worst equals yield-to-maturity at ~4.05-4.16% gross (~8.1% tax-equivalent at the CA top bracket).

**Pledge language (verbatim, OS p. 17):** *"The County is empowered and is obligated to annually levy ad valorem taxes for the payment of the Series A Bonds … upon all property within the District subject to taxation … without limitation of rate or amount."* The County of Fresno (not the District's general fund) levies and collects; collections are *"irrevocably pledged"* to a County-held debt-service fund. All District GO bonds rank on a **parity** basis.

**Statutory lien (OS p. 17):** Per **Senate Bill 222 (effective 1/1/2016, codified at California Government Code §53515)**, voter-approved GO bonds are secured by a statutory lien on all ad-valorem tax revenue levied to service them; the lien *"attaches automatically … valid and binding from the time the bonds are executed and delivered … enforceable against the District, its successors, transferees, and creditors … irrespective of whether those parties have notice."* This is the strongest security form in the CA muni market and is correctly described by the screen.

This is **not** a District general-fund obligation, lease/COP, or limited-tax bond — the tax flags below therefore matter only for the levy-base growth path, never for the repayment mechanism.

**Finding (from continuing-disclosure notices):** The District has filed **Notices of Full Optional Redemption dated for the 8/1/2026 call date** that call the **5% current-interest maturities (2038/2039/2041) and the capital-appreciation bonds (2032-2037)** of this same Election-2016 Series A — but **NOT CUSIP 3582324T0 (the 4% 2043 term bond)**. (Saved: `raw/cd_*` redemption notices.) This is economically rational: the District is refunding its high-coupon 5% and accreting-CAB maturities at the first call but leaving the **4% 2043 outstanding**, because a 4% bond trading near par is not worth refunding at current rates.

**Valuation implication:** at a discount price (99.53 < 100), the 8/1/2026 par call is a **benefit** to the holder (par > market), not a call-risk haircut. The worst case for a discount bond is being held to maturity. Therefore **yield-to-worst = yield-to-maturity** here. Do not haircut yield for call risk on this name.

## h. Risks, unverifiables & verdict

**Verified strengths:** unlimited-tax GO + §53515 statutory lien (strongest CA security form); large diffuse urban tax base (top-20 = 5.3% AV); moderate direct debt (2.3% AV); current AB-1200 positive (held since 2006); genuinely liquid; AI- and ag-insulated; discount price with par-call as a holder benefit.

**Risks (honest):**
- **Operating-budget headwind** — declining enrollment (−1,199 / −1,054 students FY27/FY28) erodes LCFF revenue and the reserve glides to 2.77% by FY28. *General-fund* pressure; **does not impair the GO.**
- **Rising parity GO debt** — Measure H ($500M, 2024) adds parity debt over time; raises the levy tax rate, not repayment risk. Monitor combined debt-to-AV as new series price.
- **Credit ≠ price** — in a rising-rate regime this long-duration (2043) 4% bond carries mark-to-market duration risk independent of credit; the 93.6 print in March 2026 shows the rate-driven volatility. This is a HOLD-to-maturity / HTM-ladder instrument.

**UNVERIFIABLE (flagged, not assumed clean):**
- **Current total assessed valuation** — documented at $20.45B (FY 2017-18, 2018 OS); the precise current AV is unverifiable from retrieved docs (no district-wide AV table in the 2025-26 Second Interim or Measure M audit). Direction of error favorable (AV grew). To close: pull the latest senior OS AV table or the FY24-25 ACFR.
- **Current top-taxpayer / overlapping-debt tables** — same staleness; both metrics were healthy in 2018 and the urban base structure is stable.

**Verdict: BUY / AFFIRM IN BOOK.** Every material screen claim verified against EMMA and the Official Statement; the security is the strongest CA muni form on a clean, diffuse, AI- and ag-insulated tax base with a current positive financial certification. Correct the mark to **99.53** (a ~0.5-pt discount, par call is a benefit). Treat as a hold-to-maturity ladder position (duration/rate risk is the live exposure, not credit). Refresh the AV/overlapping-debt tables from the next senior OS or the FY24-25 ACFR to close the two staleness UNVERIFIABLEs.
