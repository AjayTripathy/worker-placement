Executive Summary of Financial Performance

Brown University Health and Affiliates (“Brown Health”) includes both Rhode Island operations
(“Rhode Island”) and newly acquired Massachusetts operations (“Massachusetts”). RI operations
refer to Brown Health’s legacy operations based in Rhode Island, while Massachusetts operations
refer to Massachusetts based operations that were acquired on or after October 1, 2024. Certain
financial information within this report is presented separately for Rhode Island and Massachusetts
operations for year-over-year comparison purposes.

For the nine months ended June 30, 2025, Rhode Island reported total operating revenues of
$2,797.6 million while Massachusetts reported operating revenues of $481.2 million, resulting in
total operating revenues for Brown Health of $3,278.8 million. The operating margin for Rhode
Island was $76.0 million while the operating loss for Massachusetts was ($53.3 million), resulting
in an overall operating margin of $22.7 million for Brown Health. For the quarter ended June 30,
2025, Rhode Island reported total operating revenues of $964.4 million while Massachusetts
reported operating revenues of $161.4 million, resulting in total operating revenues for Brown
Health of $1,125.8 million. The operating margin for Rhode Island was $28.4 million while the
operating loss for Massachusetts was ($32.2 million), resulting in an overall operating loss of ($3.8
million) for Brown Health.

Rhode Island operating revenues for the nine months ended June 30, 2025, increased by $154.8
million (5.9%) compared to the same period ended in 2024 driven by the Brown Health Pharmacy
program revenues ($84.9 million, 43.9%). Rhode Island operating expenses for the nine months
ended June 30, 2025, increased by $150.3 million (5.8%) compared to the same period ended in
2024 driven by increases in supplies and other expenses including drug costs ($86.6 million,
12.2%) and employee compensation and benefit costs ($82.5 million, 5.5%). As a result, Rhode
Island’s operating margin improved by $4.5 million (6.3%). Rhode Island’s operating margin
improved by $16.9 million (147.3%) for the three months ended June 30, 2025, compared to the
same period in 2024, driven primarily by the Pharmacy program margin growth ($14.4 million,
78.5%).

Massachusetts operating revenues for the nine months ended June 30, 2025, were $481.2 million
while total operating expenses were $534.5 million, resulting in a total operating loss of ($53.3
million). For the three months ended June 30, 2025, Massachusetts’s operating loss increased by
$11.1 million (52.8%) compared to the prior quarter, driven primarily by continued volume related
challenges and elevated supplies and compensation and benefits costs. Massachusetts volumes
remain 11.0% below pre-bankruptcy levels. Massachusetts is focused on provider recruitment and
on managing staffing levels to the lower volumes until those providers are onboard.

Non-operating activities for the nine months ended June 30, 2025, were $45.0 million compared to
$62.5 million for the 2024 period. Blended investment performance, for both the long-term
investment pool (LTIP) and the RISE captive pool, yielded returns of +5.9% and +11.6% for the
nine months ended June 30, 2025 and June 30, 2024, respectively.

During the quarter ended June 30, 2025, Brown Health issued $416.8 million of taxable and tax-
exempt long-term debt. Most of the proceeds were used to repay shorter-term interim borrowings
and line of credit balances.

Quarter Ended June 30, 2025 compared to Quarter Ended June 30, 2024.

For the three months ended June 30, 2025, Brown Health reported an operating loss of ($3.8
million), a ($15.3 million) decrease compared to operating income of $11.5 million for the three
months ended June 30, 2024. Excess of revenues over expenses was $48.7 million for the three
months ended June 30, 2025, compared to $13.3 million for the three months ending June 30,
2024, reflecting higher investment income in for the three months ended June 30, 2025, compared
to the three months ended June 30, 2024.

Rhode Island patient service revenue increased by $57.8 million (8.0%) for the three months ended
June 30, 2025, compared to the three months ended June 30, 2024, driven by higher acuity and
favorable payor and rate mix ($36.6 million). Inpatient revenues decreased year over year by $3.8
million associated with a decline in discharges of 4.7%, partially offset by overall rate increases.
Outpatient revenues increased $56.8 million (14.2%) as we continue to see a shift in our overall
statistics from inpatient to outpatient. For the three months ended June 30, 2025, Medicaid State
Directed Payments (SDP) and Disproportionate Share Hospital (DSH) revenues were higher by
$10.0 million (30.2%) compared to the three months ended June 30, 2024. Brown Health’s benefit
under the program declined year-over-year, due to lower program funding from the State of Rhode
Island ($3.9 million) and projected market share lost within the Medicaid population ($1.9 million).
Rhode Island uncompensated care, which includes net charity care and the provision for bad debts,
decreased by $10.7 million (46.5%) driven by a change in accounting estimate as contractual
adjustments are now taken into account prior to calculating charity care effective Q3 FY 2025. The
prior year period was not restated. During the three months ended June 30, 2024, Brown Health
recorded risk-based loss reserves totaling $6.5 million. Brown Health has since substantially exited
these downside risk-based contracts.

Brown Health pharmacy revenues increased by $35.7 million (50.7%) to $106.1 million, incurring
$20.1 million (44.5%) in additional drug costs for the three months ended June 30, 2025 compared
to the three months ended June 30, 2024. The overall pharmacy improvement in margin reflects a
year-over-year increase within specialty drug volume and expanded 340B eligibility.

Rhode Island operating expenses increased by $74.5 million (8.6%) in the three months ended
June 30, 2025 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2024, due to increases in
compensation and benefits of $30.8 million (6.1%), non-pharmacy supplies and other expenses of
$21.4 million (10.6%) and depreciation and amortization of $4.9 million (20.7%).

Rhode Island compensation and benefits costs increased by $30.8 million (6.1%) for the three
months ended June 30, 2025 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2024. The increase
was driven by additional operating and contract labor FTEs ($13.9 million), operational merit
increases, growth in physician support, and all other changes in compensation and fringe benefits
($15.6 million) for the three months ended June 30, 2025 compared to the three months ended
June 30, 2024. Self-insured health expenses increased by $1.3 million for the three months ended
June 30, 2025 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2024..

Rhode Island operations supplies costs increased by $41.5 million (16.8%) for the three months
ended June 30, 2025 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2024. This increase includes
the above-mentioned growth within the retail and contract pharmacies ($20.1 million).

Balance Sheet Discussion.

Cash and cash equivalents increased by $132.5 million to $288.3 million at June 30, 2025 from
$155.8 million as of September 30, 2024. The increase was driven by the receipt of SDP funds.

Rhode Island Days in AR improved to 37.8 days as of June 30, 2025, from 38.2 days as of
September 30, 2024. Massachusetts Days in AR remain elevated at 56.2 as of June 30, 2025,
reflecting payor delays primarily at the physician organization. Brown Health is focused on denials
management, and Massachusetts is scheduled to go live on Epic in Q4, a major step in unifying
the billing and collection process.

Assets limited as to use increased $346.8 million to $2,266.4 million at June 30, 2025, from
$1,919.6 million at September 30, 2024. The increase reflects bond proceeds received and
favorable investment returns for the nine months ended June 30, 2025, less funds requisitioned.

Other accrued expenses increased $152.8 million to $225.3 million at June 30, 2025, from $72.5
million at September 30, 2024, driven primarily by accrued license fees payable and deferred
revenue associated with a cash advance received from the State of Massachusetts. Brown Health
paid $125.3 million in license fees in July 2025.

Long-term debt, including the current portion, increased $500.5 million to $1,221.0 million at June
30, 2025, from $720.5 million at September 30, 2024, driven primarily by the financing activities
associated with the MA acquisition and related liquidity needs. During the third quarter, Brown
Health issued Series 2025 tax-exempt and taxable bonds totaling $416.8 million. Proceeds were
used to reimburse prior capital expenditures and to refinance interim borrowings and lines of credit
deployed in the first half of the year. The residual proceeds were deployed in short-term
investments.
