MERGED_INTO_COMMONSPIRIT

Centura Health was a 27-year joint venture between CommonSpirit Health and AdventHealth.
The breakup was announced February 14, 2023 and finalized August 1, 2023.

- CommonSpirit Health absorbed approximately 20 Colorado/Kansas hospitals and now manages
  them directly (no separate Centura disclosure entity).
- AdventHealth absorbed the remaining ~5 Colorado hospitals.
- The Centura.org domain now redirects to CommonSpirit-branded patient pages.
- No standalone Centura Health audited financial statement, annual report, or
  EMMA continuing-disclosure filing has been issued for FY2024 or FY2025.

For Centura-derived bond obligations / financial information after the breakup,
see CommonSpirit Health continuing disclosures (already downloaded as
commonspirit_mda.pdf / .txt in this directory).
