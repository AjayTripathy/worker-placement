(the &#147;Company&#148;) completed its initial public offering (&#147;IPO&#148;) of 20,000,000 units (&#147;Units&#148;), each Unit consisting of one share of common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (&#147;Common Stock&#148;), and one redeemable warrant (&#147;Warrant&#148;), each Warrant exercisable to purchase one share of Common Stock, pursuant to the Company&#146;s registration statement on Form&nbsp;S-1, as amended (File No.&nbsp;333-224351).
