<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">Each of the Charter Amendment and the Adjournment
Proposal are more fully described in the accompanying proxy statement.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">The purpose of the Charter Amendment is
to allow VectoIQ more time to complete its proposed business combination (the &#8220;Transaction&#8221;) with Nikola Corporation
(&#8220;Nikola&#8221;) pursuant to the Business Combination Agreement, dated as of March 2, 2020 (as it may be amended, the &#8220;Business
Combination Agreement&#8221;), by and among VectoIQ, VCTIQ Merger Sub Corp. (&#8220;Merger Sub&#8221;) and Nikola. VectoIQ&#8217;s
initial public offering (&#8220;IPO&#8221;) prospectus and charter provide that VectoIQ has until May 18, 2020 to complete a business
combination. While we have entered into the Business Combination Agreement with Merger Sub and Nikola and we have filed a registration
statement containing a preliminary proxy statement with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission in respect of the Transaction,
our board of directors (the &#8220;Board&#8221;) currently believes that there may not be sufficient time before May 18, 2020 to
hold a special meeting to vote for stockholder approval of the Transaction and consummate the Transaction. Accordingly, the Board
believes that in order to be able to consummate the Transaction, we will need to obtain the Extension. Therefore, the Board has
determined that it is in the best interests of our stockholders to extend the date that VectoIQ has to consummate a business combination
to the Extended Date.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">Reasons for the Proposal</P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">VectoIQ&#8217;s IPO prospectus and charter
provide that VectoIQ has until May 18, 2020 to consummate a business combination. The proposed Transaction with Nikola qualifies
as an initial business combination under VectoIQ&#8217;s charter, but the Company believes it may not be able to complete the Transaction
by May 18, 2020. The Charter Amendment is essential to allowing the Company more time to obtain approval for the Transaction at
a special meeting of its stockholders and consummate the Transaction prior to July 31, 2020. Approval of the Charter Amendment
is a condition to the implementation of the Extension. The Company believes that given the Company&#8217;s expenditure of time,
effort and money on the proposed Transaction with Nikola, circumstances warrant providing public stockholders an opportunity to
consider the proposed Transaction. The affirmative vote of the holders of at least 65% of all outstanding shares of common stock
is required to extend VectoIQ&#8217;s corporate existence, except in connection with, and effective upon consummation of, a business
combination. Additionally, VectoIQ&#8217;s IPO prospectus and charter provide for all public stockholders to have an opportunity
to redeem their public shares in the case VectoIQ&#8217;s corporate existence is extended as described above. Because VectoIQ continues
to believe that a business combination would be in the best interests of VectoIQ&#8217;s stockholders, and because VectoIQ may
not be able to conclude a business combination within the permitted time period, VectoIQ has determined to seek stockholder approval
to extend the date by which VectoIQ has to complete a business combination beyond May 18, 2020 to the Extended Date.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">We believe that the foregoing charter provisions
were included to protect VectoIQ stockholders from having to sustain their investments for an unreasonably long period, if VectoIQ
failed to find a suitable business combination in the timeframe contemplated by the charter. We also believe, however, that given
VectoIQ&#8217;s expenditure of time, effort and money on the proposed Transaction with Nikola, circumstances warrant providing
those who would like to consider the proposed Transaction, inasmuch as VectoIQ is also affording stockholders who wish to redeem
their public shares the opportunity to do so, as required under its charter. Accordingly, the Extension is consistent with VectoIQ&#8217;s
charter and IPO prospectus.</P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">The Board&#8217;s Reasons for the Charter Amendment Proposal
and Its Recommendation</P>

<P STYLE="font: bold 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">As discussed below, after careful consideration
of all relevant factors, the Board has determined that the Charter Amendment proposal is fair to, and in the best interests of,
VectoIQ and its stockholders. The Board has approved and declared advisable adoption of the Charter Amendment proposal, and recommends
that you vote &#8220;FOR&#8221; such adoption. The Board expresses no opinion as to whether you should redeem your public shares.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">We are a Delaware company incorporated on
January 23, 2018 for the purpose of entering into a merger, stock exchange, asset acquisition, stock purchase, recapitalization,
reorganization or other similar business combination with one or more businesses or entities. On May 18, 2018, we consummated our
IPO of 23,000,000 units, with each unit consisting of one share of common stock and one redeemable warrant. Each whole warrant
entitles the holder to purchase one share of common stock at a price of $11.50. On May 29, 2018, the underwriters exercised their
over-allotment option in full to purchase an additional 3,000,000 units. The units were sold at an offering price of $10.00 per
unit, generating gross proceeds of $230,000,000.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">VectoIQ&#8217;s IPO prospectus and charter
provide that VectoIQ has until May 18, 2020 to consummate a business combination. The proposed Transaction with Nikola qualifies
as an initial business combination under VectoIQ&#8217;s charter, but the Company believes it may not be able to complete the Transaction
by May 18, 2020. The Charter Amendment is essential to allowing the Company more time to obtain approval for the Transaction at
a special meeting of its stockholders and consummate the Transaction prior to July 31, 2020. Approval of the Charter Amendment
is a condition to the implementation of the Extension. The Company believes that given the Company&#8217;s expenditure of time,
effort and money on the proposed business combination with Nikola, circumstances warrant providing public stockholders an opportunity
to consider the proposed Transaction. The affirmative vote of the holders of at least 65% of all outstanding shares of common stock
is required to extend VectoIQ&#8217;s corporate existence, except in connection with, and effective upon consummation of, a business
combination. Additionally, VectoIQ&#8217;s IPO prospectus and charter provide for all public stockholders to have an opportunity
to redeem their public shares in the case VectoIQ&#8217;s corporate existence is extended as described above. Because VectoIQ continues
to believe that a business combination would be in the best interests of VectoIQ&#8217;s stockholders, and because VectoIQ may
not be able to conclude a business combination within the permitted time period, VectoIQ has determined to seek stockholder approval
to extend the date by which VectoIQ has to complete a business combination beyond May 18, 2020 to the Extended Date.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-indent: 0.5in">VectoIQ is not asking you to vote on the
