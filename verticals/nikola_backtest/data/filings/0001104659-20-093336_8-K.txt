<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">On August 10,
2020, Nikola Corporation (the &#8220;Company&#8221;) announced that <FONT STYLE="background-color: white">Republic Services,
Inc. agreed to order from the Company 2,500 electrified refuse trucks, with the ability to increase the order to up to 5,000
units.</FONT> The electric platform is expected to offer  up to 150 miles and 1,200 cans on a single charge, and the refuse
trucks are anticipated to carry up to 720kWh of energy storage. The Company <FONT STYLE="background-color: white">expects </FONT>to
begin full production deliveries in 2023 with on-road testing expected to begin in early 2022.</P>
