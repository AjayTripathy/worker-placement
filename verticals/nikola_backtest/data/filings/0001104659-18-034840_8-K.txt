<p style="margin:0in 0in .0001pt;"><b><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;font-weight:bold;">Item 3.02.&nbsp; Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities.</font></b></p>
<p style="margin:0in 0in .0001pt;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">&nbsp;</font></p>
<p style="margin:0in 0in .0001pt;text-indent:.5in;"><font size="2" face="Times New Roman" style="font-size:10.0pt;">On May&nbsp;18, 2018, simultaneously with the consummation of the initial public offering (the &#147;IPO&#148;) of units (&#147;Units&#148;) of VectoIQ Acquisition Corp. (the &#147;Company&#148;), the Company consummated the private placement (the &#147;Private Placement&#148;) of an aggregate of 800,000 units (the &#147;Private Units&#148;) at a price of $10.00 per Private Unit, generating total gross proceeds of $8,000,000. No underwriting discounts or commissions were paid with respect to the Private Placement. The Private Placement was conducted as a non-public transaction and, as a transaction by an issuer not involving a public offering, is exempt from registration under the Securities Act in reliance upon Section&nbsp;4(a)(2)&nbsp;of the Securities Act of 1933 (as amended, the &#147;Securities Act&#148;). In the Private Placement, VectoIQ Holdings, LLC (the &#147;Sponsor&#148;) purchased 468,368 Private Units, certain funds and accounts managed by subsidiaries of BlackRock,&nbsp;Inc. purchased 67,424 Private Units and Cowen Investments II LLC (&#147;Cowen Investments&#148;) purchased 264,208 Private Units. The Private Units are identical to the Units, except that if held by the original holder or their permitted assigns, the underlying warrants (i)&nbsp;may be exercised on a cashless basis, (ii)&nbsp;are not subject to redemption and (iii)&nbsp;with respect to private warrants held by Cowen Investments, will not be exercisable more than five years after the Effective Date of the Registration Statement (as such terms are defined in Item 8.01 below). If the Private Units are held by holders other than the initial purchasers or their permitted transferees, then the warrants included in the Private Units will be redeemable by the Company and exercisable by the holders on the same basis as the warrants included in the Units sold in the IPO.</font></p>
