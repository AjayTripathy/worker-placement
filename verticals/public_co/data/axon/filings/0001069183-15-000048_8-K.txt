EX-99.1 Item 8.01 Other Events On May 5, 2015, TASER International, Inc. ("the Company") issued a press release announcing an agreement pursuant to which the Company will acquire all of the outstanding capital stock of MediaSolv Solutions Corporation (“MediaSolv”), a Delaware corporation. The purchase price consists of $8.0 million cash at close, and up to an additional $5.0 million in the form of earn-outs, subject to the achievement of defined performance metrics. MediaSolv provides digital evidence management solutions to law enforcement agencies that allows for multiple sources of evidence to be digitized, stored, searched, retrieved and viewed within a single digital library. A copy of the press release issued by the Company is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated into this Item 8.01 by reference.
