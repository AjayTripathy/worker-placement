Morgan Securities LLC, as representative of the several underwriters listed therein (the “ Underwriters ”), pursuant to which the Company agreed to issue and sell 3,000,000 shares of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.00001 per share (the “ Shares ”), in an underwritten public offering. The Shares being offered were registered by the Company under an automatically effective shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-224918) filed with the SEC on May 14, 2018 and are being offered pursuant to a preliminary prospectus supplement, dated June 15, 2020, and a final prospectus supplement, dated June 16, 2020, describing the terms of the offering.
