CONTENTS On February 3, 2015, Stratasys Ltd. (“ we ” or the “ Company ”) held an extraordinary general meeting of shareholders (the “ Meeting ”). At the Meeting, our shareholders voted on three proposals, which are described in more detail in our proxy statement for the Meeting (the “Proxy Statement” ) that was attached as Exhibit 99.1 to a Report of Foreign Private Issuer on Form 6-K that we furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission on January 6, 2015. The results of the proposals presented at the Meeting, based on the presence in person or by proxy of holders of 31,277,005 (61.42 %) of our outstanding ordinary shares on the record date of January 2, 2015, are described below.
