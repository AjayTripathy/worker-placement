Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. On January 21, 2015, Woodward, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2014 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “2014 Annual Meeting”). The stockholders considered three proposals, each of which is described more fully in the Company’s proxy statement for the 2014 Annual Meeting.
