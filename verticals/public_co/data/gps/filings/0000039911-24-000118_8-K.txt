Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. On May 7, 2024, the Company held its annual meeting of shareholders (the "Annual Meeting"). As of March 11, 2024, the record date for the Annual Meeting, there were a total of 372,226,326 shares of the Company’s common stock outstanding and entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting. At the Annual Meeting, 348,804,578 shares of common stock were represented in person or by proxy and, therefore, a quorum was present.
