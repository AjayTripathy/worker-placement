Other Events. Between August 4, 2003 and August 8, 2003, we issued 500,000 shares of our registered common stock pursuant to the exercise of warrants by institutional investors who participated in our private placement of common stock in September, 2002. This completes the exercise of all warrants whose underlying shares were registered under that registration statement.
