Other Events Applied Digital Corporation (the “Company”) continues to re-energize its Ellendale data center hosting facility as additional repairs and upgrades are made to its equipment. As the repairs and upgrades are completed, the Company expects to ramp capacity over the quarter. The Company currently estimates the impact of this downtime on the Company’s revenues will continue to be material until these repairs and upgrades are substantially complete. The reduced capacity has no impact on our HPC hosting data centers under construction, which are being designed to provide higher levels of availability in accordance with industry standards. The reduced capacity also has no impact on our Cloud services business, as those services are provided using third-party facilities.
