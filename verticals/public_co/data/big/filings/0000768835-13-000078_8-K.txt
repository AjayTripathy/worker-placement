At the 2013 Annual Meeting of Shareholders of Big Lots, Inc. (“we,” “us” or “our”), Russell Solt received fewer votes for his election to our Board of Directors than votes withheld from his election. As a result, Mr. Solt offered to resign from the Board in accordance with our Corporate Governance Guidelines. On August 26, 2013, we issued a press release announcing that our Board of Directors has, after careful consideration, determined not to accept Mr. Solt's offer to resign.
