Sales Agreement On December 5, 2022,
Quantum Computing Inc. (the “Company”) entered into an At-The-Market Issuance Sales Agreement (the “ATM Agreement”)
with Ascendiant Capital Markets, LLC (“Ascendiant”) under which the Company may offer and sell, from time to time at its sole
discretion, shares of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (the “Common Stock”), with aggregate gross
sales proceeds of up to $25,000,000 through an “at the market” equity offering program under which Ascendiant will act as
sales agent. Under the ATM Agreement, the Company will set
the parameters for the sale of shares, including the number of shares to be issued, the time period during which sales are requested to
be made, limitations on the number of shares that may be sold in any one trading day and any minimum price below which sales may not be
made. Subject to the terms and conditions of the ATM Agreement, Ascendiant may sell the shares by methods deemed to be an “at the
market” offering as defined in Rule 415 promulgated under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, including sales made through The
Nasdaq Capital Market or any other trading market for our common stock. The ATM Agreement provides that Ascendiant will
be entitled to compensation for its services equal to 3.0% of the gross proceeds of any shares of common stock sold through Ascendiant
under the ATM Agreement. The Company has no obligation to sell any shares under the ATM Agreement, and may at any time suspend solicitation
and offers under the ATM Agreement. The shares will be issued pursuant to the Company’s
shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-268064). The Company filed a prospectus supplement, dated December 5, 2022 with
the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) in connection with the offer and sale of the shares pursuant to the ATM
Agreement (the “Prospectus Supplement”). The foregoing description of the material terms
of the ATM Agreement is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 1.1 to this
Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference. The legal opinion of Lucosky Brookman LLP relating
to the shares of Common Stock that may be sold pursuant to ATM Agreement is filed as Exhibit 5.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K. This Current Report on Form 8-K shall not constitute
