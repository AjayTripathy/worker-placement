(CUSIP 913017 CT4) Section 5 - Corporate Governance and Management Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. United Technologies Corporation (“UTC” or "Company") held its 2019 Annual Meeting of Shareowners on April 29, 2019. As of February 28, 2019, the record date for the meeting, 862,332,297 shares of UTC common stock were issued and outstanding. A quorum of 775,594,061 shares of common stock was present or represented at the meeting.
