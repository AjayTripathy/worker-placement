The Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “ Annual Meeting ”) of Iridium Communications Inc. (the “ Company ”) was held virtually on May 14, 2020. Of the 132,217,098 shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share, outstanding as of the close of business on the record date of March 16, 2020, 124,009,976 shares, or approximately 93.8%, were present or represented by proxy at the Annual Meeting.
