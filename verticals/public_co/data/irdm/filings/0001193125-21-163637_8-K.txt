The Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “ Annual Meeting ”) of Iridium Communications Inc. (the “ Company ”) was held virtually on May 12, 2021. Of the 134,111,085 shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share, outstanding as of the close of business on the record date of March 15, 2021, 123,984,091 shares, or approximately 92.44%, were present or represented by proxy at the Annual Meeting.
