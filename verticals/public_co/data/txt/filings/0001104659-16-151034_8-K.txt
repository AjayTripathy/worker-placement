Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities. Item 2.06 Material Impairments. On August 30, 2016, in a Current Report on Form 8-K, we reported that our Board of Directors approved a plan to restructure and realign our businesses by implementing headcount reductions, facility consolidations and other actions in order to improve overall operating efficiency across Textron. As a result of ongoing evaluations, we decided to take additional restructuring actions, principally headcount reductions, in our Textron Aviation segment, as well as other businesses, beyond that already included in our plan as originally announced. We now expect total pre-tax charges in the range of $140 million to $170 million to be incurred under this plan, of which $115 million was recorded in the third quarter of 2016. Expected cash outlays in connection with this plan are estimated to be in the range of $100 million to $120 million, approximately half of which is expected to be expended in 2016 and the remainder in 2017.
