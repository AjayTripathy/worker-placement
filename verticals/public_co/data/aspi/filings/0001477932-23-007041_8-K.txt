On September 20, 2023, ASP Isotopes Inc. (the “Company”) received a prepayment of approximately $900,000 from a customer pursuant to the terms of the previously announced supply agreement for a highly enriched isotope entered into by the Company and a US customer valued at approximately $9 million.
