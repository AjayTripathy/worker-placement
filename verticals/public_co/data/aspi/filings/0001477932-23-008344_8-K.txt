Other Events. Mutual Release of Claims On November 8, 2023, ASP Isotopes Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a release agreement with Norsk Medisinsk Syklotronsenter AS (“NMS”), Radfarma AS (“Radfarma”), and certain board members and shareholders of Radfarma related to, among other things, claims asserted in a letter the Company received from a law firm acting on behalf of NMS, dated October 25, 2022 (the “NMS Letter”), which included a claim that the grant of the former license to the ASP technology to the Company by Klydon Proprietary Ltd violated a pre-existing exclusive sub-license to the ASP technology granted to Radfarma. In the release agreement, the parties agreed to a mutual release of claims related to, among other things, the facts, circumstances and matters asserted in the NMS Letter, including claims of a license of rights to the ASP technology.
