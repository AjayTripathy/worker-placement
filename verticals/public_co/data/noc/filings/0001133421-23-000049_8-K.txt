Other Events. On September 20, 2023, the Board of Directors of Northrop Grumman Corporation (the “Company”) elected Robert J. Fleming as Corporate Vice President and President, Space Systems, effective October 9, 2023. Mr. Fleming will succeed Thomas L. Wilson, who will transition to a new role in the Company. Mr. Fleming joined the Company in 2005 and has led different businesses and functions in many of the Company’s sectors, including the Space Systems strategy and business development organizations. He is currently Vice President and General Manager of the Strategic Space Systems division in the Space Systems sector.
