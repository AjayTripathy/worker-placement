(e) On February 18, 2025, the Compensation and Human Capital Committee and the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Northrop Grumman Corporation (the "Company") approved the following compensation-related actions for the Company's named executive officers. • Approved the 2025 goals under the 2006 Annual Incentive Plan and Incentive Compensation Plan, as amended and restated effective January 1, 2024 (the "ICP"). The financial metrics selected for inclusion in the ICP (and their relative weightings) are as follows: cash flow from operations before discretionary pension funding (35%); segment operating income growth (35%); adjusted operating margin rate* (20%); and non-financial metrics (10%). The non-financial metrics in the ICP are: (1) Inclusion and Belonging, (2) Environmental Sustainability, (3) Quality and (4) Customer Satisfaction. • Awarded Restricted Performance Stock Rights ("RPSR") for the performance period 2025-2027 and approved the metrics for such RPSR awards (and their relative weightings), which will be measured in terms of cumulative free cash flow (1/3); return on invested capital (1/3); and relative total shareholder return (1/3). Awarded Restricted Stock Rights ("RSR") that will vest on February 18, 2028. Similar to prior years, the Compensation and Human Capital Committee did not award any stock options. There are no material changes from 2024 in the terms of the 2025 RPSR and RSR awards for the named executive officers. ________________ * Adjusted for net pension, purchased intangible amortization, certain other acquisition and divestiture-related items, unforeseen macroeconomic pressures and plan overlays. On February 18, 2025, the Board approved amendments to the Severance Plan for Elected and Appointed Officers of Northrop Grumman Corporation, which included a reduction in the severance timeframe from 18 months to 12 months for most executive officers (including named executive officers), other than the Chief Executive Officer, and changes to an officer's pro-rated bonus to provide the Company with discretion to determine the applicable individual performance factor for such officer. (b) On February 17, 2025, Graham Robinson notified the Board of his decision not to seek re-election at the 2025 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. He will remain on the Board until the day of the Annual Meeting.
