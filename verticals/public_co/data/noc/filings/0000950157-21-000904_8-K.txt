 (as supplemented, amended or otherwise modified prior to the date of execution of the Supplemental Indentures, the “Existing Indenture”). The Supplemental Indentures eliminate certain of the covenants, restrictive provisions and events of default
 from the Existing Indenture with respect to each series of Existing Notes. On August 25, 2021, Northrop Grumman and NGSC also entered into an amendment (the “Guarantee Amendment”) to the guarantee by Northrop Grumman of NGSC’s
 obligations with respect to the Existing Notes. The Guarantee Amendment provides for the termination of the guarantee by Northrop Grumman of NGSC’s obligations with respect to the Existing Notes.
