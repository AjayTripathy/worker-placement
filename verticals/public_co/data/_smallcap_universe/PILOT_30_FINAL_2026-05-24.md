## The SRPT methodology caveat

SRPT composite 0.125 places it in the LONG basket by framework. **The framework is correct**: SRPT honestly discloses 3 patient deaths, FDA-mandated black-box warning, indication removal, EU rejection, ESSENCE trial endpoint failure, and securities class action. It is not lying.

**But the underlying business is impaired.** Per the honesty-alpha methodology (committed in `STRATEGY_METHODOLOGY.md`):
- Strategy A "Clean Index" — admits SRPT (honesty filter passes)
- Strategy B "Honesty + Factor" — would reject SRPT at the quality-factor step (3 patient deaths is impaired-business signal that's appropriately fundamental-analyst territory)

For pure honesty-alpha testing, SRPT belongs in the basket. The framework's job ends at "not lying." Whether to override SRPT inclusion is a Strategy-B factor-overlay choice, not a framework choice.

## Framework calibration audit — FIXED 2026-05-24

After running `recompute_pilot_composite.py` across all 30 pilots, the agent self-reported composites drift from the canonical formula in **15 of 30 (50%)** cases. 4 of those drifts were large enough to flip tiers:

| Ticker | Agent reported | Recomputed | Tier change |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| AMPH | 0.125 | 0.375 | STRICT_LONG → NEUTRAL |
| HNI | _none_ | 0.500 | UNKNOWN → SHORT |
| PBH | 0.333 | 0.286 | NEUTRAL → LOOSE_LONG |
| SMPL | 0.333 | 1.000 | NEUTRAL → SHORT |

Smaller drifts came from agents using different denominators (some used N total, some used N non-UNVERIFIABLE, SMPL used max-normalized N×3). The canonical formula per `STRATEGY_METHODOLOGY.md` is `sum(weights)/N total` with UNV at weight 0 in the numerator.

**Fix shipped**: `verticals/public_co/recompute_pilot_composite.py` — run with `--write` after any new pilot batch to normalize composite + scoring fields. Pilot files now have canonical composites; the synthesis is regenerated from those.
