## The load-bearing question: going concern — RED → VERIFIED

The S-1 carried an explicit flag: *"These conditions indicate the existence of a material uncertainty that raises substantial doubt as to our ability to continue as a going concern"* (driven by a Series-C preferred redemption + 2023 term loan + 2025 convertible notes). On a corpus frozen at S-1 time, that reads RED.

**It resolved post-IPO**, and the resolution is in the primary filings (the live-DD lesson: refresh the corpus to the cutoff):
1. **The IPO recapitalized it.** Cash $719M (2025Q2) → **$820M (2026Q1), and growing every quarter** — the raise (~$460M net) plus operating cash generation cleared the redemption/debt overhang.
2. **The business inflected to operating profit.** Operating income: **−$18M (Q2'25) → +$33M (Q3) → +$88M (Q4) → +$5M (Q1'26)**. Net income turned positive in Q3–Q4'25.
3. **The post-IPO 10-K carries NO going-concern language** (the only "concern" in it is cybersecurity boilerplate).

→ Going concern **VERIFIED-resolved**. The S-1 RED was a pre-money artifact.

## Business
Molecular profiling (MI Profile tissue) + Caris Assure (blood/liquid biopsy) + a large proprietary molecular-clinical database feeding AI tools. 849k+ unique cases profiled, 6.5M+ tests, 5,550+ regularly-ordering oncologists, 100+ biopharma partners. Revenue **~$412M FY2024, ~28% CAGR 2019–24** (50% YoY in the IPO window).
