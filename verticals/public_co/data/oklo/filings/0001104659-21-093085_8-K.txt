to Section 13(a) of the Exchange Act. ¨ Item 8.01 | Other Events. On July 12, 2021, AltC Acquisition Corp. (the “ Company ”)
consummated its initial public offering (the “ IPO ”) of 50,000,000 shares (the “ Shares ”) of Class
A common stock, par value $0.0001 per share, of the Company (the “ Class A Common Stock ”), including the issuance of
5,000,000 Shares as a result of the underwriters’ full exercise of their over-allotment option, pursuant to the Company’s
registration statement on Form S-1, initially filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 15, 2021 (File No. 333-254263).
The Shares were sold at a price of $10.00 per share, generating gross proceeds to the Company of $500,000,000. Simultaneously with the closing of the IPO, the
Company completed the private sale (the “ Private Placement ”) of an aggregate of 1,450,000 shares of Class A Common
Stock (the “ Private Placement Shares ”) at a purchase price of $1.00 per Private Placement Share, to the Company’s
sponsor, AltC Sponsor LLC (the “ Sponsor ”), generating gross proceeds to the Company of $14,500,000. The Private Placement
Shares are identical to the Shares sold in the IPO, except that the Sponsor has agreed not to transfer, assign or sell any of the Private
Placement Shares (except to certain permitted transferees) until 30 days after the completion of the Company’s initial business
combination. A total of $500,000,000 was placed in a U.S.-based
trust account with Continental Stock Transfer & Trust Company, acting as trustee. An audited balance sheet as of July 12, 2021 reflecting
receipt of the proceeds upon consummation of the IPO and the Private Placement has been issued by the Company and is included as Exhibit
