On March 1, 2019, the Board of Directors (“Board”) of BWX Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Company”), approved the amendment of the Company’s Amended and Restated Bylaws (“Bylaws”) to establish majority voting in uncontested elections of directors, which provides that the number of votes cast “for” a director’s election must exceed the number of votes cast “against” that director’s election in order for the director to be elected. The Bylaws, as amended, retain plurality voting for contested director elections. In addition, the amendment to the Bylaws provides for additional officer positions for the Company. This description is qualified in its entirety by reference to, and should be read in conjunction with, the full text of the Bylaws, as amended, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 3.3 and is incorporated herein by reference.
