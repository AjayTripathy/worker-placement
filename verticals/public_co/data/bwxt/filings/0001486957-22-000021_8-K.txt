On July 12, 2022, Joel W. Duling notified BWX Technologies, Inc. ("Company," "we" or "us) of his decision to retire from his role as President, BWXT Nuclear Operations Group, Inc., a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, effective July 29, 2022. In connection with his retirement, Mr. Duling would have been obligated to repay certain tuition expenses paid by the Company on his behalf in the prior two years. In exchange for forgiveness of this repayment obligation by the Company, Mr. Duling entered into a non-competition and non-solicitation agreement with the Company, dated July 18, 2022, for a period of twelve (12) months following his retirement date.
