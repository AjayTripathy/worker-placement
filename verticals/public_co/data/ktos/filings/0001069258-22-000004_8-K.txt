( “ Kratos ” or the “ Company ” ) announced the commencement of an offer to redeem all of its outstanding 6.5% Senior Secured Notes due 2025 (the “ Senior Secured Notes ” ) on March 14, 2022 (the “ Redemption Date ” ), subject to completion of the refinancing of its revolving credit facility, for an amount in cash equal to 103.250% of the principal amount thereof plus accrued and unpaid interest thereon to, but excluding, the Redemption Date (the “ Redemption Offer ” ).
