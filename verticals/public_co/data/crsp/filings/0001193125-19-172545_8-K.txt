entirety by reference to (i) the full text of the 2018 Plan, which is filed as Exhibit 10.19 to the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2018, (ii) the full text
of the Plan Amendment, a copy of which is attached as Appendix A to the Proxy Statement, and in each case, incorporated herein by reference.
