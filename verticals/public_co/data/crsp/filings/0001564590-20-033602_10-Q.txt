Recently, we re-initiated dosing patients in our Phase 1/2 open-label clinical trials of CTX001 for transfusion-dependent beta thalassemia and severe sickle cell disease (CLIMB THAL-111 and CLIMB SCD-121). Finally, we are in the process of reopening our offices to additional staff in accordance with state and local regulations. The ultimate impact of the coronavirus pandemic on our business operations remains uncertain and subject to change and will depend on future developments, which cannot be accurately predicted.
