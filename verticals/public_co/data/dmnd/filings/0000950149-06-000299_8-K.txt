o | Pre-commencement communications pursuant to Rule 13e-4(c) under the Exchange Act (17 CFR 240.13e-4(c)) Item 8.01. Other Events On May 19, 2006, Michael Mendes, President and Chief Executive Officer of Diamond Foods, Inc.
(the “Company”), adopted a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan (the “Plan”) with a broker to purchase shares
of Company common stock in the open market. Mr. Mendes entered into the Plan in order to
facilitate the orderly acquisition of Company common stock. Pursuant to the Plan, the brokerage firm, on behalf of Mr. Mendes, will purchase in excess of
$100,000 worth of Company common stock in the open market in the mid-June 2006 time frame, at
prevailing market prices not to exceed $25.00 per share. Any transactions under the Plan will be
reported by Mr. Mendes through individual Form 4 filings with the Securities and Exchange
Commission. The Plan is intended to comply with Rule 10b5-1 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934
and the Company’s insider trading policy. In addition, during the Company’s earnings call on March 9, 2006 regarding the quarter ended
January 31, 2006, the Company announced that Mr. Mendes had adopted a 10b5-1 trading plan (the
“Sale Plan”) to sell shares in the July 2006 time frame to cover taxes that would accrue as
restricted stock issued to Mr. Mendes vests. On May 19, 2006, Mr. Mendes terminated the Sale Plan.
As a result, Mr. Mendes will cover income taxes that will arise as a result of restricted stock
vesting through use of personal funds. SIGNATURE Pursuant to the requirements of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, the Registrant has duly
