Other Events On October 10, 2006, Sam Keiper, Vice President, Corporate Affairs and Human Resources, of
Diamond Foods, Inc. (the “Company”), adopted a Rule 10b5-1 trading plan (the “Plan”) with a broker
to purchase shares of Company common stock in the open market. Mr. Keiper entered into the Plan in
order to facilitate the orderly acquisition of Company common stock. Pursuant to the Plan, the brokerage firm, on behalf of Mr. Keiper, will purchase Company
common stock in the open market in the first quarter of calendar 2007 time frame, subject to market
conditions. The plan may be terminated or amended prior to expiration, and is not the exclusive
manner by which Mr. Keiper may acquire Company shares. Any transactions under the Plan will be
reported by Mr. Keiper through individual Form 4 filings with the Securities and Exchange
Commission. The Plan is intended to comply with Rule 10b5-1 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934
and the Company’s insider trading policy. 2 SIGNATURE Pursuant to the requirements of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, the Registrant has duly
