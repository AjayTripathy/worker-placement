On July 21, 2025, D-Wave Quantum Inc. (“D-Wave”) announced that it has published a new report that highlights the potential for quantum optimization to create value across industries. D-Wave published the report in collaboration with Wakefield Research, a leading market research and thought leadership firm. In May 2025, Wakefield Research surveyed 400 business leaders from select countries in North America, Europe and Asia Pacific (APAC). The majority are familiar with quantum computing and make decisions about optimization technologies. According to the study, 46% of surveyed business leaders whose company has implemented quantum optimization or plans to do so within the next two years expect a return on investment of between $1 and $5 million, while 27% predict a return of more than $5 million in the first 12 months. In addition, a majority of the business leaders surveyed (81%) believe that they have reached the limit of the benefits they can achieve through optimization solutions running on classical computers. Many are starting to explore whether quantum technologies can help. 53% are planning to build quantum computing into their workflows and 27% are considering doing so, indicating a growing recognition of quantum computing’s real-world business value. According to Dr. Alan Baratz, CEO of D-Wave, “Leaders are increasingly recognizing that annealing quantum computing can play a pivotal role in solving business-critical problems now. This survey reflects the rapidly growing interest in applying quantum to complex optimization use cases, with business leaders unequivocally recognizing the substantial potential ROI of doing so.” A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1.
