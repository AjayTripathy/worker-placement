In light of public health concerns regarding the coronavirus (COVID-19) and government-recommended and required limits on gatherings and events, effective April 8, 2020, the Board of Directors of Lockheed Martin Corporation (“Lockheed Martin” or the “Corporation”) amended and restated the Corporation's Bylaws to: (1) explicitly allow the Board of Directors to determine, as permitted by applicable law, that a meeting of stockholders be held by means of remote communication instead of, or in addition to, a physical location; and (2) reduce the minimum period by which the Corporation must provide stockholders notice of a stockholders' meeting from 30 days to 10 days. The foregoing summary description of the amendment to the Bylaws is not intended to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the complete text of the Bylaws, a copy of which is included as Exhibit 3.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference. On April 9, 2020, the Corporation announced that its 2020 Annual Meeting of Stockholders will be held solely by means of remote communication. Item 9.01. Financial Statements and Exhibits.
