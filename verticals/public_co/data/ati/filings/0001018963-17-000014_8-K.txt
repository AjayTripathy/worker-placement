Item 5.07 | Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. As described in Item 5.03 above, the Company held its Annual Meeting on May 11, 2017. As disclosed in the Company’s proxy materials for the Annual Meeting, the following proposals were considered and voted upon at the Annual Meeting: (1) the election of three directors for three-year terms expiring in 2020; (2) approval of the 2017 Incentive Plan; (3) an advisory vote regarding the frequency with which the Company will conduct a non-binding vote to approve the compensation paid annually to its named executive officers; (4) an advisory vote regarding the compensation paid to the Company’s named executive officers in 2016; and (5) ratification of the appointment of Ernst & Young LLP (“E&Y”) as independent auditors for the Company’s fiscal year ending December 31, 2017.
