On November 29, 2023, ATI Inc. (“ATI”) issued the attached press release announcing certain long-term financial performance targets for 2025 and 2027. Additionally, ATI announced that its Board of Directors authorized the repurchase of up to $150 million of its outstanding common stock. Repurchases under the program may be made in the open market or in privately negotiated transactions, with the amount and timing of repurchases depending on market conditions and corporate needs. Open market repurchases will be structured to occur within the pricing and volume requirements of SEC Rule 10b-18. The stock repurchase program does not obligate the Company to repurchase any specific number of shares and it may be modified, suspended, or terminated at any time by the board of directors without prior notice. A copy of the Company’s press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated by reference herein.
