July 17, 2007, Pain Therapeutics, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a Sublease Agreement (the “Sublease”) with Oracle USA, Inc. for a facility consisting of approximately 30,718 square feet of office space located at 2211
Bridgepointe Parkway in San Mateo, California. The effectiveness of the Sublease was conditioned upon the Company’s receipt of a consent from the landlord, which was delivered on July 18, 2007.
