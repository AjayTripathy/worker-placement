Other Events. On April 15, 2024, Cassava Sciences, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), issued a press release announcing that Monday, May 6, 2024 is the last and final day to exercise the warrants that were previously distributed to its shareholders on January 3, 2024 (the “Warrants”). Any Warrants that are not validly exercised by 5:00 p.m. New York City time on May 6, 2024 will be redeemed by the Company on May 7, 2024 (the “Redemption Date”) for a nominal payment of $0.001 per warrant (the “Redemption Price”). Once redeemed, the Warrants will cease to exist and have no value.
