(the “Company”) held its Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”), o n May 10, 2018 at which time the Company’s stockholders approved the Company 2018 Omnibus Incentive Plan (the “2018 Plan”), which had been previously approved by the Company’s Board of Directors . ﻿ The 2018 Plan reserves 1,000,000 shares of the Company’s Common Stock to be used for the Company’s compensation programs for its executive, employee, independent contractors, consultants and directors. The 2018 Plan will allow the Company to continue to offer equity and other award, which the Company believe s is necessary to retain, motivate and attract experienced and highly qualified service providers. The 2018 Plan is described in detail in the Company’s 2018 Definitive Proxy Statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) on March 27, 2018.
