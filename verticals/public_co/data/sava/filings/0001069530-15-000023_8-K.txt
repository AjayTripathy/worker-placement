Other Matters As previously disclosed, on December 2, 2011, a securities-related purported class action was filed against Pain Therapeutics, Inc. and its executive officers in the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Texas , or the Court . This action , titled KB Partners I, L.P., Individually and On Behalf of All Others Similarly Situated v. Pain Therapeutics, Inc., Remi Barbier, Nadav Friedmann and Peter S.
