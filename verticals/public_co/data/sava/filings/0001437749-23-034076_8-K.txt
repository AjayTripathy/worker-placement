(the “Company”) expanded the size of the Board to nine members. The Board also appointed Dr. Claude Nicaise, Pierre Gravier and Robert Anderson, Jr. to the three newly created Board seats. There are no arrangements or understandings between Dr. Nicaise, Mr. Gravier and Mr. Anderson and any other person pursuant to which each of them was appointed to the Board. Dr. Nicaise, Mr. Gravier and Mr. Anderson do not have direct or indirect material interests in any transaction required to be disclosed pursuant to Item 404(a) of Regulation S‐K.
