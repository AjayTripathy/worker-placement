The 2023 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) of Cassava Sciences, Inc. (the “Company”) was held at approximately 10 a.m. Central Time on May 4, 2023, pursuant to the Notice of Annual Meeting of Stockholders dated March 27, 2023 and duly delivered to all Company stockholders of record as of March 14, 2023. Of the 41,749,435 shares of the Company’s common stock entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting, 24,773,128 shares, or approximately 59%, were represented at the Annual Meeting virtually or by proxy, constituting a quorum. At the Annual Meeting, the Company’s stockholders voted on six proposals, each of which is described in more detail in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on March 27, 2023.
