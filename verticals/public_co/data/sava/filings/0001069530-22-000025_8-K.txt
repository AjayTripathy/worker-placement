The 2022 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) of Cassava Sciences, Inc. (the “Company”) was held at approximately 10 a.m. Central Time on May 5, 2022, pursuant to the Notice of Annual Meeting of Stockholders dated March 24, 2022 and duly delivered to all Company stockholders of record as of March 15, 2022. Of the 40,022,394 shares of the Company’s common stock entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting, 24,301,757 shares, or approximately 61%, were represented at the Annual Meeting virtually or by proxy, constituting a quorum. At the Annual Meeting, the Company’s stockholders voted on four proposals, each of which is described in more detail in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on March 24, 2022.
