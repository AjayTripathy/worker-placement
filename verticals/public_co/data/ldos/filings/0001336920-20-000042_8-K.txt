(the “Company”) unanimously approved a reduction in the equity compensation to be provided to members of the Company’s Board of Directors for the 2020 fiscal year. The Company’s compensation program for directors will be modified, for the current year only, to eliminate the annual grants of stock options valued at $50,000 per director. In lieu of the annual option grants for directors, the Company will contribute $500,000 to the Leidos Relief Foundation’s Keith W.
