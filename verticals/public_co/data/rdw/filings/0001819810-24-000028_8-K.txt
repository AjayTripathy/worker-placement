On March 27, 2024 , Nathan O’Konek, Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Secretary of Redwire Corporation (the “Company”) informed the Company’s Board of Directors of his resignation, effective on or around April 26, 2024. The effective date of Mr. O’Konek’s resignation may be extended by up to 60 days by mutual agreement with the Company. Mr. O’Konek’s decision to resign did not arise from any disagreement with the Company on any matters relating to the Company’s operations, policies or practices. Peter Cannito, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the Company, expressed his thanks to Mr. O’Konek for his meaningful contributions to the Company throughout his many years of service as an officer of the Company and its predecessor. We wish him well as he pursues other opportunities aligned with his long-term plans.
