On May 2, 2025, Viasat, Inc. (the “Company”) used cash on hand to redeem all $442,550,000 remaining aggregate principal amount of the Company’s outstanding 5.625% Senior Notes due 2025 (the “Notes”) ahead of the September 15, 2025 maturity date. In connection with the redemption of the Notes, the indenture governing the Notes was satisfied and discharged.
