At the annual meeting of stockholders of Viasat, Inc. (“Viasat”) held on September 1, 2022 (the “Annual Meeting”), Viasat’s stockholders approved the amendment and restatement of the 1996 Equity Participation Plan of Viasat, Inc. (as amended and restated, the “Restated Equity Plan”). The Restated Equity Plan was previously approved by the Board of Directors of Viasat (the “Board”), and implemented the following changes: (1) an increase in the number of shares of common stock available for issuance under the plan by 3,156,000 shares to a total of 44,471,000 shares; and (2) an extension of the period during which incentive stock options may be granted from 2031 to 2032. The Restated Equity Plan became effective upon stockholder approval at the Annual Meeting. The preceding description of the Restated Equity Plan does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the complete text of the Restated Equity Plan, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this report and incorporated herein by reference.
