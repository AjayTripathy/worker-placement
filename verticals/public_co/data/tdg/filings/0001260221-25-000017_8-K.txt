On March 6, 2025, TransDigm Group Incorporated (the “Company”) conducted its Annual Meeting of Shareholders (the “2025 Annual Meeting”). At the meeting, Messrs. David A. Barr, Michael Graff, Sean P. Hennessy, W. Nicholas Howley, Gary E. McCullough, Robert J. Small, Kevin M. Stein, Jorge L. Valladares III and Mses. Jane M. Cronin and Michele L. Santana were re-elected as directors of the Company.
