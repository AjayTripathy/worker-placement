On September 20, 2019, TransDigm Group Incorporated (“TransDigm”) completed the divestiture of its Esterline Interface Technology group of businesses (“EIT”) to an affiliate of KPS Capital Partners, LP for approximately $190 million. EIT was acquired by TransDigm as part of its acquisition of Esterline Technologies Corporation in March 2019. TransDigm issued a press release announcing the completion of the divestiture.
