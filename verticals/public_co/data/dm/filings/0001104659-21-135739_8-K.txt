Other Events . On November 4, 2021,
the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors of Desktop Metal, Inc. (the “Company”) engaged a third party to conduct an
independent internal investigation as a result of a whistleblower complaint relating to, among other matters, manufacturing and
product compliance practices and procedures with respect to a subset of its photopolymer equipment and materials at its EnvisionTec
US LLC facility in Dearborn, Michigan. While the investigation remains on-going, the Company has taken initial actions, including implementing changes in the management of and procedures associated with manufacturing the applicable products. Based on the
investigation to date, the Company does not believe the matters involved will have a material impact on the Company, its financial
statements or its business. Additional Information and Where to Find It This communication relates to a proposed business
