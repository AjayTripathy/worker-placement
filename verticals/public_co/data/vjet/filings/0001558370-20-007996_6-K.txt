Section 802.01C of the NYSE’s Listed Company Manual requires that a company’s common stock trade at a minimum average closing price of $1.00 over a consecutive 30 trading-day period (the “Minimum Average Price Requirement”). The Company regained compliance after its average closing American Depositary Share price for the consecutive 30-days of trading ending June 30, 2020 was above the Minimum Average Price Requirement. ​ A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated by reference herein. ​ As previously disclosed in the filings with the SEC, the Company currently remains out of compliance with the continued listing standard set forth in Section 802.01B of the NYSE’s Listed Company Manual, which requires that the Company’s average global market capitalization over a consecutive 30 trading-day period be greater than $50 million and, at the same time, its shareholders’ equity be greater than $50 million.
