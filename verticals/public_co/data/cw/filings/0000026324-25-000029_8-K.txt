Share repurchases may be made through a variety of methods, which could include open market purchases, accelerated share repurchase transactions, negotiated block transactions, 10b5-1 plans, other transactions that may be structured through investment banking institutions or privately negotiated, or a combination of the foregoing. The timing, price, and volume of share repurchases will depend on market conditions, relevant securities laws, and corporate, tax, regulatory and other relevant considerations. The Company presently expects to repurchase outstanding shares from time to time to offset the dilutive impact of employee equity-based compensation plans, but the Company also may make discretionary, opportunistic share repurchases.
