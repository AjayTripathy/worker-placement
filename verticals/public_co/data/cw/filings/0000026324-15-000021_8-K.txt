On June 16, 2015, Curtiss-Wright Corporation (the “Company”) entered into a Third Amendment to Third Amended and Restated Credit Agreement among the Company and Certain Subsidiaries as Borrowers; the Lenders party thereto; Bank of America N.A., as Administrative Agent, Swingline Lender, and L/C Issuer; JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., and Wells Fargo, N.A., as Syndication Agents; and RBS Citizens N.A., as Documentation Agent (the “Credit Agreement”).
