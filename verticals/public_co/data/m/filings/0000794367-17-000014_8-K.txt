(d) | Consistent with the press releases issued by Macy’s, Inc. (“Macy’s”) on June 23, 2016 and February 21, 2017, the Board of Directors of Macy's has elected Jeffrey Gennette Chief Executive Officer of Macy’s effective as of March 23, 2017. Mr. Gennette will also continue in his role as President of Macy’s. The Board of Directors of Macy’s has also elected Terry Lundgren Executive Chair of Macy’s effective March 23, 2017. The actions taken by the Macy’s Board of Directors is part of the Board’s succession plan that included Mr. Gennette’s election as President of Macy’s in 2014. MACY’S, INC.
