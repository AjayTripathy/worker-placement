On May 15, 2020, the Board of Directors of Macy’s, Inc. (“Macy’s” or the “Company”) appointed Felicia Williams, 54, as interim Chief Financial Officer, effective June 1, 2020. Ms. Williams has been Senior Vice President, Controller and Enterprise Risk Officer of the Company since June 2016, Senior Vice President, Finance and Risk Management from February 2011 to June 2016 and has held a number of other positions within the Company since 2004. Ms. Williams will succeed Paula A. Price who, as previously announced, will depart the Company on May 31, 2020. The Board of Directors also appointed Paul Griscom, 39, as interim Principal Accounting Officer, effective June 1, 2020. Mr. Griscom has been Vice President, Financial Reporting and Accounting Services since May 2019, Vice President, Financial Reporting from June 2017 to April 2019 and Director of Financial Reporting from July 2016 to May 2017. Previously, he was Director, Training & Products, GAAP Dynamics from 2012 to 2016 and Senior Manager, KPMG LLP from 2000 to 2012. Ms. Williams and Mr. Griscom will each receive a monthly stipend, in addition to current salary, for the additional responsibilities during their interim tenure. Macy’s, Inc.
