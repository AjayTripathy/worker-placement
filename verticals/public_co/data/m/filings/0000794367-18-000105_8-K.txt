On October 15, 2018, the Board of Directors of Macy’s, Inc. (“Macy’s”) elected David P. Abney as an independent director, effective October 25, 2018. Mr. Abney is the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of United Parcel Service, Inc. The Board also appointed Mr. Abney as a member of the Compensation and Management Development Committee of the Board of Directors. Mr. Abney will participate in Macy’s standard non-employee director compensation arrangements described in the proxy statement for Macy’s annual meeting of shareholders held on May 18, 2018 under the caption “Further Information Concerning the Board of Directors.” Item 9.01.
