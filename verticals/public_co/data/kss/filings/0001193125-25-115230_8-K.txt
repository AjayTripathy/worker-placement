Ms. Day’s decision was not due to any disagreements with the Company on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies or practices. As a result of Ms. Day’s decision, the Board has determined to withdraw her nomination for reelection as a director of the Company at the Company’s annual meeting of shareholders to be held on May 14, 2025.
