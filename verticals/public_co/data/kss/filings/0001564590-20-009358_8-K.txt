Regulation FD Disclosure . Kohl’s Corporation (“Kohl’s”) today announced updated plans for its Investor Day. Kohl’s will host its Investor Day on Monday, March 16, 2020, beginning at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time via webcast only. Due to increasingly restrictive travel and group event guidelines put in place as a precaution relating to COVID-19, the in-person component of the event will no longer be held. Kohl’s Investor Day program, which is now expected to run from 1:00 until approximately 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time, will include executive presentations followed by a question and answer session. The webcast of the presentations and question and answer session will be available at: http://corporate.kohls.com/investors/events-and-presentations .
