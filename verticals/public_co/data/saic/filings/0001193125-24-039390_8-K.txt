On February 20, 2024, Science Applications International Corporation (the “Company”) provided notice to the New York Stock Exchange (“NYSE”) that it intends to have its shares of common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (“Common Stock”), voluntarily delisted from the NYSE after the close of trading on March 4, 2024, and it intends to voluntarily transfer the listing of its shares of Common Stock to The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) effective as of the opening of trading on March 5, 2024. The Company’s Common Stock has been authorized for listing on Nasdaq and will continue to trade under the symbol “SAIC”. On February 20, 2024, the Company issued a press release announcing its listing transfer to Nasdaq, which is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
