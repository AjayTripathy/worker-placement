On September 10, 2018, Science Applications International Corporation (“SAIC”) announced the execution of an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated as of September 9, 2018, by and among SAIC, a Delaware corporation, Engility Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Engility”), and Raptors Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of SAIC (the “Merger Sub”), pursuant to which Merger Sub will merge with and into Engility, subject to the terms and conditions set forth therein (the “Merger Agreement”). On September 10, 2018, SAIC’s Chief Executive Officer, Tony Moraco, spoke about the Merger during an all hands call with Engility employees, a transcript of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1. Additional Information and Where to Find It In connection with the proposed acquisition of Engility, SAIC will file with the SEC a registration statement on Form S-4 to register the shares of SAIC common stock to be issued in connection with the merger. The registration statement will include a joint proxy statement/prospectus which will be sent to the shareholders of SAIC and Engility seeking their approval of the proposed transaction.
