On November 15, 2018, Science Applications International Corporation (“SAIC”) and Engility Holdings, Inc. (“Engility”) issued a joint press release announcing that each company will hold a special meeting on January 11, 2019 for the companies’ respective shareholders to consider and vote on the proposals related to the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of September 9, 2018, pursuant to which SAIC will acquire Engility. The boards of directors of SAIC and Engility have each set November 29 as the record date for their respective special meetings. The SAIC special meeting will be held at 9:00 a.m., Eastern time, at 12010 Sunset Hills Road, Reston, Virginia 20190. The Engility special meeting will be held at 9:00 a.m., Eastern time, at 4803 Stonecroft Boulevard, Chantilly, Virginia 20151. Additional Information and Where to Find It In connection with the proposed acquisition of Engility, SAIC filed a preliminary registration statement on Form S-4 with the SEC on October 18, 2018 to register the shares of SAIC common stock to be issued in connection with the merger. The preliminary registration statement includes a preliminary joint proxy statement/prospectus. The definitive joint proxy statement/prospectus, when available, will be sent to the shareholders of SAIC and Engility seeking their approval of the proposed transaction.
