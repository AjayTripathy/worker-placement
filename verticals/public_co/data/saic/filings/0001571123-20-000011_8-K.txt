Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement On March 17, 2020, Science Applications International Corporation (the "Company") and Engility services, LLC, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company ("Engility"), entered into an Amendment No.1 (the "Amendment") to the Master Accounts Receivable Purchase Agreement dated as of January 21, 2020 (the "Existing Purchase Agreement"), among the Company, as a seller and seller representative, Engility, as a seller, and MUFG Bank, Ltd., as purchase (the "Purchaser"). Pursuant to the Amendment, the Purchaser's commitment to purchase eligible receivables of the Company and Engility was increased from $200 million to $300 million. The remaining terms in the Existing Purchase Agreement are unchanged. The foregoing description of the Amendment does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Amendment, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 hereto and incorporated by reference in this Current Report on Form 8-K.
