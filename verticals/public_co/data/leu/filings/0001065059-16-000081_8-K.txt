The Company held its 2016 annual meeting of stockholders on May 12, 2016. As of the record date, March 18, 2016, there were 7,563,600 shares of Class A common stock outstanding, each entitled to one vote. 85.97% of those shares were represented at the annual meeting. At the annual meeting, the Company’s stockholders voted on three proposals and cast their votes as described below. The proposals are described in detail in the Company’s proxy statement. Proposal 1 The Company’s stockholders elected nine directors (listed below) to hold office until the next annual meeting of stockholders and until his or her successor is elected and has qualified.
