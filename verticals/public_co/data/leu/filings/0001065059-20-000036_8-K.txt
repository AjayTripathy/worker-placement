On September 15, 2020 Centrus Energy Corp. (the “Company”) jointly announced with TerraPower LLC (“TerraPower”) plans to team together to establish commercial-scale, domestic production capabilities for High-Assay, Low-Enriched Uranium (HALEU), which will be needed to fuel many next generation reactor designs -- including the recently announced Natrium™ Power Storage System designed by TerraPower and GE Hitachi Nuclear Energy (GEH). The proposed investment is part of the TerraPower-led proposal for the U.S. Department of Energy’s Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program (ARDP), a program which is intended to support the deployment of two first-of-a-kind advanced reactor designs in the next 5-7 years. The TerraPower application proposes that, if selected for ARDP, TerraPower would work with Centrus to build commercial-scale capacity to produce HALEU and fabricate it into metal fuel assemblies. The proposal was submitted to the U.S. Department of Energy in a competitive process. There can be no assurance that the proposal will be accepted, and a contract awarded.
