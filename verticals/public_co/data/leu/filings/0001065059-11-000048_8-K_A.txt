Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. As previously reported, at the Company’s 2011 annual meeting of shareholders held on April 28, 2011, a majority of the shares cast voted, on an advisory basis, to hold future advisory (non-binding) votes on executive compensation on an annual basis. After consideration of the shareholder voting results, the Company has determined that future advisory (non-binding) votes on executive compensation will occur annually until the Company decides to hold the next advisory vote on the frequency of advisory votes on executive compensation.
