On June 13, 2024, Enovix Corporation (the “ Company ”) held its 2024 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “ Annual Meeting ”). At the Annual Meeting, the Company’s stockholders voted on three proposals, each of which is described in more detail in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “ SEC ”) on April 29, 2024, as supplemented by a proxy statement supplement filed with the SEC on June 5, 2024 (the “ Proxy Statement ”). The following is a brief description of each matter voted on at the Annual Meeting, including the number of votes cast with respect to each matter and, if applicable, the number of abstentions and broker non-votes with respect to each matter.
