On April 26, 2022, DeEtte Gray, President, Business and Information Technology Solutions of CACI International Inc (the “Company”), was granted a one-time special award of 10,636 restricted stock units (“RSUs”) with a grant date value of approximately $3,000,000. The RSUs will vest in equal installments on the first three anniversaries of the grant date and be subject to substantially similar terms and conditions as the RSUs granted under the Plan.
