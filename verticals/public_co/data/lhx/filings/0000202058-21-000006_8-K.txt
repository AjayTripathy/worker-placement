On March 1, 2021, L3Harris Technologies, Inc. (“L3Harris”) issued a press release announcing the signing of definitive agreements under which CAE will acquire L3Harris’ Military Training business for $1.05 billion in cash and RENK AG will acquire L3Harris’ Combat Propulsion Systems and related businesses for about $400 million in cash.
