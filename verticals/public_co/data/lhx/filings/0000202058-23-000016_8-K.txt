(d) On February 23, 2023 on the recommendation of the Nominating and Governance Committee of the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of L3Harris Technologies, Inc. (the “Company”), the Board increased the authorized number of directors constituting the Board from fourteen to fifteen and elected General Edward A. Rice, Jr. (Ret.), 66, as a director to serve on the Board. General Rice, a 35-year military veteran with the U.S. Air Force, most recently served as commander of the U.S. Air Force’s Air Education and Training Command until his retirement in 2013. General Rice’s initial term as a director on the Board commenced February 23, 2023 and expires at the Company’s 2023 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. General Rice was not appointed to any committees of the Board at this time. As a non-employee director on the Board, General Rice will be compensated for service as a director in accordance with the Summary of Annual Compensation of L3Harris Technologies, Inc. Non-Employee Directors, effective as of January 1, 2022, filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on August 4, 2021 and incorporated in this Item 5.02(d) by reference. There are no arrangements or understandings between General Rice and any other persons pursuant to which General Rice was selected as a director, and there are no transactions in which the Company was or is to be a participant and in which General Rice had or will have a direct or indirect material interest that are required to be reported pursuant to Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.
