(the “Company”) amended its Restated Certificate of Incorporation by filing a Certificate of Amendment with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware. The Certificate of Amendment reflected an amendment to limit liability of officers as permitted by law, as approved by the holders of more than a majority of the Company’s shares outstanding and entitled to vote at the 2024 Annual Meeting of Shareholders (the “2024 Annual Meeting”) and as described in Item 5.07 below. A copy of the Certificate of Amendment is filed as Exhibit 3.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated by reference into this Item 5.03.
