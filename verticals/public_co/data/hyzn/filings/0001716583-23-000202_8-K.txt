Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. On August 21, 2023, Hyzon Motors Inc. (the “Company”) announced the resignation of Mr. George Gu, the Company’s Chairman, from the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”), effective August 24, 2023. Mr. Gu’s resignation is not the result of any disagreement with the Company or its management on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies, or practices. On August 18, 2023, the Board elected Mr. Erik Anderson and Mr. Andrea Farace, who are members of the Board, as Chairman and Vice Chairman of the Board, respectively, each effective August 24, 2023, the effective date of Mr. Gu’s resignation. Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure. On August 21, 2023, the Company furnished a press release regarding the resignation of Mr. George Gu as Chairman of the Board, and the appointments of Mr. Erik Anderson and Mr. Farace as Chairman of the Board and Vice Chairman of the Board, respectively, as described above in Item 5.02 of this Current Report on Form 8-K. The press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated in this Item 7.01 by reference.
