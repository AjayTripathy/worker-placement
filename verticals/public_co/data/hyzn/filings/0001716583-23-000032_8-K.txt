Entry into a Material Agreement On February 24, 2023, Hyzon Motors USA Inc. (“Hyzon USA”), a subsidiary of Hyzon Motors Inc. (the “Company”), entered into a Technology Development Agreement (the “Agreement”) with Hyliion Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Hyliion”), with the purpose of working collaboratively to integrate a Hyzon USA fuel cell into a Hyliion powertrain on a Class 8 semi-truck (the “Product”). Subject to the terms and conditions of the Agreement, Hyzon USA and Hyliion each grant one another a worldwide, irrevocable, nonexclusive, royalty-free, non-sublicensable license on their respective intellectual property solely for the limited purpose of developing the Product. The Agreement contains various representations, warranties, covenants, indemnities and other provisions customary for transactions of this nature. The term of the Agreement is one (1) year, with the option of extending the term by mutual agreement. The foregoing summary of the terms and conditions of the Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the complete text of the Agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 hereto and is incorporated herein by reference.
