(“Holthausen”), to acquire the remaining 49.5% of the outstanding stock of Hyzon Motors Europe B.V. (“Hyzon Europe") a European joint venture operated by the Company and Holthausen. The Company will promptly provide further information concerning this contemplated transaction when and if the Company and Holthausen reach an agreement.
