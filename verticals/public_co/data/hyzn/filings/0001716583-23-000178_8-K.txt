Other Events As previously reported, on May 8, 2023, Hyzon Motors Inc. (the “Company”) received a notice letter (the “Notice”) from the listing qualifications staff of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) notifying the Company that, because the minimum bid price per share for its common stock was below $1.00 for 30 consecutive business days preceding the date of the Notice, the Company did not meet the $1.00 per share minimum bid price requirement set forth in Nasdaq Listing Rule 5450(a)(1) (the “Minimum Bid Price Requirement”). On July 26, 2023, the Company received a letter from Nasdaq notifying the Company that it had regained compliance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5450(a)(1) as a result of the closing bid price of the Company’s common stock being at $1.00 per share or greater for the 10 consecutive business days from July 12, 2023 through July 25, 2023. Accordingly, the Company is in compliance with the Minimum Bid Price Requirement and the matter is now closed. On July 27, 2023, the Company issued a press release (the “Press Release”) announcing that it received a formal notice from Nasdaq stating that the Company has regained compliance with the Minimum Bid Price Requirement and that the matter is now closed. A copy of the Press Release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference. The foregoing disclosure is qualified in its entirety by the full text of the Press Release.
