Item 8.01 Other Events. On November 21, 2016, the Company entered into an agreement (the "Purchase Agreement") with Sales On Demand Corporation ("SODC"), a privately-held corporation based in Tokyo, Japan. Pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, the Company will acquire SODC’s iRobot-related distribution business (the "Business"), for a purchase price in cash equal to the book value of the acquired net assets of the Business as of the date of the acquisition, which the parties estimate will be between $14,000,000 and $16,000,000, subject to exchange rates then in effect (such acquisition, the "Transaction"). The Transaction is expected to close in April 2017. Item 9.01 Financial Statements and Exhibits. (d) Exhibits: 99.1 Press Release issued by the registrant on November 21, 2016, furnished herewith.
