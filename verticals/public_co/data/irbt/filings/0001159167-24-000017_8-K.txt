On March 26, 2024, Glen Weinstein executed an employment agreement as the Interim Chief Executive Officer of iRobot Corporation (the “Company”). As previously disclosed, for his service as the Company’s Interim Chief Executive Officer, Mr. Weinstein will receive compensation in the form of a base stipend of $63,333 per month, a bonus accrual of $63,333 per month, and a one-time equity award with a fair market value of $1.2 million pursuant to the Company’s 2018 Stock Option and Incentive Plan, as amended, that will vest 12 months from the date of grant (the “Equity Award”). In the event of termination of his employment with the Company, Mr. Weinstein will be eligible to receive severance payments in the aggregate amount of $430,000, full vesting acceleration of the Equity Award, and one year of accelerated vesting for his other outstanding equity awards. The foregoing description is qualified in its entirety by the Employment Agreement, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
