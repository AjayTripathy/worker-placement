On August 7, 2014, based on the recommendation of the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee of iRobot Corporation (the “Company”), the Board of Directors of the Company (the “Board”) elected Michelle V. Stacy to the Board. Ms. Stacy has not been elected to any committees of the Board. In connection with her election to the Board, Ms. Stacy will be awarded restricted stock units under the Company’s 2005 Stock Option and Incentive Plan having a fair market value of $220,000, measured at the end of the tenth week of the Company’s third fiscal quarter, in accordance with the Company’s director compensation practices. There are no understandings or arrangements between Ms. Stacy and any other person pursuant to which Ms. Stacy was elected as a director. Information concerning the membership of the Board’s committees is publicly available on the Company’s website at http://www.irobot.com. The Company intends to disclose changes in the membership of the Board’s committees by posting this information on its website and/or in its public filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission. Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure. On August 11, 2014, the Company issued a press release announcing Ms. Stacy’s election to the Company’s Board. A copy of this press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Report on Form 8-K.
