Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. On January 23, 2025, Air Products and Chemicals, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2025 Annual Meeting of Shareholders (the “Annual Meeting”). There were 176,657,486 shares of common stock represented at the meeting by valid proxies or voted at the meeting, which was approximately 79.44% of the shares of common stock entitled to vote at the meeting and which constituted a quorum.
