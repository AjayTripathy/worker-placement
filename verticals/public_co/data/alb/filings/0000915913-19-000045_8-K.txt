Common Stock, $.01 Par Value | ALB | New York Stock Exchange Item 5.07 | Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. On May 7, 2019, the Company held its Annual Meeting. During this meeting, shareholders of the Company were asked to consider and vote upon three proposals: (1) approval of the non-binding advisory resolution approving the compensation of the Company’s named executive officers; (2) election of eleven nominees set forth in our 2019 Proxy Statement to the Board of Directors; and (3) ratification of the appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2019. As of the record date for the Annual Meeting, March 8, 2019, there were 105,885,909 shares of common stock outstanding and entitled to vote, of which the holders of 91,063,903 shares of common stock were represented in person or by proxy at the Annual Meeting. For each proposal, the results of the shareholder voting were as follows: 1. Advisory vote on executive compensation .
