On May 8, 2018, the Company held its Annual Meeting. During this meeting, shareholders of the Company were asked to consider and vote upon four proposals: (1) approval of the non-binding advisory resolution approving the compensation of the Company’s named executive officers; (2) election of eleven nominees set forth in our 2018 Proxy Statement to the Board of Directors; (3) approval of the amendment and restatement of the Company’s Amended and Restated Articles of Incorporation; and (4) ratification of the appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2018. As of the record date for the Annual Meeting, March 8, 2018, there were 110,729,039 shares of common stock outstanding and entitled to vote, of which the holders of 92,854,364 shares of common stock were represented in person or by proxy at the Annual Meeting.
