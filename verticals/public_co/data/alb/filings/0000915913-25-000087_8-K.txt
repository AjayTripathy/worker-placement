On May 6, 2025, the Company held its 2025 Annual Meeting of Shareholders (the “Annual Meeting”). The proposals are described in the definitive proxy statement filed by the Company on March 27, 2025 (the “Proxy Statement”). The voting results for each proposal are set forth below.
