On April 3, 2020, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Intellia Therapeutics, Inc. (the “Company”) amended the Company’s Second Amended and Restated By-laws (the “By-laws Amendment”) in order to amend Section 8 of Article VI to (i) designate the United States District Court for the District of Massachusetts as the exclusive jurisdiction for any litigation arising under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, (ii) clarify that the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware shall remain the exclusive forum for any state law claims, (iii) clarify that the exclusive state forum provision applies to actions asserting a claim of breach of or based on a fiduciary duty owed by any current and former director, officer or other employee to the Company or its stockholders and (iv) clarify that the exclusive state forum provision applies to actions asserting a claim against the Company or any current or former director, officer or other employee or stockholder. The foregoing summary and description of the provisions of the By-laws Amendment does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the By-laws Amendment, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 3.1 with this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
