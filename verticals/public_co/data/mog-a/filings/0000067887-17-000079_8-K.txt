SIGNATURE EXHIBIT INDEX EX-10.1 Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. On October 23, 2017, Moog Inc. (the “Company”) and Moog Receivables LLC, a wholly owned bankruptcy remote special purpose subsidiary of the Company (the “Receivables Subsidiary”) entered into the Ninth Amendment to the Receivables Purchase Agreement, dated October 23, 2017, by and among the Company, as servicer, the Receivables Subsidiary, as seller and PNC Bank, National Association, as issuer and administrator (the “Ninth Amendment to the Receivables Purchase Agreement”). The Ninth Amendment to the Receivables Purchase Agreement amends the Receivables Purchase Agreement dated as of March 5, 2012, as amended. Among other matters, this amendment increases the borrowing capacity from $120 million to $130 million, extends the maturity of the facility to October 23, 2019 and modifies the Program Fee pricing level. The above description does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Ninth Amendment to the Receivables Purchase Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated by reference herein.
