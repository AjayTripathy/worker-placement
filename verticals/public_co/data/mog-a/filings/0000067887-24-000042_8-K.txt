(the "Company") in order to assume the role of Finance Director, Military Aircraft Group. In this position, he reports to Jennifer Walter, Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer. Prior to that, from July 2015 through May 2022, he held a number of positions within CooperVision, Inc., a wholly-owned subsidiary of The Cooper Companies, Inc., most recently serving as Senior Director - Divisional Controller of that company. Mr. Hart is a Certified Public Accountant, holds an MBA from the University at Buffalo School of Management, and a bachelors degree in information technology from Rochester Institute of Technology.
