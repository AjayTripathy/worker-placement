On March 15, 2018, Moog Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that the Company’s Board of Directors has authorized the initiation of a dividend program under which the Company intends to pay a regular quarterly cash dividend to shareholders of its Class A and Class B common stock. The Board of Directors declared a $0.25 per share quarterly dividend payable on each of the Company's issued and outstanding shares of Class A common stock and Class B common stock on June 1, 2018 to shareholders of record at the close of business on May 15, 2018. A copy of the press release is included as Exhibit 99.1 of this report.
