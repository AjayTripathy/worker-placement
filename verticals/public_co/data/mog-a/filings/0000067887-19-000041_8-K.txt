(c) The Company has hired and appointed Michael J. Swope, age 34, to the position of Controller and Principal Accounting Officer effective March 4, 2019. In this position, he succeeds and reports to Jennifer Walter, Vice President - Finance. Mr. Swope most recently served as Principal at Tronconi Segarra & Associates LLP and as Senior Manager at that firm since November 2017. Prior to that, he was employed by EY where he was promoted from Manager to Senior Manager in October 2015. Mr. Swope is a Certified Public Accountant and holds an MBA and a bachelors degree in accounting from St. Bonaventure University.
