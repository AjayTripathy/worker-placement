Item 8.01 Other Events. On December 19, 2013, Moog Inc. (the “Company”) announced that the redemption of the Company’s 7¼% senior subordinated notes (the “Notes”) due in 2018 has been completed. The Notes, which were in the aggregate principal amount of $200 million, were repurchased at 103.625% on December 19, 2013 pursuant to an early redemption right. Interest was paid on the Notes through the redemption date. The trustee for the Notes was Wells Fargo Bank N.A. The redemption was completed using proceeds drawn from the Company’s U.S. revolving credit facility. The material terms of the Company’s U.S. revolving credit facility are set forth in Part II, Items 7 and 8 of the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 12, 2013.
