March 4, 2019, DiamondPeak Holdings Corp. (the &ldquo;<U>Company</U>&rdquo;) consummated its initial public offering (the &ldquo;<U>IPO</U>&rdquo;)
of 25,000,000 units (the &ldquo;<U>Units</U>&rdquo;). Each Unit consists of one share of Class A common stock of the Company,
par value $0.0001 per share ( &ldquo;<U>Class A Common Stock</U>&rdquo;), and one-third of one redeemable warrant of the Company
(&ldquo;<U>Warrant</U>&rdquo;), with each whole Warrant entitling the holder thereof to purchase one share of Class A Common Stock
for $11.50 per share. The Units were sold at a price of $10.00 per Unit, generating gross proceeds to the Company of $250,000,000.
The Company has granted the underwriters in the IPO (the &ldquo;<U>Underwriters</U>&rdquo;) a 45-day option to purchase up to
3,750,000 additional Units to cover over-allotments, if any.</FONT></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in"><FONT STYLE="font-family: Times New Roman, Times, Serif; font-size: 10pt">&nbsp;</FONT></P>

with the closing of the IPO, the Company completed the private sale (the &ldquo;<U>Private Placement</U>&rdquo;) of an aggregate
of 4,666,667 warrants (the &ldquo;<U>Private Placement Warrants</U>&rdquo;) to DiamondPeak Sponsor LLC (the &ldquo;<U>Sponsor</U>&rdquo;)
and accounts managed by subsidiaries of BlackRock, Inc. (the &ldquo;Anchor Investor&rdquo;), at a purchase price of $1.50 per
Private Placement Warrant, generating gross proceeds to the Company of $7,000,000. The Sponsor has committed to purchase up to
an additional 500,000 Private Placement Warrants if the Underwriters exercise their over-allotment option in full.</FONT></P>
