<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; padding-left: 0"><B>Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">On March 4, 2019,
DiamondPeak Holdings Corp. (the &ldquo;<U>Company</U>&rdquo;) consummated its initial public offering (the &ldquo;<U>IPO</U>&rdquo;)
of 25,000,000 units (the &ldquo;<U>Units</U>&rdquo;). Each Unit consists of one share of Class A common stock of the Company,
par value $0.0001 per share (&ldquo;<U>Class A Common Stock</U>&rdquo;), and one-third of one redeemable warrant of the Company
(&ldquo;&nbsp;<U>Warrant</U>&nbsp;&rdquo;), with each whole Warrant entitling the holder thereof to purchase one share of Class
A Common Stock for $11.50 per share. The Units were sold at a price of $10.00 per Unit, generating gross proceeds to the Company
of $250,000,000. The Company has granted the underwriters in the IPO (the &ldquo;Underwriters&rdquo;) a 45-day option to purchase
up to 3,750,000 additional Units to cover over-allotments, if any.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; padding-left: 0"><B>Item 3.02. Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities.</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; padding-left: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">Simultaneously with the
closing of the IPO, pursuant to the Private Placement Warrants Purchase Agreement, the Company completed the private sale of an
aggregate of 4,666,667 warrants (the &ldquo;<U>Private Placement Warrants</U>&rdquo;) at a purchase price of $1.50 per Private
Placement Warrant to the Sponsor and accounts managed by subsidiaries of BlackRock, Inc. (the &ldquo;<U>Anchor Investor</U>&rdquo;),
generating gross proceeds to the Company of $7,000,000. The Sponsor has committed to purchase up to an additional 500,000 Private
Placement Warrants if the Underwriters exercise their over-allotment option in full. The Private Placement Warrants are identical
to the Warrants sold as part of the Units in the IPO, except that the Sponsor has agreed not to transfer, assign or sell any of
the Private Placement Warrants (except to certain permitted transferees) until 30 days after the completion of the Company&rsquo;s
initial business combination. The Private Placement Warrants are also not redeemable for cash by the Company so long as they are
held by the Sponsor or its permitted transferees. No underwriting discounts or commissions were paid with respect to such sale.
The issuance of the Private Placement Warrants was made pursuant to the exemption from registration contained in Section 4(a)(2)
of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended.</P>
