<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">On October 22, 2020,
DiamondPeak Holdings Corp. (&ldquo;<U>DiamondPeak</U>&rdquo;) held a special meeting in lieu of the 2020 annual meeting (the &ldquo;<U>Special
Meeting</U>&rdquo;) of stockholders. At the Special Meeting, DiamondPeak stockholders, were asked to consider and vote on the proposals
identified in the definitive proxy statement that it filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the &ldquo;<U>SEC</U>&rdquo;)
on October 8, 2020 (the &ldquo;<U>Definitive Proxy</U>&rdquo;), in connection with the Agreement and Plan of Merger (the &ldquo;<U>Merger
Agreement</U>&rdquo;), dated as of August 1, 2020, among DiamondPeak, Lordstown Motors Corp., a Delaware corporation (&ldquo;<U>Lordstown</U>&rdquo;),
and DPL Merger Sub Corp., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of DiamondPeak, which Merger Agreement provides
for the merger of Merger Sub with and into Lordstown (the &ldquo;<U>Merger</U>&rdquo;).</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

$0.0001 per share (&ldquo;<U>DiamondPeak Common Stock</U>&rdquo;), were issued and outstanding and entitled to vote at the Special
Meeting. 18,341,403 shares of DiamondPeak Common Stock were represented in person or by proxy at the Special Meeting, and, therefore,
a quorum was present.</P>
