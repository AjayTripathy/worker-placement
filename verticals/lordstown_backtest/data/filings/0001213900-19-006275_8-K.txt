<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0"><B>Item&nbsp;8.01 Other Events. </B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0"><B>&nbsp;</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0; text-align: justify"><B><I>Separate Trading of Units, Class A Common
Stock and Warrants</I></B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 6pt 0 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">As previously reported
on Current Reports on Form 8-K, filed on March 4, 2019 and March 18, 2019, DiamondPeak Holdings Corp. (the &ldquo;Company&rdquo;)
consummated its initial public offering (&ldquo;IPO&rdquo;) of an aggregate of 28,000,000 units (the &ldquo;Units&rdquo;). Each
Unit consists of one share of Class A common stock of the Company, par value $0.0001 per share (&ldquo;Class A Common Stock&rdquo;),
and one-third of one redeemable warrant of the Company (&ldquo;Warrant&rdquo;), with each whole Warrant entitling the holder thereof
to purchase one share of Class A Common Stock for $11.50 per share. The Units were sold at a price of $10.00 per Unit, generating
gross proceeds to the Company of approximately $280,000,000.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 6pt 0 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">On April 12, 2019,
the Company issued a press release, a copy of which is attached as Exhibit&nbsp;99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K, announcing
that the holders of the Units may elect to separately trade shares of the Class&nbsp;A Common Stock and Warrants comprising the
Units commencing on April 15, 2019. Those Units not separated will continue to trade on The Nasdaq Capital Market under the symbol
&ldquo;DPHCU,&rdquo; and the Class&nbsp;A Common Stock and Warrants that are separated will trade on The Nasdaq Capital Market
under the symbols &ldquo;DPHC&rdquo; and &ldquo;DPHCW,&rdquo; respectively. No fractional warrants will be issued upon separation
of the units and only whole warrants will trade. Holders of the Units will need to have their brokers contact American Stock Transfer&nbsp;&amp; Trust Company, the Company&rsquo;s transfer agent, in order to separate the Units into shares of Class A Common Stock and
Warrants.</P>
