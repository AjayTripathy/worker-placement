<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><FONT STYLE="text-transform: uppercase"><B>INTRODUCTORY
NOTE</B></FONT></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">On October 23, 2020
(the &ldquo;<U>Closing Date</U>&rdquo;), Lordstown Motors Corp. (f/k/a DiamondPeak Holdings Corp.), a Delaware Corporation (the
 &ldquo;<U>Company</U>&rdquo;), consummated the transactions contemplated by the agreement and plan of merger (the &ldquo;<U>Merger
Agreement</U>&rdquo;), dated August 1, 2020, among the Company, Lordstown EV Corporation (f/k/a Lordstown Motors Corp.), a Delaware
corporation (&ldquo;<U>Legacy LMC</U>&rdquo;), and DPL Merger Sub Corp., a Delaware corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of
the Company (&ldquo;<U>Merger Sub</U>&rdquo;), pursuant to which Merger Sub merged with and into Legacy LMC with Legacy LMC surviving
the merger (the &ldquo;<U>Merger</U>&rdquo;). Upon consummation of the Merger, the Company was renamed Lordstown Motors Corp. and
Legacy LMC became a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">Upon the terms and
subject to the conditions set forth in the Merger Agreement, at the effective time of the Merger (the &ldquo;<U>Effective Time</U>&rdquo;),
each issued and outstanding share of common stock, par value $0.0001 per share, of Legacy LMC (&ldquo;<U>Legacy LMC Common Stock</U>&rdquo;)
was converted into 55.8817 shares (the &ldquo;<U>Exchange Ratio</U>&rdquo;) of Class A common stock, par value $0.0001 per share,
of the Company (&ldquo;<U>Class A Common Stock</U>&rdquo;), resulting in an aggregate of 75,918,063 shares of Class A Common Stock
to be issued to Legacy LMC stockholders. At the Effective Time, each outstanding option to purchase Legacy LMC Common Stock (&ldquo;<U>Legacy
LMC Options</U>&rdquo;), whether vested or unvested, was automatically converted into an option to purchase a number of shares
of Class&nbsp;A Common Stock equal to the product of (x)&nbsp;the number of shares of Legacy LMC Common Stock subject to such Legacy
LMC Option and (y)&nbsp;the Exchange Ratio, at an exercise price per share equal to (A)&nbsp;the exercise price per share of Legacy
LMC Common Stock of such Legacy LMC Option immediately prior to the Effective Time divided by (B)&nbsp;the Exchange Ratio.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">Pursuant to the Company&rsquo;s
Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation, as in effect prior to the closing of the Merger (the &ldquo;<U>Closing</U>&rdquo;),
each outstanding share of the Company&rsquo;s Class&nbsp;B common stock, par value $0.0001 per share, was automatically converted
into one share of Class&nbsp;A Common Stock.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">Stockholders holding
970 shares of the Company&rsquo;s Class A Common Stock exercised their right to redeem such shares for a pro rata portion of the
funds in the Company&rsquo;s trust account (&ldquo;<U>Trust Account</U>&rdquo;). As a result, approximately $9,835 (or approximately
$10.14 per share) was paid to such holders.</P>
