 Why invest in a real estate fund?
 Investing in a real estate fund offers several advantages over individual real estate investments, primarily through economies of scale, which allow funds to negotiate better deals and reduce costs per unit. Diversification across multiple properties and locations mitigates risk, unlike owning a single property. Lower barriers to entry enable investors to participate with smaller capital outlays, making it accessible to more people. Professional oversight by experienced managers ensures strategic decisions and efficient operations. Investors also benefit from passive income without the hassles of being a landlord. Additionally, real estate funds often provide better liquidity and flexibility compared to the typically illiquid nature of direct property investments, allowing investors to buy and sell shares more easily.

 Liquidity &amp; Exit Opportunities
 Q: How do I receive cash from my investment? We distribute available cash flow quarterly. Investors can choose to take these distributions as income or reinvest them to compound returns.
 Q: Are there additional distributions beyond quarterly cash flow? Yes. Investors also receive distributions from major liquidity events, such as the sale of a property or portfolio.
 Q: What are the long-term exit opportunities?
 Portfolio Sale / PE Exit: One likely outcome is a sale of stabilized assets or the entire portfolio to a private equity firm or institutional buyer. This typically crystallizes substantial equity growth.
 Q: What if I need to exit early? While private equity real estate is inherently less liquid than the stock market, we are committed to providing flexibility where possible:
 We allow LPs to request liquidity as projects cycle out.
 We can help facilitate a transfer of your position to a new investor.
 Depending on market conditions, Reawaken may also consider buying back shares to create a clean exit for an LP.
 Q: Is liquidity guaranteed? Liquidity cannot be guaranteed in the short term. However, our structure provides multiple paths to exit, and our track record shows we work closely with investors to create solutions when needs arise.
