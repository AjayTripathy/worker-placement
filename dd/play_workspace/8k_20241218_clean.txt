Section 8 – Other Events
Item 8.01.    Other Events.
 
In a Press Release, the Company announced that, on December 16, 2024, the Board of Directors authorized a $100.0 million increase to its previously existing share repurchase program.  The program remains effective until such time as completed and may be suspended, modified or discontinued at any time without notice. The Company may repurchase shares from time to time in open market transactions and may also repurchase shares in accelerated share repurchases, tender offers, privately negotiated transactions or by other means, and by plans pursuant to Rule 10b5-1 under the Securities and Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and the timing and amount of transactions will be determined by the Company’s management in consultation with the Finance Committee of the Board of Directors based on an evaluation of market conditions, the Company’s share price, legal requirements, restricted payment capacity under its debt instruments and other factors.
