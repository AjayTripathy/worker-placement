# PITCH — Ramaco Resources (METC) — VERDICT: Avoid. The signed document says $1 billion; the marketing says $8 billion.

*Decided 2026-08-07 at $9.59. Challenge bench REJECT 9/10; review bench waived — the evidence is documentary and physical, and leaves no long case to prosecute. Coal-only fair value ~$5. Confidence 9/10.*

**Say the awkward part first.** We are not calling fraud. We are reporting a gap: the number a licensed professional was willing to sign under securities liability, and the number investor relations published five days later, differ by a factor of eight — and only one of them has a sensitivity table.

## What we believe
Ramaco is a competent, first-quartile-cost metallurgical coal producer at a cyclical trough (cash margin $17/ton and falling, a $15M quarterly loss), worth roughly $5 a share on coal alone — with $200–300M of rare-earth story value still in the $9.59 price that the filed technical documents do not support.

## What the market believes
That the Brook Mine (Wyoming) makes Ramaco a critical-minerals company: an $8 billion project ramping toward production, riding the rare-earth policy wave.

## Why the story fails its own paperwork
- **The signed technical report** (filed 2026-07-24, qualified-person signature, Section 18 liability): NPV **$1.0B** on **$579M** capex, with sensitivities. **The furnished 8-K five days later**: **$8.0B** on **$3.2–4.0B** capex — no discount rate, no price deck, no sensitivity, and "not deemed filed." Running the engineering firm's own capex through the signed report's own sensitivity table extrapolates to a **negative** NPV.
- **It's a scandium project**: 60–68% of the claimed value is scandium, at a planned output equal to **122% of world demand** — production that would destroy the price assumption used to value it.
- **Physical ground truth**: the federal mine-safety record lists the site as **NonProducing since June 2025** — zero tons in the only two quarters ever reported, five employees, on a *coal* permit. Wyoming has no rare-earth permit category.
- **The refinery cannot legally break ground**: it requires a state Industrial Siting Council permit (it is ~11–14× over the threshold) and **no application exists** in the council's fifty-year archive or its published 2026 agendas.
- Capex guidance grew **+746%** in twelve months while first production slipped 2028→2031; zero binding offtake agreements have ever been filed.

## Why it might be priced in (the concessions)
The company's coal operations are honestly run and disclosed; liquidity is $400M (raised into the story's strength — dilution, but survivable); the drawdown from the October 2025 peak has already removed most of the mania. And shorting is expressly not recommended — 5/10 conviction at best — because this sits in a policy tape where a single federal announcement can reprice the story without touching the facts.
