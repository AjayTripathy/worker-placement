Findings

F-1   Endpoint switch after a missed Phase 2 primary  ELEVATE

R (claim & source): Tenax markets the Phase 2 HELP trial as “Positive Results … which formed the basis for the Phase 3.” [BusinessWire, 2020-06-02; Circ: Heart Failure publication, 2021]
f (method): Compare the Phase 2 primary endpoint result against the Phase 3 primary endpoint.
M (authority): ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03541603 (HELP primary = “Change in PCWP with bicycle exercise”) vs NCT05983250 (LEVEL primary = 6MWD).
Finding: HELP MISSED its primary endpoint (exercise PCWP −1.4 mmHg, 95% CI −7.8 to 4.8, p=0.65). The 6MWD result that is now the LEVEL primary was a HELP secondary (+29.3 m, p=0.033) in n=37, responder-enriched. Marketing a primary miss as “positive” and slotting the Phase 3 primary from the surviving secondary is the classic endpoint-substitution pattern — it materially elevates the risk of a Phase 3 miss.

F-2   Responder enrichment dropped (HELP → LEVEL)  ELEVATE

R (claim & source): LEVEL is positioned as a registrational confirmation of HELP’s 6MWD signal.
f (method): Compare HELP’s enrollment design to LEVEL’s eligibility (CT.gov).
M (authority): NCT03541603: open-label lead-in dose phase; only acute responders (≥4 mmHg acute PCWP drop, 37/44 = 84%) randomized. NCT05983250: no responder lead-in — all-comers PH-HFpEF.
Finding: HELP enriched for acute responders; LEVEL is unselected. A marginal 6MWD signal observed in 37 enriched patients must now replicate in 230 all-comers — a well-documented failure mode for functional endpoints.

F-3   “Novel, first-in-class” on a 25-year-old molecule  REVIEW

R (claim & source): “Novel, first-in-class K-ATP channel activator / calcium sensitizer.”
f (method): Check the molecule’s regulatory/commercial history.
M (authority): Levosimendan (Simdax, Orion Pharma) — IV calcium sensitizer marketed in Europe since ~2000 for acute decompensated heart failure.
Finding: Spin, not a lie: “first-in-class” applies only to the oral formulation in this indication. The framing oversells a repurposed legacy molecule — a microstructure tell of optimistic positioning.

F-5   Cap structure: “sub-cash” RETIRED + dilution finding  CORRECTION

R (claim & source): An initial read (on the cover/stale share count of 9.31M) suggested TENX traded below net cash (~$12.75/sh) — implying a market PoS of only ~6–13%.
f (method): Pull the full cap structure from the 10-Q equity notes (cover count is stale/partial): current common + pre-funded warrants + warrant/option overhang; use point-in-time rollforward tables, not the weighted-average EPS line.
M (authority): 10-Q equity notes: common 24,275,500 (3/31/26); pre-funded warrants 24,566,607 @ $0.01 (≈ shares, don’t expire); Aug-2024 warrants 10,232,295 @ $4.50 (expire 30 trading days after LEVEL topline); Feb-2024 0.22M @ $5.65; options 7.87M @ $7.34.
Finding: Economic base = 48.84M (not 9.31M). Net cash ≈ $2.43/sh → stock at ~3–4.5× cash, NOT sub-cash — the bargain thesis is retired. Real finding is the opposite: common 2.6×’d in one quarter (a $6.04 offering + pre-funded exercises); ~67M fully diluted; the $4.50 Aug-2024 warrants are catalyst-wired (exercise on a win → dilution that caps upside; expire on a loss).

F-6   6MWD endpoint & “powered >90%” framing  REVIEW

R (claim & source): A prespecified Blinded Sample-Size Re-estimation (BSSR) shows LEVEL “powered well over 90%.” [PR 2025-12-17]
f (method): Identify what a blinded BSSR actually measures.
M (authority): BSSR is blinded — it confirms the pooled variance assumption, not the treatment effect.
Finding: “Powered” ≠ “likely to succeed”: the >90% holds only if the true all-comers effect is the assumed 25 m (F-2 says it likely regresses below). 6MWD is effort-dependent/placebo-prone and the 25 m bar is modest; FDA has been mixed on 6MWD-only in PH/HF.

Valuation & PoS framing

  • Fixed-date binary, not event-driven. LEVEL reads 6MWD at a fixed Week 26 (last-patient-in ~Q1-2026), so the readout is calendar-fixed (~Q3–Q4 2026) regardless of outcome — no hazard-slip / timing↔outcome coupling (unlike an event-driven survival trial). The binary is purely the outcome.
  • Market-implied PoS ~22–37% (center ~30%) — from price = p·U + (1−p)·D, with failure floor D ≈ economic cash/share ~$2.4 and win-state U ≈ $25–40 (first approvable PH-HFpEF therapy). This sits inside the honesty-tells-adjusted estimate of ~25–35% (real but marginal 6MWD signal + adequate powering, minus the failed Phase-2 primary, dropped enrichment, and the PH-HFpEF graveyard). Roughly fair.
  • Model U on the fully-diluted ~67M, not the 24.3M common — a win triggers the $4.50-warrant exercise window (30 trading days post-topline), diluting the win-state.
  • PoS is unidentifiable — quote the range, not a point.

Bottom line

The framework correctly flags LEVEL as over-marketed (failed Phase-2 primary sold as “positive,” endpoint switched to a marginal enriched-population secondary, enrichment then dropped) → ELEVATE, do not take the headline PoS. But the market has already priced this — TENX trades at a normal ~3–4.5× cash with an implied PoS (~30%) in line with the honesty-adjusted estimate, and carries heavy dilution/overhang. So: no short-side honesty-alpha (the “lie” is detected and already in the price) and no contrarian long unless you hold a science view that the all-comers 6MWD replicates against the disclosed evidence. The cap-structure step earned its keep — it killed a false “sub-cash bargain” before it became a thesis.
