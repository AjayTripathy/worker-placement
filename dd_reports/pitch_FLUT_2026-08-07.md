# PITCH — Flutter Entertainment (FLUT) — VERDICT: Decline. The scary story is refuted and the stock is still not a buy.

*Decided 2026-08-07 at $93.15, −70% from the high. Both benches at 8/10 — the challenge bench filed REJECT and the review bench sustained it after two days of evidence-gathering across state regulators, federal dockets, and venue data. The most evidence-saturated court of the campaign. Confidence 8/10.*

**Say the awkward part first.** We came to this expecting a dislocation: a great business down 70% on a fear (prediction markets eating sportsbooks) we suspected was overblown. Half of that was right. The fear *is* overblown — we refuted it three independent ways. But the stock still isn't mispriced, because the −70% turns out to be pricing something else entirely, and that something is real.

## What we believe
Prediction-market substitution is **small and mostly incremental**. Three methods agree: FanDuel's own disclosure (low single digits); an eight-state regulator cross-section showing the handle declines concentrate almost entirely in states that raised betting taxes (PA −12.9%, NJ −9.8%, IL −5.1%) while unchanged states sit flat — a nationwide product like Kalshi can't produce state-clustered damage; and a venue-volume reconciliation showing gross exchange activity of 5–8% of handle nets to a ~1–1.5 point drag — meaning **70–80% of exchange volume is new demand, not diverted demand**. On the one specific claim the market punished hardest, Flutter's management is telling the truth.

## What the market believes — and what it's actually pricing
The narrative says "Kalshi is eating the sportsbooks." The tape says otherwise: both FLUT and DKNG *rallied* double digits on federal-legitimization news days. What the price actually reflects, leg by dated leg: a UK gaming tax that went **21%→40% by statute** in April against the international segment that produces 83% of group cash earnings (running −22% organically); **three guidance cuts** in a year, including −$210M to US EBITDA on August 5; leverage at **4.3×** with the buyback tranche run off; a CEO transition; and a fourth-quarter US target that requires +66% growth in the seasonally decisive period. The facts led the price at every step — the handle data inflected in December 2025, two months before the stock breaks.

## Why we might have edge
We don't — and proving that cleanly is the deliverable. The edge *candidate* was the substitution overreaction; it doesn't exist. What we have instead is a **free, dated decision system** the market isn't watching: the September–November state regulator files give the first clean NFL-season comparison with the tax-unchanged cohort (MI/TN/IN/NY/OH) split out — they publish *before* the Q3 prints and settle "bounded vs compounding" for nothing. That test is wired as a top-tier pack on our calendar.

## Why it might be priced in
Because it is — that's the verdict. At 10.1× guided EBITDA and a 2.8% owner-earnings yield, with two-thirds of equity value resting on FanDuel at ~16× a guided-down number, the market has taken the middle of a live two-sided situation, correctly. And the legal picture forbids sizing the tail: preemption is genuinely unsettled (one preliminary-posture circuit win for Kalshi against the first *final* merits judgment the other way, three circuits argued and undecided, a Supreme Court petition deadline in September). A review-bench finding sharpened it further: North Carolina just enacted the *legalize-and-undercut* template — 6% tax on prediction markets against 23% on sportsbooks, preemption codified — which entrenches the tax asymmetry by statute rather than fixing it.

## The plan
No position at any size. Two free gates, both dated: **(1)** any circuit merits ruling on preemption; **(2)** the September regulator files on the tax-unchanged cohort (our pack grades them October 20, before the prints). Both clean → re-court with the drag bounded; the remaining question would be purely the UK-tax and guidance-credibility legs at whatever price then prevails. Below ~$75 with the legal question still open, re-court on valuation alone. Until then, the sector reads: SRAD watch-listed, DKNG parked to its print, the cohort's narrative formally resolved as *tax-and-P&L, not substitution*.

## What remains unverified
Kalshi's contract-mix inputs (no independent source reaches them — the incrementality arithmetic is internally consistent but its inputs are estimates); whether the buyback is formally suspended; the federal excise-tax question on exchange wagers (genuinely unsettled in both directions, unpriced).
