# Valuation, Sizing & Catalysts — from fair value to position

Doctrine distilled from the desk's ruled case law on turning a fair-value estimate into an actual position: FV calibration, edge-vs-carry classification, EM/foreign-line verification, catalyst prediction and action, and after-tax exit math. Each rule carries its ruling date and the public-ticker precedent that set it. Personal/account context is out of scope for this plane.

## conservative-fv-reflexes
**When down-revising a fair value, never pull the growth lever and the multiple lever fully at the same time — the haircuts compound multiplicatively and overshoot low.** Diagnosed 2026-06-30 (COSMECCA FV re-audit). Two reflexes fire together: (1) anchoring a share-gainer's growth to a decelerating aggregate — the winning book compounds 30-40% while the category sum is flat (aggregate flatness ≠ winner flatness; model the customer/order book, not the Census flow); (2) stripping the premium multiple to peer-or-below by default, even for the faster/higher-margin/higher-ROE member of the comp set. A "modest" −5% on EPS × −15% on the multiple = −20% FV; COSMECCA landed ~13% too low (₩67k vs correct ~₩76k). Fix: haircut only the least-supported lever fully, partial-haircut the rest; sanity-check the revised base against the Street's LOWEST estimate — landing below the bear analyst forces a re-derivation, not "margin of safety." The fix is lever calibration, NOT loosening skepticism (earnings-quality caution on COSMECCA was correct, which is why 76k not 82k). Empirical detector: the anti-book asymmetry diagnostic (reference implementation: desk/antibook_performance.py) — persistent regret >> savings means the conservative bias is measured, not a feeling.

## prob-weighted-fv
**Bullishness on a catalyst never moves the BASE fair value; it moves the entry only where our probability diverges from what the price already pays for.** Built 2026-06-30. Method (reference implementation: desk/prob_weighted_fv.py + desk/data/catalyst_scenarios.json): three scenarios (bull/base/bear) with a shared base probability; market_implied_p_bull = clip((px − p_base·base − (1−p_base)·bear)/(bull − bear)); edge_pp = (our p_bull − market-implied p_bull)×100; UNDERPRICED ≥ +8pp / FAIR / OVERPRICED ≤ −8pp. Two traps: binary bull-vs-bear over-states edge by forcing every non-bull outcome to the bear anchor (made COSMECCA look UNDERPRICED +16%; the 3-scenario correctly gave FAIR +4.5pp) — always use three scenarios; and p_favorable from catalyst predictions is NOT p_bull (it conflates bull + lean) — set the explicit {p_bull, p_base} split per name. "Bullish but priced" = hold the accumulate-band discipline, do not pay up for the catalyst.

## fairly-paid-risk-ownable
**A position needs no claimed edge as long as we are roughly fairly paid to bear a risk we want at that size — RP_FAIR is the deploying-book default allocation class; edge is the overlay, not the entry requirement.** Ruled 2026-07-01; re-affirmed 2026-07-07 ("AVOID lists are UNPAID-RISK lists — yield traps, premium-to-history at cycle tops, narrative premiums — never defense"). Requiring verified edge for every position leaves a deploying book empty and is itself a bias. The bar that keeps it honest: (1) fairness VERIFIED, not assumed — DD confirms the premium is roughly fair (an underpaid premium, e.g. Prysmian at 46x, is still out) and no hidden tails (cap structure, covenants, related-party, litigation); (2) a risk we WANT at that size — binary/uninsurable tails capped and unlevered, correlated names sleeve-capped at factor level; (3) honest labeling — the explainer says "carry, no information edge claimed" (mislabeling carry as edge corrupts sizing and the calibration record); (4) getting paid counts the full stack: premium + dividends/buybacks + tax-asymmetry overlay + cheap optionality. The deploying question for every screen survivor: "is the risk priced at MORE than its weight?" — over-charged risk IS the fairly-paid buyer's inventory (LDOS class deserves DD, not watch-parking). **BIOTECH CARVE-OUT (ratified 2026-08-26):** RP_FAIR never applies to development-stage binary biotech — fair-at-base-rate from a generalist seat is adverse selection against specialist price-setters. Ownable only with an affirmative science view + a frozen calibration call materially off the base rate; economics gate first (prize table at unadjusted base rate must already show asymmetry), then science dive, then human review. Commercial-stage biotech with earnings exempt.

## rp-tainted-gate
**Integrity-flagged names are ownable but NEVER as RP_FAIR — they enter a separate RP_TAINTED class with a 7-condition mechanical gate, and "at fair" is not enough: max entry is 0.85×E[fv].** Blue-court precedent 2026-07-28 (YSS). Trigger: core-revenue integrity divergence + EXTERNAL corroboration (customer, auditor, regulator, or the company's own reserve/restatement) + a live maturation path. Why: the tail on a flagged name is ENDOGENOUS — the same integrity problem contaminates the inputs used to verify fairness; "it's just a flow trade" is no defense since a fill is held through the maturation window. Conditions: (1) barred from RP_FAIR, long screens/baskets, idle-capital deployment; (2) fairness re-derived on externally verified inputs only (delivered units, cash, customer-corroborated contracts) — flagged-product guidance/backlog gets zero weight, and no surviving bear-case value = inadmissible above the fraud-branch price; (3) three-branch E[fv] with the fraud/impairment branch floored at p ≥ 0.20; max entry 0.85×E[fv]; (4) ≤ 0.5% NLV per name at cost, unlevered, defined-risk, no averaging down, aggregate sleeve ≤ 2% NLV; (5) any maturation event (SEC order, restatement, auditor change, customer cure/termination, flagged-function exec departure) = same-day mechanical exit, no re-court; (6) tag lifts only on affirmative cure — never price recovery or elapsed time; (7) explainer ledgers the fraud-branch p for calibration. First application: YSS max entry ~$10.50-11.00 vs the withdrawn $14.75/$13.50 ladder.

## bond-dominance-floor
**For any EM equity carrying a political/sovereign premium, the senior sovereign hard-currency eurobond spread FLOORS what the junior equity must pay — if the equity's political premium is less than the bond spread plus a subordination cushion, buy the bond and pass the equity.** Ruled 2026-06-30 (reference implementation: desk/bond_dominance.py, wired into desk/edge_pipeline.py EM_EQUITY_CHECK). Equity bears ~2× the loss-given-political-event, so floor = max(2·spread, spread + 200bps). Verdicts: premium < spread → BOND_DOMINATES; < floor → MARGINAL (prefer the bond); ≥ floor → EQUITY_CLEARS. Three caveats: (1) compare the equity's POLITICAL-COMPONENT premium (after debiting non-political warranted discounts: ROE gap, execution, multiple), not the whole implied-CoE premium; (2) compare LEVEL to LEVEL — never the residual alpha gap vs the bond spread (that mixes a mispricing with a risk premium; a mistake actually made once on TBC); (3) clearing the floor ≠ edge — TBC cleared (~530bps political premium vs ~152bps Georgia sovereign; excess 378 > 352 floor) yet was passed on the separate edge test (premium ≈ warranted). The bond is not a substitute when the thesis is the franchise's COMPOUNDING — but that's an RP_FAIR own-at-fair thesis, not a premium harvest. Anchor: Georgia sovereign USD ~152bps new-issue Jan-2026 / ~197bps legacy Z-spread (dated — re-verify before relying).

## em-value-hard-currency
**"High volatility → deep value" is a category error in EM/high-inflation markets — judge every EM value thesis in hard-currency (USD) total return, never local multiples.** Local earnings under 30-70% inflation balloon nominally, making P/Es look cheap while currency depreciation destroys the USD translation; domestic retail buys equities as an inflation hedge, spiking local indices as the currency craters. Turkey 2022 blow-off: TUR ~doubled in USD while Turkcell's ADR fell 47% over the same window; confirmed 2026 — TUR 10-yr USD total return +24% (~2.2%/yr, a lost decade), TKC ADR +24%/5yr USD. The trap tell: a market "always cheap" on local P/E with near-zero USD compounding over 5-10yr. Also run the bond-dominance floor: if sovereign USD CDS has already re-rated (Turkey ~220bps ≈ BB-ish; dated — re-verify before relying), the fear premium was paid out to bondholders, not left for equity. Require before seeding: (a) USD total-return history, (b) the currency/inflation regime and whether it's stabilizing, (c) sovereign CDS level. Defensible expressions: a small labeled risk-premium/macro-timing sleeve tied to a NAMED regime catalyst with policy-credibility tripwires, or hard-currency earners (dollar-revenue, local-cost exporters) verified at single-name USD level.

## em-bank-two-leg
**An EM-bank durability thesis has exactly two legs — verify the funding moat from the audited deposit note, and refute sovereign fear with the realized natural experiment — then size on the binary unhedgeable tail.** Validated end-to-end on TBC Bank Group / Georgia, 2026-06-29. Leg 1 (kill the rate-windfall bear): from the audited deposit note compute CASA %; blended deposit cost = interest expense on deposits ÷ avg balances; reconcile asset yield − blended cost ≈ reported NIM within ~10bps; then kill "spread compresses on cuts" with the bank's own audited interest-rate-gap table (a matched book means cuts are neutral-to-positive). TBC: CASA 54.7%, blended cost 5.68% vs ~10% headline term rate, NIM reconciled to 7.0%. Watch-outs: cheap FX deposits masquerading as a CASA moat; group NIM flattered by a high-nominal subsidiary (TBC's Uzbekistan, ~9% of profit). Leg 2: the decisive datum is what ACTUALLY happened to deposits/reserves/currency/dollarization in the worst recent stress, not a list of fears — through the 2024-25 Georgian political crisis deposits grew +12-13%, reserves hit a record, GEL strengthened, dollarization fell. Then separate survivable stress from the binary unhedgeable tail (bank-level/correspondent sanctions, not "officials sanctioned" — that already happened with zero equity impact) and size so the tail loss is tolerable (~1% of book), with a pre-committed add-path on de-risk catalysts or non-tail dislocations.

## adr-pb-untrustworthy
**No ADR/foreign-line book-value or P/B figure — vendor-supplied OR self-computed — may anchor a valuation until re-derived as ADR_px ÷ ADR_ratio ÷ FX and reconciled via the ROE/PB/PE triangle.** For a bank, ROE/(P/B) algebraically equals 1/PE on the same equity basis; divergence >40% means an input is on the wrong basis (in practice, always book). Case law: CIB implied 9,484pp; BCH implied 20% CoE vs true 6.3% (a stale 600:1 ADR ratio vs true 200:1 was circulating — verify the ratio itself by price triangulation, ADR_px × FX ÷ local_px). RECURRED with our own arithmetic (UMC, 2026-07-19): a DD wrote "~1x book" against the $21.25 ADR; the court killed it — $21.25 ÷ 5 ≈ NT$125 = ~3.9x book, and the bear case sat above two street PTs. The ADR-ratio+FX book check is a HARD pre-court gate for every ADR/foreign-line valuation. GENERALIZED (OPHC, 2026-07-07): the same triangle catches basic-vs-diluted share-count artifacts — screened 0.56x book on basic 12.2M shares while a disclosed imminent preferred conversion made the true count 23.6M (~1.07x diluted, fairly priced). Check implied P/E = P/B ÷ ROE (>30% divergence = wrong count) and derive the count from NI ÷ diluted EPS. Apply to every P/B-based screen escalation, any market, before a court convenes.

## tina-fair-value-entry
**"Fairly valued" is not "expensive" — for a court-verified quality compounder, fair value IS the entry; WAIT is only ever a fundamentals call, never a mere price call on quality.** Ruled 2026-06-26 after a blanket wait-for-a-pullback bias was caught across an entire quality cohort. Perma-WAIT is the call that's never wrong in the moment and quietly costly over years; on a structural grower the discount-to-history never comes. Segment the "no": WAIT/AVOID stands on deteriorating fundamentals, momentum vehicles, refuted theses (Silicon2, BRBR, HIMS, ODD, Cosmax, INMD, VERU, APR stayed avoid on fundamentals) — while quality wrongly price-gated flipped to own/scale (INSP, BEISY, PGNY, LRLCY, ESTA, WST, GALD). Tranching, not timing, is the risk control. Caveat held in tension: TINA's force is regime-dependent on real yields and is historically most seductive at tops — it justifies owning quality at fair, never overpaying or reaching into junk; ground it in the current ERP/real 10Y/CAPE, don't assert it. **§TINA-CLASS (ratified 2026-08-21):** a name passing the strict bar (durable moat + anti-masking-clean accounting + net cash/trivial leverage + secular growth) adjudicated FAIR takes entry AT FAIR at 0.5-1.0%, with adds on CALENDAR tranches, not price gates — the bargain band is acceleration, never permission. Contested moat or integrity overhang fails the class (APP, SRAD are the named non-examples). The confirmation-premium column in the missed-entry review measures what price-gating quality actually costs.

## segment-level-flooring
**Aggregate risk floors hide mix erosion — every multi-segment carry name requires the profit-mix decomposition plus a SEGMENT-level kill bar, dated, at entry.** The BAH autopsy (bought ~$80, −25%): underwritten on aggregate floors (appropriations-backed, backlog-covered) while the Civil segment (~29% of revenue, advisory-heavy) carried the real erosion; the segment gate was retrofitted a year late. LDOS (2026-07-07) is the corrected template: profit-mix FIRST (Health = 27% of revenue but 54% of segment OI — a segment erodes EPS at 2× its apparent revenue weight when margins concentrate); erosion named + dated + ceilinged from the filing's own disaggregation before purchase; the bear's best datum (Health backlog −21% YoY) carried on our record at entry; a quarterly segment kill bar (Health OI ≥ $250M/qtr) + event tripwires; entry POST-derate below the street-low PT. The masking variant (NATR, 2026-08-06): Asia printed +5.3% cc and read healthy, but Japan +50.7% cc on an explicitly non-durable pull-forward masked Taiwan −15.6% and China −20.1%; Asia ex-Japan was −11.9%, and the "solid" quarter arrived with a guide cut — the decomposition was in the 10-Q, absent from the press release. Always ask what the aggregate is carrying, and whether the carrier is durable or a timing effect.

## sotp-perimeter-and-tax
**Every SOTP/spin valuation must price the stub against the asset perimeter the catalyst actually crystallizes, net of deferred tax — and conservative bending is graded as divergence just like optimistic bending.** STERV court, 2026-07-23: the deck fused TOTAL forest value (€10.80/sh gross) with a catalyst that only crystallizes the Swedish piece (€7.10-7.23/sh) and ignored €1.64/sh deferred tax; the stub flipped from "opco < 0" to −0.21 after-tax vs the catalyzed perimeter. A dated event only forces the value of what it separates; marking ALL assets against the price while pointing at a partial catalyst manufactures a fake free stub. Three standing questions: (1) WHICH assets does the catalyst crystallize — price the stub vs that perimeter AND vs the total, separately; (2) net deferred tax off appraised marks before calling anything "free" — it is real seller leakage; (3) test the re-rating mechanism against the listed-comp record, not hope (SCA 0.70×, Holmen 0.85× own-forest, US timber REITs −24..−35% of NAV — the NAV-payer lives in the private market; a listing does not summon NAV-paying flows). Same court: the deck's "honest caution" slide bent numbers pessimistic (55× vs real ~22×, "breakeven" FCF vs +€122M) — grade both directions. Blue-team bonus: verify custody-level withholding mechanics (statutory treaty rates ≠ achievable rates at the broker).

## tax-adjust-every-exit
**No exit is staged on pre-tax math — every exit in a taxable book is priced after-tax first: ST/LT status and days to conversion, which tax year the gain lands in, and what harvested losses it consumes.** Ruled at the ARX takeout (staged +57% three days after entry on "remaining arb spread ≈ cash, no edge"; reversed — the hold dominated after-tax). Three stacked legs favored holding: selling realizes an ST gain in the current tax year and burns harvested losses; holding a cash takeout to close defers the gain a full tax year; a close slipping past 12 months converts the gain to LT at roughly half the rate — and a ticking fee (ARX: 6%/yr) means regulatory delay pays twice. Bonus geometry: entry below the unaffected price bounds the deal-break tail near basis. "No edge vs cash" is a pre-tax statement; the book's hurdle is after-tax. For deals, ticking fees and deferral optionality enter the comparison explicitly.

## name-the-yield-metric
**Every yield/carry/weight number must name its metric completely — gross vs tax-equivalent vs net-of-fee, the currency, the tax character of BOTH legs, and the denominator — or the claim inflates 2-3× and can even flip the verdict.** Pattern established 2026-05-28 over three same-session muni overstatements: a "+500 bps TEY" alpha on a sub-5% product (impossible — real ≈ 8bps TEY per pp of deterioration gap); a claim that cycled +60-100 → ~0 bps under pushback; "250-300 bps gross" that was actually ~100-150 gross / ~250-270 TEY / ~225 net. Discipline: state all three (gross YTM diff; TEY = gross × 2.01 CA top-bracket or ~1.65 federal-only; net-of-fee), summarize on NET-OF-FEE, disclose duration bought alongside pickup (compute payback for a 100bps move), and sanity-check against the underlying product yield. Extensions from later instances: the CURRENCY is part of the metric (BBD 2026-07-02: BRL dividend quoted as USD inflated carry ~1pp; vendor yield fields annualize one-time specials); the DENOMINATOR is part of the metric (position weights and sleeve caps must state their base, and mid-funding the committed DEPLOYABLE book governs, never stale current NLV — violated twice, including Japan starters staged ~6× too small; ADV caps are the only legitimate shrinking force); the TAX CHARACTER of each leg is part of the metric (UPBD court 2026-07-20: taxing a qualified dividend at ordinary rates while leaving the MMF leg untaxed flipped a true +260bps carry to a false −70bps — tax both legs at their own character, stated per leg).

## japan-netnet-gates
**Japan deep-value candidates gate on the 3-year CUMULATIVE operating-cash-flow decomposition and the no-actor law — trailing-year metrics and low-multiple screens are inadmissible.** Batch ruling 2026-07-23: the four best shelf names all falsified on trailing "value" — GEOSTR's 26% "FCF yield" was inventory destock (3-yr cumulative FCF 1.3%); DENSAN's 22.5% OPM was a one-time municipal-IT windfall (guide OP −61%); NIHON SEIKO's 40% FCF was an antimony-spike peak denominator, plus a 4:1 split-artifact fake drawdown; MORI-GUMI was a genuine net-net with NEGATIVE 3-yr cumulative opCF, a >50% counterparty-locked register, and zero activist history. Why: single-year FCF/earnings on tiny Japanese cyclicals is dominated by working-capital swings, windfalls, and securities gains; a discount with no actor capable of closing it compounds at the melt rate, not the re-rate. Gates: (1) 3-yr cumulative opCF stripped of WC swings, securities gains, divestitures; (2) NO-ACTOR LAW — require an identifiable forcing agent (activist large-holding precedent, PBR plan, open register) or price the name as perpetual carry; (3) PFIC-safety metrics are safety-only, never value filters; (4) ADV ≥ ~10× the intended order (one name failed at 82% of a day's volume). Killed names carry event-converters (activist filing, PBR-plan disclosure) — diaried, not dead.

## catalyst-option-joint-pass
**When valuing a dated option on a binary catalyst, draw outcome AND readout timing from a single joint simulation pass — never multiply an assumed timing distribution by an independent success probability.** SLS REGAL Jan-2027 $10 call: outcome and timing are physically coupled — a stronger treatment effect DELAYS the events, so the high-success draws are exactly the ones that slip past expiry. Independence manufactured a spurious value bump: naive $5.13 (~19% cheap) → consistent joint $3.70 (~14% RICH at U=$20), with 14.3% chance the readout slips past expiry; the "theta-cancelling hump" collapsed. Corollaries: (1) the dominant risk to a dated call on a binary readout is a late readout, most likely precisely when the drug works — size and pick expiry accordingly (rolling to Jan-2028 recovered most bull-row value); (2) PoS from blinded public data is unidentifiable — sweep the nuisance prior and report the RANGE (REGAL: 0.43-0.83 depending on the control-arm anchor), anchor nuisances to external benchmarks, and let hard event counts beat soft sponsor figures; (3) the market may simply have picked a side of the confound — positive-EV collapses to PoS×(U−K) > premium/discount, and the verdict stays conditional on the success-state price U; (4) check whether the readout is event-driven or date-driven — a date-driven cut caps slip risk.

## catalyst-prediction-pass
**Every thesis run-down predicts its catalysts — probability × timing window × magnitude, with named leading indicators and tripwires — because a cheap thesis with no fireable near-term catalyst is dead money even when the fair value is right.** Standing habit since 2026-07-01 (Samsung C&T, Aumovio: cheap, but the discount only closes on specific events). Ground each forecast in checkables: guidance credibility, order pipeline, index-review dates, analyst-initiation timing, insider dealings, anchor-stake building, M&A base rates, legal deadlines. Re-grade sizing on it: no fireable catalyst → smaller/PASS; high-probability near-term catalyst → size up and set the watch. Upgrades: **4-IDEA FRAME (2026-07-13)** — every frozen call states what WE believe, what the MARKET believes, why we might have an EDGE, and why it might be PRICED IN (enforced in reference implementation: desk/freeze_call.py); the gap between (1) and (2) IS the trade. **Priced-confirmation rule (2026-07-12)** — a "buy on the confirmation" trigger is dead money if the confirmation is priced: CVLT, our P(confirm) ≈ 0.42 vs ~0.80 priced → the print-buy was negative-EV; the real mispricing was the underpriced down-tail (options implied ±8.5% vs realized −31%/+11%); a knife-edge metric no indicator resolves caps P near 0.5 — say so. **Predict EVERY catalyst (2026-07-03)** — act-on-outcome events and peer-read inputs get frozen probabilities too; only our own operational deadlines stay unhandicapped. **Two-call split (2026-07-14)** — every earnings event freezes an OPERATING call and a STOCK-REACTION call separately: HCA beat operationally (EPS $7.62 +11.6%) yet fell 428→362 on the forward guide cut; never regrade a stock-miss into a win because the quarter was fine — the frozen number stays a miss, the operating leg feeds attribution only. **Mega-cap controls (2026-07-13)** — predict efficiently-priced mega-caps as calibration controls (tag `control`, excluded from the edge gate): tying the market there is success and miscalibration flags a machinery problem, not stock-picking.

## nowcast-vs-tape
**Before any print we're gated on, run the nowcast-vs-tape pass (T-7 to T-3): build a mechanical band from public intra-quarter data, measure what the tape already paid, and classify the quadrant before deciding.** Generalized from the IVN Q2 nowcast (2026-07-04) across 13 names. Method: find the complex's "monthly copper" channel (company monthlies, input-cost prints, weekly demand series, earlier-landing peer prints, own-disclosure silence as a tell); compute the flat-line floor (what does ZERO improvement imply vs the threshold?); measure the tape's run since quarter-start vs sector; quadrant: unpriced-good (add/pre-position) / priced-good (chase-guard) / unpriced-bad (de-risk BEFORE the print) / priced-bad (washout watch). First sweep caught a mis-timed catalyst (HRTG's confirmed −14.7% reinsurance saving reached only ~1 month of Q2 NEP), killed a pull-forward honestly (IBEX vs the CNXC complex warning), and caught priced-for-immunity vs customers pre-warning in writing (AMV0: customer China −20%, own orders −32%). Rules: annotate refined_p, NEVER revise the frozen p; a peer's pre-warning CONSUMES its update-table delta (double-count guard); when the miss branch is cushioned (tape already prices the bad outcome), relax to the EV computation and split pre-position/confirmation legs (BKE 0.78: half pre @42.25, half on the print @43.50). Widest gap ≠ highest conviction — a structural bear keeps its coin-flip p no matter how wide the gap.

## brier-vs-climatology
**Score prediction skill against climatology — Brier_clim = p̄(1−p̄) at the category's realized base rate — never against a 0.25 coin, which manufactures fake skill wherever the base rate is lopsided.** Ruled 2026-08-05 re-benchmarking a 69-resolution book: the "reaction" category flipped from +1.5% vs coin to **−10.8% vs climatology** (base rate 0.67 — "stock is up a week later" is usually true, so the class was worse than saying "up" every time); "other" flipped from mediocre +10% to +28%, a near-tie with the best partition. A coin is only right for a genuinely 50/50 event, and most financial catalysts aren't — beats outnumber misses, stocks drift up. A scoreboard rewarding the obvious keeps base-rate-riding classes and cuts the ones taking real two-sided risk. Apply: report both numbers, rank on climatology, never claim skill on the coin number alone; a category base far from 0.50 is the tell; MIXED (y=0.5) rows quietly inflate vs-coin skill.

## catalyst-action-loop
**A catalyst pipeline is detect → grade → ACT: every graded call fires a pre-registered, machine-actionable action within a 30-minute SLA, anchored to the pre-catalyst close — never leave "if X then do Y" in a prose field.** The BKE miss (2026-07-10): the June comp printed, the call graded FAVORABLE (0.78, hit), but the add-trigger sat in prose — action came the next morning after a ~2% pop (~2.1% cost, logged). System (reference implementation: desk/catalyst_action.py + catalyst_action_registry.json): actions registered per ticker/event ({anchor, favorable/unfavorable plans, auto_confirm}), kept out of the append-only calibration ledger; resolution arms the action instantly and surfaces it at the top of the morning brief; the 30-min unlock arms off the PROPOSED grade (provisional, upgraded in place on confirmation); mechanical grades auto-confirm only when opt-in, decisive, ≥ 0.85 confidence, models agreed — everything subjective keeps the human gate (a mis-grade corrupts the scoreboard). Anchor discipline: a late action enters at the pre-catalyst close; if price has run > 1% past the anchor, STAND DOWN, don't chase (encoded, not vibes) — for a value hold, rest patient limits AT the anchor instead; strict stand-down is for latency-sensitive names. A missed-entries ledger prices every broken loop.

## owned-name-instrumentation
**Every court-cleared BUY gets forward instrumentation in the same session it stages: a standing nowcast watch on its load-bearing variable plus a frozen graded call on the next print — mechanically, never on request.** Pipeline v1.5 doctrine, 2026-07-29 (the KALMAR case: the forecasting question had to be asked by hand after staging). The kill triggers already name the load-bearing operating variable, and alt-data channels (procurement/tender flow, customs BOL, hiring velocity, subsidy awards, monthly industry series, own-PR floor detectors) usually lead the next print by weeks to months. At staging: (1) name the variable from the kill list; (2) map channels via the dispatch-index pattern (see: kg-dispatch-index); (3) register the watch enabled with flags wired to the kill list; (4) freeze a graded earnings_operating call on the next print naming the detector — the nowcast updates SIZING only, never the frozen number; (5) label it TRIPWIRE NOWCAST, not alpha (the pipeline-leads-the-stock claim tested 4-for-4 negative); a blocked source reads DATA MISSING, never zero. First instance: desk/kalmar_order_nowcast.py (TED CPV tenders + order-PR floor + throughput anchor, graded at KALMAR.HE|2026-10-29).

## edge-classification
**No position is tagged EDGE until it survives the ENUMERATE → QUANTIFY → default-REJECT SKEPTIC pipeline; everything else is RISK_PREMIUM, TRAP, or DILIGENCE — and an overblown risk premium is itself a species of edge.** Reference implementation: desk/edge_pipeline.py. Stage 1: enumerate every discrete risk you're paid to bear, exhaustively, before quantification. Stage 2: size each from primary sources; candidate asymmetries must name the mechanism + resolution-to-price path + an already-priced check. Stage 3: an independent skeptic, default NOT_ASYMMETRY, passes only what clears all six tests (non-public/underweighted, not-already-priced, edge-not-verification, durable, not-a-risk-premium-in-disguise, falsifiable). Two edge species (correction 2026-06-30): EDGE:INFO (non-public datum / masking mechanism) and EDGE:PRICING (a fully visible risk the market mis-sizes) — so "it's a risk premium" is not auto-reject; the pricing pass quantifies premium_priced (implied CoE, credit spread, multiple gap) vs premium_warranted (base rates + realized natural experiment + comps) and connects the gap to a price: RP_OVERBLOWN → EDGE:PRICING; RP_FAIR → true beta; RP_UNDERPRICED → TRAP. Self-skeptic default: "the market is roughly right" — never manufacture edge by lowballing warranted risk. First run (TBC, 2026-06-30) demoted the desk's own EDGE tag to RISK_PREMIUM: the verified funding moat was DILIGENCE (re-reading audited disclosure ≠ edge), resilience was the compensated premium itself, and the lone candidate asymmetry failed as already-priced. Even a well-verified, cheap, high-ROE name is usually RISK_PREMIUM — memos drift toward over-claiming edge.

## short-court-doctrine
**Every short clears six court gates — crowding hard-limits, carry bar, dated falsifier, mechanical squeeze kill-bar, structure rules, and the absolute MEME EXCLUSION — before any valuation work begins.** Adopted 2026-07-29 (full text: desk/data/edge_classifications/CREDIT_SHORT_SLEEVE_20260729.json). (1) Crowding: SI > 10% of float, DTC > 7, or borrow > 3% → defined-risk mandatory; SI > 20% → drop unless the falsifier is < 30 days out. (2) Carry: borrow + dividend − rebate must be < the expected 6-month edge; > 6%/yr bleed requires a catalyst inside 2 quarters. (3) Dated falsifier: a frozen calibration call per short, same machinery as longs; one catalyst slip → exit review. (4) Squeeze kill bar, pre-committed: +15% intraday on no news, or SI +5pp into rising price → cover or roll to puts, no re-litigation; size so 2× adverse < 0.5% NAV. (5) Structure: premium-selling forbidden; puts preferred at low IV percentile; short P&L is ordinary income, so shorts are hedges only. (6) MEME EXCLUSION: never short attention-driven names — full stop, not even defined-risk; test discovery_state not attention-crowded, social attention below the meme threshold, no squeeze-flow fingerprint — because flow-driven pricing breaks the fundamental tether that makes a dated falsifier gradeable (the SLS meme-premium and RCAT-overturn lessons). Structure gates run first because they kill cheapest; each courted short also states its correlation to the tail it hedges.

## nci-is-not-the-sponsor-stake
**Never read a 10-Q's noncontrolling-interest percentage as the sponsor's economic stake in an Up-C: NCI counts only the exchangeable-unit slice, while the sponsor typically ALSO holds registered Class A directly — the real overhang is the beneficial-ownership total in the sponsor's own 13D/G, taken as-exchanged.** Ruled 2026-09-05 (LGN verification): the equity note showed NCI 28.8% post-secondary, but Blackstone's 13G/A (same event date) showed 46.9% — 31.3M exchangeable B units PLUS 19.3M directly-held Class A. In Up-C structures the overhang and the TRA are one joint risk: every sponsor exchange that shrinks NCI grows the tax-receivable liability, so recompute TRA-inclusive EV at each ownership event, never treat "NCI fell" as "overhang cleared."
