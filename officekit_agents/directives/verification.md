# Verification Doctrine — how claims become facts

Scope: the desk's standing rules for turning claims — from pitches, filings, feeds, screens, or our own screens' outputs — into facts fit to size against. Every rule below was earned on a named precedent; ruling dates are kept. This is plane 1 (shippable doctrine): no personal, account, or household content.

## two-mode-diligence

**Every diligence runs two passes: Mode A verifies the author's claims; Mode B derives the decisive questions from first principles, without the author's framing, and hunts disconfirming evidence — lead the report with Mode B.**
Why: Mode A alone lets the author set the agenda; the frame is built to keep you from asking the question that sinks the thesis. Validated 2026-06-05 on BioLargo (BLGO): Mode A on a 28-page bull memo said "high-risk, claims mostly verify"; Mode B independently flipped the verdict to AVOID (thrice-renamed shell origin, ~6 flagship stories in 19 years with zero conversions to scale, ~19 straight loss years, both operational pillars false on the segment filings). On an earlier private-deal DD the strongest findings (no parent Form D, no permits, no OSHA records under any name) were all Mode B — omissions of what should exist, not slips in what was said.
How to apply: Mode A = each load-bearing claim → independent authority → VERIFIED / CONFLICTED / MANAGEMENT-CLAIM / UNVERIFIABLE. Mode B = spawn the investigator WITHOUT the promoter's claims; question set: origin and lineage (shell history, renames, reverse splits); full-history track record and archetype base rate (sub-$1 perennial going-concern serial-pivots deliver the promised 10x ~1-3% of the time; 20 years of prior failure is negative evidence, not a neutral reset); the independent bear case; insider cash-vs-equity alignment; segment-footnote real unit economics; true cap-structure look-through; the one disconfirming fact the frame prevents you asking; and a fair steelman — clean integrity ≠ good investment, bad-but-disclosed ≠ fraud. Run both in parallel; Mode B findings are usually more diagnostic because they catch omissions. See: feedback_honesty_elevate_boundary.

## entity-resolution-first

**Resolve the queryable real-world entity before dispatching any verifier — the named subject of a deck, screen, or CUSIP is rarely the right query subject.**
Why: registries index different parties than pitches do. Permits are filed by GC and property owner, not tenant (querying the issuer name yields a false SEVERE absence-of-record on a factory that exists). Form D shows the SPV, not the lead investor. Founder names out-search company names for prior ventures and patents. Muni precedent (2026-06-11): a name-fuzzy CUSIP→issuer matcher misbound five issuers in one basket; two of five misbindings changed county/fault-zone/fire conclusions, and one nearly staged a $40k order — resolve identity from the EMMA security-page issue title (primary record); fuzzy match is a search hint, never an identity.
How to apply: insert an entity-resolution stage between claim derivation and verifier dispatch — identify the queryable entity (address, GC + landlord, SPV, founder), emit new claims with the resolved subject linked to the parent claim, verify against the resolved subject.
Corollary (2026-07-09, ELE/Tether): when one external entity dominates a name's tail (controlling shareholder, sole customer, parent, counterparty), that entity is the real DD subject — diligence its solvency and intent. Resolving Tether itself flipped ELE from forced-seller-trap to fair-carry (stake 31.9% and diluting, not 37.8%; structurally cash-generative). UNVERIFIABLE ≠ clean still binds: name what stays open.

## route-through-pipeline

**Every diligence verdict must go through the dispatch pipeline (coverage graph + recall-floor validation) — never ad hoc subagents plus hand synthesis (2026-06-07).**
Why: ad hoc DD silently skips the mandatory verifications the dispatch graph exists to guarantee. On Planet Labs (PL), run ad hoc, the government-contracts recall-floor detector never fired and the load-bearing "~82% government revenue" claim sat on a single earnings-call transcript through the entire thesis. The pipeline would have dispatched the budget-document and federal-procurement checks automatically and flagged the gap loudly.
How to apply: ad hoc exploration is fine to scope a name; the final verdict routes through the dispatcher so coverage is validated against the graph. After dispatch, run the output-side coverage validator and resolve every gap — "no source applied" is never accepted silently. Any number left at secondary/unverified is a coverage failure, not an acceptable state. Government-revenue claims need the procurement primary source (USASpending/SAM class), not a transcript. See: feedback_kg_dispatch_index_pattern, feedback_recall_floor_matcher_precision.

## cap-structure-in-primary-dd

**Before asserting net-cash-per-share, "trades below cash", EV, or a market-implied PoS, pull the full cap structure from the 10-Q/10-K equity notes and raise 8-Ks and compute the economic, as-converted, pre-funded-warrant-inclusive share count.**
Why: micro-cap clinical names raise via penny-strike pre-funded warrants and convertible preferred that the cover-page share count excludes. TENX (2026-06): cover count 9.31M (stale prior-period number) made $118.8M cash look 16% above the stock price (implied PoS ~6-13%); the point-in-time economic count was 48.84M — the corrected read was ~3-4.5x cash, ~30% implied PoS: fair, not cheap. The "sub-cash binary" was a stale-share-count artifact.
How to apply: (1) read Stockholders' Equity / Warrants / Preferred notes, not the cover; (2) read the raise 8-Ks for actual deal terms; (3) compute basic, fully-diluted, and pre-funded-inclusive counts, and recompute on the last; (4) use point-in-time rollforward tables, never the weighted-average EPS line (it lags); (5) per tranche, record strike vs price and any catalyst-linked expiry — catalyst-wired warrants cap win-state upside, so model the win on the fully-diluted count; (6) down-round ratchets and overhang are findings in themselves; (7) any "below cash" claim that hasn't passed this step is flagged, not stated.

## refresh-corpus-to-cutoff

**A live diligence must refresh the filings corpus to today before reading — cutoff-blinding and corpus freshness are independent, and a stale corpus re-flags resolved risks as active.**
Why: caught on Caris (CAI), 2026-05-29 — an IPO-frozen corpus produced a RED going-concern headline and a "near-term liquidity calendar" whose every date was already past. The refreshed corpus showed the going-concern language gone, the term loan repaid, cash $819.9M vs $31.2M, and the actual live risk elsewhere (governance + insider supply, including a founder pledge grown 1.66M→25M shares). Verdict flipped.
How to apply: pull the progressive corpus (10-K/10-Q/8-K/proxy through today) before any live read; for every solvency or date-stamped claim, authority = the latest filing, not the prospectus's forward language; emit a per-finding resolution trail (RAISED/UNCHANGED/WORSENED/RESOLVED); stamp report covers with "corpus through <date>" and alarm when the as-of date outruns the newest periodic filing. Backtests are exempt (deliberately frozen). Reference implementation: verticals/public_co/live_dd.py (prepare_corpus, freshness_check, build_live_prompt).

## chart-only-disclosure

**When a filing conveys a quantitative claim only through a chart or figure, the chart IS the disclosure — fetch the image and Read it multimodally; text-grep alone is insufficient.**
Why (2026-05-29, Parabilis): an agent flagged MEDIUM "denominator deflation" on a 14/19 = 74% ORR vs 14/38 = 37% ITT gap, claiming the split was unexplained — but the filing's waterfall and swim-plot figures directly answered it (the gap was entirely a too-recently-enrolled cohort, median treatment 5.0 vs 35.7 weeks; no dropouts). The rating should have been LOW; the disclosure lived in pixels the grep couldn't see. The pattern is universal across asset classes — subscriber pie charts, mineral-resource maps, roadmap figures, contract-mix donuts.
How to apply: every DD dispatch includes a figure-level reading discipline for any quantitative claim lacking a tabular equivalent. Reference implementation: verticals/buyside_dd/edgar_figure_extractor.py (nearest-image-to-caption heuristic, downsized PNG for multimodal Read; SEC fair-access User-Agent with a contact address required or EDGAR returns 403).

## multi-venue-consistency

**Track one fact pattern's representation across venues (transcript vs filed exhibit, poster vs registry, deck vs S-1) as its own backward-walking audit — a sibling of forward claim-evolution, not an extension of it — and it applies universally, not per-sector.**
Why: forward claim-lifecycle and cross-venue drift are different questions with different ingestion and time direction. Promoted universal 2026-08-11 after the FVRR miss: "$1k+ projects grew 34%/25% on a gross order amount basis" existed only in the call transcript while the vetted 6-K exhibit said client COUNT — a basis shift that survived both review benches because a sector gate never assigned the venue diff to a services name. When the vetted document chooses the weaker metric, the choice is itself information. Validated for the no-false-positive direction on Parabilis (2026-05-29): 24 registry versions over 3 years, all AFFIRMED — honest maturation doesn't fire it.
How to apply: every transcript-sourced load-bearing claim must state VENUE-MATCH or VENUE-DIVERGENT against the same-day primary-document excerpt; classify pairwise AFFIRMED/AMENDED/TERMINATED; verdicts: affirmed-across-venues / narrative-drift / termination-inconsistency (RED). Reference implementation: verticals/public_co/m_sources/multi_venue_disclosure_consistency.py, shared classifier disclosure_classifier.py, fetch via desk/sec_fetch.py.

## catalyst-dates-from-primary

**No hard date enters an overlay, pack, or ranking until verified from the statute, law-firm memo, or company filing — and until you've checked the market hasn't already run the experiment and the subject still has the exposure.**
Why (2026-07-05, LG Corp): a spurious "Sept-10 mandatory treasury cancellation" date propagated into three records and a top-5 ranking; the primary sources showed the law effective March-6-2026 (grace pivot Sep-6, 3%-rule Jul-23, pre-existing-treasury deadline Sep-2027), the company had already cancelled its entire treasury, and the re-rate had run AND round-tripped (105k→185,900→99k in 5 weeks). A wrong date manufactures a fake forward catalyst out of a spent one.
How to apply: (1) date from primary only; (2) pull daily bars around the real enactment/announcement — a run-and-round-trip means the catalyst is SPENT; (3) verify the subject still holds the exposure the law acts on. Addition 2026-08-02: news-feed RSS pubDate is a re-crawl timestamp, not a publication date — anchor every date to the publisher's page or a government dataset, never the feed. See: feedback_catalyst_identity_binding.

## tax-change-direction

**Before framing any tax-policy catalyst as bullish, name what the change does to the rate (up vs down), to whose line, and whether a court, sunset, or prior vote already settled it.**
Why (2026-07-20, Bancolombia/CIB): two frozen calls were VOIDED as mis-specified. The reform that was "dying" was a tax INCREASE — its death is overhang-removal, producing no EPS uplift, yet it had been inverted into a cut. The emergency 50% hike being priced as a live tail had already been struck by the Constitutional Court months earlier. And "pro-business admin will cut the bank tax" was an assumption — the candidate's named eliminations conspicuously omitted the bank surtax; the only real relief was a pre-scheduled, president-independent sunset first printing ~FY2028.
How to apply: pin the mechanism to primary sources (the statute, the court ruling, the filed tax note) — pinning the mechanism is what exposes that there is no mechanism. Force yourself to write the stock-reaction leg; if there is no discrete catalyst to key it to, that's the finding. A priced or nonexistent catalyst = FAIR/dead money, not edge. See: feedback_verify_catalyst_dates_primary, feedback_prob_weighted_fv_integration.

## tape-verify-premises

**Any premise of the form "moved X% on date D / on event Y" — and every macro, commodity, valuation-multiple, or insider-transaction premise — gets one cheap primary-source check at screen stage, before it seeds a thesis.**
Why: aggregator feeds fabricate dislocations. FCT.MI "-39.9% single-day" survived generation and trap-screen; the actual exchange tape showed +0.8% that day — a feed had mislabeled a 9-month bleed as one session. A fabricated dislocation is worse than no idea: it anchors the whole thesis shape.
How to apply, per premise class (each is one or two tool calls):
- Price action: pull the daily bar for date D; sanity-check against the 13/26-week range.
- Macro (2026-07-02): rate direction and FX level from the central-bank statement and live spot — two of six country screens ran with inverted load-bearing premises ("easing" bank actually hiking to 12%; "weak" currency at a 6-year high).
- Information edge (IVN, 2026-07-02): verify the MARKET lacks the information — read 2-3 post-event analyst notes before claiming "consensus hasn't repriced"; our staleness is not the market's.
- Valuation multiples (2026-07-05): timestamp every screen's price inputs; a multiple older than last quarter gets re-derived from the live tape (Greek banks quoted "1.0-1.4x TBV" on marks predating a +60% rally; current ~1.5x flipped the verdict).
- Commodity inputs (2026-07-06, MDLZ): "cocoa crashed 70%" was true at the Feb low and stale by July (it had doubled back) — verify spot + curve, dated.
- Insider transactions (2026-07-20, OMSE): "CEO bought ~10% more" was an aggregator's holdings-VALUE tile tracking the stock price; no purchase existed. Verify against the actual Form 4/13D-A or local equivalent; raise sizes from the 424B4, never a summary site.

## live-prices-before-valuation

**Fetch the live quote from the broker connector at the start of every valuation, implied-PoS, or IV turn — never reuse a spot quoted earlier in the session.**
Why (SLS, 2026-06-02): an entire valuation grid was anchored on a stale $9.07 when the live quote was $8.42 (−7.2% on the day) — misstating implied PoS and the overvaluation gap, and eroding trust in every other number.
How to apply: contract search → price snapshot (last, bid-ask, change, prior close) even if a price was "established" minutes ago; re-pull when a session spans more than a few minutes; quote the timestamp and day-change so staleness is visible. Read-only — no confirmation needed, so there is no excuse to skip it.

## unverified-is-work-queue

**Every "what remains unverified" section is a work queue for the connector stack, not a disclaimer — run each item down to a RANGE with primary sources (principal-directed 2026-08-21).**
Why: unverified sections are where fabricated precision breeds, and also where cheap resolution lives — a decisive share-count ambiguity (net cash 62% vs 84% of market cap, the difference between the thesis working and not) sat as a disclaimer for a day and then resolved in two fetches. A deck listing an unverified item it could resolve is hiding work, not disclosing risk.
How to apply: at adjudication, every unverified item gets a resolver path (which connector closes it), a status, and — once run — a range with sources; prefer two independent sources and state which end of the prior range survived. Items needing infrastructure are BLOCKED-with-named-unblock, never silently dropped. A resolved range can flip a disposition but never bypasses the gate taxonomy (resolution feeds the taxonomy; it does not authorize adds). Scope (2026-08-26): the run-down obligation binds only actionable files — held, staged, or gated-re-entry names; killed files carry moot unknowns that revive if the file reopens. Reference implementation: desk/data/unverified_ledger.json; standing doctrine in PIPELINE_PRD.md R1.13.

## estimate-the-unverifiable

**Genuinely unverifiable items (time-gated or private) get alt-data ESTIMATES with an explicit range, basis, and probability weight — never blanks — and every courted name carrying one gets a probability prize table plus an IV-vs-realized-vol read (ratified 2026-09-03).**
Why: blanks hide decision-relevant shape; a ranged, labeled estimate forces the sizing conversation and makes the eventually-filed number gradeable (Brier on our estimate). GIII precedent: an undisclosed royalty rate estimated from industry-standard 4-8% plus the counterparty's other public JV filings, while the drain instrument (the pending 8-K/A) stays registered.
How to apply: (1) label ESTIMATE, keep the drain instrument registered; (2) prize table = scenario × probability × price with an EV line, anchored per anchor-and-adjust doctrine; (3) the prize table is a discrete distribution and the option chain is the market's — compare before choosing the instrument: per-strike edge (Σ p·payoff vs ask), width (σ_table vs IV√T), VRP sign, and shape (dated forks are bimodal, so OTM options beat lognormal-at-same-σ); expression choice is the OUTPUT of this math; (4) chain-verified IV only — the underlying-level IV feed diverges wildly from the tradeable chain (TSSI: aggregate 70.9% vs chain midpoint ~101%; the per-strike test rejected the calls the screen-level ratio endorsed); (5) resolved items leave the section — the unverified section is a live TODO of open items only, with closed items integrated into a "what we checked ourselves" table; the ledger keeps the permanent record; (6) loss-branch tax-year landing filters the expiry — cross-year option expiries only when the convexity edge numerically dominates the tax cost.

## science-brief-first

**Deep-tech names (biotech, semis, nuclear, space, quantum, fusion, battery chemistry) run SCIENCE → red bench → blue bench → adjudication: a claim-tagged science brief is produced first and fed to both benches as evidence they must spot-check (ratified 2026-09-04, GPCR precedent).**
Why, in both directions: benches without the brief re-derived the biology wrong — the red bench built its efficacy kill on an estimand-mismatched comparison, and parallel verified reports contradicted each other on a fact a shared upstream brief would have settled. Meanwhile the science bench, unpressured, carried its own unchallenged error (a stale regulatory milestone). Sequencing fixes the first; adversarial spot-check duty fixes the second.
How to apply: the brief is evidence, not advocacy — closed claim tags (VERIFIED / COMPANY-ASSERTED / UNVERIFIABLE / REFUTED), one primary citation per row, corpus refreshed to today, a MEASUREMENT TRAPS section enumerating estimand/endpoint/unit mismatches before benches build kills on them, and a dated-catalyst verifiability check (a guided readout with no registry record = flag). Benches must re-verify at primary any brief claim a kill or overturn rests on; a verdict-language brief is rejected by the validator. Reference implementation: desk/agent_steps/templates/science_brief.md, SCIENCE stage in court_queue. The runner stage is the floor; big names may also get a session-level deep-diligence agent.

## human-observer-channel

**Principal field observations are a first-class evidence channel: the pre-mortems' tripwire-less unknowns form a standing walk-around index, pre-registered and disconfirmation-first (ratified 2026-08-23).**
Why: alt-data reaches satellites and filings but not lumberyard quotes, shelf share, credit chatter, or courtroom tone — several of which map 1:1 to live kill variables no feed can see. A person is the only sensor for exactly the unknowns the pre-mortems couldn't wire.
How to apply: (1) refresh the brief when positions change — each new position's pre-mortem contributes its tripwire-less unknown as a field item with a PRE-REGISTERED interpretation; (2) the brief lists what would HURT us — that is what to look for; (3) an n-of-1 observation moves timing/sizing through existing gates only; repeats or witnessed kill variables trigger courts, never orders; (4) absence is observation; (5) MNPI hygiene: public scuttlebutt only — volunteered material nonpublic information freezes the name and is never ledgered. Sizing amendment 2026-08-24: a pre-registered, disconfirmation-inclusive field pass that resolves a NAMED gate licenses exactly one sizing rung (structural caps survive; the field-sized cohort is graded at 90/180 days) — effort is not evidence weight; gate resolution is. Reference implementation: desk/data/human_observations.jsonl.

## truth-in-labeling

**Rigor cannot be uniform across verticals, so the labeling must be: a claim word may only be as strong as its evidence tier — demonstrated < discriminated < validated < predictive — and a linter enforces it.**
Why: a four-lens audit found two overclaims that were the same bug — a tier-4 word on tier-1 evidence ("empirically validated" at N=5 with no outcomes or control; "alpha is real" on a basket the backtest had already shown non-durable). Forcing held-out-cohort rigor onto a single-deal forensic DD is impossible; forcing honest grammar onto both is not.
How to apply: run the rigor linter before publishing any claim doc; a vertical's ceiling rises only when a genuinely higher-tier apparatus routes through its published numbers. For refactors near published numbers: verify baseline green → capture a byte-identical golden master of the deterministic artifact → move-then-redirect → re-assert byte-identical. Reference implementation: rigor/ledger.json (per-vertical earned ceilings), rigor/validate.py, rigor/vocabulary.json.

## connector-is-not-a-strategy

**A real connector is not a real strategy: disconfirm the strategy use of a new signal before deploying it, especially when the connector is genuine and the cross-section looks compelling.**
Why (2026-07-09, DOL H-1B/LCA): the labor-filings nowcast produced a clean cross-section, and the obvious thesis — long the US-labor expanders — was refuted on its own data: the biggest "expander" (+79% volume) was a flood of cheap cap-season entry-level petitions (median wage −14%, junior tier 27%→63%) at the company guiding the cohort's LOWEST growth; the biggest "contractor" (−49%, on n<70 noise) grew faster; and the whole cohort had already de-rated together, so no latency window existed. Volume conflates cap-season timing, onshore/offshore mix, and visa policy with demand.
How to apply: for every new signal, name what the metric actually measures and what it conflates; run the disconfirming screen on the strategy claim before any sizing; a signal that fails as alpha may still be real diligence (here: a margin/pyramid-utilization read on large filers, routed to earnings review — not a long/short demand discriminator). See: feedback_framework_value_is_diligence_not_only_alpha, feedback_detector_composition.

## attestation-is-not-audit

**An attestation, AUP, or ISAE-3000 assurance is not an audit — label such figures "attested, not audited," treat them as management-scoped and point-in-time, and never let one clear a load-bearing gate alone.**
Why: an attestation executes procedures management specifies at a single measurement date, with no opinion on fair presentation or liability completeness — the point-in-time snapshot is exactly the classic window-dressing hole. Origin (2026-07-09, ELE/Tether): an attested "$8.2B excess reserve buffer" was written with audit-grade confidence into a forced-seller refutation; the number is meaningful as a plausible solvency floor, but the caveat was load-bearing and missing.
How to apply: check the engagement class before crediting any "independently verified" reserve, NAV, or profit number; discount confidence accordingly; a perpetually-promised "full audit coming" that never arrives is itself a soft-spot signal. This is a direct instance of UNVERIFIABLE ≠ clean — name the assurance ceiling instead of laundering the number into a clean input. Generalizes to stablecoins, exchanges, private-credit marks, insurance reserves, proof-of-reserves pages.

## prints-read-by-intelligence

**A print or filing is GRADED by an LLM read of the primary source against the exact frozen gate — never parsed by regex; detection stays cheap, grading is intelligence plus a confirmation step (2026-07-09).**
Why: frozen gates are compound, judgment-laden document reads — "no new seismicity language," "Civil segment stabilizes," a five-clause bank kill stack — that cannot be grepped. And grading writes to the calibration ledger, the scoreboard that validates the whole system: a regex mis-grade silently corrupts the one record that keeps the desk honest. Origin: a comp print landed, the watcher caught the headline but couldn't parse the number, and a frozen p=0.78 call sat open ungraded; the reflex "harden the parser" was the wrong lesson.
How to apply: watchers/regex answer only "did a release drop?" (event-aware sampling near known print dates, with a loop-not-closed alarm when a catalyst date passes on an open call). On landing, an agent reads the PRIMARY source against the pre-registered gate, evaluates every clause, and returns a proposed grade with evidence; confirm before writing; decisive or close reads escalate to a stronger model — the generator never grades itself. If the estimated date passed but the print hasn't landed, re-date, don't grade. Reference implementation: desk/grade_brief.py, desk/calibration.py.

## report-format

**Every material finding in a reader-facing report is structured claim → method → authority → finding, in plain language — never the framework's internal notation — and every pitch opens with a "What this security is" block.**
Why: the four-part discipline is the load-bearing analytics (a report that shows its verification work is trusted; one that hand-waves is discounted — 2026-05-29), but the letter-labels are jargon (2026-06-07: strip "R/f(M)" from all reader-facing material; the structure stays invisible-but-present). The opening block (2026-07-15) exists because the reader shouldn't need prior context to know what the ticker even is, and for odd instruments (prefs, foreign lines, options structures) the instrument description IS risk information.
How to apply: per finding — verbatim claim with filing-section citation; the specific verification method run; the specific external authority URL/database; a verdict WITH evidence (VERIFIED / REFUTED / UNVERIFIABLE / CONSISTENT / AFFIRMED-ACROSS-VENUES). Citations are URLs and section references, never internal detector names or version-counter language. Opening block per security: issuer + instrument type + exchange, what the business concretely does (who pays them, for what), scale, and the structural facts a holder must know (control/float, leverage, line liquidity — ADV + spread if thin). Amendment 2026-07-29: every underwrite doc carries an edge-explainer section — market believes / we believe / who's mispricing and why / why it may be priced in / mechanism falsifier / edge type; for fairly-priced names, "nobody is grossly mispricing" is an acceptable and often correct answer.

## thesis-pipeline

**Every thesis follows the canonical 11-stage pipeline end-to-end; deviations are decisions, not accidents; the invariant is that the generator never grades itself (v1.0, approved 2026-07-02).**
Stages: 0 generation (slates, factor-overlap check) → 1 trap-screen → 2 two-mode deep DD (double-pass on big positions) → 3 catalyst prediction (rubric written in advance) → 4 red team (independent skeptic, adjudicated — a healthy thesis SHRINKS) → 5 honest classification (edge explainer + mechanism falsifier; no-edge is acceptable if fairly paid) → 6 pre-registration (frozen probabilities, later Brier-scored) → 7 sizing (ladder gated on calibration band, factor-level sleeve caps, binaries unlevered, tax at exit only) → 8 propagation (closing checklist + consistency check) → 9 execution (stage → principal submits; re-poll before any status claim) → 10 resolution and learning (rubric-graded; failures become memories; fixes get codified).
Principal checkpoints: order submission, sizing-doctrine changes, resolution disputes — everything else runs and reports. Meta: exclusions are the product; a shrinking thesis is the process working; nothing is done until visible in the app. Open item: stage 10.5 winner-exit discipline (thesis-complete condition defined at entry) — undrafted (dated — re-verify before relying). Canonical text: THESIS_PIPELINE.md at repo root.

## ownership-filings-entity-resolution
**Resolve the FILER before citing any 13D/G: multiple unrelated institutions file on the same issuer in the same window, and a passive manager's 13G (BlackRock, Vanguard, State Street) is not evidence about the SPONSOR's stake.** Ruled 2026-09-05 (LGN verification): a bench cited the 2026-07-29 13G as the sponsor's position — it was BlackRock's passive filing; the sponsor group's own 13G/A landed 2026-08-07 and told the opposite story (stake unchanged at 46.9%). Apply entity-resolution-first to ownership filings exactly as to permits and Form Ds: pull the filer names from the primary doc XML, map them to the entity in the thesis (sponsor aggregators and parent LLCs, not name-alike asset managers), and only then read the numbers.
