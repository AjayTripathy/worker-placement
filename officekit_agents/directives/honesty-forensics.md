# Honesty Forensics — the lying-detector doctrine

Scope: distilled case law and methodology for the desk's honesty/claim-verification engine — what it detects, what it must not detect, how detectors compose, and the binding-integrity guards that keep false EXCLUDEs out of the verdict path. All rulings carry their original dates; personal/household context stripped by design. This is plane-1 shippable doctrine; verify code citations against current source before asserting behavior.

## negative-selection-filter

**The framework is a lying detector, not a stock picker: alpha comes from EXCLUDING names where disclosure diverges from verifiable reality, never from re-detecting honestly disclosed bad news.** Ruled 2026-05-24 during the small-cap pilot: SRPT scored composite 0.125 (LONG-tier) despite three patient deaths, an FDA black-box warning, indication removal, EU rejection, and the ESSENCE trial failure — and that output is CORRECT, because SRPT wasn't lying; the catastrophe was public and priced. Modes the framework CAN catch: (1) disclosed-but-misleading claims (INSP: 10-K said code transitions to 64582; Q1 10-Q reveals AHA recommended 64999); (2) disclosed-but-hidden distress (SHAK: "Fifth Amendment AND Waiver" in the exhibit index with no MD&A narrative); (3) disclosed-but-not-framed-as-distress (FMC: 5 covenant amendments plus $750M 8.45% subordinated notes described as routine); (4) disclosed-but-overstated growth (UPWK: CEO 10b5-1 rotation pre-committing more selling while marketing AI growth); (5) undisclosed claims (cohort-attributed programs absent from filings — NOT_IN_FILING). The forbidden mode: scoring disclosed-distress severity — that converts the engine into an edge-less fundamental analyst. Apply as a negative-selection filter (exclude composite above ~0.30–0.50 by threshold); factor screens do selection afterward and their contribution must be tracked separately from honesty alpha.

Tiered sharpening: Tier 2 (2026-05-25) — the filter is STRESS-CONDITIONAL: +57.6 pp basket lift on a curated distressed cohort (66 small-caps with prior catastrophes) collapsed to −0.7 pp on a representative SP600 boring-middle cohort; Spearman fell from −0.599 to −0.053. Always quote +57 pp with the qualifier "on a curated distressed cohort." Tier 3 (2026-05-25) — final positioning: a CATASTROPHE-WARNING SYSTEM, not a basket-alpha generator. Walk-forward (2024-05-15 → 2025-05-15, point-in-time universe) held catastrophe recall at 85% (vs 93% in-cohort) but basket alpha fell to −8.6 pp, 29th percentile of random draws; clean-disclosure failures (XNCR −61.5%, ROG −42.4%) are invisible to honesty signals by construction. Use it to flag likely catastrophes within distressed cohorts; do not build long baskets from it.

## elevate-boundary

**The ELEVATE line is MARKETED-TAKEAWAY-DIVERGENCE — the headline claim materially diverges from what the issuer's own data can support — not datum-disclosure.** Ruled 2026-06-04 after running a 23-name catalyst cohort primed and then fully de-biased, exposing two opposite failure modes that must be corrected together. Bias 1, priming inflates ELEVATEs: pre-stating the suspected tell makes the screen confirm it (IMCR: the trial's own title says "non-ocular" and excludes uveal; the primed pass never weighted this, the de-biased pass flipped ELEVATE→CLEAN; 5 of 7 primed ELEVATEs were inflation). Bias 2, "disclosed = clean" defangs the screen: a datum buried in a safety table cannot exonerate a contradicting headline, or nothing can ever ELEVATE (SLS: "Positive Outcome of Interim Analysis" on a blinded, both-arm pooled number that cannot demonstrate a drug effect; ABVX: "no new safety signals over 44 weeks" over a dose-dependent 7-vs-1 malignancy cluster in the same release — market confirmed at ~−40% for ABVX). The tiers: ELEVATE = takeaway a reader would follow to a conclusion the data contradicts; REVIEW = real risk disclosed and framed proportionately (SMMT foregrounds PFS but discloses the OS-miss/China comparator; ANNX endpoint-switch disclosed and FDA/EMA-aligned; HRMY instrument-switch disclosed and EoP2-aligned); CLEAN = framing matches data (IMCR scoped title; AMLX Ph3 primary = the Ph2 winning endpoint). Process: run de-biased — bare catalyst only, no hypothesis, quote the issuer's own scope FIRST, steelman both masking and honest cases — AND adjudicate on divergence. De-biasing alone also caught two over-marketed CLEANs (BHVN omitting its own psychiatry Ph2/3 failures; IOVA "best-in-class" on single-arm n=39). If the adjudicating analyst holds a position in a flagged name, disclose the conflict and have the divergence test re-applied independently rather than trusting the call. See: escalate-intelligence-on-decisive-findings.

## anti-masking-findings

**The ABSENCE of a predicted masking mechanism is itself a positive microstructure finding — encode it in the catalog; never kill the sector for a falsified prediction.** Ruled 2026-05-28 on two muni findings the hostile-validator caught: (1) CA county Teeter Plans EXPLICITLY CARVE OUT Mello-Roos special taxes in every county checked — so CFD delinquency signals propagate unmasked (encoded `teeter_carveout_mello_roos`, VERIFIED ANTI-MASKING); (2) the CSCDA CIA / CalCHA / CMFA SFA-I workforce-housing conduit subsector ($8–10B aggregate, 2019–2022 vintage, 45+ deals, 7+ defaults) operates COMPLETELY UNWRAPPED — spreads +70 to +475 bps because operator credit is directly exposed; framework alpha is maximal exactly where no wrap intercedes (encoded `essential_workforce_housing_unwrapped_jpa`) (dated — re-verify before relying). A third adjacent pattern: partial masking is also catalog-worthy — FHA 242 was predicted as a Cal-Mortgage analog but is DOCUMENTED PARTIAL; program tail risk and hospital-sector premium leak through the wrap. How to apply: make "does the predicted mechanism actually apply, or is it carved out / weaker than expected?" a load-bearing test in every sector scoping; catalog falsifications with severity "NONE (anti-masking)" or "MEDIUM (partial)" plus the structural reason; treat unwrapped/carved-out zones as the strongest alpha candidates. Reference implementation: knowledge_graph/masking_mechanisms.json.

## detector-composition

**Detectors are narrow high-precision exclusion filters composed via UNION — the portfolio is the universe MINUS everything any detector caught; never average signals into a composite score.** Established ~2026-05 across muni and small-cap equities. The dispositive muni chain: a 4-axis composite credit score claimed +97% in-sample hit rate but showed −11 pp lift on verified outcomes; a covenant tripwire went +11 pp in-sample → 0 pp same-universe OOS → −33 pp on a blind 33-name 2016-tier universe; the M&A leverage detector (+5 pp in-sample → +6 pp blind OOS, 100% precision, 20% recall, firing on 6% of the universe) was the ONLY signal to survive a blind test. The generalizing shape: detectors firing on 2–15% of the universe at >70% precision hold up; anything firing on >30% of names or below 50% precision is a comprehensive score in disguise — refuse to ship it. Rules: (1) design for high precision, low recall; (2) compose via UNION, never WEIGHTED_AVERAGE; (3) evaluate in the exclusion frame ("does universe-minus-exclusions beat the unscreened universe?"), not per-name prediction; (4) when a screen has gaps, add orthogonal narrow detectors rather than making one detector smarter (that path is overfit); (5) blind-OOS validation before shipping — in-sample lift is suspect by default.

## secondary-endpoint-review-flag

**Marketing that accurately labels a pre-specified, alpha-allocated key SECONDARY endpoint as "secondary" is honest hierarchical disclosure, not slot-substitution — route it to review_flags with verdict-neutral enrichment, never to ELEVATE.** Ruled 2026-06-02 on RARE/GTX-102 (Aspire Ph3, Angelman): the adjudicator read a Jan-2025 8-K quote — "the key secondary endpoint (with a 10% allocation of alpha) will be the MDRI" — as promoting a secondary AS primary and over-elevated CLEAN→ELEVATED, dropping the word "secondary." The MDRI is the registered 10%-alpha key secondary; the primary (Bayley-4 cognition) was correctly labeled; the deterministic gate said CLEAN throughout. Slot-substitution requires the secondary presented AS the primary. But do not suppress entirely: emphasizing a more-attainable composite-responder secondary is a soft tell that the hard primary is a high bar — real microstructure signal. So reclassify out of the verdict-driver path into `review_flags`, AND give the flag a verdict-neutral enrichment pass (a stronger-model read of the PoS implication, routed to the analyst note / microstructure KG, never to the EXCLUDE call) — otherwise "flag for review" is inert. On GTX-102 the enrichment yielded a usable term: 10%-alpha-to-MDRI implies below-median primary confidence, ~5–10 pp lower PoS. Reference implementation: `adjudicate.py` (`_is_secondary_emphasis_flag`, `_enrich_flag`).

## per-claim-trial-attribution

**Claim→trial attribution must be PER-CLAIM, not per-case: a program-aggregate count scored against one trial's registry manufactures a false EXCLUDE — and the error is asymmetric, so scrutinize exclusions and trust cleans.** Canonical failure (CLDX, 2026-06-01): marketing said "1,939 patients enrolled… the largest program" — combined enrollment across both Phase 3 trials (EMBARQ-CSU1 + EMBARQ-CSU2). The extractor bound it to one NCT (NCT06455202, registered 976), so evaluation refuted it as "~2× inflation" — a pure category error (1,939 ÷ 2 ≈ 976). Rated moderate, below the severe+ escalation gate, it was never re-verified and became the sole EXCLUDE driver. The asymmetry: cross-trial/aggregate conflation only ever fabricates false REFUTEs (→ EXCLUDE/ELEVATED), essentially never false CLEANs — so CLEAN verdicts are robust to this bug class and audit effort belongs on the exclusions. Also: "passed the guard" ≠ "verified correct" — moderate/unverifiable drivers below the escalation gate are merely "not-blocked." How to apply: whenever an EXCLUDE/ELEVATED rests on a COUNT (enrollment, sites, countries), check single-trial vs program total first; tells are "program," "across both trials," "combined," "largest program" near a patient count. Reference implementation: `trial_scope` + `nct_refs` tagging in extract_claims; `_neutralize_aggregate_conflations` in evaluate; `M_AGGREGATE_MISMATCH` escalation in verify_finding; REBIND_REQUIRED short-circuit to BLOCKED in adjudicate.

## extraction-artifact-nct-guard

**An NCT the extractor stamped onto a claim, when that NCT never appears in the company's own source text, is an extraction artifact, not a company claim — drop any REFUTE built on it before reasoning.** Ruled 2026-06-01 on ADCT/LOTIS-5 (live forward test): the extractor bound NCT05635266 — a 20,000-patient observational biospecimen registry — onto a Q2-2025 8-K that names no NCT and plainly reports real LOTIS-7 Phase 1b data (ORR 93.3% / CR 86.7%, verbatim in the PR header). Venue-consistency then "refuted" a fabricated trial design and the adjudicator escalated to company-level EXCLUDE under honesty-transfers. The company fabricated nothing; the deterministic gate said CLEAN throughout; the corrected call was CLEAN → HIT-consistent. The guard: a REFUTED finding is dropped when its subject matches an NCT pattern, is not a catalyst NCT, AND the literal NCT string is absent from the source document — which preserves genuine different-trial findings (where the company itself named the trial, honesty-transfer still fires) while killing extractor-invented bindings. General rule: before any NCT-keyed mismatch drives an exclusion, confirm the company actually wrote that NCT. Reference implementation: `adjudicate.py` (`_is_artifact_binding`, `_findings_for_prompt`).

## catalyst-identity-binding

**Bind every catalyst case to the SPECIFIC trial the price-moving press release reports — verified against that release — never to the program's best-known trial name.** Ruled ~2026-06 on AVIR/Atea: the case was wired to NCT04889040 (MORNINGSKY, Phase 3) because that was the flagship name recalled for the Oct-2021 COVID-antiviral failure — but the readout that actually moved the stock ~−70% on 2021-10-19 was Phase 2 MOONSONG (NCT04709835), whose primary was change-from-baseline viral load; MORNINGSKY was terminated only afterward, and the recorded price reaction (−19%) was also wrong. The verifier then checked claims against the wrong registry baseline, and — the quiet trap — a successful fetch of the wrong subject is indistinguishable from a correct one, so nothing errored; it even produced a "lucky correct" decisive refute keyed to a trial that never read out. Procedure before wiring any case: (1) find the actual price-moving press release for the readout date; (2) confirm WHICH trial it reports (name + phase + NCT + primary endpoint); (3) confirm the registry primary matches that trial; (4) only then set nct_id/cutoff. When a program has Phase 2 and Phase 3 trials in the same indication, assume confusion risk is high. A valid NCT that resolves is NOT confirmation it is the right one. Sibling of per-claim-trial-attribution (this is the case-level binding; that is the per-claim analogue).

## recall-floor-matcher-precision

**Recall-floor / APPLIES_TO matchers must gate on the claim's canonical verification attribute + referent_type — never on free-text tokens in subject or quote.** Ruled ~2026-06 on a buyside run (AHC): an OSHA recall floor keyed on "factory"/"facility" appearing anywhere in subject/quote fired on 46/262 claims — including a facility's `bedrooms=250`, a `current_margin=0.95`, and named PEOPLE — and on one claim made OSHA the only mandated source, which is nonsensical. Near-zero precision. Regated on attribute ∈ {industrial_facility_presence, building_permits, located_at_address, …} AND referent_type NOT in {person, amount, time_period, quantity, intangible}, or an existence/location predicate: fired on exactly 2/262 — the real facility-existence claims. Why tokens fail: company-description boilerplate ("manufacturing," "production") bleeds into unrelated claims, making substring matching a false-positive generator. Over-dispatch is "safe" in the sense that a no-hit isn't treated as clean, but it pollutes output and wastes verification calls — precision still matters. Apply to every dispatch-index recall floor (muni dispatch, public-company, buyside ring-dispatch): assert applicability off the same KEYS the dispatcher itself uses. See: kg-dispatch-index-pattern, detector-composition.

## microstructure-knowledge-is-the-product

**The engine exists to encode and surface microstructure knowledge — masking mechanisms, signal-channel topology, signal-to-price latency — and alpha is a byproduct; the mechanism catalog is the deliverable.** Ratified 2026-05-28 after a session of treating CA-muni scopings as pass/fail alpha hunts (with three kill recommendations on the CFD vertical alone). The reframe: every finding treated as "negative" — Cal-Mortgage masking compresses nursing-home alpha to 30–60 bps; Teeter Plans exclude Mello-Roos so CFD signals propagate fast; the CSFA LCFF intercept partially masks operator credit; CFD signals ride 3+ redundant public channels (CDIAC YFSR, Govt Code 53356.1 foreclosure filings, Govt Code 53359.5 continuing disclosure) making them un-arbitrageable — is exactly what the product is supposed to produce. A detector that fires with no price reaction confirms the channel is efficient; one that fires before price moves confirms timing alpha; both outcomes encode knowledge. How to apply: lead every scoping with "what mechanism does this asset class have?" not "is there alpha here?"; explicitly catalog wraps, intercepts, guarantees, Teeter-equivalents, statutory carve-outs, channel cadence/lag/redundancy; write findings into the knowledge-graph JSON catalogs and re-run the build script rather than producing standalone memos. Reference implementation: knowledge_graph/ — masking_mechanisms.json (21 mechanisms, 8 categories), signal_channels.json (73 channels), knowledge_graph.json, build_knowledge_graph.py (counts dated — re-verify before relying). A portable hypothesis-test-falsify loop exists: scope universe → propose detectors → cross-section test → timing test → upstream-data test → blind OOS → encode findings. Anti-pattern to self-monitor: framing verticals so negative results feel like failure instead of findings.

## value-is-diligence-not-only-alpha

**The alpha test is not the only test: a vertical that fails timing-alpha but correctly classifies distress still delivers the diligence institutional managers charge 75–150 bps/yr for — never recommend kill on alpha-failure alone.** Ruled 2026-05-28: the CA land-secured / Mello-Roos CFD vertical failed three sequential alpha tests — (1) cross-section EMMA: Northstar trading at 18c, distress fully priced; (2) CDIAC timing: framework lags or is coincident with market; (3) upstream assessor data: 1–3 month lead vs the 6–12 needed. A kill was recommended after each negative, and each was wrong: the framework correctly classified every distressed name (Diablo Grande, Northstar, Calexico, Truckee Donner), and that classification captures the management-fee differential for a self-managed book even when the market has already priced it. How to apply: on any alpha-failure, first ask "does it still classify the underlying risk correctly?" — if yes, reframe as "narrower product than hoped" and continue when marginal build cost is small; build the data sources an institutional active manager would use even when coincident with market, because replicating that research IS the product. The correct kill criterion is "the data doesn't classify the risk correctly," never "the market also reads the data." Watch the cumulative-failure trap: N consecutive negatives prove nothing about the vertical if all N were alpha-tests and diligence value is intact. See: microstructure-knowledge-is-the-product, detector-composition.
