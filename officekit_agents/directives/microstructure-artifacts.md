# Market Microstructure — artifacts, flows, and conditioning

Doctrine plane 1: shippable rules for reading price itself — when a quoted "edge," mark, move, or fill is real versus an artifact of how the number was produced, who must trade next, and how discovered the thesis already is. Distilled from graded desk case law (each rule carries its ruling date and the tickers that paid for it). All rules assume live-verified prices; data-availability specifics are perishable.

## mispricing-artifact-taxonomy

**RULE: edge = our_p − market_p is only real when market_p is a genuine same-event, same-direction EXTERNAL anchor (options-implied probability on a clean binary, or a direct prediction-market quote on the exact event); everything else is an artifact and must never be ranked or traded as a mispricing.** (Crystallized 2026-07-14 after LDOS/BAH/HCA + Hyundai-pref 005387 + INTU divergences all proved fake.)
Three recurring artifact classes:
1. **Valuation-band (circular):** inverting your own bear/base/bull FVs through the live price to get an "implied p" reads your own band, not the market — it drifts with price alone (Hyundai-pref: 0.46 at ₩223.5k → 0.00 at ₩199.7k, +16pp of fake "edge" from price-location; LDOS's 0.25 was the same). Tag such output `consistency_gauge_not_market` and exclude it from any edge sort (reference implementation: catalyst_mispricing.py).
2. **Independence-multiplied:** a compound call P(beat) × P(guide-disappoints) priced as independent legs when they are positively correlated (INTU: the promo/mix beat is the same fact that pressures the guide) fabricates a low "market_p" (~0.32) no venue quotes.
3. **Related/wrong-event proxy:** a different-event prediction market mapped onto the call (BAH: shutdown-by-Jan-31 combo instead of the direct Oct-1 market; HCA: an ACA-PTC market instead of the CMS-2449 SDP rule) is not the call's price.
Corollaries: fairly-paid-risk ownership needs no mispricing at all (Hyundai-pref was a genuine carry starter with zero measurable edge); and a refuted edge on a cheap name can still be a fundamental de-rate question routed to deep value — a quarters-scale value call, not a tradeable catalyst.

## policy-channel-vs-anchor

**RULE: a policy market can be the WRONG ANCHOR for a specific frozen call yet a REAL FUNDAMENTAL DRIVER of the name's P&L — killing the anchor must not blind you to the driver.** (Costly miss, 2026-07-14.)
HCA cut FY2026 EPS guidance ($29.10–31.50 → $28.70–30.50), stock −7%, on higher uninsured share + ACA-exchange payer-mix shifts — revenue actually beat; a margin/mix hit. The frozen 0.75 "base-or-better" call resolved unfavorable → Brier 0.562. The day before, the ACA-PTC market had been correctly dismissed as the wrong anchor (≠ CMS-2449) but wrongly dismissed as a driver: the enhanced-APTC subsidy-expiry channel (more uninsured → worse hospital payer mix → margin cut) is exactly what cut the guide.
Apply: (1) whenever a policy market is flagged wrong-anchor, separately ask "does this policy move the NAME's P&L?" — if yes, carry it as a standing fundamental channel. (2) ACA-PTC / exchange dynamics are a load-bearing driver for the whole hospital sector (HCA/THC/UHS/CYH); the enhanced-APTC expiry fight was the swing variable (dated — re-verify before relying). (3) Guidance cuts pre-announce ~a week early — HCA cut Jul-14 ahead of a Jul-21 print; never anchor a resolution watch on the scheduled print date.

## anchor-and-adjust

**RULE: in prediction classes with no measured skill, the external anchor IS the default probability; deviating more than ±0.05 requires a named mechanism — a specific in-house data channel that knows something the crowd doesn't.** (Wired and enforced 2026-07-27; reference implementation: desk/freeze_call.py, desk/implied_binary.py.)
The first partitioned Brier book (n=31) split by skill class: ops_data 0.126 / 89% hit (connector-fed nowcasts — the franchise) vs reaction 0.382 and narrative 0.256 (worse than coin). Every weak-class failure was an unanchored conviction number: HCA-STK froze 0.75 on a gut price view; FR.PA froze 0.65 on "intake +20% continues" against a +38%/+47% base; a coalition call free-handed 0.42 where a venue market existed.
Apply: measured-skill classes freeze at conviction (the nowcast is the anchor). No-skill classes (stock_reaction, political_process) default to options-implied digital or the Polymarket/Kalshi price, with provenance stamped at freeze. Reaction freezes require a `reaction_driver` (what the stock will trade on — never "the quarter") plus context (implied move, run-up, short float). Strong narrative freezes (≥0.60/≤0.40) require one documented disconfirming primary check; %-continuation reads require a base comp, with a flag when the prior base is ≥1.5× the claim (FR.PA tripped at 1.9×). Sizing-on-edge is gated on Brier(ours) < Brier(market) over n≥20 GENUINE anchors only — scenario-derived market_p is a consistency gauge and excluded.

## touch-vs-terminal-units

**RULE: P(touches level BY date) and P(finishes there AT expiry) are different units — European options pay TERMINAL, and touch ≈ 2× terminal at ~1.4y tenor (reflection principle), so convert both sides to the same basis before computing any option edge.** (Backspread court, 2026-08-02.)
The house compared its 33% "P(SPX −20% by end-2027)" against ~21% option-implied as if same units; both were touch-basis but XSP puts pay terminal. Payable edge was 15.8% vs 12.3% terminal = 1.29×, not 33/21 = 1.57× — the trade may survive, but sizing and the grading bar were wrong by roughly the touch/terminal multiple.
Sibling unit lessons from the same court: far-OTM price ratios at matched %-moneyness are vol-convexity artifacts (compare in IV space, beta-match the moneyness); QQQ crash-beta is regime-conditional (<1.0 in macro shocks, 1.5–1.7 when tech is the epicenter). See: price-unit-resolution.

## beta-bleed-is-not-dislocation

**RULE: a name falling with its whole tape is beta-bleed, not a mispriced dislocation — "no name-specific cause found" by a shallow search authorizes nothing, and no live order stages off triage without a full red/blue court.** (2026-07-20/21, KOSPI displacement.)
Cosmecca (241710): two lightweight agent passes said "verified dislocation, UNDISCOVERED, buy 0.62"; the full court reversed (red 8/10 CANCEL) — the −12.6% was a market-wide KOSDAQ AI/semi crash (−5.33% that day), and a real DD found 10–11 analysts at Strong Buy where a degraded discovery check had returned "undiscovered" (positioning sub-signals unavailable default toward 0 — the wrong direction; verify analyst coverage directly). Recurred with CRH 2026-07-21 (band-touch staged off a driver check; court killed it unanimously and corrected the driver check itself). 2-for-2 that week: every order staged off triage was reversed or resized. Protocol: touch → driver check → COURT → stage.
Further rulings folded in: (a) a "beat-lean" on a number the sell-side already prints as consensus has no edge — into crowded Strong-Buy positioning an in-line print is a sell event (see: trim-priced-in-winners). (b) Batch-layer catalyst claims require primary citations — six-for-six corrected or refuted at Stage-2 in one week (2026-07-21), worst case a fabricated court-docket number; any docket gets one direct portal check before it seeds anything. (c) A preferred-share discount move is beta-mechanical, not a catalyst: Hyundai 2우B's discount widens when the common melts up and compresses when it de-rates (2026 path 28.5%→62.9%→52.1% was beta arithmetic, and 52% sat at the multi-year mean ~51% — normal, not cheap). Always locate a discount in its multi-year range and decompose widen/compress into common-beta before calling it cheap.

## forced-seller-adverse-selection

**RULE: in any "buy the forced seller" thesis, identify WHO sells — if it's the informed fulcrum creditor (post-reorg §1145 holder who set plan value), the overhang is informed distribution and the thesis INVERTS; only mechanical sellers into a dated cliff are exploitable.** (Established 2026-07-13; post-reorg orphan-equity thesis went 2/2 AVOID — WOLF, OPI.)
The fulcrum creditor is the best-informed holder in the room; they sell §1145-free float precisely when the equity has over-recovered relative to a business the reorg did NOT fix. WOLF: creditors registered 24M shares at $35–48 ≈ 4× plan basis, into a 54%-short squeeze, with SiC revenue −19% YoY / GM −27% and re-levering at 13.875% PIK. OPI: two holders (25.3% + 19.7%) dumping into a still-impaired office book (occupancy 90.6→78.2%, 1.5× coverage, a 2027 maturity wall). A correctly-priced impaired business that discloses its impairment is clean, not a catch.
The 5 filters (run only the narrowed version): (1) still UNDISCOVERED — reject CROWDED (WOLF at 54% SI / +98% YTD is the textbook violation); (2) business not secularly impaired or still deteriorating; (3) DE-levering post-emergence, not re-levering; (4) seller identity MECHANICAL (index-deletion / ratings-forced / mandate / 40-Act) — a resale block priced far above plan basis is informed exit, auto-reject; (5) a DATED supply cliff still AHEAD — buy into a known unlock, never the aftermath. Generalizes to every forced-seller flavor (fallen-angel, lockup, index rebalance).

## value-ladder-flow-gate

**RULE: being right on fair value is not a timing signal — when your own court finds the de-rate rational and ONGOING, never bid into the predictable flow you just front-ran; every value ladder on a deteriorating name needs an exhaustion/flow gate.** (The OLLI lesson, 2026-07-08.)
The court beat the Street analytically (caught the sales cut pre-downgrade-cycle, FV $84) — then bid 71/66/61 anyway. The forecastable consequence of the court's own finding was the downgrade cycle (UBS 125→87, plus three more) and continued de-rating; both rungs filled into it (−16% in 4 sessions), with the Street's cuts landing AFTER the fills.
Apply: (1) ask "WHO SELLS NEXT?" in court — if nameable (stale targets, un-run downgrade cycle, guide-cut latency, tax-loss season), the ladder waits or sits below the expected-flow zone, not merely below spot. (2) Entry trigger = exhaustion evidence, not price: downgrade targets clustered near the tape, post-flush volume decay, or the calendar window the court itself named. (3) Run the conditioning layer on the BEAR thesis: bid only when the bear case is CROWDED (fully discovered), never while it is DISCOVERING. (4) Keep small-starter sizing, but the fix is the gate, not smaller ladders. (5) Cross-name gates need a MECHANISM, not a sector label — a gate must name the shared channel (customer, supplier, input cost, regulator); shared GICS letters are not a channel.

## vendor-price-artifact-family

**RULE: any single price that drives a conclusion is a premise and gets tape-verified with one bar check — vendor price series lie in at least four cataloged ways.** (Family cataloged as of 2026-08-26.)
1. **PTS/after-hours marks (JP):** IBKR marks Japanese positions off thin JPNNEXT after-hours prints — 7235.T showed −11.7% (¥1,400) on zero news when the Tokyo close was ¥1,585, +0.25%; one thin 20:31 JST print became the mark (2026-08-24). Thin names (~¥20M/day ADV) can be "moved" ±10% by a few hundred after-hours shares. Check kabutan (kabutan.jp/stock/?code=XXXX — shows exchange close AND PTS separately) before reacting; never grade a kill trigger or alert off a PTS-only print.
2. **Intraday-vs-close marks:** a vendor "−10.85%" was −7.0% close/close (SHAZ, ruling 2026-08-26).
3. **Unadjusted reverse splits:** yfinance recorded BYND's 1-for-30 reverse split in its splits table but never adjusted pre-split closes — the series showed +2,538% when the true split-adjusted return was −12%; this nearly flipped a whole cohort study's conclusion. Caught by smell when it should have been caught by rule.
4. **Vendor technical tables:** BBAR's vendor MA50 read 14.646 vs 18.56 bar-computed; a bench FATAL rested on it. Cross-vendor agreement is NOT verification (same upstream pipeline) — MA/drawdown claims must be BAR-COMPUTED. Also logged: an IBKR prior-close field printing 9.71 vs a real ~5.41 close (GRML).
Apply before any return >|100%| or >5× single-bar move enters an analysis: (a) check the splits table and re-base through the split factor; (b) verify close-vs-close against the exchange print; (c) for JP names use kabutan. Never let an outlier carry a conclusion unverified.

## price-unit-resolution

**RULE: resolve a price unit (agorot/pence/øre vs major currency) ONLY from broker contract metadata — priceMagnifier > 1 means the data feed is in the minor unit and orders/display divide by the magnifier; unit convention NEVER transfers across venues or even across API planes of the same broker.** (POLI.TA false-strike-on-false-strike, 2026-07-06.)
POLI.TA ran different units on different planes simultaneously: raw feed + level-1 snapshot in agorot (with the "bid/ask" actually the ±35% TASE price bands, not a book) while history bars + portal + order entry were in shekels. Two sessions each verified one plane and reached opposite conclusions; the second deleted the first's correct orders citing a vendor's convention. priceMagnifier (=100 for POLI) from contract details is the definitive answer — no convention argument needed.
Apply: (1) pull priceMagnifier + minTick before staging any non-USD-major-venue limit; (2) cross-check by numeric resolve against a display-plane bar; (3) respect the failure asymmetry — when in doubt, stage the direction that CANNOT fill if wrong (a limit far below an over-scaled book), never the one that fills instantly; (4) the human submission screen (limit vs live quote side-by-side) is the final gate; (5) deleting another lane's staged order requires a broker datum, not a convention argument. 2026-08-05 addendum (FDM): even within one broker, the orders API stored LSE limits in GBP while the instruction rail passed raw pence (0.95 → 0.95p, ~100× low) — verify on the user-visible TICKET; all six LSE instructions were re-staged in pence (133–138), and every wrong limit was unfillably LOW, zero risk.

## earnings-dislocation-graded

**RULE: an equity earnings break is not a muni-style headline dislocation — on an earnings print the headline usually IS the cash-flow news, and "a −20% day is usually correct" graded 7/7.** (Stora-template sweep graded 0-for-7 in court, 2026-08-04.)
16 raw −15%+ breaks → 7 cause-checks → 2 template-matches at triage (VMD, CTRI) → 7 full courts, 0 entries. The muni analogue (Oakland @84, Stora Enso) works when the headline channel cannot reach the pledge/floor; both equity triage-matches died on guide-credibility arithmetic contained in the release itself (VMD: unchanged guide required a +497bp H2 margin step vs +104bp precedent; CTRI: an implied 40bp FY base-margin cut inside a "RAISES" release, H1 op income flat on +32% revenue). Stora's win decomposed to ~⅓ beta + ⅓ sector + ⅓ entry mechanics (limits resting BELOW the panic) — the repeatable edge is entry mechanics plus court discipline, not the dislocation class.
Apply: (1) keep the radar — its byproducts (honesty-catalog entries, transmission data, pre-committed deep ladders) carry the value; (2) triage question stays "does the bad number touch the pillar or just the quarter," plus "does the RELEASE's own arithmetic make the guide incredible?" (implied H2 step vs history — computable same-day); (3) wrong-side-gate check on every dislocation ladder: a limit that fills only on the thesis-killing event must be an ALERT, not a resting GTC (see: resting-order-into-print).

## meme-flow-overruns-strike

**RULE: on a pre-catalyst name gone parabolic, check the actual event clock before attributing an IV spike to the binary — if the catalyst physically cannot resolve within the option's life, the premium is meme/flow, not event, and strikes must be sized for the flow regime.** (SLS/SELLAS, 2026-06-26.)
The book held SLS (basis ~$8.15) with covered $10 calls; the stock hit $11.60 (+207% YTD), IV ~178% (96th pctile) vs HV ~129%, with the Phase-3 OS trial at 78 of 80 required events. Two corrections: (1) an event-COUNT trigger is not the readout — the 80th event only starts database-lock → adjudication → unblinding → topline, a ~4–8 week chain, so the binary could not resolve inside the ~3-week option window and there was no mechanism for binary-driven early assignment; (2) the price driver was reflexive retail/option-demand flow on a 200%-YTD runner, decoupled from an unmoved event timeline.
Apply: separate the Nth-event trigger date from the topline date on every OS/PFS endpoint. Size covered-call strikes for the flow regime — a normal-vol strike gets run over when a name turns meme; strike higher for melt-up room, or accept the cap as a deliberate exit. A covered call overrun by meme momentum is, by accident, a disciplined exit from a parabola (locked gain before both the binary and the mean-reversion); "ride to assignment" is correct.

## resting-order-into-print

**RULE: a resting GTC buy on a name with no dated catalyst is an accidental short-vol position — it fills on the drift toward the print, then eats the gap; every live order carries a dated catalyst or it has no order.** (NATR, 2026-08-06.)
A court-adjudicated starter's GTC (resting since 07-27 at $19.81) filled at 10:27 ET, six hours before an after-close print; the company cut FY guidance and went −17.6% after hours (~−15% on the fill, trivially sized) — short an earnings straddle without anyone deciding to be. The failure was not the analysis: no dated catalyst existed in the ledger, so no guard could fire; a sweep found 25 other names in the same state.
Three flagged states (reference implementation: check_resting_orders_have_catalysts in desk/consistency_check.py): (1) catalyst inside 5 days with a live buy — pull it or consciously accept the gap; (2) STALE BAND — the catalyst already fired; a limit struck on pre-print facts is not a discount after a guide cut (PODD 08-05, NATR 08-06); (3) no dated catalyst — the order cannot be guarded; date it or drop it. On a court verdict, freeze the catalyst date at the same time as the entry band — the band and the date are one artifact.

## paired-price-gates-unfillable

**RULE: a price gate CONJOINED with an evidence trigger never fills — the legs are anticorrelated by construction (evidence re-rates price out of the band; price in the band means the evidence hasn't printed); split into a small position at the price gate now plus a pre-committed pack that buys the evidence at whatever price.** (KER.PA +16% miss, 2026-07-29.)
Kering: the €268 valuation gate FIRED at 248 on 07-05 and was silently re-banded to "225 AND Oct-21 comp evidence"; the stock touched 225.10 and ran +16.2% to €291 on the H1 print. Three compounding faults: (1) the unfillable conjunction; (2) gate-moving after a fire — a fired gate must fill or produce a graded missed-entry row the same day (business-risk findings take SIZE cuts, never deeper price gates — see: response taxonomy); (3) dead prose — the frozen call literally named the 7/29 print as pre-determining, but only Oct-21 made the calendar. Bonus falsifier: the ops forecast was RIGHT (predicted −3/−6 narrowing, actual −2) and the entry was still missed — the reaction bar is not the ops bar, on entry triggers too.
Apply: any freeze whose reasoning names an earlier decisive print must create a calendar row + action pack for THAT print at freeze time. Prose doesn't fire.

## broker-status-label-artifacts

**RULE: never treat a broker API status label as ground truth for an order's life — IBKR's nightly GTC re-confirmation marks live orders REPLACED in the API view while the portal shows the same order ID as Submitted; truth hierarchy = portal-row-by-ID > trade log > API status.** (Observed 2026-07-06, confirmed 2026-07-07 across seven live GTC rungs: STNG ×2, LPG ×2, FRO, FNF, OLLI.)
The nightly re-book leaves stale chain copies in the API view; the working order keeps its ID and stands. The API order list also paginates (~20 rows — count rows before concluding absence) (dated — re-verify before relying).
Apply: (1) before declaring any resting order dead, check the trade log for a fill, then verify the portal row BY ORDER ID — matching ID + Submitted = alive, label cosmetic; (2) NEVER re-stage a rung on a REPLACED label alone — hidden-alive orders double-book; (3) the investigation pattern that worked: time-bracket via the gateway's last order snapshot, kill benign theories (fills, ex-dates, price bands) with data, then let the portal ID check decide.

## executability-verification

**RULE: "close-only / no live bid-ask" is a muni-feed fact, not a universal one — verify executability by exact-ticker contract-resolve plus a live snapshot, never by a name-search miss or a precedent from another asset class.** (Falsified 2026-06-29.)
Four KRX names (COSMECCA, COSMAX, SILICON2, HUGEL) were wrongly tagged "close-only, not executable" because a company-NAME contract search returned empty and the muni close-only precedent made the wrong answer feel confirmed. All four resolve by numeric ticker (e.g. 241710) and stream live two-sided quotes (COSMECCA showed ₩70,300×29 / ₩70,500×35, is_close:false).
Apply: (1) resolve by numeric/exact ticker; (2) pull a snapshot with bid/ask + last and read is_close — a live two-sided quote settles it; (3) the real remaining gate is the ACCOUNT's market-trading permission — flag "tradeable IF permission enabled," don't assert either way. 2026-08-05 addendum: the permission gate has a regulatory floor for options — US-resident accounts are ineligible for ALL non-US single-stock options (not SEC-registered; confirmed by a live submission bounce), while foreign FUTURES options are exempt (CFTC Part 30). Consequently European names take below-market stock rungs only; CSP/covered-call structures are US-listed-options-only. Verify eligibility by ONE staged-submission attempt, never by settings-page inference. Liquidity caveats (thin ADTV, foreign-flow-dominated) are separate and survive.

## within-band-market-timing-dominates

**RULE: within a single rating band, spread variance is dominated by market timing and issuance specifics, NOT operator quality — never claim within-band signal-to-spread alpha from small-N anchor sets; require N≥20.** (2026-05-28, CA charter muni / CSFA LCFF intercept study.)
v1 (N=4): ICEF (operator score 54) and New Designs (score 87), both BB+, priced at identical 143 bps — reported as "clean masking proof." v2 (N=56 anchors, CSFA 2010–2024): the pair was a coincidence (both at the BB+ band median ~145 bps, at different times). The canonical counter-example: Granada Hills Charter, THREE BBB- issuances at operator score 89, priced 224 bps (2017), 71 bps (2019), 70 bps (2021) — a 153 bps swing on the same obligor, same rating, same score, over 38 months. Within-band r(spread, op_score) = −0.38 at N=5: weak, meaningless.
Apply: distinguish across-rating effects (real — the rating-uplift mechanism, R²~19%) from within-rating claims (need large-N backtests). The framework's honest value is rating-band entry plus operator-quality EXCLUSION (avoiding operator distress at the same yield), not within-band spread arbitrage. Anti-pattern check: any "look at this single-pair comparison" moment should immediately trigger "what's the within-band stdev; what would the full anchor set show?" — the Granada Hills swing is the prior, not the surprise.

## trim-priced-in-winners

**RULE: when a held name's operating beat is already priced and the marginal debate has shifted to a line the print won't resolve, the catalyst reaction is negative-asymmetric — trim into strength, decided ex-ante, and freeze BOTH an operating call and a stock-reaction call on every held-name print.** (BRBY.L retro, 2026-07-17.)
Both frozen operating calls (0.63/0.72) graded HIT (+5% comps, Americas +12%, China +9%, wholesale guide raised) and the stock fell −5.4%, round-tripping a week's run-up — a positioning selloff, thesis intact. The signal was ex-ante: a faded consensus long (−19% YTD vs a +6% sector, rating drifted to Hold) whose marginal debate (H1 margin phasing, named by UBS pre-print) the beat could not resolve — so being right on comps had ~no upside power and any cautious margin word had full downside power.
Apply: (1) two-call doctrine on HELD names, not just fresh entries — the divergence between a high operating p and a low reaction p on the same page IS the trim signal (sibling of the HCA split; see: anchor-and-adjust reaction_driver); (2) pre-print rubric: "faded consensus long? marginal debate outside this print's reach?" → if yes, plan = trim-into-strength, set before the date; (3) a priced-in operating win is not edge — the response is a SIZE trim into the event, not a hold. Converse ruling (Cosmecca, 2026-07-20): a "beat-lean" on a number the sell-side already prints as consensus has no edge — into crowded Strong-Buy positioning an in-line print is a sell event (operating 0.58 kept, reaction frozen 0.44).

## overnight-dislocation-reversal

**RULE: the overnight→intraday cross-period reversal is real in liquid names but DEAD in thin names — in a thin name the reversion premium and the transaction cost are the SAME OBJECT (the bid-ask bounce), so a marketable round-trip pays the illiquidity premium it hopes to earn; thin-tape gap-fade is KILLED.** (Tested + court-affirmed 2026-07-13.)
Literature: Lou-Polk-Skouras (JFE 2019) — overnight return continues overnight, reverses intraday, ~3.24%/mo in the value-weighted overnight-loser decile, clientele-driven, not news; the paper EXCLUDES microcaps, so the thin-tape single-name flavor sits outside its clean universe. Desk probe (2yr daily bars, 9 liquid vs 9 thin): liquid replicates (slope −0.109, t=−5.2; down-gap decile +17bps gross, 55% hit, net +7–12bps); thin is dead (slope +0.0001, t=0.0; −2bps before costs; net −52 to −152bps; dislocation-day intraday p5/p95 −844/+800bps adverse-selection tail; survivorship biases thin upward, so truth is worse). The only capture route is becoming the resting liquidity provider — filled precisely when wrong.
Apply: (1) never build a thin-tape overnight-fade; (2) a single-name buy-and-HOLD of a dislocation is a discretionary bet the probe is silent on — size small, govern by the name's real catalyst, don't rationalize as edge; (3) SALVAGE: the liquid-name reversal is a free passive-entry execution overlay — when already buying a liquid name on a no-news overnight down-gap, enter at the OPEN, don't chase; validate vs first-30-min-VWAP fills before trusting magnitude. Not a standalone book.

## conditioning-layer-discovery-state

**RULE: gate every divergence through discovery_state (UNDISCOVERED / DISCOVERING / DISCOVERED_CROWDED) before trading it — the edge connectors find divergences; this layer answers "is it already priced?", and for >$5B names retail-quiet is NOT un-priced without institutional confirmation.** (Built 2026-06-24; reference implementation: verticals/buyside_dd/CONDITIONING_LAYER_SPEC.md, verticals/buyside_dd/connectors/discovery_state.py.)
Architecture: ATTENTION channels (StockTwits, Wikipedia pageviews, GDELT, Google Trends — availability per-channel is perishable (dated — re-verify before relying)) + POSITIONING (SEC fails-to-deliver, FINRA short interest; positioning reads lag ~26d, surfaced in asof_lags, never backfilled) + Phase-2 INSTITUTIONAL channel (analyst coverage density, 13F-filer diff, options IV/OI positioning) with a cap_tier guard downgrading >$5B retail-quiet names to UNDISCOVERED_RETAIL_ONLY.
Graded saves: RCAT — a real USASpending divergence (~$9M obligated vs a $100M+ narrative) flagged DISCOVERED_CROWDED (23.2% SI, 516k-share FTD spike, live social spike; low days-to-cover only because ADV ballooned = squeeze fuel) — a genuine divergence already priced; shorting it = shorting into a squeeze. MVIS — same shape, killed. PODD — the decisive Phase-2 test: retail gauges read UNDISCOVERED while institutional_score hit 0.90 (23 analysts, 747 13F filers trimming, and the elegant tell: IV 46.2% BELOW realized HV 51.2% with option volume 0.42× average — options pricing no fresh fear because the recall already happened). An empty actionable slate from a conditioned run = the engine working.
Standing cautions: (1) a degraded positioning score defaults toward 0 = "undiscovered," the wrong direction — verify analyst coverage directly when sub-signals are unavailable (the Cosmecca false-tag); (2) USASpending divergences need entity resolution plus a "low ≠ understated" check before trusting (name-collision case law); (3) the layer is CONTROL, not alpha — its outputs also feed the flow gate (bear-thesis crowding, see: value-ladder-flow-gate) and the priced-in-beat check (see: trim-priced-in-winners).
