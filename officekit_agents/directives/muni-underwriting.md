# Municipal Underwriting — the muni stack doctrine

Distilled doctrine for underwriting, screening, and trading a hold-to-maturity municipal book (CA-centric case law, CUSIP-agnostic method). Covers the segregated-levy insulation thesis, the extended screen suite, EMMA data mechanics, liquidity/growth machinery, and the rating-conditional wrap curve. Screens surface; DD adjudicates.

## dislocation-buy

**Buy unlimited ad-valorem GO bonds marked down on a rating or operating headline that cannot reach the segregated debt-service levy; the standard screen's exclusions are, inverted, the alpha list.** The clean-book underwrite hard-flags NEGATIVE/QUALIFIED fiscal certs and downgrades — but for a hold-to-maturity holder of an unlimited ad-valorem GO those bonds are money-good (Moody's: GO defaults were 5 of ~71 historical muni defaults with ~100% recovery; zero modern CA school-GO defaults), because debt service is paid from a segregated county levy ring-fenced from the operating fund. Hard gates (built 2026-06-20; buy-the-dip kills without them):
1. UNLIMITED ad-valorem GO only — never revenue/COP/lease/limited-tax, where real impairment is possible.
2. The distress must be RING-FENCED: operating cert, enrollment, pension, hazard headline = dislocation signal. SECURITY-reaching distress (disclosure fraud, bond-proceeds misuse, taxpayer/debt concentration eroding the AV base, structural call traps) = real risk, screened out.
3. Every candidate is BUY-PENDING-DD — confirm the levy/lien insulation per name.
A pure low-coupon discount on a POSITIVE-cert name is not a dislocation, just coupon math. Validating case: Oakland Unified GO bought at 83.75 — both agencies rate the GO ABOVE the operating district (Moody's GO A1 vs issuer A2; Fitch GO AA- vs IDR BBB, a four-notch "California lockbox" gap) (dated — re-verify before relying). The rating architecture itself prices the insulation; the market prices the operating headline. Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit dislocation_finder.py.

## section-53515-lien-not-teeter

**The universal CA school-GO insulation mechanism is the Government Code §53515 statutory first lien + a county-held interest-and-sinking fund used solely for the bonds + the county's standing over-levy — NOT the Teeter Plan.** Framework correction (2026-06-20) affecting every CA school-GO DD: Alameda and Los Angeles counties do not apply Teeter to school-district GO levies. Stop asserting "Teeter 100% advance" as the universal insulation; cite §53515 + the segregated fund, and check Teeter applicability per county. The over-levy structure (Oakland carried ~18 months of coverage) is arguably stronger than Teeter — it builds a structural surplus rather than back-filling delinquencies.

## extended-underwriting-screens

**Run 11 composable screens (fire/AI-sensitivity/quake base + flood, SGMA groundwater, taxpayer-concentration, debt-burden, fiscal-health, bond-structure, water-revenue, issuer-litigation), each a self-contained module returning a per-CUSIP field dict.** Built 2026-06-19. Calibration is load-bearing:
- HARD exclusions: cert NEG/QUAL, disclosure-uncovered, geo-unresolved, insulation score <90, fire tail ≥30%, near-par-call (<2 yrs to call AND price ≥ call−1 on a fresh mark), capital appreciation bonds, taxpayer/debt concentration, water DSCR breach, and security-reaching litigation only.
- FLOOD = REVIEW, never FLAG: fire's AV-erosion channel is strong (insurers actively withdrawing) but flood has NFIP backstop + levee systems + Prop 13 AV stickiness; a fire-equivalent ≥30% threshold flagged the entire Central Valley core (low discrimination). Recalibrated to the top ~15% tail because FEMA NRI rates nearly all developed CA high (median 59%).
- FISCAL (enrollment/pension) + SGMA = REVIEW: the GO levy is insulated from the general fund; UNVERIFIABLE pension is a coverage note, not a flag.
- Water-revenue underwrite catches engineered coverage: one sewer credit showed organic DSCR 0.79x dressed to 1.10x via rate-stabilization transfers — always recompute DSCR without RSF draws.
- Community-college districts grade on accreditation status (good standing → clear; probation/show-cause → FLAG; warning/unknown → REVIEW), never auto-FLAG for lacking a K-12 fiscal cert.
Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit/underwrite_candidates.py, grade_universe.py, muni_credit/ modules (flood_overlay, sgma_overlay, av_concentration, fiscal_health, structure_screen, water_revenue_underwrite, issuer_litigation, accjc_overlay).

## litigation-severity-split

**Only litigation that reaches bondholders hard-excludes: SEC enforcement, securities fraud, disclosure violations, bond-proceeds misuse. Operating-level governance failure (general-fund fraud, board corruption, embezzlement) is REVIEW on an insured statutory-lien GO.** Case law (Stockton reconciliation, 2026-06-19): the screen's first cut hard-excluded a district over a 2023 state-auditor general-fund fraud finding; the hand-written DD had correctly graded it BUY/monitor because the fraud cannot reach a segregated county levy. Honesty-alpha corollary: do not re-exclude honestly-disclosed, structurally ring-fenced operating bad news — the disclosure itself is a positive signal.

## screens-surface-dd-adjudicates

**Always full-DD the actual book holdings; screens are recall, DD is the verdict — and every DD gap found goes back into the pipeline the same session.** The 2026-06-20 full-book pass surfaced four screen-invisible failure modes, all now wired in:
1. CERT STALENESS — cached state fiscal-cert lists ran a full fiscal year behind and missed a live QUALIFIED downgrade; certifications now re-fetched each cron (the CDE portal sits behind a Radware captcha — a bare user-agent fails, the full Sec-Ch-Ua/Sec-Fetch browser fingerprint passes), parsed newest-period-wins.
2. The Atwater premium-call trap — a bond bought above par with an UNVERIFIABLE call and a par call inside a year collapses yield; soft-flag whenever price ≥100.5 and the call schedule is unverifiable.
3. SGMA county-altitude false positives — basin mapping must be at subbasin level, plus an ag-AV gate so large urban tax bases don't inherit an agricultural-overdraft flag.
4. INSURER-FROM-OS overrides EMMA's often-wrong Insured boolean — parse the official statement for the wrap.
See: feedback_did_we_actually_intention_to_mechanism.

## issuer-matcher-hardening

**Name-to-issuer resolution must fail LOUD: ambiguity or collision routes to UNRESOLVED and a hard FLAG, never a silent best-guess pick.** Fuzzy matching of vendor shorthand (e.g. OpenFIGI) to a district registry silently misbound three top-5 buys (2026-06-19). The four fixes, generalizable to any entity resolver:
1. Expand shorthand abbreviations before matching (VLY→VALLEY, CLG→COMMUNITY COLLEGE, ESD→ELEMENTARY, etc.).
2. District-TYPE agreement gate — extract type (ELEM/HIGH/UNIFIED/CC) from both sides; cross-type pairs are penalized so a college can't bind to a same-place-name K-12.
3. Detect institution class from the RAW name + ticker, independent of normalization.
4. Top-2 candidates within 0.05 of different entities → UNRESOLVED-AMBIG; two distinct CUSIP-6 roots → one district → UNRESOLVED-COLLISION; both hard-FLAG and route to EMMA official-statement confirmation, the authority.
Revenue-bond guard: revenue markers (SWR/WTR/UTIL/REV/PFA/SANITAT…) without a school marker short-circuit to the revenue track — a city sewer revenue bond had matched a same-name school district and passed every GO screen as a clean school GO. Gotchas: downstream stores read the underwriting cache before the credit cache, so fixes must sync both; validate any matcher change against a regression panel of known-good bindings before re-running the store. See: feedback_dd_entity_resolution. Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit school_go_issuer_credit.match_district().

## liquidity-gate

**Gate muni inclusion on the realized MSRB trade tape — recency, frequency, and two-sidedness — because munis have no NBBO and a scanner ask says nothing about buildability; spread is measured but is NOT a floor criterion for a hold-to-maturity ladder.** Trade types: S = dealer→customer (your buy side), P = customer→dealer (your sell side), D = inter-dealer. Floor (tunable): last trade ≤90 days, ≥12 trades/yr, ≥1 two-sided day (both an S and a P — a one-way tape means you can buy but may be stuck holding). Same-day S−P is the honest effective spread (controls for rate drift). Key reframe: an HTM ladder pays the spread once at purchase and redeems at par, so the cost is the buy-side half-spread amortized ≈1–3 bps/yr — negligible; illiquidity only bites on forced early sale. Applied 2026-06-09: the actively-traded sleeve measured ~0.2pt same-day spread (institutional-tight) vs the industry ~1–2% odd-lot round-trip average; a third of one candidate basket had no two-sided print in a year → build multi-week opportunistically, not same-day (dated — re-verify before relying). Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit/liquidity_gate.py, wired into build_option_B.py after credit-verify, before sizing.

## yield-reach-credit-axis

**To push yield above the insulated GO book, the axis that pays is credit/pledge subordination — not physical hazard and not state-revenue sensitivity.** Empirics (2026-06-22, n≈260): fire and seismic exposure tiers priced within ~1 bp of each other on high-grade GO — §53515 makes unlimited GO money-good and the market knows it, so relaxing hazard screens takes real AV-erosion risk for ZERO yield (hazard is only ever a buy on a headline dislocation). State-GO/university exposure to a state general-fund revenue shock adds only ~20–40 bps. The real reach = +80–290 bps TEY in charter-school revenue, hospital revenue, RPTTF tax-allocation, and conduits (dated — re-verify before relying). Two underwritable insulation parallels to §53515:
1. RPTTF tax-allocation (post-2012 successor agencies): segregated incremental-tax lien; city general-fund turmoil does not reach it; sector default base-rate ≈ 0.
2. System hospitals (large multi-site A1–AA obligated groups) ≈ money-good; district hospitals are the trap (single-county, Medicaid-concentrated, falling ratings) — except where DSCR is overwhelming.
Charter gauntlet method (conduit issuer; the obligor is the SCHOOL — resolve via the EMMA OS): decisive factors are charter renewal status/term, enrollment trend, DSCR, days-cash, and operator type (multi-site CMO vs single-site). It bifurcates hard; the gauntlet caught covenant non-compliance, a 1.00x-at-floor coverage print, a blank covenant cert, a defunct re-CUSIP, and multiple taxable mislabels. Per-CUSIP tax status is JS-gated on EMMA — confirm on each official statement before any TEY gross-up. See: feedback_muni_taxable_gate.

## emma-continuing-disclosure

**The EMMA continuing-disclosure endpoint (15c2-12 event notices, due within 10 business days) is the fastest honesty channel in munis — watch it as a holdings delta feed, and triage its two noise patterns.** Mechanics: POST /Security/ContinuingDisclosurePartialView with the plaintext CUSIP and date-range "All", after accepting the Disclaimer6 cookie — returns the full filing history per CUSIP. Triage rules (validated on 25 live names):
1. Atwater pattern — on-time OPERATING-data filings mask chronically late AUDITED financials (three consecutive failure-to-file notices behind a punctual ops report); check audit currency separately from filing currency.
2. Wrap-event noise — insurer notices (e.g. the AGM merger, 08/2024) fire on every wrapped CUSIP as "financial difficulty"; that is insurer-channel news, not issuer credit.
Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit/emma_continuing_disclosure.py; cd_monitor.py runs the holdings delta watch on the daily cron.

## emma-annual-filings-av-underwrite

**For agency-unrated school GOs, underwrite off the annual-filing PDFs on the same CD endpoint — and the metric that matters is GO debt as a % of assessed value, not the tax-base story.** The Annual Financial Information links carry AV totals and history, GO debt schedules, Teeter confirmation, and top-taxpayer tables (land-use mix only in the issuance OS — vintage caveat). Case law: a district with 48% office-CRE AV concentration was harmless at 1.0% debt/AV (96x asset coverage); a clean comparable sat at 0.44% (230x). Identity discipline first — bind by EMMA title, never fuzzy match.

## emma-security-details-and-ytw

**Pull per-CUSIP coupon/maturity/call analytics from the EMMA security-details page — and know that EMMA's reported trade yield (YX) is MSRB yield-to-WORST, not YTM.** Mechanics: GET /Security/Details/<cusip> renders only after accepting the disclaimer (POST the page's own VIEWSTATE to /Disclaimer.aspx); descriptive fields are plain text between markup — match on a tag-stripped copy, not raw HTML. The trade tape is a `var tradeData` JS object: rows newest-first with dollar price (PX), reported yield (YX), date, and trade type; take PX and YX from the same row, preferring the most recent dealer-sale. The YX gotcha cost a mislabel: a basket "avg gross YTM" built from EMMA traded yields is really avg YTW. Report both — YTW from YX (authoritative; captures the full call + sinking schedule) and YTM computed independently; the gap is the call give-up (~47 bps avg on one CA basket, concentrated in premium names) (dated — re-verify before relying). Bond math that validates against EMMA: street convention, semiannual, 30/360, fractional first period, bisection solve; modified duration off the worst-date cash-flow set. Do NOT trust a single-next-call YTW model on premium bonds with sinking funds — anchor YTW to YX, use your engine for YTM + duration only. Guard near-term calls priced off stale marks (they produce nonsense deep-negative yield-to-call — suppress to to-maturity and flag). Always name the yield metric: gross YTW / gross YTM / tax-equivalent / net-of-fees are four different numbers. Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit/compute_bond_analytics.py.

## ccrc-underwriting

**CCRC/senior-living is not a blanket trap: the mature nonprofit obligated-group tier is investment-grade and underwritable, and state mortgage insurance (Cal-Mortgage/CHFFA) is the key insulation.** Built 2026-05-27/30. The trap framing (fill-up risk, single-site, poor recovery, housing-wealth correlation) applies to start-ups and standalones; A-/AA- rated, Type-A life-care, decades-old diversified obligated groups sit in a different class (dated — re-verify before relying). Method: roll sub-communities up to the obligated-group credit level before screening (one operator's 12 communities = 1 obligor); screen on occupancy, days-cash, and the CMS certification-number resolution (federal nursing-home star rating / special-focus / civil-monetary-penalty as an independent operating signal); compose detectors as a high-precision UNION exclusion floor, not a ranker — a curated non-distressed universe legitimately excludes zero. Cal-Mortgage state loan insurance addresses exactly CCRC's weaknesses (recovery + wealth-shock correlation), moving the insured subset toward the money-good tier. Risk-budget: the insured mature-OG tier earns a real ~3–5% sleeve slot, not 0%. Open discipline: always price the universe (YTM/TEY) to judge compensation, and confirm insurance per-CUSIP. Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit/detectors/ccrc_occupancy.py, ccrc_days_cash.py, cms_ccn_resolver.py, cal_mortgage_cascade_monitor.py; run_ccrc_union_screen.py.

## bond-scanner-book-growth

**Grow the book with a standing incremental scanner that diffs new universe names against held + already-seen and funnels only the NEW through the validated screen — and respect EMMA's rate limit: incremental batches, never a full pass.** Funnel (2026-06-17; reuse, don't reinvent): feasible price/maturity → sector classification → the authoritative EMMA issue-title gate (security type ∈ insulated set AND not federally taxable — this catches the COP/CFD/lease/taxable names the vendor pre-filter misses) → liquidity floor → de-minimis-aware after-tax TEY as the selection metric → fit-to-need (ladder-rung gap + new-issuer preference). Two guards: (1) outlier quarantine — abnormal yield on a "safe" pledge (gross YTW >5% or TEY >9.5%) is cheap-for-a-reason, DD before trusting; (2) MIN_TEY floor — premium callables with negative yield-to-worst pass pledge + liquidity but are uninvestable. THE GOTCHA: a full-universe pass (~1000 names × 2 EMMA hits) trips a 403 block whose short error page silently scored as sector OTHER → ~978 false rejects. Fix: raise on 403/short-page, mark fetch_blocked (retryable, not a reject), back off, refresh session every 3 blocks, stop after 8; base throttle 0.7s/name; cadence = daily --max-new 80. Universe-refresh gotcha: the broker BOND.MUNI scanner returns only top-50-by-yield per query, so one wide coupon band returns all premium 5–6s and zero 3% discounts — partition by coupon band so the de-minimis discounts (highest after-tax TEY) surface. Scheduler note: in-session cron creation is not durable in this environment; true persistence needs OS launchd/crontab. Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit/bond_scanner.py, refresh_universe.py.

## wildfire-overlay

**Score per-district wildfire exposure with a pure table join — FEMA National Risk Index census tracts × the NCES EDGE district-to-tract relationship file, building-value-weighted — because the credit channel is insurer withdrawal dragging the assessed-value base, and fires are correlated statewide (cap the AGGREGATE).** Mechanics: the NRI tract data lives on the official ArcGIS FeatureServer (OpenFEMA does not carry NRI; the static-file path is dead); "No Rating" = no modeled exposure → 0, not missing; NRI ratings are exposure-conditioned (a town that already burned scores 95 but rates Moderate — read score AND rating). Join via the NCES GRF LEA↔tract file. The channel: insurer withdrawal → property uninsurable/unmarketable → values stall → downward reassessment + frozen turnover drag the AV base the unlimited levy rides on; the benign historical default record predates this channel. Fires are not single-fault events (2017/2020 sieges were statewide), so cap aggregate high-fire exposure across the book, unlike per-zone earthquake caps. Case law: seismic diversification had silently CONCENTRATED fire risk — the low-seismic comfort names were 34–100% high-fire-tract names (2026-06-10); hedging one hazard can concentrate another, so check the joint distribution. Tail-vs-mean fix (2026-06-17): the high-tract share is a TAIL metric — always surface both the mean score and the tail, and BAND it (≥30% FLAG, 15–30% REVIEW); a tail-only ≥30% cut had let 16–28%-tail names grade CLEAR. Data-integrity lesson: a partial re-run once OVERWROTE the shared per-issuer store with only that run's CUSIPs, silently wiping fire fields for the optimizer — partial re-runs must MERGE into shared overlays, never replace. Promoted to a generic 18-hazard connector for any insurability-shaped claim. Reference implementation: verticals/muni_credit/wildfire_overlay.py; verticals/buyside_dd/connectors/fema_nri_hazard.py.

## rating-conditional-wrap-compression

**Insurance-wrap spread compression is conditional on the underlying credit: ~0 bps at AA-, ~140 bps at BBB-, ~360 bps at CCC- (AGM measurement, 2026-05-28) — so wrap alpha lives only where the underlying is sub-IG, and any wrap study needs at least 3 rating-tier points.** This reconciles the academic result (Cornaggia/Hund/Nguyen 2024: post-2008 muni insurance "statistically insignificant on average") — the average pools IG (wrap ≈ 0) with distressed (wrap extreme), hiding the conditional curve. Cross-sector confirmations: a state housing-agency wrap on A1 underlying ≈ 60 bps; an unwrapped CCC-equivalent housing JPA at +475 bps over MMD AAA; Cal-Mortgage on BBB-/B+ CCRC/nursing underlying = full decoupling, ~30–60 bps realizable; FHA Section 242 hospital wrap leaks ~25–50 bps of program tail risk vs Cal-Mortgage (dated — re-verify before relying). Apply:
- Sector selection: predict edge by mapping the sector's underlying-rating distribution × wrap type; wraps on already-IG school GO produce no framework alpha.
- Thesis construction: "buy the wrap on a junky underlying" works precisely where the obligor universe clusters BBB- to B+.
- New wraps (AGM, BAM, Cal-Mortgage, FHA 242/542c, GNMA, Section 8 HAP, Freddie TEL all follow the pattern): characterize the compression curve with wrap-on-AA / wrap-on-BBB / wrap-on-sub-IG points before claiming anything — a single-point measurement misleads.
- Negative-finding interpretation: when a wrap doesn't compress, the underlying is probably already IG; the claim is "the wrap doesn't need to work here," not "the wrap doesn't work." See: anti-masking-findings-have-value.
